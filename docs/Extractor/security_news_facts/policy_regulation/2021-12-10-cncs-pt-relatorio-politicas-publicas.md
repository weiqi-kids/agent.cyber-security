## 摘要

Portugal's CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory published a report surveying national cybersecurity strategies and public programs, including citizen perception indicators and Portugal's positioning within the EU.

## 事實內容

The Cybersecurity Observatory of CNCS published the "Report on Public Cybersecurity Policies." This document fulfills one of the Observatory's objectives: to monitor the development and application of public policies in the cybersecurity area. The report surveys national strategies and public programs related to cybersecurity and analyzes available indicators on citizen perceptions regarding the intersection of public policies and cyberspace security. The analysis covers the national landscape and also addresses Portugal's positioning within the European Union context.
