## 摘要

Finland's Cybersecurity Act has been passed by Parliament, bringing NIS 2 Directive obligations into force on 8 April 2025; affected entities must now register and comply with new risk management and reporting obligations.

## 事實內容

Finland's Parliament has passed the Cybersecurity Act, implementing the EU's NIS 2 Directive requirements into Finnish law. Key details:

- **Effective date**: 8 April 2025
- **New obligations**:
  - Risk management obligations for many sectors
  - Incident reporting obligations
  - Registration requirement: One of the first steps is registering for a list of entities subject to the Act
- **Affected sectors**: Multiple sectors now subject to NIS 2-derived cybersecurity requirements
- The Cybersecurity Act represents Finland's national transposition of the EU NIS 2 Directive

Organisations subject to the Act must take immediate steps to register and begin implementing the required risk management and reporting measures.

## 受影響系統

- Finnish organisations in sectors covered by NIS 2 Directive
- Critical infrastructure operators
- Essential and important entities under NIS 2 classification
