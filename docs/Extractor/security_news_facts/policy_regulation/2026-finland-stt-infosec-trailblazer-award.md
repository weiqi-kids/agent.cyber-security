# Finnish News Agency STT receives Information Security Trailblazer award

## Summary
Traficom granted its Information Security Trailblazer award to Finnish News Agency STT for open communication after falling victim to a cyberattack in summer 2022.

## Key Details
- **Award**: Information Security Trailblazer
- **Authority**: Traficom
- **Recipient**: Finnish News Agency (STT)
- **Reason**: Open communication post-cyberattack
- **Incident**: Summer 2022 cyberattack on STT

## Context
This award recognizes transparency and information sharing after security incidents, encouraging organizational openness.

- Transparency recognition
