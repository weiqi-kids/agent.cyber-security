## 事實內容

I had the pleasure to take part in the TRANSITS II training in Utrecht in January 2020. One of the great things about these trainings is that they are not just about tools and techniques, but about getting to know people from other CERTs/CSIRTs, especially from the European community. However, the training part was also fun to have. TRANSITS II is split into three modules, i.e. forensics, communication and NetFlow, distributed over three days. While the netflow part gets a full day on its own, forensics and communication alternate over the other two days. For the practical parts, prepared laptops are provided, which is a good thing because everyone can start working right away, but had the disadvantage that we couldn't practice outside of the course hours and couldn't take any data/scripts...
