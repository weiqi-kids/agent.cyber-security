Canadian Centre for Cyber Security launches a new initiative to strengthen critical infrastructure cyber readiness against escalated and severe cyber threats in Canada.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (Cyber Centre) announced a new initiative aimed at helping Canada's critical infrastructure operators prepare for severe cyber threats.

Key points:
- The initiative focuses on strengthening critical infrastructure cyber readiness for a resilient Canada under escalated threat conditions.
- The announcement was made via a formal press release from the Communications Security Establishment (CSE).
- The initiative relates to the "Critical Infrastructure Resilience and Escalated Threat Navigation" program published the same day.
- The program signals heightened threat awareness and urgency for Canadian critical infrastructure operators to act.
