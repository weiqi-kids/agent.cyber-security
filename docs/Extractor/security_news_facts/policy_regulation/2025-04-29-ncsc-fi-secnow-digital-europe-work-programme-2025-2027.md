## 摘要

EU's Digital Europe Programme Work Programme for 2025–2027 published with EUR 390 million allocated to cybersecurity; calls will focus on AI, quantum-safe algorithms, with first calls opening during 2025.

## 事實內容

The 2025–2027 Work Programme of the EU's Digital Europe Programme has been published at the end of March 2025. Key details:

- **Total cybersecurity allocation**: EUR 390 million over the 2025–2027 period
- **Technology focus areas**:
  - Artificial intelligence (AI) development and application
  - Quantum-safe algorithms and post-quantum cryptography
  - Other emerging cybersecurity technologies
- **Timeline**: First funding calls expected to open during 2025
- The Work Programme sets the strategic direction and funding priorities for cybersecurity investments across the EU
