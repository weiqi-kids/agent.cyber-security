## 摘要

CNCS published the full Minimum Cybersecurity Capabilities Roadmap (Roteiro), defining five capability phases for organizations to gradually implement cybersecurity measures aligned with the QNRCS framework.

## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) defined a cybersecurity capability model aiming to improve processes, people, and technology within Portuguese organizations, with special focus on SMEs. The Roteiro presents a set of actions divided into five phases, designed for gradual adaptation, implementable through internal means or by outsourcing solutions. These activities, framed within the National Cybersecurity Reference Framework (QNRCS), represent a minimum cybersecurity capability baseline, providing organizations with necessary instruments to address threats in the digital world.
