## 摘要

Canadian Cyber Centre releases formal guidance on cyber incident reporting, detailing required information sharing and technical artifacts for critical infrastructure entities.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security has published ITSM.00.140, a management series document effective January 29, 2026, establishing formal guidelines for cyber incident reporting and information sharing requirements.

**Key Components:**

**Contextual Information Requirements:**
- Summary of observed activity or incident
- Threat form identification (malware, DOS, actor, motivation, vector, impact)
- Access method (phishing, vulnerability exploitation, etc.)
- Timeline of events
- Scope of incident (affected systems, compromised data)
- Network traffic details
- Mitigation actions taken
- Current incident status
- Indicators of compromise (IOCs)
- Next steps and contact information

**Technical Artifacts Required:**
- Suspicious/malicious IPs and domains
- Malicious file hashes
- Suspicious URLs
- Malware samples (documents and files)
- Security logs (event, system, access, IDS/IPS, network, firewall, EDR, DNS, VPN, database, mail server logs)
- Forensic artifacts (disk images, memory dumps, registry entries, system drives)

**Legal Framework:**
- Bound by Communications Security Establishment Act and Privacy Act
- Non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) with critical infrastructure partners
- Raw victim data not shared with external entities
- IP addresses and domains presumed not to be owned by reporting organization

**Pre-Incident Sharing Encouraged:**
- Alert rules and thresholds
- Security logs from network devices, servers, workstations, and applications
- EDR/XDR system configurations

**Recommended Information Sharing:**
- Threat intelligence reports
- Indicators of compromise
- Best practices and security recommendations
- Vulnerability information and patches
- Incident reports
- Anonymous sharing mechanisms
- Automated threat intelligence sharing platforms (STIX/TAXII)
- Collaborative analysis and research

The publication emphasizes pre-approval from executive, legal, and operational teams before incident occurrence, and encourages sharing with managed security service providers.

## 受影響系統

- Critical infrastructure entities in Canada
- Government departments and agencies subject to CCCS reporting requirements
- Organizations with managed security service providers
