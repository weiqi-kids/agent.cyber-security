## 摘要

Portugal endorses the International Counter Ransomware Initiative (CRI) guidance document for organisations during ransomware incidents, recognizing it as an important guide for mitigation and resilience, with emphasis on the role of the insurance sector.

## 事實內容

Portugal subscribed to the "Guidance for organisations during Ransomware incidents" published by the International Counter Ransomware Initiative (CRI). Portugal recognized the document as an important guidance resource for mitigating this type of cybersecurity incident. The guidance is built on a collaborative approach with the insurance sector, addressing the role that insurance companies play in cybersecurity resilience and protection against incidents. The CRI guidance aims to help organizations respond effectively to ransomware attacks, covering incident response procedures, negotiation considerations, and recovery strategies. Portugal's endorsement demonstrates national alignment with international best practices for ransomware mitigation.

## 受影響系統

- Organizations across all sectors facing ransomware threats
- Cybersecurity incident response teams
- Insurance sector (cyber insurance)
