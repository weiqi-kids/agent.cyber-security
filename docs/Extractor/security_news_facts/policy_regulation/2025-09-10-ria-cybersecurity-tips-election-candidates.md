## 摘要

RIA published a cybersecurity guide for KOV 2025 local election candidates, covering how to protect against cyber threats and disinformation during digital campaigns.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) published a cybersecurity checklist (meelespea) for KOV 2025 (local government elections 2025) candidates to help run campaigns safely and responsibly in the digital environment. The guide outlines key recommendations on:
- Protection against cyber threats during digital campaigns
- Defense against disinformation
- Responsible use of the digital environment for campaigning

This guidance aims to help political candidates maintain cybersecurity awareness during election campaigns.

## 受影響系統

- KOV 2025 election candidates (Estonia)
- Digital campaign infrastructure
