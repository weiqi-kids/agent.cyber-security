## 摘要

NCSC publishes analysis of May 2020 US sanctions against Huawei that led to modifications in UK security mitigation strategy for Huawei equipment.

## 事實內容

The UK National Cyber Security Centre released a summary of its analysis regarding the May 2020 US sanctions imposed on Huawei. The report:

- Analyzes the technical and security implications of US export restrictions on Huawei
- Explains how sanctions affect Huawei's ability to source critical components
- Details why NCSC modified its security mitigation strategy for Huawei equipment in UK networks
- Addresses supply chain security concerns arising from sanctions

The US sanctions restricted Huawei's access to semiconductor technology, forcing the company to seek alternative suppliers. The NCSC concluded that these supply chain changes increased security risks, requiring adjustments to the UK's mitigation strategy for Huawei equipment in telecommunications infrastructure.

## 受影響系統

- UK telecommunications infrastructure
- Huawei network equipment
- 5G network deployments
