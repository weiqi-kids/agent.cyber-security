## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) released a new layout of the Quadro Nacional de Referência para a Cibersegurança (QNRCS). The document was initially presented in June at the annual C-DAYS conference, where topics of digital economy and organizational threats and vulnerabilities in cyberspace were addressed. The updated version features a new layout facilitating perception and understanding for all interested parties. The document provides a set of recommendations for organizations to define a strategy involving their entire structure. The QNRCS has five core objectives: identify, protect, detect, respond, and recover. Organizations can adhere voluntarily, benefiting from a homogeneous approach that promotes a national response to cyber threats.
