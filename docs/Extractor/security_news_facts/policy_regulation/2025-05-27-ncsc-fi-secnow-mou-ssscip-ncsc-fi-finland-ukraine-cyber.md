## 摘要

Finland (NCSC-FI) and Ukraine (SSSCIP) signed a Memorandum of Understanding to strengthen cyber security cooperation, facilitate best practices sharing, and enhance technical information exchange between the two countries.

## 事實內容

Finland and Ukraine have formalized their cybersecurity cooperation by signing a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between:

- **SSSCIP**: State Service of Special Communications and Information Protection of Ukraine
- **NCSC-FI**: National Cyber Security Centre Finland (under Traficom)

The MoU aims to:
- Reinforce collaboration between the two countries' cyber security authorities
- Facilitate sharing of best practices in cybersecurity
- Enable technical information exchange between Finnish and Ukrainian cybersecurity organisations
- Promote cyber security and cyber protection jointly

This agreement represents a formal bilateral commitment between Finland and Ukraine in the cybersecurity domain, reflecting ongoing European solidarity with Ukraine in the cyber realm.
