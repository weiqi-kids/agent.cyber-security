## 事實內容
On February 18, 2025, in New York, a thematic event was co-organized by Latvia, the Dominican Republic, the Republic of Indonesia, and UNIDIR (United Nations Institute for Disarmament Research). The event was held as a side event to the UN Open-ended Working Group (OEWG) and focused on strengthening national cybersecurity capabilities and incident response capacity. CERT.LV's Director participated in this international forum, contributing to discussions on enhancing global cybersecurity resilience and cooperation.
