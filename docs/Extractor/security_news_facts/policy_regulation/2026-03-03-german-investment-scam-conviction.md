## 摘要

德國法院判決投資詐騙網路主謀，該網路於 2017–2019 年在阿爾巴尼亞設立呼叫中心，詐騙受害者近 800 萬歐元，員工達 600 人。

## 事實內容

A German court convicted the alleged mastermind behind a global online investment fraud network in a significant cybercrime prosecution outcome.

**Criminal operation details:**
- **Active period:** 2017–2019
- **Operation base:** Call center in Albania
- **Peak workforce:** Up to 600 employees
- **Total victim losses:** Approximately €8 million (~$9.4 million USD)
- **Primary victims:** Individuals in German-speaking countries (Germany, Austria, Switzerland)

**Modus operandi:**
1. Trained call-center agents posed as investment advisers
2. Agents contacted potential victims and built trust through repeated interactions
3. Victims were persuaded to deposit funds into fake online trading platforms
4. The trading platforms were entirely controlled by the fraudulent network
5. Deposited funds were stolen; victims had no legitimate investment positions

**Legal outcome:**
- German court found the defendant guilty of running the fraudulent call-center operation
- Conviction represents a cross-border law enforcement success (Germany prosecuting Albania-based operation)

**Significance:**
This conviction demonstrates the capacity of European law enforcement to prosecute cybercrime and investment fraud operations run across borders, and serves as a legal precedent in the ongoing fight against online investment fraud schemes.

## 受影響系統

- Online trading platforms (fake, controlled by fraudsters)
- Financial accounts of victims in German-speaking countries
