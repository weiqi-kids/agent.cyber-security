## Summary

SK-CERT and the National Security Authority (NBU) of Slovakia are organizing the KYBER2026 conference — a NIS2-focused event titled "KYBER2026: NIS2, CRA and the Future of Cyber Resilience in Slovakia." The conference is scheduled for April 27–28, 2026, at Hotel SITNO.

## Key Details

- **Event**: KYBER2026 conference
- **Dates**: April 27–28, 2026
- **Location**: Hotel SITNO, Congress Hall K1
- **Day 1 (April 27)**: 09:00–16:00
  - Opening by Roman Konečný, Director of NBU
  - 09:05–10:20: Report on cybersecurity for 2025 in Slovakia
- **Focus topics**: NIS2 Directive, Cyber Resilience Act (CRA), Slovak national cyber resilience strategy
- **Organizer**: NBU (Národný bezpečnostný úrad / National Security Authority of Slovakia)

## Relevance

- NIS2 transposition and CRA implementation are current top regulatory priorities across the EU
- This conference reflects EU member states' active engagement with new cybersecurity regulatory obligations
- Relevant for policy makers, compliance officers, and critical infrastructure operators in the EU/CEE region
