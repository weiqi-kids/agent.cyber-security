# New requirements for strong electronic identification make services more secure

**Source:** NCSC-FI Security Now!  
**Link:** https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/ajankohtaista/vahvan-sahkoisen-tunnistuksen-uudet-vaatimukset-tekevat-asioinnista-entista  
**Published:** 2023-05-24

## Summary

The Finnish Transport and Communications Agency's regulation on strong electronic identification and trust services enters fully into force in June 2023. Includes two important sections that make the use of electronic services even more secure.

## Key Points

- New regulation effective June 2023
- Enhanced security for electronic services
- Strong electronic identification requirements
- Trust services regulation update
