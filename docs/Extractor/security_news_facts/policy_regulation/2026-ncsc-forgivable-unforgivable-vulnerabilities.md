## 摘要

UK NCSC發布評估「可原諒」與「不可原諒」漏洞的方法論，旨在消除漏洞類別並使頂層緩解措施更易實施。

## 事實內容

The UK National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) published research designed to eradicate vulnerability classes and make top-level mitigations easier to implement. The methodology introduces a framework for categorizing vulnerabilities as "forgivable" (those arising from honest mistakes or complexity) versus "unforgivable" (those resulting from negligence or ignoring known best practices).

The approach aims to help organizations prioritize remediation efforts by focusing on unforgivable vulnerabilities that should never occur in properly designed and maintained systems. This includes vulnerabilities resulting from failure to apply known mitigations, use of deprecated technologies, or ignoring security best practices.

By distinguishing between these categories, the NCSC hopes to drive systemic improvements in software development and deployment practices, ultimately reducing the overall attack surface. The methodology provides practical guidance for security teams and software developers.
