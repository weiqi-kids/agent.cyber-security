## 摘要

Portugal published Regulation No. 183/2022 in the Official Gazette (Diário da República), establishing technical instructions for permanent contact points, security officers, asset inventories, annual reports, and incident notifications under the Cyberspace Security Legal Framework (DL 65/2021).

## 事實內容

Regulation No. 183/2022 (Regulamento n.º 183/2022, de 21 de fevereiro) was published in the Diário da República, 2nd series. The regulation establishes technical instructions for the following obligations under the Cyberspace Security Legal Framework (Decreto-Lei n.º 65/2021 of 30 July):

**Obligations covered**:
- Communication and information related to permanent contact points
- Security officers (responsável de segurança)
- Asset inventory
- Annual reports
- Incident notification procedures

The regulation underwent a public consultation process, with the final decision and reasoned report published on the CNCS website. The full text of the regulation is available for download at cncs.gov.pt.

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese public administration entities
- Critical infrastructure operators
- Essential service operators and digital service providers subject to DL 65/2021
