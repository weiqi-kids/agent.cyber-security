## 摘要

NIS Cooperation Group consultation on coordinated implementation roadmap for post-quantum cryptography transition, closing September 29.

## 事實內容

A survey is underway until September 29 (18:00 CEST) to collect suggestions, comments, and contributions on next steps for defining and implementing the coordinated implementation roadmap for transition to post-quantum cryptography (PQC). The survey was launched under the work of the NIS Cooperation Group. The initiative aims to coordinate EU-wide approaches to migration from classical to quantum-resistant cryptographic algorithms, ensuring organizations are prepared for future quantum computing threats to current encryption standards.

Original language: Portuguese (CNCS Portugal)

## 受影響系統

- Cryptographic infrastructure across EU member states
- Organizations relying on public-key cryptography
- National cybersecurity agencies and critical infrastructure operators
