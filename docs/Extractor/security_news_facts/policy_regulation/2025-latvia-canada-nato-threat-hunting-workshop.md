## 摘要

33 cybersecurity professionals from 11 NATO/EU nations attended a 4-day threat hunting workshop in Riga, strengthening proactive defense capabilities.

## 事實內容

In early November 2025, Canadian and Latvian cyber experts conducted a four-day Threat Hunting Workshop in Riga, Latvia:

**Participation:**
- 33 cybersecurity professionals from 11 NATO and EU member states
- Military and civilian organizations represented
- Led by Canadian Armed Forces Cyber Command and Latvian institutions

**Training Content:**
- Expanded focus on Linux environments
- Real-world threat detection techniques
- Updated Threat Hunt Playbook with new workflows, artifacts, and automation
- Hands-on labs combining lectures with interactive exercises

**Objectives:**
- Help NATO allies become proactive defenders
- Enable threat identification before incidents occur
- Foster intelligence sharing across NATO partners

**Strategic Significance:**
CERT.LV's Threat Hunting Team Lead emphasized: "By sharing timely and relevant information, NATO partners can detect, understand, and counter emerging threats more effectively than any nation could on its own."

The workshop strengthened NATO's collective cyber resilience through multinational interoperability and shared defense protocols.

## 受影響系統

- NATO cybersecurity infrastructure
- Linux environments (training focus)
- Threat hunting capabilities across 11 nations
