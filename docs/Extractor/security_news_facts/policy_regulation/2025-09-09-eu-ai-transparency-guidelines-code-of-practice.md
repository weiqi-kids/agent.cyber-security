## 摘要

EU public consultation until October 2 on guidelines and code of practice for transparent AI systems to inform users when interacting with AI.

## 事實內容

A public consultation running until October 2 is seeking contributions for the development of Guidelines and a Code of Practice on transparent Artificial Intelligence systems. The initiative aims to ensure that users are informed when they are interacting with an AI system. The consultation is part of the EU AI Act implementation framework, addressing transparency obligations for AI systems deployed in various contexts. Organizations and individuals are invited to submit written contributions.

Original language: Portuguese (CNCS Portugal)

## 受影響系統

- AI system developers and deployers in the EU
- Organizations subject to EU AI Act transparency requirements
- Digital service providers using AI-driven user interactions
