## Summary

CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory publishes first sectoral report focusing on social aspects of cybersecurity, analyzing citizen attitudes and behaviors.

## Factual Content

This document focuses on social aspects of cybersecurity - how citizens in general think and act in this domain. It uses various recognized sources such as Eurobarometer, Eurostat, National Statistics Institute, and Directorate-General for Education and Science Statistics. Prioritizing the most recent data and those allowing temporal readings, this Report aims to inform the community about this component to contribute to building effective education and awareness strategies and conscious public policies. This is the first Sectoral Report of several to be launched by the Cybersecurity Observatory, covering six defined Observation Lines (Risks & Conflicts, Economy, Society, Ethics & Law, Public Policies, and Innovation & Future Technologies).
