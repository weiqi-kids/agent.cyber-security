## 摘要

Portugal's Council of Ministers approved a legislative authorization proposal establishing a new Cybersecurity Legal Framework, transposing EU NIS2 Directive (2022/2555).

## 事實內容

On 6 February 2025, the Portuguese Council of Ministers approved the legislative authorization proposal ("proposta de Lei de autorização legislativa") establishing a new Cybersecurity Legal Framework ("Regime Jurídico da Ciberseguran&ccedil;a"). This law transposes Directive (EU) 2022/2555 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 14 December 2022, commonly known as the NIS2 Directive. The approval marks a significant step in Portugal's formal alignment with EU cybersecurity obligations for essential and important entities.

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese government entities
- Essential and important service operators in Portugal
- Organizations subject to NIS2 requirements in Portugal
