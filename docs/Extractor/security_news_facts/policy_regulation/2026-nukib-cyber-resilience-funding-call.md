## 摘要
NUKIB announces fourth funding call for cyber resilience product capacity building, accepting applications February 2 - April 2, 2026.

## 事實內容
The National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NÚKIB) of the Czech Republic, acting as the National Coordinating Centre for Research and Development in Cybersecurity, has announced its 4th Call for project submissions.

**Funding Call Details:**
- **Program Name:** "Cyber Resilience Boost: Support for Building Capacity for Cyber Resilience of Products II"
- **Application Period:** February 2, 2026 - April 2, 2026 (until 7:00 PM)
- **Call Number:** 4th in the series
- **Focus:** Building capacity for cybersecurity resilience of products

**Purpose:**
This funding initiative aims to support research and development activities that enhance the cybersecurity resilience of products, aligning with broader EU cybersecurity objectives and the Cyber Resilience Act.

**Target Recipients:**
- Research institutions
- Development organizations
- Product manufacturers
- Cybersecurity technology companies

This represents continued Czech government investment in strengthening the cybersecurity ecosystem and product security capabilities.
