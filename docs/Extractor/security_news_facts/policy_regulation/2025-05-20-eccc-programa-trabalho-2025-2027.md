## 事實內容

The European Cybersecurity Competence Centre (ECCC) approved its Work Programme for the period 2025-2027 under the Digital Europe Programme, providing a total investment of €390 million in strategic cybersecurity projects. The action plan aims to strengthen European capabilities in key cybersecurity areas, with emphasis on developing emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence and post-quantum cryptography. The programme also highlights the implementation of key European legislation, including the Cyber Solidarity Regulation, the NIS 2 Directive, and the Cyber Resilience Act. The initiative supports both public and private sectors, promoting cooperation between Member States and the development of a genuinely European cybersecurity ecosystem.

- Digital infrastructure across the EU
