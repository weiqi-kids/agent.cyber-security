## 摘要

Czech NUKIB published National CVD Policy to increase ICT product security and protect vulnerability researchers from legal consequences.

## 事實內容

The Czech National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NÚKIB) published the National Policy for Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure (CVD). The policy aims not only to increase the security of information and communication technology (ICT) products but also to protect vulnerability discoverers from negative legal impacts. The policy includes the establishment of a "CVD coordinator," which according to the new Cybersecurity Act is the government CERT. This framework establishes clear procedures for responsible disclosure of security vulnerabilities.

## 受影響系統

- Czech ICT vendors and organizations
- Security researchers in Czech Republic
