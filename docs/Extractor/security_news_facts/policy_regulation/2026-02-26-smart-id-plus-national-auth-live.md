## 摘要

Estonia's RIA launched Smart-ID+ in the national authentication service today, introducing a new and more secure login flow for national e-services to reduce fraud risks.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) launched the Smart-ID+ solution in the national authentication service (riigi autentimisteenus) on February 26, 2026. The new solution introduces a new, more secure login flow for national e-services. Key improvements:
- Reduces fraud risks (pettuseriskid)
- Ties login more clearly to the user's own actions
- Introduces a new authentication flow for all national e-services

Smart-ID+ is a security upgrade to the existing Smart-ID authentication system used across Estonian government e-services. This represents a significant policy change in Estonia's national digital identity infrastructure.

## 受影響系統

- National authentication service (riigi autentimisteenus)
- Smart-ID authentication
- All Estonian national e-services
