## Summary
Estonia launches €1.5M funding program starting January 12, 2026, supporting cybersecurity companies to develop innovative solutions in collaboration with researchers.

## Description
The Estonian government announced a new funding initiative to support cybersecurity companies in developing innovative, internationally competitive solutions through collaboration with researchers. The program, launched jointly by the Enterprise and Innovation Foundation and the Estonian Information System Authority (RIA), allocates €1.5 million to the IT sector. Applications will be accepted starting January 12, 2026.

This initiative aims to strengthen Estonia's cybersecurity industry by bridging the gap between academic research and commercial development, fostering innovation in the cybersecurity domain.

## Technical Details
- **Funding Amount**: €1.5 million
- **Launch Date**: January 12, 2026
- **Administering Bodies**: Enterprise and Innovation Foundation, Estonian Information System Authority (RIA)
- **Target**: Cybersecurity companies collaborating with researchers
- **Objective**: Develop internationally competitive cybersecurity solutions
