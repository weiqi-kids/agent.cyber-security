## 摘要

The second phase of Portugal's #LerAntesClicarDepois (Read Before You Click) safe internet campaign was launched in Porto on 30 October, integrated into the Digital Skills Forum promoted by INCoDe.2030.

## 事實內容

On 30 October, in Porto, the second phase of the #LerAntesClicarDepois ("Read Before Clicking") campaign was launched as part of the Digital Skills Forum, promoted by INCoDe.2030. The CNCS Department of Development and Innovation coordinator, Isabel Baptista, reinforced the importance of digital literacy and critical thinking before engaging with online content. The campaign targets Portuguese internet users, promoting awareness of phishing and social engineering threats. This initiative aligns with European Digital Skills Week and national cybersecurity awareness objectives.
