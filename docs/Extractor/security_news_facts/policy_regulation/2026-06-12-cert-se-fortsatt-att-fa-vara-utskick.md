## 摘要

CERT-SE joins Sweden's National Cybersecurity Centre on 1 July 2026; subscribers must re-consent to continue receiving communications.

## 事實內容

CERT-SE published a standalone notice informing subscribers that on 1 July 2026 it will formally become part of Nationellt cybersäkerhetscenter (NCSC), aligning with the Swedish government's effort to consolidate cyber operations. Due to this transition, fresh consent for personal data processing is required. Subscribers are directed to cert.se for detailed instructions; consent must be given from 1 July 2026. Further context is available at the linked announcement about the consolidation of cyber activities under NCSC.
