# Regulation M72B affects e-services implementation requirements

## Summary
The Finnish Transport and Communications Agency's Regulation M72B on strong electronic identification and trust services enters fully into force in summer 2023. The reformed regulation includes requirements affecting electronic services implementation.

## Key Details
- **Regulation**: M72B
- **Topic**: Strong electronic identification and trust services
- **Effective**: Summer 2023
- **Impact**: E-services implementation requirements
- **Authority**: Finnish Transport and Communications Agency

## Context
This regulation sets technical and operational standards for organizations providing electronic services, ensuring consistent security baselines across Finland's digital service landscape.

- Technical implementation requirements
- Affects e-service providers
