## 摘要

NUKIB released a new methodology document on cybersecurity requirements for ICT public procurement, balancing security with fair competition principles.

## 事實內容

NUKIB (Národní úřad pro kybernetickou a informační bezpečnost) published a new methodological document focused on public procurement in the field of information and communication technologies (ICT). The document explains how to set cybersecurity requirements in a way that aligns with the principles of fair market competition. The methodology aims to guide contracting authorities on integrating cybersecurity requirements into ICT tenders without creating unjustified barriers to competition.

## 受影響系統

- Czech public sector ICT procurement processes
- Government contracting authorities
