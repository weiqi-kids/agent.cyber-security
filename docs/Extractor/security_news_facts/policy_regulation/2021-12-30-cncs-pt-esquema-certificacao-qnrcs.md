## 摘要

Portugal's CNCS published a draft Certification Scheme for the National Cybersecurity Reference Framework (EC QNRCS), open for public comment until 14 January 2022, covering identification, protection, detection, response, and recovery measures.

## 事實內容

CNCS published the draft Certification Scheme for the National Cybersecurity Reference Framework (EC QNRCS — Esquema de Certificação para o Quadro Nacional de Referência para a Cibersegurança). The scheme aims to certify that candidate organizations have implemented the measures established in the QNRCS framework for:
- Identification, protection, detection, response, and recovery against information security threats
- Security of networks and information systems

A companion set of documents was published for public consultation. Interested parties could submit contributions by email to drsc@cncs.gov.pt, preferably by 14 January 2022. The consultation process results in a decision and reasoned report, which will be published on the CNCS website.

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese organizations seeking QNRCS certification
- Public administration, essential services operators, digital service providers
