## 摘要

CNCS approves draft regulation (Instrução Técnica) implementing Decreto-Lei 65/2021, covering requirements for permanent contact points, security officers, asset inventories, annual reports, and incident notifications for covered entities.

## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) approved the draft Regulation that configures the Technical Instruction (Instrução Técnica) related to Decreto-Lei n.º 65/2021. The technical instruction covers the following requirements for entities subject to the decree-law:

1. **Permanent contact points** (pontos de contacto permanente): Communication requirements for designated permanent contacts
2. **Security officer** (responsável de segurança): Information requirements for designated security officers
3. **Asset inventory** (inventário de ativos): Requirements for maintaining an inventory of network and information system assets
4. **Annual report** (relatório anual): Annual reporting obligations
5. **Incident notification** (notificação de incidentes): Procedures and requirements for reporting cybersecurity incidents

This draft regulation implements the complementary technical specifications required by Decreto-Lei 65/2021, which itself implements Portugal's Legal Regime of Cyberspace Security (Lei 46/2018) transposing the EU NIS Directive.

## 受影響系統

- Entities covered by Decreto-Lei 65/2021 (public administration, critical infrastructure operators, essential services operators in Portugal)
