## 摘要
CNCS published a draft Certification Scheme for the National Cybersecurity Reference Framework (EC QNRCS), open for public consultation until January 14, 2022. The scheme will certify organizations' implementation of QNRCS measures for identifying, protecting, detecting, responding to, and recovering from cybersecurity threats.

## 事實內容
The CNCS is developing a Certification Scheme for the National Cybersecurity Reference Framework (EC QNRCS — Esquema de Certificação para o Quadro Nacional de Referência para a Cibersegurança).

Key details:
- **Purpose**: Certify that organizations have implemented the measures established in the QNRCS for identification, protection, detection, response, and recovery from information security threats
- **Public consultation deadline**: January 14, 2022
- **Submission method**: Contributions via email to drsc@cncs.gov.pt
- **Available documents**: Draft EC QNRCS and associated documentation published for public review
- **Process**: After consultation, CNCS will review contributions and publish final decision report

This scheme is part of Portugal's effort to create a national certification ecosystem that attests organizational cybersecurity maturity.

## 受影響系統
- Organizations seeking national cybersecurity certification
- Critical infrastructure and essential service operators
- Public administration
