# Regulation makes smart devices more secure

**Source:** NCSC-FI Security Now!  
**Link:** https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/regulation-makes-smart-devices-more-secure  
**Published:** 2023-01-31

## Summary

From 1 August 2024, devices not meeting information security requirements can be withdrawn from market. Manufacturers, importers and vendors must ensure products are sufficiently secure.
