## 摘要

ECCC opens €145.5 million in funding under Digital Europe and Horizon Europe Programmes.

## 事實內容

European Cybersecurity Competence Centre (ECCC) funding announcement:
- New calls for proposals under Digital Europe (DEP) and Horizon Europe (HE) Programmes
- Combined budget of up to EUR 145.5 million
- Represents significant funding opportunity for European cybersecurity initiatives

This is a major policy initiative to strengthen European cybersecurity capabilities.

## 受影響系統

- European cybersecurity projects eligible for DEP and HE funding
- Organizations applying for ECCC funding
