## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security published a comprehensive collection of guidance documents covering incident response and business continuity planning:

1. What to do when your organization has been compromised by a cyber attack (ITSAP.00.009)
2. Developing your incident response plan (ITSAP.40.003)
3. Developing your business continuity plan (ITSAP.10.005)
4. Developing your IT recovery plan (ITSAP.40.004)
5. Improving cyber security resilience through emergency preparedness planning (ITSM.10.014)

These documents provide practical guidance for Canadian organizations on preparing for, responding to, and recovering from cyber incidents. The guidance emphasizes the importance of proactive planning, regular testing, and integration of cyber incident response with broader business continuity and emergency management frameworks.

Key recommendations include establishing clear incident response procedures, defining roles and responsibilities, maintaining communication plans, implementing backup and recovery capabilities, and conducting regular exercises to test response plans.
