## 摘要

Portugal's new Cybersecurity Legal Framework (Decreto-Lei n.º 125/2025) enters into force; CNCS releases non-binding MyCiber platform simulator for entities to assess their compliance scope.

## 事實內容

Portugal's new Cybersecurity Legal Framework (Decreto-Lei n.º 125/2025 of 4 December 2025) has entered into force. The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS), acting as the National Cybersecurity Authority, has made available a non-binding Simulator to help entities assess whether they fall within the scope of the framework.

The Simulator is integrated into the MyCiber electronic platform referenced in Article 8(1) of Decreto-Lei n.º 125/2025. Full platform functionalities are to be made available gradually, as the necessary regulations under the decree are approved and published.

Key regulatory timeline: the regulation governing the electronic platform's operation is to be approved and published following the ongoing public consultation on the draft Regulation of the Cybersecurity Legal Framework (open until 22 April). This regulation is covered under Article 8(7) of the decree.

The MyCiber platform will enable essential, important, and relevant public entities to fulfill their registration obligations, designate a Cybersecurity Officer, and appoint a Permanent Contact Point.

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese essential entities, important entities, and relevant public entities under Decreto-Lei n.º 125/2025
- MyCiber electronic platform
