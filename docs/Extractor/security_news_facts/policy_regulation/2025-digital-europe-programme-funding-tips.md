## 摘要

NCSC-FI shares lessons learned from four years of Digital Europe Programme, providing seven tips to improve funding proposal quality based on recurring deficiencies identified by reviewers as competition intensifies.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre Finland (NCSC-FI) published guidance for organizations seeking Digital Europe Programme funding, based on experience from four years of the programme.

**Programme Maturity:**
- Digital Europe Programme: Four years old
- Trend: Increasing competition as applicants gain experience
- Challenge: Funding calls becoming more competitive
- Need: Higher quality proposals to succeed

**Guidance Development:**
- Source: Analysis of earlier call rounds
- Input: Reviewer feedback on common deficiencies
- Objective: Help applicants avoid recurring mistakes
- Benefit: Proposals that address common issues score higher

**Content:**
- Format: Seven tips and tricks
- Focus: Improving proposal quality
- Target: Organizations applying for Digital Europe funding
- Advantage: Following tips puts applicants "well ahead of others"

**Recurring Deficiencies Identified:**
- Reviewers have identified certain recurring problems
- These deficiencies lower awarded points
- Patterns observed across multiple application rounds
- Addressable through proper preparation

**Significance:**
- Practical guidance from national cybersecurity authority
- Based on real reviewer feedback
- Helps Finnish organizations compete for EU funding
- Increases success rate of funding applications
- Supports European cybersecurity capacity building

**Target Audience:**
- Organizations planning to apply for Digital Europe funding
- Cybersecurity project developers
- Research institutions
- Public and private sector entities
