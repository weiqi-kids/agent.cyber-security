# European Cybersecurity Competence Centre's National Coordination Centre operational in Finland

**Source:** NCSC-FI Security Now!  
**Link:** https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/research-and-development-cyber-security-reinforced-finland-and-europe-european-cybersecurity  
**Published:** 2023-02-03

## Summary

National Coordination Centre began operations officially in early 2023 under Finnish Transport and Communications Agency. Part of EU Network of National Coordination Centres. Purpose is to improve national cybersecurity capacities, support research, and accelerate technological development.
