## 摘要

EU's Digital Europe Programme announces €50 million funding for cybersecurity projects in 2025.

## 事實內容

The EU's Digital Europe Programme continues with a second call for cybersecurity projects in 2025:
- Total of €50 million available for funding under this call
- Part of the ongoing Digital Europe Programme
- Second funding call of 2025 for cybersecurity initiatives

This represents a significant policy initiative to strengthen cybersecurity capabilities across the EU.
