## 摘要

NUKIB co-authored a new guide for ethical and responsible use of AI in Czech public administration, along with a practical ten-point checklist.

## 事實內容

NUKIB (Národní úřad pro kybernetickou a informační bezpečnost) participated in developing a new "Guide for Ethical and Responsible Use of AI in Public Administration" (Průvodce pro etické a odpovědné využívání AI ve veřejné správě), along with a practical ten-point checklist (Desatero). The guide was created in response to increasingly intensive use of artificial intelligence in the day-to-day work of government agencies. Its purpose is to ensure that AI deployment is safe, responsible, and beneficial. An inter-ministerial group of experts prepared the guide, with NUKIB's involvement. The preparation was guaranteed by the Section for Civil Service of the Office of the Government of the Czech Republic.

## 受影響系統

- Czech public administration agencies
- AI systems deployed in government
