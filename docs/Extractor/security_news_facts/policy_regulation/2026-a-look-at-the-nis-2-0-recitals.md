## 事實內容

The EU commission dropped a large cyber security package on December 16th 2020, including a first draft for a new version of the NIS Directive . In front of the actual normative legal text, there are 84 recitals , describing the intents of the regulation. I’ve now read through them and this blogpost is my first reaction (not the official position of CERT.at) to that proposal. I haven't really looked at the normative text yet, feedback to that will come in another blogpost. I’ll quote some bits from the recitals of interest and comment on them.
