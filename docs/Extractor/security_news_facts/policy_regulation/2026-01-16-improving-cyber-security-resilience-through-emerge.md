## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security management guidance ITSM.10.014 on integrating cyber security into emergency preparedness planning to improve organizational resilience.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security released management guidance document ITSM.10.014 on improving cyber security resilience through emergency preparedness planning. This UNCLASSIFIED publication is intended for organizational management and covers how to integrate cyber security considerations into emergency preparedness frameworks. The document addresses the interplay between cyber incidents and physical emergencies, noting that cyber attacks can trigger or amplify physical emergencies and vice versa. Key topics include developing cyber-aware emergency response plans, establishing communication protocols during cyber-physical incidents, coordination with government agencies, and building organizational resilience. The guidance aligns with national emergency management frameworks and is relevant for critical infrastructure operators, government departments, and large organizations. Released alongside other business continuity and incident response guidance.

## 受影響系統

- Critical infrastructure operations
- Government departments and agencies
- Business continuity and disaster recovery processes
