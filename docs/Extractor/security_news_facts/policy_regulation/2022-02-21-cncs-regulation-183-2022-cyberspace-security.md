## 摘要
Regulation No. 183/2022 of February 21 was published in Portugal's Diário da República, establishing the Technical Instruction for communications related to permanent contact points, security officers, asset inventories, annual reports, and incident notifications under Decree-Law No. 65/2021.

## 事實內容
The Portuguese Diário da República (2nd series) published Regulation No. 183/2022 of February 21, which configures the Technical Instruction for:

- Communications regarding **permanent contact points**
- **Security officer** designations
- **Asset inventories**
- **Annual reports**
- **Incident notification obligations** to the National Cybersecurity Authority

This regulation implements complementary legislation to Decree-Law No. 65/2021 of July 30 (Legal Framework for Cyberspace Security).

**Background**: The regulation was developed following a public consultation process. The final decision and detailed reasoning report are available on the CNCS website.

**Applicability**: Organizations subject to the Portuguese Cyberspace Security Legal Framework, including public administration, critical infrastructure operators, essential service operators, and digital service providers.

## 受影響系統
- Portuguese public administration
- Critical infrastructure and essential service operators
- Digital service providers
