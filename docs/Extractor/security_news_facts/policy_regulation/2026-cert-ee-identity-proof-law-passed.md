## Summary
Estonian Parliament approves legislation with 75 votes allowing citizens to use national mobile app for identity verification, effective July 2025.

## Facts Content
**Approval Date**: June 4, 2025
**Legislative Body**: Riigikogu (Estonian Parliament)
**Vote Result**: 75 votes in favor

**Legislation Details**:
- Enables use of Estonia state app for identity verification
- Citizens can prove identity via mobile application
- Legal framework for digital identity verification

**Implementation Timeline**:
- Law becomes effective: July 2025
- Solution becomes usable: July 2025

**Significance**:
- Legal foundation for mobile identity verification
- Eliminates need for physical document presentation in certain scenarios
- Modernizes identity verification legal framework

**Notes**:
- Original language: Estonian
- Follows development of Eesti äpp (Estonia App) identity features
- Part of broader e-governance legal framework
- Demonstrates legislative support for digital transformation
