## 摘要

European Commission opens consultation on implementing regulation defining cybersecurity risk management technical requirements under NIS2 Directive.

## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) announced a public consultation opened by the European Commission (open until July 25, 2024) on a Draft Implementing Regulation that establishes the technical and methodological requirements for cybersecurity risk management measures. The regulation also specifies the conditions under which an incident must be considered significant, as provided in Articles 21 and 23 of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2 Directive) of the European Parliament. This implementing regulation is critical for harmonizing cybersecurity incident reporting thresholds and risk management standards across EU member states. Organizations subject to NIS2 obligations should review and respond to this consultation.

Original language: Portuguese (CNCS)

## 受影響系統

- All organizations subject to the NIS2 Directive (EU 2022/2555)
- Critical infrastructure operators across EU member states
