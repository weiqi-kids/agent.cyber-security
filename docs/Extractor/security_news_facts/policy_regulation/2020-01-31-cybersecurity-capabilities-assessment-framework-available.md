## Summary

CNCS publishes assessment framework defining three capability levels (Initial, Intermediate, Advanced) for cybersecurity measures aligned with QNRCS.

## Factual Content

The document presents three capability levels (Initial, Intermediate, and Advanced) for each cybersecurity measure inscribed in the National Cybersecurity Reference Framework (QNRCS), enabling organizations to fulfill five Cybersecurity objectives - identify, protect, detect, respond, and recover - considering their context and size. Capability levels applicable to each organization depend on their specific characteristics, and the framework is flexible enough for application in different contexts. For example, for an organization with only 5 employees, it may not be necessary to define a security policy fully aligned with market standards or even have a formal documented hiring procedure.
