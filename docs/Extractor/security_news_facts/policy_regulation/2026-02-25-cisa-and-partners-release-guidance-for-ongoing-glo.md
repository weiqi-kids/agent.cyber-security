## 摘要

CISA and international partners (NSA, ASD/ACSC, CCCS, NCSC-NZ, NCSC-UK) release joint guidance and Emergency Directive ED 26-03 on ongoing global exploitation of Cisco SD-WAN systems via CVE-2026-20127 and CVE-2022-20775.

## 事實內容

- CISA and partner agencies released a joint advisory and Emergency Directive (ED) 26-03 addressing ongoing exploitation of Cisco SD-WAN systems.
- Malicious cyber actors observed targeting Cisco SD-WAN systems of organizations globally.
- Actors exploiting **CVE-2026-20127** (Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Controller and Manager Authentication Bypass Vulnerability) for initial access.
- Post-exploitation: privilege escalation using **CVE-2022-20775** (Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Path Traversal Vulnerability) and establishing long-term persistence.
- Both CVEs added to CISA KEV Catalog on February 25, 2026.
- FCEB agencies required under ED 26-03 to: (1) inventory all Cisco SD-WAN systems, (2) collect artifacts for threat hunting, (3) fully patch available updates, (4) hunt for evidence of compromise.
- Joint authoring organizations: CISA, NSA, ASD's ACSC (Australia), Canadian Centre for Cyber Security, NCSC-NZ (New Zealand), NCSC-UK (United Kingdom).
- Organizations urged to review Cisco's security advisories and apply SD-WAN Hardening Guidance.

## 受影響系統

- Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Controller — CVE-2026-20127 (Authentication Bypass), CVE-2022-20775 (Path Traversal)
- Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Manager — CVE-2026-20127, CVE-2022-20775
