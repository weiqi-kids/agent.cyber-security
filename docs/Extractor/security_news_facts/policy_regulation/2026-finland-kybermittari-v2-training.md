# New Kybermittari version available with training events starting October 2022

## Summary
NCSC-FI published Kybermittari (Cybermeter) tool in 2020 for evaluating and improving organizational cybersecurity. Further developed version now available with public training events starting October 10.

## Key Details
- **Tool**: Kybermittari (Cybermeter)
- **Original**: 2020
- **Update**: Further developed version
- **Purpose**: Evaluate/improve organizational cybersecurity
- **Training**: Events starting October 10, 2022
- **Availability**: Free on NCSC-FI website

## Notes
- Tool maturity (iterative development)
- Training support
- Confidence: high
