## 摘要

EU's Digital Europe Programme announces second 2025 funding call with €50 million for cybersecurity projects.

## 事實內容

The European Union's Digital Europe Programme has launched its second cybersecurity funding call for 2025:
- **Total funding available**: €50 million
- **Programme**: Digital Europe Programme
- **Focus area**: Cybersecurity projects
- **Call type**: Second call for 2025

This represents continued EU investment in strengthening cybersecurity capabilities across member states and supporting innovative cybersecurity initiatives.

- Organizations applying for Digital Europe Programme funding
- Cybersecurity innovation initiatives
