## 摘要

Portugal's CNCS launched the National Roadshow 2022 — 76 awareness sessions nationwide explaining the Legal Framework for Cyberspace Security (Decreto-Lei n.º 65/2021) to public administration, critical infrastructure operators, essential service operators, and digital service providers.

## 事實內容

The National Roadshow 2022 commenced at CNCS premises with strong attendance. The roadshow consists of 76 four-hour awareness sessions held in-person across all district capitals and islands throughout Portugal.

**Target audience**:
- Public administration
- Critical infrastructure operators
- Essential service operators
- Digital service providers

**Legal basis**: Decreto-Lei n.º 65/2021, of 30 July — the Legal Framework for Cyberspace Security

**Topics covered**:
- Security requirements for networks and information systems
- Rules for incident notification
- Obligations of entities covered under the legal framework

The sessions aim to build organizational capacity to understand and comply with the applicable legal obligations.

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese public administration entities
- Critical infrastructure operators
- Essential service operators and digital service providers
