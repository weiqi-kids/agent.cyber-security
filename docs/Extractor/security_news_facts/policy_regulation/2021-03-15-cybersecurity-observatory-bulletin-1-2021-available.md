## Summary

Bulletin analyzes EU Cybersecurity Strategy, including NIS Directive revision and critical infrastructure directive expansion.

## Factual Content

This edition identifies the main measures of the EU Cybersecurity Strategy, such as the revision of the Network and Information Security (NIS) Directive and the expansion of the critical infrastructure directive, and proposes a perspective on the context of this initiative. Additionally, it discloses a set of publications considered of interest.

## Affected Systems

- EU member states implementing NIS Directive
- Critical infrastructure operators
