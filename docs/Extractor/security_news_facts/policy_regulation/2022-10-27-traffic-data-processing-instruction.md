# Instruction on recording traffic data processing information

**Source:** NCSC-FI Security Now!  
**Link:** https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/instruction-recording-information-traffic-data-processing-enters-force-27-october-2022  
**Published:** 2022-10-27

## Summary

Instruction applies to recording and storing event information related to traffic data processing under Section 145 of Act on Electronic Communications Services. Applies to all communications providers. Replaces 2004 recommendation.
