## 摘要

Traficom awards 2024 Information Security Trailblazer for scam call/message prevention cooperation, nearly stopping spoofed Finnish number scams.

## 事實內容

Award recognition details:
- 2024 Information Security Trailblazer award given to parties planning and implementing scam prevention measures
- Result: scam calls from spoofed Finnish phone numbers have all but stopped
- Finnish know-how and pioneering measures garnering international interest
- Addressing global scam calls and messages problem

Successful public-private cooperation initiative with measurable impact.
