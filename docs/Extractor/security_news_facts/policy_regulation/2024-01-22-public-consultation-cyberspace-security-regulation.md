## 摘要

CNCS published a draft regulation on implementing the Legal Framework for Cyberspace Security (RJSC) in Portuguese public administration entities, open for public consultation.

## 事實內容

The draft Regulation on implementing the Legal Framework for Cyberspace Security (Regime Jurídico da Segurança do Ciberespaço, RJSC) in public administration entities was made available for public consultation by CNCS. The regulation defines cybersecurity requirements, risk management obligations, and incident reporting procedures applicable to Portuguese public sector organizations. The public consultation process allows stakeholders, organizations, and citizens to submit feedback on the proposed regulatory framework. This represents a step toward operationalizing Portugal's national cybersecurity legal framework and aligning with NIS2 transposition requirements.

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese public administration entities
