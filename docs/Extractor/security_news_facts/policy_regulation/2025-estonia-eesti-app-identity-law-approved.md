## Summary
Estonian Parliament approved law on June 4, 2025 (75 votes) enabling people to use Eesti state app for identity verification. New solution becomes operational in July 2025.

## Description
On June 4, 2025, the Estonian Parliament (Riigikogu) approved with 75 votes a bill enabling people to use the Eesti state app for identity verification. The new solution will become operational in July 2025, providing an alternative digital identity verification method through the national mobile application.
