## 摘要

The Estonian Academy of Sciences' cybersecurity commission, in collaboration with election-organizing state agencies, assessed election technology risks and published findings as input for improving election security.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Academy of Sciences' cybersecurity commission, in collaboration with state agencies organizing elections, assessed risks related to Estonian election technology over the past year. The results were published today in the national code repository (e-riigi koodivara). This work serves as important input for further development of election security. The assessment covers key directions for securing election technology infrastructure.

- Commissioning body: Estonian Academy of Sciences cybersecurity commission
- Collaborators: State agencies organizing elections
- Output: Risk assessment published in national code repository
- Purpose: Input for improving election security
- Scope: Estonian election technology risk assessment

## 受影響系統

- Estonian election technology infrastructure
- E-voting and election management systems
