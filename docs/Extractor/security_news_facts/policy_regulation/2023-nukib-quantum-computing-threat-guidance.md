## 摘要
NUKIB releases three support materials explaining quantum computing threat to cryptography and necessary protective steps.

## 事實內容
The National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NÚKIB) of the Czech Republic has developed three support materials addressing the emerging threat quantum computers pose to current cryptographic standards.

**Quantum Computing Context:**
Quantum computers are experiencing significant development and advancement. However, with the evolution of their capabilities, the threat they represent to current cryptographic standards in cybersecurity is becoming increasingly concrete and substantial.

**Document Purpose:**
NÚKIB's three support materials aim to:
1. Explain the nature of the quantum computing threat to cryptography
2. Clarify necessary steps to prevent this threat
3. Outline actions required in coming years

**Threat Timeline:**
The materials emphasize that protective measures must be implemented in the coming years, indicating:
- Proactive rather than reactive approach needed
- Long-term planning required
- Migration to post-quantum cryptography necessary

**Cryptographic Impact:**
Quantum computers threaten:
- Current encryption algorithms
- Key exchange mechanisms
- Digital signature schemes
- Authentication protocols

**Strategic Importance:**
By preparing these materials, NÚKIB demonstrates foresight in addressing long-term cryptographic threats and helping organizations prepare for the post-quantum era.

**Target Audience:**
- Organizations subject to cybersecurity regulations
- Security architects and engineers
- Policy makers
- IT leadership planning long-term security strategies

This represents important guidance for long-term cryptographic resilience planning.
