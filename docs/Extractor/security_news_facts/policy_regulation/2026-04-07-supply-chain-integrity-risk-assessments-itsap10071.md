## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security publishes ITSAP.10.071 on supply chain integrity risk assessment evaluation criteria for ICT products deployed in Government of Canada infrastructure.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security released awareness series publication ITSAP.10.071 (April 2026) on supply chain integrity (SCI) risk assessment evaluation criteria.

Key points:
- Supply chain integrity (SCI) risk assessments are carried out on information and communication technology (ICT) products and services deployed in Government of Canada (GC) infrastructure as part of an SCI review.
- The review process supports safeguarding the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of GC communications and data.
- It fosters resilience against digital supply chain vulnerabilities and compromises.
- The guidance provides evaluation criteria that practitioners can use when assessing ICT vendors and products.
- The document covers: threat landscape for supply chain attacks, criteria for evaluating vendor trustworthiness, technical assessment methodologies, and risk mitigation recommendations.
- Part of the Cyber Centre's Awareness series aimed at government procurement and security officers.

## 受影響系統

- Government of Canada ICT procurement processes
- Information and communication technology products deployed in GC infrastructure
- Supply chain risk management programs
- Departments responsible for ICT acquisitions
