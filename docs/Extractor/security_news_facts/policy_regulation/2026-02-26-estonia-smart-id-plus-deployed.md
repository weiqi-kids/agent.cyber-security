## 摘要

Estonia's State Information System Authority (RIA) officially activated Smart-ID+ in the national authentication service on February 26, 2026. The new solution introduces a more secure login flow for government e-services, reducing fraud risks and tying login actions more clearly to individual user activity.

## 事實內容

- **Event**: RIA (Riigi Infosüsteemi Amet) deployed Smart-ID+ in the national government authentication service (riigi autentimisteenus) effective February 26, 2026.
- **Change**: A new, more secure login flow is introduced for government e-services (riigi e-teenused).
- **Security improvement**: The updated system reduces fraud risks (pettuseriskid) by more clearly linking the login action to the specific user's own activity.
- **Affected service**: All government e-services using the national authentication service.
- **Note**: This is the actual deployment announcement (confirmation of live activation), following the earlier pre-announcement on February 20, 2026 that deployment would occur on this date.

## 受影響系統

- Estonia national authentication service (riigi autentimisteenus)
- All Estonian government e-services using Smart-ID+ authentication
