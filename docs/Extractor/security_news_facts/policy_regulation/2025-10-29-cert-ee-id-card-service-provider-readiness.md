## 摘要

Estonia's RIA reminds service providers that new Thales ID cards will be issued from November 14, replacing IDEMIA; providers must ensure their IT systems are compatible to maintain uninterrupted service delivery.

## 事實內容

New ID cards will be adopted on November 14, 2025, when Thales replaces the previous manufacturer IDEMIA as the supplier of Estonian ID cards. The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) reminds service providers to prepare their IT systems in advance to ensure smooth service delivery. The transition requires technical changes on the part of service providers to ensure their authentication systems properly handle the new Thales-manufactured cards. Failure to prepare could result in service disruptions for users trying to authenticate with the new ID cards.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian ID card authentication infrastructure
- Service providers' IT systems
- DigiDoc4 and Web eID
