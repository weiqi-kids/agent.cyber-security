## 摘要

SK-CERT publishes data protection principles for KYBER2026 conference registration in accordance with GDPR and Slovak data protection law.

## 事實內容

The National Security Authority (NBÚ) has published privacy policy guidelines for the KYBER2026 conference registration process, ensuring compliance with European and Slovak data protection regulations.

**Regulatory Framework:**
- European Parliament Regulation No. 2016/679 (GDPR)
- Slovak Act No. 18/2018 on personal data protection

**Data Controller:**
The National Security Authority (NBÚ) of Slovakia serves as the data controller for personal information collected during conference registration.

**Key Principles:**
- Transparency in data collection
- Compliance with GDPR requirements
- Clear definition of data controller responsibilities
- Lawful basis for processing registration data

**Purpose:**
Ensure proper handling of personal data for event participants while maintaining compliance with data protection regulations.

## 受影響系統

- KYBER2026 conference registration system
- NBÚ data processing infrastructure
- Event management platforms
