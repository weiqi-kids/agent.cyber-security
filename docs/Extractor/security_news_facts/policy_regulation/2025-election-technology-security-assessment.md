## Summary
Estonian Academy of Sciences cybersecurity commission assesses election technology risks over past year with state agencies, publishing results in e-state code repository.

## Factual Content
The Estonian Academy of Sciences cybersecurity commission assessed risks related to Estonian election technology over the past year in cooperation with state agencies organizing elections, and published the results today in the e-state code repository. The work is important input for further development of election security.

**Assessment Details:**
- Assessor: Estonian Academy of Sciences cybersecurity commission
- Duration: Past year
- Collaboration: State election agencies
- Publication: E-state code repository

- Focus: Election technology risk assessment
