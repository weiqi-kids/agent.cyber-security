# €1.8M information security support granted to 77 Finnish companies

## Summary
Approximately EUR 1.8 million in information security development support has been granted to 77 companies across various sectors and sizes. 656 applications received, 86 processed so far, with EUR 6 million total funding available.

## Key Details
- **Funding Granted**: EUR 1.8 million (so far)
- **Recipients**: 77 companies (various sectors and sizes)
- **Applications**: 656 received, 86 processed
- **Total Budget**: EUR 6 million available
- **Focus**: Society-critical companies

## Context
This funding program supports cybersecurity capability development across Finland's critical infrastructure sectors.

## Notes
- National cybersecurity investment
- Broad sector coverage
