## 摘要

芬蘭漏洞揭露實務尚未普及，NCSC-FI 研究提出統一發布安全聯絡資訊（security.txt）的建議做法。

## 事實內容

A proposed new practice for coordinated vulnerability disclosure involves organisations publishing contact details and policies regarding vulnerabilities in a consistent location. This practice was studied in a thesis project carried out for the NCSC-FI. The research addresses how organisations can be notified about vulnerabilities in their online services and how to establish rules with vulnerability discoverers. The resulting article includes tips for publishing a security.txt file, which is not yet widely adopted in Finland.

## 受影響系統

- Organisational online services
- Vulnerability disclosure processes
