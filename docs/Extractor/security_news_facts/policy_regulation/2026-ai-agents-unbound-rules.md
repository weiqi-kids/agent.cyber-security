## 摘要

MIT CSAIL research reveals AI agents lack consensus on behavioral standards, with 30 agents analyzed showing opaque development practices and inadequate safety disclosures.

## 事實內容

MIT's Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (CSAIL) published its 2025 AI Agent Index analyzing 30 AI agents that can take action online through software services. The research highlights the absence of consensus or standards governing AI agent behavior.

**Types of AI Agents Analyzed:**
- Chat applications with tools: Manus AI, ChatGPT Agent, Claude Code
- Browser-based agents: Perplexity Comet, ChatGPT Atlas, ByteDance Agent TARS
- Enterprise workflow agents: Microsoft Copilot Studio, ServiceNow Agent

**Key Findings:**
- Key aspects of real-world development and deployment remain opaque
- Little information is made publicly available to researchers or policymakers
- Growing concerns about behavioral rules for AI agents

**Real-World Impacts:**
- AI community frustration with OpenClaw platform and Moltbook network
- Ongoing issues with AI-generated code submissions to open source projects
- Consequences of deploying agents without behavioral rules
