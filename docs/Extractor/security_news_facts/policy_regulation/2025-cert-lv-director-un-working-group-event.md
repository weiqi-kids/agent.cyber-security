## 摘要

Latvia, Dominican Republic, Indonesia, and UNIDIR co-organized UN event on Feb 18, 2025, in New York to strengthen national cybersecurity and incident response capacity.

## 事實內容

On February 18, 2025, a thematic event on cybersecurity and incident response capacity was held in New York:

**Co-organizers:**
- Latvia
- Dominican Republic
- Republic of Indonesia
- UNIDIR (United Nations Institute for Disarmament Research)

**Main Topics:**
- Strengthening national cybersecurity and incident response capacity
- Exchange of experiences and best practices among countries
- Development of effective national cybersecurity mechanisms
- International incident response preparedness
- Legal frameworks, operational structures, and resource allocation

**Key Outcomes:**

CERT.LV Director Baiba Kaškina emphasized: "Cybersecurity cooperation networks must be built on mutual trust, common goals" and collaborative effort.

Speakers highlighted the importance of participation in international organizations:
- FIRST (Forum of Incident Response and Security Teams)
- TF-CSIRT (Trusted Framework for Computer Security Incident Response Teams)
- APCERT (Asia Pacific Computer Emergency Response Team)

Ambassador Hari Prabowo reinforced "cross-regional collaboration in building resilient cybersecurity ecosystems."

**Strategic Significance:**
- UN Open-ended Working Group recognized as valuable platform for sharing best practices
- Necessity for continued international cooperation to address emerging cyber threats
- Focus on building trust and common goals across nations

## 受影響系統

- National cybersecurity frameworks
- International incident response cooperation
- UN cybersecurity governance
