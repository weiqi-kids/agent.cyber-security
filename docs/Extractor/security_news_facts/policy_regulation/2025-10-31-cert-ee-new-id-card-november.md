## 摘要

Estonia's Police and Border Guard Board will start issuing new design ID cards from November's second half; new cards from Thales (replacing IDEMIA) bring changes to appearance, chip, and software.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Police and Border Guard Board (PPA) will begin issuing new ID cards in the second half of November 2025. The changes affect both the card's appearance, chip, and its software. According to RIA, service providers who rely on ID card authentication must ensure their IT systems are ready for the transition from IDEMIA-manufactured cards to the new Thales-manufactured cards before November 14, 2025. This represents a significant transition in Estonian national identity infrastructure that requires coordination across all digital service providers.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian national ID card (physical card and software)
- Service providers using ID card authentication
- DigiDoc4 and related ID software
