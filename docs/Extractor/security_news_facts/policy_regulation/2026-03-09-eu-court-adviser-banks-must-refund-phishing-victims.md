## 摘要
Advocate General Athanasios Rantos of the EU Court of Justice (CJEU) issued a formal opinion stating that EU law requires banks to immediately refund account holders affected by unauthorized transactions, even when the customer was at fault, unless the bank has good reason to suspect fraud.

## 事實內容
- **發布者**: Advocate General Athanasios Rantos, Court of Justice of the European Union (CJEU)
- **類型**: Formal opinion (non-binding but highly influential on CJEU rulings)
- **核心主張**: EU law requires banks to immediately refund unauthorized transaction amounts as a first step
- **例外條件**: Banks may withhold refund only if they have "good reason to suspect fraud," which must be communicated in writing to the competent national authority
- **適用範圍**: All EU member states; applies to unauthorized transactions including those resulting from phishing attacks
- **影響**: If adopted by the CJEU, this interpretation would strengthen consumer protections and place the burden of proof on banks to demonstrate customer fraud rather than negligence
- **背景**: The opinion addresses the growing volume of phishing-related unauthorized transactions across EU banking systems

## 受影響系統
- EU banking and financial services sector
- Payment service providers operating in EU jurisdiction
