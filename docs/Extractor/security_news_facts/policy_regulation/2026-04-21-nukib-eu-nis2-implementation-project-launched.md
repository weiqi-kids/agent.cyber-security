## 摘要

On April 15, 2026, the EU-funded project "Implementation of the EU Cybersecurity Directive NIS2" was officially launched in the Czech Republic, with the participation of the Estonian Ambassador, NUKIB Director, European Commission representatives, and the e-Governance Academy.

## 事實內容

- The project "Implementation of EU Cybersecurity Directive NIS2" was officially launched on April 15, 2026.
- Attending the launch: Estonian Ambassador Jana Vanaveski, NUKIB Director Lukáš Kintr, European Commission representatives, and representatives of the e-Governance Academy (eGA).
- The project is funded by the European Union through the "Technical Support Instrument" (TSI) grant program.
- The TSI is administered by the Task Force for Reform and Investment (SG REFORM) under the European Commission.
- NUKIB (Národní úřad pro kybernetickou a informační bezpečnost) is the Czech national cybersecurity authority leading the implementation.
- Announcement published by NUKIB on 2026-04-21.
- Estonia's involvement reflects knowledge-sharing from one of the EU's most advanced digital governance countries.

## 受影響系統

- Czech organizations subject to NIS2 Directive requirements
