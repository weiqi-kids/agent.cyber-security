## 摘要

芬蘭交通通訊局 Regulation M72B 強力電子識別法規 2023 年夏季全面生效，影響電子服務實作。

## 事實內容

The Finnish Transport and Communications Agency's Regulation M72B on strong electronic identification and trust services enters fully into force in the summer of 2023. The reformed regulation includes new requirements that affect the implementation of electronic services, requiring service providers to comply with updated standards for strong authentication.

## 受影響系統

- Electronic identification services
- Trust services
- E-services requiring strong authentication
