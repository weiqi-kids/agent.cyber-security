## 摘要

The EU CyberNet cyber cooperation initiative led by Estonia's RIA entered its second phase in September, focusing on expanding cooperation to the Indo-Pacific region after six years of successful operation.

## 事實內容

After six years of successful operation, the EU CyberNet cyber cooperation initiative led by Estonia's Information System Authority (RIA) began its second phase in September. The new phase focuses on expanding cooperation primarily to the Indo-Pacific region. EU CyberNet has been a platform for international cybersecurity capacity building and cooperation led by Estonia.

- Initiative: EU CyberNet (Estonia-led cyber cooperation)
- Phase: Second phase commenced September 2025
- Focus: Expansion to Indo-Pacific region
- Previous: Six years of successful operation (first phase)
- Lead organization: RIA (Estonian Information System Authority)

## 受影響系統

- International cybersecurity cooperation framework
- Indo-Pacific region partner countries
