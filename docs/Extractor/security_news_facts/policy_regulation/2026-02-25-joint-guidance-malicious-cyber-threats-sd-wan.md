## 摘要

International cybersecurity agencies release joint guidance on malicious cyber threats targeting Software-Defined Wide Area Networks (SD-WAN), with recommendations for network operators.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security, in collaboration with international partners, released joint guidance on malicious cyber threats to SD-WAN networks. Malicious cyber threat actors are actively targeting Software-Defined Wide Area Network (SD-WAN) infrastructure used by enterprises, government agencies, and telecommunications providers.

Key points from the guidance:
- SD-WAN platforms are increasingly targeted due to their role as central network control points
- Threat actors exploit misconfigured management interfaces, unpatched vulnerabilities, and weak authentication mechanisms
- Common attack methods include exploitation of internet-exposed management consoles, man-in-the-middle interception, and lateral movement via compromised SD-WAN controllers
- Recommended mitigations include: restricting management interface access, enforcing MFA, applying vendor patches promptly, and monitoring for anomalous routing changes
- Network operators should implement zero-trust principles and segment SD-WAN management from production traffic

The guidance is aimed at network architects, administrators, and security teams managing SD-WAN deployments.

## 受影響系統

- SD-WAN platforms (all vendors)
- Enterprise wide-area network infrastructure
- Telecommunications providers using SD-WAN
- Government network operators
