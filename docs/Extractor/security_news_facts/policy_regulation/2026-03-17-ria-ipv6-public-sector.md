## 摘要

Estonia's RIA calls on the public sector to transition to IPv6, as Estonian digital government development enters a new phase; most public services still rely on IPv4.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) announced that Estonia's digital government development has entered a new stage, with public services to gradually migrate to IPv6 address space. Although the national network (riigivõrk) has been IPv6-ready for years and technical capability exists, the majority of public sector services still rely on IPv4 addresses. RIA is calling on public sector organisations to proactively adopt IPv6 as part of national digital infrastructure modernisation.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian public sector e-services
- National network (riigivõrk)
- IPv4/IPv6 infrastructure
