## 摘要

CERT.at's personal analysis of the NIS2 draft (November 2021), recommending the EU Council abandon rushing the text that year, publish a new consolidated draft, and seek further public comment due to inconsistencies in key provisions.

## 事實內容

This is a TLP:WHITE summary of a presentation at the 15th CSIRTs Network meeting in Ljubljana on November 11, 2021. Published as CERT.at's personal opinion, not the official Austrian government position.

**Overall recommendation**: The EU Council should not rush to finalise the NIS2 text in 2021. Too much is in flux and internal consistency has been lost through rapid iterations. A new consolidated draft with a fresh round of public comments is recommended.

**Key issues identified in the NIS2 draft:**

1. **"National CSIRT" definition confusion**: Historically, "national CSIRT" meant the default coordinating/advising CSIRT with no enforcing powers — the main liaison for international relations and the primary info-sharing hub. The NIS2 draft conflates this role with sector-specific or enforcement bodies, creating definitional ambiguity.

2. **Scanning provisions**: Text on network scanning by national authorities lacked clarity on scope, authority limits, and proportionality safeguards. (Additional text added in update on 2021-11-18.)

3. **Recitals vs normative text misalignment**: Earlier blog posts by the author noted tension between the explanatory recitals and the operative normative provisions of the draft.

4. **High-speed tinkering limit reached**: The pace of informal changes has made the text difficult to assess for coherence.
