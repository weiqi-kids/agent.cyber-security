## 事實內容

After looking at the recitals a few weeks ago, here is my feedback on the normative text of the NIS2 proposal: Art. 1(2)(c) [...] lays down obligations on cybersecurity information sharing. It doesn't just provide obligations, it also provides a legal basis for information sharing. From our perspective, that's even more important than the obligation. Art. 2(2)(a) the services are provided by one of the following entities: Overall, the NIS2 is moving from the service-orientation towards a focus on organisations. Here, "service" appears and it looks like a holdover from the old text.
