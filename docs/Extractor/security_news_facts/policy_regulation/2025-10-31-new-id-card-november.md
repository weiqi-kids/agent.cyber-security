## Summary
Police and Border Guard Board will start issuing new ID cards from second half of November with updates to appearance, chip, and software; technical transition may cause temporary service disruptions.

## Factual Content
The Police and Border Guard Board (PPA) will start issuing new ID cards from the second half of November. Updates concern the card's appearance, chip, and its software. According to RIA, the technical transition will take time, and therefore not all e-services related to using the new ID card may work fully immediately in the first days.

**Key Details:**
- Issuer: PPA
- Start date: Second half of November 2025
- Changes: Appearance, chip, software
- Warning: Possible temporary e-service disruptions during transition
- Transition period needed for full functionality
