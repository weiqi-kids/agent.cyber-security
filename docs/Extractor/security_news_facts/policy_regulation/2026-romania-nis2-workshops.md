## 摘要

Romania's DNSC launched national workshop series for NIS2 Directive implementation based on Emergency Ordinance 155/2024.

## 事實內容

Romania's Directorate for Cybersecurity (DNSC) announced a new national series of workshops dedicated to implementing the NIS2 Directive, based on Emergency Ordinance (O.U.G) nr. 155/2024. The workshops aim to support organizations in understanding and complying with the updated Network and Information Security directive requirements.

## 受影響系統

- Romanian organizations subject to NIS2 Directive
- Essential and important entities under O.U.G 155/2024
