## 摘要

Portuguese cybersecurity initiatives funded under the PRR recovery plan — C-Academy, C-Network, C-HUB, the Cybersecurity Observatory, and the Cybersecurity Alliance — are recognized in the European Commission's 3rd Annual Report on the State of the Digital Decade 2030.

## 事實內容

The European Commission's third Annual Report on the State of the Digital Decade 2030 recognized several Portuguese cybersecurity initiatives developed by CNCS (National Cybersecurity Centre). The recognized initiatives, funded under Portugal's PRR (Recovery and Resilience Plan), include C-Academy (cybersecurity training), C-Network (professional network), C-HUB (innovation hub), the Cybersecurity Observatory, and the Cybersecurity Alliance (cooperation platform). The report tracks progress towards the EU's Digital Decade 2030 goals and highlights Member States' contributions. Portugal's recognition in the report underscores the effectiveness of its national cybersecurity capacity-building programmes at the European level.

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese national cybersecurity capacity-building programmes
- EU Digital Decade 2030 goals
- National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PRR) funded projects
