## 摘要

NCSC-FI highlights Windows 10 end-of-support, EU Instant Payments Regulation, and digital security research findings.

## 事實內容

This weekly review covers significant policy and regulatory developments:
- **Windows 10 end of support** - Microsoft ending support for widely-used operating system
- **EU's new Instant Payments Regulation** - regulatory framework for real-time payment systems
- **Scam calls impersonating NCSC-FI** - warning about fraudulent calls claiming to be from the cyber security centre
- **Cyber Security Nordic fair** returning with InfoSec 2025 seminar organized by Traficom and National Emergency Supply Agency
- **Digital Security Barometer 2025** - findings published by Digital and Population Data Services Agency
- Malware review introducing Ranbyus

The Windows 10 end-of-support represents significant security challenge for organizations still using the platform.

## 受影響系統

- Windows 10 installations
- EU instant payment systems
- Organizations targeted by NCSC-FI impersonation scams
- Digital payment infrastructure
