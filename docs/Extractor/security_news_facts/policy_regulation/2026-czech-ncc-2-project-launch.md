## 摘要

Czech NUKIB launched 4-year NCC-CZ 2.0 project funded by Digital Europe Programme to strengthen national cybersecurity competencies.

## 事實內容

The Czech National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NÚKIB) launched a four-year project "National Coordination Centre 2.0 – The Czech Republic" (NCC-CZ 2.0) on January 1, 2026. The project (ID 101227749) is supported by the Digital Europe Programme and implemented in consortium collaboration with CyberSecurity Hub (CSH). The project aims to further strengthen the development of national competencies in cybersecurity and support the involvement of Czech entities in European initiatives.

## 受影響系統

- Czech cybersecurity ecosystem
- European Digital Europe Programme participants
