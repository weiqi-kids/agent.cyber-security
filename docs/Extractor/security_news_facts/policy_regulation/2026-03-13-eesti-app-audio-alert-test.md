## 摘要

Estonia's RIA, together with Rescue Board and Women's Home Defence, conducted the first-ever test of audio safety alert transmission via the Estonian app and "Ole valmis!" app on March 16.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) announced testing of audio alert (heliga ohuteavitus) transmission via the Estonian app (Eesti äpp) and the "Ole valmis!" mobile application on Monday, March 16, 2026, between 10:00–17:00. The test was conducted in cooperation with the Rescue Board (Päästeamet) and Women's Home Defence (Naiskodukaitse). The purpose was to verify newly developed technical functionality and to prepare for the March 18 nationwide public alert system (EE-ALARM) test. This represents the first time audio alerts have been tested through these mobile platforms.

## 受影響系統

- Eesti äpp (Estonian national mobile application)
- "Ole valmis!" mobile application
- EE-ALARM national alert system
