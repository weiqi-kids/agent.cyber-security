## 摘要

EU presents ICT Supply Chain Security Toolbox framework, building on Czech EU Presidency foundations.

## 事實內容

The European Union has presented the ICT Supply Chain Security Toolbox, a common non-binding framework for assessing and reducing cybersecurity risks in ICT supply chains. The foundation for this toolbox was laid during the Czech Presidency of the Council of the EU.

**Toolbox Objectives:**

**Risk Assessment Framework:**
- Identifies possible risk scenarios affecting ICT supply chains
- Provides coordinated recommendations for risk assessment and mitigation
- Addresses critical dependencies and vulnerabilities in supply chains

**Key Recommendations:**
- Support for multi-vendor strategies to reduce single points of failure
- Reducing dependencies on high-risk suppliers
- Implementing supply chain security best practices

**Regulatory Integration:**
The toolbox complements the implementation of Article 22 of the NIS2 Directive, helping EU member states unify their approaches to supply chain security management.

**Strategic Importance:**
This framework represents the EU's response to growing concerns about:
- Supply chain attacks (e.g., SolarWinds, Log4j)
- Geopolitical risks in technology dependencies
- Need for coordinated European approach to ICT security
- Critical infrastructure protection

The toolbox provides a structured approach for both public and private sector organizations to assess and manage ICT supply chain risks while maintaining operational flexibility through its non-binding nature.
