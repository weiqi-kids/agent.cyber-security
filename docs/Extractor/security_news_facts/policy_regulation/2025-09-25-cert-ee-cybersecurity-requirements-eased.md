## 摘要

The Estonian government approved a regulation that eases network and information system cybersecurity compliance obligations for approximately 1,200 small Estonian businesses and local government bodies.

## 事實內容

The Estonian government approved a regulation drafted by the Ministry of Justice and Digital Affairs that eases the obligation to comply with network and information security cybersecurity requirements for approximately 1,200 small Estonian businesses and local government bodies. This decision acknowledges the disproportionate burden that full NIS2-level compliance would impose on smaller organizations and provides tailored requirements appropriate to their size and risk level. The regulation was approved and comes as part of the broader NIS2 transposition effort.

## 受影響系統

- Small Estonian businesses (~1,200)
- Local government bodies
- NIS2 compliance requirements
