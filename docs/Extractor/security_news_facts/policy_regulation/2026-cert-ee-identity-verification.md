## Summary
Estonia's national mobile app now allows users to verify their identity directly via smartphone by presenting digital ID card or passport data to service providers.

## Facts Content
**Launch Date**: July 7, 2025
**Service**: Eesti äpp (Estonia App) - Identity verification feature
**Functionality**: Digital identity verification via smartphone

**Technical Details**:
- Users can present digital ID card data via app
- Users can present digital passport data via app
- No physical document presentation required
- Direct smartphone-based verification

**Implementation Approach**:
- Voluntary for both service providers and users
- Gradual rollout in cooperation with service providers
- Phased implementation planned

**Official Source**: Information System Authority (RIA) of Estonia

**Notes**:
- Original language: Estonian
- Major milestone in digital identity evolution
- Part of Estonia's e-government innovation
- Reduces reliance on physical ID documents
