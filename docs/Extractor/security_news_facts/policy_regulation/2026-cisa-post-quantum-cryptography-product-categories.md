## 摘要

CISA released product category lists in response to EO 14306, identifying hardware and software categories where PQC-capable products are widely available for federal acquisition planning.

## 事實內容

In response to Executive Order 14306 (June 6, 2025, "Sustaining Select Efforts to Strengthen the Nation's Cybersecurity"), CISA is providing and regularly updating lists of hardware and software categories with example types of widely available products that use post-quantum cryptography (PQC) standards to protect sensitive information.

The lists focus on categories of available products, typically acquired by the federal government, that utilize cryptographic algorithms. Organizations should acquire only PQC-capable products when planning acquisitions and procuring products in these categories.

**Widely Available PQC Product Categories (Table 2):**
- Cloud Services (PaaS, IaaS)
- Collaboration Software (Chat/messaging)
- Web Software (browsers, servers)
- Endpoint Security (data at rest, full disk encryption)

**Product Categories Transitioning to PQC (Table 3):**
- Networking Hardware and Software
- Cloud Services (SaaS)
- Telecommunications Hardware
- Computers and Operating Systems
- ICAM Hardware and Software
- Database and Storage Systems
- Enterprise Security Tools

The guidance references NIST PQC standards including FIPS 203 (ML-KEM), FIPS 204 (ML-DSA), FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA), and NIST SP 800-208 (stateful hash-based signatures). CISA will periodically update these tables as new PQC-capable products become widely available.

## 受影響系統

- Federal government IT procurement
- Cloud services
- Endpoint security products
- Networking infrastructure
- Identity and access management systems
