## 摘要

CISA, FBI, and UK NCSC release guidance urging action against nation-state exploitation of end-of-support edge devices, referencing BOD 26-02 requirements.

## 事實內容

CISA, the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI), and the U.K.'s National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) released a fact sheet urging defensive action against malicious cyber activity by nation-state threat actors who exploit end-of-support (EOS) edge devices.

EOS edge devices include load balancers, firewalls, routers, and VPN gateways that manufacturers no longer monitor for defects or update with security patches. Nation-state threat actors exploit these devices to gain network access, maintain presence, and compromise sensitive data.

CISA's Binding Operational Directive (BOD) 26-02 requires U.S. Federal Civilian Executive Branch (FCEB) agencies to manage the lifecycle of edge devices. While BOD 26-02 only applies to FCEB agencies, CISA, FBI, and NCSC strongly encourage all organizations to follow the guidance.

Key mitigations:
1. Maintain asset inventory and conduct regular audits
2. Replace EOS edge devices and software proactively
3. Install updates and patch known CVEs on devices awaiting replacement

Organizations using EOS devices exposed to the public internet are particularly vulnerable to compromise. EOS devices pose significant risks as threat actors can exploit unresolved security gaps as entry points to access modern, supported environments.

## 受影響系統

- End-of-support load balancers
- End-of-support firewalls
- End-of-support routers
- End-of-support VPN gateways
- Other internet-facing edge devices
