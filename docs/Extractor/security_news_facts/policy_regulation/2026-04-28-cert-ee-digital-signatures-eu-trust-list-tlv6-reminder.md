## 摘要

Due to the European Commission's technical update to the EU Trust List (TLv5 to TLv6), digital signature creation and validation may break in unupdated information systems starting April 29, 2026. Organizations must update their systems to avoid disruption.

## 事實內容

- **Deadline**: April 29, 2026
- **Risk**: Digital signature creation and validation may fail in information systems that have not been updated
- **Root cause**: European Commission technical update to the EU Trust List — transition from version TLv5 to TLv6
- **Required action**: Affected organizations must update their information systems before the deadline
- **Issuer**: Riigi Infosüsteemi Amet (RIA) — reminder notification
- **Scope**: All Estonian information systems that rely on EU Trust List for digital signature operations

## 受影響系統

- Estonian information systems using digital signatures
- EU Trust List-dependent systems (TLv5 → TLv6 migration)
- Organizations using digital signature creation and validation
