## 摘要

UK NCSC summary of analysis of May 2020 US sanctions that modified Huawei security mitigation strategy.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre (UK) has published a summary of its analysis of the May 2020 US sanctions against Huawei, which caused the NCSC to modify the scope of its security mitigation strategy for Huawei equipment. The analysis:

- Reviews the technical and supply chain implications of US export restrictions
- Assesses the impact on Huawei's ability to produce secure telecommunications equipment
- Explains the rationale for changing the UK's Huawei mitigation approach
- Documents security risks arising from supply chain disruptions
- Provides context for UK policy decisions regarding Huawei's role in UK telecoms infrastructure

This analysis played a crucial role in the UK government's decision to phase out Huawei equipment from UK 5G networks.
