## 摘要

Portugal's CNCS published the National Cybersecurity Reference Framework (QNRCS), providing voluntary guidelines for organizations to define cybersecurity strategies.

## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) released the Quadro Nacional de Referência para a Cibersegurança (QNRCS — National Cybersecurity Reference Framework). The document reflects the Portuguese organizational reality and responds to the need to implement measures against threats that compromise cyberspace security. It provides recommendations enabling organizations to define a strategy involving their entire structure. Adherence is voluntary, and organizations benefit from a homogeneous approach promoting a national response to cyber threats.
