## 事實內容

The last Observatory Bulletin of 2023 was published by the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory. The edition focuses on the new EU Directive 2022/2555, known as NIS2 or SRI2 (Network and Information Security Directive 2), which establishes measures to achieve a high common level of cybersecurity across the European Union. The bulletin covers the directive's scope, obligations for essential and important entities, incident reporting requirements, and the path to national transposition. This publication supports Portuguese organizations in understanding and preparing for NIS2 compliance requirements.

## 受影響系統

- Entities subject to EU NIS2 Directive (essential and important entities)
