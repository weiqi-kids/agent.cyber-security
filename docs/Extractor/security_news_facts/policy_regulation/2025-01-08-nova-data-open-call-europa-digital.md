## 摘要

Europa Digital Programme open call extended to 27 March 2025, with €102M available to strengthen cybersecurity and digital capabilities across the EU.

## 事實內容

The Europa Digital Programme (DIGITAL-ECCC-2024-DEPLOY-CYBER-07) open call for proposals was extended to 27 March 2025. A total of €102 million is available to reinforce cybersecurity and digital capabilities across the EU. Key funding allocations include:

- €35 million for protecting critical industrial installations and critical infrastructure
- €12.8 million to create, support and expand national and cross-border Security Operations Centers (SOCs)
- €35 million for deployment of advanced cybersecurity technologies and tools
- €20 million to support member states in implementing EU cybersecurity legislation and national strategies

Sub-programmes include:
- DIGITAL-ECCC-2024-DEPLOY-CYBER-07-SOC: National SOCs
- DIGITAL-ECCC-2024-DEPLOY-CYBER-07-SOCPLAT: Cross-Border SOC Platforms
- DIGITAL-ECCC-2024-DEPLOY-CYBER-07-SOCSYS: Strengthening the SOC Ecosystem

## 受影響系統

- EU Member States cybersecurity agencies
- National Security Operations Centers (SOCs)
- Critical infrastructure operators
