## 摘要

Australia enacted a law on 10 March 2026 requiring internet users to verify their age before accessing websites with an 18+ age restriction, aiming to protect minors from harmful online content.

## 事實內容

Australia has enacted a new internet law entering into force on 10 March 2026.

Key provisions:
- **Requirement**: Internet users must prove their age to access websites with an 18-year age restriction
- **Objective**: Protect children from harmful content online
- **Scope**: All 18+ rated websites accessible in Australia

This represents a tightening of Australia's existing digital safety regulations, extending age verification requirements to a broader range of online content. The law has implications for online platforms, identity verification services, and user privacy, as age verification mechanisms typically require the collection and processing of personal data.

**Broader context**: Australia has been progressively tightening online safety legislation, having previously enacted legislation restricting social media access for minors under 16.

## 受影響系統

- Online platforms serving 18+ content to Australian users
- Age verification technology providers
- Internet service providers in Australia
