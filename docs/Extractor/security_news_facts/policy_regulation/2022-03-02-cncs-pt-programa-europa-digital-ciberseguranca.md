## 摘要

The European Commission published second calls for proposals under the Digital Europe Programme with a cybersecurity focus area ("Cybersecurity and Trust"), including support for health sector cyber protection and National Coordination Centres network deployment; deadline 17 May 2022.

## 事實內容

The European Commission published the second calls for proposals under the Digital Europe Programme, including a cybersecurity area: "Cybersecurity and Trust." Two specific topics were opened:

1. **European Cyber-shield — Support to cybersecurity in the health sector** (DIGITAL-2022-CYBER-02-SUPPORTHEALTH)
2. **Support to implementation of relevant EU Legislation: Deploying the Network of National Coordination Centres with Member States** (DIGITAL-2022-CYBER-02-NAT-COORDINATION)

**Submission deadline**: 17 May 2022

## 受影響系統

- EU member state organizations in the health sector
- National Coordination Centres for Cybersecurity across EU member states
