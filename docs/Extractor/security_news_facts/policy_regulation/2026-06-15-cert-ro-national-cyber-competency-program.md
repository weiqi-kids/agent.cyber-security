## 摘要

DNSC launched a national program to identify cybersecurity competency needs and assess their impact on key economic and societal sectors in Romania.

## 事實內容

Romania's National Directorate of Cyber Security (DNSC) announced the launch of a national program titled "Programul național pentru identificarea nevoilor de competențe cibernetice și evaluarea impactului asupra sectoarelor-cheie ale economiei și societății" (National Program for Identifying Cybersecurity Competency Needs and Evaluating the Impact on Key Economic and Societal Sectors). The program aims to map cybersecurity skill gaps and evaluate their impact on critical economic and societal sectors in Romania.
