## 摘要

Estonia launches 1.5 million EUR funding program for cybersecurity companies to develop innovative solutions in collaboration with researchers, accepting applications from January 12.

## 事實內容

The Estonian state is opening a new funding program to help cybersecurity enterprises develop innovative, internationally competitive solutions in collaboration with researchers.

Program details:
- Total funding: 1.5 million EUR
- Directed to IT sector
- Focus: Cybersecurity innovation
- Requirement: Collaboration between companies and researchers
- Application period: Starting January 12, 2026

Initiative led by:
- Enterprise Estonia (EAS - Ettevõtluse ja Innovatsiooni Sihtasutus)
- Information System Authority (RIA)

This represents a strategic investment in Estonia's cybersecurity capabilities and innovation ecosystem.
