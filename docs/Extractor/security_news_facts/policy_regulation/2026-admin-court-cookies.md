# Administrative Court ruling on cookie consent

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/administrative-court-clarifies-interpretation-cookie-rules  
**Published**: 2021-05-28

## Summary

Helsinki Administrative Court ruled in April on two NCSC-FI decisions concerning website cookies and consent for non-essential cookies. The ruling clarifies legislative interpretation and NCSC-FI will revise cookie guidance accordingly.

## Key Points

- **Court**: Helsinki Administrative Court
- **Date**: April 2021
- **Subject**: Two NCSC-FI cookie-related decisions
- **Focus**: Consent for non-essential cookies
- **Outcome**: Clarified interpretation of legislation
- **Action**: NCSC-FI to revise guidance

## Impact

Court rulings provide authoritative interpretation of ambiguous privacy regulations, helping both regulators and regulated entities understand compliance requirements.
