## 摘要

Portugal opens public consultation until May 30, 2025 on a preliminary Cybersecurity Services Certification Scheme to evaluate and monitor cybersecurity service performance.

## 事實內容

A preliminary version of the Cybersecurity Services Certification Scheme (Esquema de Certificação de Serviços de Cibersegurança) was made available for public consultation until May 30, 2025. The scheme aims to address the need for identifying and valuing cybersecurity services whose performance will be subject to periodic evaluation and monitoring. The certification scheme establishes criteria and requirements for cybersecurity service providers, strengthening the overall quality and reliability of cybersecurity services in Portugal. Stakeholders and interested parties were invited to submit contributions to shape the final scheme.

## 受影響系統

- Cybersecurity service providers in Portugal
- Organizations seeking certified cybersecurity services
- Portuguese national cybersecurity ecosystem
