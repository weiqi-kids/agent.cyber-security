## Summary

National Cyber Security Centre Finland helps operators critical to emergency supply and central government in observing and processing serious data security breaches following several targeted cyber attacks worldwide in early 2021.

## Details

**Event Type**: Government Support Initiative

**Affected Entities**: Critical operators in Finland (emergency supply and central government)

**Key Points**:
- Several targeted cyber attacks have occurred globally in early 2021
- NCSC-FI provides support for critical operators
- Focus on observing and processing serious data security breaches
- Support covers emergency supply operators and central government entities

## Impact Assessment

**Severity**: Medium

**Scope**: National (Finland) - Critical Infrastructure

**Response Actions**:
- NCSC-FI activated support mechanisms for critical operators
- Enhanced monitoring and incident response capabilities
