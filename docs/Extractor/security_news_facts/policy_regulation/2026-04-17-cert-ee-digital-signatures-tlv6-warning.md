## 摘要

European Commission is updating the EU Trust List from version 5 to version 6. Estonia's RIA warns that organizations that fail to update their systems will face broken digital signature creation and validation starting April 29, 2026.

## 事實內容

- **Warning issued**: April 17, 2026
- **Critical deadline**: April 29, 2026
- **Risk**: Digital signature creation and validation will fail in unupdated information systems
- **Root cause**: European Commission technical upgrade of EU Trust List — TLv5 to TLv6 transition
- **Required action**: Affected parties must update their information systems with the necessary changes
- **Issuer**: Riigi Infosüsteemi Amet (RIA)
- **Scope**: All Estonian organizations using EU Trust List-dependent systems for digital signatures

## 受影響系統

- Estonian information systems relying on digital signatures
- EU Trust List version 5 (TLv5) dependent systems
- Organizations processing digitally signed documents
