## 摘要

Czech NUKIB published updated version 3.0 of cryptographic recommendations for organizations subject to cybersecurity law.

## 事實內容

The Czech National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NÚKIB) issued an updated version of the document containing recommendations in the field of cryptographic means. According to Section 26(d) of Decree No. 82/2018 Coll., on security measures, cybersecurity incidents, reactive measures, particulars of submissions in the field of cybersecurity and data destruction, obligated entities under Act No. 181/2014 Coll., on cybersecurity, must take into account these recommendations issued by NUKIB for the purpose of protecting information and communication system assets.

## 受影響系統

- Czech organizations subject to cybersecurity law
- Information and communication systems
