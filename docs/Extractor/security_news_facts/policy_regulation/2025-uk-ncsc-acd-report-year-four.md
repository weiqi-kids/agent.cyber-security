## 摘要

UK NCSC releases year four Active Cyber Defence programme report covering 2020 achievements and defensive measures implemented to protect UK organizations.

## 事實內容

The UK National Cyber Security Centre published its fourth annual report on the Active Cyber Defence (ACD) programme, covering activities and achievements throughout 2020. The report:

- Documents the progress and impact of ACD initiatives during the fourth year of operation
- Highlights defensive measures implemented to protect UK public sector and wider ecosystem
- Demonstrates ongoing efforts to proactively reduce cyber threats facing UK organizations
- Provides transparency on government-led cyber defense initiatives

The ACD programme represents a shift from reactive incident response to proactive threat mitigation through technical interventions at scale.
