## 摘要
Romania's DNSC launches national workshop series for implementing the NIS2 Directive based on Emergency Ordinance no. 155/2024.

## 事實內容
The Romanian Directorate for Cybersecurity (DNSC) has launched a new national series of workshops dedicated to implementing the NIS2 Directive. These workshops are based on Emergency Ordinance (O.U.G.) no. 155/2024, which transposes the EU's NIS2 Directive into Romanian law. The initiative aims to support organizations in understanding and complying with the new cybersecurity requirements under the enhanced Network and Information Security framework. This represents Romania's effort to align with EU-wide cybersecurity standards and improve national cyber resilience.
