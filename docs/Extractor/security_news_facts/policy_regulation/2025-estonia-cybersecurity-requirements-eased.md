## Summary
Estonian government approved regulation easing network and information system cybersecurity compliance requirements for approximately 1,200 small businesses and local government institutions.

## Description
The Estonian government approved a regulation prepared by the Ministry of Justice and Digital Affairs that eases network and information system cybersecurity compliance requirements for approximately 1,200 Estonian companies and local government institutions. The regulation reduces the burden on smaller entities while maintaining essential security standards.

This policy adjustment balances cybersecurity objectives with practical considerations for resource-constrained small organizations, ensuring proportionate security requirements.

## Technical Details
- **Approval Date**: September 25, 2025
- **Affected Entities**: ~1,200 companies and local government institutions
- **Scope**: Network and information system cybersecurity requirements
- **Impact**: Eased compliance obligations for small entities
- **Ministry**: Justice and Digital Affairs
