## 摘要

Portugal's CNCS launched a 30-day public consultation on the draft Regulation of the Legal Framework for Cybersecurity, enacted by Decree-Law No. 125/2025 of December 4.

## 事實內容

- The National Cybersecurity Centre of Portugal (CNCS) opened a public consultation on a draft regulation implementing the Cybersecurity Legal Framework (Regime Jurídico da Cibersegurança).
- The legal basis is Decree-Law No. 125/2025, enacted on December 4, 2025.
- The consultation period runs for 30 working days.
- The draft regulation is available online at the official public consultation portal (consultalex.gov.pt, Consultation ID 383).
- Contributions must be submitted in writing, in Portuguese, preferably by email to cncs@cncs.gov.pt.
- The deadline for submissions is April 22, 2026 (implied from the 30 working days from March 10).

## 受影響系統

- Organizations subject to Portuguese cybersecurity law
- Public and private sector entities in Portugal under NIS2 transposition
