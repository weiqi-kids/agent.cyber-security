## 摘要

CNCS published a Risk and Crisis Communication Framework (Referencial) to help Portuguese organizations communicate effectively during cybersecurity risk and crisis situations.

## 事實內容

The National Cybersecurity Centre (CNCS) published a "Referencial de Comunicação de Risco e Crise em Cibersegurança" (Risk and Crisis Communication Framework) aimed at helping Portuguese organizations manage communications during cybersecurity risk and crisis contexts. The framework provides structured guidance on communication planning, stakeholder engagement, message crafting, and coordination with authorities during cyber incidents. It covers pre-incident preparedness, active crisis communication, and post-incident messaging. The document is intended for use by organizations across critical sectors, public administration, and any entity responsible for managing cybersecurity incidents with public communication dimensions. The framework supports Portugal's broader incident response ecosystem and NIS2 compliance requirements around incident notification and public disclosure.

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese organizations across critical sectors
- Public administration entities
