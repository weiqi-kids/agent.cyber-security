## Summary
Denmark leads the Nordic-Baltic Cyber Consortium project launched in December 2025 to strengthen cyber threat information sharing across the region, with Estonia's RIA participating.

## Description
In December 2025, the Nordic-Baltic Cyber Consortium project began substantive preparations under Danish leadership to strengthen cyber threat information sharing in the region. The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) participates in the project alongside other Nordic and Baltic nations.

This regional cooperation initiative aims to enhance collective cybersecurity capabilities through improved threat intelligence exchange and coordination among participating countries. The consortium represents a strategic effort to strengthen cyber defenses across the Nordic-Baltic region.
