## Summary
Nordic-Baltic Cyber Consortium project starts substantive preparations under Danish leadership to strengthen cyber threat information exchange in the region, with Estonian RIA participating.

## Factual Content
In December, substantive preparations began for the Nordic-Baltic Cyber Consortium project led by Denmark to strengthen cyber threat information exchange in our region. Estonia participates through the Information System Authority (RIA).

**Key Details:**
- Project: Nordic-Baltic Cyber Consortium
- Leadership: Denmark
- Goal: Strengthen cyber threat information sharing
- Region: Nordic and Baltic countries
- Estonian participant: RIA
- Status: Substantive preparations phase
