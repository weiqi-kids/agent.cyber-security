## 摘要

Estonia transposed the EU's NIS2 cybersecurity directive into national law effective January 2026, expanding cybersecurity obligations through the Cybersecurity Act amendment to raise overall security levels.

## 事實內容

From January 2026, the amendment to Estonia's Cybersecurity Act and other acts came into force, transposing the European Union's Network and Information Security 2 (NIS2) directive into Estonian law. The purpose of the amendments is to raise the level of cybersecurity. The NIS2 directive significantly expands the scope of cybersecurity obligations compared to the original NIS directive, covering more sectors and imposing stricter security requirements on essential and important entities. This legislative change represents a major expansion of Estonia's cybersecurity regulatory framework.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian essential service providers
- Important entities under NIS2 scope
- National cybersecurity regulatory framework
