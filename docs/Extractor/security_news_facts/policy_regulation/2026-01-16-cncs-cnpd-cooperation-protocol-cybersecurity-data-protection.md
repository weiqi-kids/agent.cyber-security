## 摘要

CNCS and CNPD signed a cooperation protocol to strengthen institutional collaboration between cybersecurity and data protection authorities.

## 事實內容

The National Cybersecurity Centre (CNCS) and the National Data Protection Commission (CNPD) signed a cooperation protocol on January 15, 2026, aimed at strengthening institutional collaboration and promoting knowledge sharing in the areas of cybersecurity and personal data protection. The agreement seeks to deepen both authorities' capabilities in fulfilling their respective mandates. The protocol recognizes the growing interdependence between cybersecurity and data protection as complementary pillars of digital trust.

Original language: Portuguese (CNCS Portugal)

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese organizations subject to both NIS2 and GDPR obligations
- Cross-domain regulatory coordination between cybersecurity and data protection
