## 摘要

Portugal's Cybersecurity Alliance (Aliança para a Cibersegurança) convened at CNCS headquarters, bringing together public-private sector members to coordinate national cybersecurity strategy and threat information sharing.

## 事實內容

- The Aliança para a Cibersegurança (Cybersecurity Alliance) held a meeting at CNCS premises
- The alliance is a public-private partnership coordinated by CNCS
- Members include representatives from critical sectors participating in cybersecurity governance
- The meeting focused on strengthening collaborative cybersecurity practices across Portuguese organizations

## 受影響系統

- Alliance member organizations across critical infrastructure sectors
- Portuguese national cybersecurity ecosystem
