## 摘要

EU National Coordination Centers (NCCs) met in Athens at ENISA on 11 October 2023, electing Estonia as chair and discussing cybersecurity community membership and ECCC strategic agenda.

## 事實內容

The network of National Coordination Centers (NCCs) convened in Athens, Greece, on 11 October at ENISA premises. Representatives from EU Member States, the European Commission, and ENISA attended. Following adoption of internal rules, the NCC network elected Estonia as president and Poland and France as vice-presidents. Participants exchanged views on cybersecurity community membership registration and the strategic agenda of the European Cybersecurity Competence Centre (ECCC). The NCC meeting continued on 12-13 October with the 7th Governing Board meeting of the ECCC, covering the ECCC Annual Activity Report 2022, Single Programming Documents for 2024-2026 and 2025-2027, and cybersecurity priorities under the Digital Europe Programme (DEP) and Horizon Europe.
