## 摘要

CNCS informs that the MyCiber platform for registering Cybersecurity Officers and Permanent Contact Points under Decreto-Lei n.º 125/2025 will be available after the ongoing public consultation closes on 22 April 2026.

## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS), acting as the National Cybersecurity Authority, clarifies the timeline for compliance with the Cybersecurity Legal Framework (Decreto-Lei n.º 125/2025 of 4 December 2025) regarding the designation and communication of Cybersecurity Officers and Permanent Contact Points.

Following the public consultation on the draft Regulation of the Cybersecurity Legal Framework (ongoing until 22 April 2026), the regulation governing the electronic platform's operation will be approved and published. This regulation is required under Article 8(7) of Decreto-Lei n.º 125/2025.

The MyCiber electronic platform (referenced in Article 8(1) of the decree) will enable essential, important, and relevant public entities to fulfill their registration obligations only after the platform is formally made available. Entities are therefore not yet required to register their Cybersecurity Officer or Permanent Contact Point until the platform is operational.

## 受影響系統

- Essential, important, and relevant public entities under Decreto-Lei n.º 125/2025 (Portugal)
- MyCiber platform
