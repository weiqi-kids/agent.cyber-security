## Summary
RIA introduces Smart-ID+ functionality in late February, making authentication more secure and user-friendly while helping prevent fraud and social manipulation.

## Factual Content
The Information System Authority (RIA) will introduce Smart-ID+ functionality in the national authentication service at the end of February, making authentication more secure and user-friendly than before. Additionally, this helps prevent fraud and block social manipulation and other fraud types.

**Key Features:**
- Launch: Late February 2026
- Enhanced security: More secure authentication
- Improved UX: More user-friendly interface
- Fraud prevention: Helps prevent social engineering and other scams
- Scope: National authentication service
