## 摘要

A Digital Europe Programme open call for cybersecurity deployment actions (DIGITAL-ECCC-2024-DEPLOY-CYBER-06) opens 16 January to 26 March 2024, with EU co-funding for cybersecurity capacity-building projects.

## 事實內容

The submission period for proposals under "Call Deployment Actions in the area of Cybersecurity (DIGITAL-ECCC-2024-DEPLOY-CYBER-06)," under the Digital Europe Programme, opens from 16 January to 26 March 2024. This call provides EU co-funding for cybersecurity deployment initiatives, managed through the European Cybersecurity Competence Centre (ECCC). Eligible activities include deployment of cybersecurity solutions, capacity-building measures, and infrastructure improvements. CNCS highlighted the opportunity for Portuguese organizations to apply for funding aligned with EU strategic cybersecurity objectives.
