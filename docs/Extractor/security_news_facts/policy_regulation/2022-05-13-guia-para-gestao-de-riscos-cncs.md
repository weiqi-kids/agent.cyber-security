## 摘要

CNCS published a draft Risk Management Guide for information security and cybersecurity, open for public comments until June 14, 2022, aligned with Portuguese cybersecurity legislation.

## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) published a draft Risk Management Guide (Guia para Gestão de Riscos) to collect contributions, comments, and improvement suggestions for consolidation into a final version.

Key aspects of the guide:
- Objective: Define a systematic and coherent reference approach for risk analysis, assessment, and periodic treatment in information security and cybersecurity
- Target entities: Organizations required to comply with Article 10 of Decree-Law No. 65/2021 of July 30, which mandates risk analysis for all assets ensuring continuity of networks and information systems, and for essential service operators also covering assets ensuring essential service provision
- Expected outcomes: Enable definition and implementation of technical and organizational security measures to manage risks to network and information system security, with continuous improvement toward acceptable risk levels
- Submission deadline: Contributions by email to drsc@cncs.gov.pt, preferably by June 14, 2022

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese organizations subject to Decree-Law No. 65/2021
- Essential service operators in Portugal
