## 摘要

Portugal's CNCS Observatory published the 3rd edition of its Society Report presenting indicators on cybersecurity attitudes, behaviors, awareness, and education in Portugal, including tracking of the National Cybersecurity Strategy 2019-2023.

## 事實內容

The 3rd edition of the Society Report from CNCS's Cybersecurity Observatory presents the most recent indicators on attitudes, behaviors, awareness, and education in the area of cybersecurity in Portugal. Compared to previous editions, this year's report additionally:
- Tracks certain indicators related to the National Cyberspace Security Strategy 2019-2023
- Develops a chapter on general digital usage and corresponding risk exposure
- Deepens the survey of organizations conducting external awareness activities
- Presents recommendations based on the most fragile points identified during analysis

The report is also available in a condensed "15-minute" version.

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese organizations and citizens
- National Cyberspace Security Strategy 2019-2023 implementation
