# Updated cookie guidance issued

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/ncsc-fi-issues-updated-cookie-guidance  
**Published**: 2021-09-17

## Summary

NCSC-FI updated guidance on storing cookies and using data on users' terminal devices. This helps users and providers understand and apply cookie practices and legislation.

## Key Points

- **Update**: Cookie guidance revised
- **Scope**: Storing cookies and data on terminal devices
- **Beneficiaries**: Service users and providers
- **Objective**: Clarify cookie practices and legislative requirements
- **Context**: GDPR and Finnish regulations

## Impact

Clear guidance helps organizations comply with privacy regulations while enabling users to understand their rights regarding tracking technologies.
