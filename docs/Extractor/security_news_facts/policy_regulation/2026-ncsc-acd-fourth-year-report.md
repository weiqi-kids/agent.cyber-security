## 事實內容

The UK National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) published its Active Cyber Defence (ACD) - the fourth year report, summarizing the program's achievements and future directions. The ACD program represents the NCSC's proactive approach to reducing cyber threats at scale.

Key achievements in year four include:
1. Expansion of protective DNS services blocking malicious domains
2. Enhanced takedown capabilities for phishing sites targeting UK organizations
3. Improved vulnerability disclosure and coordination processes
4. Broader adoption of security standards across government and critical sectors

The ACD approach focuses on systemic interventions that provide protection at scale rather than individual incident response. This includes working with registrars, hosting providers, and infrastructure operators to remove threats before they reach end users.

The report demonstrates the effectiveness of public-private partnerships in cyber defense and provides insights for other nations developing national cyber security programs.
