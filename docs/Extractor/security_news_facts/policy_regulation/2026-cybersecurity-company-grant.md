## Summary
Estonia launches new 1.5 million EUR grant program enabling cybersecurity companies to develop internationally competitive solutions in collaboration with researchers, starting January 12.

## Factual Content
The state opens a new grant that helps cybersecurity companies develop novel, internationally competitive solutions in collaboration with researchers. The measure, initiated by the Estonian Business and Innovation Agency and the Information System Authority, directs 1.5 million euros to the IT sector, with applications accepted starting January 12.

**Key Details:**
- Total funding: €1.5 million
- Target: Cybersecurity companies
- Collaboration: Companies + researchers
- Goal: Internationally competitive solutions
- Application start: January 12, 2026
- Organizers: Estonian Business and Innovation Agency + RIA
