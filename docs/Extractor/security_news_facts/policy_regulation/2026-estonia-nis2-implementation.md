## Summary
Estonia implemented the EU NIS2 directive in January 2026, expanding mandatory cybersecurity requirements to approximately 6,500 entities across critical sectors with executive-level responsibility and significant penalties for non-compliance.

## Description
Starting January 2026, Estonia transposed the EU's NIS2 (Network and Information Security) directive into national law through amendments to its cybersecurity legislation. The implementation significantly expands cybersecurity obligations, adding approximately 3,000 organizations to bring the total to around 6,500 entities subject to mandatory requirements.

The directive covers expanded sectors including transportation (aviation, railways), energy (electricity, district heating), port operators, financial institutions, cloud service providers, healthcare, selected food processors, postal services, and waste management firms meeting specific criteria.

Organizations must conduct regular cybersecurity risk assessments, implement proportionate protective measures, develop incident detection and response capabilities, protect sensitive data, and provide employee and leadership training. Critically, cybersecurity responsibility shifts from technical departments to executive management.

Entities receive a three-year implementation period to comply and must notify the State Information Systems Authority (RIA) within three months. Existing critical infrastructure operators have a five-year transition from their designation date.

Violations carry substantial penalties: up to €10 million or 2% of annual revenue for critical entities; up to €7 million or 1.4% of previous year's turnover for important entities.

## Technical Details
- **Directive**: EU NIS2 (Network and Information Security 2)
- **Affected Entities**: ~6,500 organizations (~3,000 newly added)
- **Transition Period**: 3 years (5 years for existing critical infrastructure)
- **Notification Deadline**: 3 months
- **Maximum Penalties**: €10M/2% revenue (critical), €7M/1.4% turnover (important)
