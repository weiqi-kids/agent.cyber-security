## 摘要

RIA announced that Smart-ID+ will be deployed to the national authentication service on February 26, 2026, bringing a new and more secure login flow that reduces fraud risks and simplifies usage.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) announced that Smart-ID+ functionality will be deployed to the national authentication service on February 26, 2026. The change will introduce a new, more secure login flow for Estonian national e-services, reducing fraud risks and making usage simpler. This pre-deployment announcement was intended to inform users and service providers to prepare for the upcoming change to the authentication infrastructure.

## 受影響系統

- Smart-ID authentication
- National authentication service (riigi autentimisteenus)
- Estonian national e-services
