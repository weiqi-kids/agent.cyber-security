## 摘要

Sixth annual ACD programme report provides key findings and full documentation from the 6th year of the Active Cyber Defence initiative.

## 事實內容

The UK National Cyber Security Centre published the sixth annual report on the Active Cyber Defence (ACD) programme. The report:

- Documents six years of ACD programme evolution and maturation
- Presents comprehensive findings on programme effectiveness
- Highlights scalable interventions protecting UK organizations
- Demonstrates sustained commitment to proactive cyber defense

After six years, the ACD programme has evolved from experimental initiatives to established protective services. The report shows how sustained investment in proactive defense reduces the burden on individual organizations and creates a more resilient national cyber ecosystem.

- UK businesses and organizations
- UK internet service providers
