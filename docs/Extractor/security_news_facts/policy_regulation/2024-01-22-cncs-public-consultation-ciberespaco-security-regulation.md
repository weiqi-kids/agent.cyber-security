## 摘要

Portuguese CNCS launches public consultation on regulation implementing cyberspace security legal framework for public administration entities.

## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) announced the availability for public consultation of a Draft Regulation related to the implementation of the Legal Framework for Cyberspace Security (Regime Jurídico da Segurança do Ciberespaço) in public administration entities. The regulation was approved by CNCS and published in the Diário da República (official gazette). The consultation allows stakeholders to submit written comments on the regulation, which aims to establish binding cybersecurity requirements for Portuguese public administration entities. This represents a significant step in the implementation of Portugal's national cybersecurity governance framework.

Original language: Portuguese (CNCS)

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese Public Administration entities
- Organizations subject to the Regime Jurídico da Segurança do Ciberespaço
