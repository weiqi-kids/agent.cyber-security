## 摘要

From June 1, 2026, people in Estonia can present identity document data stored in the Eesti app to police officers for identity verification, in addition to the physical document.

## 事實內容

- **Effective date**: June 1, 2026
- **Change**: Citizens may now use identity document data stored in the Eesti mobile application as an accepted form of identity verification with police
- **Accepted method**: Identity data from Eesti app alongside (or in addition to) physical identity documents
- **Impact**: Increased digital identity capabilities for Estonian residents; expands use of digital ID infrastructure
- **Regulatory significance**: Official recognition of digital identity documents for law enforcement interaction

## 受影響系統

- Eesti mobile application
- Estonian digital identity infrastructure
- Law enforcement identity verification processes
