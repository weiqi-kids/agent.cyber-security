## 摘要

ENISA (EU Agency for Cybersecurity) open call running until 26 March for experienced cybersecurity professionals to join its Advisory Group.

## 事實內容

An open call is running until 26 March 2025 for professionals to join the Advisory Group of ENISA — the European Union Agency for Cybersecurity ("Agência da União Europeia para a Ciberseguran&ccedil;a"). The call targets experienced cybersecurity professionals with relevant knowledge to contribute to ENISA's strategic advisory function. Successful candidates will provide expert guidance on cybersecurity policy, technical standards, and strategic priorities at the EU level.

## 受影響系統

- EU cybersecurity policy stakeholders
- Cybersecurity professionals seeking EU-level advisory roles
