## 摘要

歐洲網路安全能力中心(ECCC)開放數位歐洲計畫與地平線歐洲計畫新一輪提案徵求，合計預算達1.455億歐元。

## 事實內容

- The European Cybersecurity Competence Centre (ECCC) has opened new calls for proposals under the Digital Europe Programme (DEP) and Horizon Europe (HE) Programme.
- The combined budget for the newly opened calls is up to EUR 145.5 million.
- These funding opportunities support cybersecurity research, innovation, and capacity building across EU member states.
- Eligible organisations can submit proposals to access funding for cybersecurity-related projects under these EU programmes.

## 受影響系統

- EU cybersecurity ecosystem (research institutions, industry, public sector)
