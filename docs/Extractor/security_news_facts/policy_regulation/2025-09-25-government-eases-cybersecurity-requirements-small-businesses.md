## 摘要

The Estonian government approved a regulation easing network and information systems cybersecurity compliance obligations for approximately 1,200 small companies and local government agencies.

## 事實內容

The Estonian government approved a regulation drafted by the Ministry of Justice and Digital Affairs that eases the obligation to comply with network and information systems cybersecurity requirements for approximately 1,200 Estonian companies and local government agencies. This policy change reduces the cybersecurity compliance burden on small organizations while maintaining security standards for larger entities.

- Scope: ~1,200 small enterprises and local government agencies
- Regulation type: Eased compliance requirements for network and information systems cybersecurity
- Originating ministry: Ministry of Justice and Digital Affairs (justiits- ja digiministeerium)

## 受影響系統

- Small Estonian enterprises (~1,200 organizations)
- Estonian local government agencies
