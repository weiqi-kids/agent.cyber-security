## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security publishes foundational guidance on a lifecycle approach to cyber security and privacy risk management, providing a framework for selecting and implementing controls and assurance activities.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security published the foundational guidance document "Cyber security and privacy risk management: A lifecycle approach" (March 2026).

Key points:
- This publication serves as the parent/overview document for a series of guidelines on cyber security and privacy risk management.
- It contains definitions of assurance activities and controls for systems and organizations.
- Practitioners can use it as a foundation for selecting, tailoring, and allocating controls and assurance activities to manage cyber security and privacy risks.
- Implementing a comprehensive set of security and privacy controls and assurance activities supports holistic risk management.
- The series includes companion documents: ITSP.10.033 (controls catalogue) and ITSP.10.033-01 (medium-impact profile), all released concurrently on March 31, 2026.
- Intended audience: Practitioners, IT architects, and risk management professionals.

## 受影響系統

- Government of Canada IT systems
- Organizations implementing lifecycle-based security and privacy risk management
- Security practitioners and IT governance teams
