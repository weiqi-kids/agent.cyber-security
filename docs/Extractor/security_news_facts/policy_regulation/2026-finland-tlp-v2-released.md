# Traffic Light Protocol version 2.0 released for information sharing

## Summary
The international Forum of Incident Response and Security Teams (FIRST) released a new version of its Traffic Light Protocol (TLP), originally introduced in 2017. TLP harmonizes information sharing practices and promotes sharing between national and international public and private operators.

## Key Details
- **Protocol**: Traffic Light Protocol (TLP)
- **Version**: 2.0
- **Organization**: FIRST (Forum of Incident Response and Security Teams)
- **Original**: 2017
- **Purpose**: Harmonize sharing practices, promote information exchange
