## 摘要

Portugal published Decree-Law No. 125/2025 approving the new Legal Regime for Cybersecurity (Regime Jurídico da Cibersegurança), transposing EU Directive 2022/2555 (NIS2) into national law and expanding CNCS authority and competencies.

## 事實內容

- Decree-Law No. 125/2025 of 4 December was published in Portugal's official gazette (Diário da República)
- The decree transposes EU Directive 2022/2555 (NIS2) into Portuguese national law
- The decree will enter into force 120 days after publication
- CNCS (Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança) has 24 months after entry into force for some provisions to take effect
- CNCS is reinforced as the National Cybersecurity Authority with expanded supervisory powers including on-site inspections, remote supervision, and security audits
- CERT.PT is designated as coordinator for coordinated vulnerability disclosure
- The NIS2 Roadmap (Roteiro NIS2) with 60 free training sessions was already initiated on 27 November 2025

## 受影響系統

- Essential and important entities under NIS2 scope in Portugal
- Public sector entities and critical infrastructure operators
- Cybersecurity service providers
