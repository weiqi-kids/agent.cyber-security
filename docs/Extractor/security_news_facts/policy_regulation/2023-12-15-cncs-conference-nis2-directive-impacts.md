## 摘要

CNCS coordinator Lino Santos participated in a TSF/DN-promoted conference on 14 December at Lisbon Law School discussing the transposition of the NIS2 Directive (EU 2022/2555) and its impacts on Portuguese companies and public entities.

## 事實內容

CNCS coordinator Lino Santos was one of five panelists at a conference promoted by TSF/DN, held on 14 December at the Faculty of Law, University of Lisbon. The event addressed the status of transposition of the NIS2 Directive (Directive EU 2022/2555 on measures for a high common level of cybersecurity across the Union) into Portuguese law. Discussions covered the practical impacts on companies and public sector organizations subject to the directive's scope, including risk management obligations, incident reporting requirements, and enforcement mechanisms. The NIS2 Directive significantly expands the number of entities covered and increases enforcement powers compared to the original NIS Directive.

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese companies and public sector entities subject to NIS2
