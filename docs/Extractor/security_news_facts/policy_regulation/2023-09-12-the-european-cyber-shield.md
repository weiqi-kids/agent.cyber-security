## 摘要

CERT.at analyses the EU's proposed European Cyber Shield under the Cyber Solidarity Act, highlighting valid ideas around SOC collaboration but noting terminology confusion and unclear relationship with existing NIS2/CSIRTs Network structures.

## 事實內容

The EU has been advancing the concept of the "European Cyber Shield" through the Digital Europe Programme and the proposed Cyber Solidarity Act. CERT.at's analysis, presented at the CSIRTs Network meeting in June 2023, identifies three key findings:

1. **Valid core concept**: Chapter 2 of the Cyber Solidarity Act — supporting Security Operation Centres (SOCs) by fostering national and cross-border collaboration — is considered worthwhile.
2. **Terminology issue**: The choice of the term "Cyber Shield" is prone to confuse readers of the Act; a change is recommended.
3. **Structural ambiguity**: The relationship between the proposed new structures and the tasks of CSIRTs/CSIRTs Network (as defined in NIS2) is not entirely clear. Better integration of roles is needed.
4. **Funding approach**: EU funding for multiple consortia building closer technical cross-border collaborations is assessed as a sound investment.

The analysis was published as a long-form paper available from the CERT.at Download/Papers page.
