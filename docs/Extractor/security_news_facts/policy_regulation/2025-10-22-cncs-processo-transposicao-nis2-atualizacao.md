## 摘要

CNCS Portugal published an update on the ongoing NIS2 Directive transposition process, informing stakeholders of progress in implementing the EU cybersecurity directive into Portuguese national law.

## 事實內容

- CNCS published an update on the NIS2 Directive (Directive 2022/2555) transposition process in Portugal
- The update informs stakeholders about the current legislative timeline and progress
- NIS2 Directive aims to establish a high common level of cybersecurity across EU member states
- Portugal is working to transpose NIS2 requirements into national legislation within EU-mandated timelines

## 受影響系統

- Essential and important entities under NIS2 scope in Portugal
- Cybersecurity service providers and operators
