## 摘要

CNCS organized a workshop on the EU AI Act at Campus APP, attended by over 20 senior officials from 13 national entities, focusing on AI regulation compliance for public organizations.

## 事實內容

A workshop on the European Union AI Regulation (EU AI Act) was held on 26 March 2025 at Campus APP. The event was organized by CNCS and attended by more than 20 senior officials ("dirigentes") from 13 national entities who accepted invitations to participate. The workshop was delivered by specialist trainers and focused on the implications of the EU AI Act for public sector and government organizations. The initiative reflects CNCS's expanding role in AI governance and regulatory compliance, alongside its core cybersecurity mandate.

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese government entities subject to EU AI Act
- Public sector AI governance stakeholders
