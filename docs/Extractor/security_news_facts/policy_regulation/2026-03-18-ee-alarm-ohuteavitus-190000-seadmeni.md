## 摘要

Estonia's RIA tested the national EE-ALARM alert system on March 16 and 18, successfully reaching nearly 190,000 devices through the Estonian app, including siren networks.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) conducted tests of the national safety alert system EE-ALARM on March 16 and 18, 2026, in cooperation with the Rescue Board (Päästeamet) and Women's Home Defence (Naiskodukaitse). The March 16 test verified mobile application technical functionality, while the March 18 full-system test activated all EE-ALARM channels, including the siren network. Alerts were successfully delivered to nearly 190,000 devices via the Estonian app (Eesti äpp) as planned. The test was part of Estonia's nationwide public alert infrastructure development.

## 受影響系統
