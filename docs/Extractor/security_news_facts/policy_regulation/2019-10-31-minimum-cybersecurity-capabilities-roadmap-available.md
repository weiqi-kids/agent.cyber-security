## Summary

CNCS publishes detailed roadmap with five phases for organizations to gradually implement minimum cybersecurity capabilities.

## Factual Content

The Portuguese National Cybersecurity Centre (CNCS) has defined a cybersecurity capacity-building model aimed at improving processes, people, and technologies in national organizations, with special focus on SMEs. The Roadmap presents a set of actions divided into five phases, designed for gradual adaptation and implementation in each organization, either through internal means or by outsourcing solutions. These activities, framed within the National Cybersecurity Reference Framework, constitute the establishment of minimum cybersecurity capability, providing targeted organizations with necessary instruments to face threats and dangers of the Digital World.

## Affected Systems
