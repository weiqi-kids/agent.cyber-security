## Summary
Estonia's government file exchange service sahver.eesti.ee will close on May 19, 2025 due to changed data exchange needs and cost-efficiency considerations.

## Facts Content
**Service**: sahver.eesti.ee (file exchange environment)
**Operator**: Information System Authority (RIA) of Estonia
**Closure Date**: May 19, 2025

**Reasons for Closure**:
- Changed data exchange requirements
- Cost-efficiency considerations
- Service no longer supports long-term sustainability

**Impact**: Users must transition to alternative file exchange solutions before closure date

**Notes**:
- Original language: Estonian
- Government service sunset decision
- Users given advance notice for migration
- Reflects evolving digital infrastructure needs
