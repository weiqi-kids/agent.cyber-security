## 摘要

The EU Cyber Resilience Act entered into force, establishing new cybersecurity rules for hardware and software products.

## 事實內容

The Cyber Resilience Regulation, published on November 20, 2024, defines new cybersecurity rules to ensure greater security for hardware and software products in the European space. The regulation entered into force on December 21, 2024. Products covered by the regulation must display CE marking to indicate compliance with the new standards. Digital products must meet specific cybersecurity requirements throughout their lifecycle.

Key aspects:
- Applies to products with digital components sold in the EU market
- Requires CE marking for compliant products
- Establishes mandatory cybersecurity requirements for hardware and software
- Aims to enhance the overall cybersecurity posture of the European digital ecosystem
