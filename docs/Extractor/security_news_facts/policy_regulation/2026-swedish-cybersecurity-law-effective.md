## 摘要

Sweden's cybersecurity law entered into force during the week of January 16, 2026.

## 事實內容

The Swedish cybersecurity law ("cybersäkerhetslagen") officially entered into force during the week of January 16, 2026. This regulatory milestone was announced in CERT-SE's weekly newsletter. The law establishes legal frameworks for cybersecurity requirements and obligations within Sweden's jurisdiction.

## 受影響系統

- Swedish organizations subject to cybersecurity regulations
- Critical infrastructure operators in Sweden
