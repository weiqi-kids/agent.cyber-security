## 摘要

A new four-year EU project has been launched to strengthen cybersecurity, providing financial support for implementing new cybersecurity regulations including NIS 2 and other directives.

## 事實內容

A new four-year project has been launched at the EU level to continue strengthening cybersecurity across Europe and nationally. Key details:

- The project will provide financial support for activities related to implementing new cybersecurity regulations
- Aims include strengthening cybersecurity both in Europe and at the national level
- Financial support will be granted for implementing regulatory requirements (e.g., NIS 2 Directive compliance)
- Upcoming funding opportunities are expected to be announced during the project period
- NCSC-FI published this notice to inform stakeholders of the new EU project and future funding opportunities

## 受影響系統

- EU member states' cybersecurity regulatory compliance
- Organisations subject to NIS 2 and related cybersecurity regulations
