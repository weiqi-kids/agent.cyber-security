## 摘要

Fifth annual ACD programme report presents key findings from the 5th year of the Active Cyber Defence initiative protecting UK organizations.

## 事實內容

The UK National Cyber Security Centre published the fifth annual report on the Active Cyber Defence (ACD) programme. The report:

- Summarizes key findings and achievements from the fifth year of operation
- Tracks programme maturity and expanding scope of protective services
- Demonstrates continued effectiveness of proactive defense approach
- Provides accountability for government-funded cyber security initiatives

The fifth year report shows how the ACD programme has become embedded in the UK cyber security landscape, with established services providing continuous protection to organizations across the public and private sectors.

## 受影響系統

- UK public and private sector organizations
- UK cyber security infrastructure
- Internet-facing UK services
