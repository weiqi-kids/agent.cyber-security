## 摘要

Romanian National Cybersecurity Directorate (DNSC) launches new national workshop series dedicated to implementing the NIS2 Directive based on Emergency Ordinance No. 155/2024.

## 事實內容

DNSC (Directoratul Național de Securitate Cibernetică - National Cybersecurity Directorate of Romania) announced the launch of a new national series of workshops dedicated to implementing the NIS2 Directive (EU Directive on Security of Network and Information Systems).

**Initiative Details:**
- **Organizer:** DNSC (Romanian National Cybersecurity Directorate)
- **Topic:** NIS2 Directive implementation
- **Legal Basis:** Emergency Ordinance No. 155/2024 (O.U.G nr. 155/2024)
- **Format:** National workshop series
- **Target Audience:** Organizations subject to NIS2 requirements

**Context:**
The NIS2 Directive is the EU's updated cybersecurity legislation requiring member states to enhance security of network and information systems. Romania is implementing NIS2 through national legislation (Emergency Ordinance 155/2024), and these workshops aim to guide organizations through compliance requirements.

**NIS2 Coverage:**
- Essential and important entities
- Critical infrastructure
- Supply chain security
- Incident reporting requirements
- Governance and risk management
