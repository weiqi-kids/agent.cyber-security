# Instruction on traffic data processing recording enters force October 2022

## Summary
Instruction applies to recording and storing event information related to traffic data processing under Section 145 of the Act on Electronic Communications Services. Applies to all communications providers, replacing FICORA Recommendation 308/2004.

## Key Details
- **Effective Date**: October 27, 2022
- **Scope**: All communications providers
- **Topic**: Traffic data processing event recording/storage
- **Legal Basis**: Act on Electronic Communications Services §145
- **Replaces**: FICORA Recommendation 308/2004 (2004)
