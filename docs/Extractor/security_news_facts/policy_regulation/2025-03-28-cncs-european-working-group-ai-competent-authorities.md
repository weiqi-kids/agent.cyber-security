## 摘要

CNCS hosted the first 2025 meeting of the European Working Group of Competent Authorities on AI in Oeiras (26-28 March), with representatives from most EU member states.

## 事實內容

From 26 to 28 March 2025, the Templo da Poesia venue in Oeiras hosted the first 2025 meeting of the European Working Group of Competent Authorities on AI ("European Working Group of Competent Authorities on AI"). CNCS is a member of this working group, which includes representatives from the majority of EU member states. The meeting focused on coordinating national competent authorities responsible for AI governance and regulatory oversight under the EU AI Act framework.

## 受影響系統

- EU member state AI competent authorities
- CNCS and Portuguese AI regulatory bodies
