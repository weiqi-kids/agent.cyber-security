## 摘要

Czech NUKIB and security agencies published recommendations for evaluating trustworthiness of 5G network technology vendors.

## 事實內容

The Czech National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NÚKIB) published, together with the Ministry of Industry and Trade, Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Security Information Service, Office for Foreign Relations and Information, and Military Intelligence, the Recommendations for Assessing the Trustworthiness of Suppliers of Technologies for 5G Networks in the Czech Republic. The importance of next-generation telecommunications networks is extraordinary for society, and limiting the security risks of these networks is necessary for creating and maintaining resilient infrastructure in the Czech Republic. The goal is to offer operators and the entire electronic communications sector the perspective of security-relevant state institutions on fundamental approaches to assessing vendor trustworthiness and to propose criteria that can contribute to selecting trustworthy suppliers.

## 受影響系統

- 5G network infrastructure
- Telecommunications operators in Czech Republic
