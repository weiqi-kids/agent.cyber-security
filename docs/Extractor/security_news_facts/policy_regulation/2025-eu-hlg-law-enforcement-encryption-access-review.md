## 摘要

CERT.at reviews EU High-Level Group report on law enforcement data access, raising critical concerns about encryption backdoors' impact on cybersecurity.

## 事實內容

CERT.at analyzes the concluding report from the EU High-Level Group (HLG) on law enforcement access to encrypted data. The HLG recommends developing shared LE tools, establishing vulnerability disclosure processes, and harmonizing lawful interception obligations across EU member states.

**Critical Cybersecurity Concerns:**
CERT.at argues that implementing backdoors in encryption would "undermine cybersecurity" and violate end-to-end encryption principles. The analysis distinguishes between:
- **Services** (where provider access is feasible)
- **Products** (where access compromises security for millions of users)

**Key Issues Raised:**
- Weak historical accountability for law enforcement overreach
- Conflicts with existing EU regulations (CRA, NIS2, CVD policies)
- Jurisdictional complications with mobile Over-The-Top (OTT) services
- Risk of offshore service migration if EU imposes strict requirements
- Absence of agreed technical feasibility assessment before legislation

**CERT.at's Position:**
Policymakers should first establish cybersecurity requirements and assess technical viability before mandating law enforcement access mechanisms. The review emphasizes the fundamental tension between security requirements and surveillance capabilities.

**Policy Context:**
This review follows the EU's June 2025 roadmap for addressing law enforcement encryption concerns, representing ongoing debate over balancing security and law enforcement needs.

## 受影響系統

- EU encryption and cybersecurity policy framework
- End-to-end encrypted communication services and products
- EU member state law enforcement agencies
- Service providers operating in EU jurisdiction
