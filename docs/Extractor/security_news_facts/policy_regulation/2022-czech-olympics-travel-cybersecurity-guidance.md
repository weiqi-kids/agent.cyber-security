## 摘要

Czech NUKIB and Military Intelligence published cybersecurity travel recommendations for Olympic athletes and general public.

## 事實內容

Experts from the Czech National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NÚKIB) and Military Intelligence prepared the material "Travel (Cyber)Safely: Recommendations for Foreign Travel," designed to help Czech athletes heading to the Beijing Olympics and the general public understand cybersecurity basics and advise them on how to use technology to make their communication abroad safer. "We originally prepared the recommendations together with Military Intelligence only for Czech Olympians, but based on considerable public interest, we decided to publish the material for other interested parties and travelers as well. The material is primarily intended for those traveling to countries where user data protection is at a low level," says NÚKIB Director Karel Řehka.

## 受影響系統

- Travelers to countries with low data protection
- Czech Olympic athletes
- Mobile devices and communications abroad
