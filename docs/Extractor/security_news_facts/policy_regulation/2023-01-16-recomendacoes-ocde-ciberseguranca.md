## 摘要

OECD Ministerial Meeting on the Digital Economy (14 December 2022) adopted four cybersecurity-relevant recommendations covering digital security risk management, national strategies, product/service security, and vulnerability treatment.

## 事實內容

- **Event**: OECD Ministerial Meeting on the Digital Economy, 14 December 2022.
- **Adopted recommendations** (Council of Ministers of the OECD):
  1. **Recommendation on Digital Security Risk Management** (OECD-LEGAL-0479)
  2. **Recommendation on National Digital Security Strategies** (OECD-LEGAL-0480)
  3. **Recommendation on the Digital Security of Products and Services** (OECD-LEGAL-0481)
  4. **Recommendation on the Treatment of Digital Security Vulnerabilities** (OECD-LEGAL-0482)
- **Purpose**: Guide OECD member states in implementing public policies and practices in risk management, national strategies, product security, and vulnerability handling.
- **Applicability**: All OECD member countries.

## 受影響系統

- OECD member states' national cybersecurity strategies and policies
- Digital product and service providers in OECD countries
