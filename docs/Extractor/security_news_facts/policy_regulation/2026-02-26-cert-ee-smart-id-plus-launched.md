## 摘要

Estonia's RIA deployed Smart-ID+ to the national authentication service, introducing a new and more secure login flow that reduces fraud risks and ties authentication more clearly to the user's own actions.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) deployed Smart-ID+ to the national authentication service (riigi autentimisteenus) starting from February 26, 2026. The new solution introduces a new and more secure login flow for national e-services. The upgrade helps reduce fraud risks and links authentication more clearly to the user's own actions. This upgrade represents a significant step forward in Estonian digital identity security infrastructure, addressing social engineering and fraud attempts that exploit authentication weaknesses.

## 受影響系統

- Smart-ID authentication service
- Estonian national e-services
- National authentication service (riigi autentimisteenus)
