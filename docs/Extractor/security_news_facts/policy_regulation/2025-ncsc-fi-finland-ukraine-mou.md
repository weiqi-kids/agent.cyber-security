## 摘要

Finland and Ukraine sign cybersecurity cooperation MoU for information sharing and best practices.

## 事實內容

Memorandum of Understanding details:
- Finland and Ukraine strengthening cooperation in promoting cyber security and cyber protection
- MoU agreement signed between SSSCIP and NCSC-FI
- Aims to reinforce collaboration
- Facilitate sharing of best practices and technical information between countries' cyber security authorities

This represents significant international cooperation, particularly given Ukraine's experience with state-sponsored cyber attacks.

## 受影響系統

- Finnish-Ukrainian cybersecurity collaboration initiatives
- Information sharing platforms between authorities
