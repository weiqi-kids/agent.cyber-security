## 摘要

CNCS launched Roadmap for Minimum Cybersecurity Capabilities targeting SMEs as Portugal marks opening of European Cybersecurity Month with training and awareness initiatives.

## 事實內容

October is European Cybersecurity Month, and CNCS marked its opening with the launch of a roadmap that companies can follow to achieve minimum cybersecurity levels. This instrument provides a path and establishes priorities for actions to reach what CNCS defines as a minimum cybersecurity threshold for companies.

**Roadmap Details:**
- **Launch Date:** October 1, 2019 (opening of European Cybersecurity Month)
- **Target Audience:** Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs)
- **Participation:** Voluntary adoption
- **Approach:** Enables companies to identify threats and respond appropriately

**Key Features:**
- Establishes minimum cybersecurity thresholds
- Prioritized action plan
- Self-assessment capability (planned website)
- CNCS guidance on next steps based on assessment

**European Cybersecurity Month Activities:**
- National tour (North to South Portugal)
- Topics: Cyber-Hygiene and Emerging Technologies
- Target sectors: Public and private companies, universities, public services

**Context:**
This roadmap represents a practical implementation tool for Portugal's National Cybersecurity Reference Framework, specifically tailored to SME needs and capabilities.

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese SMEs (Small and Medium Enterprises)
- Public and private sector organizations
- Universities and public services
