## 摘要

Canada's CSE issues warning to organizations and critical infrastructure providers to strengthen cyber defences on the four-year anniversary of Russia's invasion of Ukraine, citing persistent Russian cyber threat activity.

## 事實內容

The Communications Security Establishment Canada (CSE) and its Cyber Centre issued a call to action for Canadian organizations and critical infrastructure providers to strengthen their cyber defences on the fourth anniversary of Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine (February 24, 2022 – February 24, 2026).

Key points:
- Russian state-sponsored threat actors continue to target Western organizations, including Canadian entities, as part of broader hybrid warfare operations
- Sectors at heightened risk include energy, government, defence, transportation, and financial services
- CSE urges organizations to review and test incident response plans, ensure critical systems are patched, and enhance monitoring for signs of intrusion
- International partners including CISA (US), NCSC (UK), and allied agencies have jointly assessed the ongoing threat from Russia-linked actors
- Organizations are reminded that Russian cyber operations often target supply chains and managed service providers as initial access vectors

The advisory reinforces Canada's commitment to supporting Ukraine and countering Russian aggression in cyberspace.

## 受影響系統

- Critical infrastructure (energy, water, transportation)
- Government networks
- Defence contractors and supply chains
- Financial sector
