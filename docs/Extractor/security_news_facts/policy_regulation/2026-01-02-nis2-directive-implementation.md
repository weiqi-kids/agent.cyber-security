## 摘要

Estonia implements EU NIS2 Directive into national law from January 2026, expanding cybersecurity requirements to raise security levels.

## 事實內容

Effective January 2026, Estonia has implemented amendments to its Cybersecurity Act and other related laws, transposing the European Union's NIS2 Directive (Cybersecurity Directive 2) into Estonian law.

Key aspects:
- Implementation date: January 1, 2026
- Legal framework: Amendment to Cybersecurity Act and related legislation
- EU alignment: Transposes NIS2 Directive into national law
- Objective: Raise the level of cybersecurity across critical and important sectors

The NIS2 Directive significantly expands the scope of cybersecurity obligations compared to the original NIS Directive, covering more sectors and entities, and introducing stricter security requirements and enforcement mechanisms.

## 受影響系統

- Critical infrastructure operators
- Essential service providers
- Important digital service providers
- Expanded sectors under NIS2 scope
