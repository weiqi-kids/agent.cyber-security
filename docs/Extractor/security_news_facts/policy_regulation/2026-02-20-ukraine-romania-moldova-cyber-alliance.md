## 摘要

Ukraine, Romania, and Moldova establish Cyber Alliance for Regional Resilience at International Cyber Resilience Forum in Kyiv.

## 事實內容

On February 20, 2026, at the International Cyber Resilience Forum in Kyiv, three strategic partners - Ukraine, Romania, and the Republic of Moldova - announced the establishment of the Cyber Alliance for Regional Resilience. This trilateral cooperation aims to strengthen cybersecurity capabilities and regional cyber defense coordination among the three countries.
