## Summary
ID card manufacturer changes in mid-November 2025 from IDEMIA to Thales, requiring service providers to prepare in advance to ensure smooth service operation.

## Factual Content
The ID card manufacturer will change in mid-November 2025: instead of the previous manufacturer IDEMIA, Thales will start producing Estonian ID cards. Service providers should prepare in advance to ensure smooth operation of services after the new cards are introduced.

**Key Changes:**
- Timing: Mid-November 2025
- Old manufacturer: IDEMIA
- New manufacturer: Thales
- Action required: Service provider preparations
- Impact: Service continuity depends on advance preparation
