## 摘要

Estonia's RIA announced it will introduce Smart-ID+ in the national authentication service on February 26, bringing a new, more secure login flow to national e-services that reduces fraud risks.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) announced that on February 26, it will introduce the Smart-ID+ solution in the national authentication service (riigi autentimisteenus). The planned change will bring national e-services a new, more secure login flow (sisselogimisvoog) that:
- Reduces fraud risks
- Makes the login process simpler for users
- Ties authentication more clearly to the user's own actions

This is an advance notice announcement ahead of the February 26 deployment, giving users and service providers time to prepare for the authentication system upgrade.

## 受影響系統

- National authentication service (riigi autentimisteenus)
- Smart-ID authentication
- All Estonian national e-services
