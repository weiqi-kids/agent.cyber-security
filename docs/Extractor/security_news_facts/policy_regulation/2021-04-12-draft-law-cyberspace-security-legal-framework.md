# Draft Decree-Law Regulates Cyberspace Security Legal Framework and Cybersecurity Certification

## Summary

Portuguese draft law establishes CNCS as National Cybersecurity Certification Authority, defines security requirements for networks/systems, and incident notification obligations.

## Factual Content

The draft establishes CNCS as the National Cybersecurity Certification Authority and provides the sanctioning regime for violations of Regulation provisions, enabling implementation of a national cybersecurity certification framework.

It establishes security requirements for networks and information systems (applicable to Public Administration, critical infrastructure operators, and essential service operators) and defines incident notification requirements (applicable to Public Administration, critical infrastructure operators, essential service operators, and digital service providers).

These rules do not apply to companies offering public communications networks or publicly accessible electronic communications services, which remain subject to the specific regime in the Electronic Communications Law.

Entities covered by this decree-law assume obligations to promote higher security levels of networks and information systems, including: asset inventory, designation of permanent contact point with CNCS for information exchange in case of cybersecurity incident, designation of security officer to manage security requirements and incident notification measures, and notification to CNCS of any security incident occurring in their computer networks.

## Affected Systems

- Portuguese Public Administration
- Critical infrastructure operators
- Essential service operators
- Digital service providers
