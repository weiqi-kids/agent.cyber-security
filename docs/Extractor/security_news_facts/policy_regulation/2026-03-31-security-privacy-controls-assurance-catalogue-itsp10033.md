## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security releases ITSP.10.033, a comprehensive catalogue of security and privacy controls and assurance activities for practitioners to manage cyber security and privacy risks.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security published ITSP.10.033 (March 2026), a practitioner-series document providing a catalogue of security and privacy controls and assurance activities.

Key points:
- Full title: "Security and privacy controls and assurance activities catalogue — ITSP.10.033"
- Part of the "Cyber security and privacy risk management: A lifecycle approach" series.
- The catalogue provides definitions of assurance activities and controls for systems and organizations.
- Practitioners can use it as a foundation for selecting, tailoring, and allocating controls and assurance activities to manage cyber security and privacy risks.
- Document structure covers: Introduction, Concepts and structure, Requirements/controls/activities, Controls and assurance activities organization, Implementation approaches, Security and privacy controls.
- PDF alternate format available (4.5 MB).
- Companion to ITSP.10.033-01 (medium-impact profile) published on the same day.

## 受影響系統

- Government of Canada IT systems
- Organizations implementing security and privacy risk management frameworks
- Security practitioners responsible for control selection and implementation
