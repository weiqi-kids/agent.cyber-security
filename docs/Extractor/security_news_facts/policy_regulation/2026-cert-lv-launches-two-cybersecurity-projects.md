## 摘要

CERT.LV launched two EU-funded cybersecurity projects: Nordic-Baltic Cyber Consortium (regional cooperation) and National SOC enhancement (domestic capacity).

## 事實內容

On January 1, 2026, CERT.LV initiated two significant cybersecurity projects funded through the EU's Digital Europe programme:

**Project 1: Nordic-Baltic Cyber Consortium (NBCC)**
- Duration: 48 months
- Scope: Regional partnership across 7 nations (Denmark, Iceland, Finland, Norway, Latvia, Estonia, Lithuania)
- Led by Danish Resilience Agency
- Key objectives:
  - Establish data-sharing platform among member nations
  - Procure commercial cyber threat intelligence solutions
  - Develop AI-based security tools
  - Foster government-commercial-academic collaboration

**Project 2: National Cyber Security Operations Center (CERT.LV-SOC-LV)**
- Duration: 36 months
- Scope: National capacity enhancement for Latvia
- Key objectives:
  - Sustain and enhance SOC functions
  - Strengthen staff expertise
  - Facilitate knowledge exchange
  - Improve incident management and communication

Both initiatives involve joint procurement with the European Cybersecurity Competence Centre (ECCC).

## 受影響系統

- Regional cybersecurity infrastructure (Nordic-Baltic region)
- CERT.LV Security Operations Center
- National cybersecurity capacity (Latvia)
