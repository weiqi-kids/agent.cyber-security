## Summary
After six years of successful operation, Estonia-led EU CyberNet cyber cooperation initiative enters second phase in September 2025, focusing on expanding cooperation to the Indo-Pacific region.

## Description
After six years of successful operation, the cyber cooperation initiative EU CyberNet, led by the Estonian Information System Authority (RIA), began its second phase in September 2025. The new phase focuses primarily on expanding cooperation to the Indo-Pacific region, extending European cybersecurity partnerships beyond traditional geographic boundaries.

This expansion represents a strategic effort to strengthen international cyber cooperation and share Estonia's expertise in cybersecurity and digital government with emerging partners in the Indo-Pacific.
