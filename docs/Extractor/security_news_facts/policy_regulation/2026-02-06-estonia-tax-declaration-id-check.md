## 摘要

Estonian authorities RIA, MTA, and PPA remind citizens to verify document validity and PIN code availability before the income tax declaration period beginning February 16.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Tax and Customs Board (MTA), Police and Border Guard Board (PPA), and Information System Authority (RIA) issued a joint reminder ahead of the income tax declaration season beginning February 16, 2026. Citizens are advised to:
- Check the validity of their identity documents (ID cards, passports)
- Ensure PIN codes are available and not locked
- Prepare digital authentication means in advance

The advisory is a routine pre-season reminder to ensure citizens can access e-services smoothly for tax filing. It also implicitly references the ongoing new ID card rollout and Smart-ID changes.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian e-services (tax filing portal)
- Estonian ID cards and digital authentication
- Smart-ID and mobile-ID
