## Summary
RIA introduces Smart-ID+ authentication solution on February 26, 2026, bringing a new, more secure login flow to government e-services, reducing fraud risks and simplifying usage.

## Factual Content
The Information System Authority of Estonia (RIA) will implement the Smart-ID+ solution in the national authentication service on February 26. This change introduces a new, more secure login flow to government e-services, which reduces fraud risks and makes usage simpler.

**Key Points:**
- Implementation date: February 26, 2026
- Purpose: Enhanced security for government e-services
- Benefits: Reduced fraud risks, simplified user experience
- Scope: National authentication service
