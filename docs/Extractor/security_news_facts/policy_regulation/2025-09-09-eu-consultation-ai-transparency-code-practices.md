## 摘要

The EU opens a public consultation until October 2, 2025, on Guidelines and a Code of Practices for transparent AI systems, aiming to ensure users are informed when interacting with AI systems or exposed to AI-generated content.

## 事實內容

A public consultation ran until October 2, 2025 (18:00 CEST) to collect suggestions, comments, and contributions for the development of Guidelines and a Code of Practices for transparent Artificial Intelligence systems. The objective of the initiative is to ensure that users are informed when interacting with an Artificial Intelligence system, as well as when they are exposed to AI-generated content (such as deepfakes or synthetic media). The consultation forms part of the EU's broader regulatory framework for Artificial Intelligence, complementing the EU AI Act. The initiative aims to establish clear transparency requirements that help users distinguish AI interactions from human interactions and identify AI-generated content, addressing growing concerns about misinformation and manipulation.

## 受影響系統

- AI system developers and deployers
- Users of AI-powered services
- Digital platforms operating in the EU
