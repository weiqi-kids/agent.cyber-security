## 摘要
Estonian government authentication service RIA has deployed Smart-ID+ solution to improve security of national e-services login flow.

## 事實內容
The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) deployed Smart-ID+ in the national authentication service. The new solution aims to reduce fraud risks and binds login more closely to user action.
