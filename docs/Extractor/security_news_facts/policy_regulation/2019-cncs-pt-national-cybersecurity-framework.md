## 摘要

Portugal's National Cybersecurity Reference Framework (QNRCS) has been released, providing organizations with recommendations for implementing comprehensive cybersecurity strategies against cyber threats.

## 事實內容

Portugal's National Cybersecurity Center (CNCS) announced the availability of the National Cybersecurity Reference Framework (Quadro Nacional de Referência para a Cibersegurança). This document reflects the Portuguese organizational reality and responds to the need to implement measures against threats that compromise cyberspace security.

**Framework Details:**
- **Document:** National Cybersecurity Reference Framework (QNRCS)
- **Purpose:** Provide recommendations for organizational cybersecurity strategies
- **Scope:** Involves entire organizational structure
- **Adoption:** Voluntary for entities
- **Approach:** Homogeneous approach promoting national response to cyber threats

**Key Features:**
- Recommendations for defining comprehensive cybersecurity strategies
- Structured approach for national capacity building
- Voluntary participation framework
- Promotes unified national response to cyber threats

**Context:**
This framework represents Portugal's national effort to standardize and improve cybersecurity posture across organizations, similar to frameworks like NIST CSF in the United States or national cybersecurity strategies in other EU member states.

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese organizations (all sectors)
- Public and private entities
- Critical infrastructure operators
