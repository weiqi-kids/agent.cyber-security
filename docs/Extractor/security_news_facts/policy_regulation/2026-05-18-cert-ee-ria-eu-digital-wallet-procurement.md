## 摘要

Estonia's RIA has launched a public procurement to find a partner to create and operate the EU digital identity wallet (EL digikukkur) in Estonia, enabling citizens to digitally identify themselves, sign documents, and store/present e-credentials.

## 事實內容

- **Organization**: Riigi Infosüsteemi Amet (RIA)
- **Action**: Published public procurement (riigihanke) for EU digital identity wallet implementation
- **Purpose**: Find a partner to create and operate the EU Digital Identity Wallet (EL digikukkur) for Estonia
- **EU context**: Part of the Europe-wide secure mobile solution mandated by the EU
- **Capabilities**: Digital self-identification, document signing, storage and presentation of various e-credentials
- **Significance**: Implementation of the EU Digital Identity framework in Estonia

## 受影響系統

- Estonian digital identity infrastructure
- EU Digital Identity Wallet (EL digikukkur)
- Mobile identity and e-credential ecosystem
