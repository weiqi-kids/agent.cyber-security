## Summary

NCSC-FI promotes easier adoption of strong electronic identification services for online service providers, allowing customers to identify securely using online banking codes or mobile certificates.

## Details

**Event Type**: Policy Guidance

**Target Audience**: Online service providers in Finland

**Key Points**:
- Acquiring identification services is now easier than before
- Customers can use online banking codes for identification
- Mobile certificate support available
- Focus on preventing personal data leaks
- Emphasis on ease of use

## Impact Assessment

**Severity**: Low (Guidance)

**Scope**: National (Finland) - Service Providers

**Business Impact**: Helps online service providers implement strong authentication to protect customer data
