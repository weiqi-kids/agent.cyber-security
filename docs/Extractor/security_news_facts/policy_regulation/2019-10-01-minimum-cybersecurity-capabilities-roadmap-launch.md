## Summary

CNCS launches voluntary roadmap for companies to achieve minimum cybersecurity levels, primarily targeting SMEs in Portugal.

## Factual Content

October is European Cybersecurity Month, and CNCS marks its opening with training and awareness activities for companies. The highlight this year is the launch of a roadmap that companies can follow to achieve minimum cybersecurity levels. This instrument provides a path and establishes priorities for actions to reach what CNCS defines as a minimum cybersecurity level for businesses. The roadmap follows the logic that companies should be capable of identifying threats and responding appropriately. This voluntary roadmap is primarily aimed at Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs). A website will be created later where each company can conduct its own self-assessment, allowing CNCS to recommend next steps.

## Affected Systems

- Portuguese SMEs (voluntary participation)
