## 摘要
The CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory published a report on public cybersecurity policies in Portugal, surveying national strategies and programs and analyzing citizen perceptions on the intersection of public policy and cyberspace security, including Portugal's position within the EU.

## 事實內容
The CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory (Observatório de Cibersegurança) published the Report on Public Cybersecurity Policies. Key features:

- **Scope**: Survey of national Strategies and Public Programs related to cybersecurity in Portugal
- **Analysis**: Available indicators on citizen perceptions regarding the intersection of public policies and cyberspace security
- **Context**: Analysis presented at national level and also positioning Portugal within the European Union
- **Purpose**: Fulfills one of the Observatory's objectives of monitoring the development and application of Public Policies in cybersecurity

This report provides insights on how national cybersecurity policies are being implemented and perceived by the Portuguese population.

## 受影響系統
- Portuguese national cybersecurity policy
- EU member state policy alignment
- Public administration
