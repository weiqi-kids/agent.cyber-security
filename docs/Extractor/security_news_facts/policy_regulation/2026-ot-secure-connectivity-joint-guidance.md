## 摘要

Seven international cybersecurity agencies release joint guidance on secure OT connectivity, warning that legacy systems face threats from opportunistic and highly capable threat actors.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security, along with six international partners, released joint guidance on secure connectivity principles for operational technology (OT).

**Participating Organizations (Seven Cybersecurity Agencies):**
- Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (Canada)
- UK National Cyber Security Centre
- Australian Signals Directorate's Cyber Security Centre
- Germany's Federal Office for Information Security
- Netherlands' National Cyber Security Centre
- US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency
- US Federal Bureau of Investigation

**Threat Context:**
Organizations face significant security challenges when deploying OT systems. The guidance identifies that "opportunistic and highly capable threat actors are known to target exposed and insecure OT connectivity."

**Legacy Technology Vulnerability:**
Legacy OT technologies compound vulnerabilities, as they "were never designed for modern connectivity or security requirements."

**Core Guidance Approach:**
Rather than mandating minimum standards, the framework presents "desirable end-states that organizations should achieve when designing connectivity into OT environments." This flexible approach recognizes operational constraints while establishing aspirational security goals.

**Implementation Focus:**
System owners should "use these principles as a framework to design, implement and manage secure OT connectivity, for both new and existing OT systems."

**Particular Importance:**
The guidance emphasizes heightened relevance for essential service operators, who face elevated risk exposure due to critical infrastructure dependencies.

**Additional Context:**
The full publication is available through the UK NCSC, alongside related Canadian guidance on post-quantum cryptography readiness.

This represents a coordinated international effort to address the growing threat to operational technology systems, particularly in critical infrastructure sectors. The multi-agency collaboration reflects the global nature of OT security challenges and the need for standardized approaches to secure connectivity.

## 受影響系統

- Operational Technology (OT) systems
- Legacy OT technologies
- Critical infrastructure systems
- Essential service operator systems
- Industrial control systems (ICS)
- SCADA systems
- OT connectivity infrastructure
