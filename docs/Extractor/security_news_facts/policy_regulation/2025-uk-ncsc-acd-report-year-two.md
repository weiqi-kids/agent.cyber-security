## 摘要

Second annual ACD report examines how NCSC's Active Cyber Defence programme is improving security of UK public sector and wider cyber ecosystem.

## 事實內容

The UK National Cyber Security Centre published the second annual report on the Active Cyber Defence (ACD) programme. The report:

- Documents the second year of ACD programme activities and impact
- Demonstrates improvements in UK public sector cyber security posture
- Shows expansion of protective services to wider UK cyber ecosystem
- Provides accountability and transparency for the proactive defense initiative

The ACD programme uses technical interventions at internet scale to reduce cyber threats before they reach end users. This includes services like DMARC deployment, vulnerability disclosure, and automated takedowns of malicious infrastructure targeting UK organizations.

## 受影響系統

- UK public sector organizations
- UK private sector organizations
- UK internet infrastructure
