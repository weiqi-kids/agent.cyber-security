## 摘要

Portugal's "Referencial de Educação Para a Segurança, a Defesa e a Paz" was updated with cybersecurity content — a partnership between DGE, IDN, and CNCS — adapting the 2014 framework to digital-age challenges.

## 事實內容

- **Document**: "Referencial de Educação Para a Segurança, a Defesa e a Paz" (Education Framework for Security, Defence and Peace) — republished with cybersecurity additions.
- **Partners**: Directorate-General for Education (DGE), National Defence Institute (IDN), and CNCS.
- **Context**: Update of the 2014 framework to reflect the increasing digitalization of society.
- **Purpose**: Non-prescriptive, flexible reference document used in schools under Citizenship Education, fostering personal and social development of children and youth.
- **Cybersecurity addition**: With CNCS's support, the new version incorporates cybersecurity topics relevant to citizenship and digital awareness.

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese educational system (K-12 schools)
- Students, educators across Portugal
