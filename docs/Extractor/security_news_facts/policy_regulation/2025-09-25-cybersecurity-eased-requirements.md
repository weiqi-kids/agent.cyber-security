## Summary
Estonian government approves regulation easing cybersecurity compliance obligations for approximately 1,200 small businesses and local government agencies.

## Factual Content
The government today approved a regulation prepared in the Ministry of Justice and Digital Affairs that eases the obligation to comply with network and information system cybersecurity requirements for nearly 1,200 Estonian companies and local government agencies.

**Key Details:**
- Affected entities: ~1,200 companies and local agencies
- Nature: Eased cybersecurity compliance requirements
- Approval: Government regulation
- Ministry: Justice and Digital Affairs
- Scope: Network and information systems
