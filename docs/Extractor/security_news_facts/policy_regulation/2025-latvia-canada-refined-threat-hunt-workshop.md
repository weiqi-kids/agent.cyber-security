## 摘要

February 2025 Latvia-Canada Threat Hunt Workshop welcomed 30 participants from 25+ NATO nations with refined curriculum based on October 2024 lessons.

## 事實內容

CERT.LV, Latvia's Ministry of Defence, and Canadian Armed Forces Cyber Command conducted a refined Threat Hunt Workshop in February 2025:

**Location:**
- Institute of Mathematics and Computer Science, University of Latvia
- CERT.LV Cyber Incident Response Institution

**Participants:**
- Approximately 30 cybersecurity professionals
- From over 25 NATO member nations
- Diverse professional backgrounds

**Organizers & Leadership:**
- Canadian Ambassador Brian Szwarc
- Edgars Kiukucāns (Cybersecurity Policy Department Director, Latvia)
- CERT.LV Director Baiba Kaškina
- Major Stephen McKeon (Canadian Armed Forces Cyber Command)

**Curriculum Updates:**
- Training program and Threat Hunt Playbook substantially revised
- Incorporated "the latest tools, methods, and practical examples"
- Built on lessons learned from October 2024 initial workshop

**Key Outcomes:**
- Strengthened NATO cyber resilience
- Kiukucāns: "Proactive approach helps identify and neutralize attacks before they can cause significant damage"
- Established benchmarks for future international cybersecurity training

**Partnership Focus:**
- Sharing threat-hunting methodologies based on operational experience
- Enhancing collective defense across NATO
- Latvia-Canada collaboration model for international cybersecurity training

## 受影響系統

- NATO cybersecurity capabilities
- Threat hunting methodologies
- Collective defense infrastructure
