## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security publishes ITSAP.10.006 guidance on vendor diversification strategies to reduce supply chain risks and mission-critical vulnerabilities in enterprise systems.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (Cyber Centre) released awareness series publication ITSAP.10.006 (April 2026) on vendor diversification as a supply chain risk management (SCRM) strategy.

Key points:
- Modern enterprise systems depend on complex architectures, direct and indirect supply chains, and vendor relationships that expose organizations to mission-critical risks.
- Organizations are advised to incorporate vendor diversification strategies into SCRM processes to mitigate risks and reduce vulnerabilities associated with single-vendor dependencies.
- The guidance covers: supplier relationship management, evaluating vendor security practices, contractual security requirements, and monitoring vendor compliance.
- Diversification helps organizations maintain operational continuity if a vendor experiences a security incident, goes out of business, or is otherwise unable to deliver.
- Intended audience: Government and private sector organizations managing ICT supply chains.

## 受影響系統

- Enterprise supply chain management systems
- Government of Canada ICT infrastructure
- Organizations relying on third-party vendors
