## 摘要

CERT.LV concluded a month-long threat hunting collaboration with CAF, CCCS, CERT-EU, Polish Cyber Command, and Latvian forces to safeguard national infrastructure.

## 事實內容

CERT.LV wrapped up the Seventh Threat Hunting Surge, a month-long intensive collaboration concluded July 9, 2025:

**Participating Organizations:**
- Canadian Armed Forces (CAF)
- Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (CCCS)
- CERT-EU
- Polish Cyber Command
- Latvian National Armed Forces
- National Guard Cyber Defense and Electromagnetic Warfare Battalion

**Observers:**
- Slovenia
- United Kingdom

**Objectives:**
- Strengthen ongoing threat hunting operations
- Safeguard national and public networks, systems, and digital infrastructure
- Enhance threat detection methods
- Develop new hunting solutions through shared expertise

**Key Findings:**
- Identified cybersecurity threats in national infrastructure
- Observed responsible practices among some infrastructure operators
- Organizations demonstrated maturity by collaborating to remediate vulnerabilities

**Outcomes:**
- Enhanced cooperation among allied nations
- Improved real-time information sharing capabilities
- High-level official visits from Canadian and Latvian cybersecurity leadership
- Strengthened collective cyber resilience
- Improved detection and response capabilities across participating countries

## 受影響系統

- National and public networks (Latvia)
- Digital infrastructure across participating NATO nations
- Threat hunting capabilities
