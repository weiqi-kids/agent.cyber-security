## 摘要

CERT.at analyzes European Cyber Shield initiative under Cyber Solidarity Act, supporting goals while identifying terminology and coordination concerns.

## 事實內容

The European Cyber Shield is an EU concept promoted through the Digital Europe Programme and the proposed Cyber Solidarity Act. It aims to strengthen cybersecurity by "supporting SOCs by fostering national and cross-border collaboration."

**Initiative Goals:**
- Enhance Security Operations Centers (SOCs) capabilities
- Foster national cooperation between SOCs
- Enable cross-border SOC collaboration
- Strengthen collective cybersecurity defense across EU

**CERT.at's Perspective:**

**Support for Concept:**
CERT.at acknowledges the initiative's merit and supports the underlying goal of enhancing security operations through cooperative structures. The organization views EU funding for multiple consortia to build technical cross-border collaborations as "a sound investment" worthy of pursuit.

**Terminology Concerns:**
CERT.at identifies "an unfortunate choice of terminology is prone to confuse readers of the Act." The "Cyber Shield" branding may create misleading expectations about the initiative's scope and capabilities.

**Coordination Gaps:**
The organization sees unclear relationships between proposed Cyber Shield structures and existing responsibilities outlined in the NIS2 Directive for CSIRTs networks. Key concerns:
- Overlap with existing CSIRT network mandates
- Unclear division of responsibilities between SOCs and CSIRTs
- Potential duplication of effort or coordination gaps
- Need for clearer integration with established frameworks

**Recommendations:**
CERT.at recommends:
- Clarifying connections between Cyber Shield and NIS2 CSIRT requirements
- Integrating new SOC network roles with established CSIRT frameworks
- Ensuring terminology accurately reflects initiative scope
- Avoiding confusion about respective mandates

**Positive Assessment:**
Despite concerns about terminology and coordination clarity, CERT.at supports the initiative's funding and collaborative approach as valuable for EU cybersecurity capabilities.

## 受影響系統

- EU Digital Europe Programme
- Cyber Solidarity Act (proposed)
- Security Operations Centers (SOCs) across EU
- CSIRTs networks under NIS2 Directive
