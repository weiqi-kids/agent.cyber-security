## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security releases ITSM.00.140 management guidance on mandatory cyber incident reporting requirements, information sharing obligations, and timelines for Canadian organizations.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (Cyber Centre) released management guidance document ITSM.00.140 titled "Cyber Incident Reporting Guidelines: Key Information Sharing Requirements." This publication outlines the mandatory and voluntary cyber incident reporting framework for Canadian organizations. The document describes the types of cyber incidents that must be reported, the information required in reports, reporting timelines, and the authorities to whom incidents should be reported. It covers obligations under Canadian federal law and regulations, including reporting to the Cyber Centre. The guidance emphasizes the importance of timely information sharing to enable the Cyber Centre to provide assistance, track threat trends, and issue warnings to other organizations. The document is part of the Management series (ITSM) and is classified as UNCLASSIFIED, issued under the authority of the Head of the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security.

## 受影響系統

- All Canadian organizations subject to federal cyber incident reporting requirements
- Critical infrastructure operators
- Federal government departments and agencies
