## 摘要

Portugal's National Cybersecurity Centre (CNCS) has made the Cybersecurity Services Certification Scheme publicly available, establishing a formal certification framework for cybersecurity service providers operating in Portugal.

## 事實內容

- CNCS published the Cybersecurity Services Certification Scheme (Esquema de Certificação de Serviços de Cibersegurança)
- The scheme establishes formal criteria and requirements for certifying cybersecurity service providers
- Publication is part of Portugal's broader NIS2 transposition efforts and national cybersecurity governance framework
- The scheme is intended to help organizations identify qualified cybersecurity service providers

## 受影響系統

- Cybersecurity service providers in Portugal
- Organizations seeking certified cybersecurity services
