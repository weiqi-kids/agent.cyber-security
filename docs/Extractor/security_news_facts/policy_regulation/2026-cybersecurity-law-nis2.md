## Summary
Estonia implements EU NIS2 Directive from January 2026, expanding cybersecurity law to raise overall cybersecurity levels across the country.

## Factual Content
From January 2026, the amendment to the Cybersecurity Act and other acts came into force, transposing the European Union's 2nd Cybersecurity Directive (NIS2) into Estonian law. The aim of the changes is to raise the level of cybersecurity.

**Key Details:**
- Effective date: January 1, 2026
- Legislation: Cybersecurity Act amendments
- EU directive: NIS2 (Network and Information Security 2)
- Goal: Raise cybersecurity levels
- Scope: National implementation of EU requirements
