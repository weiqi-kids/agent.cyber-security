## 摘要

Eight international cybersecurity agencies release four-principle framework for secure AI integration in operational technology, warning of risks to critical infrastructure availability.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security coordinated joint guidance on December 3, 2025, for the secure integration of artificial intelligence in operational technology (OT).

**Participating Agencies (Eight International Partners):**
- Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (Cyber Centre)
- US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA)
- US Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI)
- US National Security Agency (NSA) Artificial Intelligence Security Center
- Australian Signals Directorate's Australian Cyber Security Centre (ASD's ACSC)
- Germany's Federal Office for Information Security (BSI)
- Netherlands' National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC-NL)
- New Zealand's National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC-NZ)
- United Kingdom's National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC-UK)

**Threat Context:**
Critical infrastructure operators face a dual challenge: AI systems can "increase efficiency and productivity" and "enhance decision-making" while simultaneously introducing "significant risks" to "the availability and reliability" of essential public services.

**Four Core Security Principles:**

1. **Understand AI** – Develop foundational knowledge of AI systems

2. **Consider AI use in the OT domain** – Evaluate operational technology-specific applications

3. **Establish AI governance and assurance frameworks** – Create oversight structures

4. **Embed safety and security practices** – Integrate protective measures into AI-enabled systems

**Implementation Guidance:**
The agencies urge "CI owners and operators to review this joint guidance and implement the principles to more securely integrate AI into OT systems."

**Target Audience:**
Critical infrastructure (CI) owners and operators deploying AI in operational technology environments.

**Resource Availability:**
The full publication provides detailed recommendations accessible through CISA's resource portal.

This represents a coordinated international effort to address the unique security challenges of AI integration in critical infrastructure operational technology, balancing innovation benefits against availability and reliability risks.

## 受影響系統

- Operational Technology (OT) systems
- Critical infrastructure AI implementations
- Essential service operations
- Industrial control systems (ICS)
- SCADA systems
- AI-enabled OT decision-making systems
