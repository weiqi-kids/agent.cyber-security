## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security releases ITSP.10.033-01, a practitioner guidance providing suggested organizational security and privacy control and activity profiles for medium-impact systems.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security published ITSP.10.033-01 (April 2026), part of the "Cyber security and privacy risk management: A lifecycle approach" series.

Key points:
- Full title: "Suggested organizational security and privacy control and activity profile — Medium impact (ITSP.10.033-01)"
- This is an UNCLASSIFIED publication issued under authority of the Head of the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security.
- The document provides a suggested profile of security and privacy controls and assurance activities tailored for systems classified as "medium impact."
- It is part of a larger framework series (see also ITSP.10.033 — controls catalogue, and lifecycle approach overview).
- Intended audience: Security practitioners, IT architects, and risk management professionals in government and regulated sectors.
- PDF alternate format available (2.3 MB).
- The publication supports tailoring and allocation of security controls to manage cyber security and privacy risks in medium-impact environments.

## 受影響系統

- Government of Canada medium-impact IT systems
- Organizations applying security and privacy risk management frameworks
- Security practitioners and IT architects
