## 摘要

On 1 July 2026, Sweden's cyber operations transfer from MCF to NCSC under FRA, per a government decision of 20 November 2025, to strengthen national cybersecurity.

## 事實內容

The Swedish government decided on 20 November 2025 that cyber operations would be transferred from Myndigheten för civilt försvar (MCF — Swedish Civil Contingencies Agency for Civil Defence) to Nationellt cybersäkerhetscenter (NCSC) at Försvarets radioanstalt (FRA — the Swedish Defence Radio Authority), effective 1 July 2026. The stated purpose is to strengthen Sweden's overall cybersecurity resilience. CERT-SE, which currently operates under MCF, will therefore transition to NCSC as part of this consolidation.

## 受影響系統

- CERT-SE (Swedish CERT, moving to NCSC/FRA)
- Swedish government cyber resilience framework
