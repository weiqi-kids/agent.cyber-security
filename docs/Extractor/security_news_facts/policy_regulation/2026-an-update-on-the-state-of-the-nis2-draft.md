## 事實內容

This is a TLP:WHITE summary of my presentation at the 15th CSIRTs Network meeting in Ljubljana on November 11th. This is not a complete review of the current state of the NIS2 discussions. Overall, I think the council should abandon the idea of finishing the text this year. There is too much in flux and we all lost sense if the text is still consistent. I recommend publishing a new consolidated draft and get a new round of public comments. We reached the limit of high-speed tinkering with the text. This blog post may sound negative. That is selection bias, as I primarily write about the things that need changing, not the other parts with which I agree. Update 2021-11-18: More text on scanning and some links added.
