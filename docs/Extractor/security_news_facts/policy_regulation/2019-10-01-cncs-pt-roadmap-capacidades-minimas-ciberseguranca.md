## 摘要

CNCS launched a voluntary roadmap for minimum cybersecurity capabilities targeted at SMEs, opening the European Cybersecurity Month with training and awareness events across Portugal.

## 事實內容

October is the European Cybersecurity Month, and CNCS marked the opening with training and awareness activities for businesses. The key novelty of 2019 was the launch of the Roadmap for Minimum Cybersecurity Capabilities — a voluntary instrument that provides a path and prioritizes actions organizations should take to reach minimum cybersecurity thresholds. The roadmap follows the logic that companies must be able to identify threats and react appropriately. It targets primarily Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs). A future website was planned to allow each company to conduct a self-assessment so CNCS can advise on next steps. CNCS planned to travel across Portugal throughout the month, addressing topics of Cyber-Hygiene and Emerging Technologies to public sector companies, universities, and other public services.

## 受影響系統

- Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) in Portugal
- Portuguese public sector organizations
