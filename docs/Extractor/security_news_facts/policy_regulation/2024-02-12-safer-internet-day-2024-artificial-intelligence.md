## 事實內容

Safer Internet Day 2024 commemorations were held in Viseu, Portugal, from 6 to 8 February, marking the annual event observed in approximately 180 countries under the motto "Together for a Better Internet." The 2024 edition highlighted Artificial Intelligence as the central theme, addressing both the opportunities and risks AI presents to internet users — particularly younger users. Activities included digital literacy workshops, awareness sessions on AI-generated content, deepfakes, and online safety best practices. The event promotes responsible internet usage and builds digital resilience among Portuguese citizens, in alignment with European safer internet initiatives.

- General public internet users (focus on youth)
