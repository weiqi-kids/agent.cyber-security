## 摘要

UK NCSC publishes year three Active Cyber Defence programme report covering 2019 activities, highlighting achievements and efforts in protecting UK cyber ecosystem.

## 事實內容

The UK National Cyber Security Centre released its third annual report on the Active Cyber Defence (ACD) programme, documenting activities throughout 2019. The report:

- Details the third year of ACD programme implementation and outcomes
- Showcases achievements in proactive cyber threat reduction
- Highlights technical and operational efforts to improve UK cyber resilience
- Provides accountability and transparency for government-funded cyber defense initiatives

This report continues the series documenting the evolution and impact of the UK's proactive cyber defense strategy.
