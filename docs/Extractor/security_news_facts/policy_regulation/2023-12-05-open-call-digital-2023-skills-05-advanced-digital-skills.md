## 摘要

An EU open call under DIGITAL-2023-SKILLS-05 for Advanced Digital Skills proposals, including the Cybersecurity Skills Academy, runs from 21 November 2023 to 21 March 2024 under the Digital Europe Programme.

## 事實內容

An open call for project proposals under "DIGITAL-2023-SKILLS-05: Advanced Digital Skills," under the Digital Europe Programme, is open from 21 November 2023 to 21 March 2024. The call includes the Cybersecurity Skills Academy initiative and targets organizations with capacity to deliver advanced digital skills training programs. The CNCS highlighted this opportunity for Portuguese entities active in cybersecurity education and training. Successful applicants would receive EU co-funding to develop and deliver upskilling programs aligned with EU cybersecurity workforce priorities.
