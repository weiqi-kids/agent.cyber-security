## 摘要

CNCS published security guidelines for distance learning platforms (ZOOM, Moodle, Microsoft Teams, Google Classroom) targeting the school community, ensuring secure configuration and use during COVID-19 remote learning.

## 事實內容

In the context of COVID-19-driven distance learning, the Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) published security guidance specifically for platforms enabling audio and video communication used by the school community, including ZOOM, Moodle, Microsoft Teams, and Google Classroom (the latter to be made available shortly at publication time). The guidelines aim to ensure that configuration and use of these platforms is carried out securely.

## 受影響系統

- ZOOM
- Moodle
- Microsoft Teams
- Google Classroom
- Portuguese school community (teachers, students, administrators)
