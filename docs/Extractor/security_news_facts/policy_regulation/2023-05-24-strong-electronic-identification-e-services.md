# New requirements for strong electronic identification – information for e-services

**Source:** NCSC-FI Security Now!  
**Link:** https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/new-requirements-strong-electronic-identification-information-e-services  
**Published:** 2023-05-24

## Summary

Finnish Transport and Communications Agency's Regulation M72B on strong electronic identification and trust services enters fully into force in summer 2023. Reformed regulation includes requirements that affect electronic service implementation.

## Key Points

- Regulation M72B entering force summer 2023
- Requirements affecting e-service implementation
- Service provider obligations
- Strong authentication mandates
