## 摘要

NCSC technical paper details privacy and security design of the NHS COVID-19 contact tracing app developed to help slow coronavirus spread.

## 事實內容

The UK National Cyber Security Centre published a technical paper on the privacy and security design of the NHS COVID-19 contact tracing app. The report:

- Details the privacy-preserving architecture of the contact tracing system
- Explains security controls protecting user data and preventing abuse
- Addresses cryptographic protocols used to maintain anonymity
- Provides transparency on government handling of pandemic response technology

The app was designed to balance public health effectiveness with privacy protection, using Bluetooth-based proximity detection without centralized tracking of user locations. The NCSC's analysis helped ensure the app met security and privacy standards appropriate for sensitive health data.

## 受影響系統

- NHS COVID-19 contact tracing app
- User mobile devices (iOS and Android)
- Backend health data systems
