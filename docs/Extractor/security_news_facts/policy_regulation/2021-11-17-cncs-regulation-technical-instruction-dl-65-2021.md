## 摘要
CNCS approved a draft regulation establishing the Technical Instruction for implementing Decree-Law No. 65/2021, covering permanent contact points, security officers, asset inventories, annual reports, and incident notifications. The regulation was submitted for 30-day public consultation.

## 事實內容
The CNCS (Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança) approved the draft regulation configuring the Technical Instruction related to:

- **Permanent contact points** for security responsibilities
- **Security officer** designations
- **Asset inventories**
- **Annual reports**
- **Incident notification obligations** to the National Cybersecurity Authority

The regulation applies to:
- Public administration entities
- Critical infrastructure operators
- Essential service operators
- Digital service providers

**Public consultation**: Comments must be submitted in writing in Portuguese to dsrc@cncs.gov.pt within 30 working days of publication in the Diário da República. After consultation closes, CNCS will publish a report on all contributions received and its final decision.

This Technical Instruction implements compliance obligations established in Decree-Law No. 65/2021 of July 30, which defines the legal framework for cyberspace security in Portugal.

## 受影響系統
- Portuguese public administration
- Critical infrastructure operators
- Essential service operators
- Digital service providers
