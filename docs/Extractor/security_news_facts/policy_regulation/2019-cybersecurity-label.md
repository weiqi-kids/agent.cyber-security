## 摘要

Finland launches first European Cybersecurity label for smart devices based on EN 303 645.

## 事實內容

Traficom launched a Cybersecurity label, making Finland the first European country to certify safe smart devices. The label guarantees consumers that labeled devices have basic information security features. The Cybersecurity label can be awarded to networking smart devices meeting certification criteria based on EN 303 645. Traficom aims to raise consumer awareness of information security and safe use of connected devices.

## 受影響系統

- Smart IoT devices
- Networking consumer devices
