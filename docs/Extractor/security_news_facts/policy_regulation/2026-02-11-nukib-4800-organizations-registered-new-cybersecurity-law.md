## 摘要

More than 4,800 organizations registered with NUKIB (National Cyber and Information Security Agency) by the end of 2025 under the new Czech cybersecurity law that entered into force on 1 November 2025, transposing NIS2 requirements.

## 事實內容

- The new Czech Cybersecurity Act and related regulations entered into force on 1 November 2025
- Organizations meeting the criteria in the regulated services decree were required to register with NUKIB by end of 2025
- Over 4,800 organizations completed registration, aided by active communication efforts from NUKIB
- NUKIB proactively engaged regulated entities to encourage compliance and facilitate the registration process

## 受影響系統

- Organizations subject to Czech NIS2-transposition cybersecurity obligations
- Essential and important entities across critical sectors in Czech Republic
