## 摘要

NCSC-FI highlights cybercrime prevention award, EU funding guidance, and EU Cyber Resilience Act status update.

## 事實內容

This weekly review covers multiple regulatory and policy developments:
- Award recognition for cybercrime prevention cooperation led by Traficom
- New guidance on applying for EU cybersecurity funding
- Final "Cyber sector in transition" webinar of 2025
- Current status update on the EU's Cyber Resilience Act implementation
- Announcement of public awareness event "Be vigilant on the web!"
- Malware review featuring analysis of Waledac malware

The review emphasizes both regulatory compliance and public education initiatives.

## 受影響系統

- Organizations seeking EU funding
- Entities subject to EU Cyber Resilience Act
- Public awareness programs
