## 摘要

ENISA announced 26 experts for its 2025-2027 Advisory Group, including three Portuguese specialists, two of whom are women.

## 事實內容

The European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) published the names of 26 experts selected to form part of its Advisory Group for the 2025-2027 mandate. Three Portuguese experts were included in the list. Notably, two of the three national nominations are women. The experts were chosen by the ENISA Management Board to advise on cybersecurity matters across various domains relevant to EU cybersecurity policy and implementation.
