## 摘要

CNCS released the Cybersecurity Capability Assessment Framework, defining three capability levels (Initial, Intermediate, Advanced) for each QNRCS measure to help organizations achieve the five cybersecurity objectives.

## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) published the Quadro de Avaliação de Capacidades de Cibersegurança (Cybersecurity Capability Assessment Framework). For each cybersecurity measure within the National Cybersecurity Reference Framework (QNRCS), the document defines three capability levels — Initial, Intermediate, and Advanced — enabling organizations to fulfill the five cybersecurity objectives: identify, protect, detect, respond, and recover, adapted to their context and size. The applicable capability levels depend on the organization's specific characteristics, and the document is sufficiently flexible for different contexts. For example, an organization with only 5 employees may not need a fully market-standard security policy or a formal documented personnel recruitment procedure.
