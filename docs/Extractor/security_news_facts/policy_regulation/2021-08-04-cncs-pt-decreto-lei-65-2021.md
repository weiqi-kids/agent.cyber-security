## 摘要

Portugal officially publishes Decreto-Lei 65/2021 of July 30, which regulates the Legal Regime of Cyberspace Security (Lei 46/2018) transposing EU NIS Directive and establishes CNCS as the National Cybersecurity Certification Authority.

## 事實內容

Portugal published Decreto-Lei 65/2021 of July 30, a significant regulatory step for cybersecurity in the country. Key provisions include:

1. **Regulatory framework**: Regulates complementary legislation aspects referred to in Lei n.º 46/2018 of August 13, which approved the legal regime of cyberspace security and transposed EU Directive 2016/1148 (NIS Directive) into Portuguese law.

2. **Scope of application**: Establishes security requirements for networks and information systems and incident notification obligations for entities covered by the NIS Directive, including:
   - Public Administration entities
   - Critical infrastructure operators
   - Essential services operators in energy, transport, banking, financial market infrastructure, health, drinking water supply/distribution, and digital infrastructure sectors
   - Digital service providers

3. **CNCS as National Certification Authority**: The decree-law designates CNCS (Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança) as the National Cybersecurity Certification Authority (Autoridade Nacional de Certificação da Cibersegurança), implementing EU Regulation 2019/881 (EU Cybersecurity Act) at the national level.

4. **National Cybersecurity Certification Framework**: Establishes the institutional framework for producing national cybersecurity certification schemes, including a scheme relating to conformity with the National Reference Framework on Cybersecurity (QNRC).

The decree was approved on June 17 and officially published on July 30, 2021.

## 受影響系統

- Public Administration entities in Portugal
- Operators of critical infrastructure in Portugal
- Essential services operators (energy, transport, banking, health, water, digital infrastructure)
- Digital service providers operating in Portugal
