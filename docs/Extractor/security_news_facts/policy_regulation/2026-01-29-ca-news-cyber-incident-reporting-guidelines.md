## 摘要

Canada's Cyber Centre publishes updated cyber incident reporting guidelines outlining key information sharing requirements for organizations, aligning with evolving regulatory expectations.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security published ITSM.00.140, a management guidance document on cyber incident reporting guidelines covering key information sharing requirements. This guidance supports organizations in meeting their obligations under Canadian cybersecurity legislation and frameworks.

Key elements of the guidelines:
- **What constitutes a reportable incident**: The guidance defines thresholds and criteria for when cyber incidents must be reported to government authorities
- **Information to include in reports**: Organizations must provide incident timeline, affected systems, nature of compromise, estimated impact, and containment measures taken
- **Reporting timelines**: Guidance specifies notification windows depending on incident severity and sector
- **Recipients of reports**: The Cyber Centre, sector-specific regulators, and law enforcement where applicable
- **Benefits of reporting**: Timely reporting enables the government to provide assistance, issue national warnings, and track threat actor campaigns
- **Voluntary vs. mandatory reporting**: Distinguishes between voluntary sharing (encouraged for all) and mandatory reporting obligations for designated critical infrastructure operators

The document is part of the Management series and is targeted at security officers, executives, and compliance teams in organizations of all sizes.

## 受影響系統

- All organizations operating in Canada
- Designated critical infrastructure operators
- Federal government departments and agencies
