## 摘要

The European Commission launched a public consultation until September 29 to develop a comprehensive anti-cyberbullying strategy.

## 事實內容

A public consultation running until September 29 is seeking contributions for the European Commission's next action plan against cyberbullying. The initiative aims to create a comprehensive, inclusive and effective strategy to combat cyber harassment across the European Union, with particular focus on minors and a gender dimension. The consultation addresses the growing vulnerability of young people to online harassment and seeks to develop coordinated EU-wide approaches to prevention and response.

Original language: Portuguese (CNCS Portugal)

## 受影響系統

- Digital platforms and social media
- Youth protection frameworks
