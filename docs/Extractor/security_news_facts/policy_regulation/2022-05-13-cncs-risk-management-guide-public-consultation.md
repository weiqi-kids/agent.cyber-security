## 摘要
CNCS published a draft Risk Management Guide for Information Security and Cybersecurity for public consultation until June 14, 2022. The guide defines a reference methodology for periodic risk analysis, evaluation, and treatment aligned with Decree-Law No. 65/2021 Article 10.

## 事實內容
The CNCS published the draft "Guia para Gestão de Riscos" (Risk Management Guide) in Information Security and Cybersecurity for public feedback:

**Guide objectives**:
- Define a systematic and coherent reference approach for periodic risk analysis, evaluation, and treatment
- Assess how risks relate to the provision of goods or services

**Legal basis**: Implements compliance with Article 10 of Decree-Law No. 65/2021 (July 30), requiring covered entities to:
- Analyze risks for all assets supporting network and information system operations
- Essential service operators must also cover assets supporting essential service delivery

**Benefits for implementing organizations**:
- Definition and implementation of appropriate technical and organizational security measures
- Continuous improvement of risk management maturity
- Achieving adequate security levels for acceptable risk thresholds

**Public consultation deadline**: June 14, 2022
**Contribution method**: Email to drsc@cncs.gov.pt

## 受影響系統
- Organizations covered by DL 65/2021
- Critical infrastructure operators
- Essential service operators
- Digital service providers
