## 摘要

CARNET, NCSC-HR, and National CERT launched an awareness campaign on cyber incident reporting obligations under Croatia's Cybersecurity Act, promoting the PiXi reporting platform.

## 事實內容

CARNET, in cooperation with the National Cybersecurity Centre (NCSC-HR) and the National CERT, launched an informational campaign to raise awareness about obligations arising from the Cybersecurity Act and the importance of reporting incidents via the PiXi platform. Cyber attacks have become a daily threat to organizations in both the public and private sectors, with consequences including financial impacts. The campaign aims to increase compliance with mandatory incident reporting requirements.

## 受影響系統

- Organizations in Croatian public sector
- Organizations in Croatian private sector
