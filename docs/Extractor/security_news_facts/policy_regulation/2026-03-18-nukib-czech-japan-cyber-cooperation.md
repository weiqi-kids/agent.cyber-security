## 摘要
The Czech Republic and Japan signed a memorandum of understanding on cybersecurity cooperation, strengthening bilateral ties in cyber defense and information sharing between the two nations.

## 事實內容
The Czech Republic and Japan formalized their cybersecurity partnership by signing a memorandum of cooperation in the field of cybersecurity. The agreement, involving NUKIB (the Czech National Cyber and Information Security Agency) on the Czech side, aims to enhance bilateral cooperation on cyber threat intelligence sharing, capacity building, and joint responses to cyber incidents. This agreement reflects the growing importance of international cybersecurity partnerships among democratic nations and aligns with broader efforts to build resilient cyber defense networks across like-minded countries. The signing marks a significant step in deepening Czech-Japanese relations in the digital security domain.

## 受影響系統
- National cybersecurity agencies of Czech Republic and Japan
- Critical infrastructure and government networks in both countries
