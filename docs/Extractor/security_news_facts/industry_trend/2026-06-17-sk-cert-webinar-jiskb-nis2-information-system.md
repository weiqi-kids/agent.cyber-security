## 摘要

SK-CERT announces two free webinars on practical use of JISKB (Unified Cyber Security Information System) in connection with NIS2 requirements implementation.

## 事實內容

The Slovak National Cyber Security Centre (NCKB) NBÚ / SK-CERT is organizing two free 90-minute online webinars focused on the practical use of JISKB — the Unified Information System for Cyber Security (Jednotný informačný systém kybernetickej bezpečnosti). The webinars are designed to help organizations comply with NIS2 requirements being introduced in Slovakia. Both sessions cover the same content, allowing participants to choose a convenient time. The webinars provide an overview of working with the system and include a Q&A session.

## 受影響系統

- JISKB (Jednotný informačný systém kybernetickej bezpečnosti)
- NIS2-regulated organizations in Slovakia
