## 摘要

Canadian Cyber Centre assesses Iranian cyber threat to Canada as low-to-moderate, with elevated risk from Israel-Iran conflict spillover effects.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security issued a threat bulletin assessing Iranian cyber threats to Canada in the context of Israel-Iran geopolitical tensions.

**Threat Level Assessment:**

**Direct Threat: Low to Moderate**
Canada is "very unlikely" to be a priority target for Iranian cyber operations. However, elevated risk exists as potential collateral damage from attacks primarily aimed at the United States.

**Interconnectivity Risk:**
Canada faces heightened exposure due to "North American interconnectivity in key critical infrastructure sectors," where targeting US infrastructure could inadvertently affect Canadian systems sharing the same networks or service providers.

**Potential Targets in Canada:**

**Critical Infrastructure:**
- Water treatment and distribution systems
- Energy generation and transmission
- Transportation networks and logistics
- Other essential services with US interconnections

**Political and Social Targets:**
- Political activists advocating for Iranian political change
- Journalists reporting on Iran
- Individuals or organizations supporting regime change in Iran
- Iranian diaspora communities in Canada

**Iranian Attack Methods:**

**Sophisticated Social Engineering:**
Advanced tactics to manipulate targets into revealing credentials or installing malware.

**Spear-Phishing:**
Targeted email campaigns against specific individuals or organizations, often impersonating trusted entities.

**Vulnerability Exploitation:**
Leveraging known Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures (CVEs) in unpatched systems.

**Disruptive Tactics:**
- Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks
- Ransomware deployment
- Data destruction or manipulation

**Recommended Actions for Canadian Organizations:**

**Authentication:**
Implement multi-factor authentication (MFA) across all systems, particularly for critical infrastructure and high-value targets.

**Patch Management:**
Promptly patch operating systems and applications to eliminate known vulnerabilities that Iranian actors might exploit.

**Edge Security:**
Secure edge devices and public-facing websites against exploitation and DDoS attacks.

**Monitoring:**
Monitor for suspicious activity targeting critical infrastructure, particularly unusual authentication attempts, network probing, or reconnaissance.

**Incident Reporting:**
Contact the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security at contact@cyber.gc.ca with concerns or suspected Iranian cyber activity.

**Strategic Context:**
The assessment reflects heightened geopolitical tensions and the potential for cyber conflict spillover into allied nations sharing infrastructure and networks with primary targets.

## 受影響系統

- Canadian critical infrastructure (water, energy, transportation)
- Organizations with US infrastructure interconnections
- Political activists and journalists
- Iranian diaspora communities in Canada
- Public-facing websites and edge devices
