## 摘要
A study on Cybersecurity Education in Primary and Secondary Schools in Portugal was published by the University of Porto for the CNCS Observatory, analyzing the current state and gaps in cybersecurity awareness and education at school level.

## 事實內容
The University of Porto conducted a comprehensive study on cybersecurity education in the Portuguese primary and secondary school system, commissioned by the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory. The study examines how cybersecurity topics are taught, student and teacher awareness levels, available resources, and recommendations for strengthening cybersecurity education in the national curriculum.

## 受影響系統
- Portuguese primary and secondary school education system
- Students and teachers engaging with cybersecurity topics
