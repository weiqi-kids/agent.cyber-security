## 摘要

CISA releases guidance on barriers to securing legacy OT protocols, identifying challenges in adoption of authentication technologies by asset owners and operators.

## 事實內容

CISA released guidance titled "Barriers to Secure OT Communication: Why Johnny Can't Authenticate", which highlights known issues with insecure-by-design legacy industrial protocols and examines why technology to secure these protocols is not widely adopted.

The guidance was developed in partnership with OT equipment manufacturers and standard development organizations, by interviewing OT asset owners and operators to understand:
1. What motivates owners and operators to secure communication
2. What barriers prevent successful adoption from design through deployment and operations

Key findings:
- Legacy OT protocols lack strong protections against data alteration, device impersonation, and unauthorized access
- This makes critical infrastructure vulnerable to cyber threats
- Securing these protocols requires solutions practical for current operators as well as cyber experts

CISA provides recommendations for:
- Owners and operators to avoid negative experiences of their peers
- OT manufacturers to drive sustainable, more usable capabilities
