## 摘要

Estonia tested Eesti äpp and "Ole valmis!" app emergency alert functionality on December 4; three test alerts reached 150,000+ devices; temporary overload was quickly resolved.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Rescue Board tested the emergency alert functionality of the Eesti äpp and "Ole valmis!" mobile applications on December 4, 2025, in cooperation with RIA, Naiskodukaitse (Women's Voluntary Defence Organisation), and SMIT. The test ran from 10:00 to 12:00, during which three test notifications were sent to Eesti äpp users. The notifications went out as planned and reached over 150,000 devices. A temporary overload that occurred was resolved quickly. The successful test demonstrated the system's capability to handle large-scale emergency notifications.

## 受影響系統

- Eesti äpp
- "Ole valmis!" mobile application
- Emergency alert notification infrastructure
