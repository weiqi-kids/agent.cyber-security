## 摘要

SK-CERT announces KYBER2026 conference on April 27-28, 2026, focusing on NIS2 directive, Cyber Resilience Act, and Slovakia's cybersecurity future.

## 事實內容

The National Security Authority (NBÚ) of Slovakia is organizing the KYBER2026 conference to be held on April 27-28, 2026, at Hotel SITNO. The event will focus on key cybersecurity regulations and Slovakia's cybersecurity posture.

**Conference Details:**
- **Date:** April 27-28, 2026
- **Venue:** Hotel SITNO, Congress Hall K1
- **Time:** 9:00 - 16:00
- **Opening:** Roman Konečný, Director of NBÚ

**Key Topics:**
- NIS2 directive implementation
- Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)
- National cybersecurity report
- Future of cybersecurity resilience in Slovakia

**Program Schedule (Day 1 - April 27):**
- 09:00-09:05: Event opening by NBÚ Director
- 09:05-10:20: Cybersecurity report presentation
- Additional sessions throughout the day

The conference aims to bring together cybersecurity professionals, policymakers, and stakeholders to discuss regulatory compliance and strategic cybersecurity initiatives.

## 受影響系統

- Slovak critical infrastructure
- Organizations subject to NIS2 directive
- Cybersecurity policy framework
