## 摘要

RIA is organizing the third annual CyberWizards girls' cybersecurity summer camp in Kehtna, Estonia, August 11-16, with 87 girls aged 13-16 registered from 10 different countries.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) is organizing the third annual international cybersecurity summer camp for girls, CyberWizards, from August 11-16 in Kehtna, Raplamaa, Estonia. The camp's goal is to spark interest in technology and cybersecurity among young people. This year, 87 girls aged 13-16 from 10 different countries have registered for the camp.

- Event: CyberWizards (3rd edition, announcement)
- Dates: August 11-16, 2025
- Location: Kehtna, Raplamaa, Estonia
- Registered participants: 87 girls, ages 13-16, from 10 countries
- Organizer: RIA (Estonian Information System Authority)
