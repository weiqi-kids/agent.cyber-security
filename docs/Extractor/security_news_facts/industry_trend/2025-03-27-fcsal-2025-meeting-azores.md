## 摘要

Third meeting of the Local Administration Cybersecurity Forum (FCSAL), and first of 2025, held in Ribeira Grande, Azores, coordinated by CNCS.

## 事實內容

The third meeting of the Forum for Cybersecurity in Local Administration (FCSAL — "Fórum de Ciberseguran&ccedil;a da Administra&ccedil;ão Local") took place on 27 March 2025 in Ribeira Grande, Azores. This is the first FCSAL meeting of 2025. The forum is a community coordinated by CNCS (Centro Nacional de Ciberseguran&ccedil;a), established in the context of an earlier cybersecurity exercise, to promote cybersecurity capacity and collaboration among local government administrations across Portugal, including the autonomous regions.

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese local government administrations
- Azores regional government entities
