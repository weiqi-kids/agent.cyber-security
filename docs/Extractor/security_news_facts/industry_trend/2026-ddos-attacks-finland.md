# Tens of thousands of DDoS attacks in Finland annually

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/tens-thousands-denial-service-attacks-occur-finland-every-year-numbers-increasing  
**Published**: 2022-05-02

## Summary

Denial-of-service attacks are everyday occurrences with over 10,000 attacks reported annually in Finland alone. Organizations need to prepare for attacks as part of everyday operations and risk management.

## Key Points

- **Volume**: Over 10,000 DDoS attacks reported annually in Finland
- **Trend**: Numbers are increasing
- **Frequency**: Everyday occurrences
- **Recommendation**: Organizations must prepare as part of routine risk management
- **Approach**: DDoS preparedness should be integrated into everyday operations

## Impact

The high volume and increasing frequency of DDoS attacks demonstrates that availability threats are a persistent operational concern, not exceptional events. Organizations must build resilience into their infrastructure.
