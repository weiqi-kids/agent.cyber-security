# New guideline helps ransomware victims

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/new-guideline-helps-organisations-who-have-become-victim-ransomware  
**Published**: 2022-06-20

## Summary

NCSC-FI released a new guideline providing advice for organizations facing ransomware threats. The risk of becoming a ransomware victim has increased significantly in recent years.

## Key Points

- **Increased risk**: Ransomware threat has grown significantly
- **New resource**: Official guidance for ransomware incident response
- **Target audience**: Organizations facing ransomware situations
- **Content**: Practical advice for handling ransomware incidents

## Impact

Organizations now have official guidance for responding to ransomware incidents, helping them make informed decisions during critical situations when operations are threatened.
