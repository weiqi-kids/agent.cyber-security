## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security awareness guidance on building an incident response plan covering detection, response, and recovery processes for cyber threats, natural disasters, and unplanned outages.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security released awareness guidance document ITSAP.40.003 on developing an incident response plan (IRP). An IRP includes the processes, procedures, and documentation related to how an organization detects, responds to, and recovers from specific incidents. Cyber threats, natural disasters, and unplanned outages are examples of incidents that can impact networks, systems, and devices. The guidance covers the key components of an IRP including incident classification, roles and responsibilities, escalation procedures, communication plans, evidence collection, remediation steps, and post-incident reviews. With a proper plan, organizations can be prepared to handle incidents when they happen, mitigate threats, and limit the associated costs and disruptions. The document was released as part of a coordinated series on business continuity and resilience.

## 受影響系統

- Incident response team processes
- Communication and notification systems
