## 摘要

NCC-FI year-end review of 2024 highlights completion of first EU-funded project, EUR 1.5 million support to SMEs, EU funding training, and events organised during the year.

## 事實內容

The National Coordination Centre (NCC-FI) of Finland published its year-end review for 2024, marking the completion of the first EU-funded project. Key activities and achievements:

- **First EU-funded project completed**: 2024 marked the conclusion of NCC-FI's first EU-funded project
- **Financial support**: EUR 1.5 million in financial support provided to micro and small and medium-sized enterprises
- **Purpose**: Implementation of state-of-the-art information security solutions
- **Training**: Provided training on preparing applications for EU funding
- **Events**: Organised cybersecurity events throughout the year
- The NCC-FI described 2024 as an eventful year for the national coordination centre
