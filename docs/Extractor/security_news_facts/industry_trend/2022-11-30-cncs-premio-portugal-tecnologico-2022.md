## 摘要

Portugal's CNCS received the Sector award at the 16th edition of "Melhores & As Maiores do Portugal Tecnológico 2022" prizes, awarded by Exame Informática and VISÃO, recognising its work promoting digital literacy.

## 事實內容

- **Award**: Sector prize at the 16th edition of "Os Melhores & As Maiores do Portugal Tecnológico 2022".
- **Awarded by**: Exame Informática and VISÃO (Portuguese tech and media publications).
- **Recognition**: Highlights personalities, innovations, brands, and organisations distinguished in Portugal in science and technology.
- **Reason**: CNCS was recognised primarily for its work promoting digital literacy among citizens, schools, companies, and public entities.
