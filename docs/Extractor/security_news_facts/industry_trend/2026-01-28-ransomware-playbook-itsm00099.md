## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security releases ITSM.00.099 management playbook to help organizations reduce ransomware risk, lessen attack impact, and take preventative actions.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security released management guidance document ITSM.00.099, the Ransomware Playbook. This publication is intended to inform organizations and help them reduce the risks of ransomware attacks, lessen the impact of these attacks, and take preventative actions. As a management series document, it is targeted at organizational leadership and IT managers. The playbook provides structured guidance on ransomware prevention, detection, response, and recovery. It was released as part of a coordinated series of ransomware-related publications alongside the Ransomware Threat Outlook 2025-2027 and ITSAP.00.099.

## 受影響系統

- Organizational IT infrastructure and management processes
- Incident response teams
- Backup and recovery systems
