## 摘要
Canadian and Latvian cyber experts conducted a four-day Threat Hunting Workshop in Riga in November 2025, training NATO allies in proactive threat identification.

## 事實內容
In November 2025, cybersecurity experts from Canada and Latvia led a comprehensive four-day Threat Hunting Workshop in Riga, Latvia. The workshop was designed to transform NATO allies into proactive defenders with the capability to identify cyber threats before they materialize into incidents. This collaborative training initiative strengthens the collective cyber defense capabilities of NATO member states.
