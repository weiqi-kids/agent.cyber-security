## 摘要

Global cyberattack activity remained at historically high levels in February 2026; Finnish organizations saw 945 attacks per week, down 7% year-over-year.

## 事實內容

Global cyberattack activity was at historically high levels during February 2026. Finnish organizations experienced an average of 945 attacks per week, which represents a 7% decrease compared to the same period one year earlier. Despite the decline in Finland, the overall global trend remains at record-high levels, indicating persistent and widespread cyber threat activity worldwide. The data reflects ongoing pressure on organizations across all sectors from various threat actors.
