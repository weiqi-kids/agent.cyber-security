## 摘要

CNCS launches new phase of C-Academy with e-learning courses on the NAU platform, expanding advanced cybersecurity training to more professionals across Portugal's public and private sectors.

## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) has launched a new phase of the C-Academy, which now incorporates e-learning format training offerings. This expansion responds to the growing need for cybersecurity qualification and promotes the development of specialized competencies in cybersecurity.

The new approach broadens training access to more professionals in both the public and private sectors, addressing the increasing demands in cybersecurity.

**First available course**: An introductory course on the role of "Responsável de Cibersegurança" (Cybersecurity Officer) — a function mandated under the new Cybersecurity Legal Framework (Decreto-Lei n.º 125/2025) — is now available on the NAU platform. The course was developed in partnership with relevant stakeholders.

## 受影響系統

- Cybersecurity professionals in Portugal (public and private sector)
- Designated Cybersecurity Officers under Decreto-Lei n.º 125/2025
