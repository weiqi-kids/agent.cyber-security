## 摘要

UK NCSC second annual report on Active Cyber Defence programme improving UK cyber ecosystem security.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre (UK) has published the second report examining how the NCSC's Active Cyber Defence (ACD) programme is improving the security of the UK public sector and the wider UK cyber ecosystem. The report:

- Documents achievements and expansion of ACD initiatives during the second year
- Analyzes the impact of protective DNS, email security, and web check services
- Provides metrics on threats blocked and vulnerabilities remediated
- Shares case studies of successful defensive interventions
- Outlines plans for future ACD programme development

The ACD programme represents a shift toward proactive, automated defenses that reduce cyber risk at national scale.
