## 摘要

Princeton CITP publishes technical briefing on device fingerprinting for regulators, highlighting shift from third-party cookies to fingerprinting techniques following Google's December 2024 policy reversal.

## 事實內容

The Center for Information Technology Policy (CITP) at Princeton University published a technical briefing explaining device fingerprinting - a tracking technique that identifies users by observing device characteristics rather than storing cookies.

**Fingerprinting Techniques:**
- Screen resolution
- Installed fonts
- Graphics card behavior
- Audio processing quirks
- Combined signals produce user identifier that cannot be easily cleared or changed

**Historical Context:**
- Browser fingerprinting first detected by Jonathan Mayer at CITP in 2009
- Technique has gained renewed attention since Google's December 2024 policy shift

**Google's Policy Reversal:**
- Google previously called fingerprinting "a subversion of user choice"
- December 2024: Google reversed longstanding ban on fingerprinting in advertising products
- Shift driven by third-party cookie restrictions from browsers and platform privacy controls

**Industry Trend:**
As third-party cookies face restrictions, the tracking industry has increasingly adopted fingerprinting as an alternative identification method.

## 受影響系統

- Web browsers
- Online advertising platforms
- Google advertising products
- Digital marketing infrastructure
