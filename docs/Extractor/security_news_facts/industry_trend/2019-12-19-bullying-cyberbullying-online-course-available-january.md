## Summary

CNCS announces MOOC on bullying and cyberbullying prevention, targeting educators, parents, and professionals in educational contexts.

## Factual Content

The objective is to raise participants' awareness of bullying and cyberbullying issues, providing opportunity to deepen knowledge and develop skills enabling them to identify, prevent, and intervene against these behaviors. The MOOC is structured in four modules covering: bullying, cyberbullying and communication through screens, internet safety and cyberbullying, and intervention approaches to bullying and cyberbullying. The course, which fits within the proposals of the "School Without Bullying. School Without Violence" national plan, is aimed at teachers, educators, parents/guardians, psychologists, security forces, other professionals in educational contexts, and all interested in this topic. Completion certificate is available upon finishing all modules.
