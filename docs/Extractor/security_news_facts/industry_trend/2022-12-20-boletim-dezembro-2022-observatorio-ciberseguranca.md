## 摘要

CNCS Observatory's December 2022 bulletin focuses on Artificial Intelligence in cybersecurity: its use by threat actors and by security professionals, regulatory considerations, and security-by-design principles.

## 事實內容

- **Publication**: CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory Bulletin — December 2022 edition.
- **Main theme**: Artificial Intelligence (AI) in the context of cybersecurity.
- **Topics covered**:
  - Use of AI by threat actors (offensive applications)
  - Use of AI by cybersecurity professionals (defensive applications)
  - Importance of AI regulation in the cybersecurity context
  - Security by design concepts
