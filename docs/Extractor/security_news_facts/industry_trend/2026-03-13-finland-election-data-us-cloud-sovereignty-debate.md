## 摘要

Finland is reconsidering whether to migrate parliamentary election data to US cloud provider AWS, amid concerns about digital sovereignty following Donald Trump's second presidency and heightened distrust of American tech giants in Europe.

## 事實內容

- Finland's Ministry of Justice is considering whether to move 2027 parliamentary election data to AWS cloud infrastructure
- Data at stake includes voting locations, candidates, and voter roll information
- The system is used for election result counting (voters still use paper ballots; individual votes cannot be linked to voters)
- AWS's nearest data centers are located in Sweden
- Trump's second presidential term has increased European skepticism toward American digital giants
- Finland's Ministry of Justice is reconsidering the migration decision
- The debate reflects broader European concerns about cloud sovereignty and dependency on US technology providers

## 受影響系統

- Finnish parliamentary election infrastructure
- Government digital services and cloud procurement decisions
