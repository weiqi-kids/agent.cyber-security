## 摘要

Companies have been selected for Estonia's Cyber Accelerator program, run by RIA in cooperation with Technopol Startup Incubator and the European Cybersecurity Competence Centre (ECCC), supporting cybersecurity startups.

## 事實內容

Companies have been selected for the Cyber Accelerator program run by the Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) in cooperation with Technopol Startup Incubator and the European Cybersecurity Competence Centre (ECCC). The program supports cybersecurity startup companies in developing new products and services. The selected companies are working on solutions that will shape the future of cybersecurity. This initiative represents Estonia's commitment to fostering cybersecurity innovation through public-private and European partnerships.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian cybersecurity startup ecosystem
- European Cybersecurity Competence Centre (ECCC) programs
