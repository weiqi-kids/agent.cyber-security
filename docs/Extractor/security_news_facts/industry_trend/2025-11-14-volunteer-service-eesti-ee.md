## 摘要

New volunteer service on eesti.ee consolidates internal security volunteer opportunities, simplifying registration and providing overview of roles and training.

## 事實內容

A new volunteer service has been launched on Estonia's state portal eesti.ee, consolidating all internal security sector volunteer work opportunities in one place.

Features:
- Centralized platform for volunteer opportunities
- Clear overview of different roles
- Joining requirements and conditions
- Training opportunities information
- Focus on crisis resilience and internal security

This service aims to increase volunteer participation in Estonia's crisis preparedness and resilience efforts by making the process more transparent and accessible.
