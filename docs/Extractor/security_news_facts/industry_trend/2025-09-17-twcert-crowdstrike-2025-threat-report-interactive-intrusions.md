## 摘要

CrowdStrike's latest threat report finds interactive intrusions rose 27% year-over-year (Jul 2024–Jun 2025), with over 80% involving fileless malware, and significant increases in cloud and voice phishing attacks.

## 事實內容

TWCERT/CC summarized key findings from CrowdStrike's latest threat intelligence report covering the period July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2025.

Key statistics and trends:
- **Interactive intrusions**: Increased 27% year-over-year — these attacks involve threat actors actively interacting with compromised environments rather than using automated tools
- **Fileless malware dominance**: Over 80% of cases involved fileless malware techniques, demonstrating the limitations of traditional file-based detection methods
- **Living-off-the-land (LOTL)**: Threat actors increasingly abuse legitimate system tools (PowerShell, WMI, PsExec) to evade endpoint detection
- **Cloud targeting**: Significant increase in attacks targeting cloud infrastructure and cloud-hosted services
- **Voice phishing (vishing)**: Notable increase in telephone-based social engineering attacks targeting IT help desks to gain initial access
- **Attacker dwell time**: Despite increased sophistication, median dwell times continue to decrease due to improved detection capabilities
- **Prominent threat actors**: Nation-state actors from China, Russia, Iran, and North Korea, as well as sophisticated criminal groups, remain most active
- **Industry targeting**: Technology, telecommunications, finance, and government sectors among most heavily targeted

The report underscores the need for behavioral-based detection, zero-trust architecture, and human-centric security training.

## 受影響系統

- Enterprise IT environments (all sectors)
- Cloud infrastructure and SaaS platforms
- IT help desks and identity management systems
