## 摘要

October 2025 cyber landscape remained cloudy and chilly, slightly calmer than September.

## 事實內容

The Cyber Weather report for October 2025 indicates:
- Cybersecurity landscape continued the "cloudy and chilly autumn season"
- Overall situation calmed slightly compared with September
- Threat level remained elevated but showed improvement

The "Cyber Weather" is NCSC-FI's monthly threat assessment using weather metaphors to communicate the cybersecurity situation in Finland.

## 受影響系統

- General IT infrastructure in Finland
- All sectors affected by the overall threat landscape
