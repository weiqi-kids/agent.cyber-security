## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) announced the closure of registrations for the 2nd edition of the General Cyber-Hygiene Course (Curso Geral de Ciber-higiene). The course is a short online course composed of two modules:

1. **"Ciber(in)segurança"** — Covering cybersecurity risks and the concept of cyber insecurity
2. **"Ciber-higiene e Boas Práticas de Cibersegurança"** — Covering cyber hygiene and cybersecurity best practices

The total duration of the course is approximately two hours. The course is designed to address potential risks in internet use and provide actionable best practices for the general public and professionals.
