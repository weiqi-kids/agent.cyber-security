## 摘要
NUKIB reports 32 cyber incidents in January 2026, highest in 12 months, with DDoS attacks accounting for nearly half.

## 事實內容
The National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NÚKIB) of the Czech Republic published its cyber incident overview for January 2026, revealing a significant increase in cyber security incidents.

**Key Findings:**
- **Total Incidents:** 32 (highest in past 12 months)
- **Increase:** Significant rise compared to December 2025
- **Primary Attack Vector:** DDoS attacks (nearly 50% of incidents)
- **Significant Incidents:** Only 1 classified as significant (same as December)

**Analysis:**
The surge in incidents was primarily driven by a wave of Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks, which accounted for almost half of all recorded incidents. Despite the increased volume, the number of significant incidents remained low, indicating that most attacks were lower in impact or successfully mitigated.

This data suggests an evolving threat landscape with increased attack volume but maintained defensive capabilities.
