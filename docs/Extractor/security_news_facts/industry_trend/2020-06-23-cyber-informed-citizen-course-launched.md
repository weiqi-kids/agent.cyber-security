## Summary

CNCS launches second online course in partnership with LUSA, teaching citizens to identify fake news and verify online information.

## Factual Content

The "Cyber-Informed Citizen" course aims to provide skills helping participants better understand the term "fake news" and related concepts, through tips enabling them to identify the veracity of news or other information published online. This is the second online course developed by CNCS, this time in partnership with LUSA news agency. The course targets all citizens who consult online information and aims not only to alert to dangers of uncritical information consumption, but also to share knowledge helping citizens verify if information they consult online is true. Learning objectives include understanding what fake news is, how it emerged and spreads; understanding the importance of combating fake news and how to do it; verifying the veracity of news or online information; and understanding why we should trust journalism and not stop consuming online information. The course totals about 3 hours, and upon completion of all modules, participants can download a completion certificate (requiring 75% correct answers).
