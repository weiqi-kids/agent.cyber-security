## 摘要

CERT.br announces 14th Brazilian CSIRTs Forum and 7th MISP Workshop scheduled for July 28-30, 2026 in São Paulo.

## 事實內容

The Brazilian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT.br) has announced the dates for two major cybersecurity events in 2026:

- 14th Brazilian CSIRTs Forum
- 7th MISP Workshop

**Event Details:**
- Date: July 28-30, 2026
- Location: AMCHAM, Rua da Paz, 1431, Chácara Santo Antônio, São Paulo/SP, Brazil

Call for presentations is now open for both events. These conferences bring together Computer Security Incident Response Teams (CSIRTs) and cybersecurity professionals to share knowledge, best practices, and discuss the MISP (Malware Information Sharing Platform) threat intelligence platform.
