## 摘要

Canadian Cyber Centre warns ransomware will remain significant threat to Canada through 2027, with attackers adopting AI and cryptocurrency for faster operations.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security released its Ransomware Threat Outlook 2025-2027, warning that "ransomware attacks will remain a significant threat to Canada in the next 2 years."

**Key Findings:**

**Universal Threat:**
All Canadian organizations face risk from opportunistic, financially-motivated threat actors regardless of size or sector.

**Evolving Threat Landscape:**
- Attackers adopting artificial intelligence for faster operations
- Use of cryptocurrency for harder-to-detect transactions
- Continuous refinement of extortion methods
- Sophisticated target selection to maximize financial returns
- Operations becoming faster, cheaper, and harder to detect

**Scale of Threat:**
"Ransomware is big business" - acknowledgment of the sophisticated criminal ecosystem targeting Canadian infrastructure and enterprises.

**2024 Impact Statistics:**
- Cyber Centre issued 336 pre-ransomware notifications
- Over 300 Canadian organizations warned
- Prevented estimated losses exceeding $18 million through early threat warnings

**Primary Defense Measures (Three Foundational Protections):**
1. Regular software updates
2. Multi-factor authentication
3. Vigilance against phishing attempts

These basic practices significantly strengthen organizational cyber resilience.

**Collaboration Imperative:**
Effective ransomware mitigation requires cooperation among:
- Law enforcement agencies
- Government agencies
- Private sector organizations
- The public

**Outlook Period:** 2025-2027 (2-year threat assessment)

The report emphasizes that while threats are evolving with new technologies (AI, cryptocurrency), fundamental security practices remain critical for defense.

## 受影響系統

- All Canadian organizations (universal threat)
- Critical infrastructure
- Private sector enterprises
- Government systems
- Small and medium businesses
