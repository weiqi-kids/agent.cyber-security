## 摘要

International partnering agencies, including the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security, published joint guidance on creating and maintaining a definitive view of operational technology (OT) architecture, emphasizing the importance of cybersecurity in OT environments.

## 事實內容

- Developed with contributions from multiple international partnering agencies
- Part of a series of publications on cybersecurity in operational technology (OT)
- Focuses on creating and maintaining a comprehensive, definitive view of OT architecture
- Aims to highlight the importance of cyber security practices in industrial and operational environments
- Guidance covers methods to document, monitor, and update OT architecture inventories
- Assists organizations in understanding dependencies and potential attack surfaces in OT systems

## 受影響系統

- Industrial control systems (ICS)
- Operational technology (OT) environments
- Critical infrastructure operators
- Organizations with SCADA or industrial network environments
