## 摘要

ENISA invites potential sponsors to support Team Europe's participation in the 2nd International Cybersecurity Challenge (ICC), to be hosted by CISA in the USA in late July 2023.

## 事實內容

ENISA, together with other regional and international organizations, launched the International Cybersecurity Challenge (ICC) initiative. The first edition was held in June 2022, aiming to attract young talent and raise global awareness about cybersecurity education and skills. Teams competed in web application exploitation, cryptography, reverse engineering, hardware challenges, and attack/defense challenges.

The success of the first edition led to a 2nd edition, organized by CISA in the United States in late July 2023. ENISA is inviting potential sponsors interested in supporting Team Europe, with sponsorship options including training for the team, media sponsorship, and coverage of travel and accommodation expenses. Interested parties can contact CNCS at cncs@cncs.gov.pt.
