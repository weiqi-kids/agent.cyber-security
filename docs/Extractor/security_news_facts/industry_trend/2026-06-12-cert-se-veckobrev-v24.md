## 摘要

CERT-SE will merge into Sweden's National Cybersecurity Centre on 1 July 2026; subscribers must re-consent to continue receiving newsletters.

## 事實內容

CERT-SE announced that as of 1 July 2026 it will become part of Nationellt cybersäkerhetscenter (National Cybersecurity Centre) as part of efforts to strengthen Sweden's cyber resilience. As a result, CERT-SE must obtain fresh consent for continued processing of personal data. Subscribers will need to provide their consent to keep receiving CERT-SE publications; instructions will be published on cert.se from 1 July 2026.
