## 摘要

NCSC report analyzes cyber incident trends in the UK from October 2018 to April 2019, providing guidance on defense and recovery strategies.

## 事實內容

The UK National Cyber Security Centre published an incident trends report covering the period from October 2018 to April 2019. The report:

- Analyzes patterns and trends in cyber incidents handled by the NCSC
- Identifies common attack vectors and threat actor behaviors
- Provides statistical analysis of incident types and affected sectors
- Offers guidance on defending against and recovering from common incidents

This report gives organizations insight into the real-world threat landscape based on actual incidents handled by the NCSC, helping them prioritize security investments and response capabilities based on empirical data rather than hypothetical threats.

## 受影響系統

- UK organizations across all sectors
- Government and critical national infrastructure
- Business networks and systems
