## 摘要

CNCS (Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança, Portugal) announced the 12th edition of C-DAYS 2026 Call for Presentations and Workshops, inviting the cybersecurity community to submit innovative cases, technologies, and experiences.

## 事實內容

- This is the 12th edition of C-DAYS, Portugal's national cybersecurity community event.
- C-DAYS promotes knowledge sharing among community members as a pillar for a more cyber-secure future.
- The 2026 edition opened submissions for presentations and workshops from the broader community.
- Topics include innovative visions, relevant case studies, impactful experiences, ideas, projects, and emerging technologies related to cybersecurity.
- Organized by CNCS (Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança), the Portuguese national cybersecurity authority.
- Announcement date: 2026-04-22.
