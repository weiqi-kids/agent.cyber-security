## 摘要

Portugal's CNCS released a video series (23 modules) complementing the Minimum Cybersecurity Capabilities Roadmap to help organizations improve their technical, human, and process security capabilities.

## 事實內容

The National Cybersecurity Centre (CNCS) published a set of videos organized into 23 modules as a supplementary support tool for organizations developing minimum cybersecurity capabilities. The video series complements the "Roadmap for Minimum Cybersecurity Capabilities" and is associated with the "National Cybersecurity Reference Framework." The content presents recommendations for organizations to sustainably and coherently improve their technical, human, and process capabilities in cybersecurity.

## 受影響系統
