## Factual Content

Taiwan Computer Emergency Response Team / Coordination Center (TWCERT/CC) held the "2025 Taiwan Cybersecurity Incident Response Annual Conference" on December 3rd. This year's conference used the theme "Building Secure Products, Connecting Trust Defense Lines," inviting cybersecurity experts from ASUS, CyCraft, III, Delta Electronics, Zyxel, Synology, Panasonic Taiwan, and other industry, government, and academic sectors to gather and focus on AI-driven security threats, domestic notification coordination mechanism achievements and challenges, and practical experiences in product security governance and PSIRT vulnerability reporting, jointly exploring how to strengthen cross-domain collaboration and enterprise cybersecurity resilience.

Minister Lin Yi-ching of the Ministry of Digital Affairs stated in his opening remarks that facing the rapid popularization of AI, IoT, and smart manufacturing, once products go online, they carry not only functionality and performance but also user data and brand trust. Product security is no longer just a technical issue but an important foundation of enterprise competitiveness. He pointed out that secure design and secure development processes are necessary basics for enterprises, and increasing international regulations treat product security as part of supply chain governance, representing that "security" has become a common language in global markets.

Administrator Tsai Fu-lung of the Administration for Cyber Security also expressed that the global cybersecurity situation in 2025 remains severe, with APT, ransomware attacks, and new threats brought by generative AI making it difficult for single organizations to respond effectively alone. This conference's theme "Building Secure Products, Connecting Trust Defense Lines" echoes the global cybersecurity governance trend from "protection" to "trust." He emphasized that only by making "security" the core of design and management in products, services, and even entire supply chains can market and social trust be won.

The conference specially invited multiple cybersecurity experts to deliver keynote speeches, including speakers from ASUS, CyCraft, Institute for Information Industry, and others, conducting in-depth sharing from perspectives including security trends, TWCERT/CC annual achievements, AI offense and defense, Secure-by-Design concepts, and PSIRT notification governance.
