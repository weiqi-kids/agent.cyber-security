## 摘要

CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory published Bulletin No. 2/2022 analyzing cybercrime-as-a-service threats and their role in spreading incidents and complicating attribution.

## 事實內容

The Bulletin No. 2/2022 of the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory analyzes threats arising from cybercrime-as-a-service (CaaS), a reality that has gained particular relevance and has promoted the spread of incidents, as well as increased difficulties in attributing responsibility throughout the entire cybercrime chain. As with previous editions, the bulletin also disseminates news and publications relevant to cybersecurity in Portugal.

Key themes:
- Cybercrime-as-a-service business model analysis
- Impact on incident attribution complexity
- Trends in CaaS adoption by threat actors
