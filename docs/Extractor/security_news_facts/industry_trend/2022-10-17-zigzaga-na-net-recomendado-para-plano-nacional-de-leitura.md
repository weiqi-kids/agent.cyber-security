## 摘要

Portuguese CNCS-endorsed book "Zigzaga na Net" — thirty accessible texts on safe, healthy and conscious internet use — recommended for the National Reading Plan during Digital Skills Month.

## 事實內容

- **Publication**: "Zigzaga na Net" is a book aimed at younger audiences, compiling 30 texts on safe, healthy, and conscious internet use.
- **Endorsement**: Recommended for the National Reading Plan (Plano Nacional de Leitura) by CNCS during the Month of Digital Skills (Mês das Competências Digitais).
- **Context**: The Month of Digital Skills was launched by Portugal's government under the digitalisation and administrative modernisation area, within the INCoDe.2030 initiative.
- **Goal**: Promote digital literacy competencies among young people in Portugal.

## 受影響系統

- General public / youth in Portugal (digital literacy target audience)
