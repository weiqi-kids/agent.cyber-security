## Summary
Estonia's eesti.ee portal launches "My Pension" information service providing comprehensive overview of pension accumulation and usage.

## Facts Content
**Portal**: eesti.ee (National portal)
**Service**: "Minu pension" (My pension)
**Launch**: November 2024

**Service Provides**:
- Comprehensive pension accumulation information
- Guidance on pension usage
- Personal financial planning tools
- Calculation of savings/investment needs for retirement

**Purpose**: Enable informed personal financial planning for retirement

**Philosophy Statement**: "Money and pension literacy is as necessary as writing and arithmetic. Smart choices made today help better plan personal financial resources and provide clarity on retirement funding."
