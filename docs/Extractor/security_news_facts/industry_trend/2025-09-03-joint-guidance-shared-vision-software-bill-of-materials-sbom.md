## 摘要

International cybersecurity agencies, including the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security, published joint guidance promoting a shared vision for Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) to improve software supply chain security and transparency.

## 事實內容

- Joint guidance developed by international cybersecurity agencies
- Focuses on Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) — a formal record of software components and dependencies
- Aims to inform software producers, purchasers, and operators of the benefits of SBOM
- Covers SBOM generation, analysis, and sharing integration into security processes
- SBOM helps organizations understand and manage software supply chain risks
- Guidance encourages adoption of SBOM standards (e.g., SPDX, CycloneDX) across the software lifecycle
- Relevant for both public sector and private industry software development and procurement

## 受影響系統

- Software developers and vendors
- Organizations procuring third-party software
- Supply chain security stakeholders
- Critical infrastructure operators relying on software components
