## 摘要

Fifth edition of Portugal's National Cybersecurity Exercise (ExNCS'24) tested resilience of the European energy sector.

## 事實內容

The fifth edition of the National Cybersecurity Exercise (ExNCS'24), organized by the Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS), took place on June 19 and 20. This exercise was conducted as part of the seventh edition of CyberEurope, one of the largest cybersecurity exercises in Europe—a biennial initiative by ENISA—which tested the resilience of the European energy sector at multiple levels: technical, operational, and strategic. The exercise brought together participants from EU member states to simulate and respond to coordinated cyberattacks targeting critical energy infrastructure. Portugal's positive participation demonstrated the country's growing cybersecurity preparedness and interoperability with European partners.
