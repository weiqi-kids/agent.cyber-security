## 摘要

CERT.br publishes freely-licensed presentation slides on ransomware protection, detection, and response best practices under Creative Commons license.

## 事實內容

The Brazilian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT.br) has released a comprehensive slide deck covering best practices for ransomware protection, detection, and response. The materials are licensed under Creative Commons, allowing free use by anyone who wishes to present on ransomware security topics.

**Key Features:**
- Focus areas: Protection, Detection, and Response to ransomware attacks
- Free to use and distribute
- Suitable for training and awareness presentations
- Part of CERT.br's public education initiative

The slides are available at: https://cert.br/docs/ransomware/#divulgacao

This resource supports the broader cybersecurity community in educating organizations and individuals about ransomware threats and mitigation strategies.
