## 摘要
Q2 2024 saw threats and incidents reduce by only 3% compared to Q2 2023, but remained 5% higher than the last three quarters of 2023, with Latvia demonstrating strong cyber resilience.

## 事實內容
In the second quarter of 2024, the number of threats and incidents decreased by only 3% compared to the same period in 2023. However, the threat level remained 5% higher than the average of the last three quarters of 2023. Despite the elevated threat landscape, Latvia has demonstrated convincing cyber resilience, with cyber attacks recorded during this period not having significant or lasting impacts on society. This indicates effective incident response and mitigation capabilities.
