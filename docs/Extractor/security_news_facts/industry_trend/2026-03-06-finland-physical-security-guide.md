## Summary

Finland's National Emergency Supply Agency (HVK) published a comprehensive guide for developing organizations' physical security, targeting critical infrastructure operators.

## Factual Content

The Finnish National Emergency Supply Agency (Huoltovarmuuskeskus, HVK) published a new guide on physical security for organizations. Key details:

- The guide provides a comprehensive and practical resource for developing organizational physical security.
- It is primarily targeted at critical infrastructure ("supply security-critical") enterprises but offers useful information for all organizations.
- The guide covers physical security planning and various protection methods.
- The aim is to support organizations in developing physical security in a changing operational environment.
- Physical security is identified as a central part of organizational security management.

## Affected Systems

- Critical infrastructure organizations in Finland
- General organizational physical security
