# Vulnerabilities identified and fixed in municipalities' data networks

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/awareness-vulnerabilities-digital-services-more-important-ever-vulnerabilities-identified-and  
**Published**: 2022-05-02

## Summary

Vulnerabilities in digital services of municipalities and healthcare/social welfare operators were identified in an assessment project by the Digital and Population Data Services Agency (DVV) and Traficom's NCSC-FI. The project provided timely vulnerability information enabling necessary fixes.

## Key Points

- **Partnership**: DVV and NCSC-FI joint assessment project
- **Scope**: Municipalities and healthcare/social welfare operators
- **Outcome**: Vulnerabilities identified and operators notified
- **Result**: Organizations able to carry out necessary fixes
- **Impact**: Improved security of critical public services

## Impact

This proactive vulnerability assessment demonstrates effective government cooperation to secure critical public sector infrastructure. Timely notification enabled remediation before exploitation.
