NCSC-FI weekly review covering AI assistant security, phishing scams, and funding opportunities for SMEs.

This week's review from the National Cyber Security Centre Finland covers several cybersecurity topics:
- Information security considerations related to the use of AI assistants
- Phishing messages themed around hotel and travel bookings
- Recorded scam calls made in the name of banks
- Unsolicited verification codes sent by text message
- Funding call opening for SMEs
- Publication of the December Cyber Weather report

These topics reflect current trends in cybersecurity awareness and threat landscape in Finland.
