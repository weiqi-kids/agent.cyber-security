## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) published the fresh 2025 cybersecurity yearbook, providing a comprehensive overview of developments, threats, and lessons learned in Estonian and international cyber space over the past year. The yearbook highlights new records in cyber incidents while also documenting recurring old mistakes. This annual publication serves as a key reference document for Estonian cybersecurity policy and practice, offering insights into the evolving threat landscape and the effectiveness of cyber defense measures implemented throughout 2025.
