## 摘要

CERT.LV 完成第七次威脅獵捕行動，與加拿大、歐盟、波蘭及拉脫維亞武裝部隊等多方盟友協作一個月。

## 事實內容

CERT.LV concluded an intensive month-long seventh Threat Hunting Surge in collaboration with the Canadian Armed Forces (CAF), the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (CCCS), CERT.EU, Polish Cyber Command, the Latvian National Armed Forces, and the National Guard Cyber Defense and Electromagnetic Warfare Battalion. The surge represents a sustained multinational effort to proactively hunt for cyber threats in allied digital environments, building on prior Threat Hunting Surge operations and expanding allied participation.
