CNCS published the first Cybersecurity in Portugal report focused on the Economics theme, studying the economic dimension of cybersecurity in Portuguese enterprises, particularly SMEs.

## 事實內容

The first Cybersecurity in Portugal report (theme: Economics) by the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory studies the economic dimension of cybersecurity, focusing especially on the cybersecurity challenges in Portuguese businesses.

Key dimensions covered:
- Supply side: dimensions and characterization of the cybersecurity sector in Portugal
- Demand side: characterization and analysis of exposure levels, and cybersecurity policies, practices, and protocols in Portuguese companies, especially small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs)
- Methodology: comprehensive desk research, secondary data analysis from multiple sources, primary data from surveys conducted by the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory

The report provides insights into the state of cybersecurity investment and maturity among Portuguese enterprises.
