## 摘要

CERT.br opens registration for 2025 cybersecurity training courses on CSIRTs, incident management, and advanced incident handling.

## 事實內容

CERT.br has opened registration for its 2025 training courses. Available courses include:

- **Overview of Creating and Managing CSIRTs**: Course on establishing and operating Computer Security Incident Response Teams
- **Foundations of Incident Management**: Core practices in cybersecurity incident management
- **Advanced Topics in Incident Handling**: In-depth techniques for handling complex security incidents

Registration is open at https://cursos.cert.br/inscricao/

Note: Original language: Portuguese (CERT.br). The September 2024 announcement previewed the 2025 schedule; this item represents the formal opening of registrations in January 2025.
