## 摘要
Hacker alliance "Scattered LapSus Hunters" reportedly formed by three known groups including Scattered Spider, LapSus, and ShinyHunters.

## 事實內容
Alliance "Scattered LapSus Hunters" suspected to be composed of three known hacker groups gaining attention. Scattered Spider uses advanced social engineering targeting financial, retail, aviation sectors. LapSus targets large tech companies using internal credentials. ShinyHunters active since 2020 with multiple large-scale data leaks. All three commonly use social engineering including phishing, SIM swapping, and account compromise. Third-party supply chain security highlighted as critical after ShinyHunters attacked cloud service provider partner in August 2025.

## 受影響系統
- Enterprise systems across financial, retail, aviation, and supply chain sectors
- Third-party service providers and supply chain partners
