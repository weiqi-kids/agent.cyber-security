## 摘要

SK-CERT announces Kyber2025 NIS2 conference on June 9, 2025, at Wellness Hotel Chopok to discuss current cybersecurity developments and future challenges.

## 事實內容

The National Security Authority (NBÚ) organized the Kyber2025 NIS2 conference on June 9, 2025, bringing together cybersecurity professionals and stakeholders.

**Conference Details:**
- **Date:** June 9, 2025
- **Time:** 9:00 - 16:00
- **Venue:** Wellness Hotel Chopok, Praslička Hall

**Preliminary Program:**
- **09:00-09:05:** Event opening
  - Speaker: Roman Konečný, Director of NBÚ
- **09:05-10:00:** Cybersecurity report presentation
- Additional sessions throughout the day

**Theme:**
"What is happening and what awaits us?" - focusing on current cybersecurity landscape and future trends

**Purpose:**
- Review current state of cybersecurity in Slovakia
- Discuss upcoming challenges and regulatory changes
- Foster dialogue between government, industry, and security professionals
- Address NIS2 directive implementation

The conference is part of Slovakia's ongoing effort to strengthen national cybersecurity posture and regulatory compliance.

## 受影響系統

- Slovak critical infrastructure
- Organizations under NIS2 scope
- National cybersecurity framework
