## 摘要
SK-CERT announces Kyber2025 NIS2 conference on June 9, 2025, covering Slovak cybersecurity landscape and future trends.

## 事實內容
The National Cyber Security Centre (SK-CERT) of Slovakia has announced the Kyber2025 conference focusing on current cybersecurity developments and future challenges.

**Event Details:**
- **Title:** Kyber2025 - What's Happening and What's Coming?
- **Date:** June 9, 2025
- **Time:** 09:00 - 16:00
- **Venue:** Wellness Hotel Chopok, Praslička Hall

**Preliminary Program:**

**09:00 - 09:05** Opening
- Roman Konečný, Director of NBÚ (National Security Authority)

**09:05 - 10:00** Cybersecurity Report
- Current state assessment
- Key incidents and trends
- Future outlook

**Conference Focus:**
- NIS2 directive implementation in Slovakia
- Current cybersecurity landscape
- Emerging threats and trends
- National cybersecurity strategy
- Public-private cooperation

**Target Audience:**
- Cybersecurity professionals
- Essential service operators
- NIS2 regulated entities
- Government officials
- Security researchers

This represents a major Slovak national cybersecurity event providing strategic overview and policy guidance for the cybersecurity community.
