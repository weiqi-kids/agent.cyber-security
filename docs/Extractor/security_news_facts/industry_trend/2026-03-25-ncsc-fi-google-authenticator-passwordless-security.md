## 摘要

## 事實內容

Palo Alto Networks Unit 42 published Part 2 of their series examining passkey adoption from a security perspective, focusing on Google Authenticator and passwordless authentication:

Key analysis points:
- Passwordless authentication is often presented as the "end of account takeover," but real-world implementations present new attack vectors
- Attackers do not break protocols in theory — they target the most common implementations
- The research examines Google Authenticator specifically, including synced passkeys and their implementation within the Google ecosystem
- The aim is to help defenders understand the technology and anticipate how new attack vectors could emerge in a passwordless environment

Defensive products mentioned:
- Cortex Cloud Identity Security
- CyberArk Identity Protection
- Unit 42 Cloud Security Assessment

This is Part 2; Part 1 was titled "The Art of the Invisible Key – Passkey Global Breakthrough."
