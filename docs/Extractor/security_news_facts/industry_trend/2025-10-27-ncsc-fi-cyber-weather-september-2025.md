## 摘要

NCSC-FI September Cyber Weather report indicates rainy conditions with increased incidents continuing from late summer.

## 事實內容

The monthly Cyber Weather report characterizes September 2025 as bringing "rain fronts over the cybersecurity landscape":
- Increased incidents observed at end of summer continued into September
- Overall outlook for the month was "mostly rainy"
- Elevated threat levels sustained from August
- Continued vigilance required across cybersecurity landscape

The report indicates deteriorating conditions compared to typical summer patterns.
