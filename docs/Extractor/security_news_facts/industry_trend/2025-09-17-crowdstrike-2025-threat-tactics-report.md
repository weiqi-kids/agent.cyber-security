## 摘要

CrowdStrike 2025 threat report reveals: interactive intrusion attacks up 27% YoY; 80%+ involve fileless malware; China-linked cloud attacks up 40%; vishing up 442% in H2 2024, exceeding full-year 2024 total in H1 2025.

## 事實內容

CrowdStrike's latest threat report (covering July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2025) reveals significant evolution in hacker tactics:

Key findings:
- **Interactive intrusions up 27% YoY**: Unlike automated intrusions, attackers actively interact with compromised environments after gaining access, collecting information and customizing attack strategies
- **80%+ involve fileless malware**: Traditional file-detection-based defenses increasingly challenged
- **China-linked cloud attacks up 40%**: Cloud services remain a primary target for nation-state actors
- **Vishing (voice phishing) growth**: 442% growth in H2 2024; H1 2025 has already exceeded the full-year 2024 total

These trends indicate attackers are shifting toward more stealthy, human-operated, cloud-focused, and multi-vector attack approaches that bypass traditional security controls.

## 受影響系統

- Enterprise cloud environments
- Organizations relying on traditional file-based security detection
