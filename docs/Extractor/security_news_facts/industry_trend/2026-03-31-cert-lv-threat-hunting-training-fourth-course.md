## 事實內容

On March 24, 2026, the fourth Threat Hunt Workshop led by Canadian and Latvian cyber specialists began in Riga, Latvia. The workshop brought together 37 participants from 14 countries to equip them with the skills to act proactively and detect threats before they materialize. The training is part of the ongoing CERT.LV and Canadian Armed Forces Cyber Command (CAFCYBERCOM) collaboration to build allied cyber defense capacity across NATO partner nations.
