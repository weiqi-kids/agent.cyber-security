## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security guidance on building an IT recovery plan as part of business continuity, covering unplanned outages, cyber attacks, and natural disasters.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security released awareness guidance document ITSAP.40.004 on developing an IT recovery plan. Unplanned outages, cyber attacks, and natural disasters can happen unexpectedly, causing organizations to lose information or experience downtime that disrupts or stops critical business functions. Unplanned downtime is expensive and could have a lasting impact on business operations. The document guides organizations through developing an IT recovery plan as part of their overall business continuity strategy. Key components include identifying critical IT systems and recovery time objectives, establishing backup and restore procedures, defining recovery roles and responsibilities, and testing recovery plans regularly. The guidance is released as part of a series alongside incident response and business continuity planning documents.

## 受影響系統

- Organizational IT infrastructure and critical systems
- Data backup and restore systems
- Business continuity processes
