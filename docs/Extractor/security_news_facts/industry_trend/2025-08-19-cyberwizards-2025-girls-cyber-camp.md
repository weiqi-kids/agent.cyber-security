## 事實內容

The third CyberWizards international cyber camp took place in Estonia from August 11-16, 2025. The five-day program aimed to encourage girls to discover cybersecurity and the IT world. The camp brought over 80 young participants from 10 countries to Estonia. This is an annual initiative to promote gender diversity in cybersecurity.

- Event: CyberWizards 2025 (3rd edition)
- Dates: August 11-16, 2025
- Location: Estonia
- Participants: 80+ young people from 10 countries
- Focus: Encouraging girls to explore cybersecurity and IT
