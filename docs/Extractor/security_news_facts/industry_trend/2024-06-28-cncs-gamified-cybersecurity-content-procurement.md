## 摘要

Portugal's CNCS published a simplified design competition on the AcinGov public procurement platform for developing gamified and interactive cybersecurity educational content.

## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) published a simplified design competition (concurso de conceção simplificado) on the AcinGov electronic public procurement platform. The competition aims to develop gamified and interactive content related to cybersecurity awareness and education.

The procurement is targeted at organizations and individuals interested in creating engaging, gamified digital content to promote cybersecurity literacy among the Portuguese population. This initiative is part of CNCS's broader effort to enhance national cybersecurity awareness through innovative educational approaches.

The full competition documentation was made available on the AcinGov platform (acingov.pt).
