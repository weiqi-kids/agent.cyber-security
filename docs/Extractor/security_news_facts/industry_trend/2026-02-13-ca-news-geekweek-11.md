## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security hosted GeekWeek 11, an annual collaborative research and development event that brings together cybersecurity experts from government, academia, and industry. This edition focuses on the theme of "Detection and Deception."

Key aspects of GeekWeek 11:
- GeekWeek provides a forum for cross-sector collaboration to advance cybersecurity research and share expertise
- The "Detection and Deception" theme reflects growing interest in honeypot deployment, threat deception technologies, and improving intrusion detection capabilities
- Participants work on real-world cyber challenges and develop practical tools and techniques for national cyber defence
- The event fosters knowledge transfer between the Cyber Centre's technical experts and partner organizations
- Outcomes typically include improved detection methodologies, shared indicators of compromise, and collaborative research publications

GeekWeek is a recurring initiative that demonstrates Canada's commitment to advancing cybersecurity capabilities through collaborative research.
