## Summary

CNCS celebrates Girls in ICT anniversary with six interviews under the motto "You can't be what you can't see," addressing gender disparity in ICT sector.

## Factual Content

To adapt traditional Girls in ICT celebrations to the current context, this year CNCS commemorates this anniversary with the launch of six interviews under the motto "You can't be what you can't see." Each interviewee will have a word and epigraph setting the tone and theme of the questions. The first interview will be launched today and the remaining will be published daily (working days) until April 29.

Girls in ICT is an international event with the main goal of inspiring future generations to bridge the critical gender disparity in the sector. Reducing this disparity necessarily involves raising awareness and informing women about opportunities in the ICT educational and professional sector. This awareness and consciousness work, to be effective, should be broadly articulated through various communities constituting the sector.
