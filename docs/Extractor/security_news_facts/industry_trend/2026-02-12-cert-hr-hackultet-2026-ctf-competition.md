## 摘要
Registration is open for Hackultet 2026, the third edition of a CTF (Capture The Flag) cybersecurity competition for students organized by CARNET (Croatia's Academic and Research Network) and FER (Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing, University of Zagreb).

## 事實內容
- **活動名稱**: Hackultet 2026 (3rd edition)
- **類型**: CTF (Capture The Flag) cybersecurity competition
- **目標受眾**: Students
- **主辦單位**: CARNET (Croatian Academic and Research Network) and FER (Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing, University of Zagreb)
- **資助**: Part of CARNET's e-Sveučilište project funded by EU's National Recovery and Resilience Plan
- **目的**: Raise awareness about the importance of cybersecurity practices
