## 摘要

Mārtiņa-CTF 2025 (Nov 21-23) is a 48-hour team-based cybersecurity CTF competition open to Latvia and international participants.

## 事實內容

The third edition of Mārtiņa-CTF, organized by volunteer nonprofit "Datoriķi," returns November 21-23, 2025:

**Event Format:**
- 48-hour team-based Capture The Flag (CTF) competition
- Online phase: Nov 21 17:00 - Nov 23 17:00 EET
- In-person challenges: Nov 23 in Riga at H2O6

**Target Participants:**
- Open to participants from Latvia and beyond
- No skill level restrictions

**Challenge Categories:**
- Cybersecurity challenges
- Programming puzzles
- Technical problem-solving
- Teams can complete challenges in any order

**Registration & Communication:**
- Website: mctf.datoriki.org
- Teams register and join Discord server
- Opening ceremony: YouTube livestream Nov 21 at 16:00 (Q&A and event details)

**Organizer:**
Datoriķi, a volunteer-led nonprofit community focused on tech education in Latvia.

## 受影響系統

- Cybersecurity skills development
- CTF training infrastructure
