NCSC-FI promotes network-based approach to cyber crisis management across organizational boundaries.

## 事實內容

Even with good documentation, cyber security crisis operations models are often incomplete in practice. Operations models transcend organizational borders within partner networks. Deputy Director-General Kalle Luukkainen hopes more top executives from companies would join collaborative networks to address cyber challenges together.
