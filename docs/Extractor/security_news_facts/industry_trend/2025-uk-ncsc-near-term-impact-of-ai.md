## 摘要

UK NCSC assessment analyzing AI's impact on cyber operation efficacy over the next two years.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre (UK) has published an assessment focusing on how artificial intelligence will impact the effectiveness of cyber operations and the implications for the cyber threat landscape over the next two years. The assessment:

- Analyzes near-term AI applications in offensive cyber operations
- Evaluates how AI enhances reconnaissance, social engineering, and attack automation
- Examines defensive applications of AI and machine learning
- Forecasts changes in threat actor capabilities with AI adoption
- Provides guidance for security teams preparing for AI-enhanced threats

This near-term analysis complements longer-range forecasts and helps organizations prioritize immediate defensive investments and capability development.
