## 摘要
The 5th edition of the Risks and Conflicts Report was published by the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory, analyzing data on cyber threats and geopolitical conflicts affecting Portugal and the broader European cybersecurity landscape.

## 事實內容
CNCS Observatory published its 5th edition of the annual Risks and Conflicts Report (Relatório Riscos & Conflitos), providing comprehensive analysis of cyber threat trends, conflict dynamics in cyberspace, and their implications for national and organizational security. The report draws on incident data, threat intelligence and geopolitical analysis to present the Portuguese cybersecurity context.
