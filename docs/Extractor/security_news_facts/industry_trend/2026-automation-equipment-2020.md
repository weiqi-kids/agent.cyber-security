# 1,000 unprotected automation equipment in Finnish networks (2020)

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/approximately-1000-automation-equipment-remain-unprotected-finnish-networks  
**Published**: 2021-02-02

## Summary

NCSC-FI's 2020 mapping detected approximately 1,000 unprotected automation equipment online, consistent with previous years' figures. Equipment includes automation control systems, display panels, and building management systems.

## Key Points

- **Survey date**: 2020
- **Findings**: ~1,000 unprotected automation equipment
- **Trend**: Consistent with previous years
- **Equipment types**: Automation control systems, display panels, building management
- **Risk**: Internet-exposed critical systems
- **Purpose**: Improve situational awareness and cyber security in Finland

## Impact

The persistent presence of exposed industrial systems represents ongoing security debt. Annual surveys track progress (or lack thereof) in securing critical infrastructure.
