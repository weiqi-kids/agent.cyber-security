## Summary

CERT.br opens registration for 2026 cybersecurity training courses until January 15, offering three professional development programs for CSIRT teams.

## Factual Content

The Brazilian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT.br) announced the opening of registration for its 2026 professional training program. Registration closes at 17:00 on January 15, 2026.

**Available Courses:**

1. **Overview of Creating and Managing CSIRTs** - Foundational course for establishing and operating Computer Security Incident Response Teams
2. **Foundations of Incident Management (FIM)** - Core principles and practices for incident response
3. **Advanced Topics in Incident Handling (ATIH)** - Advanced techniques for experienced incident responders

These courses support capacity building for cybersecurity professionals and CSIRT teams across Brazil and Latin America, contributing to regional cybersecurity resilience.

**Registration Link:** https://cursos.cert.br/inscricao/
