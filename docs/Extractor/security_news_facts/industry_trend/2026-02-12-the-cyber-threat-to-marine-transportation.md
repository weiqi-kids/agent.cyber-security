## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security threat assessment on cyber risks to marine transportation sector, covering cybercriminal, state-sponsored, and non-state actor threats as digitalization expands the attack surface.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security released a cyber threat assessment focused on Canada's marine transportation critical infrastructure sector. Key judgements highlight that digitalization is expanding the sector's threat surface through increased connectivity of operational technology (OT), navigation systems, and port management platforms. The report identifies three primary threat categories: cybercriminals seeking financial gain through ransomware and extortion targeting ports and shipping companies; state-sponsored cyber actors conducting espionage and pre-positioning for potential disruption of maritime supply chains; and non-state cyber threat actors including hacktivists. The assessment notes that disruption of marine transportation could have cascading effects on Canadian supply chains and the broader economy. The report is intended for leaders in the marine transportation sector, cyber security professionals with maritime infrastructure to protect, and the general public. The outlook section provides forward-looking analysis of emerging threats.

## 受影響系統

- Marine transportation operational technology (OT) systems
- Port management and logistics platforms
- Navigation and vessel tracking systems
- Canadian maritime critical infrastructure
- Supply chain and logistics networks
