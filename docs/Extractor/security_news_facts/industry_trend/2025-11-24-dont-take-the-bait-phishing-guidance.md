## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security guidance publication (ITSAP.00.101) helping users recognize and avoid phishing attacks through awareness and best practice recommendations.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security updated or published the phishing awareness guidance document ITSAP.00.101:

- Provides guidance on how to recognize phishing attacks across various channels (email, SMS, voice)
- Covers techniques attackers use to deceive victims into revealing credentials or installing malware
- Offers practical steps for individuals and organizations to avoid falling victim to phishing
- Part of the ITSAP (IT Security Awareness Publication) series targeting general audiences
- Addresses common phishing indicators such as suspicious senders, urgent language, and malicious links
- Recommends multi-factor authentication (MFA) as a key defense against credential theft via phishing

The publication is intended for a broad audience including employees, organizations, and general internet users.

## 受影響系統

- Email systems
- End users (all platforms)
- Organizations relying on credential-based authentication
