## 事實內容
In collaboration with CERT.LV, the Latvian Ministry of Defence, and the Canadian Armed Forces Cyber Forces (CAF), a specialized training course has been created to advance threat-hunting capabilities. The course integrates international expertise and best practices to provide participants with a robust foundation for effective threat detection. This development represents a joint effort to enhance cybersecurity skills and capabilities through international partnership.
