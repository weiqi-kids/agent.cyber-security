## 摘要

CyberChess 2025 (Oct 29-30) is a major Baltic cybersecurity conference with three parallel YouTube streams covering strategic, technical, and field topics.

## 事實內容

CyberChess, a major Baltic cybersecurity event running for over 10 years, returns October 29-30, 2025:

**Format:**
- Virtual event with live streaming only
- Three parallel YouTube streams each day:
  - Omega: Strategic session (#CyberChess)
  - Alfa: Technical session (#CyberShock)
  - Beta: Field experience session (#CyberStory)

**Audience:**
- Technical experts
- Political leaders
- Service providers
- Industry representatives

**Topics:**
- Latest trends in cybersecurity
- Innovations in the field
- Strategic and technical discussions

**Organizers:**
- CERT.LV
- Latvia's Ministry of Defence
- National Cybersecurity Centre of Latvia

**Sponsors/Partners:**
- ESET
- Cloudflare
- Palo Alto Networks
- Multiple other cybersecurity organizations
