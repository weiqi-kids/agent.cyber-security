## 摘要

Czech NUKIB records 32 cybersecurity incidents in January 2026, highest in 12 months, driven by DDoS attacks.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber and Information Security Agency of the Czech Republic (NÚKIB) has published its cybersecurity incident overview for January 2026, revealing a significant increase in recorded incidents.

**Key Statistics:**

**Incident Count:**
- January 2026: 32 incidents (highest in past 12 months)
- December 2025: Lower than January 2026
- Trend: Significant upward spike

**Incident Composition:**
- DDoS attacks comprised nearly half (approximately 50%) of recorded incidents
- DDoS wave represents primary driver of incident increase
- Diverse attack types beyond DDoS also observed

**Severity Assessment:**
- Only 1 incident classified as "significant" (same as December)
- Most incidents categorized as lower severity
- Severity classification remained stable despite volume increase

**Threat Analysis:**
The surge in DDoS attacks suggests:
- Coordinated campaign targeting Czech organizations
- Accessibility of DDoS-as-a-service platforms
- Potential geopolitical motivations (Czech Republic is NATO/EU member)
- Seasonal patterns in cyber attack activity

While the volume increased substantially, the fact that only one incident was deemed "significant" indicates that Czech organizations' defensive measures are largely effective at mitigating impact, even as attack frequency rises.

## 受影響系統

- Czech Republic government and private sector organizations
- Critical infrastructure and regulated entities
