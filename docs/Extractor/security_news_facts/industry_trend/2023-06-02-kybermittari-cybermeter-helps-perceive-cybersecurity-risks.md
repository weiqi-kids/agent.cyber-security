## 摘要

NCSC-FI released an updated version of the Kybermittari (Cybermeter) cybersecurity self-assessment tool, with new support materials and upcoming training events scheduled for summer and autumn 2023.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre Finland (NCSC-FI) released a new version of the Kybermittari tool, developed based on user feedback. Kybermittari is a self-assessment tool that helps organisations perceive and evaluate their cybersecurity risks. The updated version, along with new support materials, was made available on the NCSC-FI website. NCSC-FI also announced Kybermittari presentation and training events to be held during summer and autumn 2023, inviting organisations to register for these sessions.
