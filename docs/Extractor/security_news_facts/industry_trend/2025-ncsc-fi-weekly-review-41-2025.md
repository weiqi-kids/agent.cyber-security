## 摘要

Importance of critical updates highlighted, BEC attacks via impersonation continue.

## 事實內容

This week's review emphasizes security best practices:
- Importance of applying critical updates promptly
- Critical vulnerabilities in devices must not be delayed unnecessarily
- Criminals regularly exploit unpatched devices to carry out data breaches
- Alert regarding organizational communications security
- Criminals often impersonate company executives via instant messages or email
- Attempts to persuade employees to transfer money under various pretexts (Business Email Compromise)

This highlights the ongoing importance of patch management and social engineering awareness.

## 受影響系統

- Unpatched devices with critical vulnerabilities
- Corporate communication systems targeted by BEC attacks
- Employee devices targeted by executive impersonation
