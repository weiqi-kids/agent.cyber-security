## 事實內容

Taiwan Computer Emergency Response Team/Coordination Center (TWCERT/CC) held the "2025 Taiwan Cybersecurity Incident Response Conference" on December 3. This year's theme was "Building Secure Products, Connecting Trust Defense Lines," bringing together cybersecurity experts from industry, government, and academia including ASUS, CyCraft, Institute for Information Industry (III), Delta Electronics, Zyxel, Synology, and Panasonic Taiwan.

**Key Speakers and Topics:**
- ASUS CISO Jin Qingbai: Security trends
- TWCERT/CC Director Sun Weizhe: Annual incident response results
- CyCraft Founder Qiu Mingzhang: AI offense and defense
- III Manager Li Wanping: Secure-by-Design principles
- III Manager Li Yanqin: PSIRT notification governance

**Opening Remarks:**
Minister of Digital Affairs Lin Yijing emphasized that product security is no longer just a technical issue but a foundation of enterprise competitiveness. With AI, IoT, and smart manufacturing rapidly proliferating, products carry not only functionality and performance but also user data and brand trust. Security design and secure development processes are essential basics for enterprises. Increasingly, international regulations view product security as part of supply chain governance, meaning "security" has become a common language in global markets.

Minister Lin recognized TWCERT/CC's efforts in year-round incident reporting, intelligence sharing, vulnerability disclosure, PSIRT promotion, and international organization participation, emphasizing that security defense requires joint maintenance by government, enterprises, and users.

Administrator of Cybersecurity Administration Tsai Fulong stated that 2025's global cybersecurity situation remains severe, with APT, ransomware attacks, and new threats from generative AI making it difficult for single organizations to effectively respond alone. This conference's theme "Building Secure Products, Connecting Trust Defense Lines" echoes the global trend of cybersecurity governance moving from "protection" to "trust." Only by making "security" the core of design and management across products, services, and entire supply chains can market and societal trust be won.

**Summit Discussion:**
Vice President Gong of the Cybersecurity Administration moderated a summit discussion inviting representatives from Zyxel, Delta Electronics, Synology, and Panasonic Taiwan Network Security Lab to jointly explore "product security equals brand trust" core values. From complete lifecycle spanning design, manufacturing to vulnerability notification, they shared how enterprises implement product security governance through action and called for industry to jointly build a safer and more trustworthy security ecosystem.
