## Summary

AP2SI publishes preliminary results of cybersecurity professionals study with 367 valid responses, covering demographics, employment, and professional factors.

## Factual Content

Preliminary results of the study conducted by AP2SI with support from CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory are now available. The study received 367 valid responses through an online, open, and anonymous survey. Questions aimed to achieve demographic characterization of respondents, gathering data such as gender, age, education level, geographic location, past and current professional situation (sector, employment data, career length, exclusivity, income, and job function), and factors considered important in professional activity. Future updates to the page will present composite results.
