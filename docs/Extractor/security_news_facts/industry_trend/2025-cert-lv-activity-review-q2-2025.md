Q2 2025 saw a significant increase in cyber threats and vulnerabilities in Latvia, with rising attack intensity, complexity, and attacker adaptability.

## 事實內容
During the second quarter of 2025, Latvia's cyberspace experienced a significant escalation in cyber threats and vulnerabilities, confirming long-established trends. The threat landscape is characterized not only by increasing intensity and complexity of attacks, but also by attackers' enhanced ability to adapt their tactics. This evolving threat environment is driving the development of more sophisticated technical security solutions. The report confirms that cyber threats continue to intensify and become more sophisticated.
