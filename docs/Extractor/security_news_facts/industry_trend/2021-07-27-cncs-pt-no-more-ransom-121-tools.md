## 事實內容

The No More Ransom project, of which the CNCS is a partner, has celebrated five years of existence. During this period, the project helped prevent almost one billion euros from reaching cybercriminal organizations. The project now aggregates 121 tools against ransomware on a single website. CNCS announced this milestone as part of its ongoing participation in the international anti-ransomware initiative. The initiative provides free decryption tools and prevention guidance to victims of ransomware attacks globally.
