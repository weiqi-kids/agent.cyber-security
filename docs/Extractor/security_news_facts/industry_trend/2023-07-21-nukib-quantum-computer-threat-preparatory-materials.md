## 摘要

NUKIB published three preparatory guidance materials explaining the nature of the quantum computing threat to current cryptographic standards and outlining necessary steps organizations must take in coming years to mitigate this future risk.

## 事實內容

- NUKIB prepared three support materials on the quantum computing threat to cybersecurity
- Quantum computers pose a potential future threat to most currently-used cryptographic algorithms
- The materials explain: the nature of the quantum threat, current state of quantum computing development, and steps needed to achieve quantum-resistant security
- Post-quantum cryptography (PQC) migration planning is the primary recommended mitigation
- The materials are intended for both technical and management audiences
- NUKIB plans to continue updating these materials as the threat landscape evolves

## 受影響系統

- All systems relying on classical cryptographic algorithms (RSA, ECC, etc.)
- Organizations needing to plan post-quantum cryptography migration
