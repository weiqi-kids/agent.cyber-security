## 事實內容

The fourth 2023 edition of the Cybersecurity Observatory Bulletin was published by Portugal's National Cybersecurity Centre (CNCS). The edition highlights cryptography as its central theme, addressing the foundational role cryptography plays in many information security technologies and its essentiality in ensuring secure communications. The bulletin is part of the Observatory's ongoing series monitoring cybersecurity trends and providing educational content for Portuguese organizations and citizens.
