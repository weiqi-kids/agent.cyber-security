## 摘要
Portugal's CNCS announces a hate speech detection research project launching May 1, 2021, focusing on the COVID-19 pandemic context. The project aims to identify potential targets of hate speech in the Portuguese context and monitor social media activity by the Portuguese community.

## 事實內容
- **計畫名稱**: Hate speech detection project (discurso de ódio)
- **啟動日期**: May 1, 2021
- **主辦單位**: CNCS Portugal (Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança)
- **重點**: Identifying hate speech in the context of COVID-19 pandemic in Portugal
- **範圍**: Monitoring Portuguese social media communities for hate speech targeting
- **資助**: Research project (funding details in full announcement)
