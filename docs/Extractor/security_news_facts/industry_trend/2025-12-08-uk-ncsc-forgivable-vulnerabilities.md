## 摘要

UK NCSC research introduces methodology to classify vulnerability classes for systematic elimination.

## 事實內容

The UK National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) has published research designed to eradicate entire vulnerability classes and make top-level mitigations easier to implement. The research introduces a method to assess vulnerabilities as either 'forgivable' or 'unforgivable'.

This classification framework aims to help organizations prioritize security efforts by:
- Identifying vulnerability classes that should be systematically eliminated (unforgivable)
- Recognizing vulnerabilities where occurrence is more understandable given technical constraints (forgivable)
- Making mitigation strategies more accessible and actionable

The research represents a shift in how the cybersecurity industry approaches vulnerability management, moving from reactive patching to proactive elimination of entire vulnerability classes.
