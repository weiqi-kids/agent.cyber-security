## 摘要

ENISA calls for participation in post-campaign survey after the European Cybersecurity Month (ECSM) to evaluate the impact of cybersecurity awareness campaigns on citizen behaviour change; survey takes 15-20 minutes.

## 事實內容

- **Event**: End of the European Cybersecurity Month (ECSM).
- **Action**: ENISA (European Union Agency for Cybersecurity) invites participation in a post-campaign survey.
- **Purpose**: Evaluate the impact of cybersecurity awareness campaigns on behaviour change among European citizens.
- **Duration**: Approximately 15-20 minutes to complete.
- **Survey link**: https://ec.europa.eu/eusurvey/runner/Cybersecurity_Awareness_ECSM-PostC-MS

## 受影響系統

- European citizens, cybersecurity awareness campaign participants
