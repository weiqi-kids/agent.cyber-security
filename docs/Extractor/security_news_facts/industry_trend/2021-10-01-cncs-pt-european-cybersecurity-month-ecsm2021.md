## 摘要

The European Cybersecurity Month (ECSM 2021) campaign begins, coordinated by ENISA and the European Commission and supported by EU member states and hundreds of partners including governments, universities, NGOs, and private sector companies.

## 事實內容

The European Cybersecurity Month (Mês Europeu da Cibersegurança, ECSM 2021) campaign officially launched on October 1, 2021. Key details:

- **Coordination**: The campaign is coordinated by the European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) and the European Commission.
- **Support**: Supported by EU Member States and hundreds of partners including governments, universities, think tanks, NGOs, professional associations, and private sector companies from across Europe and beyond.
- **Duration**: Runs throughout October.
- **Purpose**: Annual awareness campaign aimed at promoting cybersecurity among EU citizens and organizations, encouraging best practices and raising awareness of cyber threats.

CNCS announced Portugal's participation in ECSM 2021 as part of its national cybersecurity awareness efforts.
