# Tips for identifying information influence activities

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/tips-identifying-information-influence-activities-be-vigilant-and-act-responsibly  
**Published**: 2022-05-02

## Summary

Guidance on how to evaluate the reliability of information in the digital environment. With numerous operators vying for attention through various methods, users need to understand how to assess information reliability and recognize information influence activities.

## Key Points

- **Challenge**: Enormous number of daily messages from various sources
- **Key question**: How to evaluate reliability of information
- **Social media**: Guidance on assessing posts before sharing
- **Definition**: What constitutes information influence activities
- **Action**: Be vigilant and act responsibly with information

## Impact

As information operations become more sophisticated, individuals need practical skills to identify and resist manipulation. This guidance helps build societal resilience against influence operations.
