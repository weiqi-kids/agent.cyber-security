## 摘要

The Estonian Academy of Sciences Cybersecurity Commission assessed election technology risks over the past year and published results covering key security directions for Estonian election technology.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Academy of Sciences Cybersecurity Commission, working together with government agencies conducting elections over the past year, assessed risks related to Estonian election technology. The commission published its findings today, covering key security directions for Estonian election technology. This independent academic assessment of election security represents a rigorous approach to identifying and addressing potential vulnerabilities in Estonia's election infrastructure before upcoming elections, contributing to public confidence in the integrity of the electoral process.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian election technology infrastructure
- E-voting systems
- Electoral administration digital systems
