## 摘要

NCSC assessment focuses on how AI will impact cyber operation effectiveness and implications for the cyber threat landscape over the next two years.

## 事實內容

The UK National Cyber Security Centre published an assessment examining the near-term (two-year) impact of artificial intelligence on cyber threats. The report:

- Analyzes how AI will enhance the efficacy of cyber operations
- Forecasts changes in threat actor capabilities enabled by AI technology
- Assesses both offensive and defensive implications of AI adoption
- Provides guidance for organizations preparing for AI-enhanced threats

The assessment focuses on practical, near-term changes rather than long-term speculation, helping organizations understand imminent shifts in the threat landscape as AI tools become more accessible and sophisticated.

## 受影響系統

- All organizations facing cyber threats
- AI systems and platforms
- Security operations centers
- Threat intelligence systems
