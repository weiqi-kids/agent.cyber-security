## 摘要

## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) Cybersecurity Observatory published Bulletin 4/2020. The edition highlights a decrease in the number of incidents registered by CERT.PT starting from the usual vacation period. It gives particular attention to malware as a threat that is less visible than phishing but equally important. The bulletin also includes publications of interest on other cybersecurity aspects considered relevant in recent months.
