## 摘要

CNCS announced the 18th edition of its General Cybersecurity Course covering technical, organizational, and management dimensions of cybersecurity; registrations were subsequently closed.

## 事實內容

The main objective of this course is to raise awareness and promote reflection on cybersecurity among participants. Topics covered include: "Concepts and Cybersecurity Ecosystem," "Cyber Espionage," "Combating Cybercrime," "Cyber(In)security," and "Human Factor in Cybersecurity," among other relevant subjects. The course is delivered by a diverse panel of speakers from national and international cybersecurity domains. As of the publication date, registrations for the 18th edition had been closed.

## 受影響系統

- General audience: organizations and citizens interested in cybersecurity awareness (Portugal)
