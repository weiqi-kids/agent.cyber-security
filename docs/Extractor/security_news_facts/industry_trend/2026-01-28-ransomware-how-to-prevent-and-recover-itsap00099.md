## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security awareness guidance providing tips to help organizations prepare for and recover from ransomware attacks.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security released awareness guidance document ITSAP.00.099 on ransomware prevention and recovery. The publication provides tips to help organizations prepare for and recover from ransomware attacks. Ransomware is malicious software that encrypts an organization's data and demands payment for the decryption key. The guidance covers preventive measures including regular data backups, patch management, network segmentation, user awareness training, and multi-factor authentication. Recovery guidance covers incident response steps, when to involve law enforcement, and whether organizations should pay ransoms. The document is part of a series of ransomware-related publications released alongside the Ransomware Threat Outlook 2025-2027.

## 受影響系統

- Data backup systems
- Network and endpoint systems
