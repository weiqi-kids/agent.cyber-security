## 摘要

Latvia and Canada deliver refined threat hunting workshop to 30 NATO cybersecurity professionals.

## 事實內容

In February 2025, the Cyber Incident Response Institution (CERT.LV), the Latvian Ministry of Defence, and the Canadian Armed Forces Cyber Command conducted a refined and unique Threat Hunt Workshop, welcoming 30 participants from NATO nations.

**Workshop Characteristics:**

**Organizers:**
- CERT.LV (Latvia's Cyber Incident Response Institution)
- Latvian Ministry of Defence
- Canadian Armed Forces Cyber Command

**Participants:**
- 30 cybersecurity professionals from NATO nations
- Diverse backgrounds representing various NATO member states
- Mix of government and military cybersecurity specialists

**Workshop Focus:**
The workshop represents a continuation and refinement of Latvia-Canada cybersecurity cooperation, building on previous successful collaborations. The "refined" nature suggests iterative improvement based on lessons learned from earlier workshops.

**Strategic Significance:**
This workshop exemplifies NATO's collective defense approach extended to cyberspace:
- Capacity building among NATO allies
- Knowledge sharing on advanced threat hunting techniques
- Strengthening cyber defense interoperability
- Enhancing collective cybersecurity posture

The collaboration between a Baltic NATO member (Latvia) and a founding NATO member (Canada) demonstrates the alliance's commitment to building robust cyber defense capabilities across all member states, particularly those facing heightened geopolitical cyber threats.
