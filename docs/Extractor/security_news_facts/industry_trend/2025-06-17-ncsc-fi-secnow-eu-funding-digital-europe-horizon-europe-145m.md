## 摘要

European Cybersecurity Competence Centre (ECCC) opens new calls for proposals under Digital Europe and Horizon Europe Programmes with combined budget of up to EUR 145.5 million.

## 事實內容

The European Cybersecurity Competence Centre (ECCC) has opened new calls for proposals under two major EU funding programmes:

- **Digital Europe Programme (DEP)**: New cybersecurity-focused call for proposals
- **Horizon Europe (HE)**: New cybersecurity research and innovation call
- Combined budget: up to EUR 145.5 million across both programmes
- The announcement was shared by NCSC-FI to inform Finnish cybersecurity organisations and researchers of the funding opportunities
