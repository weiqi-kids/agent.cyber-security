## 摘要
Portugal's CNCS launches the "Cidadão Cibersocial" (Cyber Social Citizen) online course covering safe use of social networks, highlighting best practices and risks associated with growing social media usage.

## 事實內容
- **課程名稱**: Cidadão Cibersocial (Cyber Social Citizen)
- **發布日期**: June 18, 2021
- **主辦單位**: CNCS Portugal (Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança)
- **課程內容**:
  - Overview of major social networks and best practices
  - Risks associated with social media usage
  - Safe social media behavior guidance
- **背景**: Social media usage has grown sharply in recent years, bringing associated cybersecurity risks
- **目標受眾**: All citizens who use social networks
