## 摘要

Registration opens for Hackultet 2026, a CTF competition for students organized by CARNET and Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing.

## 事實內容

The Croatian Academic and Research Network (CARNET) and the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing at the University of Zagreb (FER) have announced that registration is now open for the third edition of **Hackultet**, a Capture The Flag (CTF) competition for students.

**Event Details**:
- **Organizers**: CARNET and FER (University of Zagreb)
- **Target Audience**: Students
- **Purpose**: To raise awareness about the importance of applying cybersecurity best practices
- **Funding**: Part of CARNET's e-University project funded by the European Union from the National Recovery and Resilience Plan

This competition provides students with hands-on experience in cybersecurity challenges and promotes cybersecurity education in Croatia.
