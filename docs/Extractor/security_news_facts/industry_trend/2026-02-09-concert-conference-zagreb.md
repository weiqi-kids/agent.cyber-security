## 事實內容

On Friday, February 6, the third **ConCERT Conference on Cybersecurity** was held at the National and University Library in Zagreb, organized by **CARNET's National CERT**.

**Conference Details**:
- **Date**: February 6, 2026
- **Location**: National and University Library, Zagreb
- **Organizer**: CARNET's National CERT

**Participants**: The conference brought together numerous experts from various fields including:
- Cybersecurity
- Digital forensics
- Police investigations
- Artificial intelligence
- Defense systems

**Program Highlights**:
- Opening session featured the presentation of **National CERT's Annual Report for 2025**
- Multiple expert presentations across different security domains

This event demonstrates Croatia's commitment to fostering collaboration and knowledge sharing within the cybersecurity community.
