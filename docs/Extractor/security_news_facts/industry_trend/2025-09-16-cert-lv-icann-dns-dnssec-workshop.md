## 摘要

CERT.LV、NIC.LV 與 ICANN 聯合舉辦免費 DNS 安全與 DNSSEC 一日密集工作坊。

## 事實內容

CERT.LV, NIC.LV, and ICANN jointly invited participants to a free one-day intensive workshop on DNS security and DNSSEC. The event aimed to provide hands-on training on Domain Name System security fundamentals and DNSSEC implementation, targeting professionals and practitioners seeking to strengthen DNS infrastructure security. The collaboration between the three organizations reflects ongoing efforts to improve foundational internet security in Latvia and the region.
