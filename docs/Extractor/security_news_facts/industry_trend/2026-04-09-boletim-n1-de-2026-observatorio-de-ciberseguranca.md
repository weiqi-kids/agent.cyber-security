## 摘要

CNCS releases the first 2026 bulletin from the Cybersecurity Observatory, featuring a renewed format with updated structure, content, and design covering cyber threats, vulnerabilities, and policy developments.

## 事實內容

The first Bulletin of 2026 from the Cybersecurity Observatory of the Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) is now available, in a renewed edition. This version introduces changes in content, structure, and graphic design.

The bulletin's objective is to periodically provide an accessible, actionable overview of the main risks associated with cyber threats and vulnerabilities in cyberspace, as well as developments in public policy and law aimed at preventing and mitigating these risks. The content is based exclusively on open sources.

## 受影響系統

- General audience (cybersecurity professionals, public sector, private sector in Portugal)
