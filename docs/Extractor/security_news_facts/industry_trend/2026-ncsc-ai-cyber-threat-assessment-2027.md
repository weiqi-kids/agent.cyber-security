## 摘要

UK NCSC評估AI對網路威脅的影響，預測2027年前的發展趨勢。

## 事實內容

The UK National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) published an assessment highlighting the impacts on cyber threat from AI developments between now and 2027. The report analyzes how artificial intelligence technologies will reshape the cyber threat landscape over the next 1-2 years.

Key findings include:
1. AI-enhanced reconnaissance and target selection by threat actors
2. Automated vulnerability discovery and exploit generation
3. Sophisticated social engineering using AI-generated content (deepfakes, phishing)
4. Defensive AI applications for threat detection and response
5. Escalating AI arms race between attackers and defenders

The assessment provides strategic guidance for organizations preparing for an AI-augmented threat environment. It emphasizes the need for both defensive AI capabilities and resilience against AI-enhanced attacks.

The NCSC recommends organizations invest in AI-aware security controls, enhance security awareness training to address AI-generated threats, and develop capabilities to detect AI-generated malicious content.
