## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security guidance on SIM card security risks including SIM swapping, cloning, and eSIM threats, with mitigation recommendations.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security released guidance document ITSAP.10.021 on security considerations for SIM (Subscriber Identity Module) cards, also referred to as Universal Integrated Circuit Chips (UICC). SIM cards store mobile network user information including phone numbers and authentication keys. The document addresses threats including SIM swapping (social engineering mobile carriers to transfer a victim's number), SIM cloning (duplicating SIM card data), and risks associated with eSIMs (embedded SIMs). Threat actors exploit SIM vulnerabilities to intercept SMS-based authentication codes, bypass multi-factor authentication, and gain unauthorized access to accounts. The guidance recommends using authenticator apps instead of SMS-based MFA, setting PINs on carrier accounts, and monitoring for unexpected loss of mobile service.

## 受影響系統

- Mobile devices with SIM cards
- SMS-based multi-factor authentication systems
- Online accounts using phone numbers for recovery
- eSIM-enabled devices
