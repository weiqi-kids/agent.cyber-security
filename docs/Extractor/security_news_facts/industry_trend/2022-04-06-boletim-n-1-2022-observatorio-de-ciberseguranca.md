## 摘要

CNCS published Bulletin No. 1/2022 analyzing threat actor identification methodologies in cyberspace and providing an overview of the most common threat actors affecting Portugal in recent years.

## 事實內容

The Bulletin No. 1/2022 (April) of the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory analyzes the problem of identifying threat actors in cyberspace and the methodologies used to overcome this difficulty.

Key topics covered:
- Challenges in cyber threat actor attribution
- Methodologies for threat actor identification in cyberspace
- Overview of the most common types of threat actors affecting Portugal in recent years, based on CNCS Observatory reports
- News and publications of relevance to Portuguese cybersecurity

The bulletin provides insights into attribution challenges and the threat landscape specific to Portugal.
