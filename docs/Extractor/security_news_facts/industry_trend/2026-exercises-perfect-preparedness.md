# Exercises perfect preparedness

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/exercises-perfect-preparedness  
**Published**: 2022-06-20

## Summary

Deputy Director-General Sauli Pahlman at the NCSC-FI at Traficom emphasizes the importance of cooperation when it comes to being prepared for cyber security incidents. Threats and disturbances concerning cyber security are currently a hot topic.

## Key Points

- Cyber security preparedness through exercises is critical
- Cooperation is essential for handling cyber security incidents
- NCSC-FI promotes organizational readiness through training and exercises

## Impact

Organizations need to regularly conduct cyber security exercises to ensure they can effectively respond to incidents. This collaborative approach helps build national cyber resilience.
