## 事實內容

The UK National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) published a comprehensive assessment of the cyber threat to UK business. The report analyzes the threat landscape facing British businesses across sectors, providing strategic insights for business leaders and security practitioners.

Key findings include:
1. Ransomware remains the most immediate and disruptive threat to UK businesses
2. Supply chain compromises increasingly target small and medium enterprises
3. State-sponsored espionage threatens intellectual property in strategic sectors
4. Business email compromise (BEC) continues to cause significant financial losses
5. Cloud misconfiguration and credential theft are common initial access vectors

The report emphasizes the business impact of cyber threats, including financial losses, operational disruption, reputational damage, and regulatory consequences. It provides practical guidance for boards and executives on cyber risk management.

The NCSC recommends businesses implement fundamental security controls, develop incident response capabilities, and engage with the NCSC's support services including the Early Warning service and Cyber Essentials scheme.
