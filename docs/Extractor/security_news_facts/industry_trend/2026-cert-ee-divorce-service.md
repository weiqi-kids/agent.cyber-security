## Summary
Estonia's eesti.ee portal enables electronic divorce application submission starting December 2024, with new information service providing comprehensive process guidance.

## Facts Content
**Portal**: eesti.ee (National portal)
**Service**: Divorce information service
**Launch**: Early December 2024

**Key Innovation**:
- Electronic divorce application submission
- No need to visit registry office in person
- Requires only internet connection

**Service Provides**:
- Comprehensive divorce process information
- Guidance on procedure steps
- Document submission requirements
- Property and child-related matters information

**Significance**:
- Modernizes family law administration
- Increases convenience and flexibility
- Reduces need for physical office visits
- Digital transformation of sensitive life events

**Notes**:
- Original language: Estonian
- Major digitalization of legal process
- Privacy-sensitive service design
- Part of comprehensive life event services
