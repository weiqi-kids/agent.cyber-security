## 摘要

NCSC-FI November Cyber Weather report indicating rainy conditions due to malware and vulnerability observations.

## 事實內容

The monthly Cyber Weather report from Finland's NCSC characterizes November 2025 as "generally rainy" from a cybersecurity perspective. While the month was relatively calm overall, the threat landscape was negatively impacted by:
- Increased malware observations
- New vulnerability discoveries and exploitations

The report provides situational awareness for organizations and citizens about the general cybersecurity climate.

## 受影響系統

- Systems exposed to malware campaigns
- Unpatched systems with known vulnerabilities
