## 摘要

CNCS partners in the Media@ção 2025 competition, which recognized the best works of 2025 in media literacy and cybersecurity awareness promoted jointly by educational and cultural institutions.

## 事實內容

The Media@ção 2025 competition, promoted by the School Libraries Network (RBE) and the Mission Structure for Social Communication (#PortugalMediaLab), in partnership with CNCS (National Cybersecurity Centre), the General Directorate of Education (DGE), the National UNESCO Commission (CNU), the National Cinema Plan (PNC), the National Reading Plan 2027 (PNL2027), and the DNS.PT Association (.PT), recognized the best works of 2025. The initiative fosters media literacy and cybersecurity awareness among students and the broader public through collaborative educational projects. CNCS's participation underscores the national cybersecurity authority's role in promoting digital security awareness in educational contexts.

## 受影響系統

- Educational institutions in Portugal
- Students and youth
- Media literacy and cybersecurity awareness programs
