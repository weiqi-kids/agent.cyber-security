## 摘要
In Q3 2025, CERT.LV recorded 671 cyber incidents, 5% less than Q2 2025 but 2% more than Q3 2024, showing an overall upward trend in cyber incidents.

## 事實內容
During the third quarter of 2025, Latvia's CERT.LV documented 671 cyber incidents. This represents a 5% decrease compared to the second quarter of 2025, but shows a 2% increase year-over-year when compared to Q3 2024. The data reveals an ongoing upward trajectory in cyber incident frequency over time, indicating sustained pressure on Latvia's cybersecurity landscape.
