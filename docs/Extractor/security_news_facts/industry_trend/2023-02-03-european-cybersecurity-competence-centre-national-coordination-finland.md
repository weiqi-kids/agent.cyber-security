## 摘要

歐洲資安工業技術研究能力中心的芬蘭國家協調中心於 2023 年初正式運作，加強歐盟資安研究與技術發展。

## 事實內容

The European Cybersecurity Industrial, Technology and Research Competence Centre's National Coordination Centre began official operations in early 2023 under the Finnish Transport and Communications Agency. The centre is part of the European Union's Network of National Coordination Centres. The EU-wide network aims to improve national cybersecurity capacities, support cyber security research, and accelerate technological development across the EU.
