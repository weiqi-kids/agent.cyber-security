## 摘要

Canadian Cyber Centre establishes comprehensive three-part emergency preparedness framework integrating IRP, BCP, and DRP aligned with ITSG-33, NIST, and ISO standards.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security published ITSM.10.014 establishing a comprehensive emergency preparedness strategy for cyber security resilience.

**Preparedness Framework (Three Core Components):**
- Incident Response Plan (IRP)
- Business Continuity Plan (BCP)
- Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)

The guidance emphasizes that organizations should "align with a relevant security risk management framework," referencing ITSG-33, NIST Cyber Security Framework, and ISO/IEC 27002.

**Incident Response Planning Requirements:**
- Threat and Risk Assessment (TRA) identifying critical assets
- Dedicated response team with clearly defined roles
- Communications protocols and escalation criteria
- Employee training on incident identification and reporting
- Mock drills and periodic testing

**Business Continuity Planning Components:**
- Business Impact Analysis (BIA) assessing service disruption consequences
- Identification of critical operations requiring prioritization
- Recovery strategies addressing various threat types (natural hazards, cyber attacks, infrastructure failures)
- Regular testing through simulations and exercises

**Disaster Recovery Planning Elements:**
- Inventory of IT assets ranked by criticality
- Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO)
- Recovery site selection (hot, warm, or cold sites)
- Step-by-step recovery procedures

**Resilience Strategies:**
The framework promotes:
- Network segmentation and firewall deployment
- Anti-virus/anti-malware software installation
- Regular patching and system updates
- Redundant system backups stored offline
- Cloud-based disaster recovery solutions
- Virtualization and storage replication technologies

**Internal Coordination Mechanisms:**
- Designated points of contact and team alternates
- Cross-functional response teams with management approval
- Clear chain of command establishment
- Regular plan reviews identifying improvement gaps

**External Stakeholder Engagement:**
- Pre-drafted communication messages for crises
- Protocols for notifying clients, vendors, and law enforcement
- Privacy Commissioner reporting where applicable
- Incident reporting to the Cyber Centre

**Continuous Lifecycle Approach:**
The guidance emphasizes treating emergency preparedness as a continuous lifecycle requiring ongoing assessment, testing, and adaptation rather than a one-time project.

The framework recognizes that cyber security resilience requires integration of multiple planning disciplines, alignment with established security frameworks, and commitment to regular testing and continuous improvement.

## 受影響系統

- All organizational IT and OT infrastructure
- Critical business operations and services
- Network infrastructure (segmentation, firewalls)
- Backup and recovery systems
- Communication systems
- Cloud-based disaster recovery platforms
