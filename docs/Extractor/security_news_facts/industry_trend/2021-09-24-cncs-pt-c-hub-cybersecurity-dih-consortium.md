## 摘要

Portugal formalizes the C-Hub: Cybersecurity Digital Innovation Hub (DIH) consortium on September 23, 2021, coordinated by CNCS, after recognition as a Digital Innovation Hub for integration into national and European networks.

## 事實內容

Following recognition as a Digital Innovation Hub (DIH) for integration into the National Network and designation for access to the European Network, the C-Hub: Cybersecurity DIH consortium was formally established on September 23, 2021. Key details:

- **Coordinator**: CNCS (Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança)
- **Consortium members**: Includes CNCS as coordinating entity, along with AMA (Agência para a Modernização Administrativa) and other partner entities
- **Purpose**: A cybersecurity-focused Digital Innovation Hub to integrate into Portugal's National Digital Innovation Hub Network and access the European DIH Network
- **Scope**: Supporting innovation, research, and development in cybersecurity across Portugal

This represents a significant step in Portugal's cybersecurity ecosystem development, connecting national and European innovation networks.
