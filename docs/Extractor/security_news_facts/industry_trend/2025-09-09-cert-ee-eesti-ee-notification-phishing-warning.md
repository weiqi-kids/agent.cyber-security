## 摘要

Estonia's RIA updated eesti.ee terms of use and notified users; RIA warns that official notifications always come from the official national address registrikood@eesti.ee, not from other addresses.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) updated the terms of use for the eesti.ee government portal and notified users about the change. The official notification was sent from the official national address registrikood@eesti.ee. RIA emphasized that official notifications always come from this specific national address. This communication was issued in the context of ongoing fraud attempts where criminals impersonate official government communications. Citizens are advised to verify the sender address when receiving notifications claiming to be from government portals.

## 受影響系統

- eesti.ee government portal notification system
- Estonian citizens receiving government email notifications
