# More than 10,000 denial-of-service attacks occur annually in Finland

## Summary
Each year, more than 10,000 denial-of-service attacks target Finnish organizational websites and online services. This means DoS attacks are made and blocked daily across the country.

## Key Details
- **Frequency**: >10,000 attacks annually
- **Daily Occurrence**: Attacks and blocks happen every day
- **Scope**: Country-wide
- **Targets**: Organizational websites and online services
