## 摘要

No More Ransom launches a new website marking its 5th anniversary, featuring an updated Crypto Sheriff page with ransomware information, prevention advice including regular backups and MFA for corporate networks and RDP services.

## 事實內容

As part of the 5th anniversary milestone of the No More Ransom project, a new website was launched. Key features of the updated site include:

- **Updated Crypto Sheriff page**: Provides current information about known ransomware strains and decryption tools.
- **Prevention guidance**: Offers advice on preventing ransomware infections, including:
  - Regular data backups
  - Multi-factor authentication for corporate networks
  - Securing Remote Desktop Protocol (RDP) services
- **Decryption tools**: The site aggregates 121 anti-ransomware decryption tools available for victims.

No More Ransom is an international collaboration between law enforcement and private security companies, coordinated by Europol. CNCS is a partner in the initiative. The project has helped prevent nearly one billion euros from reaching ransomware criminal organizations over its five years.
