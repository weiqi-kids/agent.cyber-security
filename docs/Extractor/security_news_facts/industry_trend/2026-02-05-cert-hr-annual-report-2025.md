## Summary

Croatian National CERT recorded 1,513 cyber incidents in 2025, a 35.9% increase over 2024, with phishing attacks (32%) as the most prevalent type.

## Factual Content

The Croatian National CERT published its annual report for 2025, revealing a significant increase in cybersecurity incidents:

- **Total incidents in 2025**: 1,513 (increase of 35.9% compared to 2024)
- **Most prevalent incident types**: Phishing attacks accounted for 32% of all incidents
- The incident count only reflects cases reported to National CERT or discovered through its own monitoring activities

The report was presented at the third ConCERT cybersecurity conference held on February 6, 2026 at the National and University Library in Zagreb.
