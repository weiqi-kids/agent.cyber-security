# CERT-FI 20th anniversary: Foundations of Finnish cyber security

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/cert-fi-20-years-laying-foundations-information-security-finland  
**Published**: 2021-07-09

## Summary

CERT-FI celebrates 20th anniversary. The Finnish Computer Emergency Response Team's work ensuring information security in Finland has reached young adulthood. Current Coordination Centre, incident response and expertise originated from CERT-FI function.

## Key Points

- **Milestone**: 20th anniversary
- **Established**: 2001
- **Evolution**: CERT-FI to NCSC-FI
- **Legacy**: Foundation for current incident response and coordination
- **Impact**: Built Finland's cyber security capabilities

## Impact

Two decades of CERT operations established Finland's institutional cyber security capacity, providing continuity and expertise accumulation critical for national resilience.
