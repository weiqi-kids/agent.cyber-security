## 摘要

UK NCSC publishes fourth annual report on Active Cyber Defence programme achievements covering 2020 activities.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre (UK) has released the year four report of its Active Cyber Defence (ACD) programme, covering activities and achievements throughout 2020. The report:

- Highlights the progress and efforts made by the ACD programme during the year
- Documents protective measures implemented across UK cyber infrastructure
- Demonstrates the impact of proactive defensive strategies
- Provides insights into threat landscape changes during 2020

The Active Cyber Defence programme represents the NCSC's approach to reducing cyber threats at scale through automated protective measures and threat intelligence sharing.
