## 摘要
A safety alert was successfully delivered to nearly 190,000 devices through an Estonian mobile application, demonstrating the reach of Estonia's national emergency notification infrastructure.

## 事實內容
The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) announced that a safety alert was distributed to approximately 190,000 devices via the Estonian mobile application. This highlights the effectiveness of Estonia's digital public safety notification system, which leverages the country's advanced e-government infrastructure to reach citizens at scale during emergencies or critical information events.
