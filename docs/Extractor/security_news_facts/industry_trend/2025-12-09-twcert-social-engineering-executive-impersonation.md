## 摘要

TWCERT/CC warns of escalating social engineering attacks where threat actors impersonate company executives via email to trick employees into creating communication groups and disclosing personal information.

## 事實內容

TWCERT/CC received multiple external threat intelligence reports indicating a wave of social engineering attacks where attackers impersonate internal company executives to manipulate employees.

Key characteristics of the attack:
- **Attack vector**: Email impersonation — attackers send emails appearing to come from senior executives or management
- **Social engineering tactics**: Messages use authority-based manipulation, framing requests as urgent administrative tasks or confidential directives
- **Common lures**: "Please create a dedicated internal company LINE group," "Urgent task from management," "Administrative requirement"
- **Goals**:
  - Harvesting personal information (phone numbers, email addresses)
  - Establishing attacker-controlled communication channels under a company guise
  - Potential for follow-on fraud, credential theft, or further social engineering
- **Indicators**: Emails may originate from free email services or lookalike domains despite appearing to come from executive addresses

TWCERT/CC recommends:
- Training employees to verify executive requests through secondary channels (phone call to known numbers)
- Implementing email authentication (DMARC, SPF, DKIM)
- Establishing clear communication protocols for sensitive or unusual requests
- Reporting suspicious emails to the security team immediately

## 受影響系統

- Corporate email systems
- Messaging platforms (LINE, WhatsApp, etc.)
- All organizational employees
