## 摘要

NCSC assessment analyzes how AI developments will impact the cyber threat landscape between 2025 and 2027, forecasting changes to attack capabilities and defensive challenges.

## 事實內容

The UK National Cyber Security Centre published an assessment examining the projected impact of artificial intelligence on cyber threats from 2025 to 2027. The report:

- Analyzes how AI advancement will influence cyber threat actor capabilities
- Forecasts potential changes in attack methodologies enabled by AI
- Assesses defensive challenges posed by AI-enhanced cyber operations
- Provides strategic guidance for organizations preparing for AI-driven threats

This forward-looking analysis helps UK organizations understand and prepare for the evolution of cyber threats as AI technology continues to develop and become more accessible to malicious actors.

- Cyber security defense systems
