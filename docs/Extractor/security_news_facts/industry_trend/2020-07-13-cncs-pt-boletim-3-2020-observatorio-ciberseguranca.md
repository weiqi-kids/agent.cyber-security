## 摘要

CNCS Observatory Bulletin 3/2020 covers CERT.PT incident statistics for H1 2020, a content analysis of phishing incidents in Q2, COVID-19 confinement period cybersecurity data, and 2019 annual figures.

## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) Cybersecurity Observatory published Bulletin 3/2020. In addition to a summary of CERT.PT incident numbers for the first half of 2020, the bulletin presents a content analysis of phishing incidents registered during the second quarter. The document also references publications with relevant data on cybersecurity incidents during the COVID-19 confinement period, as well as annual data for 2019.
