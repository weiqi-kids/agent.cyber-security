## 摘要

Czech NUKIB warned of increased DDoS attack risk during June 7-9, 2024 EU Parliament elections targeting political parties and electoral organizations.

## 事實內容

The Czech National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NUKIB) warned of increased risk of unsophisticated availability attacks (DDoS) during the period from June 7 to 9, 2024, when European Parliament elections were held in the Czech Republic and other EU member states. Potential targets included participating political parties and organizations involved in the electoral process (e.g., public administration and media). While these attacks were not yet recorded in the Czech Republic at the time of the warning, they were observed in the Netherlands where elections occurred on Thursday. NUKIB emphasized that these are attacks where attackers primarily seek media attention without causing long-term impacts on affected organizations.

## 攻擊手法

- DDoS attacks on political parties
- Disruption of electoral organization websites
- Media-seeking disruption campaigns

## 受影響系統

- Political parties
- Electoral organizations
- Public administration and media
