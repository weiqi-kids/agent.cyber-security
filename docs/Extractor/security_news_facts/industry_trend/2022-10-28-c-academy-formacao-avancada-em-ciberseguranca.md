## 摘要

CNCS launches C-Academy's first training calendar offering ~44 advanced cybersecurity courses in online, in-person, and hybrid formats; 18 courses already scheduled for December 2022.

## 事實內容

- **Announcement**: The C-Academy (Formação Avançada em Ciberseguran&ccedil;a) of CNCS presented its first training schedule at the C-DAYS Açores conference.
- **Scope**: Approximately 44 courses available, with class sizes between 15 and 40 participants, at varying difficulty levels.
- **Formats**: Online, in-person, or hybrid.
- **Status at announcement**: 18 of the 44 courses already scheduled, with sessions planned for December 2022.
- **Provider**: Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS), Portugal's national cybersecurity centre.

## 受影響系統

- Cybersecurity professionals and students in Portugal
