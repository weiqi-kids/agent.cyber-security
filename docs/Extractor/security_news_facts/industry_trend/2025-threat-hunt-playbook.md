## 摘要

CERT.LV and Canadian Armed Forces Cyber Command release Threat Hunt Playbook, a methodology document for systematic threat detection in critical infrastructure.

## 事實內容

CERT.LV and Canada's Armed Forces Cyber Command published the Threat Hunt Playbook in February 2025:

**What It Is:**
- Collaborative methodology document
- Provides "a repeatable systematic approach to identify compromises that were otherwise not detected using other security mechanisms"

**Purpose:**
- Guide organizations in conducting threat hunting operations on critical infrastructure
- Document best practices developed through active threat hunting work
- Response to heightened cyber threats following Russia's invasion of Ukraine

**Target Audience:**
- Technical security professionals
- Junior and senior analysts
- Goal: "enabling them to quickly begin operations on a target network"

**Key Content Areas:**
- Threat hunting methodology
- Detection techniques
- Practical guidance for identifying cybersecurity compromises
- Focus on critical information systems

**Access:**
- Available as PDF download from CERT.LV website
- Free public resource

**Partnership Context:**
- Ongoing collaboration between Latvian and Canadian cybersecurity experts
- Collaboration since early 2022 to defend critical infrastructure

## 受影響系統

- Critical infrastructure threat hunting operations
- Security Operations Centers (SOCs)
- Analyst training and capability development
