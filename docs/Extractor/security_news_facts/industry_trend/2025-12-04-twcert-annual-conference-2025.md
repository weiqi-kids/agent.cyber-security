## 摘要

TWCERT/CC held the 2025 Taiwan Cybersecurity Incident Response Annual Conference, bringing together security experts from ASUS, Trend Micro, ITRI, Delta Electronics, Zyxel, Synology, and Panasonic Taiwan to discuss AI-driven threats, PSIRT practices, and cross-domain collaboration.

## 事實內容

The Taiwan Computer Emergency Response Team / Coordination Center (TWCERT/CC) held the "2025 Taiwan Cybersecurity Incident Response Annual Conference" themed "Building Secure Products, Linking Trust Defense Lines."

Participants included cybersecurity experts from:
- ASUS, Trend Micro (AIsomething), ITRI
- Delta Electronics, Zyxel Holdings, Synology, Panasonic Taiwan

Focus areas discussed:
- AI-driven cybersecurity threats
- Achievements and challenges of domestic incident response coordination mechanisms
- Product security governance and PSIRT (Product Security Incident Response Team) vulnerability disclosure practices
- Strengthening cross-domain collaboration and enterprise security resilience

The Minister of Digital Affairs noted that with the rapid proliferation of AI, IoT, and smart manufacturing, product security is no longer just a technical issue but a key pillar of enterprise competitiveness.
