## 摘要

CrowdStrike 2025 report shows 27% increase in interactive intrusions, 80%+ involving fileless malware, China cloud attacks up 40%, voice fraud up 442% in H2 2024.

## 事實內容

CrowdStrike's latest threat report (July 1, 2024 - June 30, 2025) indicates interactive intrusion events increased 27% year-over-year. Unlike previous automated intrusions, attackers actively interact with compromised environments, gathering information and customizing attack strategies based on context. Over 80% of cases involved fileless malware, showing traditional file-detection-based defenses face challenges.

**Cloud and Voice Fraud as New Focus:**
- China-targeted cloud attacks increased 40% annually, highlighting cloud services remain primary battlefield for nation-state attackers
- Voice fraud grew 442% in H2 2024, exceeding full-year 2024 numbers by H1 2025
- AI technology proliferation accelerating such fraud scale and impact

**Nation-State Attacks and Industry Targets:**
- Technology sector remains most attacked industry for 8th consecutive year, mainly from cybercrime activities
- APT attacks target telecommunications and government units
- Russian actors (PRIMITIVE BEAR, VENOMOUS BEAR) attack purposes related to Russia-Ukraine war
- Chinese actors view telecommunications as high-value targets for user data and downstream customer penetration
- Related attacks grew 130% in past year

**Manufacturing and Retail High-Risk Situation:**
- CURLY SPIDER frequently deploys ransomware combined with voice fraud
- Exploits industries' high dependency on operational continuity to force rapid victim payments
- Manufacturing cannot afford production disruptions
- Retail faces customer and revenue losses during peak shopping seasons if data leaked or systems down
- Large budgets and complex, potentially outdated IT architectures attract cybercrime groups

**Generative AI as Attack Accelerator:**
Attackers actively integrating generative AI (GenAI) into operational workflows, not replacing but enhancing original tactics and techniques:

1. Writing more natural and contextually appropriate phishing emails, continuously launching attacks against EU and US with identity spoofing capabilities
2. Easier generation of fake videos, audio, and images impersonating famous figures (deepfake)
3. Lowering cybersecurity attack barriers, accelerating malicious code generation

**Actual Case - North Korea FAMOUS CHOLLIMA:**
Typical case using GenAI to:
- Forge job seeker identities
- Create deepfake videos
- Use LLM to enhance English communication and technical capabilities
- Successfully obtain remote engineer positions
- Over 320 related security incidents observed in past year

**Emerging Threat:**
April 2025 saw attackers targeting AI development tool Langflow AI (CVE-2025-3248), showing AI platforms themselves becoming potential attack surfaces.

**Defense Recommendations:**
1. Enterprises should strengthen applicant identity verification through background checks and career record verification
2. Deploy real-time deepfake detection technology in video interviews and audio assessments
3. Strengthen remote access control preventing location spoofing and endpoint bypasses
4. Comprehensively check USB and peripheral devices, verify external device security
5. Monitor user communication and operational behavior detecting abnormal translation and account synchronization operations
6. Design AI infiltration identification training courses for recruitment and IT teams

**Report Conclusion:**
Generative AI will not completely favor attackers or defenders; real difference lies in user technical capabilities. Only by synchronously strengthening technical detection, governance policies, and personnel training can resilience be maintained in constantly evolving offensive-defensive environments.

## 受影響系統

- Cloud infrastructure and services
- Telecommunications networks and government systems
- Manufacturing and retail enterprise IT systems
- AI development platforms and tools
