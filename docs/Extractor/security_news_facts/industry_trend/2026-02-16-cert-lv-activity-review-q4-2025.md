## 摘要

CERT.LV's Q4 2025 quarterly review reports that the cyber threat level in Latvia remains high with a steady upward trend, driven by both financially and politically motivated attacks, further amplified by geopolitical factors.

## 事實內容

CERT.LV published its quarterly activity review for Q4 2025 on 16 February 2026. Key findings include:
- The level of cyber threats in Latvia remains high, with a steady upward trend throughout the quarter.
- Attacks are motivated by both financial gain and political objectives.
- Geopolitical factors continue to serve as a significant catalyst for increased threat activity.
- The review reflects the broader regional threat environment in the context of ongoing geopolitical tensions in Eastern Europe.

This report is useful for tracking Baltic region threat trends and understanding the threat landscape affecting Latvia and neighboring countries.
