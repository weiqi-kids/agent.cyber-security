## 摘要

Portugal's CNCS officially launched the first two C-Network cybersecurity competency centers in Algarve and Alentejo regions, part of a national network of 7 regional centers.

## 事實內容

The first competency center of the C-Network (Rede de Centros de Competências em Cibersegurança) was officially presented on May 6 at the University of Algarve. The Centro de Competência em Cibersegurança do Algarve (CCC Algarve) is a consortium of the University of Algarve (AULg), NERA (Business Association of the Algarve Region), and AMAL (Intermunicipal Community of the Algarve).

On May 21, the official presentation of the Centro de Competências em Cibersegurança do Alentejo took place at the Polytechnic Institute of Beja. This center is a consortium of the Polytechnic Institute of Beja, the Polytechnic Institute of Portalegre, and the University of Évora.

The C-Network will ultimately comprise 7 competency centers distributed throughout Portugal, one per NUTS II region:
- Algarve
- Alentejo
- Lisbon and Tagus Valley
- Centre
- North
- Autonomous Region of the Azores
- Autonomous Region of Madeira

The mission of these centers is to develop and strengthen cybersecurity response and resilience capacity in their respective regions, supporting public and private entities in adopting best practices.

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese national cybersecurity ecosystem
- Regional public and private sector entities
