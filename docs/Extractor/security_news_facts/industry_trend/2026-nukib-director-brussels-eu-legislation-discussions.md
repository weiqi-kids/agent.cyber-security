## 摘要
NUKIB Director Lukáš Kintr met with key EU cybersecurity stakeholders in Brussels to discuss current legislation and priorities.

## 事實內容
Lukáš Kintr, Director of the National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NÚKIB) of the Czech Republic, conducted a series of high-level meetings in Brussels from January 27-29 to discuss EU cybersecurity legislation and priorities.

**Meeting Dates:** January 27-29, 2026

**Key Meetings:**
1. **Despina Spanou** - Deputy Director-General for Communications Networks, Content and Technology (DG CNECT), European Commission
2. **Juhan Lepassaar** - Director of the European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA)
3. **Miguel de Bruycker** - Director of the Belgian Centre for Cybersecurity
4. **Ambassadors** - Czech Permanent Representation to the EU and Czech Permanent Delegation to NATO

**Discussion Topics:**
- Current EU cybersecurity legislation development
- Most important European cybersecurity issues
- Coordination among EU member states
- NATO cybersecurity cooperation
- Implementation of cybersecurity directives

This represents active engagement by Czech cybersecurity leadership in shaping European cybersecurity policy and maintaining strong international partnerships.
