## 摘要

Estonia's eesti.ee government portal launched a new volunteer service that aggregates all volunteering opportunities in the security sector, making it easier for citizens to become volunteers for national crisis resilience.

## 事實內容

A new volunteer service has been completed on the eesti.ee government portal that aggregates all volunteering opportunities in the internal security sector in one place. The service makes it easier for citizens to become volunteers. This initiative is part of Estonia's broader effort to strengthen societal resilience and crisis preparedness at the national level by leveraging volunteer networks, integrating civil society participation into national security infrastructure through digital government services.

## 受影響系統

- eesti.ee government portal
- National crisis readiness volunteer network
