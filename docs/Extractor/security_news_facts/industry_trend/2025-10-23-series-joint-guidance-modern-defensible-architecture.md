## 摘要

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security, together with partner agencies, published a series of three joint guidance documents on Modern Defensible Architecture (MDA), an ASD's ACSC initiative to help organizations apply secure design and architecture in their cybersecurity strategy.

## 事實內容

- The guidance series is an initiative by ASD's ACSC (Australian Signals Directorate's Australian Cyber Security Centre)
- Aims to ensure organizations consider and actively apply secure design and architecture in their cyber security strategy
- Covers resilience planning and implementations
- The series comprises 3 publications released as a coordinated joint effort
- The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security joined international partners in publishing this guidance
- Targeted at organizations looking to modernize their defensive architecture posture

## 受影響系統

- All organizations designing or maintaining network and system architectures
- Government and private sector organizations implementing cyber security strategies
