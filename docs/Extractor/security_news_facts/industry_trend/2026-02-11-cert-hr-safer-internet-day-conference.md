## 摘要
Croatia observed Safer Internet Day 2026 with a conference "Together for a Better Internet" organized by the Centre for Safer Internet, HAKOM, and CARNET's CERT. The event brought together experts to discuss online safety for children and young people in digital environments.

## 事實內容
- **活動名稱**: "Zajedno za bolji internet" (Together for a Better Internet) Conference
- **日期**: February 10, 2026, Zagreb
- **主辦單位**: Centre for Safer Internet, HAKOM, and CARNET's National CERT
- **代表人物**:
  - Miro Đuzel, CARNET Deputy Director for National CERT
  - Tomislav Ramljak, Head of Centre for Safer Internet
  - Tonko Obuljen, HAKOM Council President
- **主題**: Online safety for children and young people in digital environments
- **框架**: Part of Safer Internet Day global initiative (180+ countries)
