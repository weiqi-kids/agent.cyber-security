## 事實內容
In February 2025, CERT.LV, the Latvian Ministry of Defence, and the Canadian Armed Forces Cyber Command jointly conducted their unique Threat Hunt Workshop. The workshop brought together 30 cybersecurity professionals from NATO member nations, drawing participants from diverse professional backgrounds. This refined iteration of the workshop builds on previous successes, representing continued collaboration between Latvia and Canada in advancing NATO's collective threat hunting capabilities.
