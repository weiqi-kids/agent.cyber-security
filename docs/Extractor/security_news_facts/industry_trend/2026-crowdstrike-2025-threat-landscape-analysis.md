## 摘要

解密「長線佈局」與跨領域攻擊：CrowdStrike深入解析2025駭客戰術演進。

## 事實內容

CrowdStrike released an in-depth analysis of 2025 hacker tactic evolution, highlighting the shift toward long-term planning and cross-domain attack strategies. The report reveals that threat actors are increasingly adopting patient, multi-stage approaches rather than quick smash-and-grab operations.

Key findings include:
1. Extended dwell time: Attackers remain undetected in victim networks for longer periods to gather intelligence and identify high-value targets
2. Cross-domain attacks: Threat actors leverage multiple attack vectors spanning IT, OT, cloud, and mobile environments
3. Supply chain infiltration: Increased focus on compromising trusted suppliers and service providers as initial access vectors
4. Living-off-the-land: Greater use of legitimate tools and processes to evade detection

Organizations should adopt a defense-in-depth strategy addressing all attack surfaces, implement continuous monitoring and threat hunting, and enhance supply chain security practices.
