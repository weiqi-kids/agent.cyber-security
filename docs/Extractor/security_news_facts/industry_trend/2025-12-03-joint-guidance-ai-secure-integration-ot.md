## 摘要

Joint guidance outlining 4 key principles for critical infrastructure owners and operators to leverage AI benefits in OT systems while minimizing cybersecurity risks.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security released joint guidance on the secure integration of artificial intelligence (AI) into operational technology (OT) environments:

- Provides **4 key principles** for CI owners and operators to follow when deploying AI in OT systems
- Addresses the balance between leveraging AI benefits and minimizing associated security risks
- Targets critical infrastructure sectors deploying AI in industrial and OT contexts
- Published as a joint guidance document with partner agencies

The 4 key principles address:
1. Safe and secure AI deployment in OT environments
2. Minimizing risk exposure when integrating AI capabilities
3. Maintaining operational resilience during AI adoption
4. Governance and accountability for AI-enabled OT systems

## 受影響系統

- Operational Technology (OT) environments with AI integration
- Industrial Control Systems (ICS) using AI/ML components
- Critical Infrastructure sectors
