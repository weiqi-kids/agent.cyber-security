## Summary

CERT.br opens registration for 2025 professional cybersecurity training courses covering CSIRT management and incident handling.

## Factual Content

CERT.br announced the opening of registration for its 2025 training program on January 23, 2025.

**Available Courses:**
1. **Overview of Creating and Managing CSIRTs** - Foundational CSIRT establishment and operations
2. **Foundations of Incident Management (FIM)** - Core incident response principles
3. **Advanced Topics in Incident Handling (ATIH)** - Advanced incident response techniques

**Registration:** https://cursos.cert.br/inscricao/

These courses support capacity building for cybersecurity professionals across Brazil and Latin America.
