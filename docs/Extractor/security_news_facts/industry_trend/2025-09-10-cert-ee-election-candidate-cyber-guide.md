## 摘要

Estonia's RIA published a cybersecurity reminder guide for KOV 2025 local election candidates, covering key recommendations for conducting digital campaigns securely and knowingly.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) published a cybersecurity guide (meelespea) for candidates in the KOV 2025 local government elections. The guide provides key recommendations for conducting digital campaign activities securely and knowingly. This advisory targets political candidates specifically, recognizing that election campaigns are increasingly digital and face unique cybersecurity risks including targeted attacks on candidates' accounts and disinformation. The guide represents part of Estonia's comprehensive approach to securing elections at all levels.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian local election digital campaigns
- Candidate digital communications and accounts
