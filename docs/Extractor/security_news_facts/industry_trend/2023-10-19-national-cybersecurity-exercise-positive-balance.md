## 摘要

The fourth edition of Portugal's National Cybersecurity Exercise (ExNCS'23), organized by CNCS in cooperation with ENISA, concluded with positive results testing national incident response capabilities.

## 事實內容

The fourth edition of the National Cybersecurity Exercise (ExNCS'23) concluded, organized by the National Cybersecurity Centre (CNCS) in cooperation with the EU Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA). The exercise tested national coordination and incident response mechanisms among Portuguese public and private entities. The event simulated cyber incidents to evaluate preparedness and cooperation protocols across critical sectors. Results were assessed as positive, reflecting improved coordination compared to prior editions. The exercise forms part of Portugal's broader effort to maintain readiness in line with EU cybersecurity frameworks.
