## 摘要

CNCS published Observatory Bulletin No. 5/2021, focused on passwords — covering weaknesses, mitigation efforts, related incident types, and the future of this security mechanism.

## 事實內容

The Cybersecurity Observatory Bulletin No. 5/2021 centers on the theme of "passwords," addressing: password weaknesses, efforts to mitigate those weaknesses, types of incidents most directly correlated with password compromise, and the future outlook for passwords as a security instrument. As is customary, the bulletin also includes news and publications disseminating relevant content for the cybersecurity field.

Key topics:
- Password weaknesses and attack vectors
- Mitigation strategies for password-related risks
- Incident types correlated with password compromise
- Future of passwords as a security mechanism

## 受影響系統

- Organizations and individuals relying on password-based authentication
