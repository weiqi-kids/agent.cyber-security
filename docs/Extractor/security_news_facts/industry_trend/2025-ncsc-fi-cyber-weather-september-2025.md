## 摘要

September 2025 brought rain fronts to cybersecurity landscape with increased incidents.

## 事實內容

The Cyber Weather report for September 2025 indicates:
- Rain fronts moved over the cybersecurity landscape
- Increase in incidents observed at the end of summer continued
- Overall outlook for the month was mostly rainy (elevated threat level)

The worsening conditions suggest a significant uptick in cyber threats as organizations returned from summer holidays.
