## 摘要

CNCS invites participation in the CIBERTEMA webinar "Do We Live in a Cyber-Resilient Society?" on 28 May 2026 at 10:30, discussing findings from the 7th edition of the Cybersecurity in Portugal report.

## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) has invited the public to attend the CIBERTEMA webinar titled "Vivemos numa sociedade ciber-resiliente?" (Do We Live in a Cyber-Resilient Society?), scheduled for 28 May 2026 at 10:30.

The webinar draws from the 7th edition of the Cybersecurity – Society report by the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory, recently published, which provides an in-depth analysis of the attitudes, behaviors, awareness, and cybersecurity education of Portuguese citizens and organizations.

The session takes place in a context where cyber threats are constantly evolving and aims to stimulate reflection and discussion on the state of Portugal's societal cyber resilience.

## 受影響系統

- General audience (cybersecurity professionals, citizens, organizations in Portugal)
