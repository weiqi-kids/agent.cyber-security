## 摘要

芬蘭社會福利與醫療服務資安改善仰賴多個合作網絡，NCSC-FI 同時擔任促進者與成員角色。

## 事實內容

The continuity of operations of social welfare and healthcare services is increasingly dependent on cyber security. Cooperation takes place on many fronts in Finland and internationally to improve cyber security in this sector. Traficom's National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC-FI) participates in multiple networks focused on healthcare cybersecurity — facilitating some networks and participating as a member in others. A large part of this cooperation occurs in voluntary groups.
