## 摘要

Estonia's Küberkiirendi (Cyber Accelerator), run by RIA and Technopol Startup Incubator, is seeking cybersecurity startups with a focus on combating cyber fraud and science-based solutions. Selected teams receive up to EUR 60,000 in funding.

## 事實內容

- **Program**: Küberkiirendi (Cyber Accelerator)
- **Organizers**: Riigi Infosüsteemi Amet (RIA) and Technopol Startup Incubator (in cooperation)
- **Target applicants**: Cybersecurity startups
- **Focus areas**: Combating cyber fraud, science-based cybersecurity solutions
- **Funding**: Selected teams receive up to EUR 60,000 in support for product/service development
- **Significance**: Government-backed initiative to advance Estonian cybersecurity innovation ecosystem

## 受影響系統

- Cybersecurity startup ecosystem (Estonia)
- Cyber fraud prevention technologies
