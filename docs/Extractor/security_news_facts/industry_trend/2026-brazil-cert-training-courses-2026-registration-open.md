## 摘要

CERT.br opens registration for 2026 training courses including CSIRT management and incident handling, deadline January 15 at 5 PM.

## 事實內容

The Brazilian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT.br) has opened registration for its 2026 training course sessions. Registration is open until 5:00 PM on January 15, 2026.

**Courses Offered in 2026:**

1. Overview of Creating and Managing CSIRTs
2. Foundations of Incident Management (FIM)
3. Advanced Topics in Incident Handling (ATIH)

These courses are designed to build capacity in the cybersecurity community for establishing and operating Computer Security Incident Response Teams, as well as developing incident management and handling skills.

Registration link: https://cursos.cert.br/inscricao/
