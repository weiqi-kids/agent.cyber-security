## 摘要

CNCS published a structured guide for selecting multi-factor authentication solutions.

## 事實內容

The National Cybersecurity Centre of Portugal (CNCS) has published the Guide for the Selection of Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Solutions. The guide presents a structured approach tailored to the specific needs of each organization in the process of adopting multi-factor authentication mechanisms. The document outlines a three-phase selection process framework to help organizations evaluate and implement appropriate MFA solutions based on their requirements and risk profiles.

Original language: Portuguese (CNCS Portugal)
