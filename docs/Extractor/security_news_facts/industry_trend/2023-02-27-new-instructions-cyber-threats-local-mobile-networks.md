## 摘要

NCSC-FI 發布本地行動網路資安威脅與風險管理新指引（僅芬蘭語），協助考慮建置本地行動網路的組織。

## 事實內容

NCSC-FI has released new instructions covering the cyber threats and risks associated with local mobile networks (private LTE/5G networks). The guidance addresses what kinds of cyber threats and risks are associated with local mobile networks and what should be considered when building such networks. The instructions are currently available in Finnish only, targeting organisations that are considering deploying local mobile networks.

## 受影響系統

- Local mobile networks (private LTE/5G)
- Organisations deploying private wireless infrastructure
