## 摘要

Estonia's Rescue Board will send nationwide test alerts via Eesti äpp and "Ole valmis!" on December 4 from 10:00-16:00, as a retest of the emergency alert functionality.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Rescue Board (Päästeamet), in cooperation with RIA and the Women's Voluntary Defence Organisation (Naiskodukaitse), will send nationwide test notifications via the Eesti äpp and "Ole valmis!" (Be Ready!) mobile applications on Thursday, December 4, from 10:00 to 16:00. This represents a second round of testing of the emergency alert functionality of these national mobile applications as part of the development of Estonia's EE-ALARM national emergency alert system.

## 受影響系統

- Eesti äpp
- "Ole valmis!" mobile application
- EE-ALARM emergency alert infrastructure
