## Summary

AP2SI launches survey to profile cybersecurity professionals in Portugal, collecting data on qualifications, functions, and employment conditions.

## Factual Content

The Portuguese Association for the Promotion of Information Security (AP2SI), supported by CNCS as part of the Cybersecurity Observatory, has launched a survey aimed at better understanding cybersecurity and information security professionals in Portugal. The survey objectives include constructing a profile of these professionals, identifying characteristics such as qualifications, main functions performed, salary levels, and reasons for staying in the same job. Results will be published in October during European Cybersecurity Month.
