## 摘要

AP2SI, with CNCS support, launched a survey to profile cybersecurity and information security professionals in Portugal, covering demographics, employment, and salary data.

## 事實內容

The Associação Portuguesa para a Promoção da Segurança da Informação (AP2SI), with support from the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory, conducted an online survey aimed at better understanding cybersecurity and information security professionals in Portugal. Objectives include building a professional profile with data on qualifications, main functions performed, salary levels, and reasons for remaining in the same job. Results were planned for release in October during the European Cybersecurity Month.
