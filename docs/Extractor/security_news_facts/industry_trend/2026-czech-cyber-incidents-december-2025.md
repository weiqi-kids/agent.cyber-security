## 摘要

Czech cybersecurity incidents in December 2025 reached below-average levels with only 1 significant incident out of 13 total.

## 事實內容

In December 2025, the number of cybersecurity incidents recorded by Czech NUKIB reached below-average values. The severity of incidents also slightly decreased – out of a total of 13 incidents, only one was classified as significant. This represents a quieter month for Czech cybersecurity compared to typical activity levels.
