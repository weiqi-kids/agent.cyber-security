## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security guidance on developing a business continuity plan to quickly resume critical operations following a cyber incident or natural disaster.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security released awareness guidance document ITSAP.10.005 on developing a business continuity plan (BCP). In the event of a cyber incident or natural disaster, organizations need a BCP to resume their most critical business operations quickly. A BCP identifies risks from various threats and their potential impact on the organization. Key elements of a BCP include identifying critical business functions and acceptable downtime thresholds, developing continuity strategies for each critical function, assigning roles and responsibilities, establishing communication plans, and testing and updating the plan regularly. The guidance emphasizes that BCPs must account for cyber incidents as a potential cause of operational disruption, not just physical disasters.

## 受影響系統

- Critical business operations and processes
- IT systems supporting essential functions
- Organizational continuity infrastructure
