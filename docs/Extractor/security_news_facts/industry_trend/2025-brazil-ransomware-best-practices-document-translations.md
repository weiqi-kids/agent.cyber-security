## 摘要

CERT.br releases English and Spanish translations of comprehensive ransomware best practices document covering protection, detection, and response strategies.

## 事實內容

The Brazilian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT.br) has published translations of its ransomware best practices document, making this valuable resource accessible to a wider international audience.

**Available Translations:**
- English: "Ransomware: Best Practices for Protection, Detection, and Response"
- Spanish: "Ransomware: mejores prácticas para protección, detección y respuesta"

The document provides comprehensive guidance on:
- Protection strategies against ransomware
- Detection techniques for early identification
- Response procedures for ransomware incidents

These translations expand CERT.br's contribution to global cybersecurity knowledge sharing, particularly benefiting organizations in English and Spanish-speaking regions.
