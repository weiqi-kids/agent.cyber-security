## 摘要

CNCS Observatory published a comprehensive study on cybersecurity education in Portugal, analyzing higher education courses, student numbers, dissertations, and course directors' perspectives over the past 10 years.

## 事實內容

The CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory published a study characterizing cybersecurity courses and the integration of cybersecurity content in Computer Science study programs in Portugal.

Key areas analyzed:
- Objectives, contents, and learning outcomes of cybersecurity courses
- Number of students and graduates over time
- Master's dissertations and doctoral theses in cybersecurity over the past 10 years
- Perspectives of course and study cycle directors on:
  - Technical, ethical, and legal training components
  - Labor market needs
  - Challenges in cybersecurity research and innovation education

The study provides a comprehensive overview of the state of cybersecurity higher education in Portugal and its evolution over the past decade.
