## 摘要

Croatia marks Safer Internet Day 2026, a global initiative involving 180+ countries to promote digital safety for children and youth.

## 事實內容

Croatia will observe Safer Internet Day on the second Tuesday of February 2026, joining a global initiative spanning over 180 countries. The campaign focuses on creating a safer, more responsible, and higher-quality digital environment for children and young people who are growing up surrounded by digital technologies. This annual event addresses internet safety as a key societal issue.
