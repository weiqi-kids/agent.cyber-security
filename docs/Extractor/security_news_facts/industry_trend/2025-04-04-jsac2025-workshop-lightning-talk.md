Following the previous blog post covering JSAC2025 Main Track Day 2, JPCERT/CC published highlights of the Workshops and Lightning Talks of JSAC2025 (Japan Security Analyst Conference 2025).

The workshop and lightning talk format at JSAC provides:
- Hands-on technical training in security analysis techniques
- Rapid-fire presentations on emerging research topics
- Practical skills development for security analysts
- Knowledge sharing among the Japanese and international security community

JSAC2025 focused on advancing incident analysis and response capabilities through both theoretical presentations and practical workshops.
