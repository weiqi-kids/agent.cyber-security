## 摘要

CNCS and the Retail and Distribution ISAC jointly launched the "Palavra-Passe Cibersegura" (Secure Password) awareness campaign to promote password security among the public.

## 事實內容

CNCS (Centro Nacional de Ciberseguran&ccedil;a) and the ISAC for Retail and Distribution (Centro de Análise e Partilha de Informa&ccedil;ão — Retalho e Distribui&ccedil;ão) joined forces to launch the "Palavra-Passe Cibersegura" (Secure Password / Cyber-Secure Password) awareness campaign. The campaign promotes safe password practices among the general public and retail/distribution sector employees. The collaboration between the national cybersecurity authority and a sector-specific ISAC reflects efforts to bridge public awareness with industry-sector cybersecurity hygiene.

## 受影響系統

- General public (password security awareness)
- Retail and distribution sector organizations in Portugal
