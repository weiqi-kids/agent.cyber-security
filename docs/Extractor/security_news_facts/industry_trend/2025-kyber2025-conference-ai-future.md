## 摘要
SK-CERT announces Kyber2025 NIS2 conference on AI and future of cybersecurity, November 3-4, 2025.

## 事實內容
The National Cyber Security Centre (SK-CERT) of Slovakia has announced the Kyber2025 conference focusing on artificial intelligence and the future of cybersecurity.

**Event Details:**
- **Name:** Kyber2025: AI and the Future of Cybersecurity
- **Dates:** November 3-4, 2025
- **Focus:** NIS2 directive and AI in cybersecurity

**Program Highlights:**

**Day 1 - November 3, 2025:**
- **Workshop:** Network Security is Not Dead (13:00-17:00)
- **Instructors:** Ján Skalný (security expert), Tomáš Belluš, Ondrej Ambruš
- **Duration:** 4 hours
- **Note:** Participants should bring laptops

**Conference Topics:**
- Artificial Intelligence applications in cybersecurity
- NIS2 directive implementation
- Future trends in cyber defense
- Network security architecture
- Practical security workshops

**Target Audience:**
- Cybersecurity professionals
- Network security engineers
- Organizations implementing NIS2
- Security architects
- Policy and compliance officers

This conference represents a major Slovak cybersecurity community event addressing current regulatory requirements (NIS2) and emerging technologies (AI).
