## 摘要

OpenAI has published a threat report on malicious uses of AI models, documenting how threat actors use AI in combination with traditional tools for influence operations, romance scams, and cyberattacks. The report includes case studies on Chinese influence operations and covers multiple threat actor campaigns disrupted over a two-year period.

## 事實內容

- OpenAI published an updated threat report on malicious uses of its AI models, the second such report in two years.
- Key insight: threat actors typically use AI in combination with other traditional tools (websites, social media accounts) rather than AI alone.
- Threat activity is rarely limited to a single platform; Chinese influence operators have used multiple AI models at various stages.
- Case studies documented in the report:
  - Romance Scams: "Operation Date Bait" and "Operation False Witness"
  - Virtual targeting: "Operation Silver Lining Playbook"
  - Covert influence operations (IO): "Operation Trolling Stone," "Operation No Bell," "Operation Fish Food"
  - Chinese state-linked IO: "China's Cyber Special Operations"
- The report highlights how threat actors use different AI models at various points in their operational workflow.
- OpenAI shares these insights to help industry and society identify and mitigate such threats.

## 受影響系統

- Social media platforms and online communities
- AI model providers (cross-platform threat)
- Organizations targeted by AI-enhanced influence operations
- Romance scam victims
