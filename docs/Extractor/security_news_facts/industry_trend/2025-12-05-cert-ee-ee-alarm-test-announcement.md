## 摘要

Estonia's Rescue Board will test the EE-ALARM emergency alert system on December 10, activating sirens with a real threat sound between 15:00-15:10 and sending notifications via apps and SMS.

## 事實內容

On Wednesday, December 10, 2025, the Estonian Rescue Board together with partners will test the EE-ALARM emergency alert system. Between 15:00 and 15:10, the siren network will be activated with a real threat sound, information will be transmitted via the Eesti äpp and "Ole valmis!" mobile applications, and on ERR channels. SMS will be used for advance notification only during this test. A mass SMS notification will be sent in the morning to inform all mobile phones in Estonia about the afternoon test and especially about the siren activation.

## 受影響系統

- EE-ALARM national emergency alert system
- Eesti äpp
- "Ole valmis!" mobile application
- Siren network
