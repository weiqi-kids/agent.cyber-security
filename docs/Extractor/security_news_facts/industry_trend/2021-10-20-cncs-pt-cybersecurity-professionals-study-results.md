## 事實內容

The results of the 2nd Edition of the Study on Cybersecurity and Information Security Professionals in Portugal have been officially published. The study was conducted by AP2SI (Associação Portuguesa para a Promoção da Segurança da Informação) through an online questionnaire open to the community, with support from the Cybersecurity Observatory at CNCS. The study provides insights into the state of the cybersecurity workforce in Portugal, including professional demographics, skills, career paths, and challenges facing cybersecurity and information security professionals in the country.
