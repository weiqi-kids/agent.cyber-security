## 摘要

Connect4Cyber event in Helsinki brought together European cybersecurity professionals for funding discussions and international cooperation.

## 事實內容

The Connect4Cyber – Brokerage and Info Day event held in late August 2025:
- **Location**: Business Finland's headquarters in Helsinki
- **Attendance**: Full house of cybersecurity professionals from Finland and across Europe
- **Topics covered**:
  - Current EU funding opportunities for cybersecurity
  - Technological development trends
  - International cooperation opportunities
- **Purpose**: Networking and brokerage for cybersecurity initiatives

The event facilitated connections between European cybersecurity stakeholders and promoted cross-border collaboration.

## 受影響系統

- International cybersecurity partnerships
