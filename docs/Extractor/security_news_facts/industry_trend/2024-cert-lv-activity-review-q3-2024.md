## 摘要
Q3 2024 report noted that geopolitical and ideological conflicts remain strong drivers of cyber attacks, with cyber threat levels significantly elevated since Russia's invasion of Ukraine.

## 事實內容
The third quarter 2024 activity review highlighted that geopolitical and ideological conflicts continue to serve as major drivers for cyber attacks. Since the Russian invasion of Ukraine, Latvia has experienced a significant increase in cyber threat levels. This sustained elevation in cyber threat activity reflects the ongoing geopolitical tensions in the region and their direct impact on the cybersecurity landscape. The report underscores how military conflicts translate into intensified cyber operations.

- Critical information systems
- Regional networks
