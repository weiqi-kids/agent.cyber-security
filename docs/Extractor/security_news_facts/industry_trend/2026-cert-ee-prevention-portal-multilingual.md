## Summary
Estonia's cybersecurity prevention portal itvaatlik.ee is now available in Estonian, Russian, and English to reach broader audience.

**Portal**: itvaatlik.ee (cybersecurity awareness and prevention portal)
**Operator**: Information System Authority (RIA) of Estonia
**Update**: Multi-language support added

**Supported Languages**:
- Estonian
- Russian
- English

**Purpose**: Cybersecurity prevention and public awareness education

**Significance**:
- Expands reach to non-Estonian speakers
- Serves Russian-speaking minority in Estonia
- International accessibility for English speakers
- Demonstrates inclusive cybersecurity awareness approach

**Notes**:

- Enhances accessibility of cybersecurity information
