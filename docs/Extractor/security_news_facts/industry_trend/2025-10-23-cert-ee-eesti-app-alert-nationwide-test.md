## 摘要

Estonia will conduct a nationwide test of the Eesti äpp and "Ole valmis!" mobile apps' emergency alert functionality on October 28, 2025 at 11:30, in cooperation with the Rescue Board.

## 事實內容

A nationwide test of the emergency alert functionality of the Eesti äpp and "Ole valmis!" (Be Ready!) mobile applications will take place on Tuesday, October 28, 2025 at 11:30. The test alerts will be sent in cooperation with the Rescue Board (Päästeamet). This announcement preceded the actual test and was intended to inform the public to avoid alarm when receiving test notifications. The nationwide testing is part of developing Estonia's EE-ALARM emergency notification system.

## 受影響系統

- Eesti äpp
- "Ole valmis!" mobile application
- EE-ALARM emergency notification infrastructure
