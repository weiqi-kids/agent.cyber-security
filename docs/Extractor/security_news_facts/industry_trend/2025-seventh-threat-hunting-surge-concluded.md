## 摘要
CERT.LV completed a month-long threat hunting collaboration with Canadian Armed Forces, CCCS, CERT.EU, Polish Cyber Command, and Latvian defense forces.

## 事實內容
CERT.LV recently concluded an intensive month-long threat hunting collaboration involving multiple international partners. The participating organizations included the Canadian Armed Forces (CAF), the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (CCCS), CERT.EU, Polish Cyber Command, Latvian National Armed Forces, and the National Guard Cyber Defense and Electromagnetic Warfare Battalion. This multinational exercise represents the seventh iteration of the Threat Hunting Surge initiative, demonstrating continued international cooperation in proactive cyber defense.

## 受影響系統
- NATO and partner nation defense networks
- Critical infrastructure monitoring systems
