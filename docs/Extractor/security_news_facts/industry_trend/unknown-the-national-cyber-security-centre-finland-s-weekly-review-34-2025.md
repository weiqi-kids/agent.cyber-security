## 摘要

Finnish NCSC weekly security review covering recent cyber threats and security guidance.

## 事實內容

This week, we highlight online scams in which criminals impersonate a pension insurance company and frighten recipients with claims that their pension payments will be stopped. Clicking the link, however, leads to a fraudulent website that attempts to steal pensioners’ online banking credentials. We also report on the BadBox malware affecting cheap smart devices. In addition, funding is available from the Finnish Transport and Communications Agency Traficom to improve the cybersecurity of communications networks and information systems in line with the requirements of the Cybersecurity Act.
