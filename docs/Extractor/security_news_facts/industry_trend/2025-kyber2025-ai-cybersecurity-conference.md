## 摘要

SK-CERT announces Kyber2025 NIS2 conference on November 3-4, 2025, focusing on AI and the future of cybersecurity, featuring workshops and expert presentations.

## 事實內容

The National Security Authority (NBÚ) organized the Kyber2025 conference held on November 3-4, 2025, with focus on artificial intelligence's role in cybersecurity.

**Conference Details:**
- **Date:** November 3-4, 2025
- **Theme:** AI and the Future of Cybersecurity
- **Type:** NIS2 conference

**Day 1 Program (November 3):**
- **Time:** 13:00 - 17:00
- **Venue:** Room S1
- **Workshop:** Network security is not dead (4 hours)
- **Instructors:**
  - Ján Skalný (security expert)
  - Tomáš Belluš
  - Ondrej Ambruš

**Workshop Requirements:**
Participants advised to bring their own equipment for hands-on sessions.

**Key Topics:**
- AI integration in cybersecurity defense
- Network security evolution
- Future cybersecurity challenges
- Practical security implementation

The conference demonstrates Slovakia's focus on emerging technologies and their impact on national cybersecurity posture.

## 受影響系統

- AI-powered cybersecurity solutions
- Network security infrastructure
- Slovak cybersecurity ecosystem
