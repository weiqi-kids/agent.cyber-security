## 摘要

August 2025 marked end of calm summer with storm clouds in data breaches and malware.

## 事實內容

The Cyber Weather report for August 2025 indicates:
- August marked the end of the calm summer season for cyber security
- Storm clouds gathered particularly in the areas of data breaches
- Malware and vulnerabilities also contributed to deteriorating conditions

This represents a significant shift from the calm summer period to increased threat activity.

## 受影響系統

- Systems affected by increased data breach activity
- Infrastructure impacted by malware campaigns
- Systems with newly discovered vulnerabilities
