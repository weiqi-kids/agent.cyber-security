## 事實內容

JPCERT/CC published the TSUBAME Report Overflow covering the monitoring period of October to December 2024. This series focuses on monitoring trends from overseas TSUBAME sensors and activities not included in the main Internet Threat Monitoring Quarterly Reports.

The report analyzes observations from the TSUBAME (Targeted cyberattacks Uncovering Based on Analyzing darknet and Malware behavior Emulation) sensor network during Q4 2024.

The report provides insights into:
- Monitoring trends from overseas TSUBAME sensors
- Threat activity patterns during the final quarter of 2024
- Activities and patterns not covered in standard quarterly reports
- Analysis of the October-December 2024 period

This report serves as supplementary intelligence to the main quarterly threat monitoring reports, focusing specifically on overseas sensor data and emerging patterns observed in late 2024.
