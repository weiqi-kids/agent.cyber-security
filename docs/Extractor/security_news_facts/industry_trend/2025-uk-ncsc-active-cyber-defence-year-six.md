## 摘要

UK NCSC sixth annual Active Cyber Defence report with key findings and full programme analysis.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre (UK) has published key findings and full report from the sixth year of the Active Cyber Defence (ACD) programme. The report:

- Reviews six years of ACD programme evolution and impact
- Documents cumulative achievements in reducing national cyber risk
- Provides updated metrics on threats mitigated and services protected
- Analyzes lessons learned and best practices from six years of operation
- Outlines future strategic direction for the programme

This comprehensive report demonstrates the sustained value of the ACD approach over multiple years.
