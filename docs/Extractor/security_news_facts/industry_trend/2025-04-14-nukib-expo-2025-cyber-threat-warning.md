## 摘要

NUKIB issued a situational report warning of expected increased cyber threat activity targeting participants and organizations related to EXPO 2025 in Osaka (April 13 – October 13, 2025).

## 事實內容

NUKIB issued a situational report warning about the potential for increased cyber threat activity in connection with EXPO 2025. The world exposition is hosted by Osaka, Japan from April 13 to October 13, 2025. Due to the international character and high visibility of this event, increased interest from cyber actors is expected; these actors may target participants, visitors, and organizations even before the start of the expo. NUKIB recommends heightened vigilance in connection with this prestigious event. The report advises organizations connected to the expo to review their security posture.

## 受影響系統

- Organizations and participants involved with EXPO 2025
- International attendees from Czech Republic
