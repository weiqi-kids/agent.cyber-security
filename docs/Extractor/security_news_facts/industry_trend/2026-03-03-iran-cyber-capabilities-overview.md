## 事實內容

Check Point Research published a comprehensive overview of Iran's cyber threat ecosystem, timed to the current Iran crisis characterized by kinetic military action and geopolitical pressure.

**Iranian cyber ecosystem structure:**

1. **State-aligned clusters:**
   - Entities aligned with the **Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC)**
   - Entities aligned with the **Ministry of Intelligence and Security (MOIS)**

2. **Deniable operators:**
   - Groups that provide plausible deniability for state-sponsored operations

3. **"Hacktivist" groups:**
   - Loosely affiliated groups conducting operations aligned with Iranian state objectives

**Primary objectives of Iranian cyber activity:**

| Objective | Activities |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Espionage** | Gaining intelligence and establishing footholds in targeted networks |
| **Disruption/Destruction** | DDoS attacks, pseudo-ransomware, data wipers — imposing costs on adversaries |
| **Information Operations** | Pairing destructive activity or data leaks with coordinated online amplification |

**Key TTPs highlighted:**
- DDoS attacks against government and critical infrastructure
- Pseudo-ransomware (deployed for disruption, not financial gain)
- Data wipers for destructive operations
- Data leak operations combined with information amplification campaigns

**Threat forecast:**
- Activity is expected to **intensify and broaden** across:
  - Middle East
  - United States
  - Other countries that Iran views as adversaries in the current conflict
- Both cyber and kinetic pressure are occurring simultaneously ("current Iran crisis at its peak")

**Significance for defenders:**
This overview provides threat intelligence on which Iranian-linked actor clusters are currently most active, their recent TTPs against targets in the Middle East and USA, and helps organizations prioritize defensive posture against Iran-nexus threats.

## 受影響系統

- Government networks globally (particularly Middle East, USA, Israel-aligned nations)
- Critical infrastructure (energy, water, financial sector)
- Media and information systems (information operations targeting)
