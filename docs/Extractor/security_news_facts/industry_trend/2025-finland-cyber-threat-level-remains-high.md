## 摘要

Traficom and Finnish Security Intelligence Service report Finland's cyber threat level remains elevated since 2022 Russia-Ukraine war, with serious data breaches and intrusion attempts more than doubling, though broad-scale paralysing attacks remain unlikely.

## 事實內容

Traficom (Finnish Transport and Communications Agency) and the Finnish Security and Intelligence Service (Supo) issued a joint press release assessing Finland's cybersecurity threat landscape.

**Threat Level Assessment:**

1. Current Status:
   - Threat level: Elevated/High
   - Duration: Over past year (since 2022)
   - Trigger: Russia's large-scale attack against Ukraine in 2022
   - Status: Remains elevated continuously

**Incident Trends:**

2. Hostile Cyber Activity:
   - Target: Finnish organizations continue to be targeted
   - Type: Hostile cyber activity from state and non-state actors
   - Trend: Ongoing and persistent

3. Data Breaches and Intrusions:
   - Serious data breaches: Increased
   - Attempted intrusions: Increased
   - Scale: More than doubled compared to previous year
   - Investigating authority: NCSC-FI at Traficom

4. Software Vulnerabilities:
   - Findings: Significantly increased
   - Impact: Greatly heightens cyber threat to society
   - Concern: Expanded attack surface

**Risk Assessment:**

5. Broad-Scale Attacks:
   - Likelihood: Low
   - Type: Cyberattacks capable of paralysing society on broad scale
   - Assessment: Joint evaluation by Traficom and Supo

**Significance:**
- National security assessment from authoritative sources
- Quantifiable increase in serious incidents (2x)
- Geopolitical context (Russia-Ukraine conflict impact)
- Persistent elevated threat environment
- Critical infrastructure and national security concerns

**Authorities Involved:**
- Traficom: Finnish Transport and Communications Agency
- Supo: Finnish Security and Intelligence Service
- NCSC-FI: National Cyber Security Centre Finland

## 受影響系統

- Finnish organization networks and systems
- Critical infrastructure
- Government systems
- Systems with unpatched software vulnerabilities
