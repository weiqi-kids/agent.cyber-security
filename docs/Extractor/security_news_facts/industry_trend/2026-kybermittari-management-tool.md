## Summary

NCSC-FI created Kybermittari, a tool that helps corporate management face cyber risks and safeguard the continuity of business operations.

## Details

**Event Type**: Tool Release

**Tool Name**: Kybermittari (Cyber Meter)

**Purpose**:
- Help corporate management face cyber risks
- Safeguard business operations continuity
- Management decision support

**Developer**: National Cyber Security Centre Finland (NCSC-FI) at Traficom

**Target Audience**: Corporate management

## Impact Assessment

**Severity**: Low (Tool Release)

**Scope**: National (Finland) - Corporate Sector

**Value**: Provides management-level cyber risk assessment and decision support capabilities

## Notes

Source is in English from NCSC-FI. This is a tool release announcement aimed at helping non-technical management understand and address cyber risks.
