## 事實內容

Cyber attacks targeting the semiconductor industry have intensified, focusing on design, manufacturing, and financial analysts. The campaigns reflect the strategic importance of semiconductor supply chains in global geopolitics and economic competition.

Threat actors are employing sophisticated techniques to compromise semiconductor design intellectual property, manufacturing processes, and market intelligence. Targets include chip design firms, fabrication facilities, supply chain partners, and financial analysts covering the semiconductor sector.

The attacks aim to steal advanced chip designs, manufacturing know-how, business strategies, and market forecasts. Given semiconductors' critical role in everything from consumer electronics to military systems, these campaigns represent significant national security and economic concerns.

Organizations in the semiconductor ecosystem should implement enhanced security measures including supply chain security, insider threat programs, network segmentation, and intellectual property protection controls.
