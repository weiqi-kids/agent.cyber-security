Coverage of JSAC2025 Day 2 presentations, including phishing criminal groups analysis and Mizuho Bank's countermeasures against KeepSpy malware.

## 事實內容

Continuing from the previous blog article, this entry introduces the presentations on the 2nd day of JSAC2025. Featured presentations include:

"Observation of phishing criminal groups related to illegal money transfers and Mizuho Bank's countermeasures - Fighting against phishing site malware 'KeepSpy'" by Speaker Tsukasa Takeuchi. The presentation details the bank's efforts to identify and counter phishing operations targeting financial institutions.

JSAC provides a technical forum for sharing real-world experiences in combating cyber threats, with presentations covering both offensive threat research and defensive strategies.
