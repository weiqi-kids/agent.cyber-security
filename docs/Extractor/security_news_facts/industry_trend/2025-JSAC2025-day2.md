## 摘要

JSAC2025 Day 2 presentations covered phishing countermeasures, ransomware infrastructure exploitation, and APT campaigns including MirrorFace's Operation AkaiRyu and PlushDaemon supply chain attacks.

## 事實內容

JPCERT/CC published highlights from the second day of JSAC2025 (Japan Security Analyst Conference 2025), held on January 21-22, 2025. The conference brought together cybersecurity experts to share technical insights.

**Phishing Attack Countermeasures:**

*Mizuho Financial Group* (Speakers: Tsukasa Takeuchi and team)
Presented analysis of phishing criminal groups related to illegal money transfers and countermeasures against KeepSpy Android malware, which "spreads links to phishing sites through smishing." Emphasized the need for information sharing and collaborative strategies beyond individual organizations.

*LAC* (Speakers: Yoshimura, Sano)
Discussed real-time phishing detection systems, noting that "phishing methods are rapidly evolving along with technical changes such as free SSL certificates services."

*NTT Communications* (Speaker: Masumoto)
Analyzed two Phishing-as-a-Service (PhaaS) kits targeting Japan, demonstrating how detection rules can be created using Indicators of Kit (IOK) methodology.

**Ransomware and Infrastructure Exploitation:**

*Sygnia* (Speakers: Zhongyuan Hau, Ren Jie Yow)
Revealed how ransomware groups exploit ESXi and NAS devices for covert operations through tunneling techniques to bypass security detection. Emphasized "the importance of monitoring NAS and ESXi devices thoroughly."

**APT Groups and Supply Chain Attacks:**

*ESET Presentations*

PlushDaemon Campaign:
- Compromised a VPN provider's website to distribute SlowStepper backdoor malware
- Demonstrated supply chain attack vector

MirrorFace's Operation AkaiRyu:
- Targeted European diplomatic institutions
- Used Expo 2025-themed lures
- Revived ANEL backdoor malware

*Itochu Cyber & Intelligence*
Analyzed MirrorFace's exploitation of Windows Sandbox for attack evasion. Provided forensic investigation techniques for both host and sandbox environments.

**Conference Details:**
- Event: Japan Security Analyst Conference (JSAC) 2025
- Dates: January 21-22, 2025
- Organizer: JPCERT/CC
- Purpose: Enhancing skills and knowledge of security analysts

## 受影響系統

- Android devices (KeepSpy malware)
- ESXi and NAS devices (ransomware exploitation)
- VPN provider websites (PlushDaemon supply chain attack)
- Windows systems with Sandbox (MirrorFace attacks)
- European diplomatic institutions (Operation AkaiRyu)
