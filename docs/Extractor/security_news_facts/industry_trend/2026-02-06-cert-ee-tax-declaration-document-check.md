## 摘要

Estonian Tax and Customs Board, Police and Border Guard Board, and RIA remind citizens to check document validity and PIN codes before the tax declaration period starting February 16.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Tax and Customs Board (MTA), Police and Border Guard Board (PPA), and the Information System Authority (RIA) jointly reminded citizens that before the tax declaration period starting February 16, it is advisable to check document expiry dates and ensure PIN codes are available. This annual reminder emphasizes the importance of valid digital identity documents for completing tax declarations through Estonian e-services, highlighting the integration of digital identity infrastructure with government services.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian digital identity documents (ID card, Mobile-ID)
- Estonian tax declaration e-service
- PIN code authentication
