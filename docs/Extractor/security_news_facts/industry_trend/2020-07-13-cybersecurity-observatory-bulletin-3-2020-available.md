CERT.PT bulletin analyzes first half 2020 incidents, includes phishing content analysis for Q2, and presents COVID-19 confinement period data.

## Factual Content

In addition to the balance of incidents registered by CERT.PT in the first half of 2020, the Bulletin presents content analysis of phishing incidents registered during the second quarter. The document also references some publications presenting relevant data on cybersecurity incidents during the confinement period, plus annual data for 2019.
