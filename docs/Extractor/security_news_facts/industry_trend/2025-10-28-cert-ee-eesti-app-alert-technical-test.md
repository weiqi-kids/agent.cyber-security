## 摘要

Estonia's Rescue Board conducted a technical test of the Eesti äpp and "Ole valmis!" mobile apps' emergency alert functionality on October 28, in preparation for a nationwide test.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Rescue Board conducted a technical test of the emergency alert functionality of the Eesti äpp and "Ole valmis!" (Be Ready!) mobile applications on October 28, 2025, in cooperation with RIA, Women's Voluntary Defence Organisation (Naiskodukaitse), and the Ministry of Interior's IT and Development Centre (SMIT). This test was a preparation step before the nationwide alert test announced for October 28, 2025 at 11:30. The testing process validates the technical infrastructure for delivering emergency notifications to citizens via mobile applications.

## 受影響系統

- Eesti äpp
- "Ole valmis!" mobile application
- EE-ALARM emergency notification infrastructure
