## 摘要
DNSC invited Romanian public institutions to an information session about participation in a cybersecurity competence-building project funded under Romania's National Recovery and Resilience Plan.

## 事實內容
The Romanian National Directorate of Cyber Security (DNSC / CERT-RO) extended an invitation to public institutions to attend an information session regarding their potential participation in a project titled "Creating New Cybersecurity Competencies for Society and the Economy." This initiative is financed through Romania's National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR). The information session aims to explain the project's objectives, participation requirements, and the benefits for public sector entities in developing cybersecurity capacity. The project represents part of Romania's broader commitment to enhancing national cybersecurity capabilities in alignment with EU recovery and resilience priorities.

## 受影響系統
- Romanian public institutions
- Government agencies eligible for PNRR-funded cybersecurity programs
