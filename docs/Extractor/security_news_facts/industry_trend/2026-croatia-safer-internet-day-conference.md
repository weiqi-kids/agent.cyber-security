## 摘要

Croatia marks Safer Internet Day with conference organized by Safer Internet Centre, HAKOM and CARNET CERT on February 10 in Zagreb.

## 事實內容

The "Together for a Better Internet" conference was held on February 10 in Zagreb to mark Safer Internet Day, organized jointly by the Safer Internet Centre, Croatian Regulatory Authority for Network Industries (HAKOM), and CARNET's National CERT.

The event brought together institutions and children to discuss security in digital environments. Opening remarks were provided by:
- Miro Đuzel, Assistant Director of CARNET for National CERT
- Tomislav Ramljak, Head of the Safer Internet Centre  
- Tonko Obuljen, President of HAKOM Council

Safer Internet Day serves as a reminder of the importance of digital safety for children and young people in Croatia.
