## 摘要

The Madeira Cybersecurity Competence Center (CCC Madeira) was officially inaugurated in Funchal on 29 April 2025, joining the C-Network coordinated by CNCS.

## 事實內容

On the morning of 29 April 2025, the Madeira Cybersecurity Competence Center (CCC Madeira — "Centro de Competências em Ciberseguran&ccedil;a da Madeira") was officially inaugurated in Funchal. The center is integrated into the C-Network, a national network coordinated by CNCS, which aims to support and expand cybersecurity capabilities across Portugal's regions, including the autonomous regions of Madeira and the Azores. CCC Madeira provides local support for cybersecurity initiatives, awareness, and capacity building in the Madeira autonomous region.

## 受影響系統

- Madeira autonomous region government and businesses
- Portuguese national C-Network cybersecurity infrastructure
