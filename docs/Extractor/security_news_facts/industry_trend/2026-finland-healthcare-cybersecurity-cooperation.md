# Social welfare and healthcare cybersecurity improved through multi-network cooperation

## Summary
Continuity of social welfare and healthcare services increasingly depends on cybersecurity. Finland improves the sector's security through cooperation across many networks, with NCSC-FI participating as facilitator and member in various voluntary groups.

## Key Details
- **Sector**: Social welfare and healthcare
- **Approach**: Multi-network cooperation (Finland and international)
- **NCSC-FI Role**: Facilitator and participant
- **Structure**: Voluntary cooperation groups
- **Focus**: Service continuity

## Context
Healthcare sector cybersecurity requires coordinated multi-stakeholder efforts due to critical nature of services and complex technical environments.
