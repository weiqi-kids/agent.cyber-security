## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security full threat assessment on ransomware trends 2025-2027, covering the threat ecosystem, state of ransomware in Canada, key judgments, myths, and forward outlook.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (Cyber Centre) released the Ransomware Threat Outlook 2025-2027, an update to the Baseline Cyber Threat Assessment. Key judgments indicate ransomware remains the most disruptive cyber threat facing Canadian organizations. The assessment covers: the ransomware threat ecosystem including ransomware-as-a-service (RaaS) models, initial access brokers, and data leak sites; the state of ransomware in Canada documenting sectors most targeted and attack trends; cyber snapshots of notable incidents; myths and misconceptions about ransomware (e.g., backups alone are insufficient defense, paying ransom does not guarantee data recovery); and an outlook for evolving tactics including data exfiltration without encryption and targeting of operational technology. The report notes that critical infrastructure sectors including healthcare, energy, and government remain high-value targets. The assessment is based on reporting and analysis by the Cyber Centre.

## 受影響系統

- Canadian organizations across all sectors
- Critical infrastructure (healthcare, energy, government)
- Organizations using ransomware-as-a-service-targeted platforms
- Backup and OT systems
