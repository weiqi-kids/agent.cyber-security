# Cyber and information influence activities come together in the Hack and Leak phenomenon

**Source:** NCSC-FI Security Now!  
**Link:** https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/ajankohtaista/hack-and-leak  
**Published:** 2023-04-04

## Summary

Hack and Leak refers to instances where the objective is to carry out a data breach, steal information critical to the victim, and use it. Considered a so-called hybrid attack.

## Key Points

- Definition of Hack and Leak phenomenon
- Hybrid attack methodology
- Information warfare tactics
- Data breach for influence operations
