## 摘要

NUKIB participated in a two-day meeting of the WP5 work package of the QARC (Quantum-Resistant Cryptography in Practice) project in late May 2026.

## 事實內容

In the last days of May 2026, a two-day meeting of representatives of the research team of Work Package 5 (WP5) of the Quantum-Resistant Cryptography in Practice (QARC) project was held. NUKIB is one of the participants in this project. The QARC project focuses on developing and implementing quantum-resistant (post-quantum) cryptographic solutions in practice, with the aim of preparing organizations and infrastructure for the cryptographic threats posed by future quantum computers.
