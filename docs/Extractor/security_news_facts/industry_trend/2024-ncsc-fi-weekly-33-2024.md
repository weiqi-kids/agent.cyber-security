Weekly review focusing on ransomware threats and protection methods for organizations and individuals.

## 事實內容

This weekly review provides an in-depth look at ransomware threats:
- Definition and characteristics of ransomware attacks
- Common infection vectors and attack patterns
- Best practices for protection against ransomware
- Recovery recommendations for affected organizations

The content serves as educational material for improving organizational resilience against ransomware threats.

## 受影響系統
