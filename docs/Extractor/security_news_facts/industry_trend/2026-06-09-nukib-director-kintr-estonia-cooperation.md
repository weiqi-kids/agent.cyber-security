## 摘要

NUKIB Director Lukáš Kintr visited Estonia as part of a presidential delegation to discuss strengthening cybersecurity cooperation between the Czech Republic and Estonia.

## 事實內容

NUKIB Director Lukáš Kintr participated in a presidential delegation visiting Estonia as part of a state visit. NUKIB was further represented in the accompanying delegation by its experts. The purpose of the visit was to discuss and strengthen cybersecurity cooperation between the Czech Republic and Estonia. The event highlights bilateral cooperation on cybersecurity matters at a senior government level.
