## 摘要

First joint NCSC and KPMG UK report benchmarking diversity and inclusion in cyber security industry.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre (UK) and KPMG UK have published their first joint report to benchmark and track levels of diversity and inclusion in the cyber security industry. The report:

- Establishes baseline metrics for gender, ethnicity, disability, and other diversity dimensions
- Analyzes representation across different roles and seniority levels
- Identifies barriers to entry and career progression for underrepresented groups
- Provides recommendations for improving diversity and inclusion
- Creates framework for measuring future progress

This inaugural report serves as a benchmark for the "Decrypting Diversity" series tracking industry progress over time.

## 受影響系統

- Professional development and education programs
- Recruitment and talent management
