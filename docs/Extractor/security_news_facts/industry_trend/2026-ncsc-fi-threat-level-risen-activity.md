# Cyber threat level risen as activity toward Finland increases

## Summary
Cyberattacks have increased globally this year. After a quieter spring period, attacks are rising in Finland. Based on NCSC-FI reports, Finnish organizations increasingly face malware, phishing, and denial-of-service attacks.

## Key Details
- **Year**: 2022
- **Global Trend**: Increased cyberattacks
- **Finland**: Rise after quieter spring
- **Attack Types**: Malware, phishing, DDoS (increasingly employed)
- **Target**: Finnish organizations

## Notes
- Threat level escalation
- Multiple attack vectors
- Confidence: high
