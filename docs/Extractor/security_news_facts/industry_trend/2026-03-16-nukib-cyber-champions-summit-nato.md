## 摘要
NUKIB hosted the Cyber Champions Summit, bringing together representatives from NATO and Indo-Pacific partner nations to strengthen international cybersecurity cooperation and coordination.

## 事實內容
The Czech National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NUKIB) hosted the Cyber Champions Summit, an international gathering attended by representatives from NATO member states and Indo-Pacific partner countries. The summit focused on strengthening cybersecurity cooperation, sharing threat intelligence, and aligning cyber defense strategies among allied and partner nations. The participation of Indo-Pacific partners alongside NATO allies reflects the increasingly global nature of cyber threats and the importance of building resilient, cross-regional cybersecurity networks. NUKIB's role as host underscores the Czech Republic's active engagement in international cybersecurity diplomacy and coalition-building.
