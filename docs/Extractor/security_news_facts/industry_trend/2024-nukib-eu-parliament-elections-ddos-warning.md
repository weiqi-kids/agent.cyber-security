## 摘要
NUKIB warns of elevated risk of unsophisticated DDoS attacks during June 7-9, 2024 European Parliament elections.

## 事實內容
The National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NÚKIB) of the Czech Republic has issued a warning about increased risk of Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks during the European Parliament elections period.

**Election Period:**
- **Dates:** June 7-9, 2024
- **Event:** European Parliament elections
- **Location:** Czech Republic and other EU member states

**Threat Assessment:**
- **Attack Type:** Unsophisticated DDoS attacks
- **Primary Goal:** Media attention and publicity
- **Long-term Impact:** Limited disruption to organization functionality
- **Sophistication:** Low technical complexity

**Likely Targets:**
1. Political parties with candidates in elections
2. Public administration organizations involved in electoral process
3. Media organizations covering elections

**Precedent:**
Similar attacks were observed in the Netherlands where elections were held on Thursday, though not yet recorded in the Czech Republic at the time of the advisory.

**Attacker Motivation:**
NUKIB emphasizes that these attacks typically aim for media coverage rather than causing lasting operational damage to targeted organizations.

**Risk Context:**
While the attacks may temporarily affect website availability or service access, they generally do not represent sophisticated threats to critical infrastructure or data security.
