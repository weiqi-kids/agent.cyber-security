## Summary
Estonia's CERT-EE organizes international cybersecurity summer camp CyberWizards for girls aged 13-16 with 87 participants from 10 countries.

## Facts Content
**Event**: CyberWizards International Cybersecurity Summer Camp
**Date**: August 11-16, 2025
**Location**: Kehtna, Raplamaa, Estonia
**Organizer**: Information System Authority (RIA) of Estonia

**Participants**:
- Total registered: 87 girls
- Age range: 13-16 years old
- Countries represented: 10 different nations
- Year: Third annual event

**Objective**: To spark young people's interest in technology and cybersecurity through hands-on activities and education.

**Notes**:
- Original language: Estonian
- Target audience: Girls specifically, promoting gender diversity in cybersecurity field
- Part of Estonia's broader cybersecurity education initiatives
