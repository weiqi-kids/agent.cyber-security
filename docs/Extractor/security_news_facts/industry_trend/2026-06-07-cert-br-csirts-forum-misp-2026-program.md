## 摘要

CERT.br published the full agenda for the 14th Brazilian CSIRTs Forum and 7th MISP Workshop 2026, to be held July 28-30 in São Paulo.

## 事實內容

CERT.br announced the complete program for two co-located events:

- **14th Brazilian CSIRTs Forum (14º Fórum Brasileiro de CSIRTs)**: Full agenda published at https://forum.cert.br/#agenda
- **7th MISP Workshop (7º Workshop MISP)**: Full agenda published at https://forum.cert.br/#misp

**Venue**: Amcham Business Center, Rua da Paz, 1431, Chácara Santo Antônio, São Paulo/SP, Brazil

**Dates**: July 28–30, 2026

These events serve as key gathering points for Brazilian and regional CSIRT community professionals, covering incident response coordination, threat intelligence sharing (MISP platform), and cybersecurity operations best practices.
