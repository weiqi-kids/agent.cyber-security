## 摘要

C-HUB, supported by regional Cybersecurity Competence Centers and CNCS, is hosting a risk management training workshop at PACT Alentejo Science and Technology Park on 29 May 2025.

## 事實內容

Registrations are open for a risk management training session ("ação de formação") organized by C-HUB, to be held at PACT — Parque do Alentejo de Ciência e Tecnologia (Alentejo Science and Technology Park) on 29 May 2025, starting at 09:30. The initiative is supported by regional Cybersecurity Competence Centers and CNCS, as part of the broader C-HUB capacity-building effort. The training focuses on cybersecurity risk management methodologies and practices for organizations in the Alentejo region and beyond.

## 受影響系統

- Organizations in Alentejo region seeking risk management training
- Portuguese C-Network and C-HUB cybersecurity ecosystem participants
