## Summary

Guest writer Tapani Veija from the "Stop the Scammers" project discusses how online scams affect all age groups, emphasizing digital skills over age-based distinctions in combating scams.

## Details

**Event Type**: Guest Article / Public Awareness

**Project**: Huijarit kuriin ("Stop the Scammers") - Consumers' Union of Finland

**Author**: Tapani Veija, Project Manager

**Key Points**:
- Hotline receives calls from both seniors and young people
- Training events see mostly seniors, webinars attract younger attendees
- Best approach focuses on digital skills, not age groups
- "Your grandma may know better than you do"

**Focus**: Digital skills development across all demographics

## Impact Assessment

**Severity**: Low (Awareness/Education)

**Scope**: National (Finland) - All Demographics

**Value**: Challenges age stereotypes and promotes inclusive digital security education
