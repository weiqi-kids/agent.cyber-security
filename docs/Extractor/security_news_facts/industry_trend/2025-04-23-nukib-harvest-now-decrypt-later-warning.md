## 摘要

NUKIB warns that quantum computers pose a future threat to current cryptographic algorithms through the "harvest now, decrypt later" (HNDL) strategy, where attackers collect encrypted data today to decrypt when quantum computers mature.

## 事實內容

NUKIB issued a warning about the "harvest now, decrypt later" (HNDL) strategy as a cybersecurity threat posed by the future development of quantum computers. Quantum computers, in addition to benefits for many industries, pose a potential threat to cybersecurity because a sufficiently powerful quantum computer could in the future be able to break most currently used cryptographic algorithms. These algorithms protect key aspects of digital security, such as communication encryption, authentication, and digital signatures. HNDL is a strategy in which an attacker already captures and stores encrypted communications today that they cannot yet decrypt. However, they hope that in the future — with the advent of quantum computers — they will have sufficient resources to break the cryptography used and decrypt the data, which may remain valuable even after a time delay. NUKIB advises organizations to begin preparing for post-quantum cryptographic transitions.

## 受影響系統

- All systems using current cryptographic standards (RSA, ECC, etc.)
- Encrypted communications and stored sensitive data
