## 摘要

Czech NUKIB warns about "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" threat where attackers collect encrypted data today for future quantum decryption.

## 事實內容

The Czech NUKIB issued a warning about the "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" (HNDL) threat strategy. While quantum computers bring benefits to many sectors, they pose a potential threat to cybersecurity. A sufficiently powerful quantum computer could break most cryptographic algorithms currently in use, which protect key aspects of digital security such as communication encryption, authentication, and digital signatures. HNDL is a strategy where attackers already capture and store encrypted communications they cannot yet decrypt, hoping that in the future – with the advent of quantum computers – they will have sufficient means to break the cryptography and decrypt this data, which may remain valuable even after time has passed.

## 攻擊手法

- Data harvesting for future quantum decryption
- Long-term encrypted data collection

## 受影響系統

- Organizations using current cryptographic algorithms
- Sensitive communications with long-term value
