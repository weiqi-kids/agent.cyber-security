## Summary
Estonia's national app will test emergency alert functionality for first time on May 14, 2025 during Defense Forces exercise Siil, including sirens in 22 locations.

## Facts Content
**Test Date**: Wednesday, May 14, 2025
**Context**: Estonian Defense Forces large-scale exercise "Siil"
**System**: Complete emergency alert system including sirens and mobile notifications

**Components Being Tested**:
- Siren systems in 22 Estonian towns
- Estonia App emergency alert function (first time)
- Complete emergency notification infrastructure

**Significance**:
- First test of comprehensive emergency alert system
- Critical national security capability
- Integration of traditional (sirens) and modern (app) alert methods

**Purpose**: Validate emergency notification system functionality for real crisis scenarios

**Notes**:
- Original language: Estonian
- Part of larger military exercise
- Demonstrates multi-channel crisis communication approach
- Public advance notice to prevent confusion
