## 摘要

Croatian CERT experts participated in NATO's Locked Shields 2026, the largest international cyber defence exercise, with approximately 4,000 participants from 42 countries.

## 事實內容

Croatian CERT experts participated in Locked Shields 2026, the largest and most complex NATO international cyber defence exercise. This year's exercise brought together approximately 4,000 participants from 42 countries. Croatia's participation was led by the Croatian Armed Forces Cyberspace Command. Participants included experts from the security-intelligence system, CARNET, the National CERT, and the private IT sector. Locked Shields is an annual live-fire cyber defence exercise organized by the NATO Cooperative Cyber Defence Centre of Excellence (CCDCOE).
