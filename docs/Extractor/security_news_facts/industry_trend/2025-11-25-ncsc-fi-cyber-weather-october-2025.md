NCSC-FI October Cyber Weather report shows continued cloudy conditions with slight improvement from September.

## 事實內容

The monthly Cyber Weather report characterizes October 2025 as "cloudy and chilly" from a cybersecurity perspective. The report notes:
- Continuation of autumn cybersecurity challenges
- Overall situation slightly calmer compared to September 2025
- Persistent threats maintaining elevated alert levels
- General trend indicating sustained vigilance required

This represents the ongoing situational awareness reporting from Finland's National Cyber Security Centre.
