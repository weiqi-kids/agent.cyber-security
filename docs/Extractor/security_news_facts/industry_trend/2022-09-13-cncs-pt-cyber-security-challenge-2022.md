## 摘要

ENISA launched the 7th European Cyber Security Challenge 2022 with teams from 33 countries competing over 3 days in web security, mobile security, hardware, cryptography, reverse engineering, and forensics; Portugal participates via CyberSecurity Challenge PT.

## 事實內容

The Cyber Security Challenge 2022, promoted by ENISA (European Union Agency for Cybersecurity), is the 7th edition of Europe's most significant youth cybersecurity competition. Details:

**Participation**: 33 countries (28 European + 5 guests), each represented by a team of 10 young national talents

**Competition categories**:
- Web security
- Mobile security
- Hardware security
- Cryptography
- Reverse engineering
- Digital forensics

**New feature for 2022**: An attack-and-defense challenge where each team must defend its own infrastructure while simultaneously attacking others

**Portugal's representation**: Via CyberSecurity Challenge PT — a consortium of:
- Centro Nacional de Ciberseguran​ça (CNCS)
- Instituto Superior Técnico
- Universidade do Porto
- AP2SI (Associação Portuguesa para a Promoção da Segurança da Informação)

Portugal's 10-member national team was selected through a qualification event held in July 2022 and has been trained by coaches and members of the Portuguese cybersecurity community.
