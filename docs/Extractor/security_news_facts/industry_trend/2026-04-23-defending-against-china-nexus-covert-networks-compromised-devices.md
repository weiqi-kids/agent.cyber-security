## 摘要

CISA, UK NCSC, and international partners jointly published advisory AA26-113A on the widespread shift by China-nexus threat actors toward using covert networks of compromised infrastructure devices (ORB networks) as TTPs, with guidance on detection and defense.

## 事實內容

A joint advisory (AA26-113A) was released by CISA together with international cybersecurity agencies including UK NCSC, Australian Cyber Security Centre (ACSC), Communications Security Establishment Canada (CSE/Cyber Centre), Germany BfV/BND/BSI, Japan NCO, Netherlands AIVD, New Zealand NCSC, and South Korea NIS — collectively analyzing the widespread shift in tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) toward China-nexus covert networks of compromised devices.

Key findings:
- **Threat actor**: China-nexus APT groups (state-affiliated)
- **TTP shift**: Increasing use of Operational Relay Box (ORB) networks — networks of compromised routers, IoT devices, and VPNs — to obfuscate attack origins
- **Target scope**: Government, critical infrastructure, and strategic sector organizations globally
- **Purpose**: Intelligence gathering, espionage, and pre-positioning within targeted networks
- **Detection challenge**: ORB networks make attribution and blocking significantly harder as traffic originates from legitimate-appearing infrastructure

The advisory includes detailed guidance on:
- Identifying compromised edge devices
- Network monitoring for ORB-related indicators
- Hardening recommendations for routers, firewalls, and IoT devices
- Incident response steps if compromise is suspected

The joint publication reflects coordinated concern across Five Eyes and allied nations about this evolving threat pattern.

## 受影響系統

- Edge network devices (routers, firewalls, VPN gateways)
- IoT devices used as relay nodes
- Government and critical infrastructure networks worldwide
