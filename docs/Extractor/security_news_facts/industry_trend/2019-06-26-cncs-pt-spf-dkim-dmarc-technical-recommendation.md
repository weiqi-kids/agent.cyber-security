## 摘要
Portugal's CNCS publishes a technical recommendation on implementing email authentication standards SPF, DKIM, and DMARC to help organizations protect against email spoofing and phishing attacks.

## 事實內容
- **文件類型**: Technical Recommendation (Recomendação Técnica)
- **發布日期**: June 26, 2019
- **主辦單位**: CNCS Portugal (Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança)
- **主題**: Email authentication protocols:
  - SPF (Sender Policy Framework)
  - DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail)
  - DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting & Conformance)
- **目的**: Help organizations reduce email spoofing and phishing risk through proper email authentication configuration

## 受影響系統
- Email servers and DNS infrastructure for Portuguese organizations
- All organizations using email communications
