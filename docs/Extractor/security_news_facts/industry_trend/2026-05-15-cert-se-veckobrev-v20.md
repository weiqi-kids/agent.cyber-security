## 摘要

CERT-SE published short tabletop exercise materials for ransomware, DDoS, and phishing incident response, available at cert.se/tema/ovningar/.

## 事實內容

CERT-SE's week 20 newsletter highlights the publication of simple, concise "Tabletop" exercises covering three cybersecurity incident scenarios: (1) ransomware (utpressningsangrepp), (2) denial-of-service attacks (överbelastningsangrepp), and (3) phishing (nätfiske). The exercises are designed to help organisations practise incident handling. They are freely available at https://www.cert.se/tema/ovningar/.
