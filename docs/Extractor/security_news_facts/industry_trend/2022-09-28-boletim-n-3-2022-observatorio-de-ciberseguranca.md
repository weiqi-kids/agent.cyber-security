## 摘要

CNCS published Bulletin No. 3/2022 analyzing effective cyber hygiene awareness campaigns in organizations, covering campaign stages, behavior change challenges, and effectiveness assessment.

## 事實內容

The Bulletin No. 3/2022 of the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory presents an analysis of the different stages of a cyber hygiene awareness campaign in organizations, aimed at making this type of action more effective and consistent with identified threats in context.

Key topics covered:
- Stages of effective cyber hygiene awareness campaigns
- Difficulties in changing behaviors and assessing the effects of awareness initiatives
- Best practices for designing context-aware awareness programs in organizations
- Curated publications and news relevant to Portuguese cybersecurity

The bulletin provides practical guidance for organizations seeking to improve their cybersecurity awareness programs, with a focus on behavioral aspects and measurable outcomes.
