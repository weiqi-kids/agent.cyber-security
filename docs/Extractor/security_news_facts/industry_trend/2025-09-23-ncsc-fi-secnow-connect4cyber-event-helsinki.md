The Connect4Cyber – Brokerage and Info Day event, held at the end of August 2025 at Business Finland's headquarters in Helsinki, brought together a wide range of cybersecurity professionals from Finland and across Europe. The event facilitated discussions on current funding opportunities, technological development trends, and international cooperation in the cybersecurity domain.
