## 摘要

UK NCSC publishes third annual report on Active Cyber Defence programme achievements covering 2019 activities.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre (UK) has released the year three report of its Active Cyber Defence (ACD) programme, covering activities and achievements throughout 2019. The report:

- Highlights the progress and efforts made by the ACD programme during the year
- Documents protective measures implemented across UK cyber infrastructure
- Demonstrates the impact of proactive defensive strategies in 2019
- Provides insights into programme evolution and expansion

The Active Cyber Defence programme represents the NCSC's approach to reducing cyber threats at scale through automated protective measures and threat intelligence sharing.
