## 事實內容

This TSUBAME Report Overflow series discusses monitoring trends of overseas TSUBAME sensors and other activities which the Internet Threat Monitoring Quarterly Reports does not include. This article covers the monitoring results for the period of October to December 2024, providing analysis of threat patterns and observations from the final quarter of 2024.

The quarterly reports provide valuable insights into evolving threat landscapes and attack trends observed through JPCERT/CC's distributed monitoring infrastructure.
