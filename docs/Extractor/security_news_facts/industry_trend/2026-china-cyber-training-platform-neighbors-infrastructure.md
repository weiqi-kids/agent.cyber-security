## 摘要

Leaked technical documents reveal China operates "Expedition Cloud," a secret training platform enabling cyberattack rehearsals against neighbors' critical infrastructure including power, energy, and transportation networks in South China Sea and Indochina regions.

## 事實內容

Recorded Future News reviewed leaked technical documents exposing China's use of a sophisticated training platform for practicing cyberattacks against neighboring countries' critical infrastructure.

**Training Platform Details:**

1. System Name: "Expedition Cloud"
   - Description: Large integrated system for cyberattack training
   - Purpose: Allow attackers to practice hacking replicas of real network environments
   - Target regions: "Main operational opponents in the South China Sea and Indochina directions"

2. Training Scope:
   - Recreates target computer networks from multiple sectors:
     * Power sector
     * Energy transmission
     * Transportation sector
     * Smart home infrastructure

3. Operational Structure:
   - Evaluates "reconnaissance groups" work
   - Evaluates "attack groups" operations
   - No specified role for defenders
   - Focus on offensive cyber operations against these networks

**Source Information:**
- Documents first reported by NetAskari blog (https://netaskari.substack.com/p/train-to-kill-chinas-secret-training)
- Documents reviewed by Recorded Future News
- Internal files describe system as part of larger infrastructure

**Implications:**
- State-sponsored cyber capability development
- Specific targeting of neighboring countries
- Focus on critical infrastructure disruption capabilities
- Organized training structure with specialized groups

**Geographic Focus:**
- South China Sea region
- Indochina region (Southeast Asian countries)

## 受影響系統

- Critical infrastructure networks in South China Sea region
- Critical infrastructure networks in Indochina region
- Power sector networks
- Energy transmission networks
- Transportation networks
- Smart home infrastructure
