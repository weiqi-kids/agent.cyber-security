## 摘要

Third ConCERT cybersecurity conference held in Zagreb with experts from multiple fields.

## 事實內容

The third ConCERT conference on cybersecurity was held on Friday, February 6, at the National and University Library in Zagreb, organized by CARNET's National CERT. The conference brought together numerous experts in cybersecurity, digital forensics, police investigations, artificial intelligence, and defense systems. The opening program was dedicated to presenting the National CERT Annual Report for 2025. Topics covered included current cyber threats, incident response, and emerging challenges in the Croatian digital landscape.
