## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security guidance on cyber security risks associated with drone use in business or operational contexts, covering threats to systems, data, and networks.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security released awareness guidance document ITSAP.00.143 on cyber security considerations for drone use. Drones are mobile vehicle systems that can function with varying degrees of autonomy from human operators and may be called remotely operated systems, remotely piloted aircraft systems, or uncrewed ground/underwater vehicles. Organizations using drones for business or operational purposes face cyber risks including unauthorized access to drone control systems, interception of data transmitted between drones and operators, exploitation of vulnerabilities in drone firmware and software, and use of compromised drones for reconnaissance or disruption. Understanding these risks enhances an organization's ability to protect systems, data, and networks. The guidance is relevant for organizations in sectors such as agriculture, construction, public safety, and critical infrastructure that are increasingly adopting drone technology.

## 受影響系統

- Drone control systems and ground stations
- Data transmission links between drones and operators
- Drone firmware and onboard software
- Organizational networks connected to drone systems
