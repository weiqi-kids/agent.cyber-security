## 摘要

CNCS opens call for cybersecurity service providers to participate as exhibitors at C-DAYS 2026 (16–18 June, Porto); applications accepted until 26 May 2026.

## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) has opened the Call for Exhibitors for C-DAYS 2026, directed at entities providing cybersecurity services. This is a new initiative introduced in this edition of the conference organized by CNCS.

Eligible entities have the opportunity to participate in the exhibition area of C-DAYS 2026, promoting their visibility and networking over the three days of Portugal's largest national cybersecurity event. Applications are open until 26 May 2026.

Selection criteria are defined in the official regulations and ensure a transparent, consistent, and equitable evaluation process aligned with the conference's objectives.
