## 摘要

UK NCSC assessment: AI will make cyber intrusion more effective and efficient through 2027, expanding access to state and non-state actors, with VRED identified as the most significant AI-driven cyber development.

## 事實內容

The UK National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) published a forward-looking assessment examining how AI developments will impact the cyber threat landscape through 2027. The report focuses specifically on cyber intrusion operations rather than broader AI-enabled threats such as influence campaigns.

**Key findings:**

- **Threat escalation**: AI will "almost certainly continue to make elements of cyber intrusion operations more effective and efficient," with increased frequency and intensity of attacks expected through automated vulnerability discovery and exploitation.
- **Vulnerability Research & Exploit Development (VRED)**: Identified as the most significant AI-related cyber development. AI will reduce time between vulnerability disclosure and active exploitation, increasing zero-day risk.
- **Actor proliferation**: AI-enabled cyber tools will "highly likely expand access" to both state and non-state actors, including criminal groups as AI tools become commercially available.
- **Digital divide risk**: A growing gap is anticipated between organizations that update defenses against AI-enabled threats and those running legacy systems with lower security levels.
- **New attack vectors**: Prompt injection attacks targeting AI systems and automated evasion techniques (malware modification and infrastructure changes) are highlighted as emerging threats.
- **Critical infrastructure at risk**: Critical National Infrastructure (CNI), supply chains, and operational technology with lower security levels face elevated risk.

The NCSC recommends organizations maintain up-to-date cyber security measures and follow NCSC guidance on secure AI deployment.

## 受影響系統

- Critical National Infrastructure (CNI)
- Supply chains
- Operational technology (OT) systems
- AI-integrated systems across the UK technology base
