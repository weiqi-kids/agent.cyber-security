## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security announces GeekWeek 11, a collaborative cyber security event with public sector, industry, critical infrastructure, and international partners, themed "Detection and Deception," scheduled May 27 – June 5, 2026.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (Cyber Centre) announced GeekWeek 11, an innovation-focused cyber security event. The event provides an opportunity for participants to step away from day-to-day work and collaborate with public sector, industry, critical infrastructure, and international partners to explore innovative ideas in cyber security. The theme is "Detection and Deception." The event is scheduled from May 27 to June 5, 2026, and will be held at the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security, 1625 Vanier Parkway, Ottawa, ON K1L 7P1. Due to the technical nature of the event, registration is by invitation. Interested parties may reach out to contact@cyber.gc.ca for future Geek events.
