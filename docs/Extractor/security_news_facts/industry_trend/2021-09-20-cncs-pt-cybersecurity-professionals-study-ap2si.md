## 摘要

The AP2SI association, with support from CNCS's Cybersecurity Observatory, launches the 2nd edition of its study on cybersecurity and information security professionals in Portugal, conducted via online survey open to the community.

## 事實內容

The AP2SI (Associação Portuguesa para a Promoção da Segurança da Informação — Portuguese Association for the Promotion of Information Security), with the support of the Cybersecurity Observatory at the Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS), launched the 2nd Edition of the Study on Cybersecurity and Information Security Professionals in Portugal. The study is conducted through an online questionnaire open to the community. This represents an important industry survey tracking the state of the cybersecurity workforce in Portugal.
