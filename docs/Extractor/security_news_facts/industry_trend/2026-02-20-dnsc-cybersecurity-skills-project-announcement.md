## 摘要

Romanian National Cyber Security Directorate (DNSC) launches selection call for public institutions in cybersecurity skills development project.

## 事實內容

The Romanian National Cyber Security Directorate (DNSC) announces the launch of a selection call for public institutions as beneficiaries in the project "Creating New Cybersecurity Skills for Society and Economy." This initiative aims to develop cybersecurity competencies across public sector organizations in Romania.
