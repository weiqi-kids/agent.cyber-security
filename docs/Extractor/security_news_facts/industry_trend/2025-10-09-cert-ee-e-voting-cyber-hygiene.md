## 摘要

Estonia's RIA urges voters to update device software and be alert to fraud schemes before the election period, where scammers impersonate election officials to steal login credentials for e-voting.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) called on voters to update their device software before elections and to be vigilant to ensure the security of their e-vote. RIA warned that fraud schemes are circulating where scammers present themselves as election officials. Citizens are reminded to update software and be careful about unsolicited communications claiming to be from election authorities. This advisory highlights the ongoing challenge of protecting democratic processes from cyber threats and social engineering attacks during election periods.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian e-voting infrastructure
- Citizen devices used for e-voting
- Estonian election authentication systems
