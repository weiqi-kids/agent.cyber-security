## 摘要

CERT.br opened enrollment (until January 15, 2026) for its 2026 cybersecurity training courses including CSIRT management, incident handling, and advanced incident topics.

## 事實內容

CERT.br announced that enrollment is open until January 15, 2026 (17:00) for its 2026 cybersecurity training courses:

Courses offered:
1. **Overview of Creating and Managing CSIRTs** — Introduction to CSIRT establishment and management
2. **Foundations of Incident Management** — Core incident management principles and practices
3. **Advanced Topics in Incident Handling** — Advanced techniques for security incident response

Enrollment URL: https://cursos.cert.br/inscricao/

Note: These courses are offered by CERT.br, the Brazilian national CERT operated by NIC.br, and are targeted at cybersecurity professionals in Brazil and Latin America.
