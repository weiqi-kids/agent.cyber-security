## 摘要

Taavi Kupper began leading RIA's incident handling department (CERT-EE) from October 1, 2025; previous head Veikko Raasuke continues as architect focusing on e-service security.

## 事實內容

As of October 1, Taavi Kupper leads the incident handling department (CERT-EE) of RIA's Cybersecurity Centre. The previous head, Veikko Raasuke, continues as an architect, focusing on ensuring the security and reliability of state e-services. This represents a leadership transition at the Estonian national CERT organization.
