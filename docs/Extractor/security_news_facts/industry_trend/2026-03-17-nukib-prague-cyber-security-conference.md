## 摘要
The seventh annual Prague Cyber Security Conference opened in Prague, bringing together cybersecurity experts, policymakers, and international partners to discuss the current cyber threat landscape and cooperation.

## 事實內容
NUKIB announced the opening of the seventh annual Prague Cyber Security Conference (PCSC 2026) in Prague. The conference is a flagship international cybersecurity event organized by NUKIB, gathering government officials, security experts, industry representatives, and international partners. Topics typically covered at PCSC include emerging cyber threats, national and EU cybersecurity policy, critical infrastructure protection, and multilateral cooperation frameworks. The conference serves as an important platform for exchanging perspectives on cybersecurity challenges and advancing cooperation among European and global partners.
