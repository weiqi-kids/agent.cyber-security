## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security awareness guidance on identifying malicious email messages including phishing, spear-phishing, and business email compromise tactics used by threat actors.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security released awareness guidance document ITSAP.00.100 on spotting malicious email messages. Email is a primary vector for cyber attacks, with threat actors using it to steal information and gain unauthorized network access. The guidance covers common malicious email types including phishing (mass untargeted campaigns), spear-phishing (targeted attacks using personalized information), whaling (targeting executives), and business email compromise (BEC). Key indicators of malicious emails include suspicious sender addresses, urgent or threatening language, unexpected attachments or links, requests for sensitive information, and poor grammar or spelling. The document notes that threat actors are technologically savvy and aggressively agile, and a successful intrusion can quickly lead to significant damage. Mitigation recommendations include email filtering, user awareness training, and multi-factor authentication.

## 受影響系統

- Email systems and clients
- Organizational networks accessed via phishing
- User accounts targeted by credential harvesting
