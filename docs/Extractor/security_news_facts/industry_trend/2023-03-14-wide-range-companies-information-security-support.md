## 摘要

芬蘭已核准約 180 萬歐元資安發展補助給 77 家企業，共收到 656 份申請，資金持續開放申請。

## 事實內容

Approximately EUR 1.8 million of support for the development of information security has been granted to 77 companies in Finland. The support recipients cover a range of sectors and include companies of different sizes. So far, 656 applications have been received, and the applications of 86 companies have been processed. Support will be granted for as long as funding is available. An appropriation of EUR 6 million has been reserved for the purpose, targeting companies critical to society.
