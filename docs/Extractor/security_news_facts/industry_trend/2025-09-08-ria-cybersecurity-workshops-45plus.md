## 摘要

RIA invites the 45+ age group to participate in free cybersecurity workshops teaching practical tips on keeping money, photos, and personal data secure.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) is organizing free cybersecurity workshops targeting the 45+ age group. The workshops teach simple and practical tips on how to keep money, photos, and personal data safe. This initiative is part of broader cybersecurity awareness and education efforts targeting older demographics who may be more vulnerable to online threats.

- Target audience: Adults 45 years and older
- Format: Free cybersecurity workshops
- Topics: Protecting money, photos, and personal data
- Organizer: RIA (Estonian Information System Authority)

## 受影響系統

- Estonian general public (45+ age group)
