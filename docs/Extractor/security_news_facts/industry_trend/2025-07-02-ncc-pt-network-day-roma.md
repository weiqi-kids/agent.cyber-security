## 摘要

Portugal's National Coordination Centre (NCC-PT) attended the NCC Network Day in Rome, the third meeting of 2025 organized by Italy's ACN, focused on EU cybersecurity project implementation under the Digital Europe Programme.

## 事實內容

The NCC Network Day took place in Rome, organized by Italy's National Cybersecurity Agency (ACN). This was the third meeting of 2025 for National Coordination Centres (NCCs) from across the EU. The meeting brought together NCCs from all 27 EU Member States, as well as coordination centres from Iceland and Norway. The focus of the meeting was on implementing cybersecurity projects under the Digital Europe Programme. Portugal's NCC-PT participated actively, contributing to the coordination of European cybersecurity investment initiatives. The NCC Network facilitates collaboration between Member States on EU-funded cybersecurity capacity-building programmes.

## 受影響系統

- EU National Coordination Centres for cybersecurity
- Digital Europe Programme cybersecurity projects
- EU Member States' national cybersecurity agencies
