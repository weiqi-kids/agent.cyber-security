## 摘要
Over 4,800 organizations registered with NUKIB under new Czech cyber security law effective November 2025.

## 事實內容
The National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NÚKIB) of the Czech Republic reported significant compliance with the new cyber security legislation.

**Key Statistics:**
- **Law Effective Date:** November 1, 2025
- **Registration Deadline:** December 31, 2025
- **Total Registrations:** Over 4,800 organizations

**Background:**
The new Cyber Security Act and related regulations came into force on November 1, 2025. Organizations meeting criteria defined in the regulation on regulated services were required to register with NÚKIB by the end of 2025.

**Registration Success:**
Thanks to active communication from NÚKIB, over 4,800 entities successfully registered their regulated services by the deadline, demonstrating strong compliance with the new regulatory framework.

This represents a significant milestone in Czech cybersecurity governance and regulatory compliance.
