## 事實內容

Chainalysis published its annual crypto ransomware report covering 2025 activity, revealing a complex and evolving threat landscape:

**Payment trends**:
- Total on-chain ransomware payments: ~$820 million in 2025 (down ~8% year-over-year)
- Claimed attacks: increased by 50% year-over-year
- Median ransom payment: grew 368% year-over-year to approximately $60,000
- This marks the second consecutive year of stagnating aggregate payments despite increasing attack volume

**Initial Access Broker (IAB) activity as leading indicator**:
- On-chain analysis shows spikes in IAB inflows typically precede increases in ransomware payments and victim leaks by approximately 30 days
- IAB activity serves as an early warning signal for ransomware campaigns

**Infrastructure convergence**:
- Financially-motivated cybercriminals and state-aligned threat actors are increasingly sharing the same bulletproof hosting providers and residential proxy networks
- This convergence complicates attribution and law enforcement response

**Law enforcement focus shift**:
- In 2025, law enforcement actions and sanctions, as well as private sector efforts, increasingly targeted infrastructure services (hosting, malware loading tools) rather than individual ransomware groups
- This strategy aims to disrupt the broader ecosystem enabling ransomware operations

The divergence between falling payments and rising attack volume suggests victim organizations are increasingly refusing to pay, relying on backups, or benefiting from insurance/negotiation strategies.
