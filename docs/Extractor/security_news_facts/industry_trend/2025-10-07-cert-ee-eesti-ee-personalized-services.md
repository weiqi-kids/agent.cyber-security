## 摘要

Estonia's eesti.ee government portal added two new personalized event services covering military service obligations and divorce procedures, delivering relevant information to users based on their personal situation.

## 事實內容

The eesti.ee government portal now offers two new personalized event services that deliver important information in a format tailored to each user's situation. The two new services cover military service obligations (kaitseväekohustus) and divorce procedures (abielu lahutamine). These personalized event services are part of the broader digital government strategy to make Estonian government services more proactive and user-centric, automatically presenting relevant information to citizens when it applies to their personal circumstances.
