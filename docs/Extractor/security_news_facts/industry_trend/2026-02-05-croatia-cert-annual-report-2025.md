## 摘要

Croatia's National CERT reports 1,513 cybersecurity incidents in 2025, a 35.9% increase from 2024, with phishing attacks being the most prevalent.

## 事實內容

The Croatian National CERT has released its **Annual Activity Report for 2025**, revealing significant growth in cybersecurity incidents.

**Key Statistics**:
- **Total incidents in 2025**: 1,513
- **Increase from 2024**: 35.9%
- **Note**: This count includes only incidents for which National CERT received reports or discovered through its own activities

**Most Common Incident Types in 2025**:
1. **Phishing attacks**: 32% of all incidents
2. Additional incident types not detailed in RSS feed

**Context**: The 35.9% year-over-year increase indicates:
- Growing cyber threat landscape in Croatia
- Increased detection and reporting capabilities
- Phishing remains the primary attack vector

This report provides valuable insights into the evolving threat landscape in Croatia and can serve as a reference for regional cybersecurity trends.
