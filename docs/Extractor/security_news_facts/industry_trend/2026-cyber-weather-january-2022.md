# January 2022 cyber weather report

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/ajankohtaista/kybersaa_01/2022  
**Published**: 2022-03-01

## Summary

January 2022 cyber weather was mainly dry with alerts on Log4shell vulnerability and FluBot malware lifted. However, Finnish diplomats were targeted by cyber espionage and Finland saw its largest volumetric DDoS attack on record.

## Key Points

- **Positive developments**: Log4shell and FluBot alerts lifted
- **Cyber espionage**: Finnish diplomats targeted
- **DDoS record**: Volumetrically largest denial-of-service attack in Finland
- **Overall assessment**: Mixed weather with both improvements and new threats

## Impact

While some threats subsided, the targeting of diplomatic personnel and record DDoS attacks demonstrate evolving threat landscape requiring continued vigilance.
