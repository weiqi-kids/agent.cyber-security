# Scenario bank for cyber exercises

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/scenario-bank-cyber-exercises-support-planning-contents-exercise  
**Published**: 2022-06-20

## Summary

NCSC-FI launched a new scenario bank service containing previously published and new scenario ideas to support the planning of cyber exercises. The aim is to facilitate ideas for and contents of exercises.

## Key Points

- **New service**: Scenario bank for cyber exercise planning
- **Content**: Previously published and new scenario ideas
- **Purpose**: Help organizations plan more effective cyber exercises
- **Accessibility**: Available to support exercise organizers

## Impact

This resource helps organizations design more realistic and effective cyber exercises by providing tested scenarios, reducing the barrier to conducting quality training exercises.
