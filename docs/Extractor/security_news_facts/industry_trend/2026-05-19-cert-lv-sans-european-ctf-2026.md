## 摘要

SANS 宣布 2026 歐洲 CTF 錦標賽，涵蓋 45 國參賽者，旨在提升網路安全實戰技能。

## 事實內容

SANS announced the CTF European Championship 2026, a new pan-European cybersecurity competition bringing together participants from 45 countries. The virtual CTF tournament is aimed at cybersecurity professionals, students, and enthusiasts looking to test their practical skills and compete for the title of the first-ever SANS European CTF Champion. The initiative was highlighted by CERT.LV as a significant regional event supporting the development of cybersecurity talent across Europe.
