The 2020 information security seminar organized by NCSC-FI and National Emergency Supply Agency was held virtually, focusing on trust, cooperation, and corporate board responsibility.

**Event Type**: Conference/Seminar

**Organizers**:
- National Cyber Security Centre Finland (NCSC-FI) at Traficom
- National Emergency Supply Agency

**Format**: Virtual event (2020 COVID-19 adaptation)

**Central Themes**:
- Trust
- Cooperation between stakeholders
- Responsibility of corporate boards
- Future questions in cybersecurity
