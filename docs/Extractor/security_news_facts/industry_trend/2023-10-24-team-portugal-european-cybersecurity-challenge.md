## 事實內容

Team Portugal, with 10 young participants in junior and senior age groups, competed in the European Cybersecurity Challenge 2023 (ECSC 2023). The competition was organized by the Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU) with support from ENISA, the EU Agency for Cybersecurity. ECSC is an annual competition promoting cybersecurity skills among young European talent. Portugal's participation reflects CNCS's commitment to developing national cybersecurity human capital and fostering EU-level cooperation in skills development.
