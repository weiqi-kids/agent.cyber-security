## 摘要

"Together for a Better Internet" conference held in Zagreb on February 10 to mark Safer Internet Day, featuring institutions and children discussing online safety.

## 事實內容

The Center for Safer Internet, HAKOM, and CARNET's CERT organized the **"Together for a Better Internet"** conference on February 10 in Zagreb to commemorate Safer Internet Day.

**Conference Details**:
- **Date**: February 10, 2026
- **Location**: Zagreb, Croatia
- **Organizers**: Center for Safer Internet, HAKOM, CARNET CERT
- **Opening Speakers**:
  - Miro Đuzel (Assistant Director for National CERT at CARNET)
  - Tomislav Ramljak (Head of Center for Safer Internet)
  - Tonko Obuljen (President of HAKOM Council)

**Purpose**: Safer Internet Day serves as a reminder about the importance of digital safety and brings together institutions and children to discuss security in the digital environment.

The event emphasizes collaboration between government institutions, educational organizations, and young people in promoting internet safety awareness.
