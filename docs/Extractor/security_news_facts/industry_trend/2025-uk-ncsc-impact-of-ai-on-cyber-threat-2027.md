## 摘要

UK NCSC assessment forecasting AI's impact on cyber threats through 2027, analyzing emerging attack capabilities.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre (UK) has published an assessment highlighting the projected impacts of artificial intelligence developments on the cyber threat landscape from 2025 to 2027. The assessment:

- Analyzes how AI advancements will enhance attacker capabilities
- Evaluates defensive applications of AI technology
- Forecasts changes in threat actor tactics and techniques
- Provides strategic guidance for organizations to prepare for AI-enhanced threats
- Examines the dual-use nature of AI in both offensive and defensive cyber operations

This forward-looking analysis helps security professionals and policymakers understand the evolving threat environment as AI technologies become more sophisticated and accessible.
