## 摘要

NCSC-FI August Cyber Weather shows end of calm summer with storm clouds in data breaches, malware and vulnerabilities.

## 事實內容

The monthly Cyber Weather report characterizes August 2025 as marking the end of calm summer conditions:
- **End of calm summer season** for cybersecurity
- **Storm clouds gathered** particularly in specific areas:
  - Data breaches - significant increase
  - Malware activity - elevated threat levels
  - Vulnerabilities - new discoveries and active exploitation

The report indicates a significant deterioration from typical summer lull, suggesting increased threat actor activity as organizations return from vacation periods.

## 受影響系統

- Systems exposed to data breach campaigns
- Malware-targeted infrastructure
- Unpatched vulnerable systems
