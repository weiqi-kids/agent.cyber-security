## 摘要

A joint guidance publication outlining desirable end-states for secure connectivity in operational technology (OT) environments, intended as design goals rather than minimum requirements for organizations.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security, in coordination with partner agencies, released joint guidance on secure connectivity principles for operational technology (OT) environments. The guidance:

- Outlines desirable end-states that organizations should achieve when designing connectivity into OT environments
- Frames the end-states as goals rather than minimum requirements
- Addresses the growing need to secure OT networks against cyber threats
- Provides actionable direction for organizations operating industrial control systems and critical infrastructure

The document is intended to help CI owners and operators improve the security posture of their OT connectivity architecture.

## 受影響系統

- Operational Technology (OT) environments
- Industrial Control Systems (ICS)
- Critical Infrastructure networks
