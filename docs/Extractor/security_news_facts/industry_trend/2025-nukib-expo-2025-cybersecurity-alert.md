## 摘要
NUKIB warns of expected increased cyber threat activity targeting EXPO 2025 participants before and during the event.

## 事實內容
The National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NÚKIB) of the Czech Republic has issued a situational report warning about elevated cybersecurity risks associated with EXPO 2025 in Osaka, Japan.

**Event Details:**
- **Location:** Osaka, Japan
- **Duration:** April 13 - October 13, 2025
- **Type:** World Exposition (EXPO 2025)

**Threat Assessment:**
Due to the international nature and high visibility of EXPO 2025, NÚKIB anticipates increased interest from cyber threat actors who may target:
- Event participants
- Visitors
- Organizations involved in the exposition
- Supporting infrastructure

**Timeline:**
Cyber threat activity is expected not only during the event but also in the period leading up to the exhibition opening.

**Types of Expected Threats:**
- Phishing campaigns targeting participants
- Credential harvesting attacks
- Social engineering attempts
- Supply chain compromises
- Espionage activities
- Hacktivist operations

**Recommendations:**
NUKIB advises increased caution and vigilance for all individuals and organizations involved in or attending EXPO 2025, including Czech participants and businesses.
