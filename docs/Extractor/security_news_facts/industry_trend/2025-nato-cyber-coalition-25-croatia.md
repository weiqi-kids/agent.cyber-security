## 摘要

Croatia hosted NATO's annual Cyber Coalition 25 exercise with 1000+ participants from 30+ countries at the Croatian Military Academy.

## 事實內容

The international NATO exercise "Cyber Coalition 25" was conducted at the Croatian Military Academy "Dr. Franjo Tuđman" from November 28 to December 5, 2025. Cyber Coalition 25 is one of NATO's largest and most significant cybersecurity exercises, serving as the leading annual NATO collective cyber defense exercise. The event brought together over 1,000 participants from more than 30 countries.
