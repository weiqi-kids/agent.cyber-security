# Cyber Exercise Scenarios for 2021 publication

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/discover-new-cyber-exercise-ideas-our-new-publication  
**Published**: 2021-02-05

## Summary

New publication "Cyber Exercise Scenarios for 2021" contains realistic threat scenarios from payment system data leaks to large-scale epidemics. Designed to help organizations identify suitable threats for their cyber exercises.

## Key Points

- **Publication**: Cyber Exercise Scenarios for 2021
- **Content**: Range of realistic threat scenarios
- **Examples**: Payment system leaks, epidemic scenarios
- **Purpose**: Help organizations design exercises
- **Approach**: Identify most suitable potential threats

## Impact

Providing realistic scenarios lowers barriers to conducting effective exercises, improving organizational preparedness through guided training resources.
