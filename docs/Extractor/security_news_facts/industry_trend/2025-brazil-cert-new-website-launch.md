## 摘要

CERT.br launches redesigned website with lighter layout, improved mobile viewing, and archived older publications.

## 事實內容

The Brazilian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT.br) has launched a completely redesigned website with several improvements:

**Key Improvements:**
- Lighter, more modern layout
- Enhanced viewing experience on mobile devices
- Historical publications moved to dedicated archival area
- Improved navigation and user experience

The new website maintains CERT.br's commitment to providing cybersecurity information and resources to the Brazilian community while offering a more accessible and user-friendly platform.

Website: https://cert.br/
