## Summary

Check Point reports Latin America is now the most heavily targeted region globally, with organizations facing approximately 3,100 cyber threats per week versus 1,500 in the U.S.

## Factual Content

According to Check Point research, Latin America has become the most heavily targeted region for cyberattacks globally:

- In 2025, Check Point tracked a 53% year-over-year rise in weekly cyberattacks in Latin America.
- As of 2026, Latin America is confirmed as the most heavily targeted region on the planet.
- An updated March 2026 threat report (unpublished) found that Latin American organizations face an average of approximately 3,100 cyber threats per week.
- U.S. organizations averaged just under 1,500 threats per week for comparison.
- Contributing factors include relatively rapid digital adoption by businesses in the region combined with relatively stagnant cybersecurity growth.

## Affected Systems

- Latin American business and organizational networks
- Digital infrastructure across the region
