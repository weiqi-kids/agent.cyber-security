## 摘要

Educational article explaining data breaches following City of Helsinki incident, including legal definitions under Finnish Criminal Code.

## 事實內容

Article covers:
- Definition of data breach as unauthorized intrusion into information systems, services or devices
- Unauthorized use of applications with obtained credentials (e.g., email accounts)
- Data breach as punishable offense under Finnish Criminal Code
- Attempted data breach also punishable
- General discussion of data breach concepts

Educational resource using Helsinki breach as context.
