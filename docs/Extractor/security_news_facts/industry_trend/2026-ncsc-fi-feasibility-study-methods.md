## Summary

NCSC-FI's feasibility study successfully tested and developed new, agile and scalable methods for improving cyber security, highlighting that complex isolated networks are not always as well isolated as assumed.

## Details

**Event Type**: Research Publication

**Key Findings**:
- Companies need to identify material requiring protection for efficient protection
- Complex isolated networks may not be as well isolated as believed
- New agile and scalable security improvement methods developed and tested

**Research Focus**:
- Material identification and classification
- Network isolation effectiveness
- Scalable security methodologies
