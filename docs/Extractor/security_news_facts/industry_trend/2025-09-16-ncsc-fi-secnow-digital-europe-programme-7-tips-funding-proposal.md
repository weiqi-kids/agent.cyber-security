## 摘要

NCSC-FI shares 7 lessons learned from Digital Europe Programme funding calls to help applicants improve proposal quality as competition increases.

## 事實內容

The Digital Europe Programme is now four years old and funding calls have become increasingly competitive as applicants better understand the programme. NCSC-FI has published a guide based on recurring deficiencies identified by programme reviewers in previous funding rounds. The article provides seven tips and best practices to help applicants improve their proposal quality and stand out from other submissions. The guidance aims to address common weaknesses that typically result in lower scores during evaluation.
