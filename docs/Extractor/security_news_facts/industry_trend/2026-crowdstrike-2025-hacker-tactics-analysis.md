## 摘要

CrowdStrike published in-depth analysis of 2025 hacker tactics evolution, revealing long-term strategic planning and cross-domain attack methodologies.

## 事實內容

CrowdStrike's 2025 threat research analyzed evolving hacker tactics, identifying key trends in adversary methodologies including long-term strategic planning (patient, persistent campaigns) and cross-domain attack techniques (targeting multiple sectors or technologies in coordinated campaigns).

**Key Trends Identified**:
- **Long-Term Planning**: Adversaries conducting patient, persistent campaigns rather than opportunistic attacks
- **Cross-Domain Attacks**: Coordinated campaigns spanning multiple sectors, technologies, or geographies
- Evolution of tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs)
- Increased sophistication in operational security and evasion

**Significance**:
- Understanding evolving threat landscapes critical for effective defense
- Organizations must adapt security strategies to counter long-term, multi-faceted campaigns
- Cross-domain awareness increasingly important

## 受影響系統

- Various systems across multiple sectors targeted by evolved threat actor campaigns
