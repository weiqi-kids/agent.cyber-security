## 摘要

Eesti app users can now verify their identity at the Agricultural Registers and Information Board (PRIA) and the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund service points, using ID-card or passport data via smartphone.

## 事實內容

Eesti app users can now verify their identity at two additional service providers: the Agricultural Registers and Information Board (PRIA) and the Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund (Eesti Töötukassa) service points. Users can present their ID-card or passport data conveniently and securely directly via their smartphone. This expands the ecosystem of organizations accepting mobile identity verification through the Eesti app.

- New service providers: PRIA (Agricultural Registers and Information Board), Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund
- Authentication method: ID-card or passport data via Eesti app on smartphone
- Security benefit: Convenient and secure mobile identity verification

## 受影響系統

- Eesti app (mobile identity verification)
- PRIA (Agricultural Registers and Information Board)
- Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund (Töötukassa)
