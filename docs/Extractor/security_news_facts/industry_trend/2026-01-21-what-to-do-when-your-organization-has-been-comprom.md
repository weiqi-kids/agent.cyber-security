## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security awareness guidance on actions to take in the critical moments after a cyber compromise is detected to minimize organizational impact.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security released awareness guidance document ITSAP.00.009 on actions organizations should take after detecting a cyber compromise. The publication provides guidance on the critical moments following compromise detection, covering immediate containment steps, evidence preservation, notification obligations, and recovery actions to lessen the impact on the organization. The guidance helps organizations understand when to engage external assistance, including the Cyber Centre's 24/7 reporting line, and how to coordinate with law enforcement. The document is part of the Awareness series and is intended for a broad organizational audience.

## 受影響系統

- Organizational IT infrastructure (post-compromise)
- Incident response processes
- Notification and communication systems
