## 摘要

CNCS releases the third episode of its podcast "Comunicar Cibersegurança," discussing risk management in cybersecurity with Fernando Mendes (GDPR.pt / Focus2Comply), covering legal obligations under Portugal's Cybersecurity Legal Framework and CNCS's Risk Management Guide.

## 事實內容

- **Episode**: Third episode of the CNCS podcast "Comunicar Cibersegurança" (Communicating Cybersecurity).
- **Topic**: Risk management in cybersecurity contexts.
- **Guest**: Fernando Mendes, founding partner of GDPR.pt and Focus2Comply.
- **Key discussion points**:
  - Systematic identification and mitigation of key organisational risks
  - Legal obligations under Portugal's "Regime Jurídico da Segurança do Ciberespaço" (Cybersecurity Legal Framework) and its regulations
  - CNCS's "Guia para a Gestão dos Riscos" (Risk Management Guide) recently published at https://www.cncs.gov.pt/pt/gestao-de-risco/
- **Host**: Ruth Ferrony (CNCS).
- **Video**: Available at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2o1KRgy36Xs

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese organisations subject to the Cybersecurity Legal Framework
- Cybersecurity and compliance professionals in Portugal
