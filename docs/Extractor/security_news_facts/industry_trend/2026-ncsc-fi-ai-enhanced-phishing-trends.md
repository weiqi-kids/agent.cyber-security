# Increased vigilance required against AI-enhanced phishing attacks

## Summary
Phishing and scam campaigns are evolving with criminals utilizing machine learning, artificial intelligence, and psychological methods. NCSC-FI estimates approximately 100 Finnish organisations have had email accounts compromised in recent months.

## Key Details
- **Technologies Used**: Machine learning, artificial intelligence
- **Methods**: Psychological manipulation
- **Impact**: ~100 Finnish organisations compromised
- **Timeline**: Last few months (prior to June 2023)

## Context
The lucrative nature of phishing campaigns drives continuous evolution of attack techniques, combining emerging AI technologies with established social engineering methods.
