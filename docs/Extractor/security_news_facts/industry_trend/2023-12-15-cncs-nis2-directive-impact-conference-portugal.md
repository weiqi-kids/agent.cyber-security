## 摘要

CNCS coordinator presents at NIS2 directive conference addressing impacts for Portuguese businesses and public sector.

## 事實內容

The CNCS coordinator Lino Santos was one of five invited speakers at a conference on "Cybersecurity: Impacts of the NIS2 Directive for Businesses and the Public Sector," organized by TSF/DN on December 14 at the Faculty of Law of the University of Lisbon. The event assessed the status of the transposition of the EU's New Cybersecurity Directive (NIS2) into Portuguese law and discussed its implications for organizations across sectors. The NIS2 Directive significantly expands the scope of cybersecurity obligations compared to the original NIS Directive.
