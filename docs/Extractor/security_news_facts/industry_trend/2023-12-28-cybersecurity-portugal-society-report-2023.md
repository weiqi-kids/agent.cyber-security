## 摘要

CNCS published the fifth edition of the Cybersecurity in Portugal — Society 2023 report, analyzing the human dimension of cybersecurity including attitudes, behaviors, and digital skill levels of the Portuguese population.

## 事實內容

The CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory published the "Cybersecurity in Portugal — Society 2023" report, now in its fifth edition. The report analyzes the human component of cybersecurity in Portugal, covering population attitudes toward online risks, cybersecurity behaviors, digital literacy levels, and incident victimization rates. Key topics include online fraud prevalence, password hygiene habits, use of security tools, and awareness of data protection rights among Portuguese citizens. The report also examines trends over multiple years, enabling comparison with previous editions. This publication informs CNCS policy decisions on public awareness campaigns and educational initiatives.
