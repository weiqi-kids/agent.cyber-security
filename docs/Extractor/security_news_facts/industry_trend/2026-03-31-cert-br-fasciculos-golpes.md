## 摘要

CERT.br published two new booklets in its Internet Security Guide series focused on identifying and avoiding online scams and fraud.

## 事實內容

CERT.br released two new booklets (fascículos) as part of its Cartilha de Segurança para Internet (Internet Security Guide):

1. **"Golpes: Não se Deixe Enganar" (Scams: Don't Be Fooled)**: Covers how to identify signs of scams, noting that fraudsters constantly create new tricks to deceive users. Available at https://cartilha.cert.br/fasciculos/#golpes

2. **"Golpes: Evite Fraudes" (Scams: Avoid Fraud)**: Covers how fraudsters exploit both emotional manipulation and security vulnerabilities to execute scams, with guidance on how to protect oneself from internet-based fraud. Available at https://cartilha.cert.br/fasciculos/#golpes-evite-fraudes

These publications are part of CERT.br's ongoing public awareness and cybersecurity education efforts for Brazilian internet users.
