## 摘要
Estonia's Information System Authority (RIA) is urging public sector organizations to adopt IPv6, aligning with broader European digital infrastructure modernization goals.

## 事實內容
The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) issued a call to public sector institutions to begin adopting IPv6 as part of national digital infrastructure modernization. The transition to IPv6 is considered essential for ensuring long-term connectivity, scalability, and security of public services. RIA emphasizes that public sector organizations should plan and implement IPv6 adoption to keep pace with the depletion of IPv4 address space and to support future digital service demands.
