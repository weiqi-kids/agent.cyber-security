## 摘要

CERT-SE published guidance on securing communication applications such as Signal, following reports of phishing attacks targeting these platforms in early 2026. Threat actors exploit QR codes, fake support messages, and social engineering rather than technical vulnerabilities to compromise private chats and contacts.

## 事實內容

- Earlier in 2026, multiple media outlets reported phishing campaigns targeting communication applications including Signal.
- Threat actors use QR codes, create fake support messages, and employ social engineering techniques rather than exploiting technical vulnerabilities.
- Attackers can leverage common default settings in communication apps to gain access to private chats and contact lists.
- CERT-SE issued advisory guidance to help users protect themselves against these social engineering-based attacks on messaging platforms.

> Original language: sv (CERT-SE, Sweden)

## 受影響系統

- Signal and other communication/messaging applications
- Mobile and desktop platforms running messaging apps
- Users of communication apps with default security settings
