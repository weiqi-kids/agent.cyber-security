## Summary

Survey of 21,000 teachers reveals cybersecurity needs of Portuguese schools during COVID-19 distance learning transition.

## Factual Content

The COVID-19 pandemic forced rapid adaptation of Schools to distance learning, particularly from March 16, 2020, a situation that continued at least until the end of the 2019/2020 school year. This circumstance brought various cybersecurity challenges and showed the importance of this area for educational digitalization needs. The survey was answered by about 21,000 teachers and presents very relevant results for understanding Schools' cybersecurity needs, both for responding to situations like the pandemic and for maintaining safe teaching activities in the future.

## Affected Systems

- Portuguese educational institutions
- Distance learning platforms
