## 摘要

NUKIB organized a CYRIS cybersecurity research and innovation group meeting on May 28, 2026, co-hosted with Aricoma, focusing on R&D utilization across organizations.

## 事實內容

On May 28, 2026, a meeting of the CYRIS (cybersecurity research and innovation) group was held, organized by the Department of Science, Research and Innovation of NUKIB in cooperation with Aricoma. The main topic was the nature of research and development and the use of its results across all types of organizations. The event took place at the premises of the parent company KKCG in Prague. The CYRIS group aims to bridge the gap between cybersecurity research and its practical application in various sectors.

## 受影響系統

- Czech cybersecurity research and innovation ecosystem
- NUKIB R&D initiatives
