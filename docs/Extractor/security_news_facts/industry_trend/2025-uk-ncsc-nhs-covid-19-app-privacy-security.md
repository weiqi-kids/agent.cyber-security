## 摘要

UK NCSC technical paper detailing privacy and security design of NHS COVID-19 contact tracing application.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre (UK) has published a technical paper describing the privacy and security design of the NHS contact tracing app developed to help slow the spread of coronavirus. The paper:

- Details the cryptographic protocols used to protect user privacy
- Explains the decentralized architecture minimizing central data collection
- Documents security controls preventing abuse and unauthorized access
- Addresses privacy concerns around location tracking and contact data
- Provides transparency on data handling and retention policies

The NCSC's involvement helped ensure the app met high standards for both security and privacy protection during a public health emergency.

## 受影響系統

- Mobile devices (iOS and Android)
