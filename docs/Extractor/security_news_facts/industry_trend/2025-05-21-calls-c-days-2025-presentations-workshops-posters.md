## 摘要

The 11th edition of the C-DAYS cybersecurity conference expands its call for submissions to include presentations, workshops, and posters on innovative cybersecurity topics.

## 事實內容

The 11th edition of the C-DAYS cybersecurity conference, organized by CNCS (Portugal's National Cybersecurity Centre), opened calls for presentations, workshops, and posters. The conference expanded beyond its traditional call for presentations to also accept workshops and poster submissions. Participants with innovative visions, relevant case studies, impactful experiences, ideas, projects, or emerging technologies aligned with the C-DAYS themes were invited to submit proposals. The initiative demonstrates Portugal's commitment to fostering innovation and development in the cybersecurity field through community engagement and knowledge sharing.

## 受影響系統

- Cybersecurity professionals and researchers
- Academic institutions
- Portuguese cybersecurity community
