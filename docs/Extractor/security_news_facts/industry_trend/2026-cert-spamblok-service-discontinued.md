## 摘要
CERT.hr announced the discontinuation of the CERT Spamblok service, thanking users for years of service usage.

## 事實內容
CERT.hr has officially discontinued the CERT Spamblok service as of January 7, 2026. The organization thanks users for their years of utilizing the CERT Spamblok service and invites them to continue following their websites for information about new future CERT services. This marks the end of a long-running anti-spam service provided by Croatia's national CERT team.

## 受影響系統

- Email servers using spamblok.cert.hr
