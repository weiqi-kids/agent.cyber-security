## 摘要

Canadian Cyber Centre identifies eight primary generative AI threats including misinformation, phishing, data privacy risks, and malicious code generation capabilities.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security published ITSAP.00.041 identifying eight primary threats associated with generative AI.

**Eight Primary Threats:**

1. **Misinformation**: "Content not clearly identified as being AI-generated can result in the spread of misinformation" and fraudulent campaigns

2. **Phishing**: Threat actors can create highly realistic, targeted attacks at scale with greater sophistication

3. **Data Privacy**: Users may unintentionally expose personally identifiable information or sensitive corporate data through prompts

4. **Malicious Code**: Both skilled and novice actors can generate functional malware using these tools

5. **Buggy Code**: Developers may inadvertently introduce security vulnerabilities into production systems

6. **Poisoned Datasets**: Training data can be compromised, enabling supply-chain attacks

7. **Biased Content**: Models trained on internet data reflect inherent biases and incomplete representation

8. **IP Theft**: Criminals can steal proprietary information more efficiently and at larger volumes

**Critical Best Practices for Organizations:**
- Implement multi-factor authentication across all systems
- Apply security patches automatically and immediately
- Monitor networks for suspicious activity patterns
- Train employees to recognize social engineering tactics
- Create clear policies on acceptable AI use and content generation

**Best Practices for Individuals:**
- Avoid sharing sensitive information with AI tools
- Verify AI-generated content through credible sources before use
- Maintain strong passwords and enable MFA
- Exercise caution with unsolicited communications and attachments

**Vendor Selection & Oversight:**
When adopting commercial AI solutions, organizations must:
- Verify dataset sources
- Validate methods and security practices
- Implement diverse review teams to identify inherent biases
- Continuously refine systems using external feedback

The guidance emphasizes proactive governance and continuous monitoring to address the dual-use nature of generative AI technology, which can be leveraged by both legitimate users and threat actors.

## 受影響系統

- Generative AI platforms and tools
- Organizational networks (phishing vectors)
- Development environments (code generation)
- Data privacy systems
- AI training infrastructure (dataset poisoning)
- Communication systems (misinformation campaigns)
