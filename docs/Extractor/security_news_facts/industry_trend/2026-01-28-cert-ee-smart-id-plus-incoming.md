## 摘要

Estonia's RIA announced it will deploy Smart-ID+ functionality to the national authentication service in late February, making authentication more secure and user-friendly while helping prevent fraud and social engineering.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) announced that it will deploy Smart-ID+ functionality to the national authentication service in late February 2026. The upgrade will make authentication more secure and user-friendly compared to current methods. Additionally, it will help prevent fraud and counter social engineering and other types of fraud attempts. This advance announcement gives service providers and users time to prepare for the upcoming authentication infrastructure upgrade.

## 受影響系統

- Smart-ID authentication
- National authentication service (riigi autentimisteenus)
- Estonian national e-services
