## 事實內容

This article explores the practical challenges security researchers face when attempting to classify and attribute APT (Advanced Persistent Threat) actors. Using the Lazarus group and its various subgroups as a case study, JPCERT/CC examines the difficulties in determining organizational structures, operational relationships, and accurate attribution. The discussion highlights methodological challenges in threat intelligence, including distinguishing between separate groups versus subgroups of larger organizations, and the implications of attribution decisions for threat tracking and response.
