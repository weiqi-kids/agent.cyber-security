## 摘要

For Cybersecurity Month October 2025, Estonia's RIA focuses on business leaders, urging them to take responsibility for their company's cybersecurity.

## 事實內容

October is Cybersecurity Month. For 2025, the Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) focused on business leaders (ettevõtete juhid) and called on them to take responsibility for their company's cybersecurity. This messaging emphasizes that cybersecurity is a leadership responsibility rather than solely a technical issue. The campaign aligns with EU-wide Cybersecurity Month (ECSM) activities and represents a strategic communications effort to shift cybersecurity from an IT department concern to a board-level priority for Estonian businesses.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian businesses (cybersecurity governance)
- Business leadership accountability
