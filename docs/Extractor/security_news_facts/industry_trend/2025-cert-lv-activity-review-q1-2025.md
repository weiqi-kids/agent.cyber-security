## 事實內容
In the first quarter of 2025, the cyber threat landscape in Latvia and globally continued its evolution with increasing intensity, complexity, and strategic direction. The report highlights that cyber operations are no longer isolated attacks or simple profit-seeking activities. Instead, they have become increasingly targeted, persistent, and strategically aligned with broader geopolitical and economic developments. This transformation represents a shift toward more sophisticated, state-aligned cyber operations with long-term strategic objectives.
