## 摘要

CSE and the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security urge Canadian organizations and critical infrastructure providers to strengthen defences against Russian-aligned cyber threats on the fourth anniversary of Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine, citing ongoing DDoS attacks, ransomware, and OT targeting.

## 事實內容

The Communications Security Establishment Canada (CSE) and its Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (Cyber Centre) issued an advisory urging Canadian organizations to stay vigilant and strengthen defences as the four-year mark of Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine approaches.

Key findings:
- Over the past four years, the Cyber Centre has observed pro-Russia cyber actors targeting countries that support Ukraine, including Canada.
- Activities have affected government and military agencies, private and public sector organizations, and critical infrastructure networks in Canada.
- Russian cyber threat actors have attempted to disrupt services via DDoS attacks targeting cloud-based platforms, supply chains, and Internet-facing systems.
- Ideologically motivated, pro-Russia non-state cyber groups are conducting malicious activity against perceived enemies; these groups are generally less sophisticated than state-sponsored actors but act independently, creating unpredictability.
- Operators of Internet-connected operational technology (OT) devices are at elevated risk as these systems are easily discoverable and vulnerable.

Recommended actions from the Cyber Centre:
- Adopt the Cyber Centre's Cross-Sector Cyber Security Readiness Goals.
- Follow Cyber Centre guidance on: ransomware, IT incident response, website defacement, and DDoS attacks.
- Consult the Cyber Centre's top 10 security actions to protect Internet-connected networks.
- Remain alert for potential service disruptions, website defacement, and increased ransomware activity.

## 受影響系統

- Canadian government and military agencies
- Private and public sector organizations
- Critical infrastructure networks
- Cloud-based platforms and supply chains
- Internet-connected operational technology (OT) devices
