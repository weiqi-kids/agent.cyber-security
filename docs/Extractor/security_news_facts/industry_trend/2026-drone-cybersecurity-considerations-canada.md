## 摘要

Canadian Cyber Centre publishes guidance on cybersecurity risks associated with business and operational drone use, covering remotely operated systems and uncrewed vehicles.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security has published ITSAP.00.143, providing guidance on cyber security considerations for organizational drone use.

**Scope of Guidance:**
The document covers various types of drone systems:
- Remotely operated systems
- Remotely piloted aircraft systems
- Uncrewed ground vehicles
- Uncrewed underwater vehicles

These are defined as "mobile vehicle systems that can function with varying degrees of autonomy from human operators."

**Purpose:**
Understanding the risks associated with the use of drones for business or operational purposes will enhance an organization's ability to protect:
- Systems
- Data
- Networks

**Context:**
Drones are increasingly used for business and operational purposes, introducing new cybersecurity considerations for organizations. The guidance aims to help organizations assess and mitigate risks when deploying drone technology.

**Key Considerations:**
The document addresses cybersecurity risks specific to:
- Varying degrees of autonomy in drone operations
- Business use cases
- Operational deployments
- System, data, and network protection

This publication recognizes the growing adoption of drone technology in commercial and operational contexts and provides a framework for organizations to evaluate security implications.

## 受影響系統

- Remotely operated drone systems
- Remotely piloted aircraft systems
- Uncrewed ground vehicles
- Uncrewed underwater vehicles
- Drone control systems
- Data transmission networks for drones
- Organizational networks connected to drone operations
