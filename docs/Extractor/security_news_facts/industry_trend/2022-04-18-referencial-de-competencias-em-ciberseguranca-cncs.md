## 摘要

CNCS published its Cybersecurity Competency Framework as a tool for training and hiring cybersecurity professionals, defining reference competencies and knowledge for cybersecurity roles.

## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) created a Cybersecurity Competency Framework (Referencial de Competências em Cibersegurança) as a useful instrument for training and contracting professionals in the cybersecurity area.

Key characteristics:
- Presents a set of reference competencies and knowledge required for fulfilling cybersecurity functions and tasks
- Designed as a non-rigid document to support the development of the cybersecurity sector across its various areas
- Also contributes to the definition and formulation of public policies in the cybersecurity domain
- Intended to serve multiple stakeholders: training providers, HR departments, policy makers

The framework provides a structured reference for the Portuguese cybersecurity workforce development ecosystem.
