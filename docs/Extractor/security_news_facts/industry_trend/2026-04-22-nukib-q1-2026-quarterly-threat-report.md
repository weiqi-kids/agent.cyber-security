## 摘要

The Czech National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NUKIB) published its Q1 2026 quarterly threat report, documenting a significant increase in cyber incidents—over 70 total, the highest in 12 months—driven by a wave of pro-Russian DDoS attacks in January and a high number of Intrusion-category incidents.

## 事實內容

- NUKIB recorded more than 70 cyber incidents in Q1 2026, the highest number in the past twelve months.
- The surge was primarily caused by a wave of DDoS attacks in January 2026 led by pro-Russian hacktivist groups.
- A relatively high number of Intrusion-category incidents also contributed to the elevated total.
- NUKIB recorded seven significant incidents; the remainder were less severe.
- Overall severity of incidents slightly increased alongside the quantity rise.
- The report covers January–March 2026, published on 2026-04-22.
- NUKIB (Národní úřad pro kybernetickou a informační bezpečnost) is the Czech Republic's national cybersecurity authority.

## 受影響系統
