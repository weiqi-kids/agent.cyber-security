## 摘要

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security published ITSAP.60.003, a quick guide for organizations and users on proper email configuration to reduce cyber threats, covering technical controls such as SPF, DKIM, and DMARC.

## 事實內容

- Published as part of the ITSAP (IT Security Awareness Publication) series
- Document identifier: ITSAP.60.003
- Provides practical guidance on email configuration settings for organizations
- Covers key email security protocols including:
  - SPF (Sender Policy Framework) — prevents email spoofing by validating sending servers
  - DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail) — provides cryptographic signing of emails
  - DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication, Reporting, and Conformance) — combines SPF and DKIM to enforce email authentication policies
- Targeted at both small/medium businesses and individual employees
- Complements the companion publication ITSAP.60.002 on email security best practices
- Aimed at reducing phishing, spam, and business email compromise (BEC) risks

## 受影響系統

- Email servers (Microsoft Exchange, Google Workspace, etc.)
- Domain DNS configuration
- Organizations managing their own email infrastructure
