## 摘要

CNCS announces 7 webinar information sessions (17-21 April 2023) about the C-Network — a planned network of 7 cybersecurity competence centres across Portugal aiming to support ~2,000 local organisations by March 2026.

## 事實內容

- **Programme**: C-Network (Rede de Centros de Competências em Cibersegurança).
- **Goal**: Support approximately 2,000 organisations across Portugal by March 2026, working closely with local agents in each region.
- **Structure**: 7 cybersecurity competence centres distributed across Portugal.
- **Purpose**: Assist local organisations with cybersecurity maturity.
- **Information sessions**: 7 webinars scheduled between 17–21 April 2023, each covering different regions/topics.
- **More info**: https://www.cncs.gov.pt/pt/c-network/

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese SMEs, public bodies, and local organisations (all sectors)
