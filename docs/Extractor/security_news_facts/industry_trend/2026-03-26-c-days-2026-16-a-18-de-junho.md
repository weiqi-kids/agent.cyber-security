## 摘要

CNCS announces 12th C-DAYS cybersecurity conference, 16–18 June 2026 in Porto, focusing on Portugal's new Cybersecurity Legal Framework.

## 事實內容

The 12th edition of C-DAYS, promoted by the Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS), returns to the Centro de Congressos da Alfândega do Porto from 16 to 18 June 2026. The event serves as a national reference space for reflection, discussion, and sharing of cybersecurity best practices.

This year's conference focuses on the structural change in Portugal's cybersecurity approach, driven by the entry into force of the new Cybersecurity Legal Framework (Decreto-Lei n.º 125/2025 of 4 December). With approximately 6,000 entities covered by this legal framework, the event aims to support adaptation to the new regulatory context and reinforce the importance of compliance.

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese organizations subject to Decreto-Lei n.º 125/2025
