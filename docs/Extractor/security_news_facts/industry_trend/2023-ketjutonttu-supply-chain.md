## 摘要

Ketjutonttu campaign improved Finnish business sector information security by identifying and correcting supply chain risks across 150 organizations and 2,312 suppliers.

## 事實內容

Campaign results:
- 150 organizations and companies participated
- Funded by National Emergency Supply Agency's Digital Security 2030 program
- Average 35 suppliers identified per participant
- Total 2,312 suppliers inspected during campaign
- Vulnerability reports and repair help offered
- 856 information security observations reported

Major supply chain security initiative with measurable impact.
