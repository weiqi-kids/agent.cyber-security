## 摘要

CERT.LV and Canadian Armed Forces developed specialized threat-hunting training course with 21 NATO cybersecurity experts, based on 2+ years of joint operations.

## 事實內容

CERT.LV, Latvian Ministry of Defence, and Canadian Armed Forces Cyber Forces developed a specialized threat-hunting training course:

**Objectives:**
- Advance threat-hunting capabilities
- Provide fundamental principles for proactive cyber attack identification
- Enhance infrastructure resilience
- Detect unauthorized entities (APTs, malware, spyware)

**Curriculum:**
- Integrates international expertise refined over 2 years of joint operations
- Best-practice methodologies and strategies
- Hands-on technical analysis skills
- Tools used in threat-hunting operations
- Specialized "Threat Hunt Playbook"

**Target Audience:**
- Inaugural session: 21 cybersecurity experts from NATO member states
- Plans to expand offerings to other nations' cybersecurity professionals

**Partnership Details:**
- CERT.LV (Latvia)
- Canadian Armed Forces Cyber Forces (CAF)
- Latvian Ministry of Defence
- State Police College (host facility)
- NATO Cooperative Cyber Defence Centre of Excellence

**Impact:**
- Since 2022: Latvia analyzed over 140,000 endpoints across 30+ organizations
- Established Latvia as leader in EU threat-hunting operations

**Quote:**
Varis Teivāns (CERT.LV Deputy Manager): "This training is the result of countless hours of dedication from both the Latvian and Canadian cybersecurity teams."

## 受影響系統

- NATO threat-hunting capabilities
- Cybersecurity training infrastructure
- EU threat detection operations
