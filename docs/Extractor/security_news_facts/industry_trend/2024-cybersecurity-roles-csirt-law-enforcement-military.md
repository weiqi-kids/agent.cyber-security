## 摘要

CERT.at analyzes distinct roles and collaboration challenges among CSIRTs, law enforcement, and military in cybersecurity domain.

## 事實內容

The article contrasts physical and cyber security responsibilities, highlighting the absence of a clear escalation model in cyberspace that exists in the physical world.

**Physical World Model:**
- Citizens handle basic security (locks, alarms)
- Police manage low-level crime and investigations
- Military addresses nation-state threats
This clear escalation model is absent in cybersecurity.

**Cybersecurity Role Definitions:**

**CSIRTs (Computer Security Incident Response Teams):**
Handle vulnerability data and incident response for their constituencies. Provide technical coordination and mitigation guidance but lack enforcement authority.

**Law Enforcement:**
Struggles with jurisdictional limitations and resource constraints in prosecuting cybercriminals across borders. Cross-border cooperation is slow and often ineffective for low-level cybercrime.

**Military:**
Role remains undefined and debated for cyber threats. Unclear when cyber incidents constitute acts of war versus criminal activity.

**Key Collaboration Challenges:**
- Global, borderless nature of cyberattacks defies jurisdictional boundaries
- Lack of consequences for low-level intrusions creates persistent threat environment
- Safe havens in non-cooperative jurisdictions enable persistent criminal operations
- Resource asymmetry: defenders must protect everything; attackers need one success

**Fundamental Contradiction:**
As CERT.at states: "We simply can't have both: a global, content agnostic network...and a global network where the behaviour that we find objectionable is consistently policed."

**Recommendations:**
The article advocates for:
- Stronger law enforcement investment and international cooperation
- ISP accountability for hosting malicious infrastructure
- Deterrence mechanisms beyond technical defenses
- Realistic expectations about what technical security can achieve

This analysis emphasizes structural challenges in cybersecurity governance that no single entity can resolve.

## 受影響系統

- Global cybersecurity governance framework
- CSIRT operations and mandates
- International law enforcement cooperation
- Military cyber doctrine development
