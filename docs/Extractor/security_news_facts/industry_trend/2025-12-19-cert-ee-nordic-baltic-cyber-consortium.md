## 摘要

In December 2025, a Nordic-Baltic Cyber Consortium project led by Denmark began preparations to strengthen cyber threat information sharing in the Nordic-Baltic region; Estonia's RIA is participating.

## 事實內容

In December 2025, a Nordic-Baltic Cyber Consortium project led by Denmark began substantive preparations to strengthen cyber threat information sharing in the Nordic-Baltic region. The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) is participating in the project from the Estonian side. The initiative represents a significant step toward enhanced regional cyber defense cooperation, bringing together Nordic and Baltic nations to improve collective intelligence sharing about cyber threats affecting the region.

## 受影響系統

- Nordic-Baltic regional cyber threat intelligence infrastructure
- National CERTs and cybersecurity agencies of participating countries
