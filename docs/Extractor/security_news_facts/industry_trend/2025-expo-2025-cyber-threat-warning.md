## 摘要

Czech NUKIB warns of increased cyber threat activity targeting EXPO 2025 participants and visitors from April to October 2025.

## 事實內容

From April 13 to October 13, 2025, the Japanese city of Osaka is hosting the world exhibition EXPO 2025. Given the international nature and high visibility of this event, increased interest from cyber actors can be expected, who may target participants, visitors, and organizations even before the exhibition begins. The Czech National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NUKIB) issued a situational report recommending increased vigilance in connection with this prestigious event.

## 攻擊手法

- Social engineering campaigns targeting event participants
- Phishing and credential theft
- Opportunistic attacks on high-profile targets

## 受影響系統

- EXPO 2025 participants and visitors
- Organizations with EXPO 2025 involvement
