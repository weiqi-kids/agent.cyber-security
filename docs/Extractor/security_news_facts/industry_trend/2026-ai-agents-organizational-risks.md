## 摘要

Kaspersky analysis warns AI agents create governance headache for CIOs/CISOs, making decisions without human oversight with risks from poor service to database destruction.

## 事實內容

Kaspersky blog on AI agent organizational risks:

**Risk Landscape:**

**Damage Range:**
- Poor customer service (lower impact)
- Destroying corporate primary databases (catastrophic impact)
- No longer theoretical what-if scenarios
- Actual damage from autonomous AI documented

**Governance Challenges:**

**For CIOs and CISOs:**
- "Massive governance headache"
- AI agents make decisions without human in the loop
- Use tools autonomously
- Process sensitive data without oversight

**Current Tool Limitations:**
Quote: "Many of our standard IT and security tools are unable to keep the AI in check."

**Key Concerns:**
- Autonomous decision-making
- Tool usage without approval
- Sensitive data processing
- Lack of human oversight
- Inadequate security controls

**Industry Response:**
- Business leaders actively addressing the issue
- Government agencies racing to provide answers
- Security experts developing solutions
- Urgent need for AI governance frameworks

**Strategic Implications:**
- Standard IT controls insufficient
- New governance models required
- Balance between AI autonomy and organizational safety
- Need for AI-specific security tools

## 受影響系統

- Organizational AI agent deployments
- Corporate databases and sensitive data
- IT governance frameworks
- Customer service systems
