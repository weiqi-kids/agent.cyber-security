## 摘要

CNCS releases second episode of its podcast "Comunicar Cibersegurança," discussing the Cybersecurity in Portugal — Economy report by CNCS Observatory, with authors from University of Minho.

## 事實內容

- **Episode**: Second episode of the CNCS podcast "Comunicar Cibersegurança" (Communicating Cybersecurity).
- **Topic**: Discussion of the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory report on the Economy theme (published the previous May).
- **Focus**: Economic dimension of cybersecurity, particularly cybersecurity challenges for Portuguese companies.
- **Guests**: Authors Francisco Carballo Cruz and João Cerejeira from University of Minho.
- **Format**: Available in both audio and video formats on the CNCS website.
