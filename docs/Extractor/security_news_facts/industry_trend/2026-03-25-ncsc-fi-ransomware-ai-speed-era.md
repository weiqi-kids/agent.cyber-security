## 摘要

Halcyon's 2026 survey reveals ransomware actors are using AI to accelerate attacks, bypass EDR tools, and move from encryption-only to double/triple extortion — with 78% of organizations saying AI makes ransomware more effective against them.

## 事實內容

Dark Reading reports on Halcyon's 2026 Method Survey Report on ransomware evolution:

Key statistics:
- 98% of organizations use EDR for ransomware defense
- Only 25% "actually trust it to defend against today's evolving ransomware threat"
- 78% of surveyed participants said AI made ransomware attacks more effective
- Only 6% believe AI tools have improved their own defenses

Evolution of ransomware tactics (past 5 years):
1. Initial phase: Focused solely on data encryption
2. Double extortion: Threaten to expose stolen data + encrypt
3. Triple extortion: Extend threats to directly contact victims/customers
4. Current trend: Speed acceleration — using AI to steal data more quickly
5. Bypass methods: Threat actors discovered methods to bypass EDR tools

Key shifts:
- Ransomware actors are using valid credentials (instead of exploits) to gain initial access
- AI is used offensively to steal data faster and with greater precision
- Attacks have shifted from opportunistic to targeted, with speed and precision

## 受影響系統

- Enterprise organizations with EDR deployments
- Any organization with sensitive data (all sectors)
