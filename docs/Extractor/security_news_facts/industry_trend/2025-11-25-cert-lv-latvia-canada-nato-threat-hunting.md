## 事實內容

In November 2025, Canadian and Latvian cyber experts led a four-day Threat Hunting Workshop in Riga, Latvia. The workshop was designed to help NATO allies become proactive defenders capable of identifying threats before they occur. This workshop represents continued bilateral cooperation between Latvia and Canada in strengthening cyber defense capabilities across the NATO alliance, building on previous Threat Hunting Surge operations.
