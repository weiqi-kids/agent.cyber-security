Scam call blocking initiative wins award, malware risks explained, Flubot malware analyzed.

## 事實內容

This week's review highlights security awareness and prevention:
- Scam call blocking initiative won the 2025 crime prevention award
- Initiative has significantly reduced the number of scam calls in Finland
- Explanation of what malware is and what kinds of risks it poses
- Tips on how to protect against malware
- Malware overview takes a closer look at Flubot malware, which spread through fraudulent text messages

This represents successful crime prevention efforts and public education initiatives.
