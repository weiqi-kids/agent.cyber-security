## Summary
For Cybersecurity Awareness Month in October 2025, Estonian Information System Authority focuses on business leaders, calling on them to take responsibility for company cybersecurity.

## Description
October is Cybersecurity Awareness Month. This year, the Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) brings focus to business leaders and calls on them to take responsibility for company cybersecurity. The campaign emphasizes that corporate cybersecurity begins with executive leadership.

By targeting business leaders directly, RIA aims to shift cybersecurity responsibility from purely technical departments to the executive level, aligning with broader trends in cybersecurity governance and the requirements of regulations like NIS2.
