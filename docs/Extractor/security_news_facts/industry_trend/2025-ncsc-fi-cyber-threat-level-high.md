## 摘要

Finland's cyber threat level remains elevated, serious data breaches doubled, vulnerabilities surged.

## 事實內容

Joint press release from Traficom and Finnish Security and Intelligence Service (Supo):
- Cyber security threat level in Finland has remained elevated over the past year
- Threat level increased in 2022 after Russia's large-scale attack against Ukraine
- Finnish organisations continue to be targeted by hostile cyber activity
- Number of serious data breaches and attempted intrusions has increased
- Number of serious cases investigated by NCSC-FI has more than doubled compared with previous year
- Findings of software vulnerabilities have also increased significantly, greatly heightening the cyber threat
- Likelihood of cyberattacks capable of paralysing society on a broad scale considered low
- State-sponsored threat actors remain a primary concern

This represents a significant national security assessment from Finland's top cybersecurity authorities.

## 受影響系統

- Finnish organizations across all sectors
- Critical infrastructure
- Software systems with newly discovered vulnerabilities
