# Cyber weather continued rainy in April 2022

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/ajankohtaista/kybersaa_04/2022  
**Published**: 2022-05-20

## Summary

April 2022 saw continued cyber security challenges with a new FluBot malware wave gaining high visibility. Positive progress was made through a joint project that identified vulnerabilities in the municipal sector and healthcare/social welfare sector.

## Key Points

- **FluBot resurgence**: New wave of malware campaign gained high visibility
- **Ongoing threats**: Scam and phishing messages remain common
- **Positive development**: Joint DPDSA/NCSC-FI project successfully identified sector vulnerabilities
- **Sectors affected**: Municipal sector and healthcare/social welfare

## Impact

The persistent threat landscape demonstrates the need for continuous vigilance. The successful vulnerability assessment project shows the value of proactive security measures in critical sectors.
