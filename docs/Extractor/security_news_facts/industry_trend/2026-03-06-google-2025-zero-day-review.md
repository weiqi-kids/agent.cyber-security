## Summary

Google's 2025 zero-day review reveals enterprise technology exploitation reached an all-time high at 48% of total zero-days, with edge devices and security appliances as primary targets.

## Factual Content

Google published its annual zero-day exploitation review for 2025. Key findings include:

- Both the raw number (43) and proportion (48%) of zero-day vulnerabilities impacting enterprise technologies reached all-time highs, accounting for almost 50% of total zero-days exploited in 2025.
- Browser-based exploitation fell to historical lows, while operating system vulnerability abuse increased.
- State-sponsored espionage groups prioritized edge devices and security appliances as entry points, with over half of attributed exploitation by these groups focused on these technologies.
- Commercial surveillance vendors (CSVs) maintained interest in mobile and browser exploitation, adapting exploit chains to bypass recently implemented security boundaries and mobile security improvements.
- Multiple intrusions linked to BRICKSTORM malware demonstrated targeting of technology companies for potential IP theft to further zero-day exploit development.

## Affected Systems

- Enterprise edge devices and security appliances
- Operating systems (increased targeting)
- Mobile platforms (CSV targeting)
- Technology company networks (BRICKSTORM)
