## 摘要

RIA updated the eesti.ee government portal's business view with a new homepage, topic pages, and personalized dashboard to make finding business-related government information more comprehensive and convenient.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) updated the eesti.ee government portal's business view (ettevõtja vaade), introducing a new homepage, topic pages, and a personalized dashboard for business users. The purpose of the update is to make finding company-related information more comprehensive and convenient. This represents a continued investment in improving Estonia's digital government services user experience, making it easier for businesses to access government services and information through a centralized portal.

## 受影響系統

- eesti.ee government portal (business user interface)
- Estonian business digital government services
