## 摘要

Portugal's Cybersecurity Challenge.PT opens registrations on its training platform targeting youth aged 16-25, aiming to select 10 talented individuals to represent Portugal at the European Cybersecurity Challenge in Poland.

## 事實內容

Registrations opened on the Cybersecurity Challenge PT training platform. The initiative targets young students aged 16 to 25 years old, with the objective of selecting 10 talented young cybersecurity professionals to represent Portugal at the European Cybersecurity Challenge (ECSC), taking place in Poland. Over the following weeks, challenges of various difficulty levels were to be launched on the training platform, spanning multiple cybersecurity domains. The competition serves as Portugal's national qualifier for the European-level contest, fostering the development of cybersecurity talent among Portuguese youth.

## 受影響系統

- Youth cybersecurity talent development
- Portuguese representation at European Cybersecurity Challenge
