## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security guidance on potential cyber security risks and mitigation measures associated with generative artificial intelligence (AI) tools.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security released awareness guidance document ITSAP.00.041 on generative artificial intelligence (AI). The publication provides information on the potential risks and mitigation measures associated with generative AI tools such as large language models and image generators. Generative AI introduces cyber security risks including data leakage through AI training processes, prompt injection attacks, AI-generated phishing content, deepfakes, and reliance on AI-generated misinformation. Organizations adopting generative AI tools should assess risks related to sensitive data input into AI systems, evaluate vendor AI security practices, and establish governance policies for AI use. The guidance is part of the broader AI security guidance series alongside ITSAP.00.040.

## 受影響系統

- Generative AI platforms and APIs
- Organizational data exposed to AI training
- Systems vulnerable to AI-generated social engineering content
