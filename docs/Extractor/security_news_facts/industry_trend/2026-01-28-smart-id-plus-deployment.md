## 摘要

RIA to deploy Smart-ID+ functionality in late February, making authentication more secure and user-friendly while preventing fraud and social engineering.

## 事實內容

The Information System Authority (RIA) of Estonia will deploy Smart-ID+ functionality in the state authentication service at the end of February 2026.

Key improvements:
- Enhanced security compared to existing Smart-ID
- Improved user experience
- Fraud prevention measures
- Protection against social engineering attacks
- Mitigation of various types of fraud attempts

The upgrade represents a significant enhancement to Estonia's national digital identity infrastructure, which is critical for accessing government and private sector digital services.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian state authentication service
- Smart-ID authentication platform
- Government e-services
