## 摘要

Estonia's RIA conducted a cyber exercise to test and strengthen the state's ability to respond to cyber threats against critical infrastructure, specifically focusing on the power grid and inter-agency cooperation.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) organized a cyber exercise the previous week (before November 25, 2025) aimed at testing and strengthening Estonia's ability to respond to cyber threats against critical infrastructure. The exercise focused specifically on power grid business continuity and inter-agency cooperation. Critical infrastructure cyber defense exercises are an important part of national cyber resilience, allowing organizations to identify gaps in their incident response capabilities before a real attack occurs.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian power grid (exercise scenario)
- Critical infrastructure protection systems
- Inter-agency incident response coordination
