## 摘要

Finland's National Emergency Supply Agency (Huoltovarmuuskeskus) has assessed that the escalating Middle East crisis does not currently pose a direct threat to Finland's security of supply. Indirect risks exist through potential energy price increases and disruption to global maritime logistics.

## 事實內容

- Finland's National Emergency Supply Agency (HVK) assessed that the escalation of the Middle East crisis does not pose an immediate threat to Finland's security of supply.
- The agency evaluated the situation across sectors critical to national security of supply from multiple development scenarios.
- Direct impacts of the Israel-US-Iran conflict escalation are assessed to be minor for Finland.
- The most significant potential indirect effect is through rising energy prices and possible disruption of the global maritime logistics system.
- Finland has limited dependence on energy from Middle Eastern routes.
- Finland does not import significant quantities of other critical raw materials from the Middle East region.
- Identified import dependencies include: potash fertilizers and boron (used in fertilizer production), imported from the broader Middle East region but not the Persian Gulf.

## 受影響系統

- Global maritime logistics chains
- Energy markets (indirect price impact)
- Fertilizer supply chains (minor dependency)
- National critical infrastructure resilience planning
