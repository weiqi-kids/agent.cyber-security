## Summary

Social platforms and threat intelligence show an alliance claiming to consist of multiple hacker organizations named "Scattered LapSus Hunters" gaining attention, allegedly composed of Scattered Spider, LapSus, and ShinyHunters organizations.

## Factual Content

Recent social platform and threat intelligence shows an alliance self-proclaimed to consist of multiple hacker organizations named "Scattered LapSus Hunters" is gaining attention. Although this alliance has not yet provided concrete technical evidence proving successful intrusion into a certain international cloud and network service provider's database, and the enterprise has not discovered new vulnerabilities or intrusion signs, its influence still cannot be ignored. In August this year, the enterprise confirmed its third-party partner vendor was attacked by another hacker organization "ShinyHunters," causing partial data leakage, notably the security incident occurred in third-party systems, not the enterprise's core infrastructure, highlighting the importance of third-party supply chain security management.

According to existing intelligence analysis, "Scattered LapSus Hunters" is suspected to consist of three well-known hacker organizations. This includes "Scattered Spider" known for complex social engineering tactics, "LapSus" launching attacks against large tech companies, and "ShinyHunters" long active on the dark web engaging in large-scale data leakage. If this alliance is indeed established, it will represent integration of different attack tactics and resources, potentially further escalating network threats and bringing greater challenges to enterprise security protection.

Three hacker organizations each adopt different and distinctive attack tactics:

**Scattered Spider** is renowned for advanced social engineering, targeting key industries including finance, retail, aviation, and supply chains. This organization commonly uses methods of impersonating IT support personnel, exploiting voice phishing (Vishing), SIM-swapping, and MFA bombing attack tactics to bypass multi-factor authentication, stealing internal credentials and conducting lateral movement. Law enforcement and security agencies from various countries have issued joint advisories and prevention recommendations.

**Lapsus** (also Microsoft code DEV-0537) is known for targeting large tech or vendors, then using internal credentials and remote desktop tools to obtain sensitive data, then using public threats or directly uploading to dark web for extortion. This group's tactics seem to "not necessarily rely on advanced zero-days" but achieve destruction and leakage by continuous social engineering, internal collaborators, or stealing management credentials, and spreading stolen data on public platforms (like Telegram) to pressure. Their attack tactics and influence have caused government-specific reports and reviews.

**ShinyHunters** has publicly disclosed numerous large-scale data leakages since 2020, with victim scope including e-commerce, cloud services, and application user data. This organization uses social engineering attack tactics and exploits unpatched system vulnerabilities for intrusion, quickly organizing stolen data then immediately selling on dark web or publicly leaking, forming continuous and highly influential data leakage events.

Among the three observed hacker organizations, social engineering has clearly become their primary and commonly used intrusion method, usually including phishing emails, SIM swapping, and internal account recruitment techniques. Facing potentially upgraded threats from teamed hacker organizations, organizations need not only strengthen existing technical protection measures but also improve employee security awareness, strengthening prevention of social engineering attack risks.

## Affected Systems

- Cloud service providers and their customers
- Financial, retail, aviation, and supply chain industries
- Organizations with third-party vendor relationships
