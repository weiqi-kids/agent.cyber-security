## 摘要

The CyberWizards 2025 international camp for girls was held for the third time in Estonia from August 11-16, encouraging girls to explore the world of cybersecurity and IT.

## 事實內容

The third edition of the international CyberWizards camp took place in Estonia from August 11-16, 2025. The five-day program aimed to encourage girls to explore the world of cybersecurity and IT. The camp brought participants from Estonia and abroad, providing hands-on cybersecurity education in an immersive format. CyberWizards is part of Estonia's broader effort to promote diversity in the cybersecurity field and inspire the next generation of female cybersecurity professionals.
