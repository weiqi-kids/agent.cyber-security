## 摘要

Two-year research by Wiz security experts compromised virtually every major AI platform, revealing vulnerabilities across five AI stack layers, with focus needed on infrastructure security rather than prompt-injection attacks.

## 事實內容

Hillai Ben Sasson and Dan Segev from Wiz conducted two years of offensive and defensive research on AI infrastructure security, revealing widespread vulnerabilities across major AI platforms.

**Research Findings:**
- Compromised virtually every major AI platform targeted
- Attacks evolved from simple supply chain exploits (e.g., Pickle format abuse) to comprehensive threat assessment
- Vulnerabilities span five distinct layers of the AI stack
- Infrastructure-focused attacks more critical than prompt-injection attacks

**Industry Context (2026 CISO AI Risk Report):**
- 83% of CISOs worried about AI access levels to company systems
- 71% believe AI has access to core business systems
- Unsanctioned AI tools frequently found in corporate environments

**Key Recommendation:**
Focus on infrastructure used to train, run, and host AI services rather than prompt-injection defenses

**Presentation:**
- To be presented at RSAC Conference in March 2026
- By Dan Segev, Security Architect in Office of the CTO at Wiz

## 受影響系統

- AI training infrastructure
- Corporate AI implementations
- Cloud-based AI environments
