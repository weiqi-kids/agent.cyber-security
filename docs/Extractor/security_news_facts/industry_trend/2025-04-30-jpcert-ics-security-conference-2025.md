## 事實內容

JPCERT/CC organized the ICS Security Conference on February 5, 2025. This event aims to share the current threat landscape for Industrial Control Systems (ICS) both in Japan and overseas, and to highlight the efforts of stakeholders in the field.

The conference helps participants improve their capabilities in ICS security, covering:
- Current threat intelligence targeting industrial control systems
- Incident case studies from ICS environments
- Best practices for securing operational technology (OT) environments
- International perspectives on ICS/OT security

ICS/OT security is a growing priority as threat actors increasingly target critical infrastructure including energy, water, manufacturing, and transportation systems with specialized ICS-targeting capabilities.
