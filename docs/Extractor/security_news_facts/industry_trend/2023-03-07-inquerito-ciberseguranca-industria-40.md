## 摘要

CNCS Observatory and Instituto Politécnico do Porto publish survey results on cybersecurity policies and practices in Industry 4.0 organisations in Portugal, targeting operational technicians and workers.

## 事實內容

- **Publication**: Results of a survey on cybersecurity in Industry 4.0 by the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory.
- **Partners**: Instituto Politécnico do Porto and Compete 2020.
- **Target audience**: Operational technicians and workers of Industry 4.0 organisations.
- **Objective**: Understand the integration of cybersecurity policies and practices in the advanced and emerging Industry 4.0 technology domain.
- **Scope**: Cybersecurity posture, practices, and awareness within Portuguese Industry 4.0 contexts.
- **Document**: Available at https://www.cncs.gov.pt/docs/inquerito-industria-40.pdf

## 受影響系統

- Industry 4.0 organisations and operational technology (OT) environments in Portugal
