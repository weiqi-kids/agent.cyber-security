## Summary
Estonia experienced 6,515 impactful cyber incidents in 2024, approximately double the 2023 figure, according to newly released cybersecurity yearbook.

## Description
According to the Estonian Information System Authority's (RIA) newly released cybersecurity yearbook, Estonia experienced 6,515 impactful cyber incidents in 2024—approximately twice as many as in 2023. This significant increase highlights the escalating cyber threat landscape facing Estonia.

The doubling of incidents year-over-year represents a major escalation in cyber threats and demonstrates the growing challenges faced by Estonia's digital infrastructure and users.
