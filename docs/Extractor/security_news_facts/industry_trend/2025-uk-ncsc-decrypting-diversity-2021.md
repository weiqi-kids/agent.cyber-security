## 摘要

Second joint NCSC and KPMG UK report benchmarking diversity and inclusion progress in cyber security.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre (UK) and KPMG UK have published the second joint report in the "Decrypting Diversity" series, benchmarking against the 2020 findings to gauge progress in diversity and inclusion within the cyber security industry. The report:

- Compares 2021 diversity metrics against 2020 baseline
- Tracks changes in representation across gender, ethnicity, disability, and other dimensions
- Analyzes effectiveness of diversity initiatives implemented since 2020
- Identifies areas of improvement and persistent challenges
- Provides updated recommendations based on progress and gaps

This annual tracking helps the industry measure the impact of diversity efforts and adjust strategies accordingly.

- Professional development and education programs
