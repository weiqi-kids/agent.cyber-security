## 摘要

CERT.LV, NIC.LV, and ICANN host a free one-day DNS security workshop on Oct 31, 2025, covering DNSSEC implementation and zone signing.

## 事實內容

CERT.LV, NIC.LV, and ICANN organize a free hands-on DNS security workshop:

**Date & Time:**
- October 31, 2025, 9:00–17:00 EET

**Location:**
- Tallink Hotel Latvia, Elizabetes Street 24, Riga, LV-1050
- In-person only

**Topics Covered:**
- DNSSEC implementation
- Zone signing and cryptographic key management
- Trust verification chains
- DNS debugging
- Zone configuration
- Automatic signing procedures
- Validation testing

**Target Audience:**
- Participants with foundational DNS and networking knowledge
- Linux/UNIX background recommended
- Must bring laptops with WiFi capability
- Instruction in English

**Registration:**
- Mandatory registration deadline: October 24, 2025
- Limited space available
- Registration through workshop portal

**Trainer:**
- Ulrich Wisser, ICANN's Regional Technical Engagement Manager for Europe

**Schedule:**
- 8:30–9:00: Check-in and refreshments
- 9:00–12:00: Morning workshop
- 12:00–13:00: Lunch
- 13:00–15:00: Afternoon session
- 15:00–15:20: Coffee break
- 15:20–17:00: Final segment

## 受影響系統
