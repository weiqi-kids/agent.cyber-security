## 摘要
CrowdStrike report shows 27% increase in interactive intrusions, 80%+ involve fileless malware, with AI accelerating attacks.

## 事實內容
CrowdStrike latest threat report (July 2024 - June 2025) shows interactive intrusion events increased 27% year-over-year. Over 80% cases involve fileless malware. China cloud-targeting attacks increased 40% annually. Voice phishing grew 442% in H2 2024. Technology sector remains most attacked for 8th consecutive year. Manufacturing and retail became attack hotspots with CURLY SPIDER using ransomware combined with voice phishing. North Korea FAMOUS CHOLLIMA used GenAI for deepfake job applications, with over 320 incidents observed. April 2025 saw attacks targeting AI development tool Langflow AI (CVE-2025-3248).

## 受影響系統
- Cloud services and infrastructure
- Technology sector organizations
- Manufacturing and retail industries
- AI development platforms
