## Factual Content

The Brazilian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT.br) launched a redesigned website at https://cert.br/ with several improvements:

**Key Features:**
- Lighter, more modern layout
- Enhanced mobile device compatibility
- Historical publications moved to dedicated archive area
- Improved navigation and user experience

The redesign reflects CERT.br's commitment to accessibility and user experience while maintaining its role as Brazil's authoritative source for cybersecurity information and incident response coordination.
