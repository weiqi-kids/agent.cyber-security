# Cyber threat level elevated, targeted attacks more frequent in Finland

## Summary
Attackers show increasing interest in Finnish organizations, with targeted attacks against carefully selected organizations becoming more frequent. Despite increased incidents, Traficom and Supo consider societal paralysis unlikely.

## Key Details
- **Trend**: Increasing interest in Finnish organizations
- **Evolution**: More targeted attacks (vs. opportunistic)
- **Assessment**: Societal paralysis unlikely (Traficom + Supo)
- **Status**: Elevated threat level maintained

## Context
This represents a strategic shift from broad opportunistic attacks to more sophisticated targeting of specific Finnish organizations.

## Notes
- Strategic threat assessment
- Joint evaluation (Traficom + Finnish Security Intelligence)
- Confidence: high
