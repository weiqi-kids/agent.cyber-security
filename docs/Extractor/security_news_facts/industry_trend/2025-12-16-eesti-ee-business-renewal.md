## 摘要

RIA renewed eesti.ee business view with new homepage, topic pages, and personalized dashboard to make business information more comprehensive and convenient.

## 事實內容

The Information System Authority (RIA) of Estonia renewed the business view of the state portal eesti.ee, introducing:
- New homepage for businesses
- Topic-based pages
- Personalized dashboard (töölaud)

Objectives:
- Make business-related information more comprehensive
- Improve convenience for finding relevant information
- Provide personalized experience for business users

The eesti.ee portal is Estonia's central gateway for government digital services. This update focuses on improving the user experience for business users, a critical segment of Estonia's digital economy.

## 受影響系統

- eesti.ee state portal (business view)
- Government e-services for businesses
