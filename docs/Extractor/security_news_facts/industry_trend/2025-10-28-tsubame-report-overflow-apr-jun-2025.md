## 事實內容

JPCERT/CC published the TSUBAME Report Overflow for the April to June 2025 period. The TSUBAME series discusses monitoring trends of overseas TSUBAME sensors and other activities which the standard Internet Threat Monitoring Quarterly Reports do not include.

This report covers:
- Fluctuations in internet scan traffic from overseas TSUBAME sensors
- Notable attack patterns and trends observed in the April-June 2025 period
- Supplementary data and analysis beyond the standard quarterly reports

TSUBAME is JPCERT/CC's internet traffic monitoring system that provides visibility into global internet scan and attack traffic patterns. The Overflow series focuses specifically on international sensor data to provide a global perspective on internet threats.
