## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) released Bulletin 3/2021 of its Cybersecurity Observatory (Observatório de Cibersegurança). This edition is dedicated to the theme of "competencies" (competências), reflecting on the motto of the C-DAYS 2021 conference organized by CNCS: "naturalizar competências" (naturalizing competencies). The bulletin also reviews the situation of the country regarding cybersecurity competencies, and as usual includes publications and news. The document is available on the CNCS website.
