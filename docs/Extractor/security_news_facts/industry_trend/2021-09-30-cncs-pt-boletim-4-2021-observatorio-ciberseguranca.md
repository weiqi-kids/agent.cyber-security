## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) released Bulletin 4/2021 of its Cybersecurity Observatory (Observatório de Cibersegurança). This edition focuses on the volume and dominant types of incidents registered by CERT.PT during the first semester of 2021, with comparisons to the same period in 2020 and 2019. The analysis considers the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic and associated social confinements on cybersecurity incident patterns. The bulletin highlights particular aspects of incident trends in Portugal's cybersecurity landscape.
