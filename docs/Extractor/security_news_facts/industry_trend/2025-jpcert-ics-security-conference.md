## 摘要

JPCERT/CC's 17th ICS Security Conference attracted 561 participants, highlighting ransomware and supply chain attacks as top ICS threats, with two-thirds of ransomware victims in manufacturing experiencing operational impact.

## 事實內容

**Event Overview:**
JPCERT/CC organized the ICS Security Conference on February 5, 2025, marking its 17th year. The hybrid event attracted 50 onsite participants and 511 remote attendees through live streaming.

**Opening Remarks - Ministry of Economy, Trade & Industry:**
Director Nobutaka Takeo identified ransomware and supply chain attacks as the most impactful cyber threats, particularly for small-to-medium enterprises. He emphasized government initiatives:
- Security guidelines
- IoT certification programs
- SBOM promotion

**ICS Security Status Review:**
Expert Adviser Toshio Miyachi highlighted:
- Two-thirds of ransomware victims operate in manufacturing
- Approximately half experienced operational impact
- Emerging malware variants (FrostyGoop, IOcontrol) targeting ICS environments
- Geopolitical tensions and emerging technologies remain high-risk factors

**International Standards - IEC 62443:**
Yukihiro Ichikawa discussed the updated IEC 62443-2-1 Edition 2.0:
- Aligns with ISO/IEC 27000 standards
- Introduces maturity level assessments for organizational security measures

**Additional Presentations:**
- Process Safety Management frameworks integrating cybersecurity
- Continuous Threat Exposure Management (CTEM) for risk mitigation
- Incident response exercises for factory environments
- Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) implementation challenges
- Asset management approaches for vulnerability response

**Participant Engagement:**
Sessions featured interactive discussions with manufacturing leaders from:
- JFE Steel
- Panasonic Automotive Systems
- NSK Ltd.

Topics addressed practical implementation challenges in OT security incident handling and vulnerability management.

## 受影響系統

- Industrial Control Systems (ICS)
- Manufacturing operational technology (OT)
- Small-to-medium enterprise systems
