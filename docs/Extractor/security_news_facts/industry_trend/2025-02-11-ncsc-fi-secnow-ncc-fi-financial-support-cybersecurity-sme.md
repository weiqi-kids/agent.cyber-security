## 摘要

NCC-FI provided approximately EUR 2 million to micro-enterprises and SMEs in 2023–2024 for cybersecurity solutions; impact assessment by 4Front Oy confirms significant positive cybersecurity improvements for recipients.

## 事實內容

Traficom's Finnish Cyber Security Centre National Coordination Centre (NCC-FI) conducted a financial support programme for 2023–2024 with the following outcomes:

- **Total support granted**: Approximately EUR 2 million
- **Recipients**: Micro-enterprises and SMEs (small and medium-sized enterprises)
- **Purpose**: Implementation of state-of-the-art information and cyber security solutions and innovations
- **Impact assessment**: Conducted by 4Front Oy
- **Results**:
  - The support had significant positive impacts on the cyber security of recipient organisations
  - Expected positive impact on national cyber security capacity
  - Ripple effects through recipient organisations' customer relationships and supply chains
- The programme represents a public investment in strengthening grassroots-level cybersecurity across Finnish businesses

## 受影響系統

- Finnish micro-enterprises and SMEs
- Finnish national cybersecurity capacity
