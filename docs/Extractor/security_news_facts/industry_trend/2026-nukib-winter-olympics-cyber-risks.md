## 摘要
NUKIB warns of increased cyber threat activity during 2026 Winter Olympics, recommends heightened vigilance.

## 事實內容
The National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NÚKIB) of the Czech Republic has issued a warning about expected increased cyber threat activity in connection with the 2026 Winter Olympic Games.

**Key Points:**
- **Event:** 2026 Winter Olympics
- **Risk Assessment:** Elevated cyber threat activity expected
- **Target Audience:** Institutions and individuals

**Recommendations:**
NUKIB advises both organizations and individuals to exercise increased caution regarding:
1. Internet activities and browsing
2. Protection of personal devices
3. Awareness of phishing and social engineering attempts

**Context:**
Major international sporting events traditionally attract heightened attention from cyber threat actors, including state-sponsored groups, hacktivists, and cybercriminals. The Olympics provide opportunities for:
- Politically motivated cyber attacks
- Credential harvesting and phishing campaigns
- Distributed denial of service attacks
- Misinformation campaigns

Organizations and individuals should maintain elevated security postures throughout the Olympic period.
