## 摘要
CrowdStrike's latest threat report covering July 2024 to June 2025 reveals a 27% increase in interactive intrusions, with over 80% involving fileless malware. China's cloud-targeted attacks grew 40%, voice phishing surged 442%, and the tech sector remained the most-attacked industry for 8 consecutive years. GenAI is being weaponized across all threat actor categories.

## 事實內容
- **報告期間**: July 2024 – June 2025
- **互動式入侵**: 27% year-over-year increase; attackers actively interact with compromised environments rather than relying solely on automation
- **無檔案攻擊**: Over 80% of interactive intrusions used fileless malware, challenging traditional file-based detection
- **雲端攻擊**: China-attributed cloud attacks increased 40%
- **語音詐騙**: Voice phishing (vishing) grew 442% in H2 2024; H1 2025 already exceeded all of 2024
- **產業目標**:
  - Tech sector: Most-attacked for 8 consecutive years (primarily cybercrime)
  - Telecom and government: Primary APT targets
  - Manufacturing and retail: Emerging hotspots, targeted by CURLY SPIDER with ransomware + vishing
- **國家級攻擊者**:
  - Russia (PRIMITIVE BEAR, VENOMOUS BEAR): Attacks linked to Russia-Ukraine conflict
  - China: Telecom attacks grew 130%; focused on user data acquisition and downstream customer infiltration
  - North Korea (FAMOUS CHOLLIMA): Used GenAI to forge job applicant identities with deepfakes; 320+ related incidents observed
- **生成式AI武器化**:
  - APT groups use GenAI to accelerate penetration and operational efficiency
  - Cybercriminals use GenAI for automated script writing and troubleshooting
  - Key applications: More natural phishing emails, deepfake generation, lowered attack barrier
- **新興威脅**: Attackers targeted Langflow AI platform (CVE-2025-3248), showing AI platforms themselves are becoming attack surfaces
- **防護建議**: Enhanced identity verification for hiring, real-time deepfake detection, remote access controls, USB/peripheral device auditing, behavioral monitoring for AI manipulation, AI infiltration identification training

## 受影響系統
- Cloud infrastructure (all major providers)
- Telecommunications networks
- Manufacturing and retail IT systems
- AI/ML development platforms
