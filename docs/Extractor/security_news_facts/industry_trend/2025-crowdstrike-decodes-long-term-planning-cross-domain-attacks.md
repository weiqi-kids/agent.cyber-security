## Summary

CrowdStrike's latest threat report shows interactive intrusion events increased 27% year-over-year from July 2024 to June 2025, with over 80% involving fileless malware. Cloud attacks from China increased 40%, and voice fraud grew 442% in H2 2024.

## Factual Content

CrowdStrike's latest threat report indicates that between July 1, 2024, and June 30, 2025, interactive intrusion events increased 27% year-over-year. This type of attack differs from previous automated intrusions where hackers actively interact with victim computer environments after gaining permissions, gathering information and customizing attack strategies based on context. Among these, over 80% of cases involved fileless malware, showing traditional file-detection-dependent defense methods face challenges.

**Cloud and Voice Fraud Become New Focus**

The report simultaneously reveals China-targeted cloud attacks increased 40% year-over-year, highlighting cloud services remain primary battlegrounds for nation-state attackers. Additionally, voice fraud growth rate reached 442% in H2 2024 and has already exceeded 2024's full-year numbers in H1 2025, showing AI technology popularization is accelerating the scale and impact of such fraud.

**Nation-State Attacks and Industry Targets**

The technology industry has been the most attacked industry for 8 consecutive years, primarily from cybercrime activities, while APT attacks target telecommunications and government units. Among these, Russian actors mainly PRIMITIVE BEAR and VENOMOUS BEAR, with attack purposes related to Russia-Ukraine war; Chinese actors view telecommunications industry as high-value targets to obtain user data and infiltrate downstream customers, with related attacks growing 130% in the past year.

**Manufacturing and Retail Industries' High-Risk Situation**

Manufacturing and retail industries also became attack hotspots in the past year. The CURLY SPIDER hacker organization frequently applies ransomware combined with voice fraud, exploiting these industries' high dependence on uninterrupted operations to force victims to pay quickly. Manufacturing industries cannot afford production interruption losses; retail industries during shopping peak seasons face data leakage or system downtime resulting in customer and revenue losses. Additionally, these industries have massive budgets and complex, potentially outdated IT architectures, making them continue attracting cybercrime group attention.

**Generative AI Becomes Attack Accelerator**

CrowdStrike indicates attackers are actively integrating Generative AI (GenAI) into operational processes, not replacing but strengthening original tactics and techniques. APT organizations use GenAI to accelerate infiltration and operational efficiency, while general cybercriminals use GenAI to automate script writing and problem resolution. Impacts mainly manifest in three aspects:

1. Writing more natural and contextually appropriate phishing emails, continuing to launch phishing attacks against EU and US, capable of identity forgery
2. Easier generation of fake videos, audio, and images, impersonating well-known figures (deepfake)
3. Lowering cybersecurity attack barriers, accelerating malicious attack program generation

**Actual Cases and Emerging Threats**

North Korea's FAMOUS CHOLLIMA organization is a typical case. They use GenAI to forge job seeker identities, create deepfake videos, and enhance English conversation and technical abilities through LLMs to successfully obtain remote engineer positions. Over 320 related security incidents have been observed in the past year. Additionally, in April 2025, hacker organizations shifted attack targets toward AI development tool Langflow AI (CVE-2025-3248), showing AI platforms themselves have become potential attack surfaces.

## Affected Systems

- Cloud service infrastructure globally
- Technology, telecommunications, manufacturing, and retail industries
- AI development platforms and tools
- Organizations using remote hiring processes
