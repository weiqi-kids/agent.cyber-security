## 事實內容

From November 21 to 23, 2025, the third edition of Mārtiņa-CTF (Martins CTF) returned with new challenges. The event invited participants from Latvia and beyond to 48 hours of intense cybersecurity challenges. As the third edition — described as a "trilogy" — the competition promised more challenges, creativity, and surprises for participants. The event is part of CERT.LV's ongoing efforts to build cybersecurity talent and community engagement in the Baltic region.
