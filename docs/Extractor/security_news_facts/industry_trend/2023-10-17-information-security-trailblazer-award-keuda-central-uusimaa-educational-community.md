## 摘要

Finnish Transport and Communications Agency Traficom awarded Keuda the Information Security Trailblazer prize for transparent communication following a November 2022 ransomware attack.

## 事實內容

The Information Security Trailblazer award given by the Finnish Transport and Communications Agency Traficom was presented to the Central Uusimaa educational community association (Keuda) in 2023. The award was granted primarily for Keuda's open and transparent communication after it became the target of a ransomware cyberattack in November 2022. The justification highlighted Keuda's exemplary approach to incident disclosure as a model for other organisations. This recognition reflects an industry emphasis on transparency and responsible communication following security incidents.

## 受影響系統

- Keuda (Central Uusimaa educational community association) internal systems (ransomware incident, November 2022)
