## 摘要

Croatia marks Safer Internet Day 2026 with focus on digital safety for children and youth.

## 事實內容

Croatia's CERT.hr announced Safer Internet Day 2026 with the theme "Together for a Better Internet." The event occurs on the second Tuesday of February globally, uniting more than 180 countries with the goal of creating a safer, more responsible, and higher-quality digital environment. In Croatia, the focus is particularly on children and youth who are growing up surrounded by digital technologies, making online safety one of the key societal issues. The initiative calls for joint action from institutions, parents, educators, and young people themselves.
