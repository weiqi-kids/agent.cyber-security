## 摘要

CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory released its 2019 Society Observation Line report, analyzing how Portuguese citizens think and act in cybersecurity, drawing on Eurobarometer, Eurostat, and national statistics data.

## 事實內容

The CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory published the Report 2019 of the Society Observation Line — the first sectoral report in a series covering six observation lines: Risks & Conflicts, Economy, Society, Ethics & Law, Public Policies, and Innovation & Future Technologies. The document focuses on the social aspects of cybersecurity, specifically how citizens in general think and behave in this domain. It uses recognized sources such as the Eurobarometer, Eurostat, the National Statistics Institute, and the Directorate-General for Education and Science Statistics. It aims to inform the community to contribute to the construction of effective education and awareness strategies and informed public policies. The document was subsequently updated on 02/01/2020 and 16/01/2020. The Cybersecurity Observatory was created in 2019 by CNCS to promote multidisciplinary knowledge sharing on cybersecurity in Portugal.
