## 摘要

Annual Finnish information security seminar held virtually, focusing on trust, cooperation and corporate board responsibility.

## 事實內容

The 2020 Information Security seminar organized by NCSC-FI at Traficom and the National Emergency Supply Agency was held as a virtual event. The seminar addressed key themes including trust, cooperation, and the responsibilities of corporate management and boards in cybersecurity. The event represented a shift to virtual formats while maintaining discussion on critical information security topics.
