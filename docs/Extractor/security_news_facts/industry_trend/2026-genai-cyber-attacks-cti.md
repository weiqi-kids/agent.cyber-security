## Summary

CERT-FR Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) report on how generative AI is being used by attackers in cyberattacks.

## Details

- **Type**: CTI Report (CERTFR-2026-CTI-001)
- **Topic**: Generative AI in cyberattacks
- **Published**: 2026-02-04

## Key Points

As an innovative, performant, and flexible digital service, generative AI is progressively integrated into the range of tools and services that attackers across all profiles may use. Generative AI models are being utilized and/or targeted by malicious actors.

## Impact

This report analyzes how threat actors leverage generative AI technologies for offensive operations, representing an evolving threat landscape.
