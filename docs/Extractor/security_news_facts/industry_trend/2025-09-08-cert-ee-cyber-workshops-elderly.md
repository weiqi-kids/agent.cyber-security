## 摘要

Estonia's RIA invites people aged 45+ to free cybersecurity workshops covering simple practical tips to protect money, photos, and personal data safely.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) invited people in the 45+ age group to participate in free cybersecurity workshops. The workshops teach simple and practical tips on how to keep money, photos, and personal data safe. This initiative specifically targets older demographics who may be more vulnerable to cyber fraud and phishing attacks. The workshops represent Estonia's community-level cybersecurity awareness efforts, recognizing that different age groups require tailored approaches to cybersecurity education.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian citizens aged 45+ (cybersecurity awareness)
- Personal device security
