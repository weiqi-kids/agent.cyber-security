## 摘要

CNCS Observatory's first 2023 bulletin addresses disinformation as a cybersecurity issue, covering how digital technologies facilitate disinformation spread and automation, including deepfakes, and the importance of regulation.

## 事實內容

- **Publication**: CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory Bulletin 1/2023 (March 2023 edition).
- **Main theme**: Disinformation as a cybersecurity problem.
- **Topics covered**:
  - How digital technologies enable the spread and automation of disinformation
  - More deceptive forgeries (deepfakes and synthetic media)
  - Importance of regulation in addressing disinformation risks
  - Cybersecurity implications of disinformation campaigns
- **Format**: Newsletter bulletin with news and documents of interest.
- **Document**: Available at https://www.cncs.gov.pt/docs/boletim-observatoriocncs-mar2023.pdf
