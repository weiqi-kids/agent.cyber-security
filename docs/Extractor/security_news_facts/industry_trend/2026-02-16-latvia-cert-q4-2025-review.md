## 摘要

CERT.LV reports that cyber threat levels in Latvia remain high with steady upward trend, driven by both financial and political motivations.

## 事實內容

The Latvian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT.LV) has published its **Activity Review for Q4 2025**, highlighting the ongoing cyber threat landscape in the country.

**Key Findings**:
- **Threat Level**: Remains **high** with a steady upward trend
- **Attack Motivations**: Both financially and politically motivated
- **Key Factor**: Geopolitical factors continue to serve as a significant catalyst for cyber threats

**Implications**:
The report indicates that Latvia, like other European nations, faces persistent cyber threats that are increasingly influenced by geopolitical tensions. The dual nature of attacks (financial and political) suggests a complex threat landscape requiring comprehensive defense strategies.

This quarterly review provides insights into the cyber threat environment in the Baltic region and serves as an indicator of broader European cybersecurity trends.
