## 摘要

November 2025 cyber threat landscape remained rainy with malware and vulnerability observations.

## 事實內容

The Cyber Weather report for November 2025 indicates:
- Overall threat level remained "rainy" (elevated)
- Month was relatively calm compared to other periods
- Conditions weakened mainly due to observations related to malware
- Vulnerability discoveries also contributed to the elevated threat level

The "Cyber Weather" is a monthly threat assessment published by NCSC-FI to communicate the cybersecurity situation in Finland using weather metaphors.

## 受影響系統

- General IT infrastructure
- Systems affected by malware campaigns
- Systems with newly discovered vulnerabilities
