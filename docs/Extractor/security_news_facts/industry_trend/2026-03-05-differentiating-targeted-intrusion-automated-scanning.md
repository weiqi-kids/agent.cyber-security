## 摘要

A guest diary by Joseph Gruen, an ISC intern as part of the SANS.edu BACS program, exploring how to differentiate between a targeted intrusion and automated opportunistic scanning. This analysis helps security practitioners understand the behavioral indicators that distinguish deliberate adversary campaigns from mass-scanning and automated exploitation attempts.

由 SANS.edu BACS 計畫實習生 Joseph Gruen 撰寫的客座日誌，探討如何區分針對性入侵與自動化機會性掃描。此分析協助安全從業人員瞭解區分蓄意對手攻擊活動與大規模掃描及自動化利用嘗試的行為指標。
