## 摘要

NCSC report highlights cyber threats faced by the sports sector and provides mitigation strategies to reduce impact on sports organizations.

## 事實內容

The UK National Cyber Security Centre published a sector-specific threat assessment for sports organizations. The report:

- Identifies cyber threats unique to sports sector including ticketing fraud, betting manipulation, and fan data theft
- Highlights ransomware risks that could disrupt major sporting events
- Addresses threats to broadcast systems and digital infrastructure
- Provides tailored security guidance for sports organizations

The sports sector faces particular risks during major events when visibility is high and disruption would be most impactful. Threats include criminals seeking financial gain through ticketing scams, state actors aiming to disrupt events for geopolitical reasons, and hackers targeting valuable fan databases.

## 受影響系統

- Sports venue networks and ticketing systems
- Broadcast and streaming infrastructure
- Athlete and fan databases
- Sports betting platforms
