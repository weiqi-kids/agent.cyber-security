## 摘要

## 事實內容

This week's review highlights several industry developments:
- End of support for Windows 10 operating system (major transition)
- EU's new Instant Payments Regulation
- Scam calls made in the name of NCSC-FI (impersonation attacks)
- Cyber Security Nordic fair returning
- InfoSec 2025 seminar details (organized by Traficom and National Emergency Supply Agency)
- Digital and Population Data Services Agency's Digital Security Barometer 2025 findings
- Malware review of Ranbyus

The Windows 10 end-of-life represents a significant security milestone affecting millions of systems.
