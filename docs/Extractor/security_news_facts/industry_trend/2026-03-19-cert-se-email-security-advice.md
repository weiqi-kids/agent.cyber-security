## 摘要
CERT-SE published guidance on email security best practices, providing recommendations for organizations to protect their email infrastructure and reduce exposure to phishing and email-based attacks.

## 事實內容
CERT-SE released practical advice on email security (säkerhet i e-post) targeting Swedish organizations. The guidance covers best practices for securing email systems, including recommendations around email authentication protocols (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), encryption in transit, protection against phishing and spoofing, and general hygiene for email infrastructure. The advisory is part of CERT-SE's ongoing effort to raise the baseline of cybersecurity practices across Swedish organizations, particularly given the prevalence of email as an attack vector in phishing campaigns, business email compromise, and malware distribution.

## 受影響系統
- Email infrastructure and services
- Organizations operating mail servers or using cloud email platforms
