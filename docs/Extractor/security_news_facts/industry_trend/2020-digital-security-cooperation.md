## 摘要

NCSC-FI, Digipooli network, and National Emergency Supply Agency collaborate to ensure digital society continuity.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre at Traficom, the Digipooli network, and the National Emergency Supply Agency are working together to produce up-to-date information on the state of Finland's digital society under emergency conditions. Particular emphasis is placed on ensuring healthcare services continue functioning. The agencies urged the ICT industry to prioritize the social and healthcare services sector.
