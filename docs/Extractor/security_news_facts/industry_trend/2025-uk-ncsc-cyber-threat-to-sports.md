## 摘要

UK NCSC report highlighting cyber threats faced by sports sector with mitigation recommendations.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre (UK) has published a report highlighting the cyber threats faced by the sports sector and suggesting how organizations can prevent or lessen their impact. The report:

- Identifies threat actors targeting sports organizations (cybercriminals, hacktivists, nation-states)
- Documents common attack types including DDoS attacks during major events, ticket fraud, and data breaches
- Analyzes threats to sports betting and fantasy sports platforms
- Examines risks to athlete and fan personal data
- Provides practical security recommendations for sports organizations of all sizes

Sports organizations face heightened risks during major sporting events and must protect diverse assets including ticketing systems, broadcasting infrastructure, and sensitive athlete information.

## 受影響系統

- Sports teams and leagues
- Event management and ticketing systems
- Sports betting and fantasy platforms
- Broadcasting and streaming infrastructure
- Fan engagement platforms
