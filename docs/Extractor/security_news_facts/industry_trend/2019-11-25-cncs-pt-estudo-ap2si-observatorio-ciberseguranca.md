## 摘要

AP2SI published preliminary results of a cybersecurity professionals survey with 367 valid responses, covering demographics, employment status, salary, and professional functions in Portugal.

## 事實內容

The Associação Portuguesa para a Promoção da Segurança da Informação (AP2SI), supported by the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory, released preliminary results of a survey on cybersecurity and information security professionals in Portugal. The study received 367 valid responses via an open, anonymous online survey. The survey covered: demographic characterization (gender, age, education level, geographic location); past and current professional situation (sector, employment data, career duration, exclusivity, salary, and role performed); and factors considered important in professional activity. Future updates with composite results were planned.
