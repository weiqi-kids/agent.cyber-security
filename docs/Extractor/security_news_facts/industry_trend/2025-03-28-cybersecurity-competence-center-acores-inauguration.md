## 摘要

The Azores Cybersecurity Competence Center (CCC-Açores) was officially inaugurated at Nonagon, Science and Technology Park on São Miguel Island, expanding Portugal's C-Network.

## 事實內容

On 28 March 2025, the Azores Cybersecurity Competence Center (CCC-Açores — "Centro de Competências em Ciberseguran&ccedil;a dos A&ccedil;ores") was officially inaugurated. The center is located at Nonagon within the Science and Technology Park on São Miguel Island. CCC-Açores is part of the C-Network ("C-Network"), a national network coordinated by CNCS aimed at expanding cybersecurity capacity across Portugal's regions, including the autonomous territories. The inauguration marks a significant step in decentralizing Portugal's cybersecurity infrastructure and capabilities.

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese national C-Network cybersecurity infrastructure
