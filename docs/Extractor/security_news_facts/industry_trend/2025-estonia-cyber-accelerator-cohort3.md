## Summary
Six high-potential cybersecurity startups selected for third cohort of Estonia's Cyber Accelerator, run by RIA with Technopol Startup Incubator and European Cybersecurity Competence Centre.

## Description
The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA), in cooperation with Technopol Startup Incubator and the European Cybersecurity Competence Centre (ECCC), operates the Cyber Accelerator supporting cybersecurity startups in developing and bringing new products and services to market. Six high-potential startups were selected for the accelerator's third cohort.

The program represents Estonia's commitment to fostering cybersecurity innovation and entrepreneurship, providing startups with resources, mentorship, and support to develop commercially viable security solutions.

## Technical Details
- **Program**: Cyber Accelerator (Küberkiirendi)
- **Cohort**: Third round
- **Selected Startups**: 6 companies
- **Partners**: RIA, Technopol Startup Incubator, European Cybersecurity Competence Centre (ECCC)
- **Focus**: Product and service development, market entry support
