## 事實內容

JPCERT/CC hosted JSAC2026 from January 21 to 23, 2026. JSAC is an annual conference dedicated to advancing the capabilities of security analysts by fostering the exchange of technical knowledge and operational insights related to incident analysis and response.

Day 1 featured presentations from security researchers covering advanced malware analysis, threat actor tracking, incident response case studies, and emerging attack techniques. JSAC2026 brings together security analysts from Japan and internationally to share practical knowledge and improve collective defensive capabilities.

The conference serves as a key venue for the Japanese security community to present original research and discuss current threat landscapes facing organizations across Asia-Pacific and globally.
