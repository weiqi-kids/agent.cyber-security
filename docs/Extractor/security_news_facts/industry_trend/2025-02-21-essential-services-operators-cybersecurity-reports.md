## 摘要

CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory published sector reports analyzing the state of cybersecurity among Essential Services Operators (OSE) defined under the earlier NIS Directive framework.

## 事實內容

A set of reports produced under the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory ("Observatório de Ciberseguran&ccedil;a") is now available. The reports analyze the state of cybersecurity across sectors of Essential Services Operators (OSE — "Operadores de Servi&ccedil;os Essenciais") as originally defined under the previous NIS Directive framework. These cross-sectoral analyses provide a comprehensive overview of cybersecurity posture across critical sectors in Portugal, supporting evidence-based policy development and sector-specific risk management.

## 受影響系統

- Essential Services Operators (OSE) in Portugal
- Critical sector organizations under NIS framework
