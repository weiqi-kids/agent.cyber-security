## 摘要

First joint NCSC and NCA report assessing cyber threats facing UK businesses across all sectors.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre (UK) and National Crime Agency (NCA) have published their first joint report assessing the cyber threat to UK business. The report:

- Provides comprehensive analysis of threats facing UK commercial sector
- Documents both cybercriminal and nation-state threats to businesses
- Analyzes common attack methods including ransomware, business email compromise, and supply chain attacks
- Offers sector-specific threat intelligence and risk assessments
- Provides actionable recommendations for business security leaders

This collaborative report combines the NCSC's technical expertise with the NCA's law enforcement intelligence to provide a holistic view of the business threat landscape.

## 受影響系統

- UK businesses across all sectors and sizes
- Supply chain and third-party service providers
- Corporate networks and cloud infrastructure
- Business communication and financial systems
