## 摘要

## 事實內容

On 9 February, the National Cybersecurity Centre (CNCS) conducted a Cybersecurity Exercise dedicated to Elections at its premises. The objective was to test coordination mechanisms between the various entities responsible for electoral process security in Portugal. The exercise simulated cyber incidents targeting electoral infrastructure and systems, evaluating response protocols, communication channels, and inter-institutional coordination. Participants included representatives from election administration bodies, law enforcement, and critical infrastructure operators. The exercise occurred in the context of upcoming Portuguese legislative elections and CNCS's mandate to protect critical national infrastructure from cyber threats.

- Portuguese public administration entities
