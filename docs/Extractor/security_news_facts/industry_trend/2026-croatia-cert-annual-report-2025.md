## Summary
Croatian National CERT's 2025 Annual Report reveals 1,513 cybersecurity incidents, a 35.9% increase from 2024, with phishing attacks being the most prevalent at 32% of total incidents.

## Factual Content
**Incident Statistics:**
- **Total Incidents in 2025:** 1,513
- **Increase from 2024:** 35.9%
- **Note:** Numbers represent only reported or self-discovered incidents by National CERT

**Incident Distribution:**
1. **Phishing Attacks:** 32% (most prevalent)
2. Other incident types mentioned but specific percentages not provided in the RSS excerpt

**Reporting Methodology:**
The recorded incidents represent only those for which the National CERT received reports or discovered through its own activities, suggesting actual incident numbers may be higher.

**Trend Analysis:**
The significant year-over-year increase of over one-third indicates:
- Growing cyber threat landscape in Croatia
- Possible increased awareness and reporting
- Evolution of attack sophistication
- Need for continued cybersecurity investment
