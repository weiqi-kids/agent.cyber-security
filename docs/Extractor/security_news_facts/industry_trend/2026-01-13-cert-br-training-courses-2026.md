CERT.br opens registration for 2026 cybersecurity training courses including CSIRT creation/management, incident management foundations, and advanced incident handling.

## Factual Content

CERT.br has opened registration (until January 15, 5 PM) for its 2026 training courses:

- **Overview of Creating and Managing CSIRTs**: Course on establishing and operating Computer Security Incident Response Teams
- **Foundations of Incident Management (FIM)**: Foundational course on incident management practices
- **Advanced Topics in Incident Handling (ATIH)**: Advanced course on incident handling techniques
