## 摘要

NCSC-FI launches Kybermittari tool to help corporate management assess and address cyber risks.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre Finland (NCSC-FI) at Traficom created Kybermittari, a new tool designed to help corporate management face cyber risks and safeguard business continuity. The tool provides management-level visibility into cybersecurity posture and enables better risk-based decision making.
