## 摘要

Canadian Cyber Centre outlines five-step business continuity planning framework for cyber security resilience, emphasizing testing and validation procedures.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security published ITSAP.10.005 outlining a structured approach to business continuity planning for cyber security.

**Planning Framework: 5-Step Lifecycle**

1. **Initiate** - Establish organizational goals, form response team with cross-functional members, secure management approval

2. **Analyze** - Conduct threat and risk assessment (TRA) and business impact analysis (BIA) to identify critical operations and determine recovery time objectives (RTO)

3. **Develop and Implement** - Create recovery strategies addressing:
   - Response team roles
   - Communication methods
   - Alternate work sites
   - IT recovery plans
   - Employee training

4. **Communicate and Integrate** - Distribute plan across organization and embed it into policies, including public relations protocols

5. **Test and Validate** - Use seminars, tabletop exercises, and live simulations to assess preparedness and refine procedures

**Critical Business Functions:**
Organizations must identify "critical and non-critical business operations" and determine which require immediate restoration during incidents.

**Recovery Strategies:**
Key best practices include:
- Establishing alternate work sites and supplier lists
- Developing communication and recovery procedures
- Creating IT recovery and disaster recovery plans
- Implementing backup systems with trustworthy verification mechanisms
- Enforcing multi-factor authentication
- Limiting administrator accounts

**Testing and Validation:**
"Learning opportunities like seminars, tabletop exercises, and live simulations will assess your response team's preparedness" and identify gaps for continuous improvement.

The framework emphasizes cross-functional collaboration, systematic risk assessment, and regular testing to ensure organizational resilience during cyber security incidents.

## 受影響系統

- All organizational systems requiring business continuity planning
- Critical business operations
- IT infrastructure and recovery systems
- Backup systems
- Communication systems
- Alternate work site infrastructure
