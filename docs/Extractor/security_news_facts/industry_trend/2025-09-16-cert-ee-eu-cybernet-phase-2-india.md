## 摘要

After six years of successful operation, Estonia's RIA-led EU CyberNet initiative launched its second phase in September 2025, expanding cyber cooperation to India and the Pacific region.

## 事實內容

After six years of successful operation, the EU CyberNet cyber cooperation initiative led by the Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) started its second phase in September 2025. The second phase focuses on expanding cooperation, particularly to the Indian and Pacific regions. EU CyberNet has been a flagship initiative demonstrating Estonia's role as a leader in European cyber diplomacy and capacity building. The expansion to new regions represents the growing global interest in Estonia's digital governance expertise.

## 受影響系統

- EU CyberNet international cyber cooperation program
- Bilateral cyber capacity building partnerships
