## 摘要

Joint guidance providing recommendations for Internet service providers (ISPs) and network defenders to mitigate cybercriminal activity enabled by bulletproof hosting providers.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security released joint guidance targeting bulletproof hosting (BPH) providers and the threats they enable:

- **Target audience**: Internet service providers (ISPs) and network defenders
- Provides recommendations to mitigate potential cybercriminal activity enabled by BPH providers
- Bulletproof hosting providers deliberately ignore abuse complaints and facilitate cybercriminal operations (ransomware, botnets, phishing infrastructure, malware distribution)
- The guidance addresses detection, blocking, and reporting of traffic associated with BPH services
- Published in conjunction with international partner agencies
- Covers both technical and procedural countermeasures

Bulletproof hosting is a significant enabler of cybercrime, providing infrastructure that is resistant to takedown requests and law enforcement cooperation.

## 受影響系統

- ISP networks
- Enterprise network perimeters
- Cybercrime investigation and defense infrastructure
