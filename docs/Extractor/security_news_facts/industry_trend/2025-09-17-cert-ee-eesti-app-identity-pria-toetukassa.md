## 摘要

Estonia expanded Eesti äpp identity verification to PRIA (Agricultural Registers and Information Board) and the Unemployment Insurance Fund service points, allowing citizens to verify identity by presenting ID in the app.

## 事實內容

Eesti äpp users can now verify their identity at the Estonian Agricultural Registers and Information Board (PRIA) and Unemployment Insurance Fund (Töötukassa) service points, in addition to the previously supported service providers. Users verify their identity by presenting their ID through the app. This expansion of the Eesti äpp identity verification function to government agency service points represents the growing adoption of mobile-based identity verification in Estonia's government service ecosystem.

## 受影響系統

- Eesti äpp identity verification function
- PRIA (Estonian Agricultural Registers and Information Board)
- Eesti Töötukassa (Unemployment Insurance Fund)
