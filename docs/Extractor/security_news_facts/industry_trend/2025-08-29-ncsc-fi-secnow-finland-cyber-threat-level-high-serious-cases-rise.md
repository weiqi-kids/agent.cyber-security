## 摘要

Finland's Traficom and Supo report that cyber security threat level remains elevated since 2022, with serious data breaches more than doubling. Risk of society-paralyzing cyberattacks assessed as low.

## 事實內容

Finland's Traficom (Finnish Transport and Communications Agency) and Supo (Finnish Security and Intelligence Service) issued a joint press release reporting on the state of cyber security in Finland over the past year:

- The cyber security threat level in Finland has remained elevated since Russia launched its large-scale attack against Ukraine in 2022
- Finnish organisations continue to be targeted by hostile cyber activity
- The number of serious data breaches and attempted intrusions has increased significantly
- The number of serious cases investigated by NCSC-FI has more than doubled compared with the previous year
- Findings of software vulnerabilities have increased significantly, which greatly heightens the cyber threat to society
- Despite these developments, Traficom and Supo consider the likelihood of cyberattacks capable of paralysing society on a broad scale to remain low
