## 摘要

CNCS and DGE published survey results from approximately 21,000 teachers on cybersecurity needs in schools during COVID-19 distance learning, highlighting the importance of cybersecurity for educational digitalization.

## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) published the results of the Cybersecurity and Distance Learning survey, conducted jointly with the Directorate-General for Education (DGE). The COVID-19 pandemic forced schools to rapidly adapt to distance learning, particularly from 16 March 2020, a situation that continued at least until the end of the 2019/2020 school year. This circumstance brought various cybersecurity challenges and demonstrated the importance of this area for the needs of educational digitalization. The survey was completed by approximately 21,000 teachers and presents highly relevant results for understanding cybersecurity needs in schools — both to respond to situations like the pandemic and to maintain secure educational activities in the future.
