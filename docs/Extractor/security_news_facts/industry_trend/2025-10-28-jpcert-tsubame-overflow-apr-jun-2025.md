JPCERT/CC TSUBAME Report Overflow covering overseas sensor monitoring trends and activities for April-June 2025.

## 事實內容

This TSUBAME Report Overflow installment discusses monitoring trends of overseas TSUBAME sensors and other activities not included in the regular Internet Threat Monitoring Quarterly Reports. The article covers the monitoring results for the period April to June 2025. TSUBAME is JPCERT/CC's Internet threat monitoring sensor network that tracks scan and attack traffic patterns globally. The report highlights fluctuations in observed attack traffic, port scanning activities, and notable threat trends detected by overseas sensors during the quarter. This data provides valuable intelligence about evolving attacker behavior patterns and infrastructure targeting trends.
