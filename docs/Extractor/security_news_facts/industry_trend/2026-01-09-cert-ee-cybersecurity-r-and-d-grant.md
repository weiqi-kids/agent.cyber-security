## 摘要

Estonia opens a new grant directed at cybersecurity companies to develop innovative internationally competitive solutions in collaboration with researchers, with EUR 1.5 million allocated and applications open from January 12.

## 事實內容

Estonia is opening a new grant that helps cybersecurity companies develop innovative, internationally competitive solutions in collaboration with researchers. The initiative, launched by Enterprise Estonia and the Information System Authority (RIA), directs EUR 1.5 million into the IT sector. Applications will be accepted starting from January 12, 2026. This investment represents Estonia's commitment to strengthening its cybersecurity industry through research and development collaboration between private sector companies and academic institutions.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian cybersecurity industry ecosystem
- Public-private research collaboration programs
