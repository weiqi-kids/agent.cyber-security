## 摘要

Finnish NCSC weekly security review covering recent cyber threats and security guidance.

## 事實內容

This week, we discuss parcel delivery scams, which are especially common during seasonal peaks such as Black Friday. We also provide an update on Microsoft 365 account breaches and look back at the recently held Critical Code webinar. We remind readers to register for the upcoming EU cyber security funding info sessions, and we share information about the European Commission’s CRA stakeholder event. In addition, we have opened a survey for NCSA customers to gather information on future assessment, approval and accreditation needs. Finally, in this week’s malware review, we take a closer look at the Shiz malware.
