## 摘要

歐洲衛星定位系統 Galileo 高精準服務（HAS）正式開放，免費提供農業、林業等應用場景。

## 事實內容

The Galileo High Accuracy Service (HAS) is now open to all users and available free of charge. The new service provides centimetre-level positioning accuracy and has potential applications in agriculture, forestry, and other precision-dependent sectors. This development is relevant to cybersecurity considerations around critical infrastructure relying on satellite positioning systems.

## 受影響系統

- Galileo satellite positioning system
- Industries using satellite positioning (agriculture, forestry, precision applications)
