## 摘要

UK NCSC fifth annual Active Cyber Defence report highlighting programme key findings and achievements.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre (UK) has published key findings from the fifth year of the Active Cyber Defence (ACD) programme. The report:

- Highlights achievements and expansion during the fifth year of operation
- Documents the maturation of ACD services and capabilities
- Provides metrics on threats blocked and protective measures implemented
- Analyzes trends in the threat landscape and programme effectiveness
- Shares insights on scaling defensive capabilities

This report demonstrates continued progress in reducing cyber risk through automated, proactive defenses.
