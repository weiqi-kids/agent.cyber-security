## 事實內容

The European Cyber Security Challenge 2021 (ECSC) commenced in Prague, Czech Republic, promoted by the European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA). Portugal is one of 18 teams competing in the event. The ECSC is a competition organized by ENISA that brings together young cybersecurity talents from across Europe in a fair-play environment. The competition tests participants' technical cybersecurity skills across various challenge categories. CNCS announced Portugal's team participation as part of its efforts to promote cybersecurity talent development in the country.
