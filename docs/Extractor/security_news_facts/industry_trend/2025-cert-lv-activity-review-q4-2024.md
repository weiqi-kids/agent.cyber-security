## 摘要
Q4 2024 saw an all-time high in compromised IP addresses in Latvia, with incident reports up 3% from Q3 and 25% year-over-year.

## 事實內容
During the fourth quarter of 2024, Latvia experienced an unprecedented increase in cyber threats, with the number of unique compromised IP addresses reaching an all-time high. This represents a significant escalation in both the volume and intensity of cyber attacks. CERT.LV logged a 3% increase in reports compared to Q3 2024 and a substantial 25% increase year-over-year compared to Q4 2023. These metrics indicate a sustained and accelerating trend in cyber threat activity targeting Latvian networks.

## 受影響系統
- Latvia's national network infrastructure
- Compromised IP addresses across sectors
- Critical information systems
