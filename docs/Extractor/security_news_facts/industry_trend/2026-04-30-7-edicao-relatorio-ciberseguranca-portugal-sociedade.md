## 摘要

CNCS releases the 7th edition of the Cybersecurity in Portugal report (Society theme), featuring a new methodology focused on assessing the cyber-resilience of Portuguese society across individuals, families, businesses, and public administration.

## 事實內容

The 7th edition of the "Cybersecurity in Portugal – Society" report has been published by the Cybersecurity Observatory of the Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS). The report analyzes the social component of cybersecurity across individuals, families, businesses, and public administration bodies.

**Methodology change**: This edition introduces a substantial change in analytical methodology, resulting from a realignment of the document's primary objective. It now focuses on identifying the state of resilience of Portuguese society against cyber threats, rather than simply cataloguing incidents or statistics.

**Key findings** (from description):
- The report covers attitudes, behaviors, awareness, and cybersecurity education of citizens and organizations
- Provides an updated panorama of cyber resilience across all societal sectors
- Developed in collaboration with academic and research partners

The report is available through the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory.

## 受影響系統

- General Portuguese society (individuals, families, businesses, public administration)
