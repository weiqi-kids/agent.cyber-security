## 事實內容

CERT.LV has published its Q4 2025 activity review, highlighting that the level of cyber threats in Latvia remains high with a steady upward trajectory. The report identifies multiple threat drivers and motivational factors affecting the country's cybersecurity landscape.

**Key Findings:**

**Threat Level:**
- Overall threat level: High and increasing
- Trend: Steady upward trajectory throughout Q4 2025
- Threat landscape remains complex and evolving

**Attack Motivations:**
The report identifies two primary attack motivations:
1. **Financially motivated attacks** - Cybercriminals targeting organizations for monetary gain through ransomware, data theft, and fraud
2. **Politically motivated attacks** - State-sponsored or ideologically driven campaigns targeting government and critical infrastructure

**Geopolitical Factors:**
Geopolitical factors continue to serve as a significant catalyst for cyber threats. This likely references:
- Regional tensions in the Baltic region
- Latvia's NATO membership and EU alignment
- Ongoing conflicts affecting European cybersecurity posture

The report provides important insights into the evolving threat landscape facing Baltic states and NATO allies in Eastern Europe, demonstrating how geopolitical events directly correlate with cyber threat activity levels.
