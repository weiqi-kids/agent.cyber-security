## 摘要

Portuguese Association for Information Security Promotion (AP2SI), supported by CNCS, launched survey to profile cybersecurity and information security professionals in Portugal.

## 事實內容

The Portuguese Association for the Promotion of Information Security (AP2SI - Associação Portuguesa para a Promoção da Segurança da Informação), with support from CNCS through the Cybersecurity Observatory platform, is conducting a survey to better understand cybersecurity and information security professionals in Portugal.

**Survey Details:**
- **Organizer:** AP2SI
- **Support:** CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory
- **Purpose:** Profile cybersecurity professionals in Portugal
- **Target Audience:** Cybersecurity and information security professionals

**Information Collected:**
- Qualifications and certifications
- Main functions performed
- Salary levels
- Job retention factors
- Professional characteristics

**Timeline:**
- Survey launch: July 5, 2019
- Results publication: Planned for October 2019
- Context: European Cybersecurity Month initiative

**Context:**
This survey represents an effort to understand the Portuguese cybersecurity workforce landscape, including skills gaps, employment trends, and professional development needs - critical data for national cybersecurity capacity building.

## 受影響系統

- Cybersecurity professionals in Portugal
- Information security practitioners
- Portuguese cybersecurity industry
