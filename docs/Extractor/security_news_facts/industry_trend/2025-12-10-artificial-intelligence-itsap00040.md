## 摘要

Canadian Centre for Cyber Security awareness guidance on artificial intelligence, covering how AI works and its cyber security implications for organizations.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security released awareness guidance document ITSAP.00.040 on artificial intelligence (AI). Artificial intelligence uses intelligent computer programs to find patterns in data to make predictions or classifications. AI can be used to perform specific tasks by analyzing data online to replicate human thought processes and decision-making abilities. The guidance covers how AI systems work, their applications in cyber security (e.g., threat detection, anomaly detection), and the risks they introduce including adversarial attacks against AI models, data poisoning, model inversion attacks, and the use of AI by threat actors to enhance phishing and malware development. The document provides a foundational understanding of AI for non-technical audiences and is released alongside the generative AI guidance ITSAP.00.041.

## 受影響系統

- AI-enabled security systems
- Machine learning models used in organizational operations
- Systems vulnerable to AI-enhanced threat actor capabilities
