## 摘要

Canadian Cyber Centre defines five-phase incident response lifecycle with team structure requirements and procedures for detection, containment, and recovery.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security published ITSAP.40.003 providing guidance on developing incident response plans (IRP).

**Planning Framework:**
Organizations should establish an Incident Response Plan (IRP) that documents "processes, procedures, and documentation related to how your organization detects, responds to, and recovers from a specific incident." Before development, conduct a threat and risk assessment to identify critical assets and potential vulnerabilities.

**Five-Phase Response Lifecycle:**

1. **Preparation** – Establish management commitment, define likely incidents, create backup processes, develop patching strategies, and assemble response teams

2. **Detection and Analysis** – Monitor networks for threats, document events, and analyze whether activation is needed

3. **Containment** – Minimize business impact by isolating affected systems and blocking threat actors

4. **Eradication** – Conduct root cause analysis, remove malicious content, patch systems, and address residual vulnerabilities

5. **Recovery & Lessons Learned** – Restore systems, validate functionality, and document improvements for future incidents

**Team Structure Requirements:**
Response teams should include:
- Critical path personnel
- Security practitioners
- IT/cybersecurity specialists
- Project engineers (OT environments)
- Legal and management representatives

Teams require alternate contact methods and backup contacts for unpredictable cyber incidents.

**Key Procedures:**
Organizations must develop policies establishing "authorities, roles, and responsibilities" for incident response, create communications plans identifying internal/external stakeholders, and provide employee training on plans and procedures.

**Detection and Containment Measures:**
Detection involves regular monitoring and documenting potential incidents. Containment may include "deactivating connectivity to your systems and devices" or temporarily isolating systems to prevent further intrusions.

The guidance emphasizes the importance of pre-incident preparation, including threat and risk assessments, team formation, and regular training to ensure effective response when incidents occur.

## 受影響系統

- Organizational networks requiring monitoring
- Affected systems needing isolation/containment
- IT and OT infrastructure
- Communication systems
- Backup and patching systems
- Legal and management coordination systems
