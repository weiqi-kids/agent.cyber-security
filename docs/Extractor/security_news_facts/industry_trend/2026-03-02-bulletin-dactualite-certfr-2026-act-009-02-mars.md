## 摘要

CERT-FR weekly security bulletin reviewing significant vulnerabilities from the past week, highlighting critical security issues requiring prioritized patching attention.

## 事實內容

CERT-FR published its weekly security bulletin CERTFR-2026-ACT-009, covering the period ending 02 March 2026. The bulletin reviews significant vulnerabilities published by CERT-FR during the previous week and emphasizes their criticality to help organizations prioritize remediation efforts.

Key characteristics of this bulletin:
- Consolidates the most impactful vulnerability advisories from the past week
- Provides criticality assessments to aid security teams in prioritization
- Does not replace individual analysis of all CERT-FR advisories and alerts
- Intended to supplement a comprehensive risk analysis approach

Notable vulnerabilities highlighted this week span multiple vendors and products, consistent with the high volume of advisories published in the period (approximately 15+ advisories covering Microsoft, IBM, Cisco, Centreon, Elastic, PostgreSQL, SPIP, Juniper, Wireshark, Stormshield, and Linux kernel distributions).

## 受影響系統

- Multiple vendor products (see individual CERT-FR advisories CERTFR-2026-AVI-0211 through CERTFR-2026-AVI-0229)
- Organizations using products from Microsoft, IBM, Cisco, Centreon, Elastic, PostgreSQL, SPIP, Juniper Networks, Wireshark, Stormshield, MISP, SUSE Linux, Red Hat Linux, Ubuntu Linux, Microsoft Edge
