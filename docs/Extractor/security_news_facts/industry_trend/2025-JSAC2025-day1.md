## 摘要

JSAC2025 Day 1 featured nine presentations covering Chinese APT ecosystems, North Korean threat actors (Kimsuky, DarkPlum), regional campaigns (Earth Freybug, Earth Estries), and IoC lifecycle management.

## 事實內容

On January 21-22, 2025, JPCERT/CC held JSAC (Japan Security Analyst Conference), aiming at enhancing the skills and knowledge of security analysts. The conference brought experts together to share technical insights related to cybersecurity.

**Day 1 Key Presentations:**

**1. China's Hacker Ecosystem**
- Speakers: Steve Su, Aragorn Tseng, Chi-Yu You (Google Mandiant)
- Focus: Campaign analysis involving malware GRAYRABBIT, BLACKSTUDIO, and CROSSWALK
- Techniques: DNS Fast-Fluxing and LLM usage

**2. Earth Freybug Campaign Analysis**
- Speakers: Theo Chen, Leon Chang (TrendMicro)
- Focus: APT targeting Asia Pacific region
- Exploitation: IBM Lotus Domino vulnerabilities
- Malware: DEATHLOTUS, CUNNINGPIGEON, SHADOWGAZE backdoors

**3. IoC Lifecycle Management**
- Speaker: Yusuke Nakajima (NTT Data Group)
- Focus: Addressing registration limits and false positives
- Methodology: Prioritization criteria based on "port numbers, the Pyramid of Pain, and risk scoring"

**4. Huapi Malware Evolution**
- Speakers: Yi-Chin Chuang, Yu-Tung Chan (TeamT5)
- Focus: Targeting edge devices
- Malware: SSHTD, Mabackdoor, and Bifrost refinement
- Infrastructure: Sophisticated multi-layered C2 infrastructure

**5. Earth Estries Intrusions**
- Speakers: Leon Chang, Theo Chen (TrendMicro)
- Focus: Long-term espionage campaign across 10+ countries
- Campaigns: Alpha and Beta
- Malware: DEMODEX and GHOSTSPIDER deployment

**6. Threat Intelligence & lazarus.day**
- Speaker: Jeonggak Lyu (FSI)
- Focus: CTI lifecycle, Pyramid of Pain concept
- Topics: Automation importance and visualization of IoC relationships

**7. Centralized Management Solutions Attacks**
- Speakers: Dongwook Kim, Seulgi Lee (KrCERT/CC)
- Focus: Andariel activities
- Techniques: Golang development; zero-day vulnerability research on third-party solutions

**8. Kimsuky Social Engineering**
- Speakers: Hankuk Jo, Sangyoon Yoo, Jeonghee Ha (NSHC Security)
- Focus: Targeting South Korean Navy personnel via LinkedIn
- Techniques: Fake personas; malicious JavaScript in spear-phishing campaigns

**9. DarkPlum Operations**
- Speakers: Amata Anantaprayoon, Rintaro Koike (NTT Security Holdings)
- Focus: North Korean actor targeting academia and think tanks
- Tools: RandomQuery downloader; MSC file exploitation; KimaLogger deployment

**Conference Overview:**
- Event: Japan Security Analyst Conference (JSAC) 2025
- Dates: January 21-22, 2025
- Organizer: JPCERT/CC
- Purpose: Enhancing security analyst skills and knowledge through expert technical insights
