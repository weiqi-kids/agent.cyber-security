## 摘要

JSAC 2025 annual technical conference held Jan 21-22, bringing together cybersecurity experts to share technical insights and enhance analyst skills.

## 事實內容

On January 21 and 22, 2025, JPCERT/CC held its annual technical conference JSAC, aiming at enhancing the skills and knowledge of security analysts. The conference brought experts in the field of cyber security together to share technical insights related to threat analysis, incident response, and security research.

JSAC serves as a key knowledge-sharing platform in the Japanese cybersecurity community, fostering collaboration between researchers, practitioners, and industry experts to improve collective defensive capabilities.
