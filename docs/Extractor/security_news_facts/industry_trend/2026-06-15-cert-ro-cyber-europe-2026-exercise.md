## 事實內容

Romania's National Directorate of Cyber Security (DNSC) issued a press release announcing the conclusion of the international cybersecurity exercise "Cyber Europe 2026," organized by the European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) and coordinated at the national level by DNSC. The exercise was held in Bucharest (București), with the press release dated 15 June 2026. Cyber Europe is a recurring pan-European cybersecurity exercise simulating large-scale cyber incidents affecting critical infrastructure and public services across EU member states. Romania participated as a national coordinator through DNSC.
