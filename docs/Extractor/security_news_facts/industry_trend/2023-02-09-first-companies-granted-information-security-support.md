## 摘要

芬蘭交通通訊局 Traficom 已核准首批資安發展補助，其餘申請仍在審查中，最晚提交者需等待決定。

## 事實內容

The Finnish Transport and Communications Agency Traficom has granted information security development support to the first cohort of companies. The processing of remaining applications is ongoing. Companies that submitted their applications later are still awaiting a decision on whether they will receive support. This initiative aims to accelerate cybersecurity improvement measures across Finnish businesses.
