## 摘要

Estonia's Rescue Board tested the nationwide EE-ALARM emergency alert system including sirens, Eesti äpp, "Ole valmis!" app, and ERR TV broadcasts; the Eesti äpp notification reached 155,000 devices successfully.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Rescue Board (Päästeamet), together with partners, tested the nationwide emergency alert system EE-ALARM on December 10, 2025. During the test, the siren network was activated, notifications were sent to the Eesti äpp and "Ole valmis!" mobile applications, and emergency alert text bars were shown on ERR channels. The Eesti äpp notification was sent out as planned and reached 155,000 devices. The successful test validated the technical functioning of Estonia's multi-channel emergency alert infrastructure.

## 受影響系統

- EE-ALARM national emergency alert system
- Eesti äpp (Estonian national mobile app)
- "Ole valmis!" (Be Ready!) mobile application
- ERR television channels
