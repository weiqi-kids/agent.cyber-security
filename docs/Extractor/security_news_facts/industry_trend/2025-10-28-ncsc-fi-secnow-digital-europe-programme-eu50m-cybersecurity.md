## 摘要

EU's Digital Europe Programme opens second call for cybersecurity projects in 2025 with €50 million in available funding.

## 事實內容

The EU's Digital Europe Programme has launched a second call for proposals in 2025 targeting cybersecurity projects. A total of €50 million is available under this funding call. The announcement was made by NCSC-FI (Finland's National Cyber Security Centre) highlighting opportunities for cybersecurity organisations and entities to apply for EU funding support.
