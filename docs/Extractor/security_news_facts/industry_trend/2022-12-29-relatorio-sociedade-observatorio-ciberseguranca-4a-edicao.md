## 摘要

CNCS Observatory publishes its 4th Society Report examining cybersecurity attitudes, behaviours, awareness, and education in Portugal, focusing on the human factor — primarily 2021 data with 2022 comparisons.

## 事實內容

- **Publication**: 4th edition of the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory Society Report.
- **Focus**: Attitudes, behaviours, cybersecurity awareness, and education in Portugal — emphasising the human factor.
- **Data period**: Primarily 2021, with some 2022 data where available.
- **Scope**:
  - Cybersecurity indicators aligned with Portugal's National Cybersecurity Strategy 2019–2023
  - Analysis of human capacity-building deficiencies
  - Recommendations for improving cybersecurity posture at the individual and organisational level
- **Available formats**: Full report and a quick-read version (15-minute summary).
- **Document URLs**:
  - Full: https://www.cncs.gov.pt/docs/rel-sociedade2022-observ-cncs.pdf
  - Summary: https://www.cncs.gov.pt/docs/rel-sociedade2022-observ-cncs15m.pdf
