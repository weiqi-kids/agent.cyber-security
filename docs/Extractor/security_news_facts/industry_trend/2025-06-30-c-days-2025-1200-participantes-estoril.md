## 摘要

The 11th edition of C-DAYS cybersecurity conference concluded with approximately 1,200 participants at the Estoril Congress Centre, featuring high-level government participation including the Minister of Presidency.

## 事實內容

The 11th edition of the C-DAYS conference, organized by CNCS (National Cybersecurity Centre) with institutional support from the Cascais City Council and the University of Porto, concluded at the Estoril Congress Centre. The event ran from June 24-26, 2025, and attracted around 1,200 participants. The closing session featured a speech by the Minister of the Presidency, António Leitão Amaro. The conference had the High Patronage of the Presidency of the Republic. C-DAYS is Portugal's leading annual cybersecurity conference, bringing together professionals, academics, government officials, and industry stakeholders to discuss cybersecurity trends, challenges, and innovations.
