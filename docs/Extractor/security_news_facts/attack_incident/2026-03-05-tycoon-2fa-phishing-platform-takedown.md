## 摘要

The Tycoon 2FA phishing-as-a-service (PhaaS) platform has been taken down in a coordinated public-private action led by Europol's European Cybercrime Centre (EC3). The service provided cybercriminals with a subscription-based toolkit to intercept live authentication sessions and gain unauthorized access to accounts, including those protected by MFA. As part of the disruption, 330 domains forming the core infrastructure were taken down. The technical disruption was led by Microsoft with support from a coalition of private partners, while seizure of infrastructure was carried out by law enforcement in Latvia, Lithuania, Portugal, Poland, Spain, and the United Kingdom.

Tycoon 2FA 網路釣魚即服務 (PhaaS) 平台已在由 Europol 歐洲網路犯罪中心 (EC3) 協調的公私聯合行動中被關閉。該服務為網路犯罪分子提供訂閱式工具包，用於攔截即時認證會話並未經授權存取帳戶，包括受多因素認證保護的帳戶。在此次打擊行動中，構成核心基礎設施的 330 個網域被關閉。技術層面的打擊由 Microsoft 主導，並獲私營合作夥伴聯盟支援，而基礎設施扣押由拉脫維亞、立陶宛、葡萄牙、波蘭、西班牙及英國的執法機構執行。
