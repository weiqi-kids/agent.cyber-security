## 摘要

DDoS attacks, Bulletproof Hosting enabler, state-sponsored influence operation exercise, and Locky malware.

## 事實內容

This week's review covers multiple threat topics:
- Denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks and their impacts
- Bulletproof Hosting phenomenon as an enabler of criminal activity
- Exercise where security authorities practiced responding to state-sponsored cyber influence operations
- European Cybersecurity Month with theme of improving everyday cybersecurity skills
- Malware review of Locky ransomware

Bulletproof Hosting refers to hosting services that ignore abuse complaints, enabling criminals to operate with impunity.

## 受影響系統

- Systems targeted by DDoS attacks
- Infrastructure using Bulletproof Hosting services
- Systems affected by Locky ransomware
