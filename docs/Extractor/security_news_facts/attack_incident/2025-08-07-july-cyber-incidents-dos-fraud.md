## 摘要

RIA registered 783 impactful cyber incidents in July 2025, with nearly half being phishing pages; key incidents included Mobile-ID service disruptions, multiple DDoS attacks, and ongoing phone scams impersonating the Health Insurance Fund.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) registered 783 impactful incidents in July, with nearly half being phishing pages (petulehed). RIA's monthly summary highlights the following significant incidents:

- **Mobile-ID service disruptions**: Failures in Mobile-ID functionality affected users
- **Multiple DDoS attacks (teenusetõkestusründed)**: Several denial-of-service attacks targeted Estonian services
- **Ongoing phone scams**: Fraudulent calls continuing primarily impersonating the Health Insurance Fund (Tervisekassa)

- Total incidents: 783 (with impact)
- Phishing pages: ~391 (nearly half)
- Attack methods: DDoS, phone fraud/vishing, service disruption
- Month: July 2025

## 受影響系統

- Mobile-ID service (Estonia)
- Estonian internet services (DDoS targets)
- General public (phone fraud/vishing targets)
- Tervisekassa (Health Insurance Fund) — impersonation target
