## 摘要

Estonia's RIA recorded 804 impactful cyber incidents in February 2026, below the six-month average, affecting digital signing, banking services, and several websites.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) registered 804 impactful cyber incidents in February 2026, which is below the average of the past six months. The incidents affected digital signing services, banking services, and the functioning of multiple websites. The monthly report highlights ongoing cyber threats targeting Estonian digital infrastructure. The lower incident count compared to recent months was noted, though the disruptions to critical digital services remained significant.

## 受影響系統

- Digital signature services (digiallkirjastamine)
- Banking services (pangateenused)
- Various websites
