## 摘要

Yellow alert issued for M365 phishing campaign with dozens of Finnish organizations reporting breaches, spreading through hacked account contacts.

## 事實內容

Major phishing campaign:
- Criminals phishing Microsoft 365 passwords with fake email messages
- Acquired credentials used to break into M365 accounts
- Dozens of Finnish organizations reported phishing messages and new data breaches
- Phishing campaign spreads between organizations through contacts of hacked user accounts

Self-propagating phishing campaign with significant organizational impact.

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft 365 accounts
- Finnish organizations
