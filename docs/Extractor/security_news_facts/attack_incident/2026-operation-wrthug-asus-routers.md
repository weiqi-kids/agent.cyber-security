## 摘要

SecurityScorecard揭露「Operation WrtHug」大規模網路行動，鎖定全球ASUS路由器利用已知漏洞。

## 事實內容

SecurityScorecard's STRIKE team released a critical report revealing a large-scale cyber operation codenamed "Operation WrtHug." This campaign targets ASUS routers globally, exploiting known vulnerabilities to transform infected devices into tools for global network threat activities.

The operation leverages existing vulnerabilities in ASUS router firmware to compromise devices and incorporate them into a larger botnet infrastructure. Infected routers can be used for various malicious activities including DDoS attacks, traffic interception, and lateral movement into connected networks.

TW-CERT issued an emergency alert urging ASUS router users to immediately update their firmware to the latest versions. Organizations should review their perimeter security devices and implement network segmentation to limit potential impact.
