## 摘要
TWCERT/CC reports social engineering attacks impersonating company executives requesting LINE group creation and employee personal data.

## 事實內容
TWCERT/CC received multiple external intelligence reports revealing attackers impersonating internal company executives to launch social engineering attacks, enticing recipients to open emails and execute instructed actions. Attack emails have obvious characteristics, using "executive instructions," "administrative needs," and "urgent tasks" as entry points.

Example email content includes:
1. "For company management convenience, please create a dedicated internal LINE group. After creation, forward the group QR Code to this email, I'll join shortly to arrange work."
2. "Please provide employee roster with personal data information"

These attacks aim to collect sensitive internal information including employee personal data, account credentials, and organizational structure information through impersonation tactics.

## 受影響系統
- Enterprise email systems
- Employee workstations
- Internal communication platforms
