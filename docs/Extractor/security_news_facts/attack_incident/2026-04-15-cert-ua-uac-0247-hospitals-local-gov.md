# UAC-0247 (UAC-0244) Intensifies Cyberattacks Against Hospitals and Local Government

## 摘要

2026年3–4月，UAC-0247對烏克蘭地方政府及公立醫院（含急救醫院）發動密集網路攻擊，FPV無人機操作員也成攻擊目標。

## 事實內容

Between March and April 2026, CERT-UA observed an intensification of cyberattacks by threat cluster UAC-0247 (also tracked as UAC-0244) targeting local government bodies and, primarily, municipal healthcare institutions, including clinical hospitals and emergency medical service hospitals. FPV drone operators were also identified as targets in this campaign. The attacks represent a continuation of this threat cluster's focus on critical infrastructure and civil administration within Ukraine during the ongoing conflict.

## 受影響系統

- Ukrainian local government bodies
- Municipal healthcare institutions (clinical hospitals)
- Emergency medical service hospitals
- FPV drone operators
