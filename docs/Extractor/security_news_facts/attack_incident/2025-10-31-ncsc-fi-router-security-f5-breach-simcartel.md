## 摘要

NCSC-FI covers router security importance, F5 data breach, water supply cybersecurity, and Europol's SIMcartel operation.

## 事實內容

This weekly review addresses multiple cybersecurity developments:
- **Router security** - emphasized as the most important protecting wall in home networks, prevents attacks toward home IT equipment
- **F5 technology company data breach** - security incident affecting major networking technology provider
- **Water supply cybersecurity** - publication of cyber security material for water supply services by VESKY 2025 project
- **Europol's SIMcartel operation** - law enforcement action shutting down infrastructure used in fraud operations
- Malware review of Expiro virus infecting Windows systems

The F5 breach is particularly significant given the company's role in critical infrastructure.

## 受影響系統

- Home network routers
- F5 technology products
- Water supply service infrastructure
- Windows systems (Expiro virus)
- SIM card fraud infrastructure
