# Another wave of FluBot malware spread by SMS

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/varoitus_1/2022  
**Published**: 2022-05-10

## Summary

FluBot malware targeting Android devices is once again being actively spread in Finland via SMS and MMS. The malware aims to steal data from devices, and infected devices should be reset to factory settings.

## Key Points

- **Threat**: FluBot malware actively spreading in Finland
- **Target platform**: Android devices
- **Distribution method**: SMS and MMS messages
- **Objective**: Steal data from infected devices
- **Remediation**: Factory reset required for infected devices
- **Important**: Receiving or opening the message is not dangerous; only clicking links/installing apps causes infection

## Impact

This represents another resurgence of the FluBot campaign, demonstrating the persistent nature of mobile malware threats. Users must remain vigilant about suspicious text messages.
