## 摘要

NUKIB again warned of fraudulent phone calls where perpetrators impersonate NUKIB employees to extract financial transfers from victims under false pretexts.

## 事實內容

NUKIB (Národní úřad pro kybernetickou a informační bezpečnost) again received reports from individuals who faced fraudulent phone calls. During these calls, perpetrators impersonate NUKIB employees. In one reported case, attackers successfully obtained more than half a million Czech crowns from a victim. NUKIB emphasized that these calls are fraudulent and that NUKIB does not, as part of its activities, monitor or control financial transfers. NUKIB filed a criminal complaint against an unknown perpetrator. The agency advises citizens not to send money under time pressure and to follow basic cybersecurity rules.

## 受影響系統

- Czech Republic citizens (financial fraud victims)
- Social engineering via telephone
