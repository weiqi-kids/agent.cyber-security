## 事實內容

This week's review reports:
- Scam calls made in the name of NCSC-FI (impersonation of cybersecurity authority)

Impersonation of NCSC-FI represents a particularly concerning abuse of trust in cybersecurity authorities.

## 受影響系統

- Users targeted by authority impersonation
