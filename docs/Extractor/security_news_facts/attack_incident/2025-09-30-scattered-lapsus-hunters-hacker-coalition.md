## 摘要

Threat intelligence indicates a self-proclaimed coalition "Scattered LapSus Hunters" consisting of multiple hacker groups (Scattered Spider, LAPSUS$, ShinyHunters) emerged. No confirmed technical proof of claimed cloud provider breach, but third-party supply chain risks highlighted.

## 事實內容

Community platforms and threat intelligence show emergence of a self-proclaimed multi-group coalition calling itself "Scattered LapSus Hunters."

Current status:
- The coalition has not yet presented specific technical evidence proving successful infiltration of an international cloud and network service provider's database
- The targeted enterprise has not detected new vulnerabilities or signs of intrusion
- However, in August 2025, the same enterprise confirmed that a third-party partner had been attacked by the hacker group "ShinyHunters," causing partial data leakage (the incident occurred in third-party systems, not the core infrastructure)

Alleged composition:
- "Scattered Spider" — known for sophisticated social engineering techniques
- LAPSUS$ — known for data extortion campaigns
- ShinyHunters — previously involved in multiple large-scale data breaches

This incident highlights the importance of third-party supply chain security management.

## 受影響系統

- Third-party supply chain systems (vendor ecosystem)
