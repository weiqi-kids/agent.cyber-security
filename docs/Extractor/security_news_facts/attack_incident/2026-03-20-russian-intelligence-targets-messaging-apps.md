## 摘要

CISA and FBI warn of ongoing phishing campaigns by Russian Intelligence Services targeting commercial messaging applications (CMAs) to bypass encryption and compromise user accounts.

## 事實內容

CISA and the FBI released a Public Service Announcement (PSA) warning about ongoing phishing campaigns conducted by cyber actors associated with Russian Intelligence Services. These campaigns target commercial messaging applications (CMAs) with the aim of bypassing encryption to compromise communications. The advisory notes that Russian state-sponsored actors are actively attempting to intercept or access communications through messaging platform account compromise. Specific targeting techniques include phishing and social engineering to gain access to messaging accounts. The advisory urges users and organizations to review their messaging application security settings and enable available security features.

## 受影響系統
