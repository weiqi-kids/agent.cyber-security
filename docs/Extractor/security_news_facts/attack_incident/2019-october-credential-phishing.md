## 摘要

October 2019 featured continued credential phishing and doubled email malware incidents.

## 事實內容

Cyber weather in October 2019 was overall rainy. Data breaches involving credential phishing continued to grow, and malware spread by email doubled compared to September. The European Cybersecurity Month provided some positive developments by giving advice on improving everyday cybersecurity skills.

- Email systems
