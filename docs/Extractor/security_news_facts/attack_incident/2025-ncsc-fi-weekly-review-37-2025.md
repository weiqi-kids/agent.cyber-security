## 摘要

NCSC-FI issues severe alert for M365 account compromises targeting all organizations and employees, publishes August Cyber Weather, summarizes Critical Code webinar on software security, and analyzes Hummer malware.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre Finland (NCSC-FI) published its weekly review for week 37 of 2025, featuring a severe security alert.

**Critical Alert:**

1. M365 Account Compromises - Severe Alert:
   - Alert level: Severe
   - Scope: M365 account compromises and attempted breaches
   - Target audience: ALL companies and organizations using M365 products
   - Also targets: Employees of M365-using organizations
   - Status: Active and ongoing threat campaign

**Reporting:**

2. August Cyber Weather Edition:
   - Monthly threat landscape report published
   - August 2025 cybersecurity conditions assessment
   - National threat intelligence update

**Industry Events:**

3. Kriittinen koodi (Critical Code) Webinar:
   - Held during week 37/2025
   - Focus: Software security as key element of security of supply
   - Topic: Supply chain security through secure software development
   - Summary and key takeaways provided

**Technical Analysis:**
- Malware review: Hummer malware
- Detailed examination of Hummer operations

**Significance:**
- Highest alert level issued (severe)
- Universal targeting (all M365 users)
- Persistent campaign (multiple weeks)
- Supply chain security emphasis
- National security of supply concerns

**Context:**
- Part of extended M365 targeting campaign
- Multiple consecutive weeks of M365 compromise reports
- Escalation to "severe" alert level indicates increased threat

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft 365 accounts across all organizations
- Employee workstations and accounts
- Systems infected with Hummer malware
