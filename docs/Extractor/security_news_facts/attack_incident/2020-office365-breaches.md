## 摘要

Office 365 user accounts in Finnish companies increasingly breached; NCSC-FI urges two-factor authentication.

## 事實內容

Office 365 user accounts in several Finnish companies have been breached with new data breaches reported daily. NCSC-FI urges all Office 365 customers to use two-factor authentication and limit the use of email forwarding rules to prevent account compromise.

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft Office 365 accounts
- Finnish companies using Office 365
