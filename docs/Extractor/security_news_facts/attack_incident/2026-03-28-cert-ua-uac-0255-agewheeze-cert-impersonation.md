## 摘要

2026年3月26–27日，UAC-0255假冒CERT-UA發送釣魚郵件，誘騙受害者下載含AGEWHEEZE惡意軟體的受密碼保護壓縮檔，目標涵蓋政府、醫療、金融、教育等多類機構。

## 事實內容

On March 26–27, 2026, CERT-UA recorded cases of email distribution purportedly from CERT-UA, urging recipients to download a password-protected archive ("CERT_UA_protection_tool.zip", "protection_tool.zip") from the Files.fm service and install "specialized software." The malicious payload delivered was identified as AGEWHEEZE malware. The campaign targeted a broad range of organizations including: state/government bodies, medical centers, security firms, educational institutions, financial institutions, software development companies, and others. This attack represents a social engineering campaign exploiting the trusted CERT-UA brand to maximize infection rates across critical sectors.

## 受影響系統

- Ukrainian government/state organizations
- Medical centers
- Security firms
- Educational institutions
- Financial institutions
- Software development companies
