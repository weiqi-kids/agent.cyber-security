## 事實內容

- The Iran-Israel military crisis has produced the most dangerous cyber threat environment Israel has ever faced.
- US-Israeli kinetic strikes against Iran, combined with Israel's "largest cyberattack in history," have created an unprecedented retaliation imperative for Iranian state and proxy cyber actors.
- Five major Iranian APT groups showed simultaneous activity on 2026-02-27, consistent with IRGC-directed coordinated operations.
- MuddyWater has deployed 5 new malware families including Rust-based tools since the escalation.
- Iranian groups active include: MuddyWater, APT33 (Refined Kitten), APT34 (OilRig), Charming Kitten, and Moses Staff.
- Operations targeting Israeli government, defense, and critical infrastructure sectors are expected to intensify.
