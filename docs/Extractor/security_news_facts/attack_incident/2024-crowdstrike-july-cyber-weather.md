## 摘要

July 2024 cyber weather report highlighting CrowdStrike security product update disruption and tax refund-themed scam campaigns.

## 事實內容

NCSC-FI's monthly cyber weather report for July 2024 documented significant security events:
- **CrowdStrike Incident**: A security product upgrade caused widespread global disruption affecting critical infrastructure
- **Phishing Campaigns**: Tax refund-themed scam messages increased in anticipation of August tax refunds
- Overall assessment: Relatively calmer cyber environment compared to previous months, with the notable exception of the CrowdStrike incident

The CrowdStrike incident represents a major supply chain security event affecting organizations worldwide.

## 受影響系統

- CrowdStrike security products
- Organizations using CrowdStrike endpoints globally
- Banking/tax systems (phishing targets)
