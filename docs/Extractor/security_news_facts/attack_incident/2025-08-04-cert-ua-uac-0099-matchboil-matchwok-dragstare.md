## 摘要
CERT-UA reports updated cyberattack tools used by UAC-0099 against Ukrainian government agencies, Armed Forces, and defense-industrial complex enterprises. Initial infection typically uses email campaigns via UKR.NET with "court notices" as lures, containing links to file-sharing services that deliver HTA files.

## 事實內容
- **威脅行為者**: UAC-0099
- **攻擊時間**: Ongoing (reported August 2025)
- **目標**:
  - Ukrainian government agencies
  - Ukrainian Armed Forces (Сили оборони)
  - Defense-industrial complex enterprises
- **攻擊手法**:
  - Email campaigns primarily via UKR.NET email service
  - Thematic lure: "court notices" (судові повістки)
  - Links shortened via URL Shortener services pointing to file-sharing services
  - Downloads double archive files containing HTA files
- **惡意工具 (updated arsenal)**:
  - MATCHBOIL
  - MATCHWOK
  - DRAGSTARE
- **攻擊目的**: Sustained intelligence gathering and compromise of Ukrainian defense entities

## 受影響系統
- Ukrainian government systems
- Ukrainian Armed Forces systems
- Defense industry enterprise networks
