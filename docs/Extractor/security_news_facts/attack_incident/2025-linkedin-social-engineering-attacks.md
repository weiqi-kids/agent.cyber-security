## 摘要

JPCERT/CC warns about unauthorized contacts through LinkedIn targeting organizational assets rather than personal information, representing a shift in social engineering tactics.

## 事實內容

JPCERT/CC has reported on recent incidents involving unauthorized contacts made through LinkedIn, a professional networking platform. These attacks represent a notable shift in social engineering tactics: rather than targeting individuals' personal information or credentials, the attackers aim to compromise organizational assets, intellectual property, or gain access to corporate networks. The use of LinkedIn provides attackers with a veneer of legitimacy and professional context that can lower targets' defenses. Organizations should educate employees about these threats and implement policies for handling unsolicited professional networking requests.

## 受影響系統

- Corporate networks
- Organizational intellectual property
- Employee endpoints
