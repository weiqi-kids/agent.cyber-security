## 摘要

National Cyber Security Centre warns of widespread fraud campaigns spreading through email, WhatsApp, and SMS, including Russian-attributed Star Blizzard attacks targeting professionals.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre (NCKB) issued a warning about extensive waves of fraudulent campaigns utilizing multiple communication channels, including email, messaging applications like WhatsApp, and SMS (smishing).

**Attack Vectors:**
- Email phishing
- WhatsApp messaging
- SMS (smishing)

**Notable Campaign - Star Blizzard:**
In late 2024, Microsoft warned about a Russian threat actor named "Star Blizzard" conducting sophisticated social engineering attacks.

**Star Blizzard Tactics:**
- Targeted professionals with "exclusive opportunity" messages
- Invitation to join private professional WhatsApp groups
- Leveraged professional networking context for credibility
- Social engineering to establish trust before exploitation

**Global Context:**
The campaigns represent a shift in threat actor tactics, moving beyond traditional email phishing to leverage trusted communication platforms and social engineering through professional networks.

**Trend:**
Increasing sophistication in fraud campaigns using multi-channel approaches and trusted communication platforms to bypass traditional security controls.

## 受影響系統

- Email systems
- WhatsApp messaging platform
- SMS infrastructure
- Mobile devices
- Professional networking channels
