## 摘要

NCSC-FI's week 35/2025 review covers child protection scam text messages, Teams call scams impersonating IT support, malware disguised as PDF editing software, and Avalanche malware analysis.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre Finland (NCSC-FI) published its weekly review for week 35 of 2025, highlighting multiple active threat campaigns.

**Active Threat Campaigns:**

1. Child Protection Services Scam:
   - Method: Scam text messages sent in name of child protection services
   - Objective: Phishing for information
   - Target: Parents and guardians
   - Exploitation: Sensitive topic to bypass skepticism

2. Teams Call Scams:
   - Platform: Microsoft Teams
   - Impersonation: IT support staff
   - Method: Call-based social engineering
   - Objective: Gain access to employees' computers
   - Target: Enterprise employees
   - Technique: Phone-based attack combined with remote access

3. PDF Editor Malware:
   - Disguise: Fake PDF editing software
   - Distribution: Malware circulating as legitimate software
   - Capabilities:
     * Information theft
     * System takeover
   - Target: Users seeking PDF editing tools

**Technical Analysis:**
- Malware review: Avalanche
- Focus: Avalanche malware operations

**Threat Characteristics:**
- Multiple simultaneous campaigns
- Social engineering focus across all campaigns
- Exploitation of trusted communication channels (Teams)
- Software supply chain risk (fake software)

**Significance:**
- Diverse attack vectors employed
- Enterprise and consumer targets
- Trust exploitation in multiple contexts
- Technical and social engineering combinations

## 受影響系統

- SMS/text messaging systems
- Microsoft Teams
- Windows systems (PDF malware targets)
- Employee workstations (IT support scam targets)
