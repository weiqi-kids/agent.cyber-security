# Social media account breaches cause stormy cyber weather in May 2023

## Summary
May 2023 saw a considerable increase in report volumes concerning social media account breaches, contributing to "stormy weather" in Finland's cyber environment. The month also featured scam calls and multiple vulnerabilities.

## Key Details
- **Month**: May 2023
- **Primary Threat**: Social media account breaches (significant increase)
- **Secondary Threats**: Scam calls, multiple vulnerabilities
- **Severity**: Described as "stormy weather"

## Context
Part of NCSC-FI's monthly "cyber weather" reporting format, providing trend analysis of cybersecurity incidents affecting Finland.

## Notes
- Metaphorical weather reporting format for threat landscape
- Indicates significant increase in social media compromise incidents
