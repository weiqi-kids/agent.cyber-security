## 摘要

Estonia's RIA recorded 1,561 impactful cyber incidents in May 2026, the highest count in six months. Over 1,047 were phishing/scam pages. Notable incidents included disruptions to public services and a EUR 700,000 fraud against the Estonian Artists' Union.

## 事實內容

- **Reporting period**: May 2026
- **Total impactful incidents**: 1,561 — the highest count in the past six months
- **Phishing/scam pages**: 1,047 incidents
- **Notable incidents**:
  - Disruptions affecting several important public services
  - Fraud against the Estonian Artists' Union (Eesti Kunstnike Liit) with damages of approximately EUR 700,000
- **Trend**: Highest monthly incident count recorded in the last six months
- **Source**: Riigi Infosüsteemi Amet (RIA) monthly cyber incident report

## 受影響系統

- Estonian public digital services
- Estonian Artists' Union (victim of fraud)
- General Estonian cyberspace
