## 摘要

Czech NUKIB joined international warning from FBI and partners about pro-Russian hacktivist groups targeting critical infrastructure.

## 事實內容

The Czech National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NÚKIB), along with Military Intelligence, the National Centre Against Terrorism, Extremism and Cybercrime, the United States, and other partners, joined the FBI's warning about cyberattacks by pro-Russian hacktivist groups targeting critical infrastructure. This international advisory highlights coordinated threat activity from politically-motivated hacker groups aligned with Russian interests.
