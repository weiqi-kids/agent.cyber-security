# Interruptions in online services are common and usually harmless

**Source:** NCSC-FI Security Now!  
**Link:** https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/interruptions-online-services-are-common-and-usually-harmless  
**Published:** 2023-02-27

## Summary

Denial-of-service (DoS) attacks against organizations' websites are common and take place constantly. Measures are taken to prepare for attacks, successfully preventing them daily. Websites may be temporarily down due to DoS attacks, other disturbances, or maintenance.
