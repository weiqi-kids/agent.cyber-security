## 摘要

Joint advisory by Canadian Centre for Cyber Security, US FBI, and international partners warning about pro-Russia hacktivist groups using unsophisticated but opportunistic tactics to target critical infrastructure globally.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (Cyber Centre) joined the United States' Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) and other domestic and international partners in issuing a joint advisory on pro-Russia hacktivist attacks against global critical infrastructure. The advisory highlights the unsophisticated and opportunistic tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) used by pro-Russia hacktivist groups. These groups target minimally secured, internet-facing virtual network computing (VNC) systems and other exposed services. Attack methods include distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, website defacement, and exploitation of publicly accessible industrial control system (ICS) and OT interfaces. Targeted sectors include water and wastewater, energy, transportation, agriculture, and government facilities. The advisory notes that while these attacks are often unsophisticated, they can cause operational disruptions to critical infrastructure. Organizations are urged to secure internet-facing systems, implement multi-factor authentication, and monitor for unauthorized access.

## 受影響系統

- Internet-facing VNC (Virtual Network Computing) systems
- Industrial control systems (ICS) and operational technology (OT)
- Water and wastewater infrastructure
- Energy sector systems
- Transportation and agriculture systems
- Government facility networks
