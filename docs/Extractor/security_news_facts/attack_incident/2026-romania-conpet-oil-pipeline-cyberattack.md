## 摘要

Romania's national oil pipeline operator Conpet experienced a cyberattack disrupting IT infrastructure and taking website offline. Operational technology systems including SCADA remained functional, with oil transport operations unaffected.

## 事實內容

Conpet, Romania's national oil pipeline operator, confirmed a cyberattack that disrupted parts of its technology infrastructure earlier this week.

**Organization Profile:**
- Operator of approximately 3,800 kilometers (2,360 miles) of pipelines
- Function: Supplies domestic and imported crude oil and petroleum products to refineries across Romania
- Status: National critical infrastructure

**Impact Assessment:**

1. Affected Systems:
   - Parts of IT infrastructure disrupted
   - Website knocked offline

2. Operational Systems Status:
   - Operational Technology (OT) systems: Fully functional
   - SCADA systems: Fully functional
   - Telecommunications systems: Fully functional
   - Oil transport operations: Not affected

**Threat Actor Claims:**
- Hackers claim data theft occurred
- Nature and extent of claimed data theft not specified in report

**Significance:**
- Attack targeted critical infrastructure operator
- Despite IT disruption, critical operational systems remained secure
- Demonstrates separation between IT and OT networks

## 受影響系統

- Conpet IT infrastructure
- Conpet website
