## 摘要

Pension scams, BadBox malware in smart devices, Traficom funding for cybersecurity improvements.

## 事實內容

This week's review covers multiple topics:
- Online scams where criminals impersonate pension insurance company
- Scammers frighten recipients claiming pension payments will be stopped
- Clicking the link leads to fraudulent website attempting to steal online banking credentials
- BadBox malware affecting cheap smart devices
- Funding available from Traficom to improve cybersecurity of communications networks and information systems per Cybersecurity Act requirements

The pension scam targets vulnerable populations with fear-based social engineering.

## 受影響系統

- Online banking systems (credential theft)
- Cheap smart devices (BadBox malware)
- Communications networks eligible for Traficom funding
