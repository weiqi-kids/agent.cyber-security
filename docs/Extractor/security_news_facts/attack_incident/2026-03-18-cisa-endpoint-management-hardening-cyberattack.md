## 摘要

CISA issued advisory AA25-077A urging organizations to harden endpoint management systems following a cyberattack against a US organization.

## 事實內容

- CISA published advisory AA25-077A on 2026-03-18
- The advisory follows a cyberattack that targeted a US organization through weaknesses in endpoint management systems
- CISA is urging all organizations to review and harden their endpoint management system configurations
- The advisory provides guidance on reducing attack surface and implementing best practices for endpoint management
- Threat actors exploited misconfigured or insufficiently secured endpoint management infrastructure

## 受影響系統

- Endpoint management systems (various vendors)
- Organizations using enterprise endpoint management solutions
