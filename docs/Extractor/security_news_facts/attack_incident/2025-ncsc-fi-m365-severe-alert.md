## 摘要

Severe alert issued for M365 account compromises targeting all organizations using M365 products.

## 事實內容

This week's review highlights critical security alerts:
- Severe alert issued concerning M365 account compromises and attempted breaches
- Target audience includes all companies and organizations using M365 products
- Alert also targets employees of affected organizations
- August edition of Cyber Weather published
- Summary of "Kriittinen koodi" (Critical Code) webinar held, focusing on software security as key element of security of supply
- Malware review of Hummer malware

The severe alert indicates a high-priority, widespread threat requiring immediate attention.

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft 365 accounts (all organizations)
- Employee accounts in M365 environments
- Systems affected by Hummer malware
