## Summary
Croatian National CERT warns of phishing websites impersonating the official e-Građani (e-Citizens) government portal, distributed via email and SMS to steal personal data and financial information.

## Factual Content
The National CERT warns citizens about the emergence of websites that mimic the e-Građani system. The official e-Građani portal has also issued warnings on their pages about this fraud.

**Attack Vector:**
- Links distributed via email and SMS messages
- Fraudulent sites visually mimic the official e-Građani system
- Messages labeled as "Official NIAS Notification"
- Site appearance closely resembles legitimate government portal

**Target System:**
- **e-Građani:** Croatia's official government digital services portal for citizens
- Used for various government services and official documentation

**Attack Objectives:**
- Theft of personal data
- Financial gain through credential harvesting
- Identity theft

**Official Response:**
Both the National CERT and the official e-Građani system have issued public warnings about this campaign.

**Notes:** Original content in Croatian language (hr); translated to English for analysis; NIAS (National Identification and Authentication System) is Croatia's government authentication infrastructure
