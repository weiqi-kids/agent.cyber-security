## 摘要

The FBI issued a Public Service Announcement attributing phishing campaigns targeting Signal and WhatsApp users to Russian intelligence-linked threat actors. The PSA represents the first direct public attribution of these campaigns to Russian intelligence services, with thousands of accounts already compromised.

## 事實內容

The FBI's Public Service Announcement directly attributes ongoing phishing campaigns targeting encrypted messaging application users (Signal, WhatsApp) to cyber actors associated with Russian Intelligence Services. Previously these campaigns were attributed more broadly without direct intelligence service attribution.

Key points:
- Attribution: Russian Intelligence Services (first direct public attribution)
- Targeted applications: Signal, WhatsApp, and other encrypted messaging apps
- Scale: Thousands of accounts compromised
- Method: Phishing campaigns targeting account credentials
- FBI issued this as a joint PSA with CISA (PSA260320)

Background on the campaigns:
- Signal phishing campaigns previously reported but not publicly attributed to Russia
- Campaigns target high-value individuals with access to sensitive communications
- Compromise of encrypted messaging accounts undermines operational security
- Potential targets: Government officials, defense personnel, journalists, activists

## 受影響系統

- Signal messaging accounts
- WhatsApp accounts
- Other encrypted messaging application accounts
