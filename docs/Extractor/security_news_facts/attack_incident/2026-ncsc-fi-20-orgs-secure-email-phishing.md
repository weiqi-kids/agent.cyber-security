# 20 Finnish organizations compromised in secure email phishing campaign

## Summary
NCSC-FI reports that email accounts have been hacked in twenty different organizations due to secure email-themed phishing. The campaign became active in mid-April, with an estimated five-figure number of phishing messages sent in Finland during April. Multi-factor authentication remains effective protection.

## Key Details
- **Affected Organizations**: 20 different organizations
- **Campaign Start**: Mid-April 2023
- **Volume**: Five-figure number of phishing messages (10,000+)
- **Theme**: Secure email phishing
- **Mitigation**: MFA still effective

## Context
Large-scale phishing campaigns targeting organizations through secure email themes represent sophisticated social engineering, exploiting trust in security communications.

## Notes
- Significant organizational impact (20 victims confirmed)
- High-volume campaign (10,000+ messages)
- MFA effectiveness noted
- Confidence: high
