## 摘要

Online scams affect all age groups; digital skills training more important than age-based approaches.

## 事實內容

The Huijarit kuriin ("Stop the Scammers") project receives calls from both seniors and young people targeted by scams. While seniors have formed the majority at training events, webinars attract younger audiences. The article emphasizes that combating online scams should focus on digital skills rather than age groups. Guest writer Tapani Veija from the Consumers' Union of Finland manages this anti-scam project.

## 受影響系統

- Finnish citizens across all demographics
- Online service users
