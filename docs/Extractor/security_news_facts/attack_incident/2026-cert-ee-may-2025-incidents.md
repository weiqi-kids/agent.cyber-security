## Summary
Estonia recorded 1,107 incidents with impact in May 2025, exceeding six-month average, with 336 phishing pages and 371 fake shops discovered.

## Facts Content
**Reporting Period**: May 2025
**Total Incidents**: 1,107 incidents with impact
**Comparison**: Exceeded past 6-month average

**Incident Breakdown**:
- Phishing pages: 336 (discovered in March)
- Fake shops: 371
- Primary objective: Stealing user credentials and data

**Incident Trends**:
- Increased fraud phone calls
- Rise in fake e-commerce sites
- Continued phishing campaigns
