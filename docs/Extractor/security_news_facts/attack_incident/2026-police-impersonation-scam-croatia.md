## 摘要

Scammers impersonating Croatian police and Europol send threatening messages falsely accusing recipients of cybercrimes to extort money.

## 事實內容

Croatia's National CERT reported increased scam messages where attackers falsely impersonate the police department, Ministry of Internal Affairs, criminal police, or international organizations like Europol. The messages attempt to intimidate recipients with false criminal proceedings and accusations of cybercrimes such as child pornography. This social engineering attack aims to extract money or personal information through fear tactics.

## 攻擊手法

- Social engineering impersonating law enforcement
- Fear-based messaging threatening criminal prosecution
- Fraudulent claims of cybercrime accusations

## 受影響系統

- Croatian citizens
- Email and messaging platforms
