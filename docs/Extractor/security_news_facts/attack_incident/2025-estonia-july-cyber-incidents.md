## Summary
Estonia recorded 783 impactful cyber incidents in July 2025 (nearly half phishing pages), including Mobile-ID disruptions, SOS2 emergency system failure, Eesti app outage, DDoS attacks on government systems, and Health Insurance Fund impersonation fraud.

## Description
The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) registered 783 impactful incidents in July 2025, with nearly half being phishing pages.

**Critical Incidents**:
- **Mobile-ID Disruptions** (July 3, 5): Service interruptions affected Mobile-ID users across different telecom networks. The July 3 incident occurred in the afternoon across multiple operators.
- **SOS2 System Failure** (June 30): Emergency call processing system experienced approximately one hour downtime due to server overload. Multiple users simultaneously updating complex events caused excessive computational load, filling server cache and triggering system failures.
- **Eesti App Outage** (July 8): National app experienced roughly one hour service disruption affecting document verification queries. Downtime coincided with newly added digital identity verification features that substantially increased user demand.

**DDoS Attacks**: Multiple organizations faced DDoS attacks including government registries, defense and justice ministry nameservers, business development agencies, and border management systems. These caused brief service interruptions but no widespread impact.

**Fraud Campaign**: Ongoing phone scams impersonated Health Insurance Fund representatives attempting to extract personal data under false pretenses about unclaimed benefits.
