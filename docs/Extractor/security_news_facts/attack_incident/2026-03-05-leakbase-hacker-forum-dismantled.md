## 摘要

The United States has led the dismantlement of LeakBase, one of the world's largest hacker forums, in a coordinated multinational law enforcement operation hosted by Europol. The forum had over 142,000 members and more than 215,000 messages, providing an enormous archive of hacked databases including hundreds of millions of account credentials from high-profile attacks. LeakBase allowed users to sell stolen data including credit/debit card numbers, banking information, and credentials. On March 3-4, 2026, law enforcement in 14 countries took synchronized actions including shutting down LeakBase, seizing data and domains, and executing search warrants and arrests in the U.S., Australia, Belgium, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Spain, and the UK.

美國主導拆除了全球最大駭客論壇之一 LeakBase，這是由 Europol 主辦的多國執法協調行動。該論壇擁有超過 142,000 名成員及超過 215,000 條訊息，提供大量被入侵資料庫的存檔，包括來自高知名度攻擊的數億組帳號憑證。LeakBase 允許用戶出售包括信用卡/借記卡號碼、銀行資訊及憑證等竊取資料。2026 年 3 月 3-4 日，14 個國家的執法機構採取同步行動，包括關閉 LeakBase、扣押資料和網域，並在美國、澳洲、比利時、波蘭、葡萄牙、羅馬尼亞、西班牙及英國執行搜索令和逮捕行動。
