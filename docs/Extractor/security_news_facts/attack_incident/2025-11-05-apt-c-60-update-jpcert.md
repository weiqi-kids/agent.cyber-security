## 事實內容

JPCERT/CC published an updated report on attack campaigns by threat group APT-C-60, building on previously reported findings. APT-C-60 is a Chinese-linked cyber espionage group known for targeting Japan and East Asian countries with sophisticated attack campaigns.

This update covers new developments in APT-C-60's tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs), including evolving malware toolsets and targeting patterns. JPCERT/CC has been actively tracking this threat actor and providing updated intelligence to help Japanese organizations defend against APT-C-60's ongoing campaigns.

Previous JPCERT/CC coverage of APT-C-60 documented their use of sophisticated malware and targeting of Japanese organizations in various sectors.
