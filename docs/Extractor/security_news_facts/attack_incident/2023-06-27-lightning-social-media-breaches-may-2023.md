# Lightning caused by social media account breaches in May's cyber weather

**Source:** NCSC-FI Security Now!  
**Link:** https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/ajankohtaista/kybersaa_05/2023  
**Published:** 2023-06-27

## Summary

In May, cyber weather saw rainy skies due to scam calls and several vulnerabilities. A considerable increase in report volumes on social media account breaches also caused stormy weather.

## Key Points

- Scam calls remained active
- Multiple vulnerabilities reported
- Significant increase in social media account breaches
- Monthly cyber weather report from NCSC-FI

## Context

This is part of NCSC-FI's monthly cyber weather series reporting on the cybersecurity landscape in Finland.
