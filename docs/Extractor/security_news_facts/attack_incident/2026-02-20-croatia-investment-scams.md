## 摘要

CERT.hr warns of increased malicious advertisement activity on social media promoting investment scams and fraudulent money recovery services.

## 事實內容

The Croatian National CERT has observed heightened activity of malicious advertisements on social networks that promote:

1. **Investment scams**: Fraudulent investment opportunities advertised through paid social media ads
2. **Recovery scams**: Scammers posing as companies specialized in recovering stolen funds from victims who have already suffered from online fraud

Victims may be contacted through various channels including phone calls, emails, or other communication methods. These scams often target individuals who have previously lost money to online fraud, making them particularly vulnerable to follow-up scams.

**Original language**: Croatian (CERT.hr)

**Attack Method**: Social engineering via paid social media advertisements

## 受影響系統

- Social media users in Croatia
- Previous fraud victims (for recovery scams)
