## 摘要

Southeast Asian scam compounds are hiring professional scammers as "AI models" to conduct live deepfake video calls for romance scams and crypto fraud at industrial scale across Cambodia, Myanmar, and Laos.

## 事實內容

Southeast Asian scam compounds — already known as modern slave farms — have added a new type of worker: professional scammers conducting video calls using deepfake technology, referred to as "AI models."

Key details reported by Malwarebytes (citing WIRED):

- Recruitment ads describe roles handling approximately 100 live video calls per day
- Operations promote romance scams and crypto hustles at industrial scale
- Operations span Cambodia, Myanmar, and Laos
- Scam operators juggle similar text sessions with dozens of victims simultaneously
- When victims request a video call, "AI models" use deepfake technology to impersonate trusted figures
- The existing chat operator workforce (many of whom are trafficking victims) has been augmented with these video call specialists

Attack methods:
- Romance scam / pig butchering (investment fraud)
- AI-generated deepfake video impersonation
- Multi-stage social engineering

## 受影響系統

- Individual users targeted via messaging apps and social platforms
- Particularly targets loneliness/financial vulnerability
