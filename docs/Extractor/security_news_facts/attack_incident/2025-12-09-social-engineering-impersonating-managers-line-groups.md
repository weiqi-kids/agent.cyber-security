# Social Engineering Alert: Attackers Impersonate Managers to Request LINE Groups and Personal Data

## 摘要
TWCERT/CC received multiple external intelligence reports of social engineering attacks where threat actors impersonate corporate managers via email, requesting employees to create LINE groups and share personal data, aiming to harvest sensitive organizational information.

## 事實內容
- **攻擊手法**: Phishing emails impersonating internal company executives
- **典型信件內容**:
  - "Please create a dedicated internal LINE group for company management. After creation, forward the group QR code to this email so I can join and arrange work."
  - "Please provide employee roster and personal information."
- **目的**: Harvest employee personal data, establish unauthorized communication channels
- **防護建議**:
  1. Verify email source legitimacy before responding to requests for sensitive information
  2. Do not click suspicious attachments or links; avoid entering credentials on suspicious websites
  3. Confirm unusual requests directly with the purported sender via alternate channels
  4. Regularly change passwords with complexity requirements and enable MFA
  5. Network administrators should deploy preventive blocking measures based on latest threat indicators
  6. Conduct regular internal security awareness training and exercises

## 受影響系統
- Enterprise email systems
- Organizations using LINE for internal communications
