## 摘要

National CERT received numerous reports of SMS messages falsely notifying citizens of traffic violations, directing them to malicious links to harvest personal and financial data.

## 事實內容

The Croatian National CERT received multiple citizen reports about SMS messages falsely claiming that their vehicle was recorded committing a traffic violation under the Road Traffic Safety Act. The messages contain a link for alleged payment of the fine. Citizens who click the link are directed to phishing pages designed to harvest personal and financial data. The campaign is an ongoing smishing (SMS phishing) attack targeting Croatian citizens.

**Attack method**: Smishing (SMS phishing), social engineering using traffic fine pretext
**Target**: Croatian citizens / general public

## 受影響系統

- Croatian citizens (mobile phone users)
- Personal and financial data
