## 摘要

Finnish citizens receiving automated robocalls impersonating the East Finland Police, prompting authorities to issue warning to hang up.

## 事實內容

Finnish citizens are receiving fraudulent phone calls impersonating the East Finland Police (Itä-Suomen poliisi). The calls use a robotic voice claiming the recipient is suspected or involved in a police matter. Recipients are instructed to press a specific number to receive more information; otherwise they are told the police will send a letter by mail. East Finland Police have issued a clear warning: "The police do not make such calls. Actions suggested in the call should not be taken." This represents a social engineering technique to either gather personal information or deceive victims into taking harmful actions.

Original language: Finnish (NCSC-FI Finland)

## 受影響系統

- Finnish citizens receiving unsolicited phone calls
- Individuals targeted by police impersonation scams
