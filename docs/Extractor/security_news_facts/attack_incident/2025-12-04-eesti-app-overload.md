## Summary
Rescue Board's emergency alert test on Eesti app successfully reached over 150,000 devices despite temporary overload that was quickly resolved.

## Factual Content
The Rescue Board tested the technical functioning of emergency alerts in the Eesti app and "Ole valmis!" applications today in cooperation with RIA, Women's Voluntary Defence Organisation, and the Ministry of Interior IT and Development Centre (SMIT). The test took place between 10:00-12:00, during which three test notifications were sent to Eesti app users. The Eesti app notification went out as planned and reached more than 150,000 devices.

**Test Results:**
- Period: 10:00-12:00, December 4, 2025
- Notifications sent: 3 test alerts
- Reach: 150,000+ devices
- Issue: Temporary overload
- Resolution: Quick response
- Status: Successful despite initial issues
