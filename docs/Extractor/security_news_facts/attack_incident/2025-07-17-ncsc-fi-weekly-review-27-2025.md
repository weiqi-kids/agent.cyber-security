## 摘要

NCSC-FI 第27週週報：資訊竊取惡意軟體風險警告，假期資安重要性提醒，以及M365環境資安改善建議。

## 事實內容

- Information-stealing malware poses significant risks; NCSC-FI warned about threats targeting credentials and sensitive data during the holiday season.
- Organisations and individuals were reminded to maintain information security practices during holiday seasons when IT staff may be reduced.
- NCSC-FI provided tips on how to improve information security in the Microsoft 365 environment, including recommended configuration hardening.

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft 365 environments
- Systems and accounts targeted by information-stealing malware
