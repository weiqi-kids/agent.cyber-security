## 摘要

NCSC-FI 第24週週報：詐騙電話冒充NCSC-FI進行詐騙，以及「資安領域轉型」線上研討會報導。

## 事實內容

- Scam calls are being made in the name of NCSC-FI; criminals are impersonating the official cyber security authority to deceive victims.
- The fraudulent calls represent a social engineering attack using authority impersonation to gain victims' trust and extract sensitive information or payments.
- NCSC-FI also reported on the "Cyber sector in transition" webinar that took place during this period.

## 受影響系統

- General public (targeted by authority-impersonation phone scams)
