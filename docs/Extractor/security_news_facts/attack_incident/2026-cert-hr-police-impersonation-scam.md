## 摘要
National CERT reports increased scam messages falsely claiming to be from police authorities, threatening recipients with criminal proceedings for alleged cybercrimes like child pornography.

## 事實內容
The National CERT has recorded an increased number of reports regarding scam messages where attackers falsely represent themselves as police departments. The message content also mentions other bodies and institutions such as the Ministry of Internal Affairs, criminal police, or international organizations like Europol. These messages attempt to intimidate recipients with alleged criminal proceedings and accusations of cybercrimes, such as child pornography and other serious offenses. This is a social engineering attack designed to exploit fear and urgency to extract money or sensitive information from victims.

## 受影響系統
- Croatian citizens receiving fraudulent messages
- Potential victims across email and messaging platforms
