## 摘要

RIA registered 759 impactful cyber incidents in August 2025, including 286 phishing pages, with fraud remaining persistently active throughout the summer period.

## 事實內容

According to RIA's monthly cyber review, various types of fraud have not disappeared. In August, RIA registered 759 impactful incidents, of which 286 were phishing pages (petulehed). The report indicates that fraudsters remained active throughout the summer period without taking any break.

- Total incidents: 759 (with impact)
- Phishing pages: 286
- Key trend: Continued fraud activity, no summer reduction
- Month: August 2025

## 受影響系統

- Estonian general public (fraud/phishing targets)
- Estonian internet users
