## Summary

Highly convincing phishing emails impersonating the Finnish Tax Administration target citizens during property tax decision period, using real names of recipients.

## Factual Content

A phishing campaign is sending highly convincing emails that impersonate the Finnish Tax Administration (Verohallinto). The timing is particularly dangerous as the Tax Administration is simultaneously sending real property tax decision notifications to citizens. In one documented case, a person received a legitimate tax authority message at 8:17 AM on a Wednesday and a phishing email at 1:04 PM the same day.

The fraudulent messages are described as extremely convincing. The key difference between the fake and real messages is that the phishing email contains a clickable link. The phishing emails address recipients by their correct first and last names, making them particularly deceptive.

## Affected Systems

- Finnish citizens' email accounts
- Finnish Tax Administration (impersonated)
