## 摘要

Scam websites exploiting ETA and ESTA travel forms, update and password security reminders.

## 事實內容

This week's review covers:
- Scam websites exploiting ETA and ESTA travel forms
- Reminder about importance of updates
- Reminder about strong passwords

ETA/ESTA scam websites target travelers with fake government authorization forms, charging fees for free services.

## 受影響系統

- Travel authorization systems (ETA/ESTA)
- Users applying for travel documents
- Systems requiring updates and strong passwords
