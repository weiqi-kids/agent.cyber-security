## 摘要

CNCS monitors global large-scale IT outage of July 19, contacting ~500 entities and coordinating with EU partners.

## 事實內容

Following the large-scale IT outage that occurred on July 19, 2024 (widely attributed to a faulty CrowdStrike Falcon sensor update), the Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) issued an update confirming it was actively monitoring the situation. CNCS maintained contact with international partners including the European CSIRTs Network and EU-CyCLONe, keeping the international panorama updated. At the national level, CERT.PT directly contacted approximately 500 entities to assess and mitigate impact. Affected entities spanned all sectors covered under the NIS Directive. The incident demonstrated the critical dependency of critical infrastructure on third-party security software and the systemic risk posed by software update failures at scale.

Original language: Portuguese (CNCS)

## 受影響系統

- Windows systems running CrowdStrike Falcon sensor
- Critical infrastructure across all NIS Directive sectors in Portugal and globally
- Approximately 500 Portuguese entities contacted by CERT.PT
