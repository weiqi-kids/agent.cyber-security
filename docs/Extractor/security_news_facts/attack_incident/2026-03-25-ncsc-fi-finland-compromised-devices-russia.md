## 摘要

Finnish security company Loihde warns that thousands of home security cameras in Finland may be unknowingly used to assist Russia, due to unpatched cheap Chinese cameras being compromised.

## 事實內容

Finnish-language article from Ilta-Sanomat (Digitoday), citing Finnish cybersecurity company Loihde:

Key points:
- Home security cameras can easily become insecure cameras
- Loihde's Technology and Development Director Janne Pirttilahti warns: "People buy the cheapest Chinese devices, and very few consumers ever update them"
- Compromised cameras allow criminals to learn household routines (useful for burglars)
- The cameras can also be used for broader area mapping
- State-level actors may be interested in this reconnaissance capability
- Thousands of such devices are estimated to exist in Finland

Threat actor: State-level actors (implicitly Russian, given geopolitical context of Finland-Russia)
Attack vector: Compromised/unpatched consumer IoT cameras (cheap Chinese devices)

Note: This is a Finnish-language article summarized and translated. Confidence is low due to translation limitations and the article being a warning/opinion piece rather than an incident report.

## 受影響系統

- Consumer IoT security cameras (especially cheap Chinese-made models)
- Home network users in Finland (and broadly in Europe)
