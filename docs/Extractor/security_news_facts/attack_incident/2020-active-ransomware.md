## 摘要

Active ransomware attacks continue in Finland; NCSC-FI recommends preparation and understanding of attack methods.

## 事實內容

Active ransomware attacks have continued targeting Finnish organizations. Successful ransomware campaigns can paralyze organizations that have not prepared adequately. NCSC-FI recommends organizations review how ransomware attacks work and implement protection measures against them.

## 受影響系統

- Finnish organizations
- Enterprise IT infrastructure
