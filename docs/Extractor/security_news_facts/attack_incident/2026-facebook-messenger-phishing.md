# Facebook credential phishing via Messenger

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/ttn_20012022  
**Published**: 2022-01-27

## Summary

Facebook Messenger is being actively used for phishing Facebook user credentials. Hacked accounts send messages to obtain recipients' telephone numbers and two-factor authentication codes to hijack their accounts.

## Key Points

- **Attack vector**: Facebook Messenger messages
- **Technique**: Compromised accounts used to spread attack
- **Target**: Telephone numbers and 2FA codes
- **Objective**: Account hijacking
- **Protection**: Be wary of all Facebook messages, even from known contacts

## Impact

This attack demonstrates how compromised accounts can be weaponized for lateral spread. Even messages from trusted contacts may be malicious, undermining social trust mechanisms.
