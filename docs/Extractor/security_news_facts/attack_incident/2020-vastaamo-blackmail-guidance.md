## 摘要

NCSC-FI publishes FAQ addressing blackmail emails and guidance for Vastaamo breach victims.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre received many questions about blackmail emails related to the Vastaamo customer data breach. NCSC-FI published frequently asked questions with answers and advice for victims whose personal information has been leaked online or who suspect their data has been compromised. The page is regularly updated as new information becomes available.

## 受影響系統

- Vastaamo breach victims
- Personal and medical records
