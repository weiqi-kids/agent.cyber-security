## 摘要

TWCERT/CC received intelligence about social engineering attacks where threat actors impersonate company executives via email to request employees create LINE groups or provide employee roster data.

## 事實內容

TWCERT/CC received multiple external threat intelligence reports of social engineering attacks where attackers impersonate company internal supervisors/executives to launch phishing campaigns.

Attack characteristics:
- Entry point: Email content using "supervisor instructions," "administrative requirements," or "urgent tasks"
- Sample messages include:
  - "Please create a dedicated company internal LINE group for management purposes. After creation, please forward the group QR Code to this email address."
  - "Please provide employee roster and personal information."
- Purpose: To trick employees into creating communication groups controlled by attackers, or to harvest employee personal data for further targeted attacks

TWCERT/CC recommends organizations notify all staff to heighten awareness and verify any requests through official channels before taking action.

## 受影響系統

- Enterprise employees (social engineering target)
- Corporate communication platforms (LINE, WeChat, etc.)
