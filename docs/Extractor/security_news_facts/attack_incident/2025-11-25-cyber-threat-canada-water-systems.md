## 摘要

Assessment of the cyber threat landscape targeting Canada's water and wastewater systems, with mitigation guidance for critical infrastructure operators in the water sector.

## 事實內容

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security released a threat assessment and mitigation guide focused on cyber threats to Canada's water systems:

- Provides a detailed assessment of the cyber threat environment facing Canadian water and wastewater infrastructure
- Identifies threat actors and techniques targeting the water sector
- Water systems represent critical infrastructure vulnerable to cyber attacks that could disrupt essential services
- The guidance outlines specific mitigation measures for water system operators
- Addresses both nation-state and cybercriminal threats to water infrastructure
- Published in the context of increasing global attacks on water utilities (e.g., attacks by threat actors on U.S. and European water systems)

The report is aimed at helping water sector organizations understand and address cyber risks that could impact public health and safety.

## 受影響系統

- Water treatment facilities
- Wastewater systems
- Water distribution infrastructure
- Industrial Control Systems (ICS) in the water sector
