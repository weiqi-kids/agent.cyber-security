## Summary
Croatian national CERT reports increased malicious social media advertising promoting fake investment opportunities and fraudulent money recovery services targeting victims of previous scams.

## Factual Content
The National CERT of Croatia has observed increased activity of malicious advertisements on social networks that encourage investment and help with recovering stolen money. Through paid advertisements on social networks, scammers present themselves as companies specialized in returning stolen funds to clients who have suffered online fraud.

**Attack Vector:**
- Paid advertisements on social media platforms
- Personal contact via calls, emails, or other communication channels
- Impersonation of legitimate recovery services

**Impact:**
- Financial losses to Croatian citizens
- Double victimization of previous scam targets
- Social engineering attacks through multiple channels

**Geographic Focus:** Croatia
