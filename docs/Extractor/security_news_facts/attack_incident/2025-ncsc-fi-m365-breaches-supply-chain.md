## 摘要

Widespread M365 breaches in September, Swedish data breach, supply chain attacks, and Tinba trojan.

## 事實內容

This week's review reports on major security incidents:
- Widespread impact of M365 account breaches observed in September
- Guidance shared on how to protect against M365 compromises
- Large-scale data breach that occurred in Sweden (cross-border impact)
- Recent supply chain attacks examined
- Information on upcoming reporting obligation for vulnerabilities under the Cyber Resilience Act (enters into force on September 11, 2026)
- Malware review of Tinba banking trojan

This represents a significant escalation in M365 compromises and notable supply chain incidents.

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft 365 accounts (widespread campaign)
- Swedish infrastructure (large-scale data breach)
- Supply chain systems
- Banking systems (Tinba trojan)
