# UAC-0241 Cyberattack Against Educational Institution in Eastern Ukraine Using GAMYBEAR

## Summary
Ukrainian CERT reports UAC-0241 cyberattack in early November 2025 against an educational institution in eastern Ukraine, using compromised Gmail account and distributing emails themed "Order № 332" with GAMYBEAR malware.

## Factual Content
**Incident Details:**
- **Alert ID:** CERT-UA#18329
- **Threat Actor:** UAC-0241
- **Target:** Educational institutions and government agencies in eastern Ukraine
- **Timing:** First decade of November 2025
- **Malware:** GAMYBEAR

**Attack Vector:**
Distribution of malicious emails with subject "Order № 332" (Наказ № 332) using a compromised Gmail account belonging to a higher educational institution in the region.

**Compromised Account:**
- **Platform:** Gmail
- **Original Owner:** Higher educational institution in eastern Ukraine
- **Use:** Sending spear-phishing emails to educational and government targets

**Target Sectors:**
- Educational institutions
- Government agencies
- Organizations in eastern Ukraine region

**Geographic Focus:**
Eastern Ukraine - region with heightened security concerns due to proximity to conflict zones and strategic importance.

**Malware Context:**
GAMYBEAR represents a custom tool used by UAC-0241 for operations against Ukrainian targets.

**Social Engineering:**
- **Email Theme:** Official administrative order
- **Subject Line:** "Order № 332"
- **Credibility:** Enhanced by using compromised legitimate institutional account

**Impact:**
- Compromise of educational institutions
- Potential access to student and staff data
- Gateway to government networks through institutional connections
- Espionage in strategically sensitive region

**Notes:** Original content in Ukrainian language (uk); translated to English for analysis; eastern Ukraine represents strategically important region; educational institutions often targeted due to less robust security and connections to government networks
