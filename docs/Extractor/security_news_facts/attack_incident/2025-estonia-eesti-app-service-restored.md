## Description
Technical issues that occurred in the Eesti app have been resolved and normal operations restored. The temporary technical problems were caused by high interest and user activity, leading to service disruptions.

This incident demonstrates the challenges of scaling digital government services to meet unexpected demand surges, particularly when new features attract significant user interest.
