## 摘要

芬蘭爆發大規模安全電子郵件主題釣魚活動，20 個組織電子郵件帳號遭入侵，4 月發送五位數量釣魚信件。

## 事實內容

The NCSC-FI has received large numbers of reports about secure email-themed phishing messages in recent months. A new campaign became active in mid-April 2023. According to NCSC-FI statistics, email accounts have been hacked in twenty different organisations. An estimated five-figure number of secure email phishing messages was sent in Finland in April. The phishing messages impersonate secure email notifications to trick users into entering credentials. Multi-factor authentication is recommended as an effective countermeasure to prevent account compromise.

## 受影響系統

- Email accounts across Finnish organisations (20+ confirmed)
- Email platforms targeted via credential phishing
