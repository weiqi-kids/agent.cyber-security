## 摘要

Malicious campaign spreading through Facebook groups lures users into voting scams that facilitate WhatsApp account hijacking.

## 事實內容

Croatia's National CERT warns about a malicious campaign spreading through Facebook groups and pages aimed at unauthorized WhatsApp account takeover. The scam involves messages or posts requesting votes for alleged children's competitions, typically with messages like "Hello! Can you please vote for Maria in this competition?" When users interact with the fraudulent voting mechanism, attackers gain the ability to hijack their WhatsApp accounts through social engineering.

## 攻擊手法

- Social engineering via Facebook messaging
- Fraudulent voting competition lures
- WhatsApp account takeover

## 受影響系統

- Facebook users
- WhatsApp accounts
