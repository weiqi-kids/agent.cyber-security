## 摘要

Finnish NCSC weekly security review and guidance.

## 事實內容

This week we highlight the end of support for the Windows 10 operating system, the EU’s new Instant Payments Regulation and scam calls made in the name of NCSC-FI. The Cyber Security Nordic fair is returning, and we share details about the InfoSec 2025 seminar organised by Traficom and the National Emergency Supply Agency as part of the event programme. We also cover the findings of the Digital and Population Data Services Agency’s Digital Security Barometer 2025, published last week. In this week’s malware review, we introduce Ranbyus.
