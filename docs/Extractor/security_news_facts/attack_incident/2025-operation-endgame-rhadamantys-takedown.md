## 摘要

International Operation Endgame dismantled Rhadamantys data theft infrastructure; SK-CERT received 800,000+ leaked records from 900+ compromised devices.

## 事實內容

Last week, news spread that law enforcement agencies, states, and private institutions in a joint operation called Endgame, led by Europol and Eurojust, dismantled the infrastructure of the Rhadamantys group, which specialized in stealing data from victim computers using malware. SK-CERT received over 800,000 leaked records from more than 900 compromised devices from foreign partners. The operation demonstrates international cooperation in combating cybercrime infrastructure.

## 攻擊手法

- Malware-based data theft
- Credential harvesting from compromised devices

## 受影響系統

- 900+ compromised devices
- Users affected by Rhadamantys malware
