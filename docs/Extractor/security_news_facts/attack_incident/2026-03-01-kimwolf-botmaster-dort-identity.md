## 事實內容

KrebsOnSecurity published an investigation into the individual known as "Dort," who controls the Kimwolf botnet — identified as the world's largest and most disruptive botnet as of early 2026.

**Background**:
- In January 2026, KrebsOnSecurity reported that a security researcher had disclosed a vulnerability which threat actor "Dort" exploited to build the Kimwolf botnet
- Kimwolf is characterized as the world's largest and most disruptive botnet

**Retaliation campaign by Dort**:
Following the January 2026 exposure, Dort conducted a sustained campaign of harassment and cyber attacks against both the disclosing security researcher and KrebsOnSecurity author Brian Krebs:
- Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS) attacks
- Doxing (publishing personal information)
- Email flooding campaigns
- Swatting: Dort caused a SWAT team to be dispatched to the security researcher's home

**Investigation scope**:
The KrebsOnSecurity investigation examines what can be determined about Dort's real-world identity based on publicly available information and OSINT (open-source intelligence) techniques.

The Kimwolf botnet case represents a high-profile example of threat actor retaliation against researchers who expose their infrastructure, and demonstrates the personal risks faced by cybersecurity investigators.

## 受影響系統

- Security researchers and journalists targeted by Dort's retaliatory campaign
- Organizations and networks targeted by Kimwolf DDoS attacks
