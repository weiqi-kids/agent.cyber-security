## 摘要

Estonia's RIA recorded 998 impactful cyber incidents in December 2025, including several notable incidents and mass fraud phone calls made in the name of various agencies during the holiday season.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) registered 998 impactful cyber incidents in December 2025. In addition to several notable incidents, the holiday month saw continued mass fraud phone calls (petukõned) made in the name of various official agencies. The high incident count indicates that attackers and fraudsters took full advantage of the Christmas period when users may be less vigilant. This monthly report highlighted that the holiday season did not bring a reduction in cyber criminal activity in Estonia.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian citizens (fraud phone call targets)
- Estonian digital services
- Various organizational systems
