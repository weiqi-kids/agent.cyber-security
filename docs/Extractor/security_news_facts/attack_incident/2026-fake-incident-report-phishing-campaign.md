## Summary

SANS ISC reports a phishing campaign using fake incident reports as lures, demonstrating evolving attacker tactics and techniques.

## Factual Content

On February 17, 2026, SANS Internet Storm Center researchers received a phishing email disguised as an incident report. The analysis revealed interesting tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) employed by the attackers.

**Key Observations:**
- Phishing emails masquerading as legitimate incident reports
- Use of encoded characters in email content (HTML entity encoding obfuscation)
- Novel social engineering approach leveraging security-themed content
- Potential for automation in phishing email analysis

The researcher notes this represents an evolving TTP where attackers exploit the credibility of security incident reporting to deceive recipients.

## Affected Systems

- Email systems
- End-user workstations
- Organizations with incident response processes
