## Summary

LastPass warns of a phishing campaign using fake unauthorized account access alerts and spoofed support email threads to steal users' vault passwords.

## Factual Content

LastPass is warning users about a phishing campaign targeting its users. The attack uses the following techniques:

- Emails impersonate a LastPass representative by spoofing the display name.
- Subject lines are crafted to mimic forwarded internal conversations between attackers and the company's customer support team.
- The emails discuss a request to change the account's primary email address.
- Email chains are forwarded to the target to create urgency.
- Malicious links are labeled as "report suspicious activity," "disconnect and lock vault," and "revoke device."

The goal is to prompt victims to respond to the perceived suspicious activity and click on the malicious links.

## Affected Systems

- LastPass password manager users
- LastPass vault credentials
