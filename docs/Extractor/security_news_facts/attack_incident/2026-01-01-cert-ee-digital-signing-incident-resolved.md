## 摘要

An incident affecting electronic signature creation in Estonia was resolved on January 1, 2026 at 14:51.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) announced that an incident affecting the creation of electronic signatures was resolved on January 1, 2026 at 14:51. The incident had previously disrupted the ability to create digital signatures in Estonia, impacting users of the country's digital signing infrastructure. The resolution announcement confirmed that normal operations had been restored. Electronic signatures are a critical component of Estonia's digital society, used extensively for government services, business transactions, and personal administrative tasks.

## 受影響系統

- Electronic signature creation services
- Estonian digital signing infrastructure
