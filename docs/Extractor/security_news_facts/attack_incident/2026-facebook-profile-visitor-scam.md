## 摘要

Growing scam falsely claims to reveal Facebook profile visitors, spreading through sponsored ads and messages to steal credentials.

## 事實內容

Croatia's National CERT warns about an increasing scam that falsely claims to reveal who has viewed your Facebook profile, despite Facebook not offering such functionality. The scam typically appears as sponsored ads, shared posts, or direct messages inviting users to click links or install applications to "see who visited their profile." This social engineering attack aims to harvest credentials and personal data.

## 攻擊手法

- Fraudulent sponsored advertisements
- Malicious links and applications
- Social engineering exploiting user curiosity
- Credential theft

## 受影響系統

- Facebook users
- Social media platforms
