## Summary
Croatian National CERT warns of growing Facebook scam that falsely claims to reveal profile visitors, though such functionality does not exist on the platform. The fraud distributes through sponsored ads, shared posts, and direct messages.

## Factual Content
**Scam Characteristics:**
The National CERT warns citizens about a growing fraud that falsely claims it can reveal who has viewed your Facebook profile, although such an option does not exist on that platform.

**Distribution Vectors:**
- Sponsored advertisements
- Shared posts
- Direct messages

**Attack Mechanism:**
Users are invited to:
- Click on malicious links
- Install fraudulent applications
- Provide access to their Facebook account

**Key Deception:**
The scam exploits user curiosity about profile visitors, a feature that Facebook explicitly does not offer, making any such claim inherently fraudulent.

**Potential Consequences:**
- Account compromise
- Credential theft
- Malware installation
- Privacy violations
- Further spread of the scam through compromised accounts
