# January 2021: Emotet botnet seized, phishing via search results

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/kybersaa-tammikuu-2021  
**Published**: 2021-03-04

## Summary

January 2021 cyber weather brightened by the seizing of the Emotet botnet. However, phishing based on search engine results took users to criminal sites instead of their online banks.

## Key Points

- **Positive**: Emotet botnet seized by law enforcement
- **Negative**: Phishing via manipulated search results
- **Impact**: Users directed to criminal phishing sites
- **Target**: Finnish banking credentials
- **Assessment**: Mixed cyber weather with improvements and new threats

## Impact

Emotet takedown represents major law enforcement success against botnet infrastructure. However, new attack vectors like SEO poisoning demonstrate criminal adaptation.
