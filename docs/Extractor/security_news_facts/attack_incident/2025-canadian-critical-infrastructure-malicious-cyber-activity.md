## 摘要

Canadian government issues joint statement warning of nation-state and non-state actors targeting critical infrastructure across multiple sectors.

## 事實內容

On November 26, 2025, the Canadian government issued a joint statement addressing malicious cyber activity targeting Canadian critical infrastructure.

**Threat Actors:**

**Nation-State Sponsored:**
State-sponsored cyber actors with strategic objectives, including pre-positioning capabilities to disrupt services during geopolitical crises or conflicts.

**Non-State Actors:**
- Financially motivated cybercriminals
- Ideologically motivated groups (hacktivists)
- Disgruntled insiders with access to systems

**Targeted Sectors:**

Critical infrastructure across five primary sectors:
- **Power/Energy**: Electrical grids and generation facilities
- **Water**: Treatment and distribution systems
- **Health**: Healthcare providers and medical infrastructure
- **Finance**: Banking and financial services
- **Transportation**: Public transit, railways, aviation

**Key Concerns:**

**Strategic Pre-Positioning:**
Nation-state actors can establish persistent access to critical infrastructure, positioning themselves to disrupt essential services during crises, conflicts, or to achieve strategic objectives.

**Criminal and Ideological Threats:**
Non-state actors target infrastructure for financial gain (ransomware, extortion) or to advance ideological causes (hacktivism, disruption).

**Government Response:**

**Commitment:**
Canada's government pledges to:
- Detect threats through enhanced monitoring and intelligence
- Respond to incidents with coordinated federal resources
- Mitigate threats through transparency and collaboration
- Work with domestic partners, industry, and international allies

**Call to Action for Infrastructure Operators:**

**Strengthen Defenses:**
- Adopt cybersecurity best practices and frameworks
- Implement secure-by-design principles in systems
- Conduct regular security assessments and audits
- Deploy defense-in-depth strategies

**Incident Reporting:**
Promptly report cybersecurity incidents to government agencies (Canadian Cyber Centre, sector-specific regulators) to enable coordinated response and threat intelligence sharing.

**Collaboration:**
Engage with government cybersecurity programs, threat intelligence sharing, and sector-specific security initiatives.

**National Security Imperative:**

The statement emphasizes that critical infrastructure protection is essential for:
- Public safety and continuity of essential services
- Economic stability and national prosperity
- National security and sovereignty

This represents a formal acknowledgment of persistent, sophisticated threats requiring whole-of-nation defense.

## 受影響系統

- Power and energy infrastructure
- Water treatment and distribution systems
- Healthcare facilities and medical infrastructure
- Financial services and banking systems
- Transportation networks (public transit, railways, aviation)
