## Summary

Traficom and partners launched Tietovuotoapu.fi website to house all information for victims of the data breach targeting psychotherapy centre Vastaamo, providing advice and contact details for assistance.

## Details

**Event Type**: Major Data Breach Response

**Affected Organization**: Vastaamo (Psychotherapy centre)

**Response Initiative**: Tietovuotoapu.fi website

**Collaborators**:
- Finnish Transport and Communications Agency Traficom
- Other public authorities
- Various organizations

**Website Content**:
- Steps victims should take
- Details of organizations to contact for assistance
- Centralized information resource

## Impact Assessment

**Severity**: Critical

**Scope**: National (Finland) - High-sensitivity personal data

**Data Type**: Psychotherapy patient records (extremely sensitive)

**Response**: Multi-agency coordinated victim support

## Notes

Source is in English from NCSC-FI. This is a major incident involving highly sensitive healthcare/mental health data, requiring coordinated government response.
