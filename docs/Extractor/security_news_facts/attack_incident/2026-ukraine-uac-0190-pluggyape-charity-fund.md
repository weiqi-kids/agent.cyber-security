# "Unreliable Fund": UAC-0190 Targeted Attacks Against Ukrainian Defense Forces Using PLUGGYAPE

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|

## Summary
Ukrainian CERT reports UAC-0190 conducting targeted cyberattacks against Ukrainian Defense Forces from October to December 2025, impersonating charitable foundations and deploying PLUGGYAPE malware.

## Factual Content
**Incident Details:**
- **Alert ID:** CERT-UA#19092
- **Threat Actor:** UAC-0190
- **Campaign Period:** October - December 2025
- **Target:** Ukrainian Defense Forces (Armed Forces of Ukraine)
- **Malware:** PLUGGYAPE

**Attack Vector:**
Attacks were conducted under the guise of charitable foundation activities, using social engineering to trick military personnel.

**Collaboration:**
Investigation conducted by:
- National Computer Emergency Response Team of Ukraine (CERT-UA)
- Incident Response Team of the Armed Forces of Ukraine (military unit A0334)

**Campaign Characteristics:**
- **Social Engineering Theme:** Charitable/humanitarian assistance
- **Target Sector:** Military and defense personnel
- **Malware Family:** PLUGGYAPE (custom tool)
- **Duration:** Multi-month sustained campaign

**Threat Actor Context:**
UAC-0190 represents a tracked threat group in Ukraine's classification system, conducting targeted operations against military personnel during active conflict.

**Impact:**
- Espionage against Ukrainian military personnel
- Potential compromise of military communications
- Intelligence gathering on defense operations
- Exploitation of humanitarian themes during wartime

**Operational Security Implications:**
Attack demonstrates adversary's willingness to:
- Exploit humanitarian themes for military espionage
- Target individual service members
- Conduct sustained multi-month campaigns
- Use custom malware (PLUGGYAPE) for operations

**Notes:** Original content in Ukrainian language (uk); translated to English for analysis; attack occurred during ongoing conflict; UAC is Ukraine's threat actor tracking designation; campaign exploits trust in charitable organizations
