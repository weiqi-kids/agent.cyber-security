## 摘要

RIA registered 1,138 impactful cyber incidents in April 2026. Some were related to daily digital service disruptions, while phishing attacks increased, causing citizens to lose thousands of euros.

## 事實內容

- **Reporting period**: April 2026
- **Total impactful incidents**: 1,138
- **Digital service disruptions**: Several incidents affected everyday digital services operation
- **Phishing attacks**: Increase in phishing attacks reported; citizens lost thousands of euros
- **Source**: Riigi Infosüsteemi Amet (RIA) monthly cyber incident report
- **Trend**: Both service disruptions and phishing/social engineering attacks increased in April

## 受影響系統

- Estonian digital services
- General Estonian cyberspace users (phishing victims)
