## Summary
Estonia recorded 1,107 impactful cyber incidents in May 2025 (exceeding six-month average), including 336 phishing pages, 371 fraud websites, ransomware attack on Tallinn company exploiting unpatched vulnerabilities, and infrastructure disruptions at Tallinn Airport and Ida-Tallinna Central Hospital.

## Description
The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) registered 1,107 impactful incidents in May 2025, exceeding the previous six-month average. The month featured 336 phishing pages and 371 fraudulent websites.

**Major Incidents**:
- **Ransomware Attack**: Tallinn-based company experienced data encryption through ransomware exploiting unpatched software vulnerabilities. Threat actors gained initial access approximately six months prior.
- **Tallinn Airport**: Automated border control systems outage affected four flights mid-month.
- **Ida-Tallinna Central Hospital**: Widespread IT failure activated crisis protocols.

**Fraud Schemes**:
- Fraudulent webshops advertised on social media with fake closure sales to harvest banking credentials
- Phone scams impersonating Health Insurance Fund, requesting PIN codes via Smart-ID or Mobile-ID under false pretenses regarding unused health benefits

RIA recommends verifying webshop legitimacy and immediately terminating suspicious calls without sharing sensitive information.
