## Summary

Israel Defense Forces claim to have bombed Iran's IRGC cyber warfare and electronic headquarters compound in Tehran.

## Factual Content

The Israel Defense Forces (IDF) announced it conducted a "wide-scale strike" targeting a collection of military sites on the Eastern edge of Tehran. The IDF claims the strike hit the headquarters of the Iranian Islamic Revolutionary Guards Corps (IRGC), including the IRGC's "cyber and electronic headquarters" and its "Intelligence Directorate." The effectiveness of the strike in significantly impacting Iran's cyberattack capabilities remains unclear. The report was published by Politico.
