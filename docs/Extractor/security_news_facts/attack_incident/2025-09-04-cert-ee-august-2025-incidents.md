## 摘要

Estonia's RIA recorded 759 impactful cyber incidents in August 2025, with fraud of various types remaining prevalent throughout the summer vacation period.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) registered 759 impactful cyber incidents in August 2025, according to their monthly report. Various types of fraud (pettused) were prevalent throughout August, with 286 being fraud websites (petulehed). The report highlighted that fraudsters did not take summer vacations, with scam operations continuing at high levels during the holiday season. This ongoing fraud activity during summer months demonstrates the persistent and automated nature of modern cyber fraud campaigns.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian citizens (fraud targets)
- Estonian digital services
- Various organizations
