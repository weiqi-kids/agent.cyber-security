## 摘要

Q1 2025 recorded 631 cyber incidents (11% increase from Q4 2024), with DNS firewall blocking 476,855 malicious access attempts amid geopolitically-aligned attacks.

## 事實內容

CERT.LV's Q1 2025 activity review highlights the evolving cyber threat landscape:

**Incident Volume:**
- 631 cyber incidents reported
- 11% increase from Q4 2024
- 11% decrease from Q1 2024

**Top Threat Categories:**
1. Fraud
2. Intrusion attempts
3. Malicious code
4. Compromised equipment
5. Service availability disruptions

**DNS Firewall Performance:**
- 476,855 blocked access attempts to malicious websites (4% increase from previous quarter)
- 284,029 automatically processed alerts (stable year-over-year)

**Targeted Sectors:**
- Financial institutions
- Law enforcement
- Education
- Healthcare
- Telecommunications

**Geopolitical Context:**
- Cyber espionage and politically-motivated attacks targeting important institutions
- Primary threats from Russian operations
- Emerging interest from Chinese and Belarusian-backed cyber-attackers
- Baltic region experiencing persistent high-threat conditions

**Strategic Observation:**
Cyber operations are "no longer just one-off attacks or attempts to profit" but increasingly "targeted, persistent and aligned with wider geopolitical and economic developments."

## 受影響系統

- Financial sector infrastructure
- Law enforcement systems
- Education institutions
- Healthcare systems
- Telecommunications networks
