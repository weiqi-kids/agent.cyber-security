## 摘要

Iran experienced a near-total internet blackout beginning February 28, 2026 at approximately 07:00 UTC, reducing national connectivity to 4% of normal levels. The shutdown coincided with U.S. and Israeli military operations (Operation Epic Fury) and mirrors measures used during last year's war with Israel, according to NetBlocks monitoring data.

## 事實內容

Internet watchdog NetBlocks confirmed a near-total internet blackout in Iran beginning around 07:00 UTC on February 28, 2026. National internet connectivity dropped to approximately 4% of ordinary levels, as measured by NetBlocks' Internet Observatory platform using network measurement data and web analytics.

Key facts:
- **Timing**: Shutdown began 07:00 UTC, February 28, 2026
- **Scale**: National connectivity fell to 4% of normal levels — classified as "near-total" blackout
- **Context**: Coincides with U.S. and Israeli combat operations (Operation Epic Fury)
- **Historical parallel**: NetBlocks noted the incident "matches measures used during last year's war with Israel"
- **Attribution**: Characterised as a government-imposed internet shutdown (not an external cyberattack)

Iran has a documented history of employing internet shutdowns as a tool during periods of civil unrest or military conflict. This event represents one of the most significant connectivity disruptions observed in Iran in recent years.

Source: Forbes reporting on NetBlocks data, published March 1, 2026.

## 受影響系統

- Iran national internet infrastructure
- Civilian and commercial internet connectivity in Iran
