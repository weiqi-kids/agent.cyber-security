## 摘要

June 2019 saw increase in invoice fraud and cyber-attack on Lahti city services.

## 事實內容

Invoice fraud was a hot topic in June 2019 along with cyber spying. In Finland, the city of Lahti was hit by a cyber-attack affecting city's online services widely. In terms of DoS attacks, June was quiet.

## 受影響系統

- Invoice payment systems
- City of Lahti online services
