## 摘要

Active phishing campaign impersonating Amazon, Microsoft, and government cybersecurity institutions targeting multiple countries including Czech Republic.

## 事實內容

On October 23, 2024, Czech NUKIB received information from partners, including Ukrainian CERT-UA, about an active phishing campaign by an unknown attacker. Attacks were confirmed in several partner countries, including dozens of cases in the Czech Republic, with the overall scope potentially growing. The attacker impersonates Amazon, Microsoft, and government cybersecurity institutions in targeted countries. According to Ukrainian CERT-UA, targets include government and military institutions, as well as private companies across various sectors.

## 攻擊手法

- Phishing emails impersonating trusted brands
- Government institution impersonation
- Multi-country coordinated campaign

## 受影響系統

- Government and military institutions
- Private sector companies
- Czech Republic and partner nations
