## Summary
Estonia recorded 851 impactful cyber incidents in November 2025, including DDoS attacks on municipal websites, ransomware targeting a Tallinn company, and certificate expiration disruptions to border control and healthcare systems.

## Description
The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) documented 851 impactful incidents in November 2025, slightly above the previous six-month average. The month featured a mix of targeted attacks and technical failures.

**Major Incidents:**
- **November 1**: Automatic border control system (ABC gates) became temporarily unavailable due to an expired server certificate.
- **November 9-10**: The Health Insurance Fund's public document registry was inaccessible due to certificate chain failure.
- **November 11**: Tallinn's official website experienced multiple DDoS outages, including one lasting 41 minutes.
- **November 12-13**: Tartu's website suffered comparable DDoS disruptions attributed to active web reconnaissance.
- **November 17**: A Tallinn-based company's server data was encrypted by ransomware. The organization had backup copies enabling service restoration. RIA noted this was the ninth ransomware case documented in 2025, warning that paying attackers finances subsequent attacks.
- **November 18**: A global Cloudflare infrastructure failure during routine database maintenance affected services across multiple countries, temporarily blocking Estonian news outlets and public broadcasters.

Additionally, Kremlin-aligned hacker group NoName057 claimed responsibility for attacks targeting Belgian military intelligence and Danish government websites during election-related tensions.
