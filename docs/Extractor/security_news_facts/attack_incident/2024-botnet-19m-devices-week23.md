## 摘要

SMS scam impersonating Traficom and police operation discovering 19 million device botnet.

## 事實內容

Major incidents reported:
- SMS scam phishing online bank credentials using unpaid fines pretext under Traficom's name
- Police operation discovered over 19 million terminal devices in closed botnet
- Botnet operated by malware installed on millions of devices worldwide
- Instructions provided to check if devices are part of botnet

Significant botnet takedown revealing massive scale of compromised devices.

## 受影響系統

- 19+ million terminal devices globally
- Finnish online banking users (phishing targets)
