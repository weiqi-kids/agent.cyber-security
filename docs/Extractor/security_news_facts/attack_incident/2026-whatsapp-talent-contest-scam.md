## 摘要

National Cyber Security Centre reports increased WhatsApp scam campaign since January 2026, where compromised accounts send voting requests for fake talent contests to steal credentials.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre (NCKB) of Slovakia has observed a resurgence of a fraudulent WhatsApp campaign since January 2026. The campaign uses compromised accounts to spread social engineering attacks.

**Attack Method:**
- Victims receive messages from compromised WhatsApp contacts
- Messages request voting for a talent contest
- Claims daughter or relative of sender can win scholarship or prize
- Messages appear legitimate due to originating from known contacts

**Attack Vector:**
- Account compromise through WhatsApp
- Social engineering leveraging trust relationships
- Credential theft mechanism

**Impact:**
- Account takeover
- Further propagation through victim's contact list
- Potential financial or data theft

The campaign demonstrates the effectiveness of trust-based social engineering, as victims are more likely to respond to requests from known contacts.

## 受影響系統

- WhatsApp users
- Slovak mobile phone users
- Social media platforms
