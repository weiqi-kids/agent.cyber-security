## 摘要

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) registered 768 impactful cyber incidents in January 2026. The incidents affected digital signing services (digiallkirjastamine), telecommunications services (sideteenused), banking services (pangateenused), and various other businesses. The monthly report highlights technical failures that tested multiple digital services across the country. January 2026 saw technical disruptions challenging several key digital infrastructure components that Estonian society depends upon.

## 受影響系統

- Digital signature services
- Telecommunications services
- Banking services
- Various businesses and organizations
