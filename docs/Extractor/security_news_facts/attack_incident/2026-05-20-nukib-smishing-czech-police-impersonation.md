## 摘要

NUKIB warns of an active smishing campaign where attackers impersonate Czech Police via SMS, directing victims to phishing sites to steal payment data.

## 事實內容

NUKIB issued a warning about an active smishing (SMS phishing) campaign in which attackers impersonate the Czech Republic Police via SMS messages with the goal of obtaining victims' payment data. Victims typically receive an SMS informing them of an alleged traffic violation (e.g., speeding) with a demand to pay a fine within a specified deadline (e.g., three days). The message contains a link to a fraudulent website posing as an official government portal. Attackers use various combinations of keywords such as "police," "gov," "portal," "euprava," and "listek" combined with unusual domain extensions (.cfd, .bond, .xyz). The specific domains may change over time. If victims enter their payment details, the information may be used for unauthorized financial transactions or further fraudulent activities.

## 受影響系統

- Czech Republic citizens (payment data at risk)
- Mobile devices (SMS-based attack vector)
