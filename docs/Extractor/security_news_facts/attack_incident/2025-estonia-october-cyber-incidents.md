## Summary
Estonia documented 1,057 impactful cyber incidents in October 2025, including DDoS attacks on government services, coordinated authentication service disruptions, emergency center network failures, and widespread phone fraud campaigns.

## Description
The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) documented 1,057 impactful cyber incidents during October 2025, with denial-of-service attacks, technical failures, and telephony/internet scams as the most prevalent threats.

**Major Incidents:**
- **October 4-5**: Four brief DDoS attacks targeted the eesti.ee portal, but existing security measures prevented significant disruption. During local elections, minimal DDoS activity caused no meaningful impact.
- **October 14**: Network equipment failure temporarily halted operations at the Emergency Response Center's Eastern division for approximately one hour, routing calls to other facilities.
- **October 30**: A coordinated attack affected Mobile-ID and Smart-ID authentication services across Estonia and Lithuania for approximately 20 minutes.

**Fraud Campaigns**: Extensive phone-based fraud persisted throughout October, with callers impersonating banking institutions, utility companies (Elektrilevi), and health services. RIA emphasized that "no government agency or bank requests passwords or PIN codes by phone."

**International Context**: Latvia experienced coordinated DDoS waves affecting multiple government websites. Amazon's October 20 infrastructure failure disrupted thousands of services globally, causing billions in estimated losses.
