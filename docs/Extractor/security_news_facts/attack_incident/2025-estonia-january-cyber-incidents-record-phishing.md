## Summary
Estonia registered 1,121 impactful cyber incidents in January 2025, with record numbers of phishing and scam pages discovered and blocked.

## Description
The Estonian Information System Authority's (RIA) monthly overview shows that 1,121 impactful cyber incidents were registered in Estonia during January 2025. The vast majority consisted of phishing and scam pages, which were discovered and blocked in record numbers.

The surge in fraudulent websites represents an escalation in cyber threats targeting Estonian users, requiring heightened vigilance from both individuals and organizations.
