## 摘要

WhatsApp verification codes being phished in Finland; attackers hijack accounts and continue phishing contacts.

## 事實內容

WhatsApp accounts' verification codes are being phished in Finland. Users must recognize phishing attempts of verification codes sent by SMS and protect themselves by enabling two-step authentication. Successful hijacks lead to account takeover, after which attackers continue phishing using the hijacked account's contact list. Users warned never to share their WhatsApp verification codes.

## 受影響系統

- WhatsApp user accounts
- Finnish mobile users
