## 摘要

伊朗飛彈攻擊杜拜數小時後，詐騙者冒充「杜拜危機管理」部門對杜拜市民發動 SIM 卡交換攻擊，試圖存取銀行帳戶。

## 事實內容

Dubai police warned that financially motivated cybercriminals attempted SIM-swap attacks against Dubai citizens within hours of Iranian missile strikes on the city. The attackers exploited the confusion and fear generated by the kinetic attack to conduct social engineering operations.

**Attack method:**
- **Social engineering vector:** Scammers contacted Dubai citizens posing as representatives of "Dubai Crisis Management" — a fictitious department supposedly tied to Dubai Police
- **Goal:** Gather personal information usable in SIM-swap attacks
- **Objective:** Gain access to victims' bank accounts through SIM-swapping mobile numbers

**Context:**
- The attacks occurred mere hours after Iranian missiles struck Dubai
- The timing was deliberate — exploiting the crisis atmosphere to lower victims' guard
- Dubai police issued an active public warning about these social engineering calls
- The fictitious department name ("Dubai Crisis Management") is designed to sound official during an emergency

**Threat type:** Financially motivated cybercrime exploiting geopolitical crisis for social engineering

## 受影響系統

- Mobile network subscriber accounts (SIM cards)
- Online banking accounts of Dubai residents
- Telecommunications infrastructure (indirect target)
