## 摘要

Booking.com confirmed a security incident in which unauthorized third parties accessed reservation information and personal data of some users; home addresses and credit card numbers were reportedly not compromised.

## 事實內容

The accommodation booking platform Booking.com confirmed a security incident in which unauthorized third parties accessed the reservation information of some users. According to information published by the company, hackers accessed personal data and reservation details of some users. The company stated that data such as home addresses or credit card numbers were not compromised as they are stored in separate systems. The Croatian National CERT issued a warning to users to be vigilant about potential phishing attempts using the compromised data.

**Affected data**: Personal data, reservation details
**Not affected** (per company statement): Home addresses, credit card numbers

## 受影響系統

- Booking.com user accounts
- User personal data and reservation information
