## 摘要

SIM swapping attacks using phishing to obtain victim information, enabling subscription transfer to criminal-controlled SIM cards.

## 事實內容

SIM swapping attack flow:
- International topic for some time
- Scam starts with phishing message through any application/communication channel
- Aims to obtain victim's personal information
- Criminals use information to request operator transfer subscription to another SIM card

Social engineering attack enabling account takeover through telecom provider manipulation.

## 受影響系統

- Mobile phone subscriptions
- eSIM implementations
