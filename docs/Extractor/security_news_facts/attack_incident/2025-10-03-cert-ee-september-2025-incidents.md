## 摘要

Estonia's RIA recorded 680 impactful cyber incidents in September 2025, slightly below the six-month average, but widespread fraud activities remained significant during the previous month.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) registered 680 impactful cyber incidents in September 2025, which is slightly below the average of the past six months. While the numerical count showed a small decline in incidents, the previous month (August) brought significant fraud activity that continued to concern authorities. The monthly report highlighted that although incident numbers were lower, the quality and impact of fraudulent activities remained a serious concern for Estonian citizens and organizations.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian digital services
- Estonian citizens (fraud targets)
- Various organizations
