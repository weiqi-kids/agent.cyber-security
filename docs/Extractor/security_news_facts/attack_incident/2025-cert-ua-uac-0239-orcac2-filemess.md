## 摘要

UAC-0239 conducted targeted cyberattacks against Ukrainian Defense Forces and local governments using social engineering themed around "countering Russian DRGs", deploying OrcaC2 framework and FILEMESS stealer.

## 事實內容

Starting from the second half of September 2025, CERT-UA detected attempts to conduct targeted cyberattacks against the Ukrainian Defense Forces and local government bodies in several regions of Ukraine. The attacks used the theme of "countering Russian diversionary reconnaissance groups (DRGs)" and impersonated the Security Service of Ukraine (SBU).

**Attack Details:**
- **Threat Actor:** UAC-0239
- **Malware Used:** OrcaC2 framework, FILEMESS stealer
- **Attack Vector:** Spearphishing with SBU impersonation
- **Attack Theme:** Countering Russian DRGs (diversionary reconnaissance groups)
- **Target:** Ukrainian Defense Forces, local government bodies
- **Attack Method:** Targeted email campaign with malicious attachments

**Timeline:**
- Second half of September 2025 onwards: Active attack campaign
- October 14, 2025: CERT-UA public disclosure

**Context:**
The attackers exploited legitimate security concerns about Russian DRGs operating in Ukrainian territory to lend credibility to their phishing emails, impersonating Ukraine's Security Service to bypass skepticism.

## 受影響系統

- Ukrainian Defense Forces systems
- Local government bodies in multiple regions
- Windows endpoints
