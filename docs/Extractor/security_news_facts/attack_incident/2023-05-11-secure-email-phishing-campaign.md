# Email accounts compromised in a secure email phishing campaign

**Source:** NCSC-FI Security Now!  
**Link:** https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/email-accounts-compromised-secure-email-phishing-campaign  
**Published:** 2023-05-11

## Summary

NCSC-FI received large numbers of reports about secure email-themed phishing messages. New campaign active since mid-April. Email accounts hacked in twenty different organisations. Estimated five-figure number of phishing messages sent in April. Multi-factor authentication remains effective prevention.

## Key Points

- Major phishing campaign targeting secure email
- 20 organizations affected
- Tens of thousands of phishing messages
- MFA effective as mitigation
