## 摘要

Scientific research analysis of sextortion spam campaigns reveals low-effort, profitable fraud schemes leveraging cryptocurrency.

## 事實內容

Sextortion scams constitute a prevalent internet fraud method wherein criminals send emails falsely claiming to have compromised victims' computers and recorded compromising webcam footage. They demand bitcoin payment within specified timeframes to prevent publication of non-existent recordings.

**Research Analysis:**
A scientific paper analyzed sextortion campaign characteristics, providing empirical data on this widespread fraud technique.

**Campaign Methods:**

**Minimal Infrastructure:**
These operations require minimal infrastructure compared to traditional spam campaigns. Perpetrators need not maintain fraudulent websites, manage physical goods shipment, or establish complex payment processing. Cryptocurrency payments eliminate financial intermediary requirements.

**Reduced Operational Overhead:**
The simplified operational model potentially increases criminal profits relative to conventional spam schemes like fake pharmaceuticals or advance-fee fraud.

**Geographic Segmentation:**
Ransom demands vary by email language, suggesting deliberate geographic targeting and price optimization strategies. Criminals adjust demands based on perceived victim purchasing power in different regions.

**Infrastructure Reuse:**
Many bitcoin addresses recur across multiple campaigns. This may indicate either limited operator pools or, more likely, shared criminal infrastructure and payment processing services.

**Economic Efficiency:**
The research validates cybersecurity professionals' observations that criminals gravitate toward low-effort, high-profit schemes over technically sophisticated alternatives. Sextortion requires minimal technical skills while achieving significant returns through volume and psychological manipulation.

**Broader Implications:**
The analysis demonstrates how cryptocurrency adoption has enabled new fraud models with lower barriers to entry and reduced traceability compared to traditional payment methods.

**Validation of Field Observations:**
The scientific research confirms CERT.at's operational observations about criminal behavior patterns and economics of cybercrime.

## 受影響系統

- Email users globally (indiscriminate targeting)
- Bitcoin payment infrastructure
- Email security and spam filtering systems
