## 摘要

Scammers exploit coronavirus topic for phishing and malware distribution campaigns.

## 事實內容

Scammers and malware distributors often exploit current, emotional topics. Coronavirus has been harnessed as part of the toolkit for scammers and criminals. Users warned to be careful about clicking on coronavirus-related content that may be scams or malware.

## 受影響系統

- General internet users
- Email systems
