## 摘要

Slovakia's NCSC warns of extensive fraud campaign waves spreading via email, WhatsApp, and SMS, including Russian Star Blizzard actor activity.

## 事實內容

Slovakia's National Cybersecurity Centre warns of extensive waves of fraud campaigns spreading not only via email but primarily through communication applications like WhatsApp and SMS (smishing). Microsoft warned in late 2024 about the Russian actor nicknamed Star Blizzard, who sent victims "exclusive opportunities" to join private professional WhatsApp groups. The campaigns use social engineering to gain trust and steal credentials or financial information.

## 攻擊手法

- WhatsApp-based social engineering
- SMS phishing (smishing)
- Email phishing campaigns
- Russian state-sponsored actor involvement (Star Blizzard)

## 受影響系統

- WhatsApp users
- SMS/messaging platform users
- Email recipients
