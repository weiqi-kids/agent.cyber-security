# October 2022 cyber weather grey with ransomware and high DDoS

## Summary
October 2022 saw rare sunny spells. Several ransomware reports received, and denial-of-service attack reports much higher than usual. Medical device information security highlighted as important topic.

## Key Details
- **Month**: October 2022
- **Ransomware**: Several reports
- **DDoS**: Much higher than usual
- **Additional**: Medical device security (US and Finland)
- **Weather**: Grey (mostly negative)

## Notes
- Multiple threat types
- Healthcare sector concern
- Confidence: high
