## 摘要

NCSC-FI reviews most common scam and phishing methods targeting money and personal information from citizens.

## 事實內容

This weekly review provides overview of prevalent attack methods:
- **Primary targets**: Money and personal information from ordinary citizens
- **Broad range of scams and phishing messages** currently active
- Emphasis on need for vigilance when examining new messages
- Recommendation for careful scrutiny of all communications
- Coverage of Cyber Security Nordic fair where NCSC-FI participated

The review serves as public awareness effort to help citizens recognize and avoid common deception tactics.

## 受影響系統

- Individual citizens' financial accounts
- Personal information systems
- Communication channels (email, SMS, messaging apps)
