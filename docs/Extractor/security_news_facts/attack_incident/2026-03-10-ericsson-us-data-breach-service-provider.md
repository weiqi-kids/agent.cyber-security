## 摘要

Ericsson Inc. (US subsidiary) disclosed a data breach affecting an undisclosed number of employees and customers after attackers hacked one of its third-party service providers in April 2025.

## 事實內容

Ericsson Inc., the U.S. subsidiary of Swedish telecommunications giant Ericsson, disclosed a data breach via notifications sent to affected individuals and a filing with the California Attorney General.

Key facts:
- **Discovery date**: April 28, 2025 — the service provider discovered the breach
- **Investigation completed**: Last month (approximately February 2026)
- **Victim data**: Personal data belonging to an undisclosed number of Ericsson employees and customers
- **Attack vector**: Third-party service provider compromise (supply chain / third-party risk)
- **Data misuse**: The compromised provider has found no evidence of data misuse since the breach
- **Notification**: Breach notification letters filed with the California Attorney General

The incident highlights ongoing third-party supply chain risk where a vendor holding sensitive corporate data becomes the attack surface.

## 受影響系統

- Third-party service provider systems handling Ericsson employee and customer data
- Personal data of Ericsson US employees and customers
