## 摘要
SK-CERT reports surge in WhatsApp scam campaign where compromised accounts send messages asking contacts to vote in talent contest.

## 事實內容
The National Cyber Security Centre (NCKB) of Slovakia has documented an increase in cases of a fraudulent WhatsApp campaign since January 2026.

**Campaign Details:**
- **Distribution Method:** WhatsApp messaging application
- **Attack Vector:** Compromised user accounts
- **Social Engineering Theme:** Talent contest voting

**Attack Mechanism:**
1. Victim receives message from a known WhatsApp contact
2. Message sent from compromised account
3. Content requests voting in a talent competition
4. Claims daughter or relative of sender can win scholarship or prize
5. Directs victims to malicious links or further compromise

**Impact:**
- Account compromise spreads through trusted contact networks
- Leverages social trust relationships
- Victims unknowingly propagate the scam to their contacts
- Potential for credential theft and financial fraud

**Notable Feature:**
The campaign exploits the trust inherent in messages from known contacts, making it more convincing than traditional phishing attempts from unknown sources.
