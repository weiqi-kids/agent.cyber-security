# Tax refund season brings criminal scam wave

## Summary
Finnish Tax Administration began tax refund payments. Scammers anticipate refunds, hoping to capture money through another wave of scam messages.

## Key Details
- **Season**: Tax refund period
- **Authority**: Finnish Tax Administration
- **Threat**: Scam messages targeting refunds
- **Timing**: Coincides with refund payments

## Notes
- Seasonal threat pattern
- Financial motivation
- Confidence: high
