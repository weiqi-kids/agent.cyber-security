# Late autumn winds kept cyber weather chilly in November

**Source:** NCSC-FI Security Now!  
**Link:** https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/ajankohtaista/kybersaa_11/2022  
**Published:** 2022-11-22

## Summary

November cyber weather chilly. Ransomware numbers increased in autumn in Finland and abroad. Extortion scams employed new themes. EU Council adopted new NIS2 Directive.
