## 摘要

NCSC-FI lifted its warning about Microsoft 365 security breaches in Finnish organisations; the large-scale phishing campaign that hijacked email accounts has subsided.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre Finland (NCSC-FI) removed its earlier warning regarding Microsoft 365 account security breaches. A phishing campaign had been actively hijacking email accounts of Finnish organisations, leading to the initial alert. The number of reports of Microsoft 365 account breaches has declined significantly. NCSC-FI noted that similar large-scale phishing and security breach campaigns may occur in the future, but at the time of this announcement there was no immediate reason for alarm.

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft 365
- Finnish organisations' email accounts
