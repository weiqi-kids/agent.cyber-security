## 摘要

Massive phishing campaign distributes AGENT TESLA infostealer malware from compromised legitimate email addresses, targeting the general public to steal login and banking credentials.

## 事實內容

The Slovak National Cyber Security Centre (NCKB) NBÚ warns of a massive phishing campaign targeting the general public. The campaign's goal is to infect victims' devices with AGENT TESLA malware, which is used to steal sensitive information such as login credentials and banking details. Attackers send malicious emails from legitimate but compromised email addresses, increasing the likelihood that recipients will trust and open them. The victim receives an email containing a malicious attachment or link that delivers the AGENT TESLA payload.

- **Malware family**: AGENT TESLA (infostealer)
- **Attack vector**: Email phishing using compromised legitimate sender addresses
- **Target**: General public in Slovakia
- **Goal**: Credential theft (login credentials, banking information)
- **Distribution method**: Mass email campaign

## 受影響系統

- Windows endpoints (primary AGENT TESLA target)
- Email users (general public)
- Banking and online service accounts
