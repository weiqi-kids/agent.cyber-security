# Alert: Banking credential theft via fake Kanta and Suomi.fi messages

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/ncsc-fi-issued-alert-attempts-steal-online-banking-credentials-fake-messages-my-kanta-pages  
**Published**: 2021-11-04

## Summary

NCSC-FI issued a severe alert about active campaigns stealing online banking credentials via fake website links in emails pretending to be from My Kanta Pages and Suomi.fi. Users should always type full website addresses directly into browsers.

## Key Points

- **Attack type**: Phishing for online banking credentials
- **Impersonated services**: My Kanta Pages and Suomi.fi
- **Method**: Fake website links sent by email
- **Alert level**: Severe
- **Recommendation**: Type full website address in browser address bar
- **Target**: Finnish citizens using government digital services

## Impact

Criminals exploiting trust in government health and citizen services platforms represents a significant threat. The attack leverages high trust in official Finnish digital services.
