## 摘要

自2026年春起，UAC-0057利用受駭帳號向烏克蘭政府機構發送釣魚郵件，部署新型惡意工具OYSTERFRESH、OYSTERSHUCK及OYSTERBLUES。

## 事實內容

Since spring 2026, CERT-UA has recorded numerous cases of email distribution among government organizations using compromised accounts. The threat actor UAC-0057 leveraged the theme of obtaining certificates through the Prometheus online platform as a lure. The campaign introduced an updated toolset comprising three new malware components: OYSTERFRESH, OYSTERSHUCK, and OYSTERBLUES. The attacks targeted Ukrainian state organizations via spear-phishing using previously compromised email accounts.

## 受影響系統

- Ukrainian state organizations (government institutions)
- Users of the Prometheus online platform
