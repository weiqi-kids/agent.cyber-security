## 摘要

Approximately 1,500 people were manipulated into granting fraudsters access to their eesti.ee accounts; the incident was not a data breach but a social engineering attack used to build trust and steal money.

## 事實內容

According to the Police and Border Guard Board (PPA) and the Estonian Information System Authority (RIA), approximately 1,500 people were manipulated by fraudsters into granting access to their eesti.ee government portal accounts. The incident was not a data breach or security vulnerability. Fraudsters' goal was to use the personal data from eesti.ee to build credibility and then extract money. This is a notable example of social engineering attacks that exploit trusted government portals; criminals gained access through manipulation rather than technical exploitation, then used the legitimately-obtained data to deceive victims further.

## 受影響系統

- eesti.ee government portal (user accounts)
- Estonian citizens (approximately 1,500 victims)
