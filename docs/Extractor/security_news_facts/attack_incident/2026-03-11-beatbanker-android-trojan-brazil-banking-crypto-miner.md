## 摘要

Kaspersky discovered BeatBanker, an Android malware campaign targeting Brazil via a fake Google Play Store phishing site, deploying both a cryptocurrency miner and banking Trojan.

## 事實內容

Kaspersky researchers uncovered BeatBanker, an Android-based malware campaign primarily targeting Brazil. Key findings:

- **Distribution**: Spread through phishing attacks via a website disguised as the Google Play Store
- **Dual-mode capability**: The malicious APKs contain multiple components including:
  1. A cryptocurrency miner
  2. A banking Trojan capable of completely hijacking the device
  3. Screen spoofing functionality for credential theft
- **Evolution**: In a more recent campaign variant, the attackers switched from the banking trojan to a known Remote Access Trojan (RAT)
- **Target**: Brazilian users, particularly those with mobile banking applications
- **Technical complexity**: The dual-mode architecture allows attackers to monetize infections through both cryptocurrency mining and financial fraud

## 受影響系統

- Android mobile devices
- Brazilian banking app users
- Mobile users downloading apps from unofficial/phishing sites
