## 摘要

NCSC-FI issued a yellow alert as criminals phished Microsoft 365 credentials via fake emails, compromising dozens of Finnish organisations and spreading laterally through hacked accounts.

## 事實內容

Criminals were conducting a phishing campaign targeting Microsoft 365 (M365) accounts using fake email messages designed to steal usernames and passwords. Once credentials were obtained, attackers used them to break into M365 accounts. Dozens of Finnish organisations reported phishing messages and new data breaches. The phishing campaign spread between organisations through the contact lists of already-hacked user accounts, indicating a self-propagating attack chain. NCSC-FI issued a yellow alert in response to the scale and impact of the campaign.

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft 365 (M365) accounts
- Finnish organisations' email infrastructure
