## Summary
Estonia recorded 759 impactful cyber incidents in August 2025, including 286 phishing pages. Cybercriminals continued operations through summer with invoice fraud, impersonation schemes, and phishing campaigns targeting Elisa, Telia, and SmartPost customers.

## Description
The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) monthly report shows various fraud types remained prevalent throughout August 2025. RIA registered 759 incidents with impact, of which 286 were phishing pages. Lead analyst Dorel Albin noted that cybercriminals took no summer break.

**Primary Threats**:
- **Invoice Fraud**: Criminals posed as legitimate companies to extract payments
- **Impersonation Schemes**: Attackers pretended to be bank employees and police officers
- **Phishing Campaigns**: Fraudulent messages circulated under the names of major Estonian providers like Elisa, Telia, and SmartPost

**Notable Case**: On August 3, a sports organization fell victim to invoice fraud when an imposter claiming to represent a travel agency redirected over €3,000 to a criminal account for supposed accommodations at Lithuanian athletic competitions.

Albin emphasized that awareness and critical thinking provide the strongest defense, particularly when receiving unsolicited payment requests, password demands, or unexpected communications.
