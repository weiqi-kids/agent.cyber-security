## 摘要

Iran is increasingly incorporating ransomware and cybercriminal tactics into state-sponsored destructive cyber operations, blurring the line between criminal extortion and geopolitical sabotage. As the Iran-Israel-US military conflict escalates, destructive cyberattacks are expected if Iran believes they can deliver meaningful retaliatory impact.

## 事實內容

- Iran has a long track record of using cyber operations to retaliate against perceived political slights.
- Increasingly, ransomware is incorporated into escalating operations that blur the line between criminal extortion and state-sponsored sabotage.
- As Iran considers its response to US and Israeli military actions (Operation Epic Fury, 28 Feb 2026), it is likely to consider destructive cyber operations.
- Iran-backed threat actors are adopting cybercriminal TTPs (tactics, techniques, and procedures) — including ransomware deployment — as cover for destructive attacks.
- This hybrid model allows Iran to maintain deniability while achieving geopolitical objectives through cyber sabotage.

## 受影響系統

- Critical infrastructure sectors globally (energy, finance, government)
- Israeli and US-aligned organizations
- Organizations operating in sectors targeted by Iranian APTs
