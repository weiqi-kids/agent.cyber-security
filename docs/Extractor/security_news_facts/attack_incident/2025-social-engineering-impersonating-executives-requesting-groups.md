## Summary

TWCERT/CC received multiple external intelligence reports revealing attackers impersonating internal company executives to launch social engineering attacks, requesting recipients to create LINE groups, provide employee lists, and personal information.

## Factual Content

TWCERT/CC recently received multiple external intelligence sharing reports discovering that attackers, impersonating internal company executive names, launched social engineering attacks to induce recipients to open emails and follow instructed actions. Organizations are advised to strengthen prevention and raise vigilance. If email content contains suspicious attachments and links, do not click to avoid being hacked.

This intelligence shows attack emails have obvious characteristics, with content mostly using "executive instructions," "administrative requirements," and "urgent tasks" as entry points. Email content examples include:

1. "For company management convenience, please create a dedicated internal LINE group for the company. After creation, please forward the group's QR Code to this email, I'll join shortly to arrange work."
2. "Please provide employee list personal information"

Attack emails typically impersonate company executives or IT departments, using urgent tones to pressure employees to immediately comply with requests. These social engineering tactics exploit employee trust in authority and urgency psychology to bypass traditional security mechanisms.

## Affected Systems

- Enterprise email systems
- Employee communication platforms (LINE, etc.)
- Organizations with insufficient security awareness training
