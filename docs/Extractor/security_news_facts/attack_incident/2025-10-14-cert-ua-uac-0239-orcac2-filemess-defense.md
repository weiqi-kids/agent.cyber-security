# UAC-0239 Cyberattacks Using OrcaC2 Framework and FILEMESS Stealer Against Ukrainian Defense (CERT-UA#17691)

## 摘要
CERT-UA reports that since late spring 2025, UAC-0239 has conducted targeted cyberattacks against Ukrainian Armed Forces and local government entities using the OrcaC2 framework and FILEMESS stealer, impersonating Ukraine's Security Service with themes about "countering Russian DRG (Diversionary and Reconnaissance Groups)."

## 事實內容
- **追蹤編號**: CERT-UA#17691
- **威脅行為者**: UAC-0239
- **攻擊時間**: Second half of spring 2025 onward
- **目標**:
  - Ukrainian Armed Forces (Сили оборони)
  - Local government entities across multiple Ukrainian regions
- **攻擊手法**:
  - Impersonation of Ukraine's Security Service (СБУ)
  - Thematic lure: "Countering Russian DRG" (диверсійно-розвідувальних груп)
  - Distributed as ZIP files disguised as documents about arresting persons who tried to cross the Ukrainian state border
- **惡意工具**:
  - OrcaC2 framework (C2 infrastructure)
  - FILEMESS (file-stealing malware)
- **攻擊目的**: Intelligence collection from defense and government entities

## 受影響系統
- Ukrainian Armed Forces systems
- Local government administration systems across multiple Ukrainian regions
