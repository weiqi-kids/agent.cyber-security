# Technical support scam calls and suomi.fi phishing

## Summary
Weekly review covers technical support scam calls and phishing messages impersonating the suomi.fi service (Finland's official government service portal).

## Key Details
- **Threat 1**: Technical support scam calls
- **Threat 2**: Phishing impersonating suomi.fi
- **Target**: Finnish citizens and organizations
- **Timeline**: Early May 2023

## Context
Impersonation of government services (suomi.fi) represents a particularly concerning threat vector, exploiting trust in official communications.

## Notes
- Dual threat types (phone + email)
- Government service impersonation
- Confidence: high
