# Rainy cyber weather in March due to high numbers of data breaches and scams

**Source:** NCSC-FI Security Now!  
**Link:** https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/ajankohtaista/kybersaa_03/2023

## Summary

Cyber weather in March was rainier than in February. Increase in data breach reports and numerous rent scam messages in early March caused storm clouds.
