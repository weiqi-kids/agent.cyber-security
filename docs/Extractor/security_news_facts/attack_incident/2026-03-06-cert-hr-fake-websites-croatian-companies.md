## Summary

Croatian National CERT warns of attackers creating fake websites impersonating Croatian companies to defraud customers using similar domain names and stolen branding.

## Factual Content

The Croatian National CERT (CERT.hr) has received multiple reports from Croatian companies that have discovered the emergence of fraudulent websites using their names, logos, photographs, and other publicly available data. These fake websites often use similar domain names and copy content from the original web pages to gain the trust of users.

The purpose of these fake sites varies, ranging from defrauding customers to other malicious activities. The attackers leverage brand recognition and visual similarity to deceive users into interacting with the fraudulent sites.

CERT.hr advises companies and users to remain vigilant, verify website URLs carefully, and report suspicious sites immediately.
