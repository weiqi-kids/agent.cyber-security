## 摘要

NCSC-FI's week 47/2025 review covers Black Friday parcel delivery scams, Microsoft 365 account breaches update, and Shiz malware analysis, alongside information about security funding opportunities and regulatory events.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre Finland (NCSC-FI) published its weekly review for week 47 of 2025, highlighting active threat campaigns during the Black Friday shopping period.

**Active Threat Campaigns:**

1. Parcel Delivery Scams:
   - Timing: Exploitation of Black Friday and seasonal peaks
   - Method: Fake delivery notifications
   - Target: Online shoppers expecting packages
   - Objective: Credential theft or financial fraud
   - Note: Especially common during high-volume shopping periods

2. Microsoft 365 Account Breaches:
   - Update on ongoing breach campaign
   - Continued targeting of enterprise accounts
   - Account compromise and unauthorized access

**Technical Analysis:**
- Malware review: Shiz malware detailed examination
- Analysis of malware operation and capabilities

**Events and Initiatives:**

3. Critical Code Webinar:
   - Recent webinar held
   - Focus: Software development security
   - Educational resource for developers

4. EU Cyber Security Funding:
   - Registration open for info sessions
   - Upcoming events to explain funding opportunities
   - Support for cybersecurity projects

5. European Commission CRA Event:
   - Stakeholder event information shared
   - Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) related
   - Regulatory compliance and industry engagement

6. NCSA Customer Survey:
   - Survey opened for feedback
   - Focus: Future assessment, approval and accreditation needs
   - Service improvement initiative

**Significance:**
- Seasonal threats targeting shopping behavior
- Ongoing enterprise account security concerns
- Strong focus on developer and software security
- Funding and regulatory engagement opportunities

## 受影響系統

- Online shoppers (parcel delivery scam targets)
- Microsoft 365 accounts
- Systems infected with Shiz malware
