## 摘要

SK-CERT warns of elevated frequency of SMS Pumping (AIT — Artificially Inflated Traffic) attacks targeting Slovak organizations, an advanced telecom fraud generating artificial SMS traffic volumes.

## 事實內容

The Slovak National Cyber Security Centre (NCKB) NBÚ warns of an increased frequency of so-called SMS Pumping attacks also targeting Slovak entities. SMS Pumping, also referred to as AIT (Artificially Inflated Traffic), is an advanced form of telecommunications fraud in which attackers artificially generate high volumes of inauthentic SMS communications. The goal is to unlawfully profit from inflated SMS traffic, often exploiting SMS-based OTP (one-time password) systems or notification triggers. Slovak organizations are among the identified targets of this escalating attack type.

- **Attack type**: SMS Pumping / AIT (Artificially Inflated Traffic)
- **Attack vector**: Telecommunications / SMS infrastructure abuse
- **Target**: Slovak private and public entities using SMS-based authentication or notifications
- **Goal**: Financial fraud via inflated SMS traffic billing

## 受影響系統

- Organizations using SMS-based OTP or 2FA systems
- Telecom operators and businesses with SMS notification services
- Slovak public and private sector entities
