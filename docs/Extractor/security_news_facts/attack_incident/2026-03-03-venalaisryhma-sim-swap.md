## 摘要

俄羅斯黑客組織宣稱已入侵歐洲（含芬蘭）民用監控攝影機，安全專家認為此事件很大程度上是俄羅斯宣傳行動。

## 事實內容

A Russian hacker group claimed to have gained access to civilian surveillance cameras across Europe, including in Finland. The story was originally reported by Swedish outlet Aftonbladet. Finnish cybersecurity commentators and NCSC-FI's daily news aggregator assessed this as largely Russian propaganda rather than a confirmed large-scale breach. The incident highlights the ongoing use of claimed cyber intrusions as an information operations tool, particularly in the context of the Russia-Ukraine conflict and broader geopolitical tensions in Europe.

**Key facts:**
- Threat actor: Unnamed Russian hacker group (identity unverified)
- Claimed target: Civilian surveillance cameras across Europe, including Finland
- Assessment: Likely propaganda / information operation; technical confirmation lacking
- Reporting chain: Russian group → Swedish media (Aftonbladet) → Finnish media (Ilta-Sanomat/Digitoday)
