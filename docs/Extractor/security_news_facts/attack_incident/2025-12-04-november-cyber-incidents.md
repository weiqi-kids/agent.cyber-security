## 事實內容

The Information System Authority (RIA) of Estonia registered 851 incidents with impact during November 2025.

Key incident types highlighted:
- DDoS (Distributed Denial of Service) attacks
- Ransomware attacks
- Technical failures and malfunctions

The combination of targeted attacks (DDoS, ransomware) alongside technical issues indicates both malicious activity and infrastructure reliability challenges during November.

- Systems affected by ransomware
- Infrastructure experiencing technical failures
