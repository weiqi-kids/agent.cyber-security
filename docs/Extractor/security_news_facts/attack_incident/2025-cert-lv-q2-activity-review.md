## 摘要

Q2 2025 saw 709 cyber incidents (28% YoY increase) with 459,346 vulnerable devices identified, driven by fraud, ransomware, and BEC attacks.

## 事實內容

CERT.LV's Q2 2025 activity review reveals escalating cyber threats in Latvia:

**Incident Volume:**
- 709 cyber incidents reported (12% increase from Q1 2025, 28% increase from Q2 2024)
- Confirms long-term upward trend in intensity and complexity

**Vulnerable Devices:**
- 459,346 vulnerable devices identified
- 62% increase from both Q1 2025 and Q2 2024
- Growth attributed to expanded telemetry sources and automated scanning

**Primary Threat Types:**

1. **Fraud & Social Engineering:**
   - Sharp rise in fraudulent campaigns
   - Attackers impersonating government institutions and well-known companies
   - New attack vectors: smart TVs, voice spoofing, double extortion tactics

2. **Business Email Compromise (BEC):**
   - Increased BEC activity targeting organizations
   - Global login data leaks from Google, Facebook, Apple elevating attack likelihood

3. **Ransomware:**
   - Growing encrypting ransomware operations
   - Attackers demonstrating improved ability to adapt

4. **DDoS Attacks:**
   - Intense DDoS activity with seasonal peaks around holidays and political events
   - Mostly countered through automated defenses

**Election Security:**
- No cyber incidents or threats suggesting external election interference detected during Latvia's electoral period
- Attributed to effective preventive measures

**Recommended Countermeasures:**
- Enhanced public cyber hygiene education
- Improved organizational technical security capabilities
- Greater AI and data telemetry integration

## 受影響系統

- Business email systems (BEC targets)
- Smart TVs (new fraud vector)
- Organizations vulnerable to ransomware
- DDoS protection infrastructure
