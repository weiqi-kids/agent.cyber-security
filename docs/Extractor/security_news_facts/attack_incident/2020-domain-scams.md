## 摘要

Domain names actively and aggressively marketed to .fi domain holders; advice on managing domain portfolios.

## 事實內容

Domain names are being actively and sometimes aggressively marketed to holders of .fi domain names. NCSC-FI recommends domain holders consider in advance which domains they need and register all desired domains with the same registrar. With hundreds of different domain extensions available on the internet, it is not practical to register all variations.

## 受影響系統

- .fi domain name holders
- Finnish organizations with online presence
