## 摘要

SK-CERT warns of ongoing smishing campaign impersonating Všeobecná zdravotná poisťovňa (Slovak health insurance provider).

## 事實內容

Slovakia's National Cybersecurity Centre warns that a smishing campaign is currently ongoing, exploiting the identity of Všeobecná zdravotná poisťovňa, a.s. (General Health Insurance Company). The center urges the public to exercise increased caution. Smishing refers to phishing attacks delivered via SMS text messages, designed to steal personal information or credentials.

## 攻擊手法

- SMS-based phishing (smishing)
- Health insurance provider impersonation
- Credential theft

## 受影響系統

- Slovak health insurance customers
- Mobile phone users
