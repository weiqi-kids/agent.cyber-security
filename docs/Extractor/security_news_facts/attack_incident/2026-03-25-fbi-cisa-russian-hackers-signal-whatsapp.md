## 摘要

FBI and CISA issued a PSA warning of ongoing Russian-linked phishing campaigns targeting Signal and WhatsApp accounts of senior officials, military personnel, civil servants, and journalists — bypassing end-to-end encryption by stealing account access.

## 事實內容

The FBI and CISA published a Public Service Announcement (PSA) warning about ongoing Russian-linked phishing campaigns targeting messaging apps:

Targets:
- Senior officials
- Military personnel
- Civil servants
- Journalists

Key findings:
- European intelligence services have joined the FBI and CISA in issuing this warning
- The same tactics used in earlier large-scale campaigns are now applied more broadly
- The goal is NOT to break end-to-end encryption, but to bypass it by stealing access to individual accounts
- Affected platforms: Signal and WhatsApp
- Attack method: Large-scale phishing campaigns to gain account access credentials/QR codes

Attribution: Russian-linked threat actors

The campaign represents a strategic intelligence collection effort targeting high-value individuals rather than the general public.

- Attack method: Phishing / account hijacking
- Target apps: Signal, WhatsApp
- Attribution: Russian-linked threat actors

## 受影響系統

- Signal messaging application
- WhatsApp messaging application
- High-value individual user accounts
