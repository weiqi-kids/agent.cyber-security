## 摘要

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security published an official threat bulletin assessing that Iran will very likely use its cyber program to retaliate against the joint US/Israel military strikes on February 28, 2026. Canadian critical infrastructure operators and possible targets are urged to remain vigilant.

## 事實內容

- **Date of military strikes**: February 28, 2026 — US and Israel launched strikes against Iran to eliminate nuclear and regime threats
- **Iran's response**: Iran retaliated with military strikes against Israel and US military bases in the Middle East
- **Canadian government position**: Canada released a statement supporting US action to prevent Iran from obtaining a nuclear weapon
- **Cyber threat assessment**: Iran will **very likely** use its cyber program to retaliate against US/Israel and allied nations

**Expected Iranian cyber threat activities:**
- Cyber attacks against critical infrastructure
- Cyber-enabled information operations
- Online harassment of military personnel
- Harassment and repression of diaspora and activist communities

**Characteristics of Iranian cyber threat activity:**
1. **Social engineering**: Iranian actors combine spear phishing with professional social media targeting (aerospace, energy, defence, security, telecom sectors)
2. **Exploiting known vulnerabilities**: Use of public scanning tools to identify vulnerable internet-exposed systems (default/weak passwords, no MFA)
3. **Disruptive/destructive attacks**: DDoS, website defacement, ransomware, wiper malware, hack-and-leak operations

**Historical precedents:**
- Pro-Iran hacktivist groups claimed DDoS attacks against US military, defense, and financial institution websites following 2025 US airstrikes on Iranian nuclear sites
- Iranian state-sponsored actors have previously conducted cyber operations against critical infrastructure (water, energy sectors)
- Iran deploys a network of hacktivist personas and social media channels to shape public opinion

**Canada-specific risk**: Pro-Iran hacktivists will likely view Canada as a target for low-sophistication disruptive cyber activity due to Canada's public support of US/Israel military actions.

**Information basis**: Assessment based on information available as of February 28, 2026.

## 受影響系統

- Critical infrastructure operators (water, energy sectors)
- US, Israeli, and allied nation government systems
- Canadian organizations with US/Israel affiliations
- Political activists, journalists, human rights advocates in diaspora communities
- Internet-connected devices with weak configurations (default passwords, no MFA)
