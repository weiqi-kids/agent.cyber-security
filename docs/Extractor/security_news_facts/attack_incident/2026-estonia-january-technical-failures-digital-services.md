## 摘要

Estonia recorded 768 incidents in January 2026, affecting digital signatures, telecommunications, banking services, and various businesses.

## 事實內容

The Information System Authority (RIA) of Estonia registered 768 impactful incidents during January 2026. These incidents affected multiple critical services including:

- Digital signature services
- Telecommunications infrastructure
- Banking services
- Various enterprise systems

The report indicates a significant volume of technical disruptions across Estonia's digital infrastructure during the first month of 2026. The nature of these incidents includes both technical failures and potential security events.

## 受影響系統

- Digital signature infrastructure
- Telecommunications networks
- Banking systems
- Enterprise IT services
