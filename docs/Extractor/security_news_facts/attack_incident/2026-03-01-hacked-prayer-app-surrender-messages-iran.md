## 摘要

During Israeli military strikes on Iran, attackers compromised the BadeSaba Calendar prayer timing app (5 million Google Play downloads) and used push notifications to send psychological warfare messages urging Iranian military personnel to surrender, with promises of amnesty. No party has claimed responsibility for the hack.

## 事實內容

Following the onset of military strikes on Iran, attackers compromised the "BadeSaba Calendar" prayer timing app — a widely-used Muslim prayer schedule application with over 5 million downloads from Google Play Store — and used its push notification infrastructure to deliver psychological operations messages to Iranian users.

Timeline and message content:
- **09:52 AM Tehran time**: First message sent — "Help Has Arrived"
- Messages sent in quick succession over a 30-minute period
- Content urged Iranian military personnel to surrender their weapons, promised amnesty, and called on army personnel to join "the forces of liberation" and to "defend your brothers"

Key facts:
- **Target app**: BadeSaba Calendar (5 million Google Play downloads)
- **Attack vector**: Compromise of push notification infrastructure
- **Purpose**: Psychological operations (PSYOP) directed at Iranian military personnel
- **Attribution**: No party has claimed responsibility as of reporting
- **Evidence**: Screenshots of messages shared with WIRED Middle East

The incident represents a notable use of civilian mobile applications as a vector for psychological operations during active conflict, using legitimate-seeming notification channels to reach a mass audience inside Iran.

## 受影響系統

- BadeSaba Calendar Android app (Google Play)
- Push notification backend infrastructure for BadeSaba Calendar
