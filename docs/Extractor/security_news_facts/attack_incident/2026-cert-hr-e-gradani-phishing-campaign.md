## 摘要

Fraudulent websites impersonating Croatia's e-Građani system spread via phishing emails to steal citizen credentials and banking data.

## 事實內容

Croatia's National CERT warned about phishing attacks targeting the e-Građani (e-Citizens) government portal. Attackers distributed emails posing as official government notifications ("Službena NIAS Obavijest") containing links to fake websites that visually mimic the legitimate portal at gov.hr. The campaign aims to steal login credentials and mobile banking tokens through social engineering tactics that create urgency and threaten service suspension.

## 攻擊手法

- Phishing emails disguised as official government notifications
- Clone websites mimicking e-Građani portal appearance
- Social engineering using urgency and threats
- Credential harvesting for authentication and banking access

## 受影響系統

- Croatian e-Građani government portal users
- Croatian citizens' banking accounts
