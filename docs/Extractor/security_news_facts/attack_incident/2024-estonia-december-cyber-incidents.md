## Summary
Estonia discovered 735 phishing and scam pages in December 2024, nearly double previous months, with fraudsters exceptionally active during holiday period.

## Description
The Estonian Information System Authority's (RIA) monthly overview shows that 735 phishing and scam pages were discovered in December 2024—nearly twice as many as in previous months. Fraudsters were exceptionally active during the December holiday period, taking advantage of increased online shopping and seasonal distractions.
