## 摘要

AI-generated deepfake videos impersonating Finnish doctors and celebrities are spreading across social media in Finland and Sweden, promoting fake medicines that fraudulently claim to cure cancer and arthritis. The scam exploits trust in known public figures including Dr. Atte Virolainen and singer Tarja Turunen.

## 事實內容

- AI-generated deepfake videos promoting fake medicines are spreading on social media in Finland and Sweden.
- The videos claim that miracle pills can cure serious diseases such as cancer and arthritis.
- Public figures are being impersonated without consent, including Dr. Atte Virolainen ("Lääkäri Atte") and Finnish singer Tarja Turunen.
- The deepfake videos are convincing enough to deceive Finnish social media users.
- This is a social engineering scam using AI-generated synthetic media for financial fraud.
- The scheme is part of a broader trend of deepfake-based fraud targeting citizens in Nordic countries.
- The Finnish public is being warned to be skeptical of medicine advertisements on social media, especially those featuring celebrities.

## 受影響系統

- Finnish and Swedish social media users
- Public trust in digital media
- Online advertising ecosystems (Facebook, Instagram, etc.)
