## 摘要

NCSC-FI's week 38/2025 review reports widespread M365 account breaches in September, large-scale Swedish data breach, supply chain attacks, upcoming EU Cyber Resilience Act vulnerability reporting obligations, and Tinba banking trojan analysis.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre Finland (NCSC-FI) published its weekly review for week 38 of 2025, covering multiple security incidents and regulatory updates.

**Active Threat Campaigns:**

1. Microsoft 365 Account Breaches:
   - Timing: Widespread impact observed in September 2025
   - Scale: Large-scale campaign
   - Target: M365 accounts across organizations
   - Guidance: Protection measures shared by NCSC-FI

2. Sweden Data Breach:
   - Location: Sweden
   - Scale: Large-scale data breach
   - Impact: Significant incident affecting Swedish entities
   - Regional concern for Nordic countries

3. Supply Chain Attacks:
   - Recent supply chain attacks examined
   - Targeting of software/hardware supply chains
   - Sophisticated threat vector

**Regulatory Updates:**

4. EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA):
   - Vulnerability reporting obligation enters force: September 11, 2026
   - Requirements for organizations explained
   - Compliance guidance provided
   - Advance notice for preparation (nearly one year)

**Technical Analysis:**
- Malware review: Tinba banking trojan
- Focus: Banking credential theft malware

**Significance:**
- Persistent M365 targeting (multi-month campaign)
- International scope (Nordic region breaches)
- Supply chain as critical attack vector
- Upcoming regulatory compliance requirements

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft 365 accounts
- Swedish organization systems (data breach)
- Software/hardware supply chain components
- Banking systems (Tinba trojan targets)
