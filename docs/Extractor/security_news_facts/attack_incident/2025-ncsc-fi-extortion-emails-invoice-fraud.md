## 摘要

Aggressive extortion email campaign, update reminders for EOL systems, summer invoice fraud.

## 事實內容

This week's review reports on current threats:
- Aggressive extortion email campaign
- Reminder to install updates during holiday season
- Importance of ensuring devices and software that have reached end of lifecycle are also updated
- Invoice fraud circulating during summer holiday season

Summer holiday season presents opportunities for attackers as staff availability is reduced.

## 受影響系統

- Email systems targeted by extortion campaigns
- End-of-life devices and software requiring updates
- Business invoice processing systems
