## 摘要

Estonia's RIA recorded 1,057 impactful cyber incidents in October 2025, with denial-of-service attacks, technical failures, and widespread telephone and internet fraud as the most common incidents.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) registered 1,057 impactful cyber incidents in October 2025. The most common types of incidents were denial-of-service attacks (teenusetõkestusründed), technical failures (tehnilised tõrked), and large-scale telephone and internet fraud (petused). The high incident count for October 2025 underscores the persistent and growing threat of both targeted cyber attacks and fraud campaigns affecting Estonian digital infrastructure and citizens.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian digital services (DDoS targets)
- Estonian citizens (fraud victims)
- Various organizations
