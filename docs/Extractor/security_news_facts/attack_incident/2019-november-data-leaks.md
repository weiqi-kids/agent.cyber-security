## 摘要

November 2019 featured large volume of data leaks mainly caused by unupdated devices.

## 事實內容

Large volume of data leaks turned November 2019 cyber weather grey and gloomy. The high frequency of leaks is mainly because users do not update their devices. Malware accessed networks of different organizations via email attachments in Finland, though Finland avoided the ransomware plaguing organizations abroad. Scams by email and text messages remained common. The Cybersecurity label published in November provided positive development.

## 受影響系統

- Unupdated devices
- Email systems
- Finnish organizations
