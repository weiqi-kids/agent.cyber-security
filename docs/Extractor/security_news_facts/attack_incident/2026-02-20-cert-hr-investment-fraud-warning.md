## 摘要

CERT.hr warns of increased activity involving malicious investment advertisements and refund scam campaigns on social media in Croatia, where fraudsters impersonate financial recovery firms to defraud victims of online scams.

## 事實內容

Croatia's National CERT (CERT.hr) issued a warning on 20 February 2026 regarding a surge in malicious paid advertisements on social media platforms. The fraudsters present themselves as companies specializing in recovering stolen funds for victims of online scams. Scammers may contact victims proactively via phone calls, emails, or other messaging channels. Victims who engage are at risk of further financial loss. The public is advised to be highly skeptical of unsolicited investment offers and recovery service advertisements.

## 受影響系統

- General public / internet users in Croatia
- Social media platforms (used as attack vector)
