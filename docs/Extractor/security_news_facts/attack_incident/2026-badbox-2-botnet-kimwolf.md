## 摘要

Kimwolf botnet operators "Dort" and "Snow" allegedly compromised Badbox 2.0 control panel (2M+ infected Android TV boxes), hunted by FBI and Google.

## 事實內容

Krebs on Security investigation into Badbox 2.0 botnet operators:

**Kimwolf Botnet:**
- Infected over 2 million devices
- Unique and highly invasive spreading methods
- Primarily infects unofficial Android TV streaming boxes
- Marketed for unlimited (pirated) movie/TV streaming for one-time fee

**Badbox 2.0 Botnet:**
- Vast China-based botnet
- Powered by malicious software pre-installed on Android TV streaming boxes
- Control panel allegedly compromised by Kimwolf operators
- FBI and Google actively hunting operators

**Attribution:**

**Kimwolf Administrators:**
- Nicknames: "Dort" and "Snow"
- Multiple sources confirm identities
- Shared screenshot of logged-in Badbox 2.0 control panel

**Evidence Source:**
- Close former associate of Dort and Snow shared screenshot
- Screenshot shows Kimwolf botmasters logged into Badbox 2.0 panel
- Provides clearer picture of who operates Badbox 2.0

**Previous Coverage:**
- Jan 8, 2026: "Who Benefitted from the Aisuru and Kimwolf Botnets?"
- Jan 2026: "The Kimwolf Botnet is Stalking Your Local Network"

**Infection Vector:**
- Unofficial Android TV boxes with pre-installed malware
- Typically marketed for pirated streaming

## 受影響系統

- Android TV streaming boxes (2M+ devices)
- Badbox 2.0 botnet infrastructure
- Kimwolf-infected devices
