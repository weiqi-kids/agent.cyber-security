## 摘要

NCSC-FI 說明 DoS 攻擊常態，強調大多數不造成嚴重損害，並提醒網站中斷可能來自攻擊或正常維護。

## 事實內容

Denial-of-service (DoS) attacks against organisations' websites and online services are common and occur continuously worldwide. In practice, measures are taken to prepare for and prevent such attacks daily. Most DoS attacks do not cause significant harm. Users may notice DoS attacks as temporary unavailability of banking or healthcare organisation websites. Website downtime may also result from other disturbances or maintenance work unrelated to attacks.

## 受影響系統

- Online services (banks, healthcare organisations, other web services)
