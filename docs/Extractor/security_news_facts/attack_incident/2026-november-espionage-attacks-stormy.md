## Summary

November 2020 saw lightning storms from cyber espionage attacks against the European Medicines Agency and FireEye, while calmer weather came as the Emotet malware warning was removed.

## Details

**Event Type**: Monthly Threat Landscape Summary

**Major Incidents**:
1. **European Medicines Agency (EMA)**: Cyber espionage attack
2. **FireEye**: Cybersecurity company compromised

**Key Points**:
- Cyber espionage attacks attracted significant attention
- Two high-profile targets compromised
- Emotet malware warning removed (positive development)
- Lightning storm conditions due to severity of incidents

## Impact Assessment

**Severity**: High

**Scope**: International

**Notable Targets**:
- European Medicines Agency (healthcare regulatory)
- FireEye (cybersecurity vendor)

**Positive Development**: Emotet threat subsided enough to remove warning
