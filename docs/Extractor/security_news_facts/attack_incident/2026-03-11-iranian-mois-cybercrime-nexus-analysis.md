## 摘要

Check Point Research documents Iran's MOIS using criminal tools, services, and operational models to pursue state objectives, creating a growing state-criminal nexus in cyberspace.

## 事實內容

Check Point Research published an analysis of how Iranian intelligence services, particularly actors linked to the Ministry of Intelligence and Security (MOIS), are increasingly pursuing state objectives through criminal tools, services, and operational models. Key findings:

- **State-criminal nexus**: Iranian MOIS-linked actors use cybercriminal intermediaries and tools, mirroring physical-world tactics where intelligence services operate through deniable criminal proxies
- **Operational blending**: State espionage objectives are pursued using commercially available criminal tooling and infrastructure
- **Deniability**: Criminal intermediaries provide plausible deniability for Iranian state operations
- **Multiple documented cases**: The report examines several cases demonstrating this evolution in Iranian cyber operations
- **Pattern recognition**: The trend shows a growing frequency of this hybrid state-criminal approach in Iranian cyber activity

## 受影響系統

- Organizations targeted by Iranian state-sponsored threat actors
- Government, defense, and critical infrastructure sectors
- Entities in Iran's geopolitical conflict zone
