## Summary

December 2020 saw significant cyber incidents including a data breach at the Finnish Parliament and the SolarWinds backdoor discovery, alongside increased scammer activity. The new EU Cybersecurity Strategy provided a positive development.

## Details

**Event Type**: Monthly Threat Landscape Summary

**Major Incidents**:
1. **Finnish Parliament Data Breach**: Dark clouds in the form of security compromise
2. **SolarWinds Backdoor**: Hackers used backdoor in monitoring platform for espionage and data breaches
3. **Scammer Activity**: Significant activity throughout December

**Key Points**:
- Two major security incidents dominated the month
- SolarWinds compromise enabled espionage operations
- Active scam campaigns
- Network performance remained stable (positive)
- Prepared organizations successfully defended against attacks (positive)

**Positive Developments**:
- New EU Cybersecurity Strategy published
- Good preparation helped organizations defend against attacks

## Impact Assessment

**Severity**: High

**Scope**: International - SolarWinds impact global; Finnish Parliament breach national

**Notable Impacts**:
- Legislative branch compromised (Finnish Parliament)
- Global supply chain attack (SolarWinds)
- Services continued functioning due to good preparation
