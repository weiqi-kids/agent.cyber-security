## 摘要

Fake tech support scams have evolved beyond simple gift card fraud to deploying a modified Havoc C2 framework. The threat actors deliver a customized version of the Havoc command and control framework that leverages indirect syscalls to evade EDR detection and registry-based fallback C2 configurations not found in stock Havoc. This represents a significant escalation in the sophistication of tech support scam operations.

偽造技術支援詐騙已從簡單的禮品卡詐騙演進為部署經修改的 Havoc C2 框架。威脅行為者投遞了 Havoc 命令與控制框架的客製化版本，利用間接系統呼叫 (indirect syscalls) 來規避 EDR 偵測，並使用原版 Havoc 未內建的基於登錄檔的備援 C2 配置。這代表了技術支援詐騙操作在精密度上的顯著升級。
