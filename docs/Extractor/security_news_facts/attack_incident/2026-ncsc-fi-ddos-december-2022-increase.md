# Clear increase in denial-of-service attacks in December 2022

## Summary
NCSC-FI received unusually many reports about denial-of-service attacks in December 2022. Most attacks have not caused noticeable harm.

## Key Details
- **Month**: December 2022
- **Volume**: Unusually high report numbers
- **Impact**: Most attacks caused no noticeable harm
- **Trend**: Clear increase

## Notes
- Quantitative threat increase
- Limited actual impact
- Confidence: high
