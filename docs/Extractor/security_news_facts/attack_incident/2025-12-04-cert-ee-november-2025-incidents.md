## 摘要

Estonia's RIA recorded 851 impactful cyber incidents in November 2025, with notable incidents including denial-of-service attacks, a ransomware attack, and technical failures disrupting services.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) registered 851 impactful cyber incidents in November 2025. The most notable incidents included denial-of-service attacks (teenustõkestusründed), a ransomware attack (lunavararünnak), and technical failures (tehnilised rikked) that disrupted services. This monthly report highlighted a diverse threat landscape affecting Estonian digital services, combining both targeted cyber attacks and technical infrastructure failures that impacted service availability across the country.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian digital services (targeted by DDoS)
- Organizations affected by ransomware
- Various service providers experiencing technical failures
