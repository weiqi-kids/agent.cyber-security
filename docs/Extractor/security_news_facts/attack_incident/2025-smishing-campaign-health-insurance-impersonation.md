## 摘要
SK-CERT warns of active smishing campaign impersonating Všeobecná zdravotná poisťovňa (General Health Insurance Company).

## 事實內容
The National Cyber Security Centre (SK-CERT) has issued an alert regarding an ongoing SMS phishing (smishing) campaign targeting Slovak citizens.

**Campaign Details:**
- **Attack Type:** Smishing (SMS-based phishing)
- **Impersonated Entity:** Všeobecná zdravotná poisťovňa, a.s. (VšZP)
- **Target:** Slovak citizens

**Threat Actor Tactics:**
Attackers send SMS messages claiming to be from the General Health Insurance Company, likely attempting to:
- Steal personal information
- Harvest login credentials
- Conduct financial fraud
- Install malware on mobile devices

**Public Advisory:**
SK-CERT urges the public to exercise increased caution when receiving unsolicited SMS messages claiming to be from health insurance providers or other trusted institutions.

**Recommended Actions:**
1. Do not click links in suspicious SMS messages
2. Verify communications directly with official channels
3. Report suspicious messages to SK-CERT
4. Do not provide personal or financial information via SMS
