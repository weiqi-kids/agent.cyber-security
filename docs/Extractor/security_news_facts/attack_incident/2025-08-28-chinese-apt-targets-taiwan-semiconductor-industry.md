## 摘要

Proofpoint disclosed three Chinese-linked APT groups (UNK_DropPitch, UNK_SparkyCarp, UNK_FistBump) conducted large-scale cyberattacks targeting Taiwan's semiconductor ecosystem from March to June 2025, targeting chip designers, manufacturers, and financial analysts.

## 事實內容

US cybersecurity firm Proofpoint disclosed that three Chinese-linked hacker organizations conducted large-scale cyberattacks against Taiwan's semiconductor industry from March to June 2025.

Threat actors (using Proofpoint's pre-confirmation naming convention with "UNK" prefix):
- **UNK_DropPitch**
- **UNK_SparkyCarp**  
- **UNK_FistBump**

Targeting profile:
- Previously, Chinese hacker operations concentrated on defense, government, and academic sectors
- This campaign's scale and concentration indicates a strategic shift toward Taiwan's semiconductor ecosystem centered on major foundry enterprises
- Targets included: chip designers, semiconductor manufacturers, and financial analysts covering the industry

Strategic significance:
- The goal appears to be obtaining advanced process technology intelligence
- This represents an escalation in targeting economically critical industrial targets rather than traditional government/military targets
- Highlights the intersection of geopolitical semiconductor competition and cyberwarfare

## 受影響系統

- Taiwan semiconductor industry (design, manufacturing, financial analysis sectors)
- Major foundry enterprises and their ecosystems
