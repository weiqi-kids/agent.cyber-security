## Description
The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) registered exactly 1,000 impactful cyber incidents during April 2025, with scam and phishing pages comprising the majority.

**Financial Impact**: Multiple individuals suffered substantial losses. The largest single loss reached €220,000. Several victims lost tens of thousands of euros. One Facebook Marketplace scam resulted in losses exceeding €14,000 per victim.

**Major Incidents**:
- **Scam Pages**: 439 fraudulent websites discovered, predominantly targeting investment schemes
- **Phishing Pages**: 201 sites designed to steal banking credentials
- **DDoS Attacks** (April 3-6): Targeted government websites and services
- **Healthcare Phone Scams**: Impersonated Health Insurance Fund
- **Facebook Marketplace**: Schemes directing buyers to fake courier payment pages
- **Educational Institution Failures**: System failures caused by human error

RIA's CERT-EE division actively blocks fraudulent sites and coordinates with international partners to combat these threats.
