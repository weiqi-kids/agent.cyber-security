## 摘要

TWCERT/CC reports on a self-proclaimed multi-group hacker alliance "Scattered LapSus Hunters" claiming to target an international cloud and network service provider, following an earlier ShinyHunters supply chain incident against the same company.

## 事實內容

TWCERT/CC published a threat intelligence report regarding "Scattered LapSus Hunters," a self-proclaimed alliance of multiple hacker groups that has attracted significant attention on social media and threat intelligence platforms.

Key points:
- **Alliance composition**: Claims to consist of multiple hacker groups acting in coordination
- **Target**: Claims to have successfully breached the database of an unspecified international cloud and network service provider
- **Evidence status**: At time of reporting, the alliance had not provided technical evidence to substantiate the claimed breach; the targeted company confirmed no new vulnerabilities or intrusion signs were detected
- **Context**: The same company was previously confirmed to have had a third-party partner compromised by "ShinyHunters" in August 2025, resulting in partial data leakage — this earlier incident is factual and confirmed
- **Risk assessment**: Even without confirmed breach evidence, the organization represents a high-value target and requires continuous monitoring
- **Attack attribution**: No formal attribution; the alliance's membership and actual capabilities remain unverified

The incident highlights the growing trend of hacker groups forming alliances and leveraging public claims to generate attention, even when technical evidence is absent.

## 受影響系統

- Targeted international cloud and network services provider (unidentified)
- Cloud service consumers of the targeted provider
