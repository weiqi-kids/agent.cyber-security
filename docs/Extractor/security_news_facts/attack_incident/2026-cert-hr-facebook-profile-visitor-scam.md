## 摘要
National CERT warns about a growing scam falsely claiming to reveal who viewed your Facebook profile, despite no such feature existing on the platform.

## 事實內容
The National CERT warns citizens about a growing scam that falsely claims it can reveal who has viewed your Facebook profile, even though such an option does not exist on the platform. The scam most commonly appears in the form of sponsored ads, shared posts, or direct messages that invite users to click a link or install an application to "see who visited their profile." This is a social engineering tactic exploiting user curiosity to distribute malware, steal credentials, or generate fraudulent advertising revenue.

## 受影響系統
- Facebook users
- Potential malware infections on user devices
- Compromised Facebook accounts
