## 摘要

NCSC-FI's week 44/2025 review reports ongoing wave of Microsoft 365 account compromises, exploitation of invoicing services for fraud, EU cybersecurity funding call opening, and Nymaim trojan analysis.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre Finland (NCSC-FI) published its weekly review for week 44 of 2025, highlighting active threat campaigns and funding opportunities.

**Active Threat Campaigns:**

1. Microsoft 365 Account Compromises:
   - Status: Ongoing wave of compromises
   - Target: Microsoft 365 user accounts
   - Scale: Widespread campaign
   - Impact: Unauthorized account access and potential data breach

2. Invoice Fraud via Invoicing Services:
   - Method: Exploitation of invoicing services
   - Objective: Financial fraud
   - Target: Organizations using invoicing platforms
   - Technique: Invoice manipulation or impersonation

**Technical Analysis:**
- Malware review: Nymaim trojan
- Detailed examination of Nymaim operations and capabilities

**Funding and Regulatory Opportunities:**

3. EU Cybersecurity Funding:
   - New funding call opened
   - Opportunity for cybersecurity projects
   - Support for security initiatives

4. EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA):
   - Opportunity to comment on CRA
   - Public consultation period
   - Stakeholder engagement in regulatory development

**Significance:**
- Persistent Microsoft 365 targeting (multi-week campaign)
- Business process exploitation (invoicing fraud)
- Financial fraud through trusted business systems
- Continued regulatory development engagement

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft 365 accounts
- Invoicing service platforms
- Business email and financial systems
