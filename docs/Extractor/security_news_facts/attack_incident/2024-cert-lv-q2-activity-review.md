## 摘要

Q2 2024 saw 388,922 compromised IPs (2-year high), with intrusion attempts up 56% QoQ driven by Russian-backed attacks, though Latvia remains resilient.

## 事實內容

CERT.LV's Q2 2024 activity review shows elevated threat levels with maintained resilience:

**Compromised IP Addresses:**
- 388,922 unique IPs (highest in 2 years)
- 11% increase from Q1 2024
- 16% growth year-over-year

**Threat Severity Distribution:**
- Significant threats with broad impact: 0.02% of categorized incidents
- Medium-impact threats: 0.65%

**Critical Trends:**

1. **Intrusion Attempts Surge:**
   - Highest level in 2 years
   - 56% increase since Q2 2023
   - 200%+ jump compared to previous year
   - Attributed to "Russian-backed cyber attacks" targeting NATO and EU member states

2. **Fraud Escalation:**
   - Compromised IPs under fraud category: +45% from Q1, +70% YoY
   - Monthly fraud losses exceeding €1 million
   - Top fraud types: phishing, phishing, and spear phishing (sic)

3. **DDoS Decline:**
   - Denial-of-service attacks nearly halved compared to Q2 2023
   - Indicates strengthened defenses

**Threat Hunting Success:**
- Foreign intruders identified in 25% of 31 analyzed organizations
- Rapid remediation enabled through data-driven responses

**Resilience Assessment:**
Despite heightened threats, Latvia remains "stable and well protected" with no significant lasting impacts on critical infrastructure or society.

## 受影響系統

- 388,922 compromised IP addresses
- NATO and EU member state networks (intrusion targets)
- Fraud victims (€1M+ monthly losses)
- Organizations targeted by phishing campaigns
