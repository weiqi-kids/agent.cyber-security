## 摘要

LeakNet is a ransomware operation active since late 2024 that encrypts, exfiltrates, and leaks victim data. Unlike traditional ransomware-as-a-service (RaaS) groups, LeakNet operates as a tightly-run group without a wide affiliate program, targeting organizations across multiple sectors.

## 事實內容

LeakNet ransomware operation has been active since late 2024 and combines data encryption with data exfiltration and publication (double extortion). The group distinguishes itself from larger RaaS operations by operating without a broad affiliate network, suggesting a more targeted and controlled approach to victim selection and attacks.

Key points:
- Active since: Late 2024
- Attack model: Double extortion (encrypt + exfiltrate + leak if ransom unpaid)
- Organization: Not a traditional RaaS with wide affiliate network; tightly-run operation
- Targeting: Compromised organizations across multiple sectors
- Data leak site: Used to publish victim data for non-paying victims

Ransomware characteristics:
- Encrypts victim data rendering systems inoperable
- Exfiltrates sensitive data before encryption
- Threatens public leakage of data if ransom is not paid
- More selective victim targeting than mass-market RaaS groups

## 受影響系統

- Organizations across multiple sectors (specific targeting criteria not publicly disclosed)
- Windows environments (typical for ransomware operations)
