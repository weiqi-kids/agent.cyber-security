## 摘要

Law enforcement agencies from eight countries disrupted SocksEscort, a residential proxy service used to compromise hundreds of thousands of routers worldwide for ransomware, ad fraud, account takeovers, and other cybercrime causing tens of millions of dollars in losses.

## 事實內容

- Operation Lightning dismantled SocksEscort, a residential proxy service used by cybercriminals
- FBI and law enforcement from Austria, France, and the Netherlands participated in the operation
- 34 domains and 23 servers across seven countries were seized
- Approximately $3.5 million in cryptocurrency linked to SocksEscort was frozen by the US
- Private sector partners Lumen's Black Lotus Labs and Shadowserver Foundation participated
- SocksEscort was responsible for tens of millions of dollars in losses
- Crimes facilitated included: ransomware, ad fraud, account takeovers, identity theft, business email compromise (BEC), romance scams, and password spraying
- Hundreds of thousands of routers worldwide were compromised to form the proxy network
- FBI Deputy Assistant Director Jason Bilnoski confirmed the operation's scope in an interview

## 受影響系統

- Residential and business routers worldwide (used as proxy nodes)
- Organizations victimized through SocksEscort-enabled attacks
