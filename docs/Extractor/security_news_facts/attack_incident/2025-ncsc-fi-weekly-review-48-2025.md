## 摘要

NCSC-FI's week 48/2025 review reports on digital skimming attacks targeting online shop payment pages, BadBox 2.0 malware in retail devices, Shai Hulud worm targeting developers, and Microsoft 365 account breaches.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre Finland (NCSC-FI) published its weekly review for week 48 of 2025, reporting multiple active threat campaigns.

**Public Event:**
- "Be vigilant on the web! Recognise and prevent digital scams"
- Location: Oodi (Helsinki)
- Date: December 2
- Focus: Public cybersecurity education

**Active Threat Campaigns:**

1. Digital Skimming Attacks:
   - Target: Online shop payment pages
   - Objective: Stealing payment card information
   - Method: JavaScript injection on e-commerce sites
   - Impact: Compromise of customer payment data

2. BadBox 2.0 Malware:
   - Infected devices found in well-known US retail chains
   - Pre-installed malware on devices before sale
   - Supply chain compromise
   - Consumer devices affected

3. Shai Hulud Worm:
   - New malware targeting developer environments
   - Spreading mechanism: Worm functionality
   - Objective: Stealing access credentials
   - Target: Software development tools and platforms
   - Threat to software supply chain

4. Microsoft 365 Account Breaches:
   - Recent account compromises reported
   - Widespread targeting of Microsoft 365 users
   - Credential theft and unauthorized access

**Technical Analysis:**
- Malware review: PromptLock detailed analysis
- In-depth examination of malware operations

**Significance:**
- Multiple concurrent threat campaigns
- Supply chain attacks (BadBox 2.0, Shai Hulud)
- E-commerce payment security concerns
- Enterprise account security (Microsoft 365)

## 受影響系統

- E-commerce payment processing pages
- Consumer devices sold through US retail chains
- Developer environments and tools
- Microsoft 365 accounts and associated services
