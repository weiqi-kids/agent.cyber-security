## 摘要
NUKIB warns of fraudulent phone calls where attackers impersonate NUKIB employees, resulting in over 500,000 CZK stolen from victim.

## 事實內容
The National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NÚKIB) of the Czech Republic has received multiple reports of fraudulent phone calls where perpetrators impersonate NUKIB employees.

**Significant Incident:**
- **Loss Amount:** Over 500,000 CZK (approximately $23,000 USD)
- **Attack Method:** Phone call impersonation
- **False Claims:** Attackers claim to be NUKIB staff

**Important Clarifications:**
NÚKIB emphasizes that:
1. NÚKIB does NOT control financial transfers as part of its activities
2. NÚKIB employees will NEVER request money transfers
3. These calls are entirely fraudulent

**Legal Action:**
NÚKIB has filed a criminal complaint against unknown perpetrators with law enforcement authorities.

**Repeat Warning:**
This advisory represents a reiteration of previous warnings, indicating persistent fraudulent activity targeting Czech citizens using NÚKIB's reputation.

**Social Engineering Tactics:**
Attackers exploit:
- Trust in government cybersecurity authority
- Technical-sounding justifications
- Urgency and authority to pressure victims
- Impersonation of legitimate institution

**Victim Impact:**
The significant financial losses demonstrate the effectiveness of these social engineering attacks and the need for public awareness.

**Recommendations:**
1. Verify caller identity independently
2. Never transfer money based on unsolicited calls
3. Report suspicious calls to NÚKIB and police
4. Be aware that legitimate government agencies do not request money transfers
