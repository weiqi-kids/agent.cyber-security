## Summary
Estonia registered 413 impactful incidents in June 2025 (lowest in six months), but automated monitoring detected record 11,500 malware-infected devices, with over 7,000 (mostly digital set-top boxes) in BADBOX 2.0 botnet.

## Description
According to RIA's monthly overview, June 2025 saw 413 impactful incidents—the lowest figure in six months. However, automated monitoring detected approximately 11,500 devices infected with malware, representing a record number.

**BADBOX 2.0 Botnet**: Over 7,000 infected devices (mostly digital set-top boxes) belonged to the BADBOX 2.0 criminal botnet operating across nearly every country globally. The botnet leverages compromised home devices for cyberattacks.

**Major Incidents**:
- **TEHIK Outages** (June 2): Health systems center experienced outages affecting health information systems and services
- **SMIT System Failures** (June 10): Disrupted emergency response, border control databases, and citizen services
- **Cinema Chain Ransomware**: Multi-country cinema chain (Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania) hit by ransomware that encrypted all virtual machines and backups, causing operational disruption
- **Phishing Surge**: Fraudulent emails impersonating LHV Bank and Omniva postal service attempting to harvest customer data and payment information

## Technical Details
- **Total Incidents**: 413 with impact (6-month low)
- **Malware-Infected Devices**: ~11,500 (record high)
- **BADBOX 2.0 Botnet**: 7,000+ devices (digital set-top boxes)
- **Attack Types**: Botnet infection, ransomware, service disruptions, phishing
- **Affected Services**: TEHIK (health systems), SMIT (emergency response), cinema operations
- **Impersonated Brands**: LHV Bank, Omniva
