## 摘要

70 M365 breach reports in August, significant acceleration post-summer with multiple accounts per organization.

## 事實內容

Major Microsoft 365 account compromise campaign details:
- NCSC-FI received 70 reports related to M365 account breaches in August
- After summer holiday season ended, number of cases rose significantly
- Organizational email accounts being compromised at an accelerating pace
- Numerous organizations exposed to breaches and subsequent phishing emails
- Within a single organization, there may be several to dozens of compromised accounts
- Criminals use stolen credentials to log in to Microsoft 365 services
- Hijacked accounts exploited to send new phishing messages and carry out invoicing fraud

This represents a major, ongoing attack campaign with significant financial and security impacts.

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft 365 accounts (organizational email)
- Multiple accounts per organization (up to dozens)
- Email systems used for phishing propagation
