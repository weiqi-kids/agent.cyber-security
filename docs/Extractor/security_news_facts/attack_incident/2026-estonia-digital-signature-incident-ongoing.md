## Summary
Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) reported ongoing issues with electronic signature creation on January 1, 2026. Updated at 14:49 to confirm incident was resolved.

## Description
The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA), in cooperation with SK ID Solutions, announced problems affecting electronic signature creation services on January 1, 2026. The initial advisory was issued at 12:39, notifying users of ongoing difficulties.

The incident was subsequently resolved at 14:49 on the same day, with an update added to the original notification confirming service restoration. This incident occurred during the New Year's holiday period and lasted approximately 2 hours and 10 minutes.
