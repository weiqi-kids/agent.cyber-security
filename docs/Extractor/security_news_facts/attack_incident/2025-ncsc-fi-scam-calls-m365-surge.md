This week's review covers multiple threats and updates:

- Entry into force of new regulation concerning radio equipment
- Upcoming webinar on software security in autumn
- July edition of Cyber Weather published
- Malware review of 911 S5 malware (botnet infrastructure)

The surge in M365 compromises indicates an escalating campaign.
