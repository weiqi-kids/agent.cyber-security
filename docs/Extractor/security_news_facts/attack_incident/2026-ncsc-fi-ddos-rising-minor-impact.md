# Denial-of-service attacks rising in autumn 2022 with minor impact

## Summary
NCSC-FI received increasing numbers of denial-of-service reports during autumn 2022, with October report rate higher than previous months. Reported attacks have only had minor effects on targets.

## Key Details
- **Period**: Autumn 2022 (October peak)
- **Trend**: Increasing report numbers
- **Impact**: Minor effects only
- **Comparison**: October higher than previous months

## Notes
- Quantitative increase documented
- Impact assessment (minimal)
- Confidence: high
