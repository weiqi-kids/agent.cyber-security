## 摘要

NCSC-FI 第28週週報：犯罪分子利用M365 Direct Send功能發送偽造釣魚郵件，以及消費者聯盟Huijausinfo反詐騙專案介紹。

## 事實內容

- Criminals are exploiting the Direct Send feature in the Microsoft 365 environment to send spoofed phishing messages, bypassing some email authentication controls.
- The Direct Send feature allows sending emails without full SMTP authentication, which threat actors abuse to spoof legitimate organisations.
- NCSC-FI published the June 2025 Cyber Weather report alongside this weekly review.
- NCSC-FI also highlighted the Huijausinfo anti-scam project by the Consumers' Union of Finland, in which NCSC-FI has participated.

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft 365 (Direct Send feature)
- Email recipients (phishing targets)
