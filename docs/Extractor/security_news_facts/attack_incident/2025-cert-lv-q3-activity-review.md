## 摘要

Q3 2025 saw 671 cyber incidents in Latvia (5% decrease from Q2), but compromised devices surged 111% quarter-over-quarter, with critical infrastructure targeted.

## 事實內容

CERT.LV's Q3 2025 activity review reveals a complex threat landscape:

**Incident Volume:**
- 671 cyber incidents recorded (5% decrease from Q2 2025, 2% increase from Q3 2024)
- Overall upward trajectory observed since 2022

**Compromised Devices:**
- 111% increase from Q2 2025
- 36% increase from Q3 2024
- Indicating rapid escalation in successful breaches

**Major Attack Methods:**
- Microsoft SharePoint and WinRAR vulnerabilities actively exploited
- At least one compromise detected in Latvia's critical infrastructure
- Three ransomware cases registered (attackers bypassing defenses)
- Russia-linked NoName057(16) DDoS group activity
- IoT and botnet operations surging

**Social Engineering:**
- Fraudulent Google ads campaigns
- SMS/email phishing impersonating government agencies
- ClickFix mechanisms exploiting user actions

**Affected Sectors:**
- Critical infrastructure (energy, water, heating systems)
- OT systems targeted through supply-chain vulnerabilities

**Strategic Observations:**
Attacks now exploit both technological and human vulnerabilities simultaneously, requiring multi-layered defenses including DNS firewalls, SOC services, and employee training.

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft SharePoint servers
- WinRAR installations
- Critical infrastructure (energy, water, heating)
- OT systems
- IoT devices
