## 事實內容

SANS Internet Storm Center (ISC) published a guest diary analyzing the initial stages of romance scams. Romance scams represent a significant financial threat, with victims losing billions of dollars globally each year to these confidence tricks.

The analysis examines the early-stage tactics used by romance scammers including:
1. Profile creation on dating platforms and social media using stolen or AI-generated photos
2. Initial contact messages designed to build rapport quickly
3. Moving conversations off-platform to private channels (WhatsApp, Telegram, email)
4. Establishing emotional connections through consistent communication
5. Gradual building of trust before introducing financial requests

Understanding the initial stages helps potential victims recognize warning signs before becoming emotionally invested. Red flags include suspiciously attractive profiles, rapid declarations of affection, reluctance to video chat, and requests to move to private communication channels.

Organizations should include romance scam awareness in security training programs, as these scams can lead to insider threats when victims are manipulated into compromising workplace security.

## 受影響系統

- Individual internet users on dating platforms and social media
- Organizations (via insider threat risk)
