## 摘要

Traficom creates Tietovuotoapu.fi website to help Vastaamo data breach victims with consolidated guidance.

## 事實內容

Traficom worked with other public authorities and organizations to create the Tietovuotoapu.fi website in response to the psychotherapy centre Vastaamo data breach. The site provides consolidated information and guidance for breach victims, including steps they should take and contacts for assistance. This represents a coordinated government response to help victims of the major data breach.

## 受影響系統

- Vastaamo psychotherapy centre patients
- Personal and medical data
