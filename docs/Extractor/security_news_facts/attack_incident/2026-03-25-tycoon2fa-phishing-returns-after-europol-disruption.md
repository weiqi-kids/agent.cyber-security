## 摘要

The Tycoon2FA phishing-as-a-service platform has returned to normal operational levels within days of Europol's March 4 disruption, which seized 330 domains.

## 事實內容

The Tycoon2FA phishing-as-a-service (PhaaS) platform was disrupted by Europol and partners on March 4, 2026 in a joint operation:

Disruption details:
- Microsoft led the technical disruption
- 330 domains were seized, including control panels and phishing pages used in attacks

Rapid recovery:
- CrowdStrike observed the cybercrime service returning to normal operational volumes within days
- The Tycoon2FA platform has resumed at previously observed activity levels as of ~March 25, 2026

This demonstrates the resilience of modern PhaaS platforms and their ability to rapidly reconstitute infrastructure following law enforcement disruptions.

- Platform: Tycoon2FA (Phishing-as-a-Service)
- Disruption date: March 4, 2026 (Europol + Microsoft)
- Domains seized: 330
- Recovery time: Within days
- Detection source: CrowdStrike

## 受影響系統

- Organizations targeted by Tycoon2FA-based phishing attacks
- Microsoft 365 and other enterprise services (common Tycoon2FA targets)
