## Summary
Estonian CERT registered 998 impactful cyber incidents in December 2025, including ransomware attacks on healthcare, municipal system compromises, and widespread fraud calls impersonating government institutions.

## Description
The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) reported 998 incidents with impact during December 2025, marking a particularly active month for cyber threats. Notable incidents included:

**December 1**: A primary care center was hit by ransomware, encrypting two servers. Only backups from 2021 were available, compromising patient records, medical histories, and appointment data. CERT-EE partially recovered encrypted files.

**December 5**: A Cloudflare configuration change related to a newly discovered security vulnerability caused widespread service disruptions, making multiple Estonian websites inaccessible domestically and internationally.

**December 9**: Attackers accessed a local government's security and access control servers through a compromised service provider account obtained via phishing. The account was disabled within minutes.

**December 10**: Server overload disrupted Estonia's police identification and case management system (UUSIS), affecting operations nationwide.

**December 12**: System failures at Ridango prevented ticket purchases on elron.ee and pilet.ee railway platforms.

**December 17**: Configuration errors following data center maintenance caused outages affecting eesti.ee, TARA authentication, and X-tee government services.

Throughout December, widespread scam calls impersonated various government institutions during the holiday period.
