## 摘要

Researchers have identified a suspected Russian espionage campaign targeting Ukraine using two previously undocumented malware strains. The operation begins with a phishing email containing a link to a ZIP archive with a malicious document written in Ukrainian, appearing to grant a permit for crossing a Ukrainian border checkpoint. Opening the archive triggers the download of a malware loader dubbed "BadPaw," which installs a second tool called "MeowMeow," a sophisticated backdoor allowing attackers to access infected systems and manipulate locally stored files. The campaign was documented by cybersecurity firm ClearSky.

研究人員發現一場疑似俄羅斯間諜攻擊活動針對烏克蘭，使用兩個先前未被記錄的惡意軟體變種。該行動始於包含連結的網路釣魚電子郵件，指向含有以烏克蘭語撰寫惡意文件的 ZIP 壓縮檔，偽裝為烏克蘭邊境檢查站通行許可。開啟壓縮檔會觸發下載名為「BadPaw」的惡意軟體載入器，隨後安裝名為「MeowMeow」的精密後門程式，使攻擊者能存取受感染系統並操控本機儲存的檔案。此活動由網路安全公司 ClearSky 記錄。
