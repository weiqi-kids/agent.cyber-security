## 摘要

LockBit 5.0 ransomware re-emerged with major upgrades: enhanced obfuscation, anti-analysis, and cross-platform capability targeting Windows, Linux, and VMware ESXi. ETW bypass and 442% vishing growth highlight expanded attack surface.

## 事實內容

LockBit 5.0 ransomware has re-emerged with multiple technical upgrades, demonstrating that the ransomware family remains highly active and influential in the ransomware ecosystem.

Key upgrades based on Trend Micro sample analysis:
- **Enhanced obfuscation**: Windows binary uses heavy obfuscation and packing techniques
- **Anti-analysis techniques**: Including bypassing Event Tracing for Windows (ETW) and other anti-analysis measures
- **Cross-platform capability**: Variants confirmed for Windows, Linux, and VMware ESXi — enabling a single attack to impact hybrid cloud or virtualized environments simultaneously
- **Broader impact**: Organizations with mixed environments face wider operational disruption

Context:
- Threat actor intelligence shows 40% increase in China-linked cloud attacks and 442% growth in voice phishing (vishing) in H2 2024, continuing to accelerate in 2025
- This represents a continued evolution of LockBit capabilities following previous law enforcement disruption attempts

## 受影響系統

- Windows systems
- Linux systems
- VMware ESXi hypervisors
- Hybrid and virtualized enterprise environments
