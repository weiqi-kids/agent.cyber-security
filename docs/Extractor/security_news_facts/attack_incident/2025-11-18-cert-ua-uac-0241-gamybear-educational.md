## 摘要
CERT-UA detected cyberattacks by UAC-0241 against Ukrainian educational institutions in early November 2025, using a compromised Gmail account to distribute emails with subject "Order No. 332" to educational establishments and government bodies, deploying malware GAMYBEAR.

## 事實內容
- **追蹤編號**: CERT-UA#18329
- **威脅行為者**: UAC-0241
- **攻擊時間**: Early November 2025
- **目標**: Ukrainian educational institutions and government bodies
- **攻擊手法**:
  - Email distribution using a compromised Gmail account belonging to a higher educational institution in the same region
  - Subject line: "Order No. 332" (Наказ №332)
  - Malicious email campaign impersonating legitimate institutional communications
- **惡意程式**: GAMYBEAR
- **初始感染向量**: Spear-phishing emails from compromised legitimate email account

## 受影響系統
- Ukrainian educational institutions
- Regional government bodies
- End-user systems receiving malicious emails
