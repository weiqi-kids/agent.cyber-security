## 摘要

Major data breach leaked personal information of tens of thousands from Finnish psychotherapy centre in October 2020.

## 事實內容

Finland experienced a major cybersecurity incident in October 2020 when personal information of tens of thousands of people was leaked online following a data breach at psychotherapy centre Vastaamo. The incident represented a significant privacy violation affecting sensitive patient data. The month also saw continued scams and phishing incidents. The European Cybersecurity Month provided some positive developments by highlighting the importance of information security skills.

## 受影響系統

- Vastaamo psychotherapy centre systems
- Finnish citizens' personal data
