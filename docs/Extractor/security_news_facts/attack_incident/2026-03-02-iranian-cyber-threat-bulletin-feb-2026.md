## 摘要

Canada's Cyber Centre warns of heightened Iranian cyber threats following US/Israel strikes in late February 2026, urging organizations to strengthen defences against state-sponsored retaliation.

## 事實內容

On February 28, 2026, the United States and Israel conducted strikes that prompted a significant Iranian cyber threat response. The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (Cyber Centre) issued a cyber threat bulletin assessing the elevated risk of Iranian state-sponsored or affiliated cyber activity targeting Western organizations, including Canadian entities and critical infrastructure.

Key findings from the bulletin:
- Iranian threat actors have a history of retaliatory cyber operations following geopolitical escalations
- Sectors at elevated risk include government, energy, finance, and telecommunications
- Techniques observed from Iranian actors include spear-phishing, credential harvesting, destructive wiper malware, and DDoS attacks
- Organizations are advised to implement heightened monitoring, patch vulnerable internet-facing systems, and review incident response plans

The Cyber Centre advises Canadian organizations and critical infrastructure operators to treat this period as high-threat and take immediate protective measures.

## 受影響系統

- Government networks
- Critical infrastructure (energy, water, telecommunications)
- Financial sector
- Organizations with Iran-related geopolitical exposure
