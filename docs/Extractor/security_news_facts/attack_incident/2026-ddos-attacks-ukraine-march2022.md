# March 2022 cyber weather: DDoS attacks and Emotet resurgence

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/ajankohtaista/kybersaa_03/2022  
**Published**: 2022-04-21

## Summary

March 2022 saw continued cyber security challenges. Widely reported denial-of-service attacks caused nuisance but no real damage. Emotet malware, dormant for over a year, showed signs of becoming active again, along with ongoing phishing campaigns.

## Key Points

- **DDoS attacks**: Widely reported, caused nuisance but no significant damage
- **Emotet resurgence**: Malware showing signs of reactivation after year-long dormancy
- **Phishing campaigns**: Ongoing campaigns impersonating reliable operators
- **Statistics updated**: First months of 2022 figures published

## Impact

The reemergence of Emotet after extended dormancy demonstrates that botnet infrastructure can be reactivated. DDoS attacks remain a persistent nuisance threat even during geopolitical tensions.
