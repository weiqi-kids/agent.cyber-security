## 摘要

NUKIB warned of elevated risk of unsophisticated DDoS attacks targeting political parties, public administration, and media organizations during EU Parliament elections held 7-9 June 2024 in Czech Republic and other EU member states.

## 事實內容

- NUKIB issued a warning about elevated DDoS risk during EU Parliament elections (7-9 June 2024)
- Primary targets at risk: political parties running in elections, election-related organizations (public administration, media)
- Similar DDoS attacks were already observed in the Netherlands where elections were held earlier
- The DDoS attacks were assessed as low sophistication, primarily aimed at media attention rather than causing long-term damage
- No DDoS attacks had been confirmed in Czech Republic at the time of warning
- The warning was issued as a precautionary measure based on observed patterns in other EU countries

## 受影響系統

- Political party websites in Czech Republic
- Government election infrastructure
- Media organizations covering elections
