## 摘要

Russian state-sponsored APT28 created botnet of compromised Ubiquiti Edge OS routers, primarily targeting devices with default credentials.

## 事實內容

According to information from the US Department of Justice, the Russian state-sponsored actor APT28 (also known as Forest Blizzard, Fancy Bear, or Sofacy Group), operating under Russian military intelligence (GRU), created a network of compromised Ubiquiti Edge OS routers (a botnet). The compromise affects hundreds of these small office/home office (SOHO) routers, with the greatest risk of compromise for devices using default administrator passwords.

## 攻擊手法

- Botnet creation using compromised routers
- Exploitation of default credentials
- State-sponsored cyber operations

## 受影響系統

- Ubiquiti Edge OS routers
- SOHO routers with default credentials
