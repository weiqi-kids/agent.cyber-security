## 摘要

Large-scale phishing campaign using SOFTR no-code platform targets private companies and government institutions to steal email credentials.

## 事實內容

The Slovak National Cyber Security Centre (NCKB) NBÚ warns of a widespread phishing campaign primarily targeting private companies and government institutions. The campaign's goal is to steal victims' email login credentials. It spreads via emails with the theme of password expiration notifications, prompting users to take action. Attackers exploit the SOFTR no-code platform to host phishing infrastructure, lending the pages an air of legitimacy.

- **Attack vector**: Email phishing
- **Lure**: Password expiration notification
- **Platform abused**: SOFTR (no-code platform)
- **Target**: Private companies and government institutions in Slovakia
- **Goal**: Email credential harvesting

## 受影響系統

- Email accounts (corporate and governmental)
- Organizations using email-based authentication
