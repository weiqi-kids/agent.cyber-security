## 摘要

July 2020 featured extensive ransomware campaigns and rapid exploitation of newly disclosed vulnerabilities.

## 事實內容

Several extensive ransomware attacks targeted both large and small enterprises in Finland and other countries during July 2020. A large number of vulnerabilities were discovered, with vulnerabilities being exploited rapidly in targeted attacks. The EU took its first active counter-measures concerning cyber attacks by imposing sanctions against six individuals and three entities.

## 受影響系統

- Finnish enterprises
- International organizations
- Various software with newly disclosed vulnerabilities
