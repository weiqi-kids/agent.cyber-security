## 摘要

Chinese-nexus APT actor Camaro Dragon deployed a PlugX malware variant against Qatari targets within one day of the launch of Operation Epic Fury, leveraging Middle East conflict tensions as lure material.

## 事實內容

Check Point Research observed increased activity by Chinese-nexus APT actors targeting the Middle East, particularly Qatar, following the recent escalation of regional tensions.

Key findings:
- **Threat actor**: Camaro Dragon (Chinese-nexus APT)
- **Timeline**: Attack attempted within one day of the launch of Operation Epic Fury and onset of Middle East escalation
- **Malware**: PlugX variant (also known as Korplug), alongside Cobalt Strike
- **Lure strategy**: Attackers exploited the ongoing Middle East war to craft credible and engaging phishing lures, demonstrating rapid adaptation to breaking news
- **Tools preference**: Simple, accessible tools (PlugX, Cobalt Strike) favoured for rapid initial deployment
- **Region**: Qatar specifically targeted; broader increased activity across the Middle East observed

The campaign demonstrates the actors' ability to operationalize geopolitical events within hours for cyber espionage purposes.

## 受影響系統

- Qatari government and strategic sector targets
- Systems susceptible to PlugX and Cobalt Strike infection (Windows environments)
