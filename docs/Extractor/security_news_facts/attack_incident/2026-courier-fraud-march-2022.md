# Courier fraud on online marketplaces

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/ttn_04032022  
**Published**: 2022-03-18

## Summary

The number of attempted scams reported to NCSC-FI doubled since December 2021. Courier frauds have been common on online marketplaces like Tori.fi and Facebook Marketplace, where criminals try to steal sellers' banking and credit card details.

## Key Points

- **Volume increase**: Scam reports doubled since December 2021
- **Attack vector**: Courier fraud on online marketplaces
- **Platforms affected**: Tori.fi and Facebook Marketplace
- **Target**: Sellers' banking and credit card credentials
- **Tactic**: Fraudulent buyers requesting courier service
- **Advice**: Be alert and question courier service requests

## Impact

The doubling of scam reports indicates escalating online marketplace fraud. Criminals exploit the trust mechanisms of peer-to-peer selling platforms to steal financial credentials.
