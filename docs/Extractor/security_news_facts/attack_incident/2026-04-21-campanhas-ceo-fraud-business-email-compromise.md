## 摘要

CNCS alerts to a growing number of CEO Fraud and BEC incidents in Portugal; in 2025 they represented 18% of social engineering incidents reported to CERT.PT, causing significant financial losses to citizens and organizations.

## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) has issued an alert regarding a growing number of CEO Fraud and Business Email Compromise (BEC) fraud incidents affecting Portuguese citizens and organizations, which can result in significant financial losses.

**Statistics**: In 2025, CEO Fraud/BEC incidents accounted for 18% of all social engineering incidents reported to CERT.PT.

**Attack method**: These campaigns involve sending emails or text messages (SMS or via applications) in which a malicious actor impersonates an entity connected to the target organization (e.g., the CEO or a supplier) and requests actions—typically of a financial nature—from employees. The requests often include authorizing urgent fund transfers to accounts controlled by attackers.

**Characteristics**:
- Impersonation of executive leadership (CEO, CFO) or trusted suppliers
- High urgency and confidentiality pressure to bypass normal verification procedures
- Targeting both private organizations and public sector entities

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese organizations (private sector and public administration)
- Individual employees with financial authorization responsibilities
