## 事實內容

Typosquatting [1] is a popular method using similarly looking names to draw people into malicious content – such as phishing websites or fake software packages. It leverages our “brain optimization” that matches what we see with what we already know – even if it’s not exactly the same. I haven’t installed any shady software, but it’s still a good example how easily our brain could be used against us by utilizing our biases. The story started a few days ago, when I was looking into alerts generated by my automated analysis of packages uploaded to the Python Package Index (PyPI).
