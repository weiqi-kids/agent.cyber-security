## 摘要

NCSC-FI 第26週週報：微軟Entra ID認證方式秋季轉換計畫，以及BadBox 2.0惡意軟體供應鏈威脅警告。

## 事實內容

- Microsoft will begin transitioning to Entra ID authentication methods in autumn 2025; organisations are advised to start preparing well in advance to avoid disruption.
- NCSC-FI highlighted the threat posed by BadBox 2.0 malware, which can be embedded in devices during manufacturing (supply chain compromise).
- BadBox 2.0 represents a significant supply chain security risk, with compromised devices distributed to consumers before any malware infection is detectable.

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft Entra ID (authentication transition)
- Android and smart devices (BadBox 2.0 supply chain malware)
