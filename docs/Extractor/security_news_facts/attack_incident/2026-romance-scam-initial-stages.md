## 摘要

SANS ISC guest diary analyzes romance scam phases: wrong number pretext, compliment hooks, platform migration, and wealth displays before financial requests.

## 事實內容

SANS ISC guest diary by Fares Azhari (ISC intern) analyzes romance scam initial stages:

**Attack Methods (6 Phases):**

1. **Initial Contact:**
   - "Wrong number" pretext
   - Low effort, high reward approach
   - Provides plausible deniability

2. **The Hook:**
   - Over-the-top compliment messages
   - Rapid rapport building
   - Emotional disarmament

3. **Credibility Building:**
   - False identities as foreign business professionals
   - Common claim: Business Analyst in UK
   - Explains grammatical errors while appearing legitimate

4. **Platform Migration:**
   - Request to move to "personal" numbers
   - Claim: business line restrictions
   - Creates operational control and obscures activity

5. **Long-term Grooming:**
   - Multiple operators handling different phases
   - Writing style changes indicate team handoffs
   - Specialized teams for emotional relationship development

6. **Wealth Display:**
   - Share luxury item images
   - Investment and charitable work photos
   - Establish financial credibility before money requests

**Red Flags:**
- Random unsolicited contact (wrong number claim)
- Rapid platform switching requests
- Consistent refusal of live video calls
- Inconsistent grammar patterns (scripted responses)
- Photos with covered faces or generic imagery

**Security Recommendations:**
- Don't share personal information with unknown contacts
- Demand video verification before deepening connections
- Use reverse image searches on all photos
- Never send money, gift cards, or documents to online-only acquaintances

## 受影響系統

- Social media platforms
- Messaging applications
- Individual users (financial fraud victims)
