# August cyber weather: rainy in Finland and the world

**Source:** NCSC-FI Security Now!  
**Link:** https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/ajankohtaista/kybersaa_08/2022  
**Published:** 2022-10-04

## Summary

NCSC-FI assessed cyber threat level in Finland has increased due to more malicious traffic that is more serious in nature. Finnish News Agency STT and Wärtsilä victims of ransomware. Phishing and scam campaigns active.
