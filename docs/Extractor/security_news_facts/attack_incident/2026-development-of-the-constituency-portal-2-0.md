## 事實內容

Dieser Blogpost ist auch auf Deutsch verfügbar. Our partner Intevation GmbH develops the next generation of the "Consituency-Portal", our tool for administration of contact information. The new version follows the current version, which is a further developed „ do-portal “ (originally developed by CERT-EU). Since 2017 we adapted this software to our needs within the scope of CEF 2016-AT-IA-0089 . Over the years our requirements grew significantly and the software architecture was no longer able to stand up to those, also the code maintenance got harder over time. Therefore, we decided for a new version of the software in spring this year: The software design will be majorly enhanced and will be based on Laravel and PHP.
