## 摘要

ThreatFabric researchers discovered new malicious Android applications specifically targeting Czech and Slovak markets for sophisticated money and cryptocurrency theft.

## 事實內容

Security researchers at ThreatFabric uncovered a new malicious application campaign for the Android operating system directly targeting the Czech and Slovak markets.

**Capabilities:**
- Sophisticated theft of money from mobile devices
- Cryptocurrency theft from user wallets
- Multi-stage infection process

**Attack Vector:**
- Series of malicious applications
- User lured through deceptive advertising
- Initial app installation leads to additional malicious downloads
- Social engineering to trick users into installation

**Targeting:**
The malware specifically focuses on Czech and Slovak users, indicating a coordinated campaign against Central European banking and cryptocurrency users.

**Current Status:**
The malicious applications are currently in active distribution targeting the region.

**Risk Level:**
High severity due to direct financial theft capabilities and sophisticated multi-stage attack chain.

## 受影響系統

- Android mobile devices
- Banking applications
- Cryptocurrency wallets
- Czech and Slovak mobile users
