## 摘要

NUKIB received intelligence from partners including Ukraine's CERT-UA about an active phishing campaign by an unknown attacker impersonating Amazon, Microsoft, and government cybersecurity agencies across multiple countries.

## 事實內容

On Wednesday, October 23, NUKIB received information from partners, including Ukrainian CERT-UA, about an active phishing campaign by an unknown attacker. Attacks were confirmed in several partner countries, including dozens of cases in the Czech Republic, with the total scope continuing to grow. The attacker impersonates companies Amazon and Microsoft, as well as government cybersecurity institutions in targeted countries. According to Ukrainian CERT-UA, the targets are government and military institutions, as well as private companies in various sectors. The campaign spans multiple countries simultaneously, indicating a coordinated and organized threat actor.

## 受影響系統

- Government and military institutions
- Private companies in various sectors
- Czech Republic organizations (confirmed dozens of cases)
