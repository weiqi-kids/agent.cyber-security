## 摘要
National CERT warns about phishing pages imitating Croatia's e-Citizens system, distributed via email and SMS as "Official NIAS Notification" to steal personal data and financial information.

## 事實內容
The National CERT of Croatia has issued a warning about the emergence of phishing websites that impersonate the e-Citizens system. Official warnings have also been posted on the genuine e-Citizens system pages. The fraudulent links, which visually mimic the official e-Citizens website, are being distributed through email and SMS messages disguised as "Official NIAS Notification." The goal of this fraud campaign is to steal personal data and achieve financial gain. These pages have been deliberately created to harvest sensitive citizen information through social engineering tactics.

## 受影響系統
- Croatia's e-Citizens (e-Građani) system users
- Citizens receiving fraudulent emails and SMS
