## 摘要
A Russian online activist group operating under the name "Morningstar" claims to be secretly capturing images from Finnish citizens' security cameras, publishing them on their Telegram channel. Finnish NCSC director Pekka Jokinen characterized this as a hybrid influence operation aimed at undermining trust in digital society.

## 事實內容
- **攻擊者**: Russian network activist group using codename "Morningstar"
- **活動**: Publishing images on Telegram that the group claims were captured from citizens' security cameras
- **影響範圍**: Image material from multiple countries; experts consider the material authentic
- **定性**: Hybrid influence operation, one of many methods used to undermine trust in digital society
- **官方回應**: Pekka Jokinen, Director of Finland's National Cyber Security Centre (Traficom), stated that hybrid influence operations continuously seek new methods and their ultimate purpose is to weaken trust in digital society
- **法律限制**:
  - Authorities have limited means to intervene under current legislation
  - Telecom operators are obligated to remove devices from the network that produce harmful traffic or cause interference
  - Capturing home camera footage may constitute unlawful surveillance, requiring a police report for action
  - No entity will proactively remove the device from the network on behalf of the owner
- **建議**: Secure home security cameras with strong passwords and firmware updates; review camera privacy settings

## 受影響系統
- Internet-connected security/surveillance cameras
- IoT devices with default or weak credentials
