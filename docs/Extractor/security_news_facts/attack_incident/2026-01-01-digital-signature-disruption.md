## 摘要

RIA and SK ID Solutions reported problems with electronic signature creation on January 1, incident resolved by 14:49.

## 事實內容

The Information System Authority (RIA) of Estonia, in cooperation with SK ID Solutions, announced problems with creating electronic signatures on January 1, 2026.

Update note in original announcement indicates:
- Initial report: 12:39:19 on January 1, 2026
- Resolution: 14:49 on January 1, 2026

This represents a service disruption affecting Estonia's critical digital signature infrastructure during New Year's Day. The incident was resolved within approximately 2 hours and 10 minutes.

## 受影響系統

- Electronic signature creation services
- SK ID Solutions infrastructure
- Digital authentication systems
