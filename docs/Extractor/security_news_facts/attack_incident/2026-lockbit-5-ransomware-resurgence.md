## 摘要

LockBit ransomware group returned with version 5.0, demonstrating significantly higher technical sophistication and cross-platform attack capabilities.

## 事實內容

The LockBit ransomware group, despite previous law enforcement disruptions, has returned to active operations with LockBit 5.0, showcasing enhanced technical complexity and expanded attack capabilities. The new version demonstrates cross-platform functionality, targeting Windows, Linux, and potentially other operating systems.

**LockBit 5.0 Characteristics**:
- Advanced encryption techniques
- Cross-platform compatibility (Windows, Linux)
- Improved evasion and anti-analysis capabilities
- Enhanced speed and efficiency in file encryption
- Continued use of double extortion tactics (data theft + encryption)

**Threat Assessment**:
- Persistent threat despite law enforcement actions
- Expanded target range due to cross-platform capabilities
- Continued threat to critical infrastructure and enterprises

## 受影響系統

- Windows systems
- Linux servers
- Enterprise networks and critical infrastructure
