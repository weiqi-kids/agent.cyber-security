## 摘要
CNCS Portugal was informed at 11:30 on April 28 of a failure in the national electrical grid affecting some European countries. After contacting responsible entities, CNCS confirmed the event is a technical failure, not a cyberattack.

## 事實內容
On April 28, 2025, a failure in Portugal's national electrical grid occurred, affecting some European countries. CNCS was notified at 11:30 and immediately contacted the incumbent entities responsible for grid operations. Investigations confirmed this was a technical infrastructure failure rather than a malicious cyber incident or cyberattack. CNCS maintained monitoring and coordination posture during the event.

## 受影響系統
- National electrical grid infrastructure
- Portuguese energy distribution systems
- Some European countries affected by the cascading failure
