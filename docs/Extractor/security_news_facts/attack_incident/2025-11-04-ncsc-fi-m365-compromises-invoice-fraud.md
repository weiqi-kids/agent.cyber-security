## 摘要

NCSC-FI reports ongoing wave of M365 account compromises and exploitation of invoicing services in fraud schemes.

## 事實內容

This weekly review highlights active threat campaigns:
- **Ongoing wave of Microsoft 365 account compromises** - sustained attack campaign targeting M365 accounts
- **Exploitation of invoicing services in invoice fraud** - criminals abusing legitimate invoicing platforms
- Opening of new EU cybersecurity funding call
- Opportunity to comment on EU Cyber Resilience Act
- Malware review focusing on Nymaim trojan

The combination of M365 compromises and invoice fraud represents significant business risk.

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft 365 accounts
- Invoicing service platforms
- Business financial systems
