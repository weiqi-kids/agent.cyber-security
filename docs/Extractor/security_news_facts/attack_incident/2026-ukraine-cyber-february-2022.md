# February 2022: Ukraine war impact and medical device vulnerabilities

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/ajankohtaista/kybersaa_02/2022  
**Published**: 2022-03-24

## Summary

The war in Ukraine has not had major impact on Finland's cyber environment, but international tensions inevitably affect the digital world and preparedness requirements. Multiple critical vulnerabilities were discovered in the PCT Axeda platform widely used in medical devices.

## Key Points

- **Geopolitical context**: Ukraine war raises digital environment tensions
- **Finland impact**: No major direct impact yet, but preparedness critical
- **Ongoing threats**: Scams and phishing remain common
- **Medical devices**: Multiple critical vulnerabilities in PCT Axeda platform
- **Healthcare risk**: Widely deployed in medical device infrastructure

## Impact

Geopolitical tensions highlight the need for enhanced cyber preparedness even without direct attacks. Critical vulnerabilities in medical device platforms pose significant healthcare security risks.
