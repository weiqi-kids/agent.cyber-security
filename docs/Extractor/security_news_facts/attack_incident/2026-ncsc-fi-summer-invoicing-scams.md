# Summer peak in company invoicing fraud and scams

## Summary
Fraud and scams concerning company financial transactions become more common during summer holiday season. Organizations should ensure all employees are familiar with payment rules and conventions.

## Key Details
- **Season**: Summer holiday period
- **Threat**: Financial transaction fraud targeting companies
- **Trend**: Seasonal increase
- **Mitigation**: Employee awareness of payment procedures

## Notes
- Seasonal threat pattern
- Business email compromise context
