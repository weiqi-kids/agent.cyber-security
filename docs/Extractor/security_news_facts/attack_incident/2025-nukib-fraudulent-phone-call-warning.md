## 摘要
NUKIB warns of ongoing fraudulent phone call campaign where attackers impersonate trusted institutions to steal money.

## 事實內容
The National Cyber and Information Security Agency (NÚKIB) of the Czech Republic has issued a warning about a new campaign of fraudulent phone calls targeting Czech citizens.

**Attack Methodology:**
- Unknown attacker impersonates employees of trusted institutions
- Claims victim's account is blocked or compromised
- Creates urgency requiring immediate money transfer to "reserve account"
- Pressures victims to transfer funds quickly

**Important Clarifications:**
- This type of cyber fraud does not fall under NÚKIB's direct jurisdiction
- NÚKIB does not handle financial transaction verification
- Legitimate institutions never request money transfers under time pressure

**Recommended Security Practices:**
1. Never send money under time pressure
2. Independently verify caller identity through official channels
3. Hang up and call the institution directly using publicly listed numbers
4. Be skeptical of unsolicited calls requesting financial actions
5. Report suspicious calls to police

**Social Engineering Tactics:**
Attackers exploit:
- Trust in established institutions
- Fear of account loss
- Time pressure to bypass rational decision-making
- Urgency to prevent victim verification

This represents a persistent social engineering threat targeting vulnerable populations through voice phishing (vishing).
