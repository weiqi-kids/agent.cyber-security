## 摘要

Netherlands intelligence agencies AIVD and MIVD warn of a large-scale Russian cyber campaign targeting Signal and WhatsApp accounts of government officials, journalists, and military personnel globally via social engineering rather than encryption attacks.

## 事實內容

The Dutch General Intelligence and Security Service (AIVD) and Military Intelligence and Security Service (MIVD) issued a public warning on Monday, 9 March 2026, about an active large-scale Russian-linked cyber campaign.

Key findings:
- **Target profiles**: Government officials, journalists, military personnel — globally
- **Target platforms**: Signal and WhatsApp (end-to-end encrypted messaging applications)
- **Attack method**: Account takeover via social engineering — tricking users into handing over account access credentials or linking devices, rather than breaking encryption
- **Objective**: Silently read conversations from inside compromised accounts
- **Attribution**: Russian-linked threat actors
- **Scope**: Described as "large-scale" by Dutch intelligence

The campaign does not attempt to defeat end-to-end encryption but instead targets the account itself, bypassing cryptographic protections entirely by obtaining account control at the application level.

## 受影響系統

- Signal messaging accounts
- WhatsApp messaging accounts
- Devices used by government officials, journalists, military personnel
