# May 2022 sees FluBot malware subside bringing needed sunshine

## Summary
May cyber weather was less rainy than earlier spring. FluBot malware subsiding marked important improvement in Finnish cybersecurity. Traficom issued new regulation with more effective tools for tackling scam calls. Reported DDoS attacks in May were smaller than usual.

## Key Details
- **Month**: May 2022
- **Positive**: FluBot malware subsided (major improvement)
- **Regulation**: New Traficom tools against scam calls
- **DDoS**: Lower than usual report numbers
- **Overall**: Improvement from earlier spring

## Notes
- Notable positive developments
- Regulatory effectiveness
- Confidence: high
