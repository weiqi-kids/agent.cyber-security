## 摘要

NCSC-FI 第29週週報：冒充警察的SMS/電郵詐騙活動，國際警方聯合行動摧毀殭屍網路，以及未更新設備淪為犯罪工具警告。

## 事實內容

- Criminals impersonating Finnish police have been sending SMS and email scams attempting to extract online banking credentials from victims.
- An international police operation successfully shut down a botnet used by criminals; the Finnish police participated in this operation.
- Unpatched devices continue to be exploited as easy targets for criminals building new botnets. Device owners may unknowingly become accessories to crimes if their devices are compromised.
- NCSC-FI urged users to install updates promptly to prevent device compromise.

## 受影響系統

- Consumer devices (unpatched, used in botnet construction)
- Online banking platforms (targeted by credential phishing)
