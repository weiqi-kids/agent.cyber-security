## 摘要

August 2020 saw spike in security notifications including Emotet malware warnings as workers returned from summer holidays.

## 事實內容

Cyber weather for August 2020 was rainy with NCSC-FI publishing an Emotet malware warning that remains in effect. As workers returned from summer holidays, there was a spike in information security notifications. Contributing factors included a long-standing increase in scams and phishing attempts targeting organizations.

## 受影響系統

- Finnish organizations
- Email systems vulnerable to Emotet
