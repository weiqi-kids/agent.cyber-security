## 摘要

CERT-FR alert CERTFR-2026-ALE-003: Joint C4 services identified a surge in attack campaigns targeting instant messaging accounts, particularly targeting government and political sectors.

## 事實內容

CERT-FR published alert CERTFR-2026-ALE-003 on 20 March 2026. The joint work of member services of the Cyber Crisis Coordination Center (C4 — Centre de Coordination des Crises Cyber) identified a resurgence of attack campaigns targeting instant messaging accounts.

Key observations:
- These campaigns particularly target sovereign sectors (political figures, government personnel, etc.)
- The alert was published as a joint effort from French C4 coordination center
- Multiple attack campaigns are actively targeting instant messaging platforms

This is classified as an attack_incident as it describes actual ongoing attack campaigns, not just vulnerability disclosures.

- Alert ID: CERTFR-2026-ALE-003
- Type: Active attack campaign
- Target sector: Government, political figures (sovereign sectors)
- Attack vector: Targeting of instant messaging accounts
- Attack method: Phishing/account hijacking of messaging platforms
- Source: French CERT (CERT-FR) / C4 (Centre de Coordination des Crises Cyber)

## 受影響系統

- Instant messaging applications and accounts
- Government/political sector users
