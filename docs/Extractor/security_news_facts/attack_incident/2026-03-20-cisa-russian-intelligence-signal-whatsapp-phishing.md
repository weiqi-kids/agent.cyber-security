## 摘要

CISA and the FBI issued a joint Public Service Announcement warning that Russian intelligence-linked threat actors are actively targeting users of encrypted messaging apps including Signal and WhatsApp through phishing campaigns that have already compromised thousands of accounts.

## 事實內容

CISA and the FBI jointly released a Public Service Announcement (PSA) on March 20, 2026 attributing ongoing phishing campaigns targeting encrypted messaging application accounts to cyber actors associated with Russian Intelligence Services. This marks the first public attribution directly linking these campaigns to Russian intelligence rather than broadly attributed threat groups.

Key points:
- Threat actors: Cyber actors associated with Russian Intelligence Services
- Targeted platforms: Signal, WhatsApp, and other commercial messaging applications
- Attack method: Phishing campaigns targeting account credentials
- Scale: Thousands of accounts already compromised
- First public attribution directly to Russian intelligence services
- Joint warning from CISA and FBI (IC3 PSA260320)

Tactics observed:
- Phishing messages impersonating legitimate contacts or services
- Credential harvesting through fake login pages
- Potential use of compromised accounts for further targeting
- Focus on high-value targets with access to sensitive information

## 受影響系統

- Signal messaging application accounts
- WhatsApp messaging application accounts
- Other commercial encrypted messaging platforms
- Government officials, journalists, activists, and other high-value targets
