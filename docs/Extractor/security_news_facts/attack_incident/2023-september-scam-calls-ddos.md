## 摘要

September marked by record scam calls from fake numbers and DDoS attacks, with data breach notifications decreasing.

## 事實內容

September 2023 cyber weather:
- Colored by scam calls and denial-of-service attacks
- Record number of scam calls from fake numbers reported before Traficom regulation (effective early October)
- Positive note: decrease in data breach, breach attempt, and data leak notifications

Mixed threat landscape with telephony-based attacks prominent before regulatory intervention.

## 受影響系統

- Phone systems (caller ID spoofing)
- Organizations (DDoS targets)
