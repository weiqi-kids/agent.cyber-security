# Severe alert: FluBot malware spreading again

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/ncsc-fi-issued-severe-alert-malware-being-spread-sms  
**Published**: 2021-11-26

## Summary

FluBot malware that caused problems earlier in 2021 is spreading again. NCSC-FI received increasing reports about dozens of message variants used to spread the malware and issued a severe alert.

## Key Points

- **Threat**: FluBot malware resurgence
- **Distribution**: SMS messages with multiple variants
- **Volume**: Increasing numbers of reports
- **Alert level**: Severe
- **Target platform**: Android devices

## Impact

The repeated resurgence of FluBot campaigns throughout 2021 demonstrates the persistent nature of mobile malware threats and the need for user education on SMS-based attacks.
