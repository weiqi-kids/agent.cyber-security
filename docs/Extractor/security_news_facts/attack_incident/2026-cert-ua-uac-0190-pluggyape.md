## 事實內容

CERT-UA (Ukraine Computer Emergency Response Team) issued alert #19092 regarding targeted cyber attacks by threat actor UAC-0190 using PLUGGYAPE malware. The alert title references "Untrustworthy Foundation" ("Неблагонадійний фонд" in Ukrainian), suggesting the attacks may involve fake charitable organizations or foundations as lures.

PLUGGYAPE represents a malware family or variant used in these targeted operations. Given CERT-UA's focus on threats to Ukrainian organizations, these attacks likely target government entities, critical infrastructure, NGOs, or other strategic sectors within Ukraine.

The use of targeted attacks with custom or semi-custom malware indicates a sophisticated threat actor, potentially with state sponsorship or significant resources. UAC-0190 designation follows CERT-UA's naming convention for tracking specific threat actor groups.

Organizations in Ukraine should review CERT-UA's full alert for specific indicators of compromise (IOCs), implement recommended mitigations, enhance monitoring for PLUGGYAPE-related activity, and report any suspicious incidents to CERT-UA.
