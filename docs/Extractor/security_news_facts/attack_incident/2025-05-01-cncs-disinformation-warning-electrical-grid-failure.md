## 摘要
CNCS Portugal warned the public against spreading disinformation circulating in the context of the national electrical grid failure, advising people to verify information from official sources before sharing news online.

## 事實內容
Following the April 28 national electrical grid failure, CNCS Portugal issued a public warning about the circulation of disinformation and false news related to the event. CNCS advised citizens to consult and verify information only from official sources and refrain from sharing unverified information on social media and messaging platforms. The incident highlighted how infrastructure disruptions can be exploited for information operations and online manipulation.

## 受影響系統
- Public information ecosystem
- Social media platforms where disinformation circulated
- General public receiving messaging about the grid failure
