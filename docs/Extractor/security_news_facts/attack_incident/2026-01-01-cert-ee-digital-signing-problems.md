## 摘要

Estonia's RIA and SK ID Solutions report problems with electronic signature creation on January 1, 2026; incident later confirmed resolved at 14:49.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) and SK ID Solutions announced that problems were occurring with electronic signature creation on January 1, 2026. The announcement noted that the incident was affecting the ability to create electronic (digital) signatures. A subsequent update confirmed the incident was resolved at 14:49 on January 1, 2026. Electronic signature services are critical to Estonia's digital society, and disruptions on public holidays can have significant impact on citizens and businesses that rely on these services for legal and administrative purposes.

## 受影響系統

- Electronic signature creation services
- SK ID Solutions signing infrastructure
- Estonian digital signing ecosystem
