## 摘要

April 2019 featured Office 365 phishing and international 'Big Game Hunting' ransomware phenomenon.

## 事實內容

Finnish cyber weather in April 2019 was marred by familiar Office 365 credential phishing and related scams. Internationally, the 'Big Game Hunting' phenomenon, meaning ransomware attacks targeted at large organizations, created significant threats and grey clouds over the cybersecurity landscape.

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft Office 365 accounts
- Large organizations (Big Game Hunting targets)
