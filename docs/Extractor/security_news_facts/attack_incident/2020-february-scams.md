## 摘要

February 2020 featured fraudulent tech support calls, billing scams, and increased Office 365 breaches.

## 事實內容

February 2020 saw dreary and stormy cyber weather conditions. Fraudulent callers posed as technical support and believable billing scams were observed. Office 365 security breaches increased. Multiple platforms and software including Tomcat, JBoss, and Exchange products required vulnerability patches. Domestic communications networks underwent six major disruptions in February.

## 受影響系統

- Finnish organizations and individuals
- Office 365 accounts
- Tomcat, JBoss, Exchange servers
- Communications networks
