# "Countering Russian DRGs": UAC-0239 Cyberattacks Using OrcaC2 Framework and FILEMESS Stealer

## Summary
Ukrainian CERT reports UAC-0239 conducting targeted cyberattacks since mid-September 2025 against Defense Forces and local government in multiple Ukrainian regions, using themes about countering Russian diversionary-reconnaissance groups and deploying OrcaC2 framework with FILEMESS stealer.

## Factual Content
**Incident Details:**
- **Alert ID:** CERT-UA#17691
- **Threat Actor:** UAC-0239
- **Campaign Period:** Starting from second half of September 2025
- **Malware:** OrcaC2 framework and FILEMESS stealer

**Target Organizations:**
- Ukrainian Defense Forces
- Local government agencies in multiple regions of Ukraine

**Social Engineering Theme:**
Attacks use the theme of "countering Russian diversionary-reconnaissance groups" (протидія російським диверсійно-розвідувальним групам), allegedly sent from the Security Service of Ukraine (SBU).

**Attack Vector:**
- **Impersonation:** Security Service of Ukraine (SBU)
- **Theme:** Counter-intelligence against Russian sabotage groups
- **Credibility:** Exploits legitimate national security concerns during active conflict

**Malware Capabilities:**
- **OrcaC2 Framework:** Command and control infrastructure for persistent access
- **FILEMESS:** File stealing malware for data exfiltration

**Geographic Scope:**
Multiple regions of Ukraine affected, indicating broad campaign across the country.

**Target Profile:**
- Military and defense personnel
- Local government officials
- Security-focused positions with access to sensitive information

**Operational Context:**
Attack exploits:
- Ongoing conflict environment
- Legitimate security concerns about Russian infiltration
- Authority of security services
- Urgency of counter-intelligence operations

**Impact:**
- Espionage against defense and government personnel
- Theft of sensitive files and documents
- Persistent network access via OrcaC2
- Intelligence gathering on Ukrainian security operations
- Potential compromise of counter-intelligence efforts

**Notes:** Original content in Ukrainian language (uk); translated to English for analysis; DRG (diversionary-reconnaissance groups) are actual threat in Ukraine conflict context; impersonation of SBU particularly effective social engineering; attack exploits real security concerns
