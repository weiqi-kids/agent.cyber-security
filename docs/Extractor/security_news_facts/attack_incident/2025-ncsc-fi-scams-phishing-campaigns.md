## 摘要

NCSC-FI analyzes most common scams and phishing targeting money and information theft.

## 事實內容

This week's review analyzes current attack trends:
- Most common ways attackers have recently been trying to deceive ordinary citizens
- Main targets are money and information
- Broad range of scams and phishing messages currently active
- Recommendation for everyone to remain alert and examine new messages carefully
- NCSC-FI present at Cyber Security Nordic fair

This represents an overview of the current threat landscape targeting Finnish citizens.

## 受影響系統
