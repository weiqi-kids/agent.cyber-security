## 摘要

NCSC-FI's week 46/2025 review warns of Black Friday themed threats including fake online shops and phishing messages, introduces ClickFix malware distribution technique, and announces software development management webinar.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre Finland (NCSC-FI) published its weekly review for week 46 of 2025, focusing on Black Friday related cyber threats.

**Active Threat Campaigns:**

1. Black Friday Scams:
   - Fake online shops targeting deal seekers
   - Phishing messages themed around special offers
   - Exploitation of seasonal shopping behavior
   - Criminal targeting of discount shoppers

2. ClickFix Technique:
   - New malware distribution method
   - Used to distribute malware to victims
   - Social engineering component
   - Likely involves fake error messages or system prompts

**Technical Analysis:**
- Malware review: M0yv malware operational analysis
- Detailed examination of M0yv functionality

**Events and Resources:**

3. Software Development Management Webinar:
   - Date: November 18, 2025
   - Host: NCSC-FI
   - Participation: Free of charge
   - Registration: Available through provided link
   - Focus: Secure software development practices

4. Monthly Reporting:
   - October Cyber Weather report published
   - Monthly threat landscape assessment available

**Threat Timing:**
- Pre-Black Friday warnings
- Proactive threat intelligence
- Seasonal threat pattern recognition

**Significance:**
- Timely warnings before major shopping event
- Introduction of new malware distribution technique (ClickFix)
- Educational resources for secure development
- Regular monthly threat assessment

## 受影響系統

- E-commerce platforms (fake shop victims)
- Email systems (phishing targets)
- Systems vulnerable to ClickFix technique
- Systems infected with M0yv malware
