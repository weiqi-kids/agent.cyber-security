## 摘要

Since January 2026, CERT-UA has been recording cases of phishing emails impersonating Ukrainian central government agencies and regional administrations, urging recipients to update mobile apps used by civilian and military systems. The campaign deploys SHADOWSNIFF and SALATSTEALER stealers (threat actor UAC-0252).

## 事實內容

- **Threat actor**: UAC-0252
- **Malware families**: SHADOWSNIFF (stealer), SALATSTEALER (stealer)
- **CERT-UA reference**: CERT-UA#20032
- **Campaign active since**: January 2026
- **Attack vector**: Phishing emails impersonating central executive authorities (central government agencies) and regional administrations (obласних адміністрацій) of Ukraine
- **Lure**: Recipients are urged to update mobile applications of widely-used civilian and military systems
- **Target**: Ukrainian government personnel and military personnel using civilian/military mobile applications
- **Goal**: Credential theft / information stealing via stealers delivered via phishing
- The campaign exploits trust in official government communications to distribute malicious payloads

**Original language**: Ukrainian (CERT-UA)

## 受影響系統

- Ukrainian government and military personnel mobile devices
- Mobile applications used by Ukrainian civilian and military systems
- Ukrainian central executive authorities and regional administrations (as impersonated targets)
