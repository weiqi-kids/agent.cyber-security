## 摘要

South Korea's National Tax Service accidentally exposed the mnemonic recovery phrase of a seized cryptocurrency cold wallet in publicly released photos, resulting in the theft of approximately $4.4 million in crypto assets. The wallet was seized during a law enforcement operation targeting 124 high-value tax evaders, which had confiscated digital assets worth approximately 8.1 billion won ($5.6 million).

## 事實內容

- South Korea's National Tax Service conducted law enforcement raids targeting 124 high-value tax evaders, seizing digital assets worth 8.1 billion won (approximately $5.6 million).
- A seized Ledger hardware cold wallet was used to store the confiscated cryptocurrency.
- When the agency publicly announced the success of the operation, they released photos that inadvertently showed a handwritten note with the wallet's mnemonic recovery phrase (seed phrase).
- The seed phrase is the master key that allows restoration of the wallet's assets to any compatible device.
- An attacker discovered the exposed seed phrase in the published images and used it to transfer approximately $4.4 million (reported as $4.8M in headline, with $4.4M directly stolen) in crypto assets to a different wallet.
- The incident highlights critical operational security failures in handling seized digital assets:
  1. Printing/writing seed phrases in proximity to the hardware wallet
  2. Photographing sensitive cryptographic material
  3. Publishing photos without redacting sensitive information
- This represents a case of accidental public disclosure leading to direct financial theft, not a traditional cyberattack.

## 受影響系統

- Ledger hardware cold wallet (seized by South Korea's National Tax Service)
- Confiscated cryptocurrency assets (approximately $4.4M–$4.8M)
- South Korea's National Tax Service operational security procedures
