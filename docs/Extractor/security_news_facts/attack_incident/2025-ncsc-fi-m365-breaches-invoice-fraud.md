## 摘要

Ongoing M365 account compromises, exploitation of invoicing services, and Nymaim trojan analysis.

## 事實內容

This week's review covers multiple active threats:
- Ongoing wave of Microsoft 365 account compromises
- Exploitation of invoicing services in invoice fraud schemes
- Opening of a new EU cybersecurity funding call
- Opportunity to comment on the EU Cyber Resilience Act
- Malware review focusing on the Nymaim trojan

The M365 compromise campaign represents a sustained threat to organizations, while invoice fraud shows criminals exploiting trusted business processes.

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft 365 accounts
- Invoicing services and platforms
- Systems affected by Nymaim trojan
