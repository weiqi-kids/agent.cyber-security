# UAC-0226 Targeted Espionage Activity Against Innovation Centers and Government Using GIFTEDCROOK Stealer

## 摘要

UAC-0226 conducted targeted espionage campaign from February 2025 against military innovation centers, military units, law enforcement, and local governments in eastern Ukraine using GIFTEDCROOK stealer.

## 事實內容

Starting in February 2025, CERT-UA tracked targeted activity conducted for espionage purposes against innovation centers in the military sphere, military formations, law enforcement agencies of Ukraine, and local government bodies, especially those located along the eastern border of the country.

**Attack Details:**
- **Threat Actor:** UAC-0226
- **Malware Used:** GIFTEDCROOK stealer
- **Campaign Type:** Espionage/intelligence gathering
- **Target Profile:**
  - Military innovation centers
  - Military formations
  - Law enforcement agencies
  - Local government bodies in eastern regions
- **Geographic Focus:** Eastern border regions of Ukraine
- **Attack Method:** Targeted cyberattacks (specific delivery method not detailed)

**Timeline:**
- February 2025 onwards: Active espionage campaign
- April 6, 2025: CERT-UA public disclosure

**Context:**
The targeting of military innovation centers is particularly significant, suggesting interest in Ukraine's military technology development. The focus on eastern border regions aligns with active conflict zones and strategic military importance. The use of a stealer-type malware indicates intelligence collection objectives rather than destructive attacks.

## 受影響系統

- Military innovation centers
- Military unit systems
- Law enforcement agency systems
- Local government infrastructure
- Organizations in eastern Ukraine border regions
