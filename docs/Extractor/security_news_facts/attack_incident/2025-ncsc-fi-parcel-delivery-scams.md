Parcel delivery scams surge during Black Friday, M365 breaches continue, Shiz malware analyzed.

## 事實內容

This week's review covers ongoing attack campaigns:
- Parcel delivery scams especially common during seasonal peaks such as Black Friday
- Update on Microsoft 365 account breaches (ongoing campaign)
- "Critical Code" webinar held recently
- EU cyber security funding info sessions announced
- European Commission's CRA (Cyber Resilience Act) stakeholder event information
- Survey opened for NCSA customers on future assessment, approval and accreditation needs
- Malware review of Shiz malware

These represent seasonal social engineering attacks and ongoing credential theft campaigns.

## 受影響系統

- Systems affected by Shiz malware
