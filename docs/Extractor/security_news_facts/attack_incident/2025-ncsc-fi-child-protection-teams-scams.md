## 摘要

Child protection scam texts, Teams call scams, malware disguised as PDF software, Avalanche malware.

## 事實內容

This week's review covers multiple active threats:
- Scam text messages sent in the name of child protection services used to phish for information
- Teams call scams where criminals impersonate IT support attempting to gain access to employees' computers
- Malware disguised as PDF editing software enabling criminals to steal information or take over systems
- Malware review focusing on Avalanche

These attacks combine social engineering with technical exploitation across multiple vectors.

## 受影響系統

- Users targeted by child protection scam SMS
- Microsoft Teams users targeted by IT support impersonation
- Systems infected with fake PDF software malware
- Systems affected by Avalanche malware
