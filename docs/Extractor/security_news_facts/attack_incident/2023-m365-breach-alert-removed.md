## 摘要

M365 account phishing campaign subsided, breach reports declined, alert removed with warning of possible future campaigns.

## 事實內容

Campaign resolution:
- Phishing campaign hijacking email accounts of Finnish organizations subsided
- Number of Microsoft 365 account breach reports declined
- Warning removed due to decreased activity
- Similar large-scale phishing and breach campaigns possible in future
- No current reason for alarm

Resolution of major phishing campaign with forward-looking warning.

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft 365 accounts (campaign subsided)
