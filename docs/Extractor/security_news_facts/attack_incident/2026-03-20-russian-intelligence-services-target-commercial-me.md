## 摘要

CISA and FBI warn of ongoing phishing campaigns by Russian Intelligence Services targeting commercial messaging applications (Signal, WhatsApp, Telegram) to compromise accounts of US government officials, military personnel, political figures, and journalists.

## 事實內容

CISA and the Federal Bureau of Investigation released a Public Service Announcement (PSA) warning about ongoing phishing campaigns by cyber actors associated with Russian Intelligence Services targeting commercial messaging applications (CMAs).

**Target profiles:**
- Current and former U.S. government officials
- Military personnel
- Political figures
- Journalists

**Attack objective:** Bypass encryption to compromise individual user accounts on commercial messaging platforms including Signal, WhatsApp, and Telegram.

**Attack method:** Phishing campaigns designed to trick targets into granting account access or linking attacker-controlled devices to victim accounts, thereby enabling interception of encrypted communications.

CISA and FBI urge users to exercise caution with unsolicited links or QR codes related to messaging application account management, and to enable additional security features on their accounts.

## 受影響系統

- Commercial Messaging Applications (Signal, WhatsApp, Telegram, and similar platforms)
- Individual user accounts of high-value targets
