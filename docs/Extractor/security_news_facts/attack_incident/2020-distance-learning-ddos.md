## 摘要

Attempted DoS attacks targeting distance learning environments increase strain on maintenance.

## 事實內容

NCSC-FI and National Bureau of Investigation published an announcement about attempted obstructions targeted at distance learning environments. While attempts have not caused significant effects, attempted denial-of-service attacks increase the workload of already strained maintenance operations.

## 受影響系統

- Distance learning platforms
- Educational institution IT systems
