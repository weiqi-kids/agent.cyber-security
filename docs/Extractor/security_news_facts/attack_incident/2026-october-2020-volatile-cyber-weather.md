## Summary

October 2020 saw a major data breach leaking tens of thousands of personal records online, ongoing scams and phishing, with positive note from European Cybersecurity Month awareness efforts.

## Details

**Event Type**: Monthly Threat Landscape Summary

**Major Incidents**:
1. **Major Data Breach**: Personal information of tens of thousands leaked online (lightning storm)
2. **Scams and Phishing**: Continued grey skies with more forecasted
3. **European Cybersecurity Month**: Positive sunlight highlighting information security skills

**Key Points**:
- Significant data breach caused lightning storm conditions
- Scams and phishing remained persistent
- October marks European Cybersecurity Month (awareness campaign)
- Volatile conditions with both severe incidents and positive developments

## Impact Assessment

**Severity**: High (Major data breach)

**Scope**: National impact from breach; international awareness campaign

**Notable Impact**: Tens of thousands affected by data leak

**Positive Development**: Increased security awareness through EU campaign
