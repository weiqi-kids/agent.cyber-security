## 事實內容

In May, cyber weather was less rainy than earlier in the spring. The fact that the FluBot malware subsided marked an important improvement in the cyber security of people in Finland. The number of denial-of-service (DoS) attacks reported to the National Cyber Security Centre Finland in May was also smaller than usual.
