## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) registered 768 impactful (mõjuga) incidents in January 2026. The incidents affected:
- Digital signing (digiallkirjastamine)
- Telecommunications and communication services (sideteenused)
- Banking services (pangateenused)
- Other businesses and services

The monthly incident report characterises January as a month where technical incidents put multiple digital services to the test. The report is part of RIA's regular monthly cybersecurity situation overview for Estonia's digital infrastructure.
