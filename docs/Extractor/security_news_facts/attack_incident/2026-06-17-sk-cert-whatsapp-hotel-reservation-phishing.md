## 摘要

Phishing campaign on WhatsApp targets users who have booked accommodation, prompting them to verify reservations to avoid cancellation — a social engineering lure using reservation context data.

## 事實內容

The Slovak National Cyber Security Centre (NCKB) NBÚ warns of a phishing campaign spreading via WhatsApp that specifically targets users who have booked accommodation at hotels or other lodging establishments. Attackers contact victims via private WhatsApp messages, urging them to verify their accommodation reservation to prevent cancellation. The use of reservation-specific context information suggests attackers may have obtained booking details from a third-party source or data breach. The campaign is designed to trick victims into disclosing sensitive personal or payment information.

- **Attack vector**: WhatsApp private messages
- **Lure**: Accommodation reservation verification / cancellation threat
- **Target**: Users with active hotel/accommodation bookings in Slovakia
- **Method**: Social engineering using context-aware phishing

## 受影響系統

- WhatsApp users with accommodation bookings
- Online booking platforms (potential source of leaked reservation data)
