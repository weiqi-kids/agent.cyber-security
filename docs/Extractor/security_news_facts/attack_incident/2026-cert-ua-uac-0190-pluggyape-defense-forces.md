# UAC-0190 Targeted Cyberattacks Against Ukrainian Defense Forces Using PLUGGYAPE

## 摘要

UAC-0190 conducted targeted cyberattacks against Ukrainian Armed Forces from October to December 2025, disguising operations as charitable foundation activities while deploying PLUGGYAPE malware.

## 事實內容

During October-December 2025, the National Cyber Incident Response Team CERT-UA, in cooperation with the Armed Forces of Ukraine Cyber Incident Response Team (military unit A0334), investigated a series of targeted cyberattacks against representatives of the Ukrainian Defense Forces.

**Attack Details:**
- **Threat Actor:** UAC-0190
- **Malware Used:** PLUGGYAPE
- **Attack Vector:** Social engineering disguised as charitable foundation activities
- **Target:** Ukrainian Defense Forces personnel
- **Attack Method:** Targeted spearphishing/social engineering

**Timeline:**
- October-December 2025: Active attack campaign
- January 12, 2026: CERT-UA public disclosure

**Context:**
The attackers impersonated charitable foundations to gain trust of military personnel, a social engineering tactic exploiting the wartime context in Ukraine where legitimate charitable organizations are active.

## 受影響系統

- Ukrainian Armed Forces personnel and systems
- Defense sector organizations
