## 摘要

NCSC-FI's week 34/2025 review warns of pension insurance scams targeting pensioners' banking credentials, reports BadBox malware in cheap smart devices, and announces Traficom funding for cybersecurity improvements under Cybersecurity Act.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre Finland (NCSC-FI) published its weekly review for week 34 of 2025, covering scam campaigns and funding opportunities.

**Active Threat Campaigns:**

1. Pension Insurance Scam:
   - Impersonation: Pension insurance company
   - Method: Threatening messages about stopped pension payments
   - Link destination: Fraudulent website
   - Objective: Steal online banking credentials
   - Target: Pensioners (vulnerable population)
   - Social engineering: Fear of financial loss

2. BadBox Malware:
   - Affected devices: Cheap smart devices
   - Distribution: Pre-installed malware
   - Risk: Supply chain compromise
   - Target: Budget device purchasers
   - Concern: Consumer electronics security

**Funding Opportunities:**

3. Traficom Cybersecurity Funding:
   - Source: Finnish Transport and Communications Agency (Traficom)
   - Purpose: Improve cybersecurity of communications networks and information systems
   - Compliance: Requirements under Cybersecurity Act
   - Target: Organizations subject to Cybersecurity Act
   - Objective: National cybersecurity capacity building

**Significance:**
- Targeting of vulnerable populations (elderly)
- Banking credential theft (financial fraud)
- Supply chain security concerns (BadBox)
- Government support for compliance (funding)
- Regulatory enforcement and support balance

## 受影響系統

- Online banking systems (scam targets)
- Cheap smart devices (BadBox malware)
- Communications networks (funding recipients)
- Information systems subject to Cybersecurity Act
