## 摘要

Proofpoint reveals three China-linked APT groups conducted large-scale cyberattacks against Taiwan's semiconductor industry from March to June 2025, targeting designers, manufacturers, and financial analysts.

## 事實內容

TWCERT/CC reported on findings from US cybersecurity company Proofpoint disclosing a significant Chinese cyber espionage campaign targeting Taiwan's semiconductor sector.

Key findings:
- **Threat actors**: Three China-linked APT groups designated by Proofpoint as UNK_DropPitch, UNK_SparkyCarp, and UNK_F[partial — designation not fully disclosed]
- **Campaign period**: March to June 2025
- **Targets**: Semiconductor industry personnel including:
  - Chip designers
  - Manufacturing and production staff
  - Financial analysts covering the semiconductor sector
- **Strategic context**: Attacks framed within semiconductor geopolitical competition; Taiwan's semiconductor industry (including TSMC and the broader ecosystem) is considered a critical strategic target for China
- **Proofpoint naming convention**: Organizations not yet attributed with high confidence are prefixed "UNK"; confirmed attributions use "TAG" prefix
- **Attack techniques**: Consistent with Chinese APT tradecraft including spear-phishing, credential harvesting, and targeted intrusions aimed at intellectual property theft
- **Sectors affected**: Semiconductor design houses, foundries, and financial institutions covering semiconductor equities

The campaign reflects the strategic importance of semiconductor technology in US-China geopolitical competition and Taiwan's position as a key manufacturing hub.

## 受影響系統

- Taiwan semiconductor design companies
- Semiconductor manufacturing facilities
- Financial institutions with semiconductor sector coverage
- Supply chain partners of semiconductor manufacturers
