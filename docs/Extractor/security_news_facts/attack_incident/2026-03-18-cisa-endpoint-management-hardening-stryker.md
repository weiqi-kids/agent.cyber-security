## 摘要

CISA issued an alert urging organizations to harden endpoint management systems following the March 11, 2026 cyberattack against U.S.-based medical technology firm Stryker Corporation. Attackers targeted Microsoft environments via endpoint management system weaknesses.

## 事實內容

CISA issued guidance for organizations to strengthen their endpoint management systems following the cyberattack against Stryker Corporation on March 11, 2026. The attack targeted Stryker's Microsoft environment, demonstrating that endpoint management systems (such as Microsoft Intune, SCCM, and similar tools) can serve as attack vectors if not properly secured.

Key points:
- Victim: Stryker Corporation (U.S.-based medical technology firm)
- Attack date: March 11, 2026
- Attack vector: Endpoint management systems targeting Microsoft environment
- CISA's response: Issued guidance recommending endpoint management hardening
- Sectors most at risk: Healthcare, critical infrastructure, enterprises using Microsoft management tools

Recommended mitigations include:
- Restricting administrative access to endpoint management systems
- Implementing multi-factor authentication
- Monitoring for abnormal activity in management consoles
- Network segmentation for management infrastructure

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft endpoint management environments (Intune, SCCM)
- Organizations using centralized endpoint management
- Healthcare and medical technology sector
