## 摘要

Threat group ShinyHunters claims to have stolen data from approximately 100 high-profile companies and 400 websites by exploiting Salesforce customer data, with named victims including Salesforce, Snowflake, Okta, LastPass, Sony, and AMD.

## 事實內容

ShinyHunters told The Register on 9 March 2026 that it has stolen data from approximately 100 high-profile companies in a latest campaign targeting Salesforce customer data.

Key claims (unverified at time of publication):
- **Scope**: Data stolen from approximately 400 websites and ~100 high-profile companies
- **Named victims**: Salesforce, Snowflake, Okta, LastPass, Sony, AMD, and others
- **Method**: "Recon and exploitation" ongoing for several months
- **Attack vector**: Salesforce customer data platform used as the common entry point/pivot
- **Threat actor**: ShinyHunters — a well-known financially motivated cybercriminal group with history of large-scale data thefts (previously linked to Snowflake campaign breaches)

Note: These are ShinyHunters' own claims reported to The Register; independent verification of the full victim list had not been completed at the time of publication. ShinyHunters has a history of credible large-scale data theft operations.

## 受影響系統

- Salesforce platform and its customer data
- Organizations using Salesforce: Snowflake, Okta, LastPass, Sony, AMD (claimed)
- Any company relying on Salesforce as a shared infrastructure component
