## 摘要

Large-scale technical support scam calls targeting Finnish organizations and individuals using spoofed numbers.

## 事實內容

Finnish organizations and individuals received large numbers of calls from scammers claiming to represent Microsoft technical support. Callers claim to have detected information security problems on victims' computers, then ask victims to open their computers. Calls appear to come from Finnish numbers but are believed to be from spoofed phone numbers. Scammers speak English or Finnish.

## 受影響系統

- Finnish individuals and organizations
- Windows computers
