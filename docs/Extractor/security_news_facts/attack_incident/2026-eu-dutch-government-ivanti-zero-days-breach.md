## 摘要

Dutch government agencies and European Commission confirm cyberattacks exploiting Ivanti zero-day vulnerabilities, resulting in unauthorized access to work-related data including names, email addresses, and phone numbers.

## 事實內容

Multiple European government entities confirmed cyberattacks following the disclosure of Ivanti zero-day vulnerabilities:

**Dutch Government Breach:**
- Affected entities: Dutch Data Protection Authority and Judicial Council
- Confirmation: Statement to parliament on Friday
- Timeline: Not specified when attacks occurred
- Compromised data: Work-related information including names, business email addresses, and telephone numbers
- Investigation status: Full extent still being investigated
- Officials involved: State Secretary for Interior and State Secretary for Justice and Security

**European Commission Breach:**
- Affected system: Central infrastructure managing mobile devices
- Service targeted: Appears to be Ivanti EPMM (not explicitly confirmed)
- Similarity: Breach resembles the Dutch incident
- Detection: Cyberattack detected by the Commission

**Attack Vector:**
- Related to Ivanti zero-day vulnerabilities
- Both incidents show similar characteristics

## 受影響系統

- Ivanti EPMM (Enterprise Mobile Management)
- Dutch Data Protection Authority systems
- Dutch Judicial Council systems
- European Commission mobile device management infrastructure
