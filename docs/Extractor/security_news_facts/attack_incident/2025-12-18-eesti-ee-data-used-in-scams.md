## 摘要

Approximately 1,500 Estonians fell victim to social engineering, granting scammers access to their eesti.ee accounts through manipulation, not data breach.

## 事實內容

According to assessments by the Police and Border Guard Board (PPA) and Information System Authority (RIA) of Estonia, approximately 1,500 people have been manipulated by scammers into granting access to their eesti.ee accounts.

Critical clarifications:
- NOT a data breach or security vulnerability
- Attack method: Social engineering and manipulation
- Scammers' objective: Use eesti.ee data to build trust and extract money
- Impact: ~1,500 victims

The eesti.ee portal is Estonia's central government services portal containing personal data. Scammers manipulated victims into voluntarily providing access credentials, then used the legitimate data from the portal to appear trustworthy in subsequent fraud attempts.

This represents a sophisticated social engineering attack exploiting trust in government digital services.

## 受影響系統

- eesti.ee government portal accounts (~1,500 compromised)
- Citizens' personal data
- Government digital identity ecosystem
