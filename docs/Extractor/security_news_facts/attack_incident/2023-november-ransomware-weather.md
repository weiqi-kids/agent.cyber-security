## 摘要

November continued rainy cyber weather with multiple ransomware detections, cybercriminals exploiting public vulnerabilities fast.

## 事實內容

November 2023 cyber weather:
- October storms left behind with warning removal
- Late autumn cyber weather continued mostly rainy
- Multiple ransomware detection reports (lightning in malware/vulnerability terms)
- Cybercriminals exploiting newly public vulnerabilities quickly
- Reminder to maintain updates during Christmas time

Sustained ransomware campaign with rapid vulnerability exploitation.

## 受影響系統

- Organizations affected by ransomware
- Systems with newly disclosed vulnerabilities
