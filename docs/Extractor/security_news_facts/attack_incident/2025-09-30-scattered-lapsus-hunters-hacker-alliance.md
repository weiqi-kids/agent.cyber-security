## 摘要
A purported hacker alliance called "Scattered LapSus Hunters" has gained attention, allegedly combining Scattered Spider, Lapsus$ (DEV-0537), and ShinyHunters. While no concrete technical evidence of new breaches has been provided, the potential merger of these groups' social engineering, credential theft, and data exfiltration capabilities represents a significant escalation in threat landscape.

## 事實內容
- **聯盟名稱**: Scattered LapSus Hunters
- **組成成員**:
  1. **Scattered Spider**: Known for advanced social engineering targeting financial, retail, aviation, and supply chain industries; uses vishing, SIM-swapping, MFA fatigue bombing
  2. **Lapsus$ (DEV-0537)**: Targets large tech companies using stolen internal credentials and remote desktop tools; publicly leaks data on Telegram for extortion
  3. **ShinyHunters**: Active since 2020; targets e-commerce, cloud services, and app user data; quickly sells or publicly leaks stolen data on dark web
- **共同特徵**: All three groups heavily rely on social engineering as primary intrusion method
- **相關事件**: In August 2025, ShinyHunters was confirmed to have attacked a third-party vendor of a major international cloud/network service provider, resulting in partial data exposure
- **潛在風險**: If alliance is confirmed, it represents integration of different attack methodologies and resources, potentially escalating cyber threats significantly
- **防護建議**:
  1. Strengthen social engineering awareness training
  2. Comprehensively review supply chain and third-party partner security measures
  3. Establish cross-department collaborative incident response plans
  4. Implement enhanced identity verification and access controls

## 受影響系統
- Enterprise identity and authentication systems
- Third-party vendor and supply chain systems
- Cloud service platforms
