## 事實內容

The Government of Canada released a backgrounder on malicious cyber activity targeting Canadian critical infrastructure. This advisory highlights increasing threats to essential services including energy, telecommunications, financial services, transportation, and government operations.

The document describes sophisticated threat actors conducting reconnaissance, attempting unauthorized access, and in some cases successfully compromising critical infrastructure networks. Attack methods include phishing campaigns, exploitation of known vulnerabilities, supply chain compromises, and advanced persistent threat (APT) techniques.

Critical infrastructure operators face threats from multiple sources including state-sponsored actors seeking intelligence or positioning for potential disruption, cybercriminals seeking financial gain through ransomware, and hacktivists targeting specific sectors or organizations.

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security urges critical infrastructure operators to implement enhanced security measures, report suspicious activity, engage with government threat intelligence sharing programs, and prioritize patching of known vulnerabilities.
