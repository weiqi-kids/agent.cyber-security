## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) registered 680 impactful cyber incidents in September, which is slightly below the average of the past six months. Although the number of incidents showed a slight decline, the month still brought notable events, including disruptions to banking services and continued mass distribution of phishing messages.

- Total incidents: 680 (with impact)
- Notable events: banking service disruptions, mass phishing campaigns
- Trend: slightly below six-month average

## 受影響系統

- Estonian banking services
- General public (phishing targets)
