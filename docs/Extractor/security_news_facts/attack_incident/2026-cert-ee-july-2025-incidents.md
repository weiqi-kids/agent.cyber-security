## Summary
Estonia's CERT-EE recorded 783 incidents with impact in July 2025, nearly half being fraud pages, including Mobiil-ID disruptions, DDoS attacks, and scam calls.

## Facts Content
**Reporting Period**: July 2025
**Total Incidents**: 783 incidents with impact
**Incident Composition**:
- Fraud pages: Nearly 50% of all incidents
- DDoS attacks: Multiple service disruptions
- Mobiil-ID service failures: System disruptions affecting national ID service
- Fraudulent phone calls: Primarily impersonating Health Insurance Fund (Tervisekassa)

**Significant Impacts**:
- Critical infrastructure services affected (Mobiil-ID)
- Ongoing social engineering campaigns targeting citizens
- Continued targeting of Estonian entities with DDoS attacks
