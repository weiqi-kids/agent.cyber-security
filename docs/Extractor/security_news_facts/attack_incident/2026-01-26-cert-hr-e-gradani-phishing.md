## Summary

Croatian National CERT warns of phishing sites imitating the e-Gradani government portal, distributed via email and SMS as fake "Official NIAS Notification" to steal personal data.

## Factual Content

The Croatian National CERT is warning citizens about the appearance of websites imitating the e-Gradani (e-Citizens) government system. Links that visually mimic the official e-Gradani page are being distributed via email and SMS messages disguised as "Official NIAS Notification."

- **Attack Vector**: Phishing emails and SMS messages
- **Target**: Croatian citizens using the e-Gradani government portal
- **Objective**: Theft of personal data and financial gain
- **Method**: Fraudulent websites designed to closely resemble the official government portal

The official e-Gradani website has also posted warnings about this ongoing phishing campaign.
