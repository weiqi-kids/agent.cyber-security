## 摘要

芬蘭 NCSC-FI 12 月收到異常多的 DoS 攻擊報告，大多數攻擊未造成明顯損害。

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre Finland (NCSC-FI) has received an unusually high number of reports about denial-of-service (DoS) attacks in December 2022. The volume is notably higher than typical months. Most of the attacks have not caused any noticeable harm to the targeted organisations, suggesting they were either successfully mitigated or were of limited intensity.

## 受影響系統

- Finnish organisations' online services and websites
