# Alert on FluBot campaign removed

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/ajankohtaista/ttn_02062022  
**Published**: 2022-06-20

## Summary

The FluBot malware epidemic has subsided following an international law enforcement operation. The infrastructure used to spread the malware is currently inactive, though malware spread by text messages may return in the future.

## Key Points

- **FluBot takedown**: International law enforcement operation successfully dismantled FluBot infrastructure
- **Current status**: Infrastructure inactive, no active threat
- **Alert status**: Removed due to subsided threat
- **Future risk**: SMS-based malware campaigns may reappear

## Impact

The successful takedown demonstrates the effectiveness of international law enforcement cooperation against mobile malware. However, the operational playbook remains available to other threat actors, requiring continued vigilance.
