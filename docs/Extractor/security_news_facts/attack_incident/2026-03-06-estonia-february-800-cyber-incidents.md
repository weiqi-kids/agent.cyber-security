## 摘要

Estonia's RIA registered 804 impactful cyber incidents in February 2026, below the six-month average, affecting digital signing, banking services, and multiple websites.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) registered 804 impactful (mõjuga) incidents in February 2026, which is below the six-month average. The incidents affected:
- Digital signing (digiallkirjastamine)
- Banking services (pangateenused)
- Functioning of multiple websites (veebilehtede toimimine)

The monthly incident report provides an overview of the cybersecurity situation in Estonia's digital infrastructure. The figure of 804 represents a lower-than-average month compared to the preceding half-year period.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian digital signing infrastructure
- Banking services
- Various websites and online services
