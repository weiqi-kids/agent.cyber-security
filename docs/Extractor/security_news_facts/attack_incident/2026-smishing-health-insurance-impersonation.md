## 摘要

National Cyber Security Centre warns of active smishing campaign impersonating Všeobecná zdravotná poisťovňa (General Health Insurance Company) to steal credentials and financial information.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre (NCKB) issued an alert regarding an ongoing smishing campaign that fraudulently impersonates Všeobecná zdravotná poisťovňa, a.s. (VšZP), Slovakia's General Health Insurance Company.

**Attack Method:**
- SMS-based phishing (smishing)
- Identity spoofing of legitimate health insurance provider
- Messages appear to originate from VšZP
- Social engineering to create urgency

**Target:**
- Slovak health insurance subscribers
- General public in Slovakia
- Mobile phone users

**Warning:**
SK-CERT urges the public to exercise increased caution when receiving messages claiming to be from health insurance providers. The campaign is part of a broader wave of fraudulent SMS campaigns targeting Slovak citizens.

**Related Campaigns:**
The alert references previous warnings about similar smishing campaigns, indicating a persistent threat pattern.

## 受影響系統

- Slovak mobile phone users
- VšZP insurance subscribers
- SMS messaging infrastructure
