## 摘要

Black Friday sales day attracts both customers and criminals; guidance on safe shopping.

## 事實內容

The global sales day Black Friday attracts customers with major discounts but high customer volumes also attract criminals. NCSC-FI advises keeping a cool head when making purchase decisions and ensuring shopping on safe websites. Scammers exploit the shopping frenzy to target consumers.

## 受影響系統

- Online shoppers
- E-commerce platforms
