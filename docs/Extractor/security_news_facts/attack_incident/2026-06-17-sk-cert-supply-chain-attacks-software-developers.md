## 摘要

SK-CERT warns of globally increasing supply chain attacks specifically targeting software developers, with a sharp rise in infostealer infections on developer devices observed since late 2025.

## 事實內容

The Slovak National Cyber Security Centre SK-CERT warns of an increased frequency of supply chain attacks (supply-chain attacks) specifically targeting software developers or products with a digital element. Since the end of 2025, a global increase in infections of developer devices via infostealer malware has been observed. Attackers use this malware to steal sensitive data from development environments, which can then be leveraged to compromise software products or services downstream. The campaign poses a systemic risk to software supply chains and the integrity of products distributed by affected developers.

- **Attack type**: Supply chain attack targeting software developers
- **Malware type**: Infostealer (data-stealing malware)
- **Attack vector**: Developer device compromise
- **Timeline**: Increased activity since end of 2025
- **Target**: Software developers and organizations producing products with digital components
- **Goal**: Stealing sensitive development credentials and data to enable downstream supply chain compromise

## 受影響系統

- Software developer workstations and environments
- Development tools (IDEs, CI/CD pipelines, code repositories)
- Software products and digital services built by affected developers
