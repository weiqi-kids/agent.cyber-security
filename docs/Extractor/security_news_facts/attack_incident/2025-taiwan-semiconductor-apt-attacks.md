## 摘要
Three China-linked APT groups (UNK_DropPitch, UNK_SparkyCarp, UNK_FistBump) conducted large-scale attacks on Taiwan semiconductor industry March-June 2025.

## 事實內容
Proofpoint disclosed three China-associated hacker groups launched large-scale cyber attacks targeting Taiwan semiconductor industry from March to June 2025. UNK_FistBump hacked Taiwan university student email accounts May-June 2025, impersonating job seekers to send phishing emails to HR departments with Cobalt Strike Beacon and rare Voldemort backdoor. UNK_DropPitch targeted investment bank analysts specializing in Taiwan semiconductor/tech April-May 2025, deploying HealthKick backdoor via malicious DLLs (libcef.dll, pbvm90.dll). UNK_SparkyCarp launched phishing attacks against Taiwan semiconductor company in March 2025, establishing fake C2 domains (accshieldportal[.]com, acesportal[.]com) mimicking internal systems to steal credentials. Attacks demonstrate strategic shift toward Taiwan's semiconductor ecosystem core, potentially aiming to obtain advanced manufacturing technology or disrupt China-targeted technology blockade response strategies.

## 受影響系統
- Taiwan semiconductor manufacturing, packaging, testing companies
- Research institutions and financial investment advisors
- HR departments and engineers
