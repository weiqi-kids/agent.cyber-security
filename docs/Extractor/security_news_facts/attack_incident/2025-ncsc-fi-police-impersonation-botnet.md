## 摘要

Police impersonation scams, international botnet takedown, update importance highlighted.

## 事實內容

This week's review covers multiple topics:
- SMS and email scams sent in the name of police
- Criminals posing as authorities attempting to scam people into revealing online banking credentials
- International police operation successfully shut down a botnet used by criminals
- Importance of updating devices highlighted
- Non-updated devices are easy targets for criminals constructing new botnets
- Device owners may unknowingly become accessories to crime

This represents both active scam campaigns and successful law enforcement action.

## 受影響系統

- Online banking systems (credential theft)
- Non-updated devices vulnerable to botnet recruitment
- Botnet infrastructure (taken down)
