## Summary

Microsoft Defender identifies malicious Chromium browser extensions impersonating AI assistants, harvesting LLM chat histories from ChatGPT and DeepSeek across 900,000 installs and 20,000+ enterprise tenants.

## Factual Content

Microsoft Defender investigated malicious Chromium-based browser extensions that impersonate legitimate AI assistant tools. Key findings:

- Extensions collected full URLs and AI chat content from platforms such as ChatGPT and DeepSeek.
- Approximately 900,000 installs were reported.
- Microsoft Defender telemetry confirmed activity across more than 20,000 enterprise tenants.
- Users frequently interact with AI tools using sensitive inputs.
- Exposed data types include proprietary code, internal workflows, strategic discussions, and other confidential data.
- The extensions harvest LLM chat histories and browsing data.

## Affected Systems

- Chromium-based browsers (Chrome, Edge, etc.)
- ChatGPT platform (data harvesting)
- DeepSeek platform (data harvesting)
- Enterprise environments (20,000+ tenants)
