# Targeted UAC-0226 Espionage Activity Against Innovation Centers and Government Using GIFTEDCROOK Stealer

| Item | Content |
|------|---------|

## Summary
Ukrainian Government CERT-UA reports UAC-0226 conducting targeted espionage operations since February 2025 against military innovation centers, military formations, law enforcement, and local governments especially along eastern border, using GIFTEDCROOK stealer malware.

## Factual Content
**Incident Details:**
- **Alert ID:** CERT-UA#14303
- **Threat Actor:** UAC-0226
- **Campaign Start:** February 2025
- **Malware:** GIFTEDCROOK (stealer)
- **Objective:** Espionage

**Target Profile:**
1. **Military Innovation Centers:** Development hubs for defense technology
2. **Military Formations:** Active military units
3. **Law Enforcement Agencies:** Police and security services
4. **Local Government:** Especially municipalities along eastern border

**Geographic Focus:**
Particular emphasis on organizations located along Ukraine's eastern border - the most strategically sensitive region due to proximity to conflict zones.

**Attack Objectives:**
Espionage operations aimed at:
- Military innovation and R&D intelligence
- Operational military information
- Law enforcement capabilities and investigations
- Local government vulnerabilities in border regions

**Malware Capabilities:**
**GIFTEDCROOK** stealer designed for:
- Data exfiltration
- Credential theft
- Document harvesting
- Sensitive information collection

**Strategic Targeting:**
Focus on military innovation centers indicates interest in:
- Next-generation defense technologies
- Ukrainian military modernization efforts
- Weapons development programs
- Tactical innovation initiatives

**Border Region Significance:**
Eastern border targeting suggests intelligence gathering on:
- Local defense preparedness
- Government coordination in frontline areas
- Law enforcement counter-intelligence
- Civil-military coordination

**Threat Assessment:**
- **Sophistication:** Targeted selection of high-value organizations
- **Persistence:** Multi-month campaign (February onwards)
- **Custom Tooling:** GIFTEDCROOK purpose-built stealer
- **Strategic Focus:** Military innovation and border security

**Impact:**
- Espionage against military R&D
- Compromise of defense innovation projects
- Law enforcement intelligence leakage
- Border security vulnerability mapping
- Operational intelligence collection

**Reporting Authority:**
Government Computer Emergency Response Team of Ukraine (CERT-UA) - official national cyber incident response authority.
