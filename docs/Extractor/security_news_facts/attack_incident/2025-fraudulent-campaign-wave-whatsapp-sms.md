## 摘要
SK-CERT warns of extensive fraud campaign waves spreading through WhatsApp and SMS, including Russian APT activity.

## 事實內容
The National Cyber Security Centre (SK-CERT) has issued a warning about extensive waves of fraudulent campaigns spreading through multiple communication channels.

**Distribution Channels:**
- Email
- WhatsApp messaging
- SMS (smishing)

**Notable Campaigns:**

**1. Star Blizzard (Russian APT):**
- Active since late 2024
- Microsoft attributed to Russian threat actor "Star Blizzard"
- Targets: Professional individuals
- Method: "Exclusive opportunity" to join private professional WhatsApp groups
- Goal: Credential harvesting, intelligence gathering

**2. Widespread Fraud Campaigns:**
Multiple fraudulent campaigns targeting general public through:
- Fake urgent messages
- Impersonation of trusted entities
- Social engineering via messaging apps
- Phishing links in SMS

**Evolution of Threats:**
The campaigns demonstrate evolution beyond traditional email phishing to:
- Messaging application-based attacks
- SMS-based fraud (smishing)
- Leveraging trusted communication channels
- Exploiting social connections

**Risk Assessment:**
The shift to messaging platforms and SMS makes these campaigns more personal and harder to detect, as users tend to trust messages received through these channels more than email.

**Recommendations:**
1. Maintain skepticism of unsolicited messages
2. Verify sender identity through independent channels
3. Do not click links in unexpected messages
4. Report suspicious messages to SK-CERT
5. Enable multi-factor authentication on critical accounts
