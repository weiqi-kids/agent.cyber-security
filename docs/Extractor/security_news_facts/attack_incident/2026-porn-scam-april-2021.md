# Large-scale porn scam/sextortion campaign

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/large-number-porn-scam-messages-being-sent-do-not-believe-scammers-claims  
**Published**: 2021-04-01

## Summary

Scammers actively sent adult entertainment-themed extortion messages (porn scams). Recent campaign included messages translated into good Finnish. The messages are scams and demanded ransom should not be paid under any circumstances.

## Key Points

- **Campaign type**: Sextortion/porn scam
- **Volume**: Large number of messages
- **Language quality**: Well-translated Finnish messages
- **Claims**: False claims about compromising material
- **Demand**: Ransom payment (typically cryptocurrency)
- **Advice**: Do not pay ransom, messages are scams

## Impact

Well-localized sextortion campaigns exploit embarrassment and fear. The use of quality Finnish translation indicates professional criminal operations targeting Finnish victims specifically.
