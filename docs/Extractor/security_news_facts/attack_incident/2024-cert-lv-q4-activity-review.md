## 摘要

Q4 2024 saw record-high compromised IP addresses in Latvia, with incidents up 3% from Q3 and 25% year-over-year, driven by pro-Russian actors.

## 事實內容

CERT.LV's Q4 2024 activity review reveals significant escalation in cyber threats:

**Compromised IP Addresses:**
- All-time high number of unique IP addresses compromised in Latvia
- Indicating significant increase in threat intensity

**Incident Reports:**
- 3% increase from Q3 2024
- 25% increase from Q4 2023

**Threat Actors:**
- Primary threats from pro-Russian actors
- Emerging interest from cyber-attackers possibly linked to China (new development)
- Minor threats from Belarus and North Korea-linked groups

**Attack Methods:**
- DDoS attacks
- Vulnerability exploitation
- Exposed internet-connected equipment
- Sophisticated social engineering and phishing campaigns

**Impact Assessment:**
- Cyber attacks "have generally not had a significant impact on public safety and essential and critical services"
- Reflects effective defensive measures already in place

**Strategic Implications:**
Latvia demonstrates strong cyber resilience, but the report emphasizes ongoing needs for:
- Investment in defense solutions
- User awareness campaigns
- Cyber hygiene practices
- Given the rapidly evolving threat landscape

## 受影響系統

- Internet-connected equipment (exposed vulnerabilities)
- Networks targeted by DDoS
- Users targeted by phishing campaigns
