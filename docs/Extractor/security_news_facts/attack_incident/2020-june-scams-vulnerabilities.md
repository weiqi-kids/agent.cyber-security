## 摘要

June 2020 saw increased phone/email scams during summer holidays and phishing email chains targeting security breaches.

## 事實內容

June 2020 cyber weather was rainy with stormy spells. Phone and email scams showed predictable increase during summer holiday season. Phishing email chains designed to carry out security breaches were observed. Several critical vulnerabilities were also discovered. NCSC-FI's cyber weather report now uses arrow symbols to illustrate development of cyber threats.

## 受影響系統

- Finnish organizations
- Email systems
- Software with critical vulnerabilities
