## Summary
Estonian Information System Authority warns voters before elections to update device software and beware of fraud schemes where scammers impersonate election commission workers to steal PIN codes.

## Description
Ahead of elections, the Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) calls on citizens to update their device software and remain vigilant to ensure secure e-voting. Fraud schemes are circulating where scammers impersonate election commission workers and attempt to extract PIN codes from voters.

RIA emphasizes the importance of cyber hygiene during the election period, including keeping software up to date and never sharing PIN codes with anyone claiming to be from election authorities. Legitimate election officials will never request PIN codes.

## Technical Details
- **Threat**: Social engineering fraud targeting voters
- **Attack Vector**: Impersonation of election commission workers
- **Target**: Voter PIN codes
- **Recommendations**: Update device software, never share PIN codes
- **Context**: Election period security advisory
