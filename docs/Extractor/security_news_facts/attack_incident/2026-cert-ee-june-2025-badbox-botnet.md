## Summary
Estonia recorded 413 incidents in June 2025, with automated monitoring detecting 11,500 malware-infected devices, over 7,000 belonging to BADBOX 2.0 botnet.

## Facts Content
**Reporting Period**: June 2025
**Total Incidents**: 413 incidents with impact (lowest in past 6 months)
**Malware Detections**: 11,500 infected devices

**BADBOX 2.0 Botnet**:
- Infected devices: Over 7,000 devices
- Primary device type: Digital TV boxes (digiboksid)
- Botnet classification: BADBOX 2.0
- Detection method: Automated monitoring system

**Incident Statistics**:
- Lowest monthly incident count in 6 months
- Significant increase in automated malware detections
- Large-scale IoT device compromise

**Source Authority**: Estonian Information System Authority (RIA)

**Notes**:
