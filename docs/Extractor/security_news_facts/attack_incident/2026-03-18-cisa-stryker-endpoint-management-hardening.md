## 摘要

CISA urges endpoint management hardening following a March 11, 2026 cyberattack against US medical technology firm Stryker Corporation that affected their Microsoft environment.

## 事實內容

CISA is aware of malicious cyber activity targeting endpoint management systems of U.S. organizations, based on a March 11, 2026 cyberattack against U.S.-based medical technology firm Stryker Corporation, which affected their Microsoft environment. CISA issued an alert urging organizations to harden their endpoint management systems to defend against similar attacks. The incident involved targeting of endpoint management infrastructure — systems used to manage, monitor, and secure networked devices across an organization. The attack on Stryker, a major medical device manufacturer, underscores the risk to healthcare-adjacent technology companies.
