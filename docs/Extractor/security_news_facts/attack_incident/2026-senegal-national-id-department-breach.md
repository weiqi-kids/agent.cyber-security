## 摘要

Senegal's Directorate of File Automation (DAF), managing national ID cards, passports, and biometric data for 19.5 million residents, suspended operations after cyberattack. Authorities claim data integrity remains intact despite ransomware claims.

## 事實內容

A cybersecurity incident affecting the government of Senegal forced the closure of a critical office managing sensitive citizen information.

**Affected Agency:**
- Name: Directorate of File Automation (DAF)
- Responsibilities:
  * National ID cards management
  * Passport management
  * Biometric data management
  * Other sensitive citizen information
- Population affected: 19.5 million Senegalese residents

**Incident Response:**

1. Official Actions:
   - Notice sent last week to residents
   - Temporary suspension of DAF operations
   - Systems restoration in progress

2. Official Statements:
   - Senior police official involved in response
   - Claim: Integrity of citizens' personal data "remains intact"
   - DAF did not respond to requests for comment

**Threat Actor:**
- Claims: Ransomware group claims responsibility
- Nature: Ransomware attack implied by claims

**Concerns:**
- Sensitivity of affected data (national IDs, passports, biometrics)
- Large population impact (19.5 million residents)
- Operational disruption (office temporarily closed)
- Discrepancy between official statements and threat actor claims

**Investigation Status:**
- Systems restoration ongoing
- Full scope of breach not publicly confirmed
- Response coordinated by senior police officials

## 受影響系統

- Directorate of File Automation (DAF) systems
- National ID card management systems
- Passport management systems
- Biometric data storage systems
