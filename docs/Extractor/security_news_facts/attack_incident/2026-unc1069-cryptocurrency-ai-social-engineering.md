## 摘要

North Korean threat actor UNC1069 conducted sophisticated intrusion against FinTech entity using seven unique malware families, AI-generated video deception, and social engineering via compromised Telegram account and fake Zoom meeting.

## 事實內容

Mandiant investigated an intrusion targeting the cryptocurrency and decentralized finance (DeFi) sector attributed to UNC1069, a North Korean financially motivated threat actor.

**Threat Actor Profile:**
- Identity: UNC1069
- Attribution: North Korea
- Motivation: Financial gain
- Active since: At least 2018
- Evolution: Continues to evolve tradecraft

**Attack Campaign Details:**

1. Social Engineering Scheme:
   - Compromised Telegram account used
   - Fake Zoom meeting as lure
   - ClickFix infection vector employed
   - AI-generated video used to deceive victim
   - Tailored approach for cryptocurrency sector

2. Malware Deployment:
   - Total: Seven unique malware families deployed
   - New tooling designed to capture host and victim data:
     * SILENCELIFT
     * DEEPBREATH
     * CHROMEPUSH

3. Target Sector:
   - Cryptocurrency vertical
   - Decentralized finance (DeFi)
   - FinTech entities in crypto space

**Technical Capabilities:**
- Custom malware development
- AI-enhanced social engineering
- Multi-stage infection process
- Data exfiltration tools
- Browser-focused data capture (CHROMEPUSH suggests Chrome targeting)

**Significance:**
- Evolution of North Korean cyber tradecraft
- Integration of AI in social engineering
- Sophisticated multi-malware deployment
- Continued targeting of cryptocurrency sector for financial gain

## 受影響系統

- FinTech entities in cryptocurrency/DeFi sector
- Telegram accounts (compromised for social engineering)
- Zoom meeting participants (fake meetings)
- Chrome browsers (CHROMEPUSH malware)
