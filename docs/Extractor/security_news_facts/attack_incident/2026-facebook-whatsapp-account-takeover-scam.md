## 摘要
National CERT warns about a malicious campaign spreading via Facebook groups and pages aimed at unauthorized takeover of WhatsApp accounts, using fake children's competition voting requests.

## 事實內容
The National CERT warns citizens about a malicious campaign spreading through Facebook groups and pages, with the goal of unauthorized takeover of WhatsApp user accounts. Users receive messages or posts via Facebook requesting votes in an alleged children's competition, typically with messages like: "Hi! Could you please vote for Maria in this competition?" The scam exploits social engineering to trick users into providing WhatsApp verification codes or other authentication information, leading to account compromise. This represents a cross-platform attack using Facebook as the distribution vector to compromise WhatsApp accounts.

## 受影響系統
- Facebook users
- WhatsApp accounts
- Users in social groups and pages
