## 摘要

European Commission's central mobile device management infrastructure was cyber-attacked on January 30, potentially exposing staff names and mobile numbers. The incident was contained within 9 hours with no mobile device compromise detected.

## 事實內容

On January 30, 2026, the European Commission's central infrastructure managing mobile devices identified traces of a cyber-attack. The attack may have resulted in unauthorized access to staff names and mobile numbers of some Commission staff members.

**Response Timeline:**
- Incident detected: January 30, 2026
- Containment and system cleaning: Within 9 hours
- Result: System cleaned, no compromise of mobile devices detected

**Scope of Impact:**
- Potentially affected data: Staff names and mobile numbers
- Systems compromised: Central mobile infrastructure
- Mobile devices: No compromise detected
