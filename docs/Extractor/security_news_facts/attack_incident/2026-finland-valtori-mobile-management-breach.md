## 摘要

Finnish National Bureau of Investigation (KRP) is investigating a suspected data breach at Valtori's mobile device management service. Unknown parties allegedly gained unauthorized access on January 29, compromising configuration and user data.

## 事實內容

The Finnish National Bureau of Investigation (Keskusrikospoliisi, KRP) is investigating a suspected data breach targeting Valtori's mobile device management service.

**Incident Details:**
- Date of suspected intrusion: January 29, 2026
- Target: Valtori's mobile device management service
- Threat actor: Unknown party
- Access: Unauthorized entry into the service
- Compromised data: Configuration and user data

**Investigation Status:**
- Current charge: Aggravated computer break-in (törkeä tietomurto)
- Note: Criminal charges may be refined during preliminary investigation
- Investigating authority: Finnish National Bureau of Investigation (KRP)

**Context:**
- Valtori is a Finnish government ICT service provider
- The breach affects mobile device management infrastructure
- This incident appears part of a wider pattern of attacks on mobile management systems in Europe

## 受影響系統

- Valtori mobile device management service
