## Summary

NCSC-FI removed the yellow warning for Emotet malware as its spread has slowed down, though caution with email attachments remains advised.

## Details

**Event Type**: Threat Level Reduction

**Malware**: Emotet

**Previous Threat Characteristics**:
- Spread through email attachments in name of Finnish organizations
- Designed to steal information from targeted organizations
- Capable of network infiltration
- Could initiate ransomware attacks

**Current Status**:
- Spread of Emotet has slowed significantly
- Warning no longer necessary
- Caution still advised for email attachments

## Impact Assessment

**Severity**: Low (Threat Reduction)

**Scope**: National (Finland) - All Organizations

**Status Change**: Yellow warning removed due to decreased activity

**Continued Precautions**: Email attachment handling vigilance maintained

## Notes

Source is in English from NCSC-FI. This represents a positive development with threat level reduction, though baseline caution remains recommended.
