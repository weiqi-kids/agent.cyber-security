RIA registered 998 impactful incidents in December, including several significant incidents and mass scam calls impersonating various authorities.

## 事實內容

The Information System Authority (RIA) of Estonia registered 998 incidents with impact during December 2025.

Key incident types:
- Multiple significant security incidents
- Mass scam phone calls impersonating government agencies and authorities
- Various technical disruptions during the holiday season

The title "No Christmas Peace in Cyberspace" reflects that cyber threats and scam activities continued unabated during the traditionally quiet holiday period.

## 受影響系統

- Government digital services
- Citizens targeted by scam calls
- Various digital infrastructure
