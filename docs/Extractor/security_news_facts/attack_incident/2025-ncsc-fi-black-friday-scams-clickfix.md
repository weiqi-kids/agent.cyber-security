## 摘要

Black Friday scams include fake online shops and ClickFix malware distribution technique.

## 事實內容

This week's review highlights seasonal attack campaigns:
- Black Friday deals attracting criminals who create fake online shops
- Phishing messages themed around current special offers
- ClickFix technique being used to distribute malware (notable attack method)
- Software development management webinar scheduled for November 18 (free participation)
- October Cyber Weather report published
- Malware review of M0yv malware

ClickFix is a notable social engineering technique that tricks users into executing malicious commands.

## 受影響系統

- Online shopping platforms
- End-user devices targeted by fake shopping sites
- Systems affected by ClickFix and M0yv malware
