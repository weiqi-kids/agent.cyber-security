## 摘要

NCSC-FI 第23週週報：詐騙網站冒用ETA/ESTA旅行申請表單，以及軟體更新與強密碼重要性提醒。

## 事實內容

- Scam websites are exploiting the ETA (Electronic Travel Authorisation) and ESTA (Electronic System for Travel Authorization) travel form branding to deceive travellers into submitting personal and payment information to fraudulent sites.
- Victims may pay fake fees and have their personal data (passport details, payment information) stolen.
- NCSC-FI reminded readers about the importance of applying updates promptly and using strong passwords to reduce exposure to cyber threats.

## 受影響系統

- Travel application portals (ETA/ESTA brand spoofing)
- General users (targeted by fake travel form websites)
