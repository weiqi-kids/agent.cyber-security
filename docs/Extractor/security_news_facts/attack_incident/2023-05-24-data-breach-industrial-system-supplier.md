## 摘要

工業系統供應商遭資料外洩，其客戶組織需迅速採取行動應對潛在供應鏈風險。

## 事實內容

A data breach has occurred against an industrial system supplier. Preparedness efforts in organisations must also cover incidents affecting their suppliers. At worst, a key supplier may be hit by a cyber attack, which will also require its client organisations to take quick action. This incident highlights the importance of supply chain security and the need for organisations to have incident response plans that cover third-party breaches.

## 受影響系統

- Industrial control systems
- Supply chain organisations and their clients
