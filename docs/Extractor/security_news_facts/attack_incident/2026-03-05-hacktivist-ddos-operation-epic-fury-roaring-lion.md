## 摘要

Radware reports on retaliatory hacktivist DDoS activity in the Middle East following Operation Epic Fury/Roaring Lion. Two groups, Keymous+ and DieNet, drive nearly 70% of all attack activity between February 28 and March 2. Hacktivists prioritized pillars of national infrastructure, directing 53% of all attacks toward government institutions. Despite regional tensions, the hacktivist offensive concentrates on Kuwait, Israel, and Jordan, collectively representing more than 76% of all attack claims. On March 2, pro-Russian group NoName057(16) entered the pro-Iranian/pro-Palestinian campaign, signaling a broadening of the hacktivist threat.

Radware 報導中東地區在「史詩怒火/咆哮雄獅」行動後的報復性駭客主義 DDoS 活動。兩個團體 Keymous+ 和 DieNet 在 2 月 28 日至 3 月 2 日期間驅動了近 70% 的所有攻擊活動。駭客主義者優先攻擊國家基礎設施支柱，將 53% 的攻擊導向政府機構。儘管區域緊張，駭客主義攻勢集中在科威特、以色列和約旦，合計佔所有攻擊聲明的 76% 以上。3 月 2 日，親俄團體 NoName057(16) 加入親伊朗/親巴勒斯坦陣營，標誌著駭客主義威脅的擴大。
