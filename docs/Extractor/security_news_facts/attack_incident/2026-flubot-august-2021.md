# August 2021 cyber weather: FluBot subsides, phishing continues

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/ajankohtaista/kybersaa_elokuu_2021  
**Published**: 2021-09-21

## Summary

August saw full range of cyber weather. FluBot alert removed after no new reports. Aggressive banking credential phishing continues causing considerable financial losses. Vastaamo psychotherapy centre data breach from a year ago still causing trouble.

## Key Points

- **FluBot**: Alert removed, no new reports
- **Phishing**: Aggressive campaigns targeting banking credentials
- **Financial impact**: Considerable losses to private persons
- **Vastaamo aftermath**: Data breach from year ago continues causing issues
- **Overall**: Mixed weather with improvements and ongoing challenges

## Impact

While some threats subsided, financial losses from phishing demonstrate the real-world impact. The lingering effects of Vastaamo breach highlight long-term consequences of data breaches.
