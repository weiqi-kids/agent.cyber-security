# WARNING: Scam Campaign Impersonating Croatian Police with False Criminal Charges

| **Category** | attack_incident |

## Summary
Croatian National CERT reports increased scam messages falsely impersonating police and law enforcement agencies, threatening recipients with fabricated criminal charges including child pornography and cybercrime to extort victims.

## Factual Content
The National CERT is recording an increased number of reports of scam messages where attackers falsely present themselves as police authorities and mention other bodies and institutions such as:
- Ministry of Internal Affairs
- Criminal Police
- International organizations like Europol

**Attack Methodology:**
Messages attempt to intimidate recipients with alleged criminal proceedings and false accusations, specifically mentioning:
- Child pornography
- Cybercrimes
- Other fabricated criminal activities

**Psychological Tactics:**
- Fear-based social engineering
- Authority impersonation
- Threat of legal consequences
- Use of recognizable law enforcement names

**Impersonated Entities:**
- Croatian Police (Policijska uprava)
- Ministry of Internal Affairs
- Criminal Investigation Police
- Europol (European Union Agency for Law Enforcement Cooperation)

**Impact:**
Financial extortion through intimidation and false criminal accusations
