## 摘要

UAC-0241 conducted cyberattack against educational institutions in eastern Ukraine using compromised Gmail account to distribute emails containing GAMYBEAR malware.

## 事實內容

In the first ten days of November 2025, CERT-UA detected the distribution of emails with the subject "Order No. 332" targeting educational institutions and government agencies. The attack used a compromised Gmail account belonging to a higher educational institution in eastern Ukraine.

**Attack Details:**
- **Threat Actor:** UAC-0241
- **Malware Used:** GAMYBEAR
- **Attack Vector:** Compromised email account (Gmail)
- **Email Subject:** "Order No. 332" (Наказ № 332)
- **Target:** Educational institutions and government agencies in eastern Ukraine
- **Attack Method:** Email-based distribution using compromised legitimate account

**Timeline:**
- First ten days of November 2025: Attack campaign active
- November 18, 2025: CERT-UA public disclosure

**Context:**
The use of a compromised legitimate email account from an educational institution increases the likelihood of successful phishing as recipients may trust emails from known institutional addresses.

## 受影響系統

- Educational institutions in eastern Ukraine
- Government agencies
- Gmail email infrastructure
