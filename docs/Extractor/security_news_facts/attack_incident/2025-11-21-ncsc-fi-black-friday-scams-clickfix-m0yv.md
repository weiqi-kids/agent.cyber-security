## 摘要

NCSC-FI warns about Black Friday scams including fake online shops, phishing messages, and ClickFix malware distribution technique.

## 事實內容

This weekly review highlights cyber threats during Black Friday shopping season:
- **Fake online shops** created by criminals to exploit Black Friday deals
- **Phishing campaigns** themed around current special offers and promotions
- **ClickFix technique** being actively used to distribute malware
- Announcement of November 18 webinar on software development management (free registration)
- Publication of October Cyber Weather report
- Malware review of M0yv malware operation

Attack methods exploit consumer interest in Black Friday deals to compromise systems and steal payment information.

## 受影響系統

- Online shoppers during Black Friday
- E-commerce platforms
- Systems exposed to ClickFix technique
- Software development environments
