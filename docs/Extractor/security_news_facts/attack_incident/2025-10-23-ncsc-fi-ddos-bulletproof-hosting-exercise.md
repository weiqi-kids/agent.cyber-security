## 摘要

NCSC-FI discusses denial-of-service attacks, bulletproof hosting enabling crime, and state-sponsored cyber influence exercise.

## 事實內容

This weekly review covers multiple threat vectors and response capabilities:
- **Denial-of-service (DoS/DDoS) attacks** and their operational impacts
- **Bulletproof Hosting phenomenon** - infrastructure that enables criminal activity by providing hosting resistant to takedown
- **Security authorities exercise** - practiced responding to state-sponsored cyber influence operations
- **European Cybersecurity Month** - promoting improvement of everyday cybersecurity skills
- Malware review introducing Locky ransomware

The exercise demonstrates preparation for state-level cyber threats and influence campaigns.

## 受影響系統

- Organizations targeted by DoS/DDoS attacks
- Bulletproof hosting infrastructure
- Systems targeted by state-sponsored influence operations
- General public (cybersecurity awareness)
