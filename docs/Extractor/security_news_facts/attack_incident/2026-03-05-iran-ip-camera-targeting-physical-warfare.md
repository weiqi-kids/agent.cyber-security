## 摘要

Check Point Research identified intensified targeting of IP cameras from two manufacturers starting February 28, 2026, originating from infrastructure attributed to Iranian threat actors. The targeting extends across Israel, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait, UAE, and Cyprus -- countries experiencing significant missile activity linked to Iran. On March 1st, camera-targeting activity was also observed focused on Lebanon. Earlier, more targeted activity was observed against cameras in Israel and Qatar on January 14-15, dates surrounding Iran's temporary closure of airspace amid expectations of a potential U.S. strike. The findings are consistent with the assessment that Iran leverages camera compromise for operational support and battle damage assessment (BDA) for missile operations, meaning camera-targeting activity may serve as an early indicator of kinetic activity.

Check Point 研究識別出自 2026 年 2 月 28 日起，針對兩家製造商 IP 攝影機的攻擊活動加劇，來源基礎設施歸因於伊朗威脅行為者。攻擊範圍涵蓋以色列、卡達、巴林、科威特、阿聯酋及賽普勒斯，這些國家都正經歷與伊朗相關的重大飛彈活動。3 月 1 日，也觀察到針對黎巴嫩的攝影機攻擊活動。更早期間，1 月 14-15 日曾觀察到針對以色列和卡達攝影機的定向攻擊，該日期恰逢伊朗在預期美國可能發動打擊時暫時關閉領空。研究結果與伊朗利用攝影機入侵進行作戰支援及飛彈行動戰損評估 (BDA) 的評估一致，意味著攝影機攻擊活動可能作為動能行動的早期預警指標。
