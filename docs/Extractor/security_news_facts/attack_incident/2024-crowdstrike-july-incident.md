July cyber weather report highlighting CrowdStrike security product update causing widespread global disruption.

## 事實內容

The July 2024 cyber weather report notes:
- Generally calmer cyber activity compared to previous months
- Significant incident: CrowdStrike security product upgrade caused widespread global disruption
- Tax refund-themed scam messages increased in anticipation of early August refunds

The CrowdStrike incident was a major security event affecting organizations worldwide.
