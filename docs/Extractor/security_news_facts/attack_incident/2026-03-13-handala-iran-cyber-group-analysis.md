## 摘要

Iranian threat group Handala (also tracked as Void Manticore) has escalated destructive wiping attacks and hack-and-leak operations against US and Israeli organizations amid US-Israel airstrikes on Iran, targeting tech giants including Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Palantir, IBM, Nvidia, and Oracle.

## 事實內容

- Handala (Void Manticore) is an Iranian threat actor known for destructive wiping attacks combined with hack-and-leak operations
- Iran's leadership publicly declared operations of US tech giants in the Middle East (Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Palantir, IBM, Nvidia, Oracle) as "legitimate targets"
- Check Point Research published detailed analysis of Void Manticore TTPs
- The group's techniques remained largely consistent from 2024 to 2026: manual hands-on operations, off-the-shelf wipers, and publicly available deletion/encryption tools
- The group uses underground criminal services for initial access and malware acquisition
- Researchers describe recent activity as opportunistic rather than strategic, seeking to demonstrate retaliatory effect
- US organizations are now explicitly targeted following expansion from Israel-focused operations
- Indicators tend to be short-lived, consisting largely of commercial VPN services and open-source offensive tools

## 受影響系統

- US and Israeli organizations across technology, government, and critical infrastructure sectors
- Organizations with operations in Middle East regions
