## 摘要

CNCS (Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança, Portugal) issued an alert about a growing number of CEO Fraud and Business Email Compromise (BEC) incidents affecting Portuguese citizens and organizations, which can result in significant financial losses.

## 事實內容

- CNCS observed a growing number of CEO Fraud and Business Email Compromise (BEC) incidents in Portugal.
- In 2025, BEC/CEO Fraud incidents accounted for 18% of all social engineering incidents reported to CERT.PT.
- These campaigns involve sending emails or SMS messages impersonating executives or business partners to authorize fraudulent financial transfers.
- Victims include both individual citizens and organizations, with potential for significant financial losses.
- CNCS issued this alert on 2026-04-21 to warn the community and provide guidance.
- Organizations are advised to implement verification procedures for financial transfer requests, regardless of apparent sender identity.

## 受影響系統

- Organizations and individuals using email/SMS communications
- Finance and accounting departments particularly at risk
