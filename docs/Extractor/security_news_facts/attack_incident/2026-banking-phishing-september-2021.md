# Multi-agency alert: Banking credential phishing campaign

**Source**: NCSC-FI (Finland)  
**Link**: https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/ajankohtaista/rikolliset-urkkivat-suomalaisten-pankkitunnuksia  
**Published**: 2021-09-30

## Summary

Kela (Social Insurance Institution), NBI (National Bureau of Investigation) and NCSC-FI issued joint guidance as criminals phish banking credentials by impersonating Finnish banks and My Kanta Pages. Citizens must learn to spot scams and warn family and friends.

## Key Points

- **Multi-agency response**: Kela, NBI, and NCSC-FI joint advisory
- **Impersonated entities**: Finnish banks and My Kanta Pages
- **Target**: Banking credentials
- **Recommendation**: Careful login practices, scam awareness
- **Social responsibility**: Warn family and friends about scams

## Impact

The multi-agency response indicates the severity of the threat. Criminals exploiting trust in critical health and financial services requires coordinated public awareness efforts.
