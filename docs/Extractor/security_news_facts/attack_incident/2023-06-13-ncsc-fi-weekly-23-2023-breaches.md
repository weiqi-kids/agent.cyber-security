# The National Cyber Security Centre Finland's weekly review – 23/2023

**Source:** NCSC-FI Security Now!  
**Link:** https://www.kyberturvallisuuskeskus.fi/en/news/national-cyber-security-centre-finlands-weekly-review-232023  
**Published:** 2023-06-13

## Summary

In this second weekly review for June, unfortunate news about phone number spoofing, compromised online games and breached firewalls. Positive news: the National Bureau of Investigation and the Southwestern Finland Police Department have completed their preliminary investigation of a major online banking fraud case.

## Key Points

- Phone number spoofing incidents
- Compromised online games
- Breached firewalls
- Major online banking fraud case investigation completed

## Context

Weekly situational awareness report from NCSC-FI covering current cyber security incidents and law enforcement actions in Finland.
