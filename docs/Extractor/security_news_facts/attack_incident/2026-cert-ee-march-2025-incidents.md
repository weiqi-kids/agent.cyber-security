## Summary
Estonia recorded 533 incidents with impact in March 2025, the lowest in six months, with 185 phishing pages and 105 fraud pages discovered.

## Facts Content
**Reporting Period**: March 2025
**Total Incidents**: 533 incidents with impact
**Comparison**: Lowest figure in past 6 months

**Incident Breakdown**:
- Phishing pages: 185 (targeting banking and user credentials)
- Fraud pages: 105
- Increased targeted attacks noted

**Trend Analysis**:
- Overall incident volume decreased
- Quality over quantity: more sophisticated targeted attacks
- Continued focus on credential theft
