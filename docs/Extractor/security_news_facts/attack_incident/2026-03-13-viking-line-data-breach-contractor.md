## 摘要

Finnish ferry operator Viking Line disclosed a data breach affecting customers who used the pre-order service for tax-free products. The breach occurred at a subcontractor and does not affect the entire customer database or passenger records.

## 事實內容

- Viking Line confirmed a data breach affecting the "pre-order" service (pre-ordering tax-free products before travel)
- The breach occurred at a Viking Line subcontractor, not directly at Viking Line systems
- The breach does not affect the company's full customer registry or passenger data
- Communications head Christa Grönlund confirmed the scope was limited to pre-order customers
- Specific data types stolen were not fully disclosed at time of reporting
- The incident was reported by Finnish media (Iltalehti, Ilta-Sanomat)

## 受影響系統

- Viking Line pre-order customer data (tax-free product pre-orders)
- Subcontractor systems handling pre-order transactions
