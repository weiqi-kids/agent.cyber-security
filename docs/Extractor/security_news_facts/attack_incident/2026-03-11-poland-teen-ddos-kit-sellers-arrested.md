## 摘要

Polish police referred seven juvenile suspects aged 12-16 to family court for allegedly selling DDoS tools online linked to attacks on organizations.

## 事實內容

Polish police referred seven suspected juvenile cybercriminals to family court over an alleged scheme to sell DDoS kits online. Key details:

- Suspects were aged between 12 and 16 at the time of the alleged offenses
- All face charges related to selling DDoS tools through online channels
- Poland's Central Bureau for Combating Cybercrime (CBZC) described the operation as purely profit-driven
- The DDoS tools sold online were linked to attacks on various organizations
- CBZC stated that popular websites suffered service disruptions as a result of tools sold by the suspects

The case highlights the lowering age threshold for cybercrime participation and the accessibility of attack tools that enable even juveniles to participate in cybercriminal enterprises.

## 受影響系統

- Organizations targeted by DDoS attacks using the sold tools
- Online services and websites in Poland and potentially elsewhere
