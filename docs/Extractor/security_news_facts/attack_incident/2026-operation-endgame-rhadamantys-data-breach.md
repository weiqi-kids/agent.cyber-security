## 摘要

International Operation Endgame dismantled Rhadamantys malware infrastructure, leading to recovery of over 800,000 stolen credentials from 900+ compromised devices affecting Slovak victims.

## 事實內容

A coordinated international law enforcement operation led by Europol and Eurojust successfully dismantled the infrastructure of the Rhadamantys cybercrime group, which specialized in stealing credentials and data through malware.

**Operation Details:**
- **Name:** Operation Endgame
- **Leadership:** Europol and Eurojust
- **Participants:** Law enforcement agencies, states, and private institutions

**Malware Activity:**
- Rhadamantys group specialized in credential theft
- Targeted computers worldwide with data-stealing malware
- Sophisticated infrastructure for data exfiltration

**Data Recovery:**
- SK-CERT obtained over 800,000 leaked credentials from foreign partners
- Data originated from more than 900 compromised devices
- Significant number of Slovak victims identified

**Impact:**
The operation represents a major disruption to cybercrime infrastructure and demonstrates effective international cooperation in combating credential theft operations.

## 受影響系統

- Personal computers (900+ devices)
- Slovak users and organizations
- Global credential theft victims
- Windows-based systems
