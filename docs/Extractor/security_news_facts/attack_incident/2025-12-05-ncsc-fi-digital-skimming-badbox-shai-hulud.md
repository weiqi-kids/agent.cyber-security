## 摘要

NCSC-FI reports on digital skimming attacks, BadBox 2.0 malware in retail devices, Shai Hulud worm, and Microsoft 365 breaches.

## 事實內容

This weekly review covers multiple active attack campaigns:
- **Digital skimming attacks** targeting online shop payment pages to steal credit card information
- **BadBox 2.0 malware** found pre-installed on devices sold through major US retail chains
- **Shai Hulud worm** spreading through developer environments, stealing access credentials
- **Microsoft 365 account breaches** - recent wave of compromised accounts
- Public awareness event "Be vigilant on the web! Recognise and prevent digital scams" scheduled at Oodi on December 2
- Malware review of PromptLock ransomware

## 受影響系統

- E-commerce payment pages
- Retail devices with BadBox 2.0 pre-installed
- Developer environments exposed to Shai Hulud
- Microsoft 365 accounts
