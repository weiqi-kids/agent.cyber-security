## 摘要
Ukraine's CERT-UA reports targeted cyberattacks by UAC-0190 against Ukrainian Armed Forces personnel throughout February–March 2025, using malware PLUGGYAPE disguised as charitable fund activity to steal information.

## 事實內容
- **追蹤編號**: CERT-UA#19092
- **威脅行為者**: UAC-0190
- **攻擊時間**: February–March 2025 (ongoing at reporting)
- **目標**: Ukrainian Armed Forces (СОУ) personnel
- **攻擊手法**:
  - Disguised as charitable fund activity
  - Used malware tool PLUGGYAPE
  - Social engineering leveraging trust in charitable organizations
- **惡意程式**: PLUGGYAPE (information-stealing malware)
- **攻擊目的**: Intelligence gathering on Ukrainian defense personnel

## 受影響系統
- Ukrainian Armed Forces personnel devices and systems
- Systems of individuals associated with Ukrainian defense and military
