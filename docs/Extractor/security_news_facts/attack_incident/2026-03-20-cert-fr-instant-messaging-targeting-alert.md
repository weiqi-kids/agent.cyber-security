## 摘要

CERT-FR issued alert CERTFR-2026-ALE-0020 warning of targeted attacks against instant messaging applications, advising users and organizations to exercise heightened vigilance.

## 事實內容

CERT-FR published alert CERTFR-2026-ALE-0020 ("Note d'alerte – Ciblage des messageries instantanées") reporting active targeting of instant messaging platforms. The alert indicates that threat actors are actively exploiting or abusing instant messaging applications, potentially through malware distribution, credential theft, social engineering, or exploitation of application vulnerabilities. Instant messaging platforms are attractive targets due to their widespread use in both personal and professional contexts. CERT-FR urges users and organizations to apply available security updates, enable multi-factor authentication, and be vigilant about unsolicited messages or suspicious file attachments.

## 受影響系統

- Instant messaging applications (multiple platforms, see advisory)
- End users and organizations relying on instant messaging for communications
