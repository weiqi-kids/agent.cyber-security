## 摘要

TWCERT/CC received multiple reports of social engineering attacks impersonating company executives to request LINE group creation and employee personal information.

## 事實內容

TWCERT/CC recently received multiple external intelligence reports of attackers impersonating internal company executives to launch social engineering attacks, tricking recipients into opening emails and following instructions.

**Attack Characteristics:**
Malicious emails display distinct features, using "executive instructions," "administrative requirements," and "urgent tasks" as entry points.

**Email Content Examples:**
1. "For company management convenience, please create a dedicated internal LINE group. After creation, forward the group QR Code to this email. I'll join later to arrange work."
2. "Please provide employee list with personal information"

**Attack Vector:**
- Email spoofing to impersonate company executives
- Exploiting authority and urgency to lower recipient vigilance
- Requesting creation of communication channels (LINE groups)
- Collecting employee personal information

**Recommended Mitigations:**
1. Increase vigilance for suspicious emails, verify email source authenticity, avoid clicking suspicious attachments or links to prevent malware installation or phishing site redirection. If accidentally entering suspicious websites, do not input any personal information, account passwords, or financial data.
2. Check sender information for anomalies and reconfirm email authenticity with executives or IT department. If suspected as social engineering attack, immediately report to IT department for follow-up processing.
3. Regularly update passwords following complexity principles and enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) to enhance security protection and reduce intrusion risks.
4. Network administrators should reference latest threat intelligence and compromise indicators, properly deploy preventive blocking measures to intercept and filter suspicious emails.
5. Recommend units continuously strengthen internal security awareness training and exercises to improve personnel's ability to identify social engineering attacks and enhance defense awareness.

## 受影響系統

- Enterprise email systems
- Employee workstations
- LINE messaging platform
- Corporate communication channels
