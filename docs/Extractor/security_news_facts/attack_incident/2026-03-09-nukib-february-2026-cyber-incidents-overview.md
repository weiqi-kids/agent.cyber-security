## 摘要

NUKIB (Czech National Cyber and Information Security Agency) published its monthly overview of cyber security incidents for February 2026. The number of incidents decreased to 23 compared to the previous month, though still above average. Two incidents were classified as significant.

## 事實內容

- In February 2026, NUKIB recorded 23 cyber security incidents, a decrease from January 2026
- Despite the decline, the February figure remains above the long-term average
- The primary factor behind the decrease was a reduction in DDoS attacks, which dropped to one-third of January's levels
- Out of the total incidents, 2 were classified as significant ("významné")
- The report was published on March 9, 2026
- NUKIB is the Czech Republic's national authority for cyber and information security

## 受影響系統

- Czech Republic national cyber infrastructure
- Organizations subject to NUKIB oversight
- Systems targeted by DDoS attacks in the Czech Republic
