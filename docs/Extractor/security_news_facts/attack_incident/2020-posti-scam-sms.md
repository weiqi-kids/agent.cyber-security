## 摘要

Phishing and subscription trap SMS messages sent impersonating Posti (Finnish postal service).

## 事實內容

Phishing and subscription trap text messages are being sent in Posti's name, including at least three different types of scams. Users warned to be careful when opening links as they may lead to malware, phishing or other scams. Users advised not to enter information on websites whose authenticity they doubt.

## 受影響系統

- Finnish mobile phone users
- Potential malware victims
