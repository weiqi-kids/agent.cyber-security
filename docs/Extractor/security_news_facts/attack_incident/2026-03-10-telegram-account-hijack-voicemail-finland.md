## 摘要

Criminals in Finland are hijacking Telegram accounts by registering accounts under victims' phone numbers and obtaining verification codes via voicemail interception, even when victims do not actively use voicemail.

## 事實內容

Finnish cybersecurity incidents have been reported where criminals register a Telegram account using a victim's phone number. The attack exploits voicemail services: the attacker calls the victim's phone, which redirects to voicemail, and retrieves a Telegram-sent verification code left as a voicemail message.

A notable case involved a 35-year-old man from Oulu who received a Telegram notification stating his elderly mother's number had been used to join Telegram — despite the mother's phone being off for months and reportedly not having a voicemail service active. The Finnish Transport and Communications Agency (Traficom) Cybersecurity Centre noted that many users are unaware they even have voicemail enabled on their subscriptions.

The Finnish NCSC-FI security expert Samuli Könönen commented that people may not use voicemail even if the service is technically active on their subscription, leaving them unaware of this attack vector.

**Attack Method**: Social engineering / voicemail interception for two-factor authentication bypass

## 受影響系統

- Telegram accounts linked to Finnish phone numbers
- Telecom voicemail services (any carrier)
