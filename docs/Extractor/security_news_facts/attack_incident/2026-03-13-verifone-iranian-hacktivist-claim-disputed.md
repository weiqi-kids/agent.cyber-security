## 摘要

An Iranian-linked hacktivist group claimed to have hacked payment device maker Verifone, targeting its Israeli office and allegedly stealing data. Verifone denied the claim, stating no evidence of any incident was found and no client service disruption occurred.

## 事實內容

- A self-proclaimed hacktivist group widely suspected of being an Iranian intelligence front claimed to have hacked Verifone
- The group claimed to have disrupted Verifone's Israeli office and stolen data
- Verifone (New York-based payment device manufacturer) issued a statement denying the breach
- Verifone stated: "Verifone has found no evidence of any incident related to this claim and has no service disruption to our clients"
- The group is assessed by researchers as potentially linked to Iranian state-sponsored cyber operations
- The incident occurred in the context of escalated Iranian cyber activity following US-Israel military strikes

## 受影響系統

- Verifone payment device systems (disputed)
- Verifone Israeli operations (claimed)
