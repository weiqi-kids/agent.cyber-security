## 摘要

Unit 42 has identified an active phishing campaign using a malicious replica of the Israeli Home Front Command RedAlert application. This campaign weaponizes a legitimate-looking Android package (APK) to deliver mobile surveillance and data-exfiltrating malware. Additionally, a surge in hacktivist activity has been observed, with estimates of 60 individual groups active, including pro-Russian groups as of March 2, 2026. Multiple Iranian state-aligned personas and collectives have claimed responsibility for disruptive operations, several associated with the newly established "Electronic Operations Room" formed on February 28, 2026.

Unit 42 發現一個積極的網路釣魚活動，使用以色列後方司令部 RedAlert 應用程式的惡意複製品。此活動將外觀合法的 Android 套件 (APK) 武器化，以投遞行動監控和資料外洩惡意軟體。此外，觀察到駭客主義活動激增，估計有 60 個個別團體活躍，包括截至 2026 年 3 月 2 日的親俄團體。多個伊朗國家支持的人物和團體聲稱對破壞性行動負責，其中數個與 2026 年 2 月 28 日新成立的「電子作戰室」有關。
