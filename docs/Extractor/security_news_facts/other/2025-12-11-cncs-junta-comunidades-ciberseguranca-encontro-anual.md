## 摘要

CNCS hosted the 1st edition of C-Communities, an annual gathering of cybersecurity communities in Portugal, bringing together over 100 members from sectors including water, media, ports, energy, retail, health, and digital infrastructure.

## 事實內容

- The 1st edition of C-Communities was held on 10 December at GALP premises in Lisbon
- Over 100 members from various cybersecurity communities attended
- Communities represented include ISAC sectors (Water, Media, Ports, Energy, Retail/Distribution, Health, Digital Infrastructure), Azores regional ISAC, and the Alliance for Cybersecurity
- Data from the 2025 Risks & Conflicts Report showed 92% of surveyed professionals consider cybersecurity risk increasing in 2024, with 90% expecting further increase in 2025
- The event included presentations, collaborative exercises, and panel discussions
- CNCS Coordinator Lino Santos highlighted communities as "our greatest asset"
- The meeting reinforces cross-sector collaboration and information sharing on cybersecurity threats

## 受影響系統

- Critical sector organizations participating in Portuguese ISACs
- Public-private sector cybersecurity collaboration in Portugal
