## 摘要

NCSC-FI publishes book with 20 real-life-based cyber exercise scenarios for 2020.

## 事實內容

For organizations wanting to improve cybersecurity through cyber crisis exercises but needing scenario ideas, NCSC-FI offers help. The new book "Kyberharjoitusskenaariot 2020" contains 20 different scenarios based on real-life events to support exercise planning. This complements the cyber exercise guide from previous year.
