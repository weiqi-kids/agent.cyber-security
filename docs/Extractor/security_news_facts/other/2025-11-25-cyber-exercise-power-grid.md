## 摘要

RIA organized cyber exercise to test and strengthen Estonia's ability to respond to cyber threats against critical infrastructure, focusing on power grid resilience.

## 事實內容

Last week, the Information System Authority (RIA) of Estonia organized a cyber exercise with the objective of testing and strengthening the state's capability to respond to cyber threats directed at critical infrastructure.

Exercise focus:
- Critical infrastructure cyber threats
- Electric power system resilience
- Inter-agency cooperation and coordination
- Response capability testing

The exercise simulated cyber attacks against the electricity system to validate:
- Detection and response procedures
- Continuity of power grid operations under cyber attack
- Coordination between government agencies and critical infrastructure operators

This type of exercise is crucial for ensuring resilience of critical national infrastructure against increasingly sophisticated cyber threats.

## 受影響系統

- Simulated: Electric power grid infrastructure
- Participating: Government agencies and critical infrastructure operators
