## 摘要
A competition for R&D projects in Artificial Intelligence, Data Science and Cybersecurity relevant to Public Administration opened with applications accepted until July 31, 2024, offering funding for innovative projects in these fields.

## 事實內容
CNCS Portugal publicized a competitive call for IC&DT (Innovation and R&D) projects focused on Artificial Intelligence, Data Science and Cybersecurity with relevance to the Public Administration sector. Applications were open until July 31, 2024. The competition aimed to stimulate research and development that could improve cybersecurity capabilities within Portuguese public sector organizations.

## 受影響系統
- Portuguese Public Administration
- AI and cybersecurity research institutions
