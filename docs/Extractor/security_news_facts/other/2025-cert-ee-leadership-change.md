## Summary
Taavi Kupper becomes head of RIA's CERT-EE from October 1, 2025, while former head Veikko Raasuke continues as architect focusing on state e-service security.

## Factual Content
From October 1, Taavi Kupper leads RIA's Cybersecurity Centre Incident Response Department (CERT-EE). The former head, Veikko Raasuke, continues as an architect, focusing on ensuring the security and reliability of state e-services.

**Leadership Change:**
- New CERT-EE head: Taavi Kupper
- Effective date: October 1, 2025
- Former head: Veikko Raasuke
- Raasuke's new role: Architect (e-service security and reliability)
- Organization: RIA Cybersecurity Centre
