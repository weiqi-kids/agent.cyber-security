## 摘要
CNCS and the Safe Internet Center launched a population-wide survey on cybersecurity behaviors and perceptions, in collaboration with Audax_Iscte researchers, targeting adults 18+ to understand their sense of online security, digital usage habits, and cybersecurity knowledge. Survey open until September 24.

## 事實內容
The CNCS (Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança) and Centro Internet Segura (Safe Internet Center), as part of the Cybersecurity Observatory, launched a study on cybersecurity behaviors and perceptions among the general population (18+):

**Research objectives**:
- Understanding the perceived sense of security when using the Internet and digital technologies
- Assessing precautions people take in digital usage
- Exploring knowledge and behaviors regarding cybersecurity

**Partnership**: Supported by Audax_Iscte research team (ISCTE University Institute of Lisbon)

**Survey details**:
- Open to all adults 18 and over in Portugal
- Available until September 24, 2022
- Online participation via Qualtrics platform

This study is part of the Observatory's ongoing work to monitor societal cybersecurity awareness and behaviors in Portugal.

## 受影響系統
- Portuguese general public and digital users
- Organizations designing awareness campaigns
