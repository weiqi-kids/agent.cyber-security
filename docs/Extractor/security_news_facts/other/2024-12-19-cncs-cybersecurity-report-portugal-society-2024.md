## 摘要

CNCS published the 2024 Cybersecurity in Portugal Society report with attitudes and behavior data.

## 事實內容

The Cybersecurity in Portugal, Society 2024 Report, developed by the Cybersecurity Observatory of the National Cybersecurity Centre (CNCS), analyzes attitudes, behaviors, and education/awareness in cybersecurity based on data from 2023 and 2024. The report presents conclusions and identifies strategic recommendations for organizations and citizens. Key areas include digital security awareness levels among Portuguese society and behavioral patterns related to cybersecurity.

Original language: Portuguese (CNCS Portugal)
