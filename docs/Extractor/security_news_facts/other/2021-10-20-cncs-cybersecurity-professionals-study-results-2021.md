## 摘要
The 2nd edition of the Study on Cybersecurity and Information Security Professionals in Portugal has been published, conducted through an online questionnaire by AP2SI with support from the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory.

## 事實內容
The results of the 2nd Edition of the Study on Cybersecurity and Information Security Professionals in Portugal have been published by AP2SI (Associação Portuguesa para a Promoção da Segurança da Informação) with support from the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory.

- Survey conducted via open online questionnaire to the community
- Data allows understanding of characteristics and evolution of professionals in this highly dynamic and decisive area for national cybersecurity
- Study covers skills, roles, compensation, and workforce trends in Portuguese cybersecurity sector

## 受影響系統
- Cybersecurity workforce in Portugal
- Organizations hiring cybersecurity professionals
