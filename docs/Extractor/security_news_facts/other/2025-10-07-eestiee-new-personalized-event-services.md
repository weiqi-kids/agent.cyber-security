## 摘要

Estonia's state portal eesti.ee launched two new personalized event services: mandatory military service and divorce, now accessible in a more user-friendly format.

## 事實內容

The Estonian state portal eesti.ee added two new personalized event services. The compulsory military service (kaitseväekohustus) and divorce (abielu lahutamine) services are not new themselves, but are now available through the state portal in a more convenient and user-need-adapted format. The portal provides important information tailored to the user's specific situation.
