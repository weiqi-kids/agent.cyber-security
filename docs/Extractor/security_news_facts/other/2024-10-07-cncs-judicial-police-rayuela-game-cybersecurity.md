## 摘要
CNCS partnered with the Judicial Police (PJ) on the Rayuela game, launched October 3 as part of the Mission Cybersecure initiative during European Cybersecurity Month, aimed at raising cybersecurity awareness among the public.

## 事實內容
The Rayuela game was publicly presented at Judicial Police headquarters on October 3, 2024, as part of the Mission Cybersecure (Missão Cibersegura) initiative marking the European Cybersecurity Month. CNCS served as a partner in this awareness initiative designed to engage citizens in learning about online safety and cybersecurity practices through an interactive gaming experience.

## 受影響系統
- General public cybersecurity awareness
- Portuguese law enforcement and cybersecurity awareness programs
