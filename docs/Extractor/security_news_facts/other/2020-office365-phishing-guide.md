## 摘要

NCSC-FI publishes comprehensive guide for organizations on preventing Office 365 credential phishing.

## 事實內容

Persistent Microsoft Office 365 credential phishing and stealing affects many Finnish organizations. NCSC-FI compiled a comprehensive guide titled "Protection against Microsoft Office 365 credential phishing and data breaches." The guide is aimed at those responsible for maintenance and information security at organizations, including corporate management and data protection officers.

## 受影響系統

- Microsoft Office 365
- Organizational email systems
