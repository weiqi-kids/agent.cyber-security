## 摘要
The application process to join the ENISA Advisory Group for the 2025-2027 mandate is open until March 26, 2025, seeking cybersecurity experts across Europe to support ENISA's mission and activities.

## 事實內容
The European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) opened applications for its Advisory Group, which provides expert guidance on ENISA's activities and priorities. Applications were accepted until March 26, 2025, seeking experts from industry, academia, and civil society across EU member states. CNCS Portugal publicized this opportunity for Portuguese cybersecurity professionals to participate in shaping EU-level cybersecurity policy and coordination.

## 受影響系統
- ENISA Advisory Group composition
- EU-level cybersecurity policy development
