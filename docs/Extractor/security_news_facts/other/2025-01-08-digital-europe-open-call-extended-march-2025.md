## 摘要
CNCS Portugal announced the Digital Europe Programme open call deadline has been extended to March 27, 2025, with 102 million euros available for cybersecurity deployment actions across European organizations.

## 事實內容
The Digital Europe Programme open call for cybersecurity deployment actions, previously set to close in January 2025, was extended with a new deadline of March 27, 2025. The call offers a total budget of 102 million euros to support organizations deploying cybersecurity solutions and strengthening capabilities. CNCS Portugal announced the new deadline to encourage organizations that had not yet applied.

## 受影響系統
- Organizations eligible for Digital Europe Programme funding
- European cybersecurity deployment activities
