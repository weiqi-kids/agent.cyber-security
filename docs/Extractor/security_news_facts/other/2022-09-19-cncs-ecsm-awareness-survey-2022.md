## 摘要
ENISA, in partnership with Anima People (behavioral science specialists), launched a pre-campaign survey for the European Cybersecurity Month (ECSM) to evaluate cybersecurity awareness campaigns' effectiveness in changing employee behavior, with CNCS promoting participation.

## 事實內容
The European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) is conducting a pre-campaign survey for the European Cybersecurity Month (ECSM) in October:

- **Partnership**: ENISA with Anima People, behavioral science specialists
- **Project purpose**: Evaluate the effectiveness of cybersecurity awareness campaigns in changing employee behavior
- **Expected outcomes**: Benefits for organizations designing successful awareness campaigns, promoting long-term behavioral change toward a more cybersecure world
- **Participation**: Survey open to the public via EU survey platform
- **Timing**: Pre-campaign survey (survey also conducted post-campaign)

ENISA promotes cybersecurity across EU citizens and organizations each October through the ECSM initiative.

## 受影響系統
- EU organizations' cybersecurity awareness programs
- Employees participating in security awareness campaigns
