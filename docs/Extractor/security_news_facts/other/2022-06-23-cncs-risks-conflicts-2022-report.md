## 摘要
The CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory published the 2022 edition of the Risks & Conflicts Report, analyzing major incidents, cybercrime, and threat actors affecting national cyberspace in 2021 with perspectives for 2022-2023, drawing on data from partner institutions and international references.

## 事實內容
The 2022 edition of the Risks & Conflicts Report was published by the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory:

**Scope**:
- Primary focus: Year 2021 incidents, cybercrime, and threat actors affecting Portuguese national cyberspace
- Perspective: Trends for 2022 and 2023

**Methodology**:
- Systematic data from partner institution contributions
- International reference frameworks
- Diverse inputs to present an integrated, comprehensive view of risks and conflicts in national cyberspace

**Available formats**:
- Full document
- Short version (Relatório em 15m — 15-minute summary)

The report serves as a reference for organizations and citizens to identify major threats and improve risk analysis and protective measures.

## 受影響系統
- Portuguese national cyberspace
- Organizations and citizens in Portugal
