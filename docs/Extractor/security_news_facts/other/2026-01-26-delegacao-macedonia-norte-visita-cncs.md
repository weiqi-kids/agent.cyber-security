## 摘要

A delegation from the Republic of North Macedonia visited CNCS to learn about Portugal's Safer Internet Centre (CIS) model, as North Macedonia develops its own Safe Internet Centre (MKSafeNet).

## 事實內容

- A four-member delegation from North Macedonia's MKSafeNet team visited CNCS
- The purpose was to learn about Portugal's Centro Internet Segura (CIS) structure and operations
- Presentations were given by CNCS, CIS (governance model, stakeholders, workflow), the Directorate-General for Education (DGE), IPDJ, and APAV (Portuguese victim support association)
- The visit focused on collaborative working models, awareness services, and resources of the CIS
- The delegation aimed to model MKSafeNet on the Portuguese experience

## 受影響系統

- Internet safety organizations in Portugal and North Macedonia
- Youth and digital safety programs
