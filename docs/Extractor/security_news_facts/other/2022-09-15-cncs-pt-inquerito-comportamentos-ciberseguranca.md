## 摘要

CNCS and Centro Internet Segura, in collaboration with Audax_Iscte, launched a survey for Portuguese adults (18+) to assess cybersecurity behaviors, perceptions, knowledge, and awareness; open until 24 September 2022.

## 事實內容

The National Cybersecurity Centre (CNCS) and Centro Internet Segura (CIS), as part of the Cybersecurity Observatory activities, are conducting a study in collaboration with a team from Audax_Iscte. The survey targets the general Portuguese population (adults 18+) and aims to understand:

- Perceived sense of security when using the Internet and digital technologies
- Precautions adopted in digital usage
- Knowledge and behavior regarding cybersecurity topics

**Survey deadline**: 24 September 2022

The study seeks to explore cybersecurity knowledge and behaviors in the broader population to inform policy and awareness program development.
