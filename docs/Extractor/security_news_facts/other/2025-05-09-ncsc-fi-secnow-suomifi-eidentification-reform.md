## 摘要

Finland's DVV is reforming Suomi.fi e-Identification; from 12 May 2025, users may be prompted to enable Suomi.fi Messages when strongly authenticating in government electronic services, including Traficom's e-services.

## 事實內容

The Digital and Population Data Services Agency (DVV) of Finland is reforming the Suomi.fi e-Identification system. Key changes:

- Effective from 12 May 2025
- When strongly authenticating in an authority's electronic service, users may be prompted to enable Suomi.fi Messages
- The change applies to Traficom's e-services (including NCSC-FI services)
- Strong authentication (strong e-identification) is the mechanism used across Finnish government digital services
- The reform affects the identification flow used by citizens accessing government online services

This is an administrative/infrastructure change in Finnish government digital identity services, with no direct cybersecurity threat.

## 受影響系統

- Suomi.fi e-Identification system
- Finnish government electronic services
- Traficom e-services
