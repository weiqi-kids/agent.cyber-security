## 摘要
The 18th edition of the CNCS General Cybersecurity Course was announced, providing general training on cybersecurity topics including technical, organizational, and management dimensions, taught by a diverse panel of speakers on national and international cybersecurity.

## 事實內容
The CNCS General Cybersecurity Course (Curso Geral de Cibersegurança) 18th edition was announced. Key details:

- **Objective**: Raise awareness and promote reflection on cybersecurity among participants
- **Scope**: Technical, organizational, and management dimensions of cybersecurity
- **Topics covered**: Cybersecurity concepts and ecosystem, cyber espionage, combating cybercrime, cyber(in)security, human factor in cybersecurity, and other relevant topics
- **Format**: Taught by a diverse panel of speakers covering national and international cybersecurity perspectives
- **Note**: Registrations for the 18th edition were subsequently closed

The course is part of CNCS's ongoing effort to build cybersecurity knowledge among citizens, entities, and professionals in Portugal.

## 受影響系統
- Portuguese cybersecurity professionals and students
- Organizations seeking cybersecurity training
