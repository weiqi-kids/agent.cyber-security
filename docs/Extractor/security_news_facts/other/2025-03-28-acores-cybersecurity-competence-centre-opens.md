## 摘要

The Azores Cybersecurity Competence Centre was officially inaugurated at the Nonagon Science and Technology Park.

## 事實內容

The Azores Cybersecurity Competence Centre (CCC-Açores) was officially inaugurated at the Nonagon Science and Technology Park on the island of São Miguel. The ceremony marked the official launch of the centre, which aims to develop cybersecurity capabilities and talent in the Azores region. The inauguration was attended by Pedro Batista, Regional Director of Communications and Digital Transition, representing the Regional Government. The centre is part of broader efforts to strengthen cybersecurity infrastructure and expertise across Portugal, including its island territories.

Original language: Portuguese (CNCS Portugal)
