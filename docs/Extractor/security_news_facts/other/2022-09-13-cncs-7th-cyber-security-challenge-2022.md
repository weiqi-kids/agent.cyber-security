## 摘要
The 7th edition of the ENISA-promoted Cyber Security Challenge 2022 launched, featuring 33 countries (28 European + 5 guests) each fielding 10 young cybersecurity talents over 3 days of challenges spanning web security, mobile security, hardware, cryptography, reverse engineering, and forensics, with a new attack-and-defense competition format.

## 事實內容
The Cyber Security Challenge 2022 was launched as Europe's premier cybersecurity talent identification competition:

**Format and structure**:
- **Organizer**: ENISA (European Union Agency for Cybersecurity)
- **Participants**: Teams from 33 countries (28 European + 5 invited), each with 10 young national talent representatives
- **Duration**: 3 days of challenges
- **New feature**: Attack-and-defense challenge where each team defends its own infrastructure while attacking others simultaneously

**Challenge domains**:
- Web security
- Mobile security
- Hardware security
- Cryptography
- Reverse engineering
- Digital forensics

**Portugal's team**:
- Selected through a qualifying competition in July 2022
- Partnership: CNCS, Instituto Superior Técnico, Universidade do Porto, and AP2SI
- Being trained by coaches including alumni from previous national teams

**Objective**: Identify cybersecurity talent, promote cooperation, and foster community among young security professionals across Europe.

## 受影響系統
- Cybersecurity education and talent pipeline across EU
- Youth cybersecurity skills development
