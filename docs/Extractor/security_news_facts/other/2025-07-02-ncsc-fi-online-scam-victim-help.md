## 摘要

NCSC-FI發布網路詐騙受害者指南，說明受害後應採取的緊急步驟，以及可獲得技術、財務和心理支援的資源。

## 事實內容

- Falling victim to an online scam can result in financial loss, compromise of personal and sensitive information, emotional distress, and loss of sense of security.
- NCSC-FI published guidance outlining first steps victims should take upon realising or suspecting they have been scammed, emphasising the importance of acting quickly to minimise harm.
- The article provides guidance on where scam victims can find assistance for technical issues (e.g., account recovery, device remediation), financial matters (e.g., reporting fraud to banks), and emotional wellbeing support.

## 受影響系統

- General public (online scam victims)
