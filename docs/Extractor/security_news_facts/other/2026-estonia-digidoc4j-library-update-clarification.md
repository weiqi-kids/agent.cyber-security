## 摘要

RIA clarifies that digital signature services will continue working as SK ID Solutions implements 3072-bit RSA keys for validity confirmation signing.

## 事實內容

The Information System Authority (RIA) of Estonia issued a clarification that previous information about potential digital signature service disruptions starting February 4 is no longer relevant. Digital signature functionality will continue to operate normally because SK ID Solutions is implementing 3072-bit RSA keys for validity confirmation signing.

This announcement resolves concerns about potential service interruptions and confirms the continuity of digital signature services through the cryptographic key upgrade process.

## 受影響系統

- DigiDoc4j library
- Digital signature services
- SK ID Solutions infrastructure
