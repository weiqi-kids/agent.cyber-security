## 摘要
CNCS released a set of 23 video modules as supplementary material to the Roadmap for Minimum Cybersecurity Capabilities and the National Cybersecurity Reference Framework, presenting recommendations to help organizations improve their cybersecurity capabilities.

## 事實內容
The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança published a new support tool for organizations to develop minimum cybersecurity capabilities:

- **Format**: 23 video modules organized as instructional content
- **Purpose**: Complement to the Roadmap for Minimum Cybersecurity Capabilities (Roteiro para as Capacidades Mínimas de Cibersegurança)
- **Associated with**: The National Cybersecurity Reference Framework (Quadro Nacional de Referência para a Cibersegurança)
- **Content**: Various recommendations for organizations to improve cybersecurity through sustained and coherent improvement of technical, human, and process capabilities

The videos are available on CNCS's YouTube playlist. This initiative is part of CNCS's ongoing efforts to build cybersecurity capacity across Portuguese organizations.

## 受影響系統
- Portuguese organizations (all sectors)
- Public administration
- Small and medium enterprises
