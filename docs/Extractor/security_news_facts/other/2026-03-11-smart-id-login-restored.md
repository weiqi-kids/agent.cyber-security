## 摘要

Smart-ID login in the Estonian national app (Eesti äpp) has been restored to normal operation following a disruption related to the Smart-ID+ upgrade.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) confirmed that Smart-ID login in the Estonian national mobile application (Eesti äpp) is functioning normally again. The brief disruption was caused by compatibility issues following the introduction of the Smart-ID+ upgrade in the national authentication service on February 26, 2026. The incident has been resolved.

## 受影響系統

- Eesti äpp (Estonian national mobile application)
- Smart-ID authentication
- National authentication service (riigi autentimisteenus)
