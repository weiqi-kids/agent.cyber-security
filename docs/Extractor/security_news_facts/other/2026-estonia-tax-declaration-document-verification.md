## 摘要

Estonian authorities remind citizens to verify document validity and PIN codes before tax declaration period starting February 16, 2026.

## 事實內容

The Tax and Customs Board (MTA), Police and Border Guard Board (PPA), and Information System Authority (RIA) issued a joint reminder for citizens to check their document validity periods and PIN code availability before the tax declaration period begins on February 16, 2026.

This is a routine public service announcement to ensure smooth tax declaration process and prevent issues related to expired documents or missing authentication credentials.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian e-Government services
- Tax declaration systems
