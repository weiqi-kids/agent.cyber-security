## Summary
RIA releases new DigiDoc version 3.0.0 for Android with modern technology platform while maintaining core functionality, rolling out via Google Play over one week.

## Factual Content
RIA has completed a new version 3.0.0 of RIA DigiDoc for Android devices. This is a generation change – the application is based on a modern technology platform while maintaining the application's core functionalities and business logic. The new DigiDoc will reach users through the Google Play store within a week.

**Update Details:**
- New version: 3.0.0
- Platform: Android
- Change: Complete platform modernization
- Preserved: Core functionality and business logic
- Distribution: Google Play Store
- Rollout period: One week
