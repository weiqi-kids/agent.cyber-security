## Summary
Eesti app users can now verify identity at PRIA and Unemployment Insurance Fund service points, presenting ID card or passport data conveniently via smartphone.

## Factual Content
Eesti app users can now verify their identity at the Agricultural Registers and Information Board (PRIA) and Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund service points, in addition to existing service providers, presenting ID card or passport data conveniently and securely directly via smartphone.

**New Integration:**
- New service points: PRIA, Estonian Unemployment Insurance Fund
- Feature: Identity verification via smartphone
- Method: Present ID card or passport data through app
- Benefits: Convenience, security
- Platform: Eesti app
