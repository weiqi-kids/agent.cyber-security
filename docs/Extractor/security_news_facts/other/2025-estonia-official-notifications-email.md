## Summary
Estonian Information System Authority updated eesti.ee portal terms of use and notified users from official address registrikood@eesti.ee. Clarification provided on recognizing official notifications after sender address raised user questions.

## Description
The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) updated the eesti.ee government portal's terms of use and notified portal users. The notification was sent officially from the government address registrikood@eesti.ee. Since the sender address raised questions from some users, RIA provided clarification on recognizing official notifications.

This communication helps users distinguish legitimate government notifications from phishing attempts using similar-looking addresses.
