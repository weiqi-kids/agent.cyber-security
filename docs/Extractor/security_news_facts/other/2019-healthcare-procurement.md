## 摘要

Kyber-terveys project develops information security requirements list for public procurements in healthcare.

## 事實內容

The Kyber-terveys project focused on cybersecurity in healthcare developed a list of information security and data protection requirements for public procurements in the social and healthcare sector. Most criteria can be used in other sectors too. The list has been successfully used in the North Ostrobothnia hospital district with very positive results. Addressing security from procurement phase ensures efficient risk management and cost control.
