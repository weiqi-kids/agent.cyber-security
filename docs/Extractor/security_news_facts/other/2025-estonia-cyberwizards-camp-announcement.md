## Summary
Estonian Information System Authority organizes third annual CyberWizards international girls' cybersecurity camp August 11-16 in Kehtna, Rapla County, with 87 registered participants aged 13-16 from ten countries.

## Description
The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) is organizing the third annual CyberWizards international cybersecurity summer camp for girls on August 11-16, 2025, in Kehtna, Rapla County. The camp aims to spark interest in technology and cybersecurity among youth. This year, 87 girls aged 13-16 from ten different countries registered for the camp.
