## 摘要
TWCERT/CC held its 2025 annual conference on December 3, themed "Building Secure Products, Linking Trusted Defense Lines," gathering experts from ASUS, CyCraft, III, Delta Electronics, Zyxel, Synology, and Panasonic Taiwan to discuss AI-driven threats, incident coordination, and PSIRT governance.

## 事實內容
- **主題**: "Building Secure Products, Linking Trusted Defense Lines"
- **與會者**: Representatives from ASUS, CyCraft (Bey-Chu), Institute for Information Industry (III), Delta Electronics, Zyxel Networks, Synology, Panasonic Taiwan
- **關鍵議題**:
  - AI-driven cybersecurity threats and defense
  - Taiwan's national incident reporting and coordination achievements
  - Product security governance and PSIRT vulnerability reporting practices
  - Secure-by-Design principles
- **政府發言**: Minister Lin Yi-Jing of the Ministry of Digital Affairs emphasized product security as a foundation for enterprise competitiveness, noting international regulations increasingly treat product security as part of supply chain governance
- **高峰座談**: Focused on "Product Security as Brand Trust" — covering the full lifecycle from design, manufacturing, to vulnerability reporting
- **產業趨勢**: APT attacks, ransomware, and generative AI threats highlighted as persistent challenges requiring cross-sector collaboration
