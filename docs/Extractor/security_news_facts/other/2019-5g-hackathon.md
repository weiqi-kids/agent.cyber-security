## 摘要

World's first open 5G Cyber Security Hackathon held in Oulu with 70 top hackers.

## 事實內容

On November 29, 2019, 70 top hackers from around the world gathered in Oulu, Finland to test their skills against 5G and cybersecurity during the weekend. Oulu, located near the Arctic Circle, is known as a global 5G and 6G hotspot and home to top-tier cybersecurity research and development.
