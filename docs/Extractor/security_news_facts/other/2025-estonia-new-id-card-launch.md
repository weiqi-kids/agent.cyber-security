## Summary
Estonian Police and Border Guard Board begins issuing new ID cards in mid-November 2025 with updated appearance, chip, and software. Technical transition may cause temporary e-service issues.

## Description
Starting in the second half of November 2025, the Estonian Police and Border Guard Board (PPA) will issue new ID cards with updates to card appearance, chip technology, and software. The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) noted that the technical transition will take time, and not all e-services related to the new ID card may function fully during the first few days.

Users and service providers should be prepared for potential temporary service disruptions as the new ID card infrastructure is deployed. The transition represents a significant update to Estonia's digital identity infrastructure.

## Technical Details
- **Launch Date**: Mid-November 2025
- **Changes**: Card appearance, chip technology, software
- **Issuer**: Police and Border Guard Board (PPA)
- **Expected Issues**: Temporary e-service disruptions during initial deployment
