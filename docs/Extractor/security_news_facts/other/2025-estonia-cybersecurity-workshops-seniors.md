## Summary
Estonian Information System Authority invites 45+ age group to free cybersecurity workshops teaching simple practical tips for keeping money, photos, and personal data secure.

## Description
The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) invites the 45+ age group to participate in free cybersecurity workshops. Participants learn simple and practical tips for keeping money, photos, and personal data secure. The workshops target middle-aged and senior citizens who may be more vulnerable to cyber threats.
