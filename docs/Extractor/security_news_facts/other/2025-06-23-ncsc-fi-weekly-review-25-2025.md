## 摘要

NCSC-FI 第25週週報：詐騙受害建議、DNS管理提醒、赫爾辛基資料洩露調查報告，以及雲端服務準則評估研討會摘要。

## 事實內容

- NCSC-FI shared advice for victims of scams and reminded administrators about the importance of proper DNS management to prevent DNS hijacking and related attacks.
- The Finnish Safety Investigation Authority published its report on the Helsinki data breach, which NCSC-FI highlighted in the weekly review.
- NCSC-FI published an updated guideline on the operation of information security inspection bodies.
- Perspectives and outcomes from the previous week's event on cloud service criteria and evaluation were also shared.

## 受影響系統

- DNS infrastructure (admin responsibility reminder)
- Helsinki city systems (data breach referenced)
