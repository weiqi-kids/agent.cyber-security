## Summary
Rescue Board announces new emergency alert test on December 4, 2025, between 10:00-16:00 to prepare for nationwide test on December 10.

## Factual Content
The Rescue Board, in cooperation with RIA and Women's Voluntary Defence Organisation, will send test notifications via the Eesti app and "Ole valmis!" mobile application throughout Estonia on Thursday, December 4, between 10:00-16:00. During testing, app users may receive multiple test notifications. The purpose of testing is to check app functionality as part of the national emergency alert system and prepare for the nationwide emergency alert system test on December 10.

**Test Details:**
- Date: December 4, 2025
- Time window: 10:00-16:00
- Apps: Eesti app, Ole valmis!
- Multiple notifications possible
- Purpose: Function check and preparation for December 10 nationwide test
