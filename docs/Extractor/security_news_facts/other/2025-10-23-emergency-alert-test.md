## Summary
Nationwide emergency alert testing scheduled for October 28, 2025 at 11:30 via Eesti app and Ole valmis! app to verify functionality as part of national emergency alert system.

## Factual Content
On Tuesday, October 28, 2025 at 11:30, nationwide app emergency alert testing will take place. In cooperation with the Rescue Board, test notifications will be sent via the Eesti app and "Ole valmis!" mobile applications. The purpose of testing is to check the apps' functioning as part of the national emergency alert system.

**Test Details:**
- Date and time: October 28, 2025, 11:30
- Apps: Eesti app, Ole valmis!
- Scope: Nationwide
- Purpose: Verify emergency alert system functionality
- Organizer: Rescue Board with partners
