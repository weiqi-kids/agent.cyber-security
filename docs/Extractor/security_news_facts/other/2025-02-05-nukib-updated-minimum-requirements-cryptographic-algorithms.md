## 摘要

Czech Republic's NUKIB published an updated version of its Minimum Requirements for Cryptographic Algorithms document, reflecting evolving security assessments of existing algorithms and the emergence of new threats including quantum computing.

## 事實內容

- NUKIB (Národní úřad pro kybernetickou a informační bezpečnost) updated its Minimum Requirements for Cryptographic Algorithms
- The document reflects continuous evolution in cryptographic algorithm security assessments
- New algorithms are emerging while new attacks against existing ones may be discovered
- The update ensures alignment with current cryptographic knowledge
- Regular updates to this document are necessary given rapidly changing cryptographic landscape
- The document provides guidance for entities subject to Czech cybersecurity obligations

## 受影響系統

- Organizations subject to Czech cybersecurity regulations
- Systems using cryptographic algorithms for security
