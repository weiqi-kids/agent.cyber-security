## 摘要

Croatia's CERT.hr shut down its Spamblok DNSBL service as of January 7, 2026.

## 事實內容

CERT.hr announced the termination of its CERT Spamblok service effective January 7, 2026. Users who implemented this service through the spamassassin-cn plugin on their email servers are advised to comment out or remove configuration entries referencing spamblok.cert.hr. The organization thanked users for their multi-year usage of the service.

## 受影響系統

- Email servers using CERT Spamblok DNSBL service
- Spamassassin-cn plugin configurations
