## 摘要

CNCS issued a public advisory urging citizens to verify information from official sources and avoid sharing false news circulating in connection with the national power grid failure.

## 事實內容

In response to the spread of disinformation surrounding the national power grid failure, CNCS issued a public advisory on 1 May 2025. The advisory urged the public to:

- Consult and verify information from official and reliable sources
- Refrain from sharing unverified news or rumors
- Be alert to the circulation of false information during crisis situations

The advisory emphasized that disinformation spreads rapidly during disruptive events and that responsible information behavior is critical to public safety and coordinated response efforts.

- Social media and information ecosystem
