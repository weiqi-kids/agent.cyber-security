## Summary
Estonia scheduled nationwide emergency alert app testing for October 28, 2025, at 11:30, sending test notifications through Eesti app and Ole valmis! app to verify functionality.

## Description
On Tuesday, October 28, 2025, at 11:30, a nationwide emergency alert app test will take place. In cooperation with the Rescue Board, the Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) and Women's Voluntary Defence Organization will send test notifications through the Eesti app and "Ole valmis!" mobile applications.

The test aims to verify the apps' functionality as part of the national emergency alert system, ensuring the digital alert infrastructure is operational and ready for actual emergency situations.

## Technical Details
- **Test Date**: October 28, 2025
- **Test Time**: 11:30
- **Apps**: Eesti app, Ole valmis! mobile application
- **Purpose**: Verify app functionality in national emergency alert system
- **Partners**: Rescue Board, RIA, Women's Voluntary Defence Organization
