## 摘要

The 3rd Hackultet cybersecurity CTF competition for students concluded; the team "Bez Konsenzusa" won with 7,550 points, with 15 teams (75 students) participating.

## 事實內容

The 3rd Hackultet, a Capture the Flag (CTF) cybersecurity competition for students, concluded. The winning team was "Bez Konsenzusa" with 7,550 points. The team is composed of students from the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing and the Algebra Bernays University. A total of 15 teams comprising 75 students participated in the competition. This is an annual event organized to develop cybersecurity talent among Croatian university students.
