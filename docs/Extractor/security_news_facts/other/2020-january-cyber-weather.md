## 摘要

January 2020 cyber weather featured scams, phishing, and VPN/Citrix vulnerabilities; network performance solid.

## 事實內容

January 2020 saw grey and rainy cyber weather with reports of several scams and phishing messages. Vulnerabilities discovered in VPN and Citrix remote access and virtual services were actively exploited for data breaches. Network performance was solid with only one major incident. Good preparation prevented reported attacks from impacting service functioning.

## 受影響系統

- VPN services
- Citrix remote access services
