## Summary
Estonia's Rescue Board tested emergency alert functionality in the Eesti app on December 4, 2025, successfully reaching over 150,000 devices despite temporary overload issues that were quickly resolved.

## Description
The Estonian Rescue Board tested the technical functionality of emergency alerts in the Eesti app and "Ole valmis!" applications on December 4, 2025, in cooperation with the Estonian Information System Authority (RIA), Women's Voluntary Defence Organization, and the Ministry of Interior's IT and Development Center (SMIT).

The test ran from 10:00 to 12:00, during which three test notifications were sent to Eesti app users. The notifications were delivered as planned and reached more than 150,000 devices. A temporary overload situation occurred but was resolved quickly.

This test was conducted in preparation for the nationwide emergency alert system test scheduled for December 10, 2025.

## Technical Details
- **Test Window**: December 4, 2025, 10:00-12:00
- **Apps Tested**: Eesti app, Ole valmis! app
- **Test Notifications**: 3 sent during test period
- **Reach**: 150,000+ devices
- **Issue**: Temporary overload (quickly resolved)
- **Partners**: RIA, Women's Voluntary Defence Organization, SMIT
