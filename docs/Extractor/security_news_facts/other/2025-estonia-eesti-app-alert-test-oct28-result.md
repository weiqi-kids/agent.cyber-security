## Summary
Estonia's Rescue Board conducted technical test of emergency alert apps on October 28, 2025. Eesti app reached 130,000+ devices successfully; Ole valmis! app experienced initial technical issues, test notification sent later.

## Description
The Estonian Rescue Board conducted a technical functionality test of the Eesti app and "Ole valmis!" mobile applications on October 28, 2025, in cooperation with the Estonian Information System Authority (RIA), Women's Voluntary Defence Organization, and the Ministry of Interior's IT and Development Center (SMIT).

The Eesti app notification was sent as planned and reached more than 130,000 devices. However, the "Ole valmis!" app experienced initial technical problems with message delivery, and the test notification was sent out later after issues were resolved.
