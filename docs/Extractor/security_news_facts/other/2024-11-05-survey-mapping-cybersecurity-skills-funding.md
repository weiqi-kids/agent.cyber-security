## 摘要
A survey is available until November 29, 2024 to support the European Cybersecurity Competence Centre (ECCC), European Commission and member states in mapping available funds for cybersecurity skills development.

## 事實內容
The European Cybersecurity Competence Centre (ECCC), European Commission and EU member states launched a survey to map available funding streams for cybersecurity competence and skills development across Europe. The survey was open until November 29, 2024, gathering information from organizations about funding sources, gaps and opportunities in cybersecurity workforce development.

## 受影響系統
- Cybersecurity training and education organizations
- ECCC and member states cybersecurity skills programs
