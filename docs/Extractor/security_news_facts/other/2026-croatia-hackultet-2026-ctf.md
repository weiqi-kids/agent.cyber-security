## Summary
Registration has opened for Hackultet 2026, a third annual CTF competition for students organized by Croatian Academic and Research Network (CARNET) and FER University of Zagreb.

## Factual Content
The Croatian Academic and Research Network (CARNET) and the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing of the University of Zagreb (FER) have announced the opening of registration for the third Hackultet – a Capture The Flag (CTF) competition for students.

**Event Details:**
- **Organizers:** CARNET and FER (Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing, University of Zagreb)
- **Target Audience:** University students
- **Format:** CTF competition
- **Edition:** Third annual event

**Purpose:**
The competition is conducted as part of CARNET's e-University project funded by the European Union from the National Recovery and Resilience Plan. The goal of the competition is to raise awareness about the importance of applying cybersecurity measures.
