## 摘要

CNCS Portugal held the 2nd edition of the Cybersecurity Forum for Local Administration (Fórum de Cibersegurança da Administração Local), bringing together municipal and regional government representatives to discuss cybersecurity challenges.

## 事實內容

- The 2nd edition of the Cybersecurity Forum for Local Administration took place
- The forum gathers representatives from local government entities across Portugal
- Topics included cybersecurity challenges specific to municipal administrations, incident response, and regulatory compliance
- CNCS used the forum to promote awareness and capacity building in local government cybersecurity

## 受影響系統

- Local and regional government entities in Portugal
- Municipal IT systems and digital services
