## 摘要

Portugal's National Cybersecurity Exercise tested the national communication ecosystem, evaluating coordination and response capabilities across government, critical infrastructure, and communications sector entities.

## 事實內容

- CNCS coordinated a National Cybersecurity Exercise focusing on the communications ecosystem
- The exercise tested coordination, incident response, and communication protocols between public and private sector entities
- Participants included representatives from critical communications infrastructure sectors
- The exercise was designed to identify gaps and improve national resilience in cybersecurity incident response

## 受影響系統

- National communication infrastructure
- Government agencies and critical sector operators in Portugal
