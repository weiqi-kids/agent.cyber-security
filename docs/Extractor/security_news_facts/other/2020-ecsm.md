## 摘要

European Cybersecurity Month (ECSM) campaign launches in October with focus on digital security skills.

## 事實內容

October 2020 marks the return of the European Cybersecurity Month (ECSM), an annual EU campaign promoting cybersecurity awareness. The campaign aims to improve citizens' information security skills and will feature prominently on Traficom's website and social media channels. ECSM is designed for all users of digital services.
