## 摘要
An open call for proposals under DIGITAL-2023-SKILLS-05: Advanced Digital Skills is underway from November 21, 2023 to March 21, 2024, offering funding opportunities to strengthen cybersecurity and digital skills across Europe.

## 事實內容
The European Commission launched the DIGITAL-2023-SKILLS-05 call under the Digital Europe Programme (DIGITAL), targeting advanced digital skills development including cybersecurity. Organizations can submit proposals between November 21, 2023 and March 21, 2024. CNCS Portugal highlighted this opportunity for national entities interested in EU funding for digital skills initiatives.

## 受影響系統
- Digital Europe Programme beneficiaries
- Cybersecurity training and education institutions
