## 摘要

NCSC-FI publishes guidance for individuals who fall victim to online scams, covering first steps, technical help, financial recovery, and emotional support resources.

## 事實內容

NCSC-FI published a consumer guidance article outlining steps for individuals who have been or suspect they have been victimized by online scams. Key content:

- Falling victim to an online scam can result in financial loss, compromise of personal and sensitive information, emotional distress, and loss of sense of security
- Acting quickly is essential to minimise harm
- The article outlines first steps to take immediately upon realising or suspecting a scam
- Guidance is provided on where to find help for technical issues, financial matters, and emotional wellbeing
- The article addresses multiple impact dimensions of online scam victimisation
