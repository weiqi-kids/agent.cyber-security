## Summary
Estonia's Rescue Board successfully tested the EE-ALARM nationwide emergency alert system on December 10, 2025, reaching 155,000 devices through the Eesti app.

## Description
The Estonian Rescue Board tested the nationwide emergency alert system EE-ALARM together with partners on December 10, 2025. The test included activating the siren network in the afternoon, sending notifications to the Eesti app and "Ole valmis!" (Be Ready!) mobile application, and displaying emergency alert tickers on ERR channels.

The Eesti app notification was sent according to plan and successfully reached 155,000 devices, demonstrating the system's capability to deliver critical emergency information to citizens through digital channels.

## Technical Details
- **System**: EE-ALARM (nationwide emergency alert system)
- **Test Date**: December 10, 2025 (afternoon)
- **Reach**: 155,000 devices via Eesti app
- **Components Tested**: Siren network, Eesti app, Ole valmis! app, ERR broadcast tickers
- **Status**: Successful
