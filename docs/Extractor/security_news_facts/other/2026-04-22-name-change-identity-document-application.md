## 摘要

Estonia introduces a new e-service allowing citizens to apply for new identity documents simultaneously when changing their name through marriage, divorce, or name change.

## 事實內容

- From April 2026, it is now possible to begin the application for new identity documents under the new name directly through the e-population register (e-rahvastikuregister).
- The service applies to cases of marriage, divorce, or general name changes.
- Citizens can initiate the new document application immediately at the point of name change, streamlining the administrative process.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian e-population register (e-rahvastikuregister)
- Estonian identity document issuance system
