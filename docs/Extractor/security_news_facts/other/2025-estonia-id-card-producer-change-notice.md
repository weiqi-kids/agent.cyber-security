## Summary
ID card producer changes from IDEMIA to Thales in mid-November 2025. Service providers advised to prepare in advance to ensure smooth service operation after new cards launch.

## Description
The ID card producer will change in mid-November 2025 from IDEMIA to Thales. Service providers are advised to prepare in advance to ensure smooth operation of services after the new cards are introduced. This transition represents a significant change in Estonia's digital identity infrastructure requiring proactive preparation.

## Technical Details
- **Change Date**: Mid-November 2025
- **Previous Producer**: IDEMIA
- **New Producer**: Thales
- **Required Action**: Service provider preparation and testing
- **Impact**: Service compatibility with new ID cards
