## Summary
The "Together for a Better Internet" conference organized by Croatian Safer Internet Centre, HAKOM, and CARNET's CERT marked Safer Internet Day on February 10, 2026, focusing on digital safety for children and youth.

## Factual Content
**Event Details:**
- **Conference Name:** "Together for a Better Internet" (Zajedno za bolji internet)
- **Date:** February 10, 2026
- **Location:** Zagreb, Croatia
- **Organizers:**
  - Safer Internet Centre (Croatia)
  - HAKOM (Croatian Regulatory Authority for Network Industries)
  - CARNET's National CERT

**Key Participants:**
- Miro Đuzel, Assistant Director of CARNET for National CERT
- Tomislav Ramljak, Head of Safer Internet Centre
- Tonko Obuljen, President of HAKOM Council

**Context:**
Safer Internet Day is a global initiative involving over 180 countries worldwide, aimed at creating a safer, more responsible, and higher-quality digital environment. The conference brought together institutions and children to discuss digital safety issues.
