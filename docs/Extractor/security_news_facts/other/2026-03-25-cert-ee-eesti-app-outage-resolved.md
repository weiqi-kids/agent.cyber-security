## 摘要

Technical outages that temporarily affected the Estonian national app (Eesti äpp) on March 25, 2026 have been resolved.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) confirmed that technical outages that occurred in the Eesti äpp (Estonian national mobile application) on March 25, 2026 have been resolved. The brief disruption was related to the EE-ALARM national emergency alert system infrastructure, which had recently been tested nationwide on March 18. The update clarified that additional information about safety alerts remains available through official channels.

## 受影響系統

- Eesti äpp (Estonian national mobile application)
- EE-ALARM emergency alert system
