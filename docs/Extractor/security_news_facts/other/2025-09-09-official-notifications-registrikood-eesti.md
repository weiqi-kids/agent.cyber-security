## 摘要

RIA clarified that official eesti.ee portal notifications are sent from the official state address registrikood@eesti.ee, after some users questioned the sender address following updated terms of service notification.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) updated the terms of use for the state portal eesti.ee and notified users. The notification was sent from the official state address registrikood@eesti.ee. Since the sender address raised questions among some users, RIA published guidance on how to recognize official notifications. This is a public awareness communication to help users distinguish legitimate official notifications from potential phishing attempts.

- Official notification address: registrikood@eesti.ee
- Context: Terms of service update notification caused user confusion
- Purpose: Anti-phishing awareness — helping users recognize legitimate official communications

## 受影響系統

- eesti.ee portal users (Estonia)
