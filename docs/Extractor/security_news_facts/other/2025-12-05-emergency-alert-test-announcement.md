## Summary
Rescue Board announces nationwide emergency alert system test on December 10, 2025, between 15:00-15:10, including siren activation and mobile notifications.

## Factual Content
On Wednesday, December 10, the Rescue Board will test the entire emergency alert system EE-ALARM together with partners. Between 15:00-15:10, the siren network will be activated with a real danger signal, information will be transmitted via the Eesti app and "Ole valmis!" mobile application, and on ERR channels. SMS will only be used for pre-notification in this test. Already in the morning, SMS messages will be sent to all mobile phones in Estonia informing about the afternoon test and especially the siren activation.

**Test Schedule:**
- Date: December 10, 2025
- Time: 15:00-15:10
- Siren test: Real danger signal
- Mobile notifications: Eesti app, Ole valmis! app
- TV: ERR channels
- Pre-notification: SMS in the morning
- System: EE-ALARM
