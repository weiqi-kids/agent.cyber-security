## 摘要

AP2SI and CNCS launching 4th edition of study on cybersecurity professionals in Portugal.

## 事實內容

The Portuguese Association for the Promotion of Information Security (AP2SI), with the support of the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory, is promoting the 4th edition of the Study on Cybersecurity and Information Security Professionals in Portugal. This initiative aims to analyze the landscape of the cybersecurity sector, including workforce composition, skills gaps, compensation, and professional development trends. The study provides insights for policymakers and industry stakeholders to strengthen the cybersecurity workforce ecosystem in Portugal.

Original language: Portuguese (CNCS Portugal)

- Organizations hiring or developing cybersecurity talent
