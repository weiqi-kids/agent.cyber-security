## Summary
Romanian National Cybersecurity Directorate (DNSC) announces the launch of a selection process for public institutions to participate in the "Creating New Cybersecurity Competencies for Society and Economy" project.

## Factual Content
**Project Details:**
- **Title:** "Creating New Cybersecurity Competencies for Society and Economy"
- **Organizer:** Directoratul Național de Securitate Cibernetică (DNSC) - National Cybersecurity Directorate
- **Target Beneficiaries:** Public institutions
- **Purpose:** Developing new cybersecurity competencies

**Announcement Type:**
Official announcement regarding the launch of the selection call for the public institution beneficiary category within the project framework.

**Context:**
This represents a structured government initiative to enhance cybersecurity capabilities across Romania's public sector, focusing on building competencies that benefit both society and the economy.
