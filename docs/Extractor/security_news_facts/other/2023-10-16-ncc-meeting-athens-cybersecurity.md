## 摘要
The network of National Coordination Centers (NCCs) gathered in Athens, Greece on October 11 at ENISA premises to coordinate European cybersecurity competence development and collaboration activities.

## 事實內容
The NCC network, which includes Portugal's NCC coordinated by CNCS, met at the European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) headquarters in Athens. These centers coordinate national efforts within the European cybersecurity ecosystem, supporting R&D investment, industrial capacity development, and cross-border cooperation under the European Cybersecurity Competence Centre (ECCC) framework.

## 受影響系統
- European cybersecurity coordination network
- National Coordination Centers across EU member states
