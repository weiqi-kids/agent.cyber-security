## 摘要
DNSC announced it will no longer issue the NIS Responsible certification, marking a procedural change in Romania's cybersecurity compliance framework.

## 事實內容
The Romanian National Directorate of Cyber Security (DNSC / CERT-RO) published an official announcement stating that it will no longer issue the "Responsible NIS" (responsabil NIS) certificate. This represents a change in administrative procedure related to Romania's implementation of the NIS (Network and Information Security) Directive. Organizations previously relying on this certification for compliance purposes may need to adapt their processes accordingly. Further details on the transition arrangements were expected to be communicated through official DNSC channels.

## 受影響系統
- Romanian organizations subject to NIS Directive compliance
- Entities previously holding or seeking NIS Responsible certification
