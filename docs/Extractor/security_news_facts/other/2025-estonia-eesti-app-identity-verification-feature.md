## Description
From July 7, 2025, Eesti app users can prove their identity directly via smartphone by presenting digital ID card or passport data to service providers. The solution's use is voluntary for both service providers and users, with implementation happening gradually in cooperation with service providers.

This feature enhances convenience of digital identity verification while maintaining security, eliminating the need to physically present identification documents at service points.

## Technical Details
- **Launch Date**: July 7, 2025
- **Feature**: Digital identity verification via smartphone
- **Data Sources**: ID card, passport
- **Adoption**: Voluntary, gradual rollout
- **Partners**: Service providers (cooperating on implementation)
