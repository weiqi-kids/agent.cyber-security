## 摘要
Team Portugal, composed of 10 young talents in junior and senior categories, participated in the 8th European Cybersecurity Challenge 2023 (ECSC 2023), a competition organized by ENISA to identify and develop young cybersecurity talent across Europe.

## 事實內容
ECSC 2023 is an annual European cybersecurity competition for young professionals. Portugal's Team Portugal was selected through the national Cybersecurity ChallengePT process coordinated by CNCS. The team competed against peers from other European nations in practical cybersecurity challenges covering areas including web security, reverse engineering, cryptography, and forensics.
