## 摘要

Portuguese CNCS releases 2019 societal cybersecurity observation report analyzing citizen behavior.

## 事實內容

The Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) published the 2019 Social Observation Line Report (Relatório 2019 da Linha de Observação Sociedade). This document focuses on the social aspects of cybersecurity, examining how citizens in general think and act in this domain. The report draws on multiple recognized sources including Eurobarometer, Eurostat, the National Statistics Institute, and the Directorate-General for Education and Science Statistics. The report aims to inform the community about this social component of cybersecurity and to contribute to evidence-based public policies.

Original language: Portuguese (CNCS)
