## Summary
Estonian Information System Authority's cybersecurity awareness portal itvaatlik.ee now available in three languages: Estonian, Russian, and English.

## Description
The Estonian Information System Authority's (RIA) cybersecurity awareness portal itvaatlik.ee is now available in three languages—Estonian, Russian, and English. This expansion improves accessibility for Estonia's diverse population and international audiences, supporting broader cybersecurity education efforts.
