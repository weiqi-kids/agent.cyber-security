## 摘要

Finnish DNSSEC adoption increased dramatically with validation grade reaching 90% of internet traffic.

## 事實內容

The number of registrars providing DNSSEC increased from under 10 to over 250 registrars by end of 2019. The validation grade for ensuring genuine websites and email senders rose to 90% of Finnish internet traffic. Finland now ranks among the best countries globally for DNSSEC validation grades. Traficom provides DNSSEC to Finnish registrars free of charge.
