## Summary
Taavi Kupper leads RIA's Cybersecurity Center incident response department (CERT-EE) starting October 1, 2025. Previous head Veikko Raasuke continues as architect focusing on government e-service security.

## Description
Starting October 1, 2025, Taavi Kupper leads the incident response department (CERT-EE) of the Estonian Information System Authority's Cybersecurity Center. The previous head, Veikko Raasuke, continues as an architect, focusing on ensuring the security and reliability of government e-services.

This leadership transition maintains continuity while allowing the previous head to contribute expertise in a specialized architectural role.
