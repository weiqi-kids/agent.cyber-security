## Summary
Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) updated the eesti.ee government portal's business view with a new homepage, topic pages, and personalized dashboard to improve information accessibility.

## Description
The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) redesigned the eesti.ee government portal's business view, introducing a new homepage, topic pages, and a personalized dashboard. The update aims to make finding business-related information more comprehensive and convenient for entrepreneurs.

The redesign enhances user experience for business users accessing government services and information through the national portal, improving navigation and personalization features.
