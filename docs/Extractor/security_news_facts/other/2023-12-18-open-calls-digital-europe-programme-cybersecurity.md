## 摘要
CNCS Portugal announced funding opportunities under the Digital Europe Programme with a call for cybersecurity deployment actions open from January 16 to March 26, 2024, supporting organizations seeking EU investment for cybersecurity initiatives.

## 事實內容
The Digital Europe Programme launched a new call for proposals titled "Call Deployment Actions in the area of Cybersecurity (DIGITAL)" with submissions accepted from January 16 to March 26, 2024. CNCS Portugal publicized this opportunity to encourage national entities to apply for EU funding to strengthen cybersecurity capabilities across organizations and sectors.

## 受影響系統
- Organizations applying to Digital Europe Programme
- National cybersecurity ecosystem
