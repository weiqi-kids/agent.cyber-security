## 摘要
The 3rd edition of the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory Society Report was published, presenting the latest indicators on cybersecurity attitudes, behaviors, awareness, and education in Portugal, including digital usage and risk exposure analysis, with recommendations for identified weaknesses.

## 事實內容
The 3rd edition of the Society Report from the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory was published. This report:

- **Covers**: Latest indicators on attitudes, behaviors, awareness, and education in cybersecurity in Portugal for 2021
- **New this year**:
  - Tracking indicators related to the National Cyberspace Security Strategy 2019-2023
  - A chapter on general digital usage and corresponding risk exposure
  - Deeper survey of organizations conducting external awareness activities
  - Recommendations addressing the weakest points identified
- **Formats available**: Full document and a "Report in 15 minutes" summary version

The report is part of the Observatory's series monitoring the human and societal dimensions of cybersecurity in Portugal.

## 受影響系統
- Portuguese organizations and citizens
- Cybersecurity awareness programs nationwide
