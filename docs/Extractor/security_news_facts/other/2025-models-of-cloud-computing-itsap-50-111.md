August 2025 | Awareness series




Cloud service providers (CSPs) offer 3 service models and 4 deployment models. Service models provide customers with options to access a CSP’s services, while deployment models offer customers different ways of using them. This publication provides an overview of the different models of cloud computing, allowing you to choose the best option for your organization.
