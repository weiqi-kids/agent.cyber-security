## 摘要

Guidance on selecting video conference solutions based on information confidentiality requirements.

## 事實內容

When selecting video conference solutions, organizations should consider the purpose and confidentiality of information. Meeting organizers should ensure only desired persons can participate and that participants only have necessary access rights. NCSC-FI provides guidance on secure video conferencing practices.

## 受影響系統

- Video conferencing platforms
- Remote meeting infrastructure
