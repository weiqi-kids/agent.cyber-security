## 摘要

Emergency alert system test succeeded, with Eesti app notification reaching 155,000 devices, sirens activated, and messages displayed on ERR channels.

## 事實內容

On December 10, 2025, the Rescue Board tested the nationwide emergency alert system EE-ALARM in cooperation with partners.

Test components:
- Siren network activation
- Notifications via Eesti app: Reached 155,000 devices successfully
- Notifications via "Ole valmis!" mobile app
- Emergency alert banners on ERR (Estonian Public Broadcasting) channels

The test was conducted in the afternoon and the Eesti app notification was delivered as planned, demonstrating the effectiveness of Estonia's multi-channel emergency notification infrastructure.

## 受影響系統

- Eesti app (155,000 devices reached)
- Ole valmis! mobile app
- EE-ALARM emergency alert system
- National siren network
