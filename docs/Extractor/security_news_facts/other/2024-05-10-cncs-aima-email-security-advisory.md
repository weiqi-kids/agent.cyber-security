## 摘要

CNCS alerts citizens about a legitimate email from AIMA (Agency for Integration, Migrations and Asylum) containing a payment link, advising cyber hygiene best practices when handling such messages.

## 事實內容

The Portuguese National Cybersecurity Centre (CNCS) issued an advisory regarding an email sent by AIMA – Agência para a Integração, Migrações e Asilo (Agency for Integration, Migrations and Asylum) to foreign nationals awaiting appointment scheduling for residence permit issuance. The email contained a link with instructions for paying associated fees.

AIMA confirmed the authenticity of the message, which is part of a legitimate administrative procedure aimed at simplifying legal compliance through digital means. However, CNCS advised recipients to apply good cyber-hygiene practices when receiving such messages, including:

- Verifying that the message was sent from a legitimate email address (in this case: manifestacao.interesse@aima.gov.pt)
- Confirming that the link in the message points to a secure domain (in this case: services.aima.gov.pt)

The advisory serves as a reminder to verify sender identity and link legitimacy even for messages that appear to be from official government bodies.

## 受影響系統

- Foreign nationals in Portugal awaiting residence permit appointments
- AIMA digital services
