## Summary
Estonia's eesti.ee portal adds two personalized event services for military conscription and divorce proceedings, delivering information in formats tailored to user situations.

## Description
The eesti.ee government portal now offers two new personalized event services that bring essential information to users in formats precisely matching their situations. Both military conscription and divorce services are not new themselves, but are now more conveniently accessible through the government portal and customized to individual user needs.

## Technical Details
- **Portal**: eesti.ee (government portal)
- **New Services**: Military conscription, divorce proceedings
- **Feature**: Personalized information delivery
- **Improvement**: Enhanced convenience and user-specific customization
