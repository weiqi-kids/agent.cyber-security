## 摘要
SK-CERT publishes privacy policy for Kyber2025 conference registration in compliance with GDPR.

## 事實內容
The National Cyber Security Centre (SK-CERT) of Slovakia has published the privacy policy governing personal data processing for Kyber2025 conference registration.

**Legal Framework:**
- **Regulation:** EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR)
- **Slovak Law:** Act No. 18/2018 on personal data protection

**Data Controller:**
National Cyber Security Centre of Slovakia

**Purpose:**
The privacy policy document explains how personal data collected during conference registration will be:
- Collected
- Processed
- Stored
- Protected
- Used

**Conference Context:**
This relates to the Kyber2025 conference, a cybersecurity event organized by SK-CERT focusing on NIS2 directive implementation and cybersecurity trends.

**Note:** This is administrative documentation for conference management rather than a security incident or technical advisory.
