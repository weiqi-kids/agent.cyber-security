## 摘要

Smart-ID login functionality in the Estonian national app (Eesti äpp) has been restored to normal operation after previously reported issues.

## 事實內容

The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) announced that Smart-ID login functionality in the Eesti äpp (Estonian national mobile application) is now functioning normally again. The restoration follows earlier disruptions related to the Smart-ID+ upgrade that was introduced to the national authentication service on February 26, 2026, which caused compatibility issues with some national mobile applications.

## 受影響系統

- Eesti äpp (Estonian national mobile application)
- Smart-ID authentication service
