## 摘要

NUKIB published an updated version 3.0 of its Recommendations in the Field of Cryptographic Means, providing guidance on acceptable cryptographic algorithms for entities subject to Czech cybersecurity law (Act No. 181/2014 Sb.).

## 事實內容

- NUKIB (Národní úřad pro kybernetickou a informační bezpečnost) published Recommendations v3.0 on Cryptographic Means
- The recommendations are issued under §26(d) of Decree No. 82/2018 Sb. on security measures
- Organizations subject to Czech Cybersecurity Act No. 181/2014 Sb. are obligated to follow these recommendations
- The purpose is to protect assets of information and communication systems
- The document reflects both ongoing evolution of cryptographic algorithm security and current knowledge in the field
- Regular updates are necessary given the dynamic nature of cryptography

## 受影響系統

- Information and communication systems subject to Czech cybersecurity law
- Organizations obligated under Czech Cybersecurity Act
