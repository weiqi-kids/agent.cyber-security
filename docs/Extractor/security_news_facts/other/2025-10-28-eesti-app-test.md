## Summary
Rescue Board's technical test of emergency alert apps completed successfully with Eesti app notification reaching 130,000+ devices, while Ole valmis! app had initial technical issues.

## Factual Content
The Rescue Board conducted a technical test of the Eesti app and "Ole valmis!" mobile applications' functionalities today in cooperation with RIA, Women's Voluntary Defence Organisation, and the Ministry of Interior IT and Development Centre (SMIT). The Eesti app notification went out as planned and reached more than 130,000 devices. The "Ole valmis!" app had technical problems with notification delivery initially, and the test notification was sent out later.

**Test Results:**
- Date: October 28, 2025
- Eesti app: Successful, 130,000+ devices reached
- Ole valmis! app: Initial technical issues, later resolved
- Partners: RIA, Women's Defence, SMIT
- Purpose: Technical functionality test
