## 摘要

CNCS published sector reports analyzing cybersecurity state for Essential Services Operators defined under Portuguese law.

## 事實內容

A set of reports produced under the Cybersecurity Observatory is now available, analyzing the state of cybersecurity across the sectors of Essential Services Operators (OSE - Operadores de Serviços Essenciais), as defined under Law No. 46/2018 (Legal Framework for Cybersecurity). The documents contain general recommendations applicable to all OSEs as well as sector-specific guidance. The reports provide a cross-sectoral overview of cybersecurity maturity and challenges across critical infrastructure sectors in Portugal.

Original language: Portuguese (CNCS Portugal)

## 受影響系統

- Essential Services Operators (OSE) in Portugal
- Critical infrastructure sectors
- Energy, transport, banking, healthcare, and digital infrastructure sectors
