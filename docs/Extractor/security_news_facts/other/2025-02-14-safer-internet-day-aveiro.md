## 摘要

Safer Internet Day 2025 celebrated on 11 February in Aveiro, featuring themed workshops for students and a seminar on digital safety.

## 事實內容

The Aveiro Congress Center hosted celebrations for Safer Internet Day on 11 February 2025. The program included:

- More than a dozen themed workshops aimed at students from the local school community in the Aveiro district
- A seminar dedicated to digital safety and online responsibility

The event brought together hundreds of participants from the educational community to promote safer internet practices and digital literacy among young people.
