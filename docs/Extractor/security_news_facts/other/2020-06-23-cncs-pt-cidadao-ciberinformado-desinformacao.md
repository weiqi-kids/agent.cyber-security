## 摘要

CNCS and LUSA launched the "Cidadão Ciberinformado" online course helping citizens identify fake news and verify online information, with certificate upon 75% completion across all modules.

## 事實內容

On 23 June 2020, the Centro Nacional de Cibersegurança (CNCS) launched the "Cidadão Ciberinformado" (Cyber-Informed Citizen) online course in partnership with LUSA (the Portuguese national news agency). This is the second online course developed by CNCS. The course aims to provide citizens with skills to better understand the term "fake news" and related concepts, offering tips to identify the veracity of news or other information published online. Learning objectives include: understanding what fake news is, how it originated and spreads; understanding the importance of combating fake news; verifying the veracity of online news or information; and understanding why to trust journalism. The course is structured over approximately 3 hours and is directed at all citizens who consult information online. Participants can download a Certificate of Completion after completing all modules with a minimum of 75% correct responses. Each module contains various topics with texts, videos, and support materials.
