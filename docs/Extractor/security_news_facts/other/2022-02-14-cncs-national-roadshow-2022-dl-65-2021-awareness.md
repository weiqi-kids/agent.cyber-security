## 摘要
CNCS launched the National Roadshow 2022 — 76 awareness sessions held across Portugal on the Legal Framework for Cyberspace Security (Decree-Law No. 65/2021), targeting public administration, critical infrastructure operators, essential service operators, and digital service providers.

## 事實內容
The National Roadshow 2022 began at CNCS premises with strong attendance. Key details:

- **Format**: 76 awareness sessions across all district capitals and islands
- **Duration**: 4 hours each session, in-person
- **Target audience**: Public administration, critical infrastructure operators, essential service operators, digital service providers
- **Content**: Obligations under the Legal Framework for Cyberspace Security (Regime Jurídico da Segurança do Ciberespa­ço) — Decree-Law No. 65/2021
  - Network and information systems security requirements
  - Incident notification rules
- **Objective**: Equip entities with knowledge about their legal obligations, facilitating, clarifying, and supporting compliance

## 受影響系統
- Portuguese public administration
- Critical infrastructure operators
- Essential service operators
- Digital service providers
