## Description
The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) is conducting a market study to gather information for preparing Estonia's digital wallet procurement. The digital wallet is an important component of Estonia's electronic identity ecosystem, aiming to fulfill European Union eIDAS regulation requirements and improve user experience.
