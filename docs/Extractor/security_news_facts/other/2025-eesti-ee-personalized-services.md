## Summary
State portal eesti.ee adds two new personalized event services for defense obligation and divorce, delivering relevant information in user-specific format.

## Factual Content
The state portal eesti.ee offers two new personalized event services that bring important information to users in a form tailored to their exact situation. Both defense obligation and divorce services are not new in themselves, but through the state portal they are now available more conveniently and customized to individual needs than before.

**New Services:**
- Defense obligation service
- Divorce process service
- Feature: Personalized to user situation
- Benefit: More convenient access, better customization
- Platform: eesti.ee state portal
