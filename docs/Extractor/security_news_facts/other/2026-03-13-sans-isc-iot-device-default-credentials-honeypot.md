## 摘要

A SANS ISC guest diary documents honeypot observations showing how rapidly IoT devices with default credentials are discovered and exploited by threat actors, emphasizing the critical importance of changing default credentials during device setup.

## 事實內容

- Guest diary published by SANS Internet Storm Center (ISC) based on honeypot and vulnerability assessment observations
- End users frequently skip changing default credentials when deploying new IoT devices
- Honeypot data shows attackers quickly discover devices with default credentials after internet exposure
- The diary walks through real-world examples demonstrating attack timelines
- Key recommendation: Always change default credentials immediately upon device deployment
- Applies to both home and enterprise network environments

## 受影響系統

- IoT devices with default credentials (home routers, cameras, industrial devices)
- Home and corporate network environments
