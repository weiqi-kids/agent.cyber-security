## Summary
RIA clarifies that official eesti.ee notifications come from registrikood@eesti.ee address after user questions about recent terms of service update notification.

## Factual Content
RIA updated the eesti.ee state portal terms of service and notified portal users. The notification was sent officially from the state address registrikood@eesti.ee. Since the sender address raised questions among some users, we explain below how to recognize official notifications.

**Key Points:**
- Official notification address: registrikood@eesti.ee
- Context: Terms of service update
- Purpose: User education on recognizing legitimate notifications
- Platform: eesti.ee
- Issue: User confusion about sender address
