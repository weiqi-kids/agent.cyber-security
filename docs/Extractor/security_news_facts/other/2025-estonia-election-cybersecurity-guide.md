## Summary
Estonian Information System Authority published cybersecurity guide for KOV 2025 election candidates on conducting secure digital campaigns, protecting against cyber threats and disinformation.

## Description
The Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) published a cybersecurity guide for KOV 2025 (local government) election candidates to help conduct campaigns safely and consciously. The guide highlights key recommendations for protecting against cyber threats and disinformation while organizing campaigns responsibly in the digital environment.
