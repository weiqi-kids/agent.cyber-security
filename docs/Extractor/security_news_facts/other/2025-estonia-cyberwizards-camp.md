## Description
The third international CyberWizards camp took place in Estonia from August 11-16, 2025, aiming to encourage girls to discover the world of cybersecurity and IT. The five-day program brought over 80 youth from ten countries to Estonia.

The camp represents Estonia's commitment to addressing gender diversity in cybersecurity and technology fields by inspiring young women to pursue careers in these areas.
