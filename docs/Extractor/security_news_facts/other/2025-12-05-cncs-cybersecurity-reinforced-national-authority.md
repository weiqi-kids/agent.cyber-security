## 摘要

Decree-Law No. 125/2025 approving new Cybersecurity Legal Framework reinforces CNCS's role as National Cybersecurity Authority.

## 事實內容

With the entry into force of Decree-Law No. 125/2025, which approves the new Cybersecurity Legal Framework (RJC), the National Cybersecurity Centre (CNCS) will have its attributions and competencies reinforced as the National Cybersecurity Authority. The new framework consolidates CNCS's mission to ensure that Portugal achieves and maintains a high level of cybersecurity. The Decree-Law implements the EU NIS2 Directive requirements into Portuguese law and strengthens the institutional mandate for national cybersecurity governance.

Original language: Portuguese (CNCS Portugal)

## 受影響系統

- Portuguese national cybersecurity governance
- Organizations subject to the new Cybersecurity Legal Framework
- Essential and important entities under NIS2
