## 摘要

National CERT, EUROPE DIRECT Rijeka, and the City of Rijeka co-hosted the opening of an educational exhibition on cybersecurity awareness for citizens at the Rijeka Korzo promenade.

## 事實內容

The Croatian National CERT, in cooperation with the EUROPE DIRECT Rijeka center and the City of Rijeka, participated in the opening of the educational exhibition "Secure Europe in the Digital Age" (Sigurna Europa u digitalnom dobu). The exhibition was set up on the Rijeka Korzo promenade and presents key cybersecurity topics in a visually attractive way, teaching citizens how to recognize modern digital threats. The opening was attended by the director of the Rijeka Development Agency Porin and other stakeholders. This is a public outreach initiative aimed at improving citizen cybersecurity awareness.
