## Summary
RIA, Tehnopol Startup Incubator, and ECCC's Cyber Accelerator selects six high-potential startups for third round to support product development and market entry.

## Factual Content
RIA, in cooperation with Tehnopol Startup Incubator and the European Cybersecurity Competence Centre (ECCC), is implementing a Cyber Accelerator that supports cybersecurity startups in developing and bringing new products and services to market. Six high-potential startups were selected for the accelerator's third round.

**Program Details:**
- Organizers: RIA, Tehnopol, ECCC
- Selected: 6 startups
- Round: Third cohort
- Focus: Product development and market entry
- Sector: Cybersecurity
