## 摘要
The CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory published Bulletin No. 5/2021 focused on passwords — their vulnerabilities, mitigation efforts, related incident types, and the future outlook for this security mechanism.

## 事實內容
Bulletin No. 5/2021 of the CNCS Cybersecurity Observatory was published with "passwords" as the central theme:

- **Coverage**: Password vulnerabilities and weaknesses
- **Mitigation approaches**: Efforts and methods to address password-related security risks
- **Incident correlation**: Types of incidents most directly linked to password security
- **Future outlook**: Expected evolution of passwords as a security instrument
- **Additional content**: News and publications relevant to cybersecurity

The bulletin is available on the CNCS website as part of the Observatory's regular publication series.

## 受影響系統
- Organizations using password-based authentication
- End users across all sectors
