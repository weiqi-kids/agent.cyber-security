## 摘要

March 2020 state of emergency saw increased remote work; Finnish networks handled capacity strain well.

## 事實內容

The state of emergency declared in March 2020 saw marked increase in remote work. While communications networks' data transfer volumes rose by dozens of percent according to mobile operators, Finland's network capacity was sufficient to withstand additional strain. Despite some challenges, cyber weather for March was as rainy as expected.
