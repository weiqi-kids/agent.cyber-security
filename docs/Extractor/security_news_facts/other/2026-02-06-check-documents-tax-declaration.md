## 摘要

Estonian authorities remind citizens to check document validity and PIN codes before the tax declaration period starting February 16.

## 事實內容

The Tax and Customs Board (MTA), Police and Border Guard Board (PPA), and Information System Authority (RIA) of Estonia remind citizens to verify their documents' validity period and ensure they have their PIN codes available before the income declaration period that begins on February 16, 2026.

This is a public service announcement to ensure smooth digital authentication during the tax filing season.

## 受影響系統

- Estonian e-ID authentication services
- Tax declaration digital services
