## 摘要

NCSC-FI quiz with 3,700 responses shows difficulty of identifying phishing sites by appearance alone.

## 事實內容

NCSC-FI organized a May Day quiz on identifying phishing sites in late April and early May, receiving approximately 3,700 responses. Results reflected the difficulty of spotting phishing sites based only on appearance, highlighting the need for user education on phishing detection.
