## Summary

Romania's national digital education project #SigurantaOnline warns about digital scams that can turn March 8 surprises into fraud opportunities for cybercriminals.

## Factual Content

The Romanian national digital education project #SigurantaOnline has issued a warning ahead of International Women's Day (March 8, 2026) about digital traps that can transform online gift purchases and surprises into fraud. The campaign aims to raise awareness about seasonal scam campaigns that exploit holiday shopping activities.
