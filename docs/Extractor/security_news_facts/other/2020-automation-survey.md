## 摘要

NCSC-FI surveys unprotected automation equipment on networks to improve situational awareness.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre Finland (NCSC-FI) at Traficom searches for unprotected automation equipment on data networks. The aim is to improve situational awareness and cybersecurity in Finland. Results are compared to previous years to track trends.

## 受影響系統

- Industrial automation systems
- Internet-exposed automation equipment
