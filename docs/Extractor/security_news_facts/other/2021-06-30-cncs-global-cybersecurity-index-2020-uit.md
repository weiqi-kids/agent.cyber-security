## 摘要
The ITU (International Telecommunication Union) Global Cybersecurity Index 2020 has been published. Portugal rose significantly in the rankings, now placed 14th globally (previously 42nd in 2018) and 8th in Europe (previously 25th).

## 事實內容
The ITU's Global Cybersecurity Index 2020 report has been made available. Key findings for Portugal:

- **Global ranking**: 14th place (improved from 42nd in 2018)
- **European ranking**: 8th place (improved from 25th in 2018)
- **Strengths identified**: Legislative and regulatory measures, existence and implementation of incident response capabilities at national and sectoral level (CERT/CSIRT), and cooperation and synergies between agencies and sectors
- **Near-maximum scores**: Strategic and organizational measures; awareness, training, and education for cybersecurity
- The report analyzes evidence of national cybersecurity across multiple dimensions

This significant improvement reflects the portfolio of legislative, technical, organizational, capacity-building, and cooperation initiatives implemented in Portugal to strengthen the national cybersecurity framework in recent years.

## 受影響系統

- Public administration, private sector, and citizens
