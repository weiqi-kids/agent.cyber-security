## 摘要

NCSC-FI 第31週週報：激進勒索郵件活動、假期間軟體更新提醒，以及暑假期間發票詐騙警告。

## 事實內容

- An aggressive extortion email campaign has been reported; recipients are being threatened to pay or face exposure.
- NCSC-FI reminded organisations and individuals to install updates and ensure end-of-lifecycle devices and software are updated during the holiday season.
- Invoice fraud is circulating during the summer holiday season, targeting businesses and individuals who may have reduced vigilance.

## 受影響系統

- End-of-lifecycle devices and software (unpatched systems)
