## 摘要

FICORA, Trafi, and Transport Agency functions merge into Traficom on January 1, 2019; NCSC-FI continues operations.

## 事實內容

The Finnish Communications Regulatory Authority (FICORA), Transport Safety Agency (Trafi), and certain official functions of the Transport Agency will merge into Finnish Transport and Communications Agency Traficom on January 1, 2019. The National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC-FI) has been part of FICORA since 2014 and will continue its operations as before under the new agency structure.
