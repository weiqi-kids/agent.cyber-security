## 事實內容

The 6th edition of the Cybersecurity Report on Risks and Conflicts, produced by the Cybersecurity Observatory of the National Cybersecurity Centre (CNCS), has been published. The data results from contributions by various partner entities, both public and private. Key findings indicate a significant increase in the number of cybersecurity incidents occurring in national cyberspace in 2024. The report highlights evolving threat patterns affecting Portuguese organizations across sectors, drawing on data from CNCS and its network of partners. This edition also identifies emerging conflict-related cyber threats targeting critical infrastructure.

Original language: Portuguese (CNCS Portugal)

## 受影響系統

- Private sector organizations in Portugal
