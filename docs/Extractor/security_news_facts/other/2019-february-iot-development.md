## 摘要

February 2019 featured good network performance and IoT security development despite scams and malware.

## 事實內容

Despite glimpses of sun, February 2019 cyber weather was chilly and rainy. Scammers tried to access usernames and passwords and distribute malware via email. No data breaches were detected in Finland in February, but regular vulnerabilities keep exposing organizations' networks to criminals. Good network performance and IoT security development provided positive developments.
