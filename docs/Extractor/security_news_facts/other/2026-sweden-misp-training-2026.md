## Summary
Swedish CERT-SE announces MISP (Malware Information Sharing Platform) training exercises for 2026 to strengthen organizational capabilities in cyber threat management and information sharing.

## Factual Content
**Training Announcement:**
CERT-SE asks: "Does your organization need to strengthen its ability to handle cyber threats and work effectively with information sharing?"

**Purpose:**
Help organizations improve:
- Cyber threat handling capabilities
- Effective information sharing practices
- Collaborative threat intelligence
- Incident response coordination

**Platform Context:**
MISP (Malware Information Sharing Platform) is an open-source threat intelligence platform used for:
- Sharing indicators of compromise (IoCs)
- Collaborative threat analysis
- Incident response coordination
- Cross-organizational intelligence sharing

**Target Audience:**
Organizations seeking to enhance their:
- Threat intelligence capabilities
- Information sharing workflows
- Cybersecurity defense posture
- Collaborative response capabilities

**Strategic Importance:**
MISP training supports Sweden's national cybersecurity strategy by:
- Building community capabilities
- Enabling information sharing networks
- Strengthening collective defense
- Standardizing threat intelligence practices
