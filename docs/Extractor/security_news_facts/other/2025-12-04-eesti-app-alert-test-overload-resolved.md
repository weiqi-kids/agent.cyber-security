## 摘要

Emergency alert technical test succeeded despite temporary overload, reaching over 150,000 devices with three test notifications sent between 10:00-12:00.

## 事實內容

The Rescue Board tested the technical functionality of emergency alerts in the Eesti app and "Ole valmis!" app in cooperation with RIA, Women's Voluntary Defence Organisation, and SMIT (Ministry of Internal Affairs IT and Development Centre) on December 4, 2025.

Test details:
- Time window: 10:00-12:00
- Three test notifications sent via Eesti app
- Successful delivery: Over 150,000 devices
- Issue encountered: Temporary system overload
- Resolution: Quick recovery from overload

The test validated the technical infrastructure while identifying and resolving a capacity issue under load conditions.

## 受影響系統

- Eesti app (150,000+ devices)
- Ole valmis! mobile app
- Emergency notification backend infrastructure
