## 摘要

NCSC-FI publishes cloud security criteria (PiTuKri) in English and guidance for individuals/SMEs.

## 事實內容

NCSC-FI's "Criteria to Assess the Information Security of Cloud Services" (PiTuKri) have raised interest nationally and internationally. The Criteria are mainly aimed at government organizations and are now available in English. NCSC-FI also published guidelines for secure use of cloud services for private individuals and for small organizations and enterprises.
