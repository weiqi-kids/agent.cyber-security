## 摘要

NCSC-FI provides tips for identifying suspicious websites and avoiding phishing attacks.

## 事實內容

Phishing for login details and personal data concerns all internet users. Phishing is carried out constantly in the name of virtually all online service providers. NCSC-FI provides tips to help users identify suspicious websites and remain vigilant when entering sensitive information like passwords or phone numbers online.
