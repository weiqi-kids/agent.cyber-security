## 摘要

Guidance on responding to online scams, minimizing harm, and accessing technical and emotional support.

## 事實內容

NCSC-FI guidance for scam victims:
- Falling victim to online scam is no trivial matter
- Victims may lose money or have personal and sensitive information compromised
- Impact can extend to emotional distress and loss of sense of security
- Importance of acting quickly to minimize harm
- Article outlines first steps to take if scammed or suspicious
- Guidance on where victims can find help with technical issues, financial matters, and emotional wellbeing

This represents important victim support information and incident response guidance.

## 受影響系統

- Scam victims requiring assistance
- Financial systems for reporting fraud
- Support services for technical and emotional help
