## 摘要

CNCS notified of a national power grid failure at 11:30, affecting several European countries; monitoring contact maintained with counterpart agencies and relevant authorities.

## 事實內容

At 11:30 on 28 April 2025, CNCS was notified of a failure in Portugal's national electricity grid ("falha na rede elétrica nacional"). Upon contacting the responsible entities, it was confirmed that the failure affected several European countries. CNCS maintained contact with counterpart agencies in the affected countries and with relevant Portuguese authorities to monitor the situation and coordinate response. The statement was brief and did not attribute the outage to a cyberattack, indicating an infrastructure or operational failure rather than a confirmed security incident.

## 受影響系統

- National electricity grid (Portugal)
- Multiple European countries' power infrastructure
