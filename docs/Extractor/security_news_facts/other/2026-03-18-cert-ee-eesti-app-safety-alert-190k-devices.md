## 摘要

Estonia's RIA successfully tested the EE-ALARM emergency alert system through the Eesti äpp mobile application, reaching approximately 190,000 devices during the test.

## 事實內容

- Estonia's Information System Authority (RIA) conducted a test of the EE-ALARM emergency alert system
- The alert was distributed via the official Eesti äpp (Estonia app) mobile application
- The test reached approximately 190,000 devices
- This demonstrates the capability of Estonia's digital emergency notification infrastructure

## 受影響系統

- Eesti äpp mobile application users
- Estonian public alert infrastructure
