## 摘要

Portugal and CNCS hosted the European Working Group of Competent Authorities on AI for the first time.

## 事實內容

From March 26 to 28, CNCS hosted the first 2025 meeting of the European Working Group of Competent Authorities on AI (EWGCA-AI) in Oeiras, Portugal. This was the first time that Portugal and CNCS organized this meeting, which brought together representatives from the majority of EU Member States across various domains. The working group addresses AI governance and competent authorities' responsibilities under EU AI-related regulations. CNCS is a member of this working group.

Original language: Portuguese (CNCS Portugal)
