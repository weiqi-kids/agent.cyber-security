## Summary
New volunteer service launched on eesti.ee consolidating internal security sector volunteer opportunities, making volunteering clearer with overview of roles and training.

## Factual Content
A new volunteer service has been completed on the state portal eesti.ee, consolidating all internal security sector voluntary work opportunities in one place. The service makes becoming a volunteer clearer by offering an overview of different roles, joining conditions, and necessary training opportunities.

**Key Features:**
- One-stop platform for volunteer opportunities
- Focus: Internal security sector
- Information provided: Roles, conditions, training
- Goal: Strengthen Estonia's crisis resilience through volunteers
- Improved clarity and accessibility
