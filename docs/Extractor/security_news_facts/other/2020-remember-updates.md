## 摘要

Reminder to maintain cybersecurity by continuing updates during exceptional circumstances.

## 事實內容

During exceptional times, focus may shift elsewhere, but cybersecurity maintenance including updates remains critical. NCSC-FI reminds users and organizations to continue taking care of updates even under exceptional circumstances.
