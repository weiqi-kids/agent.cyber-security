## Summary
Estonia launched a new volunteer service on the eesti.ee portal consolidating internal security volunteer opportunities, making the volunteering process clearer with role overviews, joining conditions, and training information.

## Description
A new volunteer service was launched on Estonia's eesti.ee government portal, consolidating all internal security volunteer work opportunities in one location. The service simplifies the volunteering process by providing an overview of different roles, joining conditions, and available training opportunities.

This initiative aims to strengthen Estonia's crisis resilience by increasing volunteer participation in internal security roles, making it easier for citizens to understand and join volunteer programs supporting national security and crisis management.
