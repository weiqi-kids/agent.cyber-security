## Summary
Third annual CyberWizards international camp in Estonia brought over 80 young women from 10 countries for five-day program encouraging exploration of cybersecurity and IT.

## Factual Content
From August 11-16, the international CyberWizards camp took place in Estonia for the third time, aimed at encouraging girls to discover the world of cybersecurity and IT. The five-day program brought to Estonia over 80 young people from ten countries.
