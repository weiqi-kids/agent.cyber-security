## 摘要

CNCS announced an online MOOC on bullying and cyberbullying prevention and intervention, structured in four modules, targeting teachers, parents, psychologists, and other educational professionals.

## 事實內容

The online course "Bullying e Ciberbullying: Prevenir & Agir" (Bullying and Cyberbullying: Prevent & Act) was announced as available from January, under the national plan "Escola Sem Bullying. Escola Sem Violência" (School Without Bullying. School Without Violence). The MOOC is structured in four modules covering: bullying, cyberbullying and screen communication; internet safety and cyberbullying; intervention approaches; and why to trust journalism and not stop consuming information online. The course takes approximately 3 hours and targets teachers, educators, parents/guardians, psychologists, security forces, other educational professionals, and all interested parties. Participants receive a certificate upon completing all modules with at least 75% correct responses.
