## 摘要

Guidance on making emergency calls even without network coverage or during operator disturbances.

## 事實內容

Emergency calls can always be made even during disturbances in operator networks or power blackouts. In rare cases where emergency calls cannot be connected normally, users can still make emergency calls by switching off their mobile phone, switching it back on, and making the call when the phone asks for lock or PIN code.
