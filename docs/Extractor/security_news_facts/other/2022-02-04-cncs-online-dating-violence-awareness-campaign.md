## 摘要
The "Online Dating Violence" awareness campaign (#logoutaoabuso) was launched by the Safe Internet Center (CIS) during Safer Internet Month to raise awareness of harmful behaviors in digital relationships, focusing on control and harassment in online dating.

## 事實內容
The "#logoutaoabuso" (logout of abuse) campaign was launched on February 4, 2022, as part of Safer Internet Month activities:

- **Campaign name**: "Violência no Namoro Online" (Online Dating Violence) — #logoutaoabuso
- **Organizer**: Centro Internet Segura (CIS) — Safe Internet Center
- **Partners**: Commission for Citizenship and Gender Equality (CIG), Continente supermarkets, and Metropolitano de Lisboa
- **Objective**: Raise awareness of harmful, controlling, and aggressive behaviors in digital relationships; promote conscious, responsible, and healthy online behavior
- **Distribution**: Social media, TV, and Continente stores and Lisbon Metro
- **Support line**: Linha Internet Segura: 800 21 90 90 (free, 8:00–22:00), email: linhainternetsegura@apav.pt

The campaign emphasizes that internet safety is also emotional safety, addressing an increasingly common phenomenon of digital abuse in romantic relationships.

## 受影響系統
- General public, particularly young people
- Online platforms used for communication and dating
