## Summary
Romanian DNSC reports that over 500 people in Brașov participated in testing their knowledge about online fraud as part of the national #SigurantaOnline (#OnlineSafety) campaign.

## Factual Content
**Campaign Details:**
- **Campaign Name:** #SigurantaOnline (#OnlineSafety)
- **Organizer:** Directoratul Național de Securitate Cibernetică (DNSC) - National Cybersecurity Directorate
- **Location:** Brașov, Romania
- **Date:** February 18, 2026 (press release date)
- **Participants:** Over 500 people
- **Scope:** National campaign

**Activity:**
Citizens tested their knowledge about online fraud, indicating an interactive educational component to raise awareness about cyber threats and scam techniques.

**Campaign Goals:**
The national #OnlineSafety campaign aims to:
- Educate citizens about online fraud
- Test and improve public awareness
- Build resilience against cyber scams
- Reach diverse communities across Romania

**Impact:**
Strong local turnout of 500+ participants in Brașov demonstrates effective public engagement with cybersecurity awareness initiatives.
