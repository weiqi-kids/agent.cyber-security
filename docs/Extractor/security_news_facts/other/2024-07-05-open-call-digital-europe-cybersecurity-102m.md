## 摘要
Digital Europe Programme open call is running until January 21, 2025, offering 102 million euros for cybersecurity deployment actions across Europe, with CNCS Portugal encouraging national organizations to apply.

## 事實內容
The European Commission opened a new call under the Digital Europe Programme for cybersecurity deployment actions, with a total budget of 102 million euros available. Applications were accepted until January 21, 2025. The call supports organizations deploying cybersecurity solutions, improving resilience, and strengthening capabilities across sectors. CNCS Portugal publicized this funding opportunity to encourage participation from Portuguese entities.

## 受影響系統
- European cybersecurity ecosystem
- Portuguese organizations eligible for Digital Europe funding
