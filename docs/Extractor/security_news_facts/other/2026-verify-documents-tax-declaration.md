## Summary
Tax and Customs Board, Police and Border Guard Board, and RIA remind citizens to check document validity and PIN code availability before tax declaration period starting February 16.

## Factual Content
The Tax and Customs Board (MTA), Police and Border Guard Board (PPA), and Information System Authority (RIA) remind citizens that before the tax declaration period beginning on February 16, it is advisable to review document validity periods and PIN code availability.

**Key Points:**
- Tax declaration period starts: February 16, 2026
- Action required: Check document validity and PIN codes
- Issuing agencies: MTA, PPA, RIA
- Purpose: Ensure smooth tax filing process
