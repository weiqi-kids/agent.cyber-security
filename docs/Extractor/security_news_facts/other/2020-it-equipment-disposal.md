## 摘要

Guidance on securely managing IT equipment disposal and reuse to prevent data leaks.

## 事實內容

Recycling and reuse of IT equipment is often neglected in organizations' data management. Careless data management at a device's end of life may jeopardize earlier information security measures. Under the Waste Act, IT equipment must be reused to the greatest extent possible. When done carelessly, reusing IT equipment may increase data leak risk. The Kanta-Häme health care district illustrates how information security, data protection, and recycling requirements can all be fulfilled.

## 受影響系統

- IT equipment in disposal/recycling phase
- Storage devices with residual data
