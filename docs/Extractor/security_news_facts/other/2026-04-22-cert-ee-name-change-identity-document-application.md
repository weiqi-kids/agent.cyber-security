## 摘要

From April 2026, upon marriage, divorce, or name change, people can immediately begin applying for new identity documents with the updated name via the Estonian e-population register.

## 事實內容

- **Effective date**: April 2026
- **Change**: When changing name (due to marriage, divorce, or legal name change), citizens can now immediately initiate application for new identity documents through the e-population register (e-rahvastikuregistris)
- **Previous process**: Required separate steps to update identity documents after name change
- **New process**: Integrated — name change and new document application can happen simultaneously online
- **Impact**: Streamlined administrative process for identity document management in Estonia

## 受影響系統

- Estonian e-population register (e-rahvastikuregister)
- Estonian identity document issuance system
