## 摘要

NCSC-FI publishes report on information security standards, certification, and development.

## 事實內容

The new report "Luottamuksen lähteillä" (From trustworthy sources) contains information on information security development, standardization and certification, and their current and future state. The report is designed as background material for organizations and experts seeking suitable standards for use as well as for their development work.
