## Description
Last week, the Estonian Information System Authority (RIA) organized a cyber exercise aimed at testing and strengthening the state's capacity to respond to cyber threats directed at critical infrastructure. The exercise focused on electricity system continuity and interagency cooperation.

The drill evaluated how government agencies, critical infrastructure operators, and security organizations coordinate their response to sophisticated cyber attacks targeting essential services, particularly the electrical grid.
