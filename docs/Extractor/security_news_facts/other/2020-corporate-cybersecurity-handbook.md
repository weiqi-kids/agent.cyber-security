## 摘要

NCSC-FI publishes cybersecurity handbook for senior corporate management.

## 事實內容

The National Cyber Security Centre Finland (NCSC-FI) at Traficom created a cybersecurity handbook to help senior corporate management secure their digital business operations. The handbook aims to raise board-level awareness and responsibility for cybersecurity.
