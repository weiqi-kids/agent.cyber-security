## Summary
Rescue Board successfully tested nationwide emergency alert system EE-ALARM, with Eesti app notification reaching 155,000 devices as planned.

## Factual Content
The Rescue Board tested the nationwide emergency alert system EE-ALARM together with partners today. During this, the siren network was activated in the afternoon, notifications were sent to the Eesti app and "Ole valmis!" mobile application, and emergency alert text ribbons were displayed on ERR channels. The Eesti app notification went out as planned and reached 155,000 devices.

**Test Details:**
- System: EE-ALARM (nationwide emergency alert)
- Components tested: Siren network, mobile apps, TV channels
- Eesti app reach: 155,000 devices
- Status: Successful test
- Partners: Rescue Board, RIA, and others
- Date: December 10, 2025
