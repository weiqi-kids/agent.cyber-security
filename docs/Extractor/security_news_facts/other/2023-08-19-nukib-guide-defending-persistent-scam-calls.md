## 摘要

NUKIB published guidance on defending against persistent scam calls after receiving multiple reports of fraudulent phone calls where perpetrators impersonate NUKIB employees to steal money, including one case involving over 500,000 Czech crowns lost.

## 事實內容

- NUKIB received multiple reports of fraudulent phone calls impersonating NUKIB staff
- In one documented case, a victim was defrauded of over 500,000 Czech crowns (approximately €20,000+)
- Fraudsters use NUKIB employees' names to create false authority
- NUKIB clarified that it does not monitor financial transfers as part of its operations
- A criminal complaint was filed against unknown perpetrators
- NUKIB reminds the public that these calls are fraudulent and advises never to send money under time pressure
- The scam pattern involves claiming accounts are blocked and requesting urgent transfer to a "reserve account"

## 受影響系統

- Czech public (individuals targeted by phone scams)
- NUKIB's institutional reputation (being impersonated)
