## 摘要

Estonia to send test emergency notifications via Eesti app and Ole valmis! on December 4, 10:00-16:00, preparing for December 10 nationwide test.

## 事實內容

The Rescue Board, in cooperation with RIA and Women's Voluntary Defence Organisation, will send test notifications via the Eesti app and "Ole valmis!" mobile app on Thursday, December 4, 2025.

Test details:
- Date: December 4, 2025
- Time window: 10:00-16:00
- Users may receive multiple test notifications
- Purpose: Verify app functionality as part of national emergency alert system
- Preparation for: December 10 nationwide emergency alert system test

This is a technical validation test ahead of the comprehensive nationwide emergency alert system test scheduled for December 10.

## 受影響系統

- Eesti app
- Ole valmis! mobile app
- Emergency notification infrastructure
