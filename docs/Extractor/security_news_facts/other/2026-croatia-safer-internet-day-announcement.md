## Summary
Croatia announces participation in Safer Internet Day 2026 on the second Tuesday of February, part of a global initiative involving over 180 countries focused on creating a safer digital environment for children and youth.

## Factual Content
**Event Details:**
- **Theme:** "Together for a Better Internet" (Zajedno za bolji internet)
- **Date:** Second Tuesday in February (February 11, 2026)
- **Scope:** Global initiative with over 180 countries participating
- **Focus:** Digital safety for children and youth

**Background:**
In an era where children and young people grow up surrounded by digital technologies, internet safety has become one of the key social issues. Safer Internet Day aims to create a safer, more responsible, and higher-quality digital environment.

**Context:**
The announcement highlights the growing importance of digital safety education as technology becomes increasingly integrated into youth development and daily life.
