## 摘要

Guidance on VPN split tunnelling to address capacity issues from increased remote work.

## 事實內容

Increased remote work traffic has strained organizations' VPN capacity, resulting in slowed or stopped work. One possible solution is VPN split tunnelling. NCSC-FI provides guidance on managing VPN capacity during periods of high remote work demand.

## 受影響系統

- Corporate VPN infrastructure
- Remote work systems
