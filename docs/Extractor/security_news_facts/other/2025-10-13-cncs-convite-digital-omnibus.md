## 摘要

CNCS Portugal issued an invitation for stakeholders to submit contributions to the Digital Omnibus initiative, seeking input to shape digital policy developments at the European level.

## 事實內容

- CNCS invited public and private stakeholders to submit contributions for the Digital Omnibus initiative
- The initiative aims to consolidate digital regulations and policies at the EU level
- Stakeholders were invited to participate in shaping Portugal's position on EU digital policy
- The call was directed at organizations involved in digital infrastructure, cybersecurity, and related sectors

## 受影響系統

- Digital policy stakeholders in Portugal
- EU digital regulation participants
