## Summary
RIA renovates eesti.ee business portal view with new homepage, topic pages, and personalized dashboard to make information discovery more comprehensive and convenient.

## Factual Content
The Information System Authority (RIA) renovated the eesti.ee state portal's business view, bringing businesses a new homepage, topic pages, and personalized dashboard. The aim of the update is to make finding business-related information more comprehensive and convenient than before.

**Key Features:**
- New homepage for businesses
- Topic pages
- Personalized dashboard
- Goal: More comprehensive information discovery
- Improved convenience
- Target users: Business/company users
