## 摘要

NCSC-FI publishes cybersecurity guide helping small businesses protect against common threats.

## 事實內容

Information security breaches can cause significant financial and reputational harm to small businesses. NCSC-FI released a new guide to help small business owners protect themselves against the most common cyber threats. The guide provides practical, accessible security measures tailored for small enterprises.
