## 摘要

ENISA, partnering with behavioral science specialists Anima People, launched a survey during European Cybersecurity Month (October) to evaluate the effectiveness of cybersecurity awareness campaigns in changing employee behavior.

## 事實內容

The European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) promotes cybersecurity awareness among EU citizens and organizations each October through the European Cybersecurity Month. In partnership with Anima People (behavioral science specialists focused on security), ENISA launched a critical evaluation project assessing the effectiveness of cybersecurity awareness campaigns in achieving long-term behavioral change among employees.

The survey results will benefit organizations designing successful awareness campaigns and help drive long-term security behavior conducive to a more cybersecure world.

Participation link: https://ec.europa.eu/eusurvey/runner/Cybersecurity_Awareness_ECSM-PreC

## 受影響系統

- Organizations and employees across EU member states
- Cybersecurity awareness program designers
