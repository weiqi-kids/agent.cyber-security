## 摘要

General guidance on how to proceed if targeted by identity theft or data breach.

## 事實內容

NCSC-FI provides general advice for individuals who have been targeted by identity theft or whose personal data has been made public as a result of a data breach. The guidance covers protective steps and response procedures applicable to various data breach scenarios, not limited to specific incidents.
