## 摘要

Portugal's Centro Internet Segura launched the #logoutaoabuso campaign during Safer Internet Month to raise awareness of online dating violence and controlling/harassing behaviors on digital platforms.

## 事實內容

As part of Safer Internet Month activities, the "Online Dating Violence" campaign was launched by Centro Internet Segura (CIS). The campaign aims to alert about harmful behaviors in dating relationships arising from digital platform use and to provoke reflection and eliminate limiting and aggressive practices. The campaign is disseminated via social media, Continente stores, and Lisbon Metro carriages.

Key points:
- Partners: Comissão para a Cidadania e a Igualdade de Género (CIG), Continente, Metropolitano de Lisboa
- Support line: Linha Internet Segura at 800 21 90 90 (8h00–22h00, free)
- Email: linhainternetsegura@apav.pt
- Start date: 4 February 2022

## 受影響系統

- General public, especially young people using digital platforms
